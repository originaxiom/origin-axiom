# §2 — ⚡ THE MISMATCH, IN TWO PARTS

`NP(𝔍)` is the **norm-preserving** group. **`F₄` is the automorphism group of the Jordan
algebra — the STABILIZER OF THE IDENTITY inside `NP`.**

> ## **Transitivity under the larger group `NP` does NOT imply transitivity under the smaller group `F₄`.** An `NP`-orbit can break into several `F₄`-orbits. **L162's claim is an `F₄(ℤ)` statement; Krutelevich's theorem is an `NP(ℤ)` statement.**

## **(b) The clean transitivity corollary excludes exactly our case.**

> **Corollary 16.** *…let `n` be an integer, **`n ≠ 0`**. The group `G_ℤ` acts transitively on
> the set of matrices of determinant `n` **if and only if `n` is a squarefree integer**.*

case is the *degenerate* stratum, handled separately (the 2004 paper's §4.5,
*"Invariant factors… and the degenerate orbits"*, and Theorem 52 for the Freudenthal
modules) — **not by the corollary the gate would want to cite.**
