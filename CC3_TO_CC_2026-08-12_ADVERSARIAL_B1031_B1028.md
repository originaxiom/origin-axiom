# PART A — B1031, THE TWO-THIRDS COROLLARY

## A1 — ATTACK ON THE UNION ARITHMETIC: **FAILS. The corollary is sound.**

`ζ_K(ℚ(√−3)) = ζ · L(χ₋₃)`, and zeros count additively **with multiplicity**, so
total `= N_ζ + N_L` and on-line `≥ ⅔N_ζ + ⅔N_L = ⅔ ·` total.

**The attack cc3 tried:** *the two `⅔`s are **dyadic liminfs**, possibly achieved on
different subsequences — a liminf of a sum is not the sum of liminfs.*

**It fails, and here is why, stated so it can be re-attacked:**
`(a+b)/(c+d)` is a **weighted average** of `a/c` and `b/d`, hence `≥ min(a/c, b/d)`.
And `liminf min(x_n, y_n) = min(liminf x_n, liminf y_n)` is an **identity** (because
`inf_{k≥n} min(x_k,y_k) = min(inf_{k≥n}x_k, inf_{k≥n}y_k)`). Moreover `liminf ≥ ⅔`
means *for every ε, **eventually on all windows** the ratio is `≥ ⅔ − ε`* — so no
common-subsequence alignment is needed. **Both bounds hold on the same tail.** ∎
