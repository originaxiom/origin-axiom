# CHALLENGE 1 — **the document says "forces" in its closing paragraph and "conditional" in its opening layer. A reader quotes the closing.**

**Layer 0, the honest boundary:**
> *"A1–A6 are **'motivated but not laws of nature'** by the theorem's own words… The

  and *"mostly **deflates** … largely **one axis in costumes**."*
- `philosophy/P000`: *"the member is **contingent** — m=1 is the **most-selected** member,
  **not forced outright**."*

> **THE CHALLENGE: the closing paragraph is the paragraph that travels.** It is what gets
> quoted, and it drops the conditional that Layer 0 states forty lines from the top.
> **This is the claim-line-drop mechanism operating at document scale — the defect
> `claim_drop.py` was built to find in arcs, in the framework's own summary.**
