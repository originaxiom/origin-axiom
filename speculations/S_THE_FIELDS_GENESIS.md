## 3. A proposed passage from the principle to the field

**Step 1 — an unread description is nothing again.** [POSTULATE, or a reading of PF2; the crux; the owner: state it as a candidate]
- PF2 says the remainder is a description that cannot be exhausted. A description that nothing distinguishes —
  uniform, unread, every point alike — admits no description of its own content. So by PF1's own logic it collapses
  toward nothing.
- So the description must be *read*: something must vary over it.
- A thing that varies over a space, compatibly with that space's own moves, is a section of a bundle over it: a field.
- **The field is the seed's being-read**, forced by the same impossibility one level up.
- **Candidate PF4: "what exists is read."** Stated as a candidate face of the principle (the owner, 2026-10-14). A
  derivation from PF1 and PF2 is attempted in parallel.

**Step 2 — the minimal reading is the seed's own arithmetic.** [MATH given Step 1; LEAP on "minimal"]
- The smallest non-abelian local system the seed supplies is 2T, from its own trace field (F-MC).
- So the field's local system need not be chosen. The seed's arithmetic gives it, if "minimal" is read as "the one the
  seed itself provides". The leap is "minimal".

**Step 3 — the field's quanta are forced.** [MATH given Step 2]
- The zero modes are the cohomology. Their number, three (the triplet), and their forms (theta functions) follow.

**Step 4 — the minimal interaction is at the involution's fixed points.** [candidate MATH; decides P-129's first premise in Λ's frame; B1651, designed; frame-dependent, see B1649's addendum]
- An interaction of the quanta is a pairing of zero modes. At the puncture the only coordinate-free number such a
  pairing carries is its residue.
- A bulk Higgs gives nothing (by hand; B1651's design):
  - its non-trivial-character components have no zero modes on the closed torus;
  - its trivial component's coupling integrates a function odd under z ↦ −z.
- So **a Higgs can couple the zero modes only at the elliptic involution's four fixed points:** the puncture and the
  three half-periods, the cone points of the pillowcase. At each, the triplet is isotropic, so one generation is
  exactly massless (checked numerically before any seal).

**Step 5 — the field cannot stay still.** [LEAP; the owner's own words; at the *modular* cusp]
- The symmetry must break: non-canonicity is total, and no resting choice exists (B700).
- But *which way* it breaks is not forced by any invariant (B990, B1646). So the field's state — the Higgs's direction,
  τ, any Wilson line — is not given by the principle as a value.
- **It can still be forced as a destination, by dynamics.** That is the reading of "can't stay still" that would matter
  for physics:
  - every content on record runs to the cusp (the SM seat's W62);
  - at the cusp the theta triplet tends to the monodromy's isotropic eigenline (W63; the SM seat's P-113 reading);
  - the isotropic cone is where a hierarchy lives (B1645, B1646).
- An image, not an argument: a real vector can rest along a line; an isotropic vector e₁ + i e₂ is a rotation, a
  vector that cannot stay still. **The field's ground state at the puncture is already isotropic** (R, by Jacobi).
- If the dynamics drives τ toward the cusp and the Higgs toward the cusp's isotropic eigenline, then the state is a
  destination, not a choice. What remains free is *how far from the cusp* the field rests. That would be the single
  number the end datum (FK10's unit) or the observer must supply.

**Step 6 — the observer.** [LEAP; the record's measurer question, L241]

- On this passage, the free numbers of the SM would be the coordinates of where the reading happens. That is a

## 5. What the passage FORBIDS (the test that it is a frame, not a lens)

1. **No hierarchy without the cusp.** If Step 5 holds, m₂/m₃ and V_cb must scale as powers of |q| = e^{−π Im τ}, with
   exponents fixed by the Higgs's weight and character. A hierarchy found at a generic interior τ would refute it.
2. **One attractor for both quark sectors.** If both sectors' Higgs directions tend to the same isotropic eigenline,
   their 2–3 misalignment, V_cb, vanishes at the cusp and is small near it. A large V_cb with small m₂/m₃ would refute
   it. (Observed V_cb ≈ 0.04, context, not a test.)
3. **The first generation's lift is off-diagonal along R.** If the leading-order zero is forced (Step 4), whatever lifts
   it must enter off-diagonally, giving the Gatto–Sartori–Tonin form θ_C ≈ √(m_d/m_s). A diagonal lift would refute the
   passage's account of the light masses.
4. **No bulk coupling.** If Step 4's elimination fails — a bulk Higgs zero mode couples the zero modes — the fixed
   points are a choice again, and the passage loses Step 4.
