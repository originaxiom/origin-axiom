## What this entry claims, exactly

Not that the methods are historically connected (no evidence either way), and not that the
house method produces theorems of that calibre — it claims **convergence**: two independent
efforts to make machine mathematics trustworthy arrived at the same instrument set
(vacuity controls with false-analogue objects · first-unjustified-step naming · blind
per-failure-mode review · provenance verification · declared priors with recorded
disbelief · saturation-stop). The convergence is evidence that this instrument set is not
a house idiosyncrasy but what the problem itself demands — the same way two grammars
reaching one endpoint was evidence about the endpoint.

One asymmetry worth keeping: their process ran ~36 hours with a human whose role was
encouragement and handoff; ours runs continuously with the owner as a live epistemic
partner. Their C.6 ("set the epistemic contract first") is the compressed form of what
WORKING_RULES §0 + the firewall + the seal discipline do here every day.
