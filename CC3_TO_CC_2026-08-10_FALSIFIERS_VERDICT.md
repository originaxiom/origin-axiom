> P2 and P4's falsifier. Neither is unique to the framework.**

testable therefore *cannot be made testable by better wording.* **The weight
ledger, which is the programme's sharpest result, is also what makes two of its
eight predictions unfalsifiable.** That tension is real and I do not see a way
around it.
