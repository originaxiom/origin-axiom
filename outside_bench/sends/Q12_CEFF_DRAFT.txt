SUBJECT: c_eff of Zhat for surgeries on the figure-eight — is the ceiling known?

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dear Dr Harichurn,

I am writing with one bounded question, and I expect the answer may simply be a
pointer to your own paper. I have not been able to read arXiv:2508.10087 — the
machine I compute on cannot reach arXiv — so I may be about to describe
something you proved last year. If so, please just say which theorem, and I
will stop.

WHAT I DID

Starting from Gukov and Manolescu's recursion (171)-(172) in section 9.3 of
arXiv:1904.06057, I solved it directly in the series ansatz

    F_{4_1}(x,q) = sum_{k>=1} Xi_k(q) x^{k-1/2},   Xi_1 = 1,

which turns the recursion into a triangular integer system. Two things came out
that I did not expect to be able to get:

  - Zhat_0(S^3_{-1/2}(4_1)) exact to q^39524, where equation (13) prints ten
    coefficients. The whole of Table 10 (r = 2..10) is reproduced, including the
    isolated +q^112 in the r=6 line, and the p/r = -1 assembly is termwise
    identical to Ramanujan's F_0 in equation (175) over the first 3000 terms.

  - The lowest power of q in Xi_k is exactly -floor((k-1)^2/4). With m = 2k-1
    that is -m^2/16 + O(m), so the constant c in the condition (177) is exactly
    -1/16, and the range of applicability is exactly p/r in (-4,0) — page 73
    reaches c = -1/16 experimentally, "by calculating more terms".

THE QUESTION

Measuring the Cardy growth of those series, with a three-parameter fit
log a_n = A sqrt(n) + B log n + C calibrated on eta^{-1} and Rogers-Ramanujan
to better than 0.01%, I get c_eff(p/r = -1) = 1/7 to seven figures, and at the
other two integer slopes 3/10 and 1/2 — which are 6|H_1|/(b1 b2 b3) for the
Seifert manifolds those surgeries give. Across slopes the values fit

    c_eff(Q) = (6/pi^2) max_{y>0} [ y h(y) - y^2/Q ],   Q = |p/r|,
    h(y) = lim_k (1/k) log Xi_k(e^{-y/k}),

to within 0.2% at every slope I can reach, with h(0) = 2 log phi.

The part I actually want to ask about is what that forces. The block widths give
y/4 <= h(y) <= y/4 + 2 log phi, so the maximum diverges above |p/r| = 4 — your
range — and below it the maximum is controlled by the k -> infinity limit of
Xi_k's edge sequence, which stabilises and appears to be

    2 sum_{j>=0} q^{j(j+1)} / (q;q)_infinity,

whose own c_eff is 1. That would make

    sup_{0 < |p/r| < 4} c_eff = 1,

approached at the threshold and never attained. Numerically the values climb
0.143, 0.30, 0.50, 0.78, 0.90, 0.964 as |p/r| goes 1, 2, 3, 3.8, 3.95, 3.99.

So:

  1. Is that ceiling in your paper? A ceiling of exactly 1 on c_eff for
     surgeries on a knot complement feels like the sort of thing that would be
     stated, and I would rather cite it than re-derive it.

  2. Is the ceiling universal? The mechanism is that the edge sequence is
     (something theta-like)/(q;q)_infinity, which smells like Garoufalidis-Le
     stability of the colored Jones tail. If that shape is generic, c_eff < 1
     for every knot and every slope. I cannot test it without F_K for a second
     hyperbolic knot, which I also cannot download.

  3. Is there a reason for 1 — is it the free boson, or a coincidence of this
     knot?

I am happy to send the 39524 coefficients, or the code, to anyone who wants
them; they are exact integers and they reproduce every published coefficient I
have been able to check against. If the answer to (1) is yes, that is the most
useful reply I could get, and I would be glad of it.

With thanks for your time,

Dritero M.
