# PREREGISTRATION — THE ASSUMPTION LEDGER: decompose the far bank, compute what is computable

*Outside bench, memo 235. Sealed and committed BEFORE the certificate is written.*

## The occasion

Four rounds of literature (memo 234 + addenda) produced one structural result: **the field's leading
values programme assumes exactly what we are stuck on.** The G₂-MSSM grants *"conical singularities
at which chiral matter is supported"* and says of compact G₂ manifolds *"there do not **curently**
[sic] exist any concrete ideas about that space either!"*

**We cannot construct that object.** This cell does the thing a small bench can do instead: **turn
the assumption into necessary conditions and compute them.**

The method already worked twice this session, unsystematically — Joyce–Karigiannis needs `b₁ > 0` on
the singular locus and **Y₃ has b₁ = 0**; the charge sum rule needs `b₂ ≥ 2` with the ℤ/3 moving
harmonic forms (memo 233). Neither was planned. This cell systematises it.

## WHAT IS COMPUTED, AND ON WHAT

For each candidate object: build the **3-fold cyclic cover**, **fill** to the branched cover to get
its Q, then compute **b₁(Q)** (A4) and the **deck-ℤ/3 fixed-point structure** (A8).

**Candidates — the record's own, named individually (K4, never "the family"):**
m004 (the object) · **m202, s959** (cell 8's 4/4 witnesses) · **v2873, t12833, t12835** (B1330's
best-case set) · m003 (the sister).

**Preregistered in advance:** several candidates are **multi-cusped**, so "the 3-fold cyclic
branched cover along the knot" may not be defined for them. Where the construction does not apply,
the cell reports **FRAME-DOES-NOT-REACH** and says so — it does **not** substitute a different
construction to keep the row populated.

## THE OUTCOMES, REFUTING ONE FIRST

- **A — a computable condition FAILS for m004 but HOLDS for an alternative.** The route is closed for
  m004 and open for that alternative; the redirect is **earned** rather than asserted.
- **B — it fails for every candidate the frame reaches.** The route is closed for those objects and
  the destination needs a different kind of object. A genuine no-go.
- **C — the conditions HOLD for m004.** No closure: the assumption narrows, and the output is a
  **specification** sharper than the field currently states.

## THE FENCE — the one overreach this cell exists to avoid

**A failed necessary condition closes the route FOR THAT OBJECT, never the route itself.** A4 failing
on Y₃ says **Joyce–Karigiannis cannot resolve that locus**; it says nothing about whether some other
construction can. Any sentence of the form *"the G₂ route is closed"* is forbidden output of this
cell.
