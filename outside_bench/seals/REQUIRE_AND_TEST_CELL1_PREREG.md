## THE LEMMA UNDER TEST

**Lemma (to be proved in the memo, tested here).** Let M be an orientable finite-volume hyperbolic
3-manifold with an orientation-reversing isometry σ, and let H ≤ π₁(M) have finite index with its
**conjugacy class fixed by σ\*** . Then σ lifts to an orientation-reversing isometry of the cover M_H,
so **M_H is amphichiral.**

**Corollary (the non-circular, falsifiable prediction).** A subgroup defined by a **characteristic**
quotient has a σ\*-fixed class **by construction, with no computation**. For a knot complement the
degree-n cyclic cover is the kernel of π₁ ↠ H₁ = ℤ ↠ ℤ/n — characteristic. **Therefore every cyclic
cover of m004 must be amphichiral.**

**Why this is not circular** (#164 — control passing is not instrument working): σ\*-fixedness is
established here **by construction of the quotient**, never by observing that the cover came out
amphichiral. A single chiral cyclic cover refutes the lemma.

## THE PARTS, AND THE TWO OUTCOMES OF EACH

**PART C — the prediction.** Build the cyclic cover of m004 of every degree n = 2 … 30 directly from
the abelianization (a ↦ id, b ↦ the n-cycle; the relator `aaabABBAb` has exponent sums (1, 0), so H₁

`outside_bench/certificates/the_mirror_and_the_bulk.py::amphichiral_det` — an isometry to the mirror
whose cusp maps have determinant +1. `is_isometric_to(mirror)` alone is orientation-blind (B1235's
lesson) and is not used as the decider.

- **C = A** — every cyclic cover tested is amphichiral. The lemma survives its falsifier.
- **C = B** — at least one cyclic cover is **chiral**. **The lemma is REFUTED**, B1163 does not climb,
  and requirement 2 is not a strike against the object.

**PART V — non-vacuity.** The lemma is worthless if no cover of m004 is chiral. Re-derive, do not cite:
enumerate covers of degree ≤ 7 and count chiral ones by the same orientation-aware method.

- **V = A** — chiral covers exist. The lemma has content.
- **V = B** — no chiral cover exists at this depth. **PART C's result is vacuous and must be reported
  as vacuous**, whatever it says.

**PART E — the escape routes.** Enumerate the chirality escape routes the record itself names, and
classify each as **equivariant** (the lemma applies; it cannot supply the bit) or **choice-consuming**
(the lemma does not apply; the choice is the observer's, and the cell says which choice). The routes,

(3) the sibling m202 route (B1302/B1321); (4) the three-record carrier m129 (B1323 F9).

- **E = A** — every named route is choice-consuming. The bit is the observer's on all four.
- **E = B** — at least one named route is equivariant and yields a chiral object. That route is the
