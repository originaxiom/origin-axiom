# PREREGISTRATION — CELL 6: the first alternative object, scored on the same table

*Outside bench, 2026-09-14. Sealed before the scoring runs. Gate 5 untouched.*

## Why this cell exists

Cells 1–5 built a table of **predicates**. Cell 5 then produced something the programme has never had:
**a named alternative object** that passes a predicate m004 fails, while keeping the atom.

**m412** (= `otet05_00001`), from the word `abbC` on the three-record carrier S₁,₂:
vol **5.074708** = 2.5 × vol(m004), 2 cusps, |Sym| = 8, H₁ = ℤ/2 ⊕ ℤ ⊕ ℤ; **all five tetrahedron
shapes have minimal polynomial x² − x + 1** — m004's own shape, the regular ideal tetrahedron; **chiral
by both methods.**

Require-and-test has so far asked *"does m004 have P, and how many objects do?"* This cell asks the
question the whole inversion was built for: **score a specific rival on the same predicates.**

## THE COSTS, ASKED IN THE SAME BREATH AS THE GAINS

A rival that gains a predicate and silently loses the chain's premises is not a rival. Fixed now, so
the accounting cannot be trimmed after the gains are seen:

- **cusps:** m004 has 1, m412 has 2. The chain's C4/A1 carrier story and every one-cusped instrument
  (the D2 index frame, the knot-complement dictionary) assume one cusp. **Recorded as a cost.**
- **knot-ness:** H₁(m004) = ℤ. H₁(m412) = ℤ/2 ⊕ ℤ ⊕ ℤ, so **m412 is not a knot complement**, and Reid's
  uniqueness-of-the-arithmetic-knot does not apply to it.
- **commensurability:** if m412 is commensurable with m004, then by `frontier/B803_commensurability_audit`
  — *"any derivation routing through it is class-level not the object's specific"* — the arithmetic
  face is **inherited, not independent evidence.** Tested, not assumed: same invariant trace field is
  necessary, not sufficient; the volume ratio 2.5 is **not an integer**, so m412 is **not a cover of
  m004**, and that is recorded either way.

## THE TWO OUTCOMES

- **OUTCOME A — m412 STRICTLY DOMINATES m004 on these four predicates** (has everything m004 has, plus
  at least one m004 lacks). Then the programme has, for the first time, a **named rival that is better
  on its own stated requirements**, and the costs above are the price to be argued about.
- **OUTCOME B — m412 does NOT strictly dominate.** It gains P_chir but loses something else, and the
  fork is a trade like every other trade in the record — which is itself the fourth instance of the
  pattern §4 names.

## WHAT THIS CELL MAY NOT CONCLUDE

That m412 should replace m004 — that is the owner's call and a whole chain's worth of work, not a
four-predicate score. That m412 supplies physics: nothing here touches the SM. That the four predicates
are the right four; they are the four this programme has measured base rates for, and no more. No value.
