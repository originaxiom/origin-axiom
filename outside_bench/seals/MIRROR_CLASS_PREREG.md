## 1. Design, and it is B1218's inverted

Deliberately **not** a new method. `open_claim_sweep.py` extracts openness assertions and scans for a
**SETTLED** arc deciding them, IDF-ranked. This extracts **permanence** assertions and scans for an
**OPEN** arc bearing on them. Same units, same IDF, opposite poles. `B1218` is credited in the file.

**The discriminating idea, stated before building:** this record contains *many correct* permanence
claims — nine proved value-negatives, the normalisation no-go, Mostow's scale-freedom. **An
instrument that flagged those would be pure noise.** The discriminator is that a *correct*
permanence claim matches **settled** arcs, while a *drifted* one matches an **OPEN** arc on its own
subject. **Only OPEN-arc matches are flagged.**
