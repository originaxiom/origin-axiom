## C. What this programme will and will not count as an answer

**Counts as an answer to "is m004 the right object?":** a predicate P derived from a requirement, a
verdict for m004 against P, AND a base rate for P over a named census with its population size
printed. All three, or the row is not answered.

**Does NOT count:** that m004 has a property, absent a base rate (`frontier/B282_…`: *"the program's
apparent richness collapses to a single number-theoretic atom"*; and the four NEGATIVE foreign
controls B438, B440, B444, B445, which found 5₂ carries the same structure). Nor a re-framing of
which requirements matter, made after a cell returns.

**The programme's own stop condition:** if the base rates come back high across every row — most
objects satisfy most predicates — then the §A requirements do no selecting work and "which object" is
the wrong question rather than the unasked one. That outcome is to be reported, not absorbed.
