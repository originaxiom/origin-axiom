THE MECHANISM, AND WHAT IT COSTS MEMO 177's UNIVERSALITY HOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   c_edge(K) = c_eff( 1 / Phi_K ),  Phi_K the colored Jones tail;
   so the ceiling on c_eff over all convergent surgeries on K is c_eff(1/Phi_K).
   For 4_1: Phi = (q;q)_inf, ceiling 1.
   Tails of alternating links are NOT all (q;q)_inf -- by Armond-Dasbach the tail is
   that of the reduced all-A state graph, and richer graphs give higher products and
   Andrews-Gordon-type series.  So THE CEILING IS KNOT-BY-KNOT, and memo 177's hope
   that c_edge = 1 universally has no support beyond this one knot.
   c_eff = 6 on this route needs a knot with c_eff(1/Phi_K) >= 6 -- A QUESTION ABOUT
   COLORED JONES TAILS, which is a developed subject, and far cheaper to settle than
   computing F_K for a second hyperbolic knot.  Stated here, not answered.
