 CONTROL C-DISC -- can the instrument tell a manifold from its mirror AT ALL?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    If nothing ever differs, the certificate is not measuring parity.  CS is
    mirror-ODD: CS(M-bar) = -CS(M).  So CS + CS(mirror) = 0 always, and the
    DISCRIMINATING fact is whether CS itself is nonzero -- i.e. whether the
    odd observable has anything to say on that manifold.

    m004   amphichiral=True   CS=+0.000000000  odd observable non-trivial: False
    m412   amphichiral=False  CS=-0.125000000  odd observable non-trivial: True
    5_2    amphichiral=False  CS=-0.153204133  odd observable non-trivial: True
    -> PASS (at least one manifold where the ODD observable is non-trivial)
