------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 -- the PRECISE statement, and what the paraphrase dropped
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Definition (standard).  A JORGENSEN GROUP is a non-elementary Kleinian group
    THAT CAN BE GENERATED BY TWO ELEMENTS for which EQUALITY holds in Jorgensen's
    inequality.  The Jorgensen number is defined for a TWO-GENERATOR subgroup:

        J(G) = inf { |tr^2 A - 4| + |tr[A,B] - 2|  :  G = <A,B> },
        and J(G) >= 1 for every non-elementary Kleinian G   [Jorgensen 1976].

    Callahan (2009), as the literature states it:
        "THE ONLY TORSION-FREE JORGENSEN GROUP IS THE FIGURE-EIGHT KNOT GROUP."

    B1345 paraphrases this as "the only ORIENTABLE hyperbolic 3-MANIFOLD with
    J = 1 is the figure-eight complement", and memo 225 relayed that paraphrase.

    WHAT THE PARAPHRASE DROPS, and it is the load-bearing hypothesis:
      * "TWO-GENERATOR" is part of the DEFINITION of a Jorgensen group.  A
        manifold whose group needs three generators has no Jorgensen number in
        this sense at all -- it is not in the theorem's domain.
      * "ORIENTABLE" is not an added hypothesis: a KLEINIAN group already sits in
        PSL(2,C), which acts by ORIENTATION-PRESERVING isometries.  m000 is
        outside the domain rather than a counterexample -- which is the same
        conclusion B1345 reached, by a different route.
      * "torsion-free" (no torsion ELEMENTS in the group) is what makes it a
        manifold rather than an orbifold; that part of the gloss is fair.
