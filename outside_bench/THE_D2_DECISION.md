## §3 — THE OPTIONS

### ⭐ OPTION A — the trajectory reading: **PAY LEAP-1, with the scope premise written in and armed**
**Read:** the physical clock is a **single realized history**, and the
(1,n) ladder is the record's own distinguished path.
- **Buys:** everything in §1 — CP sign internal, expansion form
  object-side, W1 gets its clock, matter-over-antimatter re-enters.
- **Costs:** LEAP-1's original price (the unproven identification, the
  imported external weight per B721) **plus a new, explicitly stated
  scope premise**: *the Λ-conjugacy is required along a realized history,
  not between variables on the space of closings.*
- **Why it is defensible:** the ladder is preregistered (S6) and the
  reading it rejects is independently dead (S5/S5b), so this is not
  "keep the subset that passed."
- **Refuters to arm:** (a) exhibit a requirement in the AMS construction
  that CS-time be a function on the *space* of solutions rather than
  along one — kills the premise; (b) exhibit a (1,n) rung where
  monotonicity fails beyond the banked box; (c) exhibit a second
  distinguished trajectory on which it fails.

### OPTION B — the variable reading: **DECLINE; AMS-clock XOR HT-clock**
**Read:** two variables conjugate to the same Λ must agree as *variables*.
- **Buys:** maximal conservatism; LEAP-1 never enters the ledger.
- **Costs:** you lose the CP sign's internality, the object-side
  expansion form, and matter-over-antimatter — **and you lose them to a
  condition the data shows was never satisfiable in the first place.**
  By S5b, signed CS is not a function of Vol *by a law banked before the
  run*: this reading is not a stricter standard, it is an **impossible**
  one. Declining on its authority means declining on a test nothing could
  have passed.
- **Honest note:** this is the option the letter of the signed rule most
  resembles ("non-monotone ⟹ refuted"), which is why it must be named —
  but the rule was written expecting a clean binary.

### OPTION C — **HOLD** (neither pay nor decline)
**Read:** the run did not fire either signed branch, so the decision is
not ripe.
- **Buys:** nothing is committed; the ledger stays honest.
- **Costs:** LEAP-1 has now been held twice, and **the measurement says
  no further run will break the tie** — S4 shows the census cannot be
  cleaned by restriction and S5b shows the variable reading is
  structurally dead. **Holding is now a decision to hold forever**, and
  should be recorded as such rather than as "pending."

### OPTION D — **reformulate the condition and re-run**
**Read:** perhaps |CS| was the wrong variable.
- **Status: closed by S5b.** Signed CS fails single-valuedness by B289's
  law; |CS| is the repair and it fails too (S5). There is no third
