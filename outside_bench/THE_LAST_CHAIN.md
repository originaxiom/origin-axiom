## §3 — THE ASYMMETRY THAT MATTERS

**These are not the same kind of result, and the difference is the point.**

- **Thread B strengthens an existing negative.** The value wall now stands
  on a basis containing the object's own complex volume. **A hole was
  closed; nothing was lost.**
- **Thread A removes an asset.** L173 was the programme's one live
  laboratory-facing prediction. It is now shown to carry **no distinctive
  content**. **That is a subtraction from what the programme has, not an
  addition to what it has proved.**

**Interpretive, labeled:** a programme is entitled to count its negatives
as content only when they are *about the world*. Thread B's negative is
about the world — it says the object's regulators do not reach SM values.
Thread A's is about **the programme's own claim**: a prediction it held is
not a prediction. **Those should never be added into the same column.**
