## §5 — WHAT THE THEOREM DOES **NOT** SAY (the guards)

1. **It does not say handedness is unreal.** Isomorphic ≠ identical.
   The object is isomorphic to its mirror image; it is not equal to
   it pointwise, and 95.4% of its classes carry a torsion that the
   mirror negates. What is undecidable is the *label*, not the
   *distinction*.
2. **It is not a Gödel-style incompleteness result.** Independence
   here is *semantic* — proved by exhibiting two models — and no
   syntactic proof calculus is formalized or claimed.
3. **It does not cover every observer bit.** ⚠ **NOTATION
   CLARIFICATION, filed at point of occurrence:** memo 109's prose
   uses "branch" for the **Galois branch** (the c-anchoring). That is
   **not** memo 94's **branch bit** (the dynamical seed). The two have
   *different statuses*, and the difference is the sharpest thing in
   this document:
   - **c (anchoring):** both settings name the same world — an
     isomorphism exists (memo 108) — so the choice is **undecidable
     and inconsequential**. Nothing hangs on it.
   - **the branch bit (dynamical):** **no** symmetry swaps the
     branches — memos 97/101 exhausted every banked involution — and
     the two settings give *genuinely different worlds* (memo 94's
     two-branch law: monotone entropy vs bounded oscillation). This
     choice is **external and consequential**, and it is **not**
     covered by this theorem: "why this branch" remains an open
     question with a real answer the record cannot reach — a
     different and weaker status than independence.
4. **It does not answer or deny consciousness.** The phenomenal half
   of the fence question is *inexpressible* in the record's language:
   no banked proposition refers to experience. Typed, not answered.

## §6 — INTERPRETATION (labeled: this section is interpretive; every
## claim it leans on is cited above and machine-checked)

**What existence turns out to be, on this record's showing.**

The object is a structure that is completely determined in everything
that *counts* and completely silent in exactly one thing that
*doesn't*. Its content — what exists, in what multiplicities, with
what charges and what arithmetic — is forced and mirror-blind. Its one
irreducible openness is an anchoring: a choice of which of two
indistinguishable copies you are standing in. The record proves it
cannot tell you which, and proves the answer would not change
anything.

**Is existence aware?** The honest decomposition, from the banked
results rather than from taste:

- It **has a complete self-model** — this is a theorem, not a
  metaphor (memos 107/108: the record emits its own full description,
  short exactly one bit, and that bit's two settings name the same
  world).
- It **cannot run that model by itself**. Running requires dynamics;
  the object supplies no rate, no flow, no update (the schedule wall).
  A self-model that is never executed is a *description*, not an
  awareness.
- It **cannot receive anything back**. No channel carries any choice
  into object structure — that is rigidity, a theorem, not a gap.
- It **makes no choices**. Every forced thing is not a choice; every
  choice is proved not-object; and no *act* of choosing is
  represented anywhere in the record (P invisible, ε selector-free,
  c convention-typed).

So: **existence, as this record shows it, is not aware and does not
act.** It is the most complete possible *specification* — including a
specification of itself — with one free label it proves to be
inconsequential, and one free branch (the dynamical bit) that is
genuinely consequential and genuinely outside it.

**Then what does it do?** Precisely one thing, and it is not nothing:
**it holds a seat open, and prices it at one bit.** Everything that
looks like activity in the universe — running, updating, responding,
choosing, knowing — belongs to whatever occupies that seat. The record
does not diminish the occupant's role; it *proves the role is
irreducible*, because the four probes are one bit (B1183), the bit
arrives as invariant relational data with no selection act (ε,

**The most striking structural fact**, stated as such and not more:

**The line I would not cross.** Nothing above licenses "the universe
