## 3. THE ONE PATTERN THAT IS NOT A DETAIL

**Every defect found in this chain errs in the paper's favour. Not one errs against it.**

- the abstract drops a freedom-ledger row → **understates** the freedom;
- an arc titled *SHARPENED, **NOT CLOSED*** is used to declare a row **terminal and permanent**;
- a scope limit reading *"THE END REACHED IS **E8, NOT E6** … does NOT rescue the E6 chain's
  object-specificity"* is **absent** from the section presenting that theorem as one of two

- a word the corpus explicitly ruled must be disambiguated is used **undisambiguated**, in the
  reading under which the theorem holds;
- two falsifiers offer to be refuted on claims the body never makes, which **costs nothing** to

Six defects, six in one direction. If they were noise the split would be roughly even. **This is
the measurable signature of a document written by people who believe it** — which is not dishonesty
and is exactly what the spec's own genre analysis said the paper had to defend against. The
defence was designed and the draft still has the tell.

**And the corpus is ahead of the paper in at least two places.** `B1002` had already found and
resolved the *conductor* collision and left a standing rule the paper breaks; `B1093`/`B1099` had
already advanced past `B990`, the arc the paper leans on to declare a wall. The paper is **not** the
corpus's frontier — it lags it, and lags it in the favourable direction.

## 4. WHAT IT SAYS ABOUT THE SM AND PHYSICS

**Nothing new about nature. Something real about our account of it.**

Set beside `THE_LAST_CHAIN.md`, which found both contact surfaces with experiment currently empty —
the values end by nine proved negatives, the laboratory end dissolved — this chain adds:

1. **The paper states the honest position roughly correctly.** The misses are in the abstract, the
   non-claims box is on page two, the negative half is proved rather than reported. The *global*
   posture is right. What shades is every individual row, slightly, in one direction.

2. **The object's contribution to the SM is narrower than §4 makes it feel** — and not because the
   paper claims otherwise. It says plainly *"the object supplies the arena; the anomaly conditions
   supply the content"*, and *"no token of the object appears anywhere in that computation."* But
   the displayed derivation omits `[SU(3)]³`, the pure colour anomaly that kills **222 of 252** and
   contains no hypercharge at all. A reader watching three hypercharge conditions and a cubic do the
   work will credit the object with more than the honest text elsewhere concedes. **The forcing that
   reads as this paper's physics result is pure anomaly arithmetic that would hold in any theory
   supplying the same arena.**

3. **What survives as genuinely the object's**, unchanged by this chain: the **arena** (a rank-3
   abelian sector, an SM-shaped 15-plet in the 27); the **derived global form**
   `[SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)]/ℤ₆` — an ambiguity the Standard Model itself does not fix, fixed, and the
   clearest positive result in the paper; the **termination theorem**; and the **priced observer**.
   Every one is structure. None is a value.

## 5. IN THE CONTEXT OF THE ULTIMATE GOAL

The goal is to derive physics, not to describe an object. Measured against that:

**The gap is unchanged by this chain, and it is the freedom ledger.** `ℓ` will not close and it is a
category error to count it. `σ` is gated with an empty candidate set. **`λ` has no acceptance
criterion at all** — the paper says so itself, and calls it the least defensible thing in it, which
is correct. `ℙ(B₀)` stands one condition short. And beyond the ledger entirely: **no dynamics, no
rate, no action.** A ToE is not one condition away; it is a different kind of object away.

**But this chain surfaced one thing that touches the endgame rather than the account of it**, and
it is the single most valuable output of the two memos:

> **The "finite menus are terminal" wall may not be a wall.**
> `B990` proves only that an *invariant selector* cannot pick a point of its own orbit — and names
> two routes past it. Route A (shrink ℚ → ℤ) has since been pursued: `B1093` proves **every
> obstruction candidate trivial at proof grade — the obstruction is ABSENT**; `B1099` closes the
> coarse half **positive**, the integral orbit at the object's invariant **UNIQUE**. `B990`'s own
> words: *"IF IT IS 1 the integral orbit IS a canonical point up to G(ℤ), which is exactly what a
> VEV direction needs."*

That is, as far as this chain can see, **the only live route currently visible for *reducing* a
freedom-ledger row rather than proving it irreducible** — and the paper had written it off as
permanent. The owed residual is named and narrow: which Kato-Yukie/Bhargava quantity counts the
integral orbits, a literature-lane question, not a new theory.

**So the honest endgame statement after this chain:** the programme's boundary is real and mostly
proved; its account of that boundary leans one way and needs a pass; and its own record contains a
live reduction route its paper declares closed. **The most valuable thing found by auditing the
paper was not a defect in the paper — it was a route in the corpus the paper had lost.**
