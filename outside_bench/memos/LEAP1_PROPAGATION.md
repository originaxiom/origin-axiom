## THE TWO OVERSTATEMENTS, CAUGHT AT THE POINT OF PAYMENT

**T1 — "the CP sign becomes internal" is NOT "the CP sign is forced."**
LEAP-1 identifies the CP sign with the CS-clock sign. But the CS sign,
the CP sign and the chirality label are **already** in the mirror-odd
column, all locked to the **single bit c** (memo 110; banked chain memo
83 / W3). So the payment attaches the cosmological clock to a bit the
record **already carried**. **No bit is added and none is discharged —
the count stays at one discrete mirror bit**, and memo 111's ledger is
untouched because the payment never touches the trace field. *Internal to
the clock*, not *forced*.

**T2 — matter-over-antimatter is DOUBLY conditional, not singly.** The
weld book said it *"follows only WITH LEAP-1, so that consequence
inherits LEAP-1's state."* But LEAP-2 was **retired** — the arrow is not
forced (B124; memo 94's two-branch law; memos 97/101's exhaustion) — and
the ledger carries **ARROW = the branch bit**. So the consequence
inherits **both** LEAP-1's state **and the branch bit's**. Paying LEAP-1
delivers *matter-over-antimatter **given the branch bit***, weaker than
the price sheet implied. Corrected in the weld book at the point of
payment.
