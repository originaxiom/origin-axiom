## THE SEALED CRITERION FIRED **G-EXCESS** — AND THE SEAL WAS WRONG

Golden's values **1, 3, 6, 7** against an ensemble of 15 (mean 2.27,
median 1, max 8). Golden's max sits at the 93rd percentile,
P(other ≥ it) = 0.067. **By the sealed criterion that is G-EXCESS.**

**The criterion was under-specified, and this memo says so rather than
banking the headline it produced.** It required **neither** a
look-elsewhere correction **nor** a control for window size. Both are
needed; both cut against it:

**(a) N-confound.** corr(asymmetry, N) = **+0.629** — the asymmetry
**grows with window size** (asym ≈ −0.43 + 0.00237·N), and golden's large
values sit at its **largest** windows. Its values are monotone in N:
1 → 3 → 6 → 7 across 610 → 987 → 1597 → 2584. N-adjusted, golden's best
residual gives P = 0.067 — **still just the 1/15 resolution floor**.

**(b) Look-elsewhere.** Golden contributed **4** windows and the
**maximum** was quoted. Raw P = 0.067; **Šidák-corrected over 4 draws =
0.241. Not significant.**

**(c) The single most damaging fact.** The ensemble maximum is **8, from
log 2 at N = 1649** — **a non-metallic slope, at a smaller window than
golden's best (7)**. **A slope with no golden structure at all produces a
larger asymmetry.**
