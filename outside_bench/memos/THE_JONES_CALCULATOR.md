## 1. Derived, not recalled — and two wrong formulas caught by controls

The braiding on `V_N ⊗ V_N` is **not transcribed**. It is built by decomposing the tensor square
into `U_q(sl₂)` summands — highest-weight vectors killed by `Δ(E) = E⊗1 + K⊗E`, generated by
`Δ(F) = F⊗K⁻¹ + 1⊗F` — and assigning each summand the ribbon eigenvalue
`(−1)^J q^{C_J − 2C_j}`, `C = j(j+1)`.

**Two attempts failed and the controls caught both, not inspection:**

* A first attempt did transcribe the universal `R`-matrix from memory. Its coefficient
  `(q−q⁻¹)ⁿ/[n]!` **is not a Laurent polynomial**, which showed up as a non-terminating exact
  division. Wrong formula.
* A second had the ribbon exponent **halved** (`C_J/2 − C_j`). That produced non-Laurent matrix
  entries and a failing braid relation. The Laurent condition on the `N = 2` weight-0 block
  forces the eigenvalue ratio `−q²`, not `−q`, which is what fixed it.

## 3. A braid word for `5₂`, identified by two independent invariants

> **`σ₁² σ₂² σ₁ σ₂⁻¹` on three strands.**

* Its permutation is a 3-cycle, so the closure is a **knot**.
* Jones polynomial shape `(−1, 1, −1, 2, −1, 1)` = `V(5₂)`.
* **Alexander polynomial via Melvin–Morton–Rozansky**, which is the independent check:
  writing `J_n(e^ℏ) = Σ_m p_m(n)ℏ^m`, the leading coefficient `c_{m,m}` of `p_m` gives the Taylor
  series of `1/Δ_K(e^u)`. The `ℏ²` coefficient alone separates every candidate:

| knot | `Δ` | `c₂₂` | computed here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `3₁` | `t − 1 + 1/t` | −1 | **−1** ✅ |
| `4₁` | `−t + 3 − 1/t` | +1 | **+1** ✅ |
| `5₁` | `t²−t+1−1/t+1/t²` | −3 | — |
| **`5₂`** | **`2t − 3 + 2/t`** | **−2** | **−2** ✅ |

Each quadratic fit `p₂(n) = an²+bn+c` is verified on a fourth point it was not fitted to.

**`5₂` is chiral and hyperbolic — exactly the missing case.**

**A dead end recorded so nobody repeats it:** SnapPy cannot identify these closures. Its
`braid[...]` constructor returns the closure **union the braid axis** — `braid[1,1,1]` has two
cusps — so its volumes do not identify the closure. Checked, and it cost a search that returned
zero hits before the reason was found.

## 4. What this does and does not deliver

**Delivers:** an exact colored Jones calculator for any braid word and any colour, validated
against two knots the paper itself prints, plus a verified braid word for `5₂`.

**Does not deliver, yet:** `F_{5₂}` itself. GM's route from `J_n` to `F_K` (page 6, eq (7);
page 7) is: extract Rozansky's loop expansion `J_n(e^ℏ) = 1/Δ_K(x) + P₁(x)/Δ³ ℏ + …` with
`x = q^n`, read off the coefficient of each power of `x`, and resum each into a `q`-series.
The first half of that — reorganising `Σ_m p_m(n)ℏ^m` into `Σ_k L_k(u)ℏ^k` by collecting
`c_{k+j,j}u^j` — is implemented and its `k = 0` output is what §3's Alexander check uses. The
resummation step is **running and not yet reported**; nothing about `5₂`'s blocks, its `c`, or
its `c_edge` is claimed here.
