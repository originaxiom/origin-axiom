## 1. The definition, from three independent returns

A **trialitarian algebra** over `F` is a **4-tuple `(E, L, σ, α)`**: `L` a **cubic étale
`F`-algebra**, `(E,σ)` a central simple `L`-algebra of **degree 8** with orthogonal involution, and
`α` an isomorphism from the Clifford algebra `C(E,σ)` to a twisted scalar extension of `E`.
(KMRT §43; restated in Barry–Tignol and in Dolphin–Quéguiner-Mathieu.)

A **trialitarian triple** is three central simple degree-8 algebras with orthogonal involution over
`F` such that **the Clifford algebra of one is isomorphic to the direct product of the other two**.

## 3. **THE CORRECTION TO THE RECORD: the object's form is ⁶D₄, not ³D₄**

B1077's floor-lift reads: *"`ℚ(√77)` can only enter through the **twisted ³D₄** form, where the
cubic étale algebra is a field,"* and B882's conjecture was restated as *"the dressed datum is the
**³D₄** twist of the split triple by `K`."*

**That label is wrong for `K`.** Two independent search returns state the distinction:

> *"The **³D₄** and **⁶D₄** are the two quasi-split triality `D₄` groups, where **³D₄ splits over a
> cyclic extension of degree 3** and **⁶D₄ over a Galois extension of degree 6 with Galois group
> `S₃`**."*

**`K = ℚ[x]/(x³−12x−5)` has `Gal = S₃` and discriminant 6237**, resolvent `ℚ(√77)` — **non-trivial,
hence non-cyclic**. A cubic étale algebra with a non-trivial discriminant is an `S₃`-algebra, so the
form it twists is **⁶D₄**.

**Why this is not pedantry.** Springer's construction, as the literature states it, builds a
**cyclic composition** from a symmetric composition `Σ` and a cubic étale `L` **together with an
`F`-automorphism `ρ` of order 3**. An `S₃` cubic field **has no automorphism of order 3 over `F`**.
So the object's datum, if its cubic is `K`, is **not** a cyclic composition — it is the general
**twisted composition** over an arbitrary cubic étale algebra. **The two cases are treated by
different theorems, and the record has been aiming at the wrong one.**

## 4. And the door's shape changes: it is a CONSTRUCTION question, not a classification question

**Knus–Tignol, *Triality and Algebraic Groups of Type ³D₄* (arXiv 1409.1718, Doc. Math. 2015):**

> *"outer automorphisms of order 3 exist **if and only if the algebra is the endomorphism algebra of
> an induced cyclic composition**; their conjugacy classes are in one-to-one correspondence with
> **isomorphism classes of symmetric compositions** from which the induced cyclic composition
> stems."*

**Springer's construction (1963 Göttingen notes), as restated in the current literature:** given a
symmetric composition `Σ = (S, n, ⋆)` over `F` and a cubic étale `L` with `ρ` of order 3, one builds
the cyclic composition `Σ ⊗ (L, ρ)`; a trialitarian algebra arises as `End` of a cyclic composition.

**And the 8-dimensional symmetric compositions are CLASSIFIED:** *"In dimension 8, apart from the
**para-Hurwitz** algebras, there is a new family of symmetric composition algebras termed **Okubo**
algebras"* — three 8-dimensional division composition algebras in total: octonions, para-octonions,
Okubonions. **The Okubo algebra is built on the trace-zero 3×3 matrices and its construction uses a
primitive cube root of unity** — i.e. `ℚ(ω) = ℚ(√−3)`, **the being face**. Recorded as a
co-occurrence, **not** claimed: this bench has killed one small-structure coincidence already
(B1223) and typed another by kind (memo 200).

**So B882's door is not waiting on an obstruction theorem.** The classification says the trialitarian
data is *equivalent to* a composition-algebra datum. The question becomes concrete and constructive:

> **Does the object's charge data define a symmetric composition (equivalently a twisted composition
> over its cubic), and is the cubic that construction attaches `K`?**

That is a **computation**, not a citation — and the 8-dimensional symmetric compositions being a
short classified list (para-octonion / Okubo) makes it a **finite check**, not an open search.
