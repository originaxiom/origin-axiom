# THE BIT'S LEDGER — when and how the one bit is spent: at the embedding, once, and never again
## (outside bench memo 111, 2026-08-28; certificate `certificates/bit_ledger.py`, GREEN; the owner's "when and how does it use it, and the implications?")

Memo 107 priced occupation at one bit and memo 109 proved its setting
undecidable; neither said **when** the bit is spent or **how many
times**. "When" cannot have a temporal answer (the object supplies no
time), so the question is structural — and in that form it is
decidable.

- **L1 — the bit-free floor.** The presentation level costs nothing:
  the alphabet, the grammar and the relator `abaBAbabAB` are
  integer/combinatorial data (the relator re-derived as a word,
  evaluating to ±I). Stating the record requires no field, no
  embedding, **no bit**.
- **L2 — THE CONSUMPTION POINT.** The trace field is the *abstract*
  field ℚ[X]/(X²−X+1) — free to state. The bit is spent at exactly one
  step: **choosing an embedding into ℂ**, i.e. deciding which complex
  root (½ ± i√3/2) the symbol ω denotes. The two roots are exchanged
  by conjugation and Gal = ℤ/2 acts **simply transitively** on the
  embeddings — one bit, at the passage *abstract record → realized
  record*, and there is exactly one such passage.
- **L3 — SPENT ONCE, NEVER AGAIN (the theorem).** All 13,120 reduced
  words to length 8 have traces in **ℤ[ω]** — zero non-integral, 180
  distinct values — and the traces **generate exactly ℤ[ω]**
  (tr(a) − tr(ab) = ω). **The field never grows with depth**, so no
  deeper structure — no longer word, no finer invariant, no later
  epoch — ever demands a second irrationality, hence never a second
  Galois choice.
- **L4 — downstream determinism.** With the embedding fixed, every
  quantity is a function of the traces: at both embeddings the systole
  trace and κ have equal real parts and opposite imaginary parts, and
  no third option exists.

**THE ANSWER.** *When:* not at a moment — the bit is presupposed by
any realized description, logically prior to the first tick, spent
whenever the abstract record is **coordinatized**, once per
description rather than once per event. *How:* by choosing which
complex root ω names — a single act that rigidly fixes every torsion
sign, the CS sign, the CP sign and the chirality label (memo 110's
odd column). *How many times:* **exactly once, ever** — the
observer's discrete cost does not accumulate with the depth or the
age of what is described.

**Implications (interpretive, labeled):** emergence here is not a
process that spends choices as it unfolds; the whole tower is fixed
by the forced content plus one embedding, chosen once and never
revisited. A universe of this shape does not grow more contingent the
longer it runs, and an observer does not pay again for looking
deeper. **Fence:** derived quantities may carry uniform normalization
conventions (the branch of log, a geodesic's orientation); those are
applied identically to every class and are not additional Galois
freedoms — the field-theoretic choice is exactly one, which is what
this cell counts. Gate 5 untouched.
