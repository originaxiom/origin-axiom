## The campaign, three phases

### Phase 1 — THE CONTENT LEDGER (bounded, no new mathematics)

Build the eleven-row table above as a **banked artifact** with, for each row:
the exact claim, its arc, its grade (DERIVED / DERIVED-WITH-INPUT / CONTESTED /
EXTERNAL / ABSENT), and **what it is conditional on**.

*Why first:* the programme has produced this content across ~40 arcs and has no
single place a reader can check it. `THE_SM_VERDICT.md` exists but **shipped
omitting eleven of twelve cascade arcs** (B988's own finding). **This is the
document that should have existed before any crossing was attempted.**

### Phase 2 — RESOLVE THE TWO CONTRADICTIONS

**2a. The generation count.** B897 vs B414. Establish whether they are about the
same object: B897 is a **decomposition** statement (three 9-blocks under
(su(3)_c, su(3)′, su(2)′)); B414 is a **privilege** statement (does the ℤ/3
reading survive a base-rate test?). **They may both be right about different
things** — the same shape as the L114 rank dispute, which dissolved once the two
formalisms were separated. If so, the resolution is free and the ledger stops
contradicting itself on three generations.

**2b. Rank 6 vs rank 4.** Does B864's anomaly argument remove the *need* for
rank reduction, or only identify hypercharge inside a structure that still needs
it? An anomalous *gauge* symmetry is an inconsistency, not a resolution — so I
believe the latter and am not confident. **Bounded reading + one computation.**

### Phase 3 — THE TWO FALSIFICATION RISKS, stated as risks

**3a. The twelve exotics.** A framework predicting twelve unobserved states per
generation must either make them heavy or be refuted. The mechanism exists; the
**splitting is external**. State plainly whether that is a prediction, a
parameter, or a problem.

**3b. Charge quantisation, claimed or not.** If ℤ₆ *is* charge quantisation,
that is a major derived content fact sitting unclaimed. If it is not, say why —
because the ambiguity itself is a defect in the ledger.
