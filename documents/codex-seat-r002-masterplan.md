## VI. The proof loop for every cell

Every campaign cell uses the same seven-state loop.

1. **Preregister.** State the typed question, exact dependencies and source hashes, permitted
   verdict grammar, cheapest falsifier, physical scope, and compute/credit cap.
2. **Scout.** Inventory files, literature identifiers, manifests, and the smallest reproducer.
   Scouting may locate a theorem; it may not silently broaden it.
3. **Build.** Produce an exact proof/certificate, a bounded empirical result, or a constructive
   counterexample. Prefer rational/cyclotomic or modular certificates over floating evidence.
4. **Verify independently.** A different seat reruns the artifact and attacks convention,
   completeness, equivariance, and hidden-input assumptions.
5. **Adjudicate.** Assign exactly one narrow status: `PROVED`, `REFUTED`, `CONDITIONAL`,
   `EXTERNAL_BLOCKER`, or `EMPIRICAL`. A green test does not upgrade the prose.
6. **Generate children and bank.** Register every new question exposed by the result, update the
   dependency graph and scope clauses, and publish a self-contained relay with hashes and rerun
   instructions.
7. **Recompute the cut.** Continue only on leaves that can change a trunk gate. Bank negative
   results immediately so a refuted family is never repeatedly explored under new names.

The loop repeats until there is no unregistered child and every registered leaf has a proof,
counterexample, explicit conditional dependency, empirical bound, or named external blocker.

## VIII. Anti-overclaim rules

1. **No target-selected derivation.** “Choose the SM-compatible row” is a phenomenological filter,
   not an origin-derived selector.
2. **No representation-to-particle collapse.** A doublet, parity, spin-2 summand, or dimension
   match is not a particle until a physical functor, kinetic operator, and state space are given.
3. **No index-to-spectrum collapse.** Net chirality does not prove the full massless spectrum;
   cohomology does not prove masses or couplings.
4. **No finite scan-to-theorem upgrade.** Numerical stability is `EMPIRICAL` unless a bound proves
   the asserted universal statement.
5. **No vanishing-class-to-explicit-field upgrade.** Existence of a differential trivialization
   is not the construction and normalization of a physical connection.
6. **No passed-certificate scope inflation.** The theorem is the proposition actually checked,
   with its conventions and dependencies.
7. **No hidden free bits.** Every branch choice, embedding, metric, scale, phase, threshold, and
   boundary condition belongs in the input ledger until uniquely derived.

## IX. Completion criteria

There are two distinct terminal statements.

**Audit closure** is reached when every registered question and every child generated on the way is
classified with a reproducible proof object or a precise external blocker, the documents agree,
and no live branch contains an unintegrated material claim. This means the mathematics has been
exhausted relative to the stated inputs; it does not imply a TOE.

**Parameter-free physical closure** is reached only if C0--C7 are closed, the final action and
vacuum contain no hidden fitted choices, the Standard Model spectrum and parameters follow after
canonical normalization and RG matching, gravity is present, and a frozen calculation succeeds on
nontrivial held-out observables. If any input remains conditional, the final statement must remain
conditional.

The campaign therefore does not stop because a synthesis document says “closed.” It stops only
when the dependency graph has no unaccounted leaves. Conversely, it must honestly report a
terminal obstruction when exact mathematics rules out a whole route. That is how this programme
can become either a proof of the claimed theory or a proof of exactly why the current axioms do not
yet determine one.
