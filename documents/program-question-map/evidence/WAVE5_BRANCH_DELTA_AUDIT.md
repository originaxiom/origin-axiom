## Hostile fences retained

- Two obstructions with the same slogan are not the same mathematical wall without a typed map.
- A regulator codomain containing volume is not a constructed finite-to-archimedean comparison.
- Even a constructed arithmetic comparison would not select the heterotic functor; Seam A is an
  operational wall for the physics chain.
- An exact Ruelle Euler-product identity is not analytic continuation, a torsion value, a graviton
  determinant or an action.
- Aggregate anomaly cancellation in a chosen branching is not an object-selected gauge theory.
- A carrier transition called a family is not a chiral zero mode or a generation index.
- Kinematic symmetry preservation by a direction is not a dynamically selected VEV.
- A finite global resultant table is not an all-prime, all-order local transport theorem.
- A minimal representation inside a declared category is not a proof that the object selected the
  category, the representation or its physical interpretation.
- A field degree does not bound representation multiplicity, and an invariant cannot select one
  point from a free transitive orbit.
- Three-manifold CS/Mostow data do not fix the phase of an unrelated Calabi--Yau form without a
  typed map.
