# Variation does not demand vanishing of every product

## Three distinct equations

R60's retained action gives, with outward Stokes orientation,

    delta W = 2c integral tr(delta C wedge F_C)
              - c integral_boundary tr(C wedge delta C).

Its field-space two-form is proportional to the integrated invariant
pairing of two boundary variations. Isotropy of a proposed tangent
space is a necessary variational condition. In a linear chart through
zero it also kills the displayed boundary one-form. Around a nonzero
reference connection, cancellation of the constant boundary one-form
can require a reference-dependent boundary functional; that functional
is extra input and is not derived here.

On boundary cohomology, invariant trace pairs each charged coefficient
with its linear dual. The subspace L plus its full cup annihilator is
therefore half-dimensional and isotropic in the paired space. The
actual calculation verifies this statement for the recorded silver
spaces. It does NOT construct smooth allowed traces, nonlinear
extensions, a complete variational principle or an elliptic domain.
The charged cohomology block is only part of the full parent.

Infinitesimal gauge preservation is a DIFFERENT requirement:

    delta_epsilon a = D0 epsilon + [a,epsilon].

For a boundary-parallel parameter, the first term vanishes. The second
must preserve the allowed trace space. This restricts the allowed gauge
parameters; it does not imply that every boundary-parallel charged
parameter must be allowed. The program computes the simultaneous kernel
for the six retained charged coefficient maps. General gauge parameters,
neutral channels, reality, boundary currents and supersymmetry cannot
be inferred from that kernel alone. In particular it is not automatically
a Lie algebra. The global constant unbroken gauge factor acts on the
gauge indices, while the selected spaces act on structure coefficients;
the two tensor-factor actions commute. That fact does not establish
invariance under arbitrary boundary-dependent full-parent transformations.

Finally, integrability of a closed linear mode a into a flat family
C0+t a+t^2 b+... requires

    D0 b + a wedge a = 0

at second order. A nonzero cohomology class of the quadratic term can
obstruct that flat family. It does not by itself exclude an off-shell
fluctuation or prove a linear mass. Auxiliary fields and the nonlinear
potential can encode interactions of such modes. The actual fermion
dictionary and analytic domain must decide their particle interpretation.
Thus imposing A2=0 with closure of every L times L product is an extra
trace-algebra assumption, not a consequence of boundary isotropy alone.

## A smooth nonabelian opposite control

Take sl2 matrices H=diag(1,-1), E=E12, F=E21 and the boundary space

    L = span(H dx, E dy, F dy).

With the bilinear trace pairing it has dimension three in a symplectic
space of dimension six. Its pairing vanishes because tr(H E)=tr(H F)=0
and like form directions wedge to zero. However [H,E]=2E is nonzero.
The boundary-parallel gauge parameters preserving L are precisely the
Cartan span H: requiring [epsilon,H] dx in L forces both root
coefficients of epsilon to vanish. This is a comparator with reduced
boundary gauge freedom, not a full sl2-invariant boundary condition.

On a smooth unit-volume compact product slab use constant complex
connection components C_x=x H, C_y=y E, C_r=0 with real x,y. The
holomorphic boundary variation vanishes within the stated space, but
F_xy=2xy E. In the matrix residual convention used in R62 the constant
moment term is I=[C_x,C_x^dagger]+[C_y,C_y^dagger]=y^2 H. Hence

    2 ||F_xy||^2 + ||I||^2/2 = 8 x^2 y^2 + y^4.

It is finite, quartic, and has zero Hessian at the origin. Along x=y=t
it is 9t^4. Nonzero curvature means those nonzero points are not flat
vacua; it does not make their smooth fluctuations inadmissible. This
control does not remove the conical divergence of R62: a smooth compact
slab and a zero-radius cone have different measures and domains.

## Scope toward the physical goal

The preceding m136 dimension obstruction remains correct WITH its two
allowed invariant channels. Its physical application must first derive
whether those channels are in the gauge and fermion domains. Removing
them is not free: different endpoint cochain prescriptions already give
different full spectra. Conversely the vanishing paired holomorphic
form is a useful necessary positive, not physical admission of the
chosen global complement. Derive the actual full boundary law next,
including the real action, moment map, auxiliary and fermion variations.

Sources for the supplied physical setting are
[Pantev and Wijnholt](https://arxiv.org/html/0905.1968v1), section 2.3,
and [Braun et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.06072v2), Appendix B.1.
The distinctions, actual cohomology application and comparator above
are the present authored argument; no general physical boundary theorem
is attributed to those passages.
