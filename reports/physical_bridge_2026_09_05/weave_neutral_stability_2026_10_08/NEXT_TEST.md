Post-run proposal, UNEXECUTED and outside the preceding seal.

The neutral-field packet has separated the first flux from higher
fluxes. It closes this finite-amplitude stabilization mechanism at
abs(n)=1, not every magnetic phase. The zero-flux minimum is a useful
positive control, but its charged index stays zero.

The next bounded candidate is n=2 on the SAME action, surface, odd
extending spin line and domain, with the actual global psi. Integer
two is a test case, not a generated selection or preferred physical
value. No amplitude may be chosen merely to fit the desired spectrum.

1. Refresh branch heads and review new relays before claiming novelty.
   Recover existing Yang-Mills/Higgs, neutral-field, non-BPS, parabolic

## Decide the discrete problem without a sampling exclusion

4. Seek an analytic factorization, comparison or coercive bound for the
   complete operator. Test whether a deformation-complex/index argument
   forces residual negative modes before pursuing a numerical optimum.
   Conversely, positivity on a finite grid or on the six old gauge
   roots alone does not prove stability.
5. If numerical core work is needed, specify the actual elliptic-curve
   uniformization, spin metric, transition data and truncation. Bound
   the discarded tail uniformly and control discretization errors.
   The trial space supplies upper bounds; claiming nonnegativity
   additionally needs a certified lower bound covering EVERY sector.
6. Large abs(c) is not automatically a cure. The profile tends to zero
   down the cusp, and mixed first-order coefficients or modes localized
   in its transition region can matter. Analyze those rather than
   replacing psi by its compact-core minimum.

negative mode or a proved all-amplitude obstruction with its hypotheses.

nonlinear and quantum stability remain separate even after a positive.
