In the structure factor the adjoint decomposes as End0(E),24 trivial
lines, ten copies of E and E*, and five copies of exterior2 E and its

exclude gauge equivalence even under the complex E8 group, and hence

by the defining-five detector. It is not proof that equal adjoint

by the paired roster: this detector does not define handedness or charge.
