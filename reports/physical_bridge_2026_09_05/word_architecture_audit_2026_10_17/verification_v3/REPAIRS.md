## The register law with its action hypotheses

Let rho and rho' be composition-preserving actions of the same
algebraically ordered word monoid in Aut(F2), with equal homology for
each word. Write I_g(x)=g x g^-1. The trivial centre and the Nielsen
kernel theorem give a unique h(w) with rho'(w)=I_h(w) rho(w).

Algebraic uv acts by rho(u)rho(v), so v acts first. Multiplication gives

    rho'(u)rho'(v)
      = I_h(u) rho(u) I_h(v) rho(v)
      = I_(h(u) rho(u)(h(v))) rho(u)rho(v).

Uniqueness therefore gives h(uv)=h(u) rho(u)(h(v)). Equal homology
without the action hypotheses does not imply this identity.

For chronological strings uv, u is performed first and v second.
The evaluation reverses multiplication: rho(uv)=rho(v)rho(u), and
similarly for rho'. The same calculation, in that order, gives

    h(uv)=h(v) rho(v)(h(u)).

These are two descriptions of the same composition mechanism, not
competing physical laws. The moving-frame endpoint formula already
uses chronological steps and remains

    T'_n ... T'_1 = I_g(n) (T_n ... T_1) I_g(0)^-1.

The frozen LIFT_FAMILY_AND_REGISTER section omitted the action hypothesis
and the distinction between these word orders. This dated correction

## Projective coordinate convention

gamma=1-beta. If I_gamma=[l,u], then I_beta=[1-u,1-l]. Length and nesting
