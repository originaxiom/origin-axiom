- Free-group capitals denote inverses; I_g(x)=g x g^-1. Function f g
  means f after g. L(a)=a,L(b)=ab and R(a)=ab,R(b)=b fix aBAb.

  performs u then v and acts by rho(v)rho(u). Both compared assignments

  with equal homology. The corresponding relative-register laws are

  is named. The entry-gcd is positive; the primitive-realizer law requires
  determinant-one integral matrices and kappa!=2. Degeneracies are separate.

- No floating-point tolerance, fitted constant, random seed or numerical
