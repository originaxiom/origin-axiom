## 2. Hand check of the global shifted-chain reduction

For an N-dimensional increasing-weight raising matrix with diagonal t
and subdiagonal c_i, eliminating the old c_i pivots leaves

    f_N(t) = (-1)^(N-1) t^N / (c_0 ... c_(N-2)).

One can see the recurrence directly. After the first pivot the retained
lowest target has coefficient -t^2/c_0 at the next source; the next pivot
changes this to +t^3/(c_0 c_1). Each step multiplies by -t/c_i. The
highest source and lowest target therefore have the claimed coefficient.
For N=1 no pivot is used and the answer is t. With the declared normalized
raising coefficients the product is (N-1)!/(2 ell)^(N-1). This agrees
with the source's sign, exponent and normalization in its holomorphic
chain convention.

The important domain point survives scrutiny. A pivot maps a source
line S^n L to the identical target line S^n L; the actual source metrics
make its inverse bounded. Each new elementary off-diagonal operation
instead multiplies by a positive power of psi, sending S^n L to
S^(n+r) L. In the actual pointwise metrics its norm is |psi|^r, which
is bounded and decays at the cusp for r>0. The source neutral-section
proof supplies bounded unit-frame derivatives as well. The inverse
elementary operation changes its sign, rather than using psi^-1.

These operations are holomorphic bundle maps. They commute with the
covariant Dolbeault differential, preserve compact-support cores and
intertwine the full total differential. Boundedness in the physical
norms in every degree then extends that intertwining to the same minimal
complete graph domains. The original densities give only fixed positive
slot factors: for a line S^n L its section density is proportional to
Omega^(2-n), and its form density to Omega^(-n). This checks the relevant
metric shift rather than identifying bare coordinate coefficients.

The result is conditional on the inherited actual global lines and
bounded old pivots. Within those hypotheses I found no hidden division
by a vanishing section, deleted cusp sector or new boundary condition.
Bounds may depend on the fixed finite amplitude and fixed nonzero a,b;
the proof does not claim uniformity as those coordinates approach an
excluded endpoint or infinity.

For two tensor directions the source/target transformations are

    G0 = Ra^-1 tensor Rb^-1,

where Da=La Ma Ra and Db=Lb Mb Rb. Direct multiplication gives

Thus the Koszul minus sign and degree-two transport are correct. Every
other scalar tensor summand contains a nonzero old pivot and is boundedly

compact extensions in each pair: b(-2)=b(-1) and b(3)=b(4). The global
