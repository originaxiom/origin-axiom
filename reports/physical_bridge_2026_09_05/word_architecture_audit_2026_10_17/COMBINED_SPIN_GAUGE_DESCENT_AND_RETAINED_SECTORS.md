## 1. The standalone obstruction is not the combined problem

The preceding local test concerns an action on a square root S alone,
compatible with S^2=K_Y and the canonical action on K_Y. At a branch
point its stabilizer lift has square -1. It would be incorrect to
convert that result into an exclusion of charged fields: their actual
bundle may combine the spin root with the gauge root.

Keep precisely the preceding holomorphic gauge doublet

    E=S^(-1) direct-sum S, with S^2=K_Y.

For a matter coefficient carrying this doublet AND the same spin root,
its positive complex spin bundle is

    V+=S tensor E = O_Y direct-sum K_Y.             (1)

This identity uses the specified square map, not a numerical match or
a change in field degree. Both summands have canonical actions under
every holomorphic automorphism of Y. Their pullback/composition law
is exact. Consequently the full Q8 deck action on this combined bundle
can be defined without selecting separate honest actions on S and E.
This is a construction of a combined action, not a proof that a given
pre-existing independent gauge action agrees with it.

More concretely, in local spin frames write a root transition s_ij;
the gauge transition in this split model is diag(s_ij^(-1),s_ij).
The tensor transition is diag(1,s_ij^2), exactly that of O plus K.
Changing the spin root by a two-torsion line T changes both correlated
factors, and T^2=O cancels in (1). The chosen correlation matters:
replacing the gauge E by an unrelated supplied doublet would not
permit this cancellation.

At the branch stabilizer, write the spin action as t with t^2=-1.
The tensor action is diag(1,t^2)=diag(1,-1), which squares to I.
Thus the order-four local spin and gauge lifts can give an honest
order-two action on this combined charged field. The earlier adjoint
descent is not the only possible positive descent statement.
