# What the four wording proposal establishes

October 18, 2026. Analytical intake, not a new scientific run or an
amendment to GENESIS. This assesses the owner's pasted proposal that
independently allowing both elementary word orders pays the surface,
boundary-domain and flat-line inputs. It contributes a useful extension
question, but does not establish those advertised physical payoffs.

## The genuine extension

Use function composition with the rightmost map acting first and
I_h(x)=h x h^-1. The four maps in the proposal obey

    Lbar = I_(a^-1) L,       Rbar = I_b R.

Allowing them independently, together with inverses, generates I_a and
I_b as well as the original ruled group G=<L,R>. Since G maps onto
SL(2,Z), and the kernel of Aut(F2)->GL(2,Z) is Inn(F2), this yields
Aut^+(F2). This is the conditional equality already proved in
[LIFT_FAMILY_AND_REGISTER](LIFT_FAMILY_AND_REGISTER.md). The later
[review repair](verification_v3/REPAIRS.md) supplies the companion
register law's missing action hypotheses and separates chronological
from algebraic register order.

The enlargement is not equivalent to choosing a different fixed pair
once. The four fixed-pair choices are related by a common inner change
of frame, with all based data transported. Independent choices at each
step generate additional operations. The proposal is right to distinguish
these questions.

Three qualifications matter:

- Without inverse operations, the four positive moves generate the
  special Sturmian monoid, not the entire automorphism group.
- B3 is not SL(2,Z). The ruled word group has the nontrivial cyclic
  kernel generated by I_c, with c=a b^-1 a^-1 b.
- The natural factors G and Inn(F2) intersect in <I_c>. Their product
  is not the asserted semidirect product with these embedded factors.
  The existing derivation gives (F2 semidirect G) divided by the shared
  cyclic kernel {(c^n,I_(c^-n))}. A classical description is B4/Z(B4).

Kassel and Reutenauer establish the B4 quotient and the presentation of
the positive monoid in Corollary 1.9 and section 2 of
[Sturmian morphisms, the braid group B4, Christoffel words and bases of F2](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0507219).
The monoid has relations: it is not a free four-way tree of distinct
outputs. Nor does abelianizing the letter group make the matrices L and
R commute. Their products still retain order; the lost information is
the based lift, not all ordering information.

## A precise geometric connection

There is a stronger theorem here than matching Euler characteristics:

    Aut^+(F2) = PMod(S_1,2), up to the natural isomorphism.

Here S_1,2 is a torus with two individually marked points, and PMod is
its orientation-preserving pure mapping class group. Bridson and Wade,
[Commensurations of Aut(F_N) and its Torelli subgroup](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13437),
Proposition 6.1, give the isomorphism and its proof by explicit Nielsen
maps. The full mapping class group allowing interchange of the marks is
larger. A surface S_1,2 and its moduli space M_1,2 are different objects;
the latter has complex dimension two.

Consequently, if the enlarged operation group is justified by the
foundation, the twice-marked-torus interpretation has a precise
topological basis. That is a worthwhile positive. It is not, by itself,
a derivation of a particular complex structure, bundle, spin lift,
boundary operator or physical configuration space. An orbifold
classifying-space interpretation must retain the markings and isotropy;
it is not literal equality with an arbitrary coarse moduli space.

GENESIS v1.48 adopts inverse moves at GM5b as a reading, not a derivation
from PF1. GM1, GM2 and the faithful surface realization at GM4 retain
premises. Defining a free group first can streamline that presentation,
but does not prove rank two, freeness, reversibility, the elementary
updates or the physical carrier from non-cancellation alone. F2 by itself
is also the fundamental group of a pair of pants and of a two-loop graph;
peripheral and geometric data distinguish their uses. An axiom count
cannot be reduced by hiding these commitments inside the word free.

## Slope and intercept are useful but not complex moduli

The combinatorial connection is real. General Sturmian words admit
four-morphism directive descriptions in which continued-fraction data
control slope and admissible Ostrowski digits encode intercept. The
conditions on digits and convergence are substantive; they are not
arbitrary independent bits. See sections 2.4 and 3.2 of
[Automata on S-Adic Words](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol374-icalp2026/html/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.165/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.165.html).

But a real cutting slope is not a complex modulus tau, and a real cutting
intercept is not automatically a point w of the complex Jacobian. A
construction relating these types must be supplied or derived. Finite
hyperbolic return words give quadratic irrational fixed directions;
allowing arbitrary admissible infinite directives is a different limit
construction. Thus the claim that finite hyperbolic words sweep all
slopes, or that two real parameters thereby fill M_1,2, is not established.
The zero-intercept description of the characteristic Fibonacci word
also depends on the chosen cutting convention.

## Why the index input is not yet paid

B1606's puncture_condition.py builds the move action on six local
boundary coefficients. It then explicitly appends three block-sign
matrices to form the graded action. Its reported irreducibility for that
enlarged matrix group is a conditional positive, not an identification of
those signs with four-wording transport.

B1641 computes selected inner lifts on the cohomology module containing
the matter triplet T. That is not an identification with the local
boundary module L6. The same arc finds that neither T nor its conjugate
is a subrepresentation or quotient of L6, and its October 14 addendum
explicitly leaves full Aut^+(F2) lift coherence open. The paper's
s06_chiral_three.tex preserves this limitation.

There is a direct consistency obligation. In the ordinary group,

    I_c = I_a I_b^-1 I_a^-1 I_b,
    c = a b^-1 a^-1 b.

For any honest linear representation assigning commuting diagonal
parity matrices C_a,C_b to I_a,I_b, the right side is the identity.
It cannot simultaneously assign -I to I_c. B1641 reports precisely
commuting parity signs on T for a,b and -I for c under separately chosen
uniform lifts. These values therefore cannot all be treated as one
ordinary representation of Aut^+(F2). Its supplier lift_of selects an
intertwiner separately for each automorphism; it does not establish a
composition-preserving choice.

This elementary implication is conditional on those reported values,
not a rerun of them. It does not refute their individual matrix checks,
the possibility of a central/projective lift with its cocycle retained,
or an extension of the different L6 module. In particular, the sign on T
must not be silently transferred to L6. It identifies the composition
law that the proposed identification must respect.

If a coherent admitted action on L6 is derived, if its image contains
the stated graded action, and if a single boundary domain must be
invariant under that action, irreducibility restricts that domain to
0 or L6. Even then the existing formula is n(dim Lambda_+ - 3), hence
plus or minus 3n, not automatically plus or minus 3. The cusp unit,
matter dictionary and physical fermion/domain interpretation remain
separate duties.

There is a further distinction between an invariant domain at one
fixed state and a covariant family of domains. If a move takes a state
x to gx, covariance requires

    Lambda(gx) = U_g Lambda(x).

It need not require Lambda(x)=U_g Lambda(x) unless the relevant state
and identifications are fixed. The action on the full state must be
defined before using symmetry to remove domain freedom.

## The proposed flat line motion fails as written

For a flat one-dimensional character chi:F2->U(1), natural pullback by
an inner automorphism obeys the exact identity

    chi(h x h^-1) = chi(h) chi(x) chi(h)^-1 = chi(x).

Thus inner conjugation fixes the character, even though it need not
fix a chosen frame or transport of sections. Likewise, in
E_tau=C/(Z+tau Z), replacing w by w+m+n tau gives the same point and
the same degree-zero line class. Theta sections can acquire transition
factors; that is not motion to a distinct flat line.

The eighteen lines in the paper are more specific: exact-order-nine
points at tau=omega for which 3w is fixed by the order-three move. They
are supplied together with a hypercharge dictionary and an end
condition in s05_couplings.tex. The proposed period shifts do not
transitively move between eighteen different line classes. Base modular
transformations, motion of a marked puncture, and transport of a flat
line must have separately specified maps; they cannot share the symbol
w without proof.

This does not exclude additional relative-position or nonabelian
transport data. Such data could matter physically. It does prevent
counting a change of trivialization as a new physical vacuum. Even a
genuine transitive orbit would show symmetry-related states, not by
itself a dynamical selection. If the symmetry is gauge, equivalence of
all physical observables and other fields must be established instead.

## The next useful question

The proposal sharpens the existing work rather than replacing it. The
next bridge is an action compatible with composition on the same
boundary module, matter module and coupling data. Its source is the
allowed word operations with their register, not a desired index.

1. Justify which left/right wordings and inverses are legal from the
   admitted principle. Preserve the positive monoid and group closure
   as different branches where appropriate.
2. Define the state: word marking, torus modulus, puncture position,
   flat-line class and any retained frame are distinct data. Identify
   the maps that forget each datum and what observables descend.
3. Derive the lifts on T and L6, checking their defining relations and
   central signs on a common specification. Do not substitute the
   already-tested enlarged matrix group for this derivation.
4. Establish whether the end law is invariant at one state or covariant
   over the family. Only then reconsider the extreme-domain input and
   allowed couplings.
5. Track which, if any, existing physical input is actually paid. An
   orbit census follows a derived action; it cannot define that action.

Even a complete Aut^+(F2) action is not automatically the whole
interacting architecture. It describes transformations of the stipulated
rank-two register. Which carriers coexist, how their interfaces join,
and which interactions the principle forces remain the broader goal.
Neither the useful group extension nor the failures of this proposal's
shortcuts settle the claim that existence is forced.
