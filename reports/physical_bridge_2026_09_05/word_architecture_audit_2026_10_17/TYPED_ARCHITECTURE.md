### Minimality and aperiodicity must have arguments

Minimal word complexity is a condition on an individual symbolic system.
If it is imposed, it leaves the Sturmian family; it does not select the
Fibonacci member. It does not imply that an assembly of systems remains
one Sturmian language. Nor does taking all permissible factors authorize
their arbitrary concatenation: 00 and 11 each occur in some Sturmian
language, whereas 0011 contains length-two factors whose numbers of ones
differ by two and so is not balanced.

A periodic directive can generate an aperiodic output. Repetition of the
Fibonacci substitution is the familiar example. A finite hyperbolic
monodromy, an infinite nonperiodic directive and a nonperiodic output are
therefore not interchangeable meanings of GM3. The existing
[starting audit](AUDIT.md) retains nonstationary histories while scoping
the stationary-return obstruction. No new selection rule is introduced.

In particular, the matrix group generated after inverse adoption also
contains identity, elliptic and parabolic elements. The hyperbolic-thread
subset does not exhaust that group. The newer text's distinction between
the arena and its closed hyperbolic motions must survive the calculation.
Even at the hyperbolic level, the primitive-thread census is not the
whole signed-power architecture; GENESIS section 3 already includes the
additional signed seeds recovered in R78.

## A precise finite-history law, conditional on the admitted updates

Let X be a specified state set, U an alphabet of legal update labels,
and T_u:D_u -> X a partial function for each u in U. A chronological
history u1...un is legal from x exactly when each successive map is
defined. Its action is T_un ... T_u1(x). The empty history acts as the
identity. Concatenation of legal paths is associative because composition
of functions is associative. No equal weights or physical clock occur
in this definition.

For a total shared-record action the domains are X, so all finite words
in the admitted alphabet compose. Distinct histories can have the same
action; quotienting those histories is a separate choice of observable
duties. On a restricted count sector some inverses can be partial even
though their matrices are invertible. One must not mix that partial
action with the total Z^2 action.

### Reuse AR1–AR2 with definedness retained

Write D(x) for all finite legal histories from x, including the empty
history, and O:X -> Z for the declared output tuple. Define

    x ~ y iff D(x)=D(y) and
               O(T_w x)=O(T_w y) for every w in D(x).

This is an equivalence relation. If x~y, the same first updates are
available. For any such u, a suffix v is legal from T_u x precisely when
uv is legal from x; the same holds for y. The defining equalities then
give T_u x ~ T_u y. The output agrees by the empty-history condition.
Thus the updates, their domains and the output descend to X/~.

Conversely, any equivalence preserving outputs and update definedness,
and taking equivalent inputs to equivalent successors, preserves every
finite continuation by induction. It therefore refines ~. This is the
coarsest sufficient finite-future record for these declared duties.
It is the partial-map form of the existing [AR proof](../ACT_REGISTER_PROOF.md),
not a derivation of the duties themselves or an observer/qualia mechanism.

Equality of all finite continuation data does not itself specify a global
fairness condition, a limit at infinity, or a probability on infinite
histories. If the theory requires such observations, they belong in the
specification and the quotient must preserve them too. An infinite path
is not automatically a convergent product of matrices or a carrier.

## How to calculate without repeating the root-only restriction

1. Start with the actual shared action and admitted lift, not a list of
   unbased carriers. Include the whole declared alphabet, including its
   current inverse ruling, with the determinant-negative fork separate.
2. Prove finite-history identities at generator level where possible.
   Use finite censuses as controls, not as the meaning of “every word.”
3. Declare the observables before compression. For a fixed pair of SL2(C)
   matrices the all-word trace algebra is available, but it is not a
   faithful substitute for integral orientation, based lifts or domains.
4. Keep relative placements already carried by a shared fibre. If only
   independent unbased objects were retained, classify the additional
   placement data rather than silently choose representatives.
5. For separate carriers, record the interface and coefficient matching.
   Their universal representation-gluing law does not select which
   interfaces occur. Do not identify this with temporal word composition.
6. Feed any proposed physical join into one existing action/operator
   specification. Recover the old stationary and interaction positives;
   do not splice a gauge phase from one construction to a spectrum from
   another.

These are calculation rules for the admitted architecture. They are not
an assertion that the programme's initial philosophical principle has
already forced that architecture. No complete priced-input row is paid.
