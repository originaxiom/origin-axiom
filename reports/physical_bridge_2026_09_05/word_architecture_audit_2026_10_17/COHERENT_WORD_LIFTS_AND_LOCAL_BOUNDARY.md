## The coefficient system and the lifted group

Let F2=<a,b>, I_h(x)=h x h^-1, and compose automorphisms with the
rightmost map first. Use the existing quaternion coefficient system

    rho(a)=i, rho(b)=j,
    i^2=j^2=k^2=-1, ij=k=-ji.

Here the quaternions are the existing two-dimensional complex SU(2)
matrices. This representation and the three nonzero parity characters
are inputs to this calculation, not consequences established here of
non-cancellation. Let H be the group generated by the four wordings and
their inverses. Define

    E = { (phi,g) : phi in H, g in SU(2),
                    rho(phi(x)) = g rho(x) g^-1 for all x in F2 }.

The product and inverse are

    (phi,g)(psi,h) = (phi psi, g h),
    (phi,g)^-1 = (phi^-1,g^-1).

They obey the defining intertwining equation: apply the equation for phi
to the word psi(x), then apply the equation for psi. Associativity is
therefore inherited from the direct product, not inferred from a finite
list of successful numerical relations.

Every generator has an intertwiner:

| Word map | Quaternion lift |
|---|---|
| L: a->a, b->ab | (1+i)/sqrt(2) |
| Lbar: a->a, b->ba | (1-i)/sqrt(2) |
| R: a->ab, b->b | (1-j)/sqrt(2) |
| Rbar: a->ba, b->b | (1+j)/sqrt(2) |

For example, conjugation by g_L sends i to i and j to k, whereas
conjugation by g_L^-1 sends j to -k=rho(ba). The analogous two
rotations about j verify R and Rbar. Inverse words use inverse lifts.
Thus E projects onto H. Two intertwiners of the same phi differ by a
matrix centralizing both i and j. That centralizer in SU(2) is {+1,-1}:
commuting with the diagonal matrix i makes a matrix diagonal, and
commuting with j makes its two diagonal entries equal. Consequently

    1 -> {1,epsilon} -> E -> H -> 1,
    epsilon=(id,-1),

is a central extension. The two lifts of an operation are explicit
states in this extension, not an unnoticed sign choice in a function.

This extension does not split with these SU(2) intertwiners. The based
inversion J:a->a^-1,b->b^-1 lies in H=Aut+(F2) and has J^2=id. Its
only intertwiners are +/-k, and both square to -1. A group section into
E would have to send this order-two element to an element whose square
is 1, which is impossible. This proves nonsplitting of the specified
double cover. It does not classify arbitrary U(1) rephasings or establish
a physical anomaly.

## The cohomology action and its composition order

For p in P={(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)}, put M_p(x)=chi_p(x)rho(x). Let

    V = direct sum over p of H^1(F2; M_p).

The existing cochain convention is

    z(xy)=z(x)+M_p(x)z(y),
    delta(v)(x)=(M_p(x)-1)v.

For e=(phi,g) define D_e z(x)=g^-1 z(phi(x)). This is a cocycle for
M_(p phi), because g^-1 M_p(phi(x)) g=M_(p phi)(x). It sends
delta(v) to delta(g^-1 v), so it descends to cohomology. Directly,

    D_f D_e = D_(e f).

This is a right action, or equivalently a representation of the opposite
group. A left action is U_e=D_(e^-1). Mixing these conventions would
manufacture a failed relation. B1600's literal move_matrix implements D;
its matrices must be compared to U with the inverse included.

For an inner map use the natural uniform lift

    J_h=(I_h,rho(h)).

It respects multiplication: J_h J_t=J_(ht). The cocycle identity gives

    D_(J_h) z(x) = chi_p(h) z(x)
                  + (1-M_p(x)) rho(h)^-1 z(h).

The second term is a coboundary with the opposite sign. Hence J_h acts
on H^1(F2;M_p) as chi_p(h). This derives the parity signs on cohomology
for the coherent natural inner subgroup; it does not transfer them to
the local coefficient module.

Let c=a b^-1 a^-1 b and Delta=L R^-1 L. Free-word substitution gives

    Delta(a)=a b^-1 a^-1,   Delta(b)=a,
    Delta^4(a)=c a c^-1,    Delta^4(b)=c b c^-1.

Quaternion multiplication gives

    g_Delta=g_L g_R^-1 g_L=(i+j)/sqrt(2),
    g_Delta^2=-1,          g_Delta^4=+1,
    rho(c)=-1.

Therefore the two relevant lifted elements are different:

    (Delta,g_Delta)^4=(I_c,+1)=epsilon J_c,
    J_c=(I_c,-1).

Since chi_p(c)=1, D_(J_c)=+I on V, whereas D_epsilon=-I and the
ruled fourth power acts as -I. There is no contradictory commutator:
the product J_a J_b^-1 J_a^-1 J_b is J_c, not epsilon J_c. B1617's
pointwise lift_of can choose +1 for I_c while choosing i and j for
I_a and I_b. Those separately chosen values cannot be a group section;
their individual numerical outputs need not be wrong.

This gives coherence on V for the specified E. Any restriction to the
paper's matter triplet must additionally retain its actual invariant
subspace and the convention converting D to a left action. No new
physical field assignment follows from the construction.

## The local boundary action retains two pieces

The local boundary object is not V. Before the fixed changes of block
frame, use W=direct sum over p in P of C^2. For e=(phi,g), define

    A_e : e_p tensor v -> e_(p phi^-1) tensor g v.

The inverse in the parity label is necessary: applying A_f and then A_e
gives p psi^-1 phi^-1=p(phi psi)^-1 and the matrix g h. Thus
A_e A_f=A_(e f). This is an honest left action of E.

Set C=diag(U_p), with U_(1,0)=j, U_(0,1)=i, U_(1,1)=k. B1606's
local module is expressed in the frame C^-1 A_e C. For L and R the
parity permutations are involutions, so p phi^-1=p phi; its two
generator matrices agree exactly with B1606's formula. B1641's
blocks_action uses the equivalent inverse-permutation arrangement.
The agreement on these generators does not license using the forward
parity permutation with g on arbitrary composites.

For the alternative wordings,

    g_Lbar=g_L^-1,   g_Rbar=g_R^-1,
    Lbar mod 2 = L^-1 mod 2,   Rbar mod 2 = R^-1 mod 2.

It follows that

    A_Lbar=A_L^-1,   A_Rbar=A_R^-1.

Choosing the other uniform sign only multiplies a matrix by -I, which
is already present: A_L^4=-I. Thus independently admitting all four
wordings adds operations on based words but no new matrices to this
local boundary image.

There is also an exact all-word description of that image. Conjugation
by g_L and g_R gives quarter rotations about two perpendicular axes.
They generate the 24 orientation-preserving signed permutations of the
three quaternion axes; their SU(2) lifts contain both central signs and
form the order-48 group 2O. A direct count uses the six images of one
oriented axis and four perpendicular choices for a second. Rotations by
pi about axes give the sign changes, and the quarter rotations generate
the axis permutations. The induced permutation of P is determined by
this axis action on Q8/{+1,-1}. Hence the pair of actions (on P and C^2)
is determined by g, and its image has 48 elements, not 192.

The invariant splitting needs no numerical eigenspace clustering. In
the raw frame, let

    W2 = {(v,v,v): v in C^2},
    W4 = {(v1,v2,v3): v1+v2+v3=0}.

Uniform g and permutation of the blocks preserve both spaces. They are
irreducible and inequivalent: the Q8 subgroup acts as the same
irreducible doublet on every block, so its commutant consists of arbitrary
3 by 3 scalar block matrices. The remaining action permutes all three
blocks as S3. Its commutant is exactly the span of I3 and the all-ones
matrix, of dimension two. Since the action is unitary and W2 and W4
are nonzero invariant summands, that dimension proves each is irreducible
and they are inequivalent. In B1606's coordinates the projector onto
the two-dimensional piece is

    P2=C^-1 [(all-ones 3 by 3 matrix)/3 tensor I2] C.

Consequently the invariant subspaces for this full uniform action are
0, C^-1 W2, C^-1 W4 and L6. In the paper's conditional formula
n(dim Lambda_+ - 3), their indices are -3n,-n,n,3n. The enlargement
does not remove the two middle options, set n=1, or provide the physical
operator to which that formula would apply.

## Why the cohomology parity signs cannot be substituted locally

For the natural inner lift J_h the parity permutation is trivial, but
the local action is still rho(h) on each raw block. In the B1606 frame,

    (C^-1 A_(J_h) C)|_p = chi_p(h) rho(h).

Thus the local inner actions for a and b contain i and j in addition
to their parity signs. They anticommute, while their cohomology actions
are the commuting parity signs. Removing the quaternion factor is a
change of action, not a change of coordinates justified here.

| Lifted element | Local coefficients | Cohomology V |
|---|---|---|
| epsilon=(id,-1) | -I | -I |
| J_c=(I_c,-1) | -I | +I |
| (Delta,g_Delta)^4=(I_c,+1) | +I | -I |

The separately appended grading in B1606 does destroy the diagonal
doublet W2: a sign pattern (-,+,-) sends (v,v,v) to (-v,v,-v), outside
W2 for v nonzero. That explains why it can change the boundary-domain
classification. It does not establish that this grading is forced by
the enlarged word action.

This calculation restricts the stated uniform SU(2) construction. It
does not exclude block-dependent lifts, a larger coefficient system,
moving punctures, interactions, or a covariant family of boundary
domains. Each would need its own derived or declared transport law.

## Register retention and the remaining joining duty

The pair (phi,g) keeps an operation and the relation implementing it on
the coefficients. Forgetting g identifies two states that act with
opposite signs. Forgetting phi instead loses based-word distinctions
that this finite boundary image cannot detect. The appropriate quotient
therefore depends on both the future operations and the observables,
as in the earlier register analysis; neither projection is universally
adequate.

This is a concrete use of the owner's argument, not an observer or a
qualia definition. It derives composition from the specified coefficient
action. It does not yet derive the choice of coefficient system or the
joint law that couples different parts of the architecture.

A useful next specification must distinguish a boundary domain fixed at
one full state from a transported family Lambda(gx)=A_g Lambda(x).
Even the full 48-element invariance condition above applies only where
one domain is required to be invariant; a family over changing states
has a different obligation. The Higgs, matter coupling, regulator and
geometric end data must be transported with that same state before an
input-payment claim can be made.
