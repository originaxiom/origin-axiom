## Computing the additive quotient of the specified lifted group

Use the same E, natural J_h, l,r, Delta and c as in
[the coherent-lift proof](COHERENT_WORD_LIFTS_AND_LOCAL_BOUNDARY.md).
The natural inner subgroup J(F2) is normal, generated by J_a,J_b.
Conjugation gives

    l J_b l^-1=J_(ab), r J_a r^-1=J_(ab).

Consequently

    [l,J_b]=J_a,
    [r,J_a]=J_a J_b J_a^-1.                       (1)

Thus both J_a and J_b belong to the commutator subgroup [E,E].
The entire J(F2) does. Quotienting it therefore does not change
abelianization. The preceding geometric proof identifies

    E/J(F2)=Mp2(Z)=B3/<Delta^8>.

One can now compute without a finite group census. Write l,r
additively in an arbitrary abelian quotient. The braid relation

    l r^-1 l=r^-1 l r^-1

gives [r]=-[l]. Then [Delta]=[l]-[r]+[l]=3[l], and Delta^8=1
in E/J imposes 24[l]=0. There are no further relations in that
presentation. Mapping l to 1 and r to -1 modulo 24 respects both
relations and is surjective. Hence

    E_ab=Z/24.                                    (2)

The familiar eta character has this order on the metaplectic quotient;
(2) is consistent with the paper's use of those characters. That
classical character fact and the earlier Mp2 quotient are not new
physical selections. The additional point here is that natural inner
registering moves do not supply an independent additive integer.

If n:E->Z obeys n(ef)=n(e)+n(f), it factors through (2). Since Z
has no nonzero torsion, n=0. This is a theorem about this stated group
and additivity, not about all possible end data in the programme.

## What this does and does not say about boundary winding

Suppose, conditionally, that one assigns each e a continuous unitary
map U_e:S1->U(N) with composition U_(ef)(theta)=U_e(theta)U_f(theta).
Then

    n(e)=wind(det U_e)

is integer-valued and additive, because determinants multiply and
winding is additive under multiplication. Equation (2) forces n(e)=0.
The conclusion also holds for a semidirect composition with a circle
reparametrization of degree +1, since such a pullback preserves winding.
An orientation-reversing operation changes that hypothesis and needs
its own twisted law; it is not part of this H=Aut+(F2) calculation.

This is not a proof that every boundary map must have zero winding.
The source's B_R,n is a map of the boundary circle, not a demonstrated
homomorphism from E into such maps. For a fixed chosen n it can have
the stated winding. What fails is claiming that the integer is an
additive word observable on E without supplying more structure.

Finite holonomy by itself also does not recover that integer. On S1
the flat unitary connections i n dtheta all have holonomy
exp(2 pi i n)=1, although the maps exp(i n theta) have winding n.
The single-valued gauge maps exp(-i n theta) relate these connections
if those gauge maps are admitted. If a relative framing excludes them,
that framing is essential data. Thus the monodromy, a framed connection
and a boundary transition class are different objects. An eta phase or
finite quaternion matrix cannot silently replace the latter.

If U depends on a full state x, composition can instead read

    U_(ef)(x)=U_e(fx) U_f(x),
    n(ef,x)=n(e,fx)+n(f,x).                       (3)

Equation (3) is a cocycle on a state/action groupoid, not necessarily
a homomorphism E->Z. The vanishing theorem above cannot be applied
without removing this state dependence legitimately. A nonzero example
would still need an actual derived state, maps, continuity and physical
end action; writing a cocycle does not construct them.

This parallels the existing nontrivial crossed homomorphism b:H->Z^2
in the theta-field transport. The action on its target is nontrivial,
so it is not forbidden by a statement about ordinary additive labels.
Nor does b alone supply an integer physical winding or cusp unit.

## Which changes can alter the physical count

In the source's compact problem, a norm-continuous family of Fredholm
Weyl blocks on the same graph domain has constant index. The
cover-stationary proof uses precisely this fact, and additional
missing-partner gaps, to preserve the three charged kernels under
small stationary deformations. A changing coupling or wavefunction
does not by itself change their net chiral index.

Likewise the existing smooth-transmission proof preserves the full
kernel when an artificial seam is inserted or removed with the same
remote domains. Closing a bookkeeping cut is not a new index source.

A proposed derivation must therefore specify which index-bearing data
the architecture actually generates: for example a carrier/bundle or
boundary class, a changed admissible domain, a nonlocal exterior, or
a singular/continuous-spectrum transition requiring new analysis.
These are candidate mechanisms to test, not an exhaustive theorem
about every physical theory. Invertible transport of one already
specified problem cannot alone turn its index into a different one.

The compact silver and noncompact weave constructions remain separate
controls. A protected three-profile branch is a positive conditional
existence result. It does not establish that this boundary is generated
from the principle. Conversely an additive-integer obstruction on E
cannot exclude its supplied domain or every state-dependent end law.
