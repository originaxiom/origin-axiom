## Conditional geometric reduction: two metric shapes from the family

For a marked E_tau=C/(Z+tau Z), let omega_tau=dw have a-period one.
Its oriented Hodge norm follows by integration over a fundamental cell:

    h_L(omega_tau)= (i/2) integral omega_tau wedge bar(omega_tau)=y.

For j=c tau+d, w'=w/j and tau'=(a tau+b)/j, so
dw' relative to the fibre is dw/j and y'=y/abs(j)^2. The Hodge
norm is consequently invariant, with line coefficients transforming
by j. Its square has the automorphy of d tau, since d tau'=d tau/j^2.

convention for the universal scalar in the Kodaira--Spencer map.

The induced cotangent norm is proportional to y^2. Its dual gives

    g_B = ell^2 |d tau|^2/y^2.                             (1)

The fixed Hodge/polarization prescription, not modular covariance
alone, supplies this shape. On the elliptic fibre, requiring the

    g_V = A |du+tau dv|^2/y.                              (2)

The Gauss--Manin marking gives the relative form
Theta=dw-v d tau=du+tau dv. Equations (1)--(2) recover exactly
the two metric shapes ALREADY used in the surface parent, rather than
choosing their functional dependence a second time. Their physical
use, the positive scales ell and A, and the horizontal/kinetic

arbitrary punctured fibre's complete hyperbolic metric from (1).

A common change of length unit sends ell to s ell and A to s^2 A.
