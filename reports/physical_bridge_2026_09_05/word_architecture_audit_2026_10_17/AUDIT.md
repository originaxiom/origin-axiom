# Sturmian words and the scope of the generated architecture

October 17, 2026. Source and analytical audit, not a banked arc or an
admitted scientific experiment. The paper is out of scope.

## Conclusion

The concern is justified in a specific form: minimal aperiodic factor
complexity does not select Fibonacci, and a classification of finite
hyperbolic returns is not a classification of all histories of the
grammar. But the current programme is already much larger than m004 or
one Fibonacci word. It contains other closed word states, signed states,
mixed substitution dynamics, cut phases and random walks. Those positives
must not be erased by describing this as an entirely unattempted direction.

The useful next question is **which distinctions survive the maps between
these descriptions, and which are required to compose relations**. Keep
Fibonacci and m004 as recovery cases, not as exclusive inputs or discarded
mistakes. Enlarging a mathematical family does not by itself select a
physical state, action, probability law or observable.

## Different meanings of a word

| Description | Data it keeps | What it does not automatically specify |
|---|---|---|
| An output word in a and b | Ordered symbols, with a chosen origin when pointed | The update rule that produced it |
| A Sturmian language | All finite factors at one irrational slope | A particular phase or pointed sequence |
| A substitution | Ordered images of each letter | A unique physical interpretation of iteration |
| A directive history | Which substitutions act, in order | A repeating return or a physical clock |
| An incidence matrix | Letter counts in substitution images | Their internal order and, generally, the based lift |
| A finite based L/R matrix word | Ordered positive shear updates, recoverable from its product | A choice of word lift beyond those matrices |
| A conjugacy class of a return matrix | Return data up to change of marking | A relative marking for joining it to another return |
| A punctured-torus mapping torus | The chosen geometric realization of a return | A physical field theory or an interaction with another carrier |

Thus `a, ab, ba` is not by itself a complete specification of one infinite
word or of all legal updates. In B1323 the two elementary substitutions
G(a)=a, G(b)=ab and Gt(a)=a, Gt(b)=ba already have the same incidence
matrix L. They differ as based word maps. This is existing evidence of a
distinction that abelianization can forget.

There is an important opposite control: the **positive matrix monoid**
generated by L and R is free. In a nonempty product, a final L makes the
second column the sum of the preceding columns; a final R makes the first
column that sum. The componentwise dominant column identifies the last
letter. Subtracting the other column recovers the preceding product, and
iteration reaches I. Consequently, multiplying a finite positive L/R word
does not lose that word's order. Do not transfer the general incidence-map
loss to this faithful, restricted map. Signed histories have relations;
their matrix products answer a different question.

## Minimal complexity leaves a genuine family

For irrational 0 < alpha < 1 and a phase beta, the mechanical sequence

    s_n = floor((n+1) alpha + beta) - floor(n alpha + beta)

is Sturmian. These sequences have factor complexity p(k)=k+1. Different
slopes give different languages, although their factor-complexity functions
are identical. This is the relevant distinction between minimal complexity
and a selector for one member. See the definitions and Sturmian results in
[Cassaigne, Fici, Sciortino and Zamboni](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.5843).

The frequency is visible directly, without fitting a finite sample:

    sum_{n=0}^{N-1} s_n = floor(N alpha + beta) - floor(beta),
    lim_{N -> infinity} (sum s_n)/N = alpha.

So equal complexity is not identity of the dynamical systems or all their
observables. Conversely, choosing a favoured frequency is additional
selection, not a consequence of equal complexity. The programme's
golden-extremality criteria remain conditional criteria; P019's A2 and
the chosen meaning of minimality remain premises. GENESIS already records
PF1–PF3 and GM1–GM4 as postulated rather than a derivation of existence.

### A nonquadratic comparison without numerical guessing

Take alpha = cube_root(2)-1. Its minimal polynomial is

    x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x - 1.

It has no rational root and is irreducible over Q. The associated word
still has minimal aperiodic complexity. Let r=alpha/(1-alpha), the ratio
of its two letter frequencies. This also has degree three, since alpha
and r are related by an invertible rational fractional transformation.

If an integer matrix M=[[a,b],[c,d]] preserves the frequency direction
(1,r), then

    b r^2 + (a-d) r - c = 0.

Degree three forces b=c=0 and a=d. A unimodular M is therefore only I or
-I, neither a nontrivial primitive substitution matrix. This proves the
specific obstruction: **this frequency has no such stationary
substitution return**. It does not say there is no symbolic dynamics,
rotation model, suspension, or possible physical realization.

B749 F7a already studies the nonquadratic obstruction using e-2. The
cubic example here makes the same obstruction transparent algebraically;
it is not a newly discovered family. Its purpose is to separate the
proved statement from the stronger phrase “no carrier.”

### Even a golden slope does not fix the pointed word

The distinction survives without changing the golden frequency. Set
alpha=(3-sqrt(5))/2 and beta=sqrt(2)-1. The word is Sturmian and shares
the language of the corresponding golden system. But beta is not in
Q(alpha). Yasutomi's necessary condition therefore excludes this pointed
word from being fixed by a nontrivial primitive substitution.

The full criterion also restricts the quadratic conjugates: “quadratic”
alone is not the criterion for substitution invariance of an arbitrary
pointed word. This is stated and proved in Theorem 1.2 and section 6 of
[Berthé, Ei, Ito and Rao](https://www.numdam.org/item/ITA_2007__41_3_329_0.pdf).
It does not exclude a substitutive *language* or an appropriate recoding.

B1085 already treats a cut phase as meaningful in a supplied one-dimensional
Hamiltonian. Its B1095 correction distinguishes common spectra from which
states localize at opposite boundaries. This audit preserves that result
as prior work, not a new computation or a Standard Model identification.

## Stationary returns are one part of the histories

S-adic descriptions use a sequence of substitutions rather than repeatedly
applying just one. In the Sturmian case, directive data describe the slope;
shifts or additional substitution choices also encode the phase. Describing
all languages is not the same as describing all pointed words. The two
shifts—along the output sequence and along the directive sequence—must
also be distinguished. The relevant constructions and their admissibility
conditions are in sections 3 and 5 of
[Berthé and Delecroix](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.3960).

This is an available framework, not a proof that an arbitrary formal
infinite composition converges or that a new physical ontology is forced.
The repository already cites S-adic histories in B505 and B557 and studies
mixed dynamics in B498. Its current B1662/B1663 work also treats random
histories with specified step weights. Recover those efforts before
proposing another instrument.

The sentence in GM3 linking a nonperiodic description to hyperbolic
monodromy needs an explicit type. A periodic **directive** `(LR)^infinity`
has hyperbolic one-period matrix LR, with trace 3. An aperiodic directive
need not have a finite return at all. B749 F2's actual argument instead
concerns rational invariant slopes and the finite-order/reducible versus
hyperbolic classification. That finite-return result is retained; it
does not establish an equivalence of the two kinds of periodicity.

Likewise, an almost-sure random-walk conclusion is relative to its
probability law, not a statement about every legal history. B1663 itself
limits its conclusions to its generator weights and numerical resolution.
Nothing here refutes its sampled results or supplies a preferred measure
over all slopes, phases or histories.

## Joining requires more than separate class labels

Let

    A = LR = [[2,1],[1,1]],
    A' = RL = [[1,1],[1,2]] = L^(-1) A L.

They are the same conjugacy class, yet

    A A  = [[5,3],[3,2]],  trace = 7,
    A A' = [[3,4],[2,3]],  trace = 6.

Both products use positive representatives. Thus “multiply two conjugacy
classes” does not define a unique conjugacy class by choosing their
representatives independently. Simultaneous conjugation of the pair is
harmless; an independent change in their relative marking changes the
product. A joining rule must retain, specify or explicitly average over
that relative datum. An averaging rule would introduce its own measure.

This is a concrete mathematical reason to investigate relations, not a
proof that these matrices describe interacting particles. R57 and R79
already distinguish based data, unbased classes and peripheral lifts.
R79 also shows why a change of marking must transport peripheral slopes
consistently: retaining a numerical slope while changing its basis is a
different operation, not automatically a physical effect.

## Some whole-family exclusions do not weaken on enlargement

For a fixed representation rho with invertible generator actions,

    Fix(all finite L/R words) = Fix(rho(L)) intersect Fix(rho(R)).

Even requiring a vector to be fixed by all primitive mixed words yields
this same intersection. It suffices to require it for U=LR and V=L^2 R:
V U^(-1)=L, then R=L^(-1)U. These two words are primitive because each
contains exactly one R.

Consequently, adding more histories cannot create a vector fixed by the
same two generators under the same definition of “fixed.” This does not
exclude equivariant sections, invariant observables, evolving states or
different representations. Nor does membership in the whole family
require every physical state to be fixed by every move. Any use as a
physical no-go must first justify that fixed-vector requirement.

### A positive recovered from the actual producer

B530's movement XXVIII infers a primitive substitutive description from
four return words to one marker. That implication alone is insufficient:
uniform recurrence already gives finitely many return words to each fixed
factor. But the producer itself supports the positive by a different,
exact argument. It defines

    sigma(a)=abAAB, sigma(b)=aAB, sigma(A)=abAB, sigma(B)=aA,
    pi(a)=pi(b)=0, pi(A)=pi(B)=1.

Every sigma-image contains a, and sigma(a) contains all four letters.
Therefore every sigma^2-image contains all four letters: sigma is
primitive. It is prolongable on a, with growing lengths. The binary
sequence is pi(sigma^infinity(a)), so it is primitive substitutive by
definition. The printed count of four return words is not needed for this
proof and has not been independently certified here.

The return-word derived sequence is primitive substitutive as well:
[Durand's Theorem 5 and Proposition 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.3322)
give finitely many derived sequences of a primitive-substitutive word,
and derivation of a derived sequence is another derivation of the original
word. The criterion is finiteness of the family of *derived sequences*,
not finiteness of one return alphabet. Thus the positive survives with
its proper justification. This argument does not make every Sturmian
history stationary.

## Proposed next investigation

First specify the **allowed histories and their transport**, rather than
start a larger numerical scan or replace m004 by another preferred member.

1. Reconcile the two current routes. Record what minimal factor complexity
   admits on the word route, and what GM1–GM5 admits on the record route.
   Keep positive moves, signed moves, swap legality, and noninvertible
   operations distinct. Their incidence images alone do not identify the
   routes including their lifts.
2. For the Sturmian comparison family, use slope, phase and endpoint
   convention explicitly. Specify legal substitutions, shifts, domains
   and equivalences. Recover the golden case and its existing cut model,
   together with the nonquadratic and nonfixed-phase controls above.
3. Ask whether existing registered observables and boundary laws descend
   through each proposed quotient. If joining uses only class labels,
   apply the two-product test above; supply a relative marking or a proven
   invariant construction. Do not silently choose the marking that gives
   a desired spectrum.
4. Only after this specification, propose a source-complete, independently
   reviewed experiment under the existing prospective gate. Reuse the
   old producers where their inputs match. A new spectrum or chirality
   claim requires its actual action, field space, operator domain and
   interactions; it cannot inherit them from a different carrier.

The milestone is a derivation of what information the admitted relations
require and what they allow us to quotient out. Success may show that
some extra data matter, or that a proposed quotient already preserves the
declared observables. Either outcome is useful. A phase or register is not
thereby an observer, qualia, a physical time or a Standard Model family.
Those identifications retain their separate duties.
