  T(j)=sum_(m=-j)^j m^2=j(j+1)(2j+1)/3,

Here T(n) in the second line denotes dimension n. Multiplicity from
spectator representations multiplies the trace. This is a direct trace

  8=(2,2,1) plus (1,1,4).

  28=(3,1,1)+(1,3,1)+(1,1,6)+(2,2,4).                   (1)

The dimensions add to 3+3+6+16=28. Now compute two distinct weak-algebra
restrictions of THIS SAME 28-dimensional vector space:

  T_weak(28)=2+8*(1/2)=6.

B. The diagonal su2 of SU2_L x SU2_R: each (2,2) restricts as 3 plus 1,
