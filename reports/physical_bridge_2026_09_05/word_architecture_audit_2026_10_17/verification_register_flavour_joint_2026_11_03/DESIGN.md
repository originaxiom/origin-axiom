November 3, 2026. Written before its implementation. Not a complete
execution specification or permission to import, collect or run science.
The candidate proof is REGISTER_FLAVOUR_ACTION_GRAM_AND_QUARTIC_STATE_TEST.md.
No empirical datum, optimisation, random seed or chosen favourable class
enters these controls. Fixed finite comparators cannot prove the global
physical identification or graph-domain hypotheses.

1. test_actual_two_triples_and_quartic_polynomial
   Reconstruct the source 10-by-10 compact D5 matrices from E5, J5 and
   the two literal bivector coefficients. Obtain Eplus/Eminus and their
   compact adjoints, orthogonal H norms and all mixed brackets. Compute
   the quartic trace for symbolic complex z1,z2 directly from these matrices
   and compare every coefficient with the stated polynomial, including the
   physical ell normalization. Scalar/identity substitutes are forbidden.
2. test_complete_mass_map_flavour_intertwiner
   Build all four physical Weyl slots for a noncommuting traceless
   2-by-2 gauge comparator, with the original Omega weights, q/r dagger,
   affine derivatives and sqrt(2) coefficients. Test the whole matrix
   M_(U Phi) T_in=T_out M_Phi for gL and gR with independent derivative
   symbols. A deliberately unconjugated spin target must fail at gL.
   Verify both unitary kinetic maps. This finite Lie comparator supports
   the universal analytical identity; it is not an E8 spectrum computation.
3. test_irreducible_holonomy_gram_constraint
   Use a general Hermitian 2-by-2 density. Fi kills its off-diagonal;
   Fj then equates its diagonal entries. Check a rank-one rejecting
   control, a positive scalar control and a one-generator counterfamily.
   Bind the EW density to actual D5 E,G, deriving equal amplitudes under
   the declared normalization. Do not assert compensator existence.
4. test_three_same_parent_stationary_states_and_readouts
   Transform the actual balanced D5 pair by gL and gR. Check every
   moment and F-bracket identity and the physical equal density. Compute
   the full three-probe table from matrices; assert pairwise unequal
   gauge-invariant readouts. Do not call these three fermion generations
   or an exhaustive vacuum count.
5. test_covariant_reference_and_register_composition_controls
   Test the fixed-reference difference and the contragredient-reference
   equality for gL,gR and a composite, with symbolic complex probe
   coefficients. Check quaternion commutator and register Delta fourth
   power at the spin-field action, retaining their different source labels.
   Explicitly record that this representation forgets some based data;
   finite matrix agreement is not a faithful-register theorem.
