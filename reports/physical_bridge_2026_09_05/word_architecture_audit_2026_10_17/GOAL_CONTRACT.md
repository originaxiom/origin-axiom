## Goal and relationship to physics

Derive the allowed composition and joining laws, or establish precisely
what the admitted foundations leave undetermined. Study the generated
relational architecture rather than select Fibonacci, m004 or a convenient
replacement as the whole object. Keep those cases as recovery controls.

This is a step toward the owner's larger aim of a physically consistent
Standard Model with three generations and gravity from foundational
principles. A mathematical description of all allowed compositions is
not completion of that larger aim. An honest account of remaining freedom
is an acceptable outcome of this bounded goal, not a programme-wide no-go.

The act and the relation that registers it must remain explicit wherever
later composition or observations use them. Whether a register can support
an observer or qualia remains a separate question; neither is identified
with a formal memory variable by definition.

not a no-go for the principle or an adopted replacement foundation.

## How the calculation is to be posed

1. Keep constituent minimality separate from a restriction on the joint
   system. Do not require an interacting collection to remain one
   Sturmian language unless that requirement follows from an explicitly
   admitted premise. Do not infer legal concatenation from legal factors.
2. Use full specified word maps before passing to incidence matrices or
   conjugacy classes. A faithful restricted matrix representation can
   retain order even when a larger incidence map does not.
3. Extend the existing future-observation quotient only with explicit
   domains: partial updates require agreement on which continuations are
   legal as well as agreement on their outputs. This criterion is
   conditional on the declared updates and outputs; it derives neither.
4. Treat aperiodic nonstationary histories separately from finite
   hyperbolic returns. An obstruction to a stationary substitution is
   not absence of symbolic dynamics or every geometric realization.
5. Any statistical or quantum weighting requires its own derivation or
   input tag. Admissibility does not mean physical occurrence,
   coexistence, equiprobability or superposition.
6. When testing physical joining, use one compatible action and field
   roster. Geometry, sources, bundles, kinetic metric, boundary maps and
   operator domains must travel together. No spectrum may be borrowed
   from another construction merely because a group or index agrees.
