## 1. Sewing preserves endpoint bookkeeping freedom

For a fixed supplied action, suppose S_01(q0,q1) and S_12(q1,q2) are
on-shell branches on adjacent regions. The notation presupposes
existence and differentiability on the field/trace class used. Keep all
bundle identifications and genuine remote conditions fixed. Their
joined stationary equation at the artificial seam is

    d_q1 [S_01(q0,q1)+S_12(q1,q2)] = 0.                 (1)

Let C_j(q_j) be differentiable real endpoint functionals. Define new
representatives

    S_ij^C = S_ij + C_j(q_j)-C_i(q_i).                 (2)

Then the two C_1 terms cancel exactly in (1). The total changes only by
C_2(q2)-C_0(q0). For any finite chain all interior terms telescope.
Composition and its grouping are therefore unchanged wherever the
same stationary branches exist. Multiple branches are retained as
multiple branches; neither taking a minimum nor discarding saddles
is implicit in this statement.

For a fixed complete action, the outer endpoint terms must also be
compensated in the retained end functional or source convention. With
that compensation, (2) is merely a different description of the SAME
variational problem. There is no additional physical parameter to count.
Without compensation, when q0 or q2 can vary, their equations change:
it is a different physical end problem, not a redundant convention.

At a seam with finite-dimensional real trace coordinates q, write an
outward response as p=dS/dq. Equation (2) shifts p by plus or minus dC,
with opposite signs on adjacent regions. Its linear response shifts by
the corresponding Hessian of C. The sum of the outward responses and
their linearizations is unchanged. This is just the old same-action
current cancellation, now including finite endpoint potentials. For
field-valued traces, it requires the corresponding functional
differentiability, jets and corner terms; a finite-coordinate formula
does not supply that analysis automatically.

Thus artificial-cut consistency alone does not distinguish these
representatives. It also does not show that every uncompensated C at
a genuine physical end has a supersymmetric or fermionic completion.
The statement is an invariance of a specified joined action, not an
all-architecture impossibility of deriving an end law.
