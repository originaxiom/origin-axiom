## 2. An explicit map, including the frame fibre

and z the mod-two homology class of its oriented frame fibre. A spin
structure is a linear functional xi on H1(U C,F2) with xi(z)=1.

  tilde(v+w)=tilde(v)+tilde(w)+B(v,w) z.                 (1)

It is NOT a homomorphism. For an explicit symplectic identification

  xi_q(tilde(v)+e z)=q(v)+e, e in F2.                   (2)

This is well-defined because projection to H1(C) leaves exactly the
fibre bit. It is linear: the polarization term in q(v+w) cancels
the extra B(v,w)z in (1). It has xi_q(z)=1. Conversely
q(v)=xi_q(tilde(v)), so (2) retains exactly the quadratic data and
its central frame sign. Naturality of (1) gives equivariance under
surface moves. This exhibits the map instead of identifying two
objects merely because they have an Arf bit.
