## 2. Direct harmonic and coupled-equation checks

For a coefficient b in beta=b dbar z tensor partial_z, the Chern
(1,0) derivative is proportional to

  (partial_z b + (partial_z log h)b) dz wedge dbar z.

For (1), h b=conjugate(q) is antiholomorphic, so this derivative is zero.
The Hodge-star identity on one-forms then gives dbar^dagger beta_q=0.
On a curve dbar beta_q=0 automatically. Thus beta_q is the harmonic
representative of its H1(K^-1) class for these actual line metrics.
Its norm squared is N(q), and the Serre pairing is

  <q,beta_q>_Serre=integral_Y q(z) conjugate(q(z))/h(z) d^2z=N(q).

With q0 the registered invariant quadratic differential pi^*(dz_E^2),
normalize its dual class by

  beta0=beta_(q0)/N(q0),  <q0,beta0>_Serre=1.              (2)

Q8 invariance follows from invariance of q0 and the unique uniformizing
metric. Near an upstairs ramification point q0=4w^2 dw^2, so beta0
vanishes smoothly to conjugate order two. No delta source, independent
puncture projector or chosen boundary-localized profile has been added.

In the adjoint Higgs complex, the extension tangent has

  v=(a,varphi)=(beta0,0),                                (3)

where a is valued in the upper root K^-1. The Higgs and a have the same
upper nilpotent direction, so [Phi,a]=0. Consequently D1 v=0. Further,
D0^dagger v=dbar_A^dagger a=0; the adjoint Higgs contribution vanishes
because varphi=0. This is a genuine harmonic class in the previously
computed H1(K^-1) part of the coupled complex.

It also checks the real linearized curve equations directly. Complete
a to the unitary connection variation delta A=a-a^dagger (with the
corresponding complex tangent and its conjugate). The harmonic identity
above and its conjugate give d_A delta A=0. Since delta Phi=0, both
the first-order moment-map equation and the holomorphy equation hold.
Thus at this recovery point no additional first-order harmonic-metric
correction is needed to realize this class. Finite-amplitude corrections
are not zero by this argument; the full solution still changes with e.

  M chi=D0^dagger v,  M=D0^dagger D0,
  v_h=v-D0 M^-1 D0^dagger v.                             (4)

Since D1D0=0, v_h remains closed and is gauge orthogonal. Its metric is

  ||v_h||^2=||v||^2-<D0^dagger v,M^-1 D0^dagger v>.        (5)

selected Gram power. It follows by expanding the positive norm and

extension coordinate e defined by [beta]=e[beta0], (1)-(3) therefore give

  G_(e bar e)(e=0,Y)=C_A/N(q0).                           (6)

## 4. The surface line must also enter the norm

For the canonical surface field, L^2=K_X^-1 rather than K_Y^-1. At a
fixed base point let n(z)>0 be the actual induced Hermitian metric on
that root line, including the base-canonical factor, and use the fibre

the analogous Serre-dual harmonic expression replaces h by n:

  beta_q=conjugate(q)/n,  N_n(q)=integral_Y |q|^2/n d^2z.   (7)

Here q represents a section of K_Y tensor (L^2)^*; its suppressed base
line is part of the frame, not discarded data. Equation (7) gives the
same inverse dual-norm expression when (3) is an actual tangent of
that background and no additional action weight has been dropped.
A nonconstant kinetic weight changes both the adjoint and (7).

In the PARTICULAR factorized recovery metric n=h_B(tau)h_Y(z), with
h_B constant along the fibre, this reduces to

  G_surface(e=0,tau)=C_A h_B(tau)/N(q0,tau).               (8)

It is not an assertion that the full physical surface admits that
factorization or that its D-equation is already solved. Mixed metric,
warp and horizontal gauge responses have to be carried for the actual
parent, using its exact inner product rather than (8) by resemblance.

There is a useful covariance check. Under the canonical modular map,
q0 pulls back with j^-2, hence N(q0,gamma tau)=|j|^-4 N(q0,tau) for
transported fibre metrics. Meanwhile h_B(gamma tau)=|j|^4 h_B(tau).

This verifies the weight bookkeeping without assuming that the
coefficient is any particular power of y. Covariance alone does not
fix the positive invariant factor in N(q0,tau).
