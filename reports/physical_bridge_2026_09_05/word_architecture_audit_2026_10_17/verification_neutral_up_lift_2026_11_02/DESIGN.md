1. `test_actual_neutral_generator_and_full_residual_compatibility`:
   reconstruct standard E8 roots, the old D5 subsystem and actual
   EM Cartan. All D5 roots have zero Z charge, the up coefficient
   has charge+/-2/3 and kappa(Z^2)=4/3. Reuse or reconstruct the
   explicitly bound E,G,H with disclosed source closure. Check the
   commutator/grade moment algebra with Q0+c psi Z,R0+d psi Z,
   including every adjoint cross term. An explicitly charged
   Cartan substituted for Z must fail a commutation control.
   This does not solve the global psi/domain estimates by a matrix.
2. `test_spin_pole_and_dual_multiplication_data`: use the ACTUAL
   pole law and all four extending spins. The neutral S section
   exists only at eta=O. Check b(3)=b(4)=1 and the actual flat lines
   of both up coefficients, with H0(S3 L),H0(S4 L) both dimension1
   and dual H1 spaces dimension1. An altered endpoint or an
   arbitrary nontrivial-spin psi must fail the corresponding
   criterion. Dimensions alone are not the global multiplication
   isomorphism: its nonvanishing remains the analytical duty.
3. `test_first_order_physical_mass_blocks_and_exceptional_planes`:
   derive the two generator coefficients, the all-degree determinant
   and actual source cubic contractions of spin directions(a,b) and
   (a,-b). Recover ad-bc and ad+bc, not supplied texture entries.
   Assemble two2-by-2 conjugate-charge blocks, check their full4-by-4
   determinant with nonzero symbolic physical overlap factors and
   nonzero EM charge. At a=3/5,b=4/5,c=0,d=1 recover rank4. Each
   exceptional plane must lose first-order rank; no all-orders
   persistence is inferred on those planes. Positive kinetic
   congruences preserve rank, not unknown overlap magnitudes.
4. `test_full_complement_schur_and_unbounded_inverse_controls`:
   construct a finite model with four kernel/cokernel modes and an
   invertible massive complement; retain nonzero light-heavy
   couplings. Derive its exact Schur zero-equation, verify the
   first-order matrix and second-order response directly, and
   recover invertibility for explicit small nonzero rational eps
   when the leading block is invertible. Include a countercontrol
   where a singular leading block is lifted only at higher order,
   so a vanishing first-order entry is not called all-orders zero.
   A decaying scalar comparator and its divergent inverse make the
