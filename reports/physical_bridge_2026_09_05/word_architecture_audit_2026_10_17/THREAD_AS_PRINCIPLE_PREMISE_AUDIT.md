## What is retained and what needs checking next

The parity action through inner conjugations is diagonal independently
of a particular chronological word once that representation is supplied.
That algebraic fact is different from which parity states a chosen word
visits, whether their span is all diagonal matrices, or whether its
chronological mean vanishes. B1621's finite Fibonacci checks alone do
not prove those latter assertions for all admissible histories.

The same distinction applies to ticks: the triplet representation is a
weave object, but applying it to LR does not cover every word. The new
SM-seat W107 proposal/output was encountered during intake and is NOT
blind to this lane now. No independent recomputation or acceptance of
its reported mod-8 criterion is supplied by this audit. Main and the
SM seat must compare the same operation group, lift conventions,
positive-word population and weighting before importing its consequences.

The current task is to finish semantic grading of the candidate set and
trace corrections into current surfaces. This first tranche does not
complete main's every-place request. Its immediate payoff is narrower
and useful: selected-thread limitations cannot serve as architecture-wide
