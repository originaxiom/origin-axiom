## 3. W158's negative has an exact structural reason

The special directions use a squared spinor (a,b). Substituting the
actual quaternion units and the displayed to_xyz map gives, before
an irrelevant nonzero projective normalization,

    S = [[-i a b, -b^2], [-a^2, i a b]]
      = (i b, a)^T (-a, i b).

For a nonzero spinor this matrix has rank one. Its conjugate reading
epsilon conjugate(S) epsilon^T has the same rank.

There is a direct check of the consequence that does not rely on the
reported floating-point mass floor or the full determinant formula.
The actual W93 producer is

    Y_ab = sum_ij (C_a^T epsilon S C_b)_ij M_ij.

Write rank-one S=u v^T, l_a=C_a^T epsilon u and r_b=C_b^T v. Then

    Y_ab = l_a^T M r_b,  Y = L^T M R,

where L and R have two rows and three columns. Therefore rank(Y)<=2
for EVERY M in this coupling law. Positive nonsingular kinetic
normalizations cannot restore its missing rank. This independently
checks the central mathematical negative by hand from the actual source.

It applies to all exact rank-one Higgs matrices in that law, not only
the sampled 26 pairs. It does not exclude non-null directions, additional
admitted operators, or a different derived physical construction. The
small nonzero masses in W158's floating output are not a mechanism that
rescues the exact zero. Conversely this argument does not certify its
reported chi-square value or all imported source dependencies.

The constructive information is precise: a viable nonzero lightest
mass requires departure from this rank-one locus, unless the coupling
law itself changes. What fixes that departure is now an explicit
dynamical duty. Its numerical size must not be selected from a fit and
