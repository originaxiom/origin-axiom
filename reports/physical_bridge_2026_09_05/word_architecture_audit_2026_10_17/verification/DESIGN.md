### Topological scope of the B144 opposite control

For a solid torus D^2 x S^1, choose boundary coordinates (meridian,
longitude). The maps (z,t)->(z,conjugate(t)) and
(z,t)->(conjugate(z),t) extend the boundary matrices h=diag(1,-1) and
h2=diag(-1,1). Both reverse the three-dimensional orientation. For
phi=[[0,1],[1,0]], h2 phi=phi h, so these maps assemble to an
orientation-reversing self-map of the glued manifold. Nevertheless
h phi h^-1=-phi!=phi. Also -I extends orientation-preservingly by
(z,t)->(conjugate(z),conjugate(t)).

without assuming every boundary matrix extends over a piece. The code
