## 2. A commutator retains one kind of relational information

For a group, [a,b]=1 exactly when a and b commute. At the specified
quaternion representation, [i,j]=-1 and i^2=j^2=(ij)^2=-1 follow directly
from ij=-ji. The central sign cannot be removed by rephasing the two lifts
by central scalars. It is erased on taking a quotient that identifies
the centre with the identity.

But [a,b]=1 is not absence of every relation or distinction. The free
abelian group Z^2 has distinct independent records and a nondegenerate
alternating determinant pairing, while every internal group commutator
is trivial. Its pairing and a noncommutative central extension are
different structures. A physical phase additionally requires an actual
representation/observable that detects the central element. The existing
common-register and level-four notes already exhibit modules that retain
or cancel different central factors; no universal field sign is inferred.

## 3. Two-dimensional pairing preservation is exact, but does not select a word

For J=[[0,1],[-1,0]] and ANY 2-by-2 matrix M over a commutative ring,
direct multiplication gives

  M^T J M=(det M)J.

Thus on the rank-two integer lattice Sp(J,Z)=SL(2,Z). Determinant-one
shears preserve J; a swap reverses it. This is an identity for every
such M, not a discrimination of the golden word or a preferred state.
The positive shears alone generate a monoid; their inverse closure is
required for the corresponding full group statement.

Moreover the homology/count action forgets natural inner automorphisms
of the based free group. It is not the full faithfully retained register.
Do not derive a physical equivalence by dropping that kernel. The newly
recorded level-four map is an explicit positive with a rejecting inner
control, illustrating exactly this distinction.

Over characteristic two the word ALTERNATING is essential: B(v,v)=0.
Skew-symmetry alone does not imply a zero diagonal there. A one-dimensional
alternating form is degenerate over any field, and the least nonzero
nondegenerate alternating space has dimension two. This conditional
minimal-dimension statement does not show that every relation must be
alternating or nondegenerate.

An integer pairing kJ also requires its primitiveness/perfectness and
scale convention to be stated. Nonzero determinant over Q and a perfect
integer pairing are not interchangeable hypotheses.

## 4. What the Fibonacci minimum can actually say

The attached statement about one integer record holds for ORIGIN-FIXING
INTEGER LINEAR automorphisms: GL(1,Z)={+1,-1}. It is false for all
reversible integer transformations: n -> n+1 is an invertible affine
map with an unbounded orbit. Linearity and a distinguished origin must
not disappear from the input account.

A simple conditional minimum can be proved without a claimed exhaustive
run. Fix the class of primitive nonnegative integer 2-by-2 matrices with
|det|=1 and spectral radius greater than one. Define cost to be the SUM
OF ENTRIES. Cost at most two gives only permutation matrices among the
unimodular candidates. At cost three, an entry two plus an entry one
has determinant zero or absolute determinant two, so is inadmissible.
The three-one candidates with an off-diagonal zero are triangular and
have spectral radius one. The remaining two are

  F=[[1,1],[1,0]],  P F P=[[0,1],[1,1]].

They are primitive and expanding, so this restricted minimum is correct.
The source's phrase "together with its transpose" is wrong here: F is
symmetric. Record exchange gives P F P instead. "Minimal complexity"
without the stated cost and candidate class is not this theorem.

F has determinant minus one, is anti-symplectic for J, and has eigenvalues
phi and -phi^(-1). Its square has determinant one and reciprocal positive
eigenvalues. If determinant PLUS one is required from the start, an
expanding nonnegative primitive matrix has trace at least three and
both off-diagonal entries positive, hence entry cost at least five.
That minimum is achieved by [[2,1],[1,1]] and its record exchange.

With L=[[1,1],[0,1]], R=[[1,0],[1,1]] and P the swap, F=LP and F^2=LR.
These conventions concern the COUNT action; the Wilson-period action
must be compared through its actual transported/dual convention.
No allowed minimum selects which history the system physically follows.
An algebraic iteration index is not yet physical time or an oscillator.

## 5. At p=2 the commutator is not the whole extension

The exact sequence Z2 -> Q8 -> F2^2 is non-split; the argument using
the unique order-two element is valid. However the same nondegenerate
commutator pairing and central Z2 also occur in the order-eight dihedral
group. A nonsplit central extension and its commutator do not by themselves
identify Q8.

The missing information is the square function q. For a lift x_v of
v in F2^2, define x_v^2=(-1)^q(v). Central rephasing by -1 leaves this
square unchanged, and direct multiplication gives

  q(v+w)=q(v)+q(w)+B(v,w) modulo two.

If B(x,y)=1, the four choices of q(x),q(y) give four refinements.
Only q(x)=q(y)=1 has q=1 on all three nonzero directions; it is Q8.
The others have the dihedral type with inequivalent square behaviour.
If FULL Sp(2,F2) equivariance is required, its transitive permutation
of the three nonzero vectors forces q to be constant there. The
polarization identity then forces that constant to be one. Thus Q8
is conditionally distinguished by the retained square data and full
move symmetry, without assuming an SU2 realization in that finite
classification. Requiring only exchange of two basis vectors is weaker.

This is already the genus-one content of main's B1707/W111 and its
transvection argument, not a new breakthrough. Main's October 21 relay
also explicitly regrades two-record persistence as a characterization,
not a weaker derivation of two records from the founding premise.

Minimizing a FINITE nontrivial central phase group by cardinality gives
Z2. But finiteness, centrality and cardinality as the minimized cost
are additional conditions. Noncommutativity alone does not force that
centre, and cardinality minimality is not description-length minimality.
The proposed p=2 step is a clear candidate duty, not a discharged input.
