## First earning duty: the registered physical bundle and its end law

**Question:** does the actual generated transport determine the combined
physical coefficient data and their measuring maps, or only permit them?
Reuse the existing full registered embedding and word lifts; do not assign
Q8 to a flavour doublet merely because both have dimension two.

Trace the full generator actions, based relations, puncture commutator,
flat holonomies, marking changes and relevant cover transitions through
one proposed physical map. Retain the local two-plus-four module, the
separate matter triplets, gauge companions and measuring/reference sector.
Check that no representation or type is exchanged silently between them.

The target in the existing parent's spin/flavour sector is the combined
bundle with its Hermitian norm, connection, determinant-to-canonical-line
pairing, compatible physical gauge/background data, complete end domain
and registering maps. A representation embedding alone is not this map.
Likewise equality of one central sign is not equality of all transitions.

**Successful reduction:** one previously independent physical transport,
relative metric or end datum is actually determined, with its remaining
scale and family freedom explicit. A derived family with common observable
constraints is also progress.

**Discriminator:** an incompatible group relation, failed global section,
changed operator domain, or an inequivalent compatible family blocks only
that proposed identification. Exhibit the residual freedom or failed map;
do not call it a theorem against the full generated architecture.

## Second earning duty: allowed fields and interactions from that same map

**Question:** which scalar and higher-channel interactions are allowed or
forced by the retained registered structure and actual parent action?
The scalar quaternion opening motivates this question; numerical success
does not select its answer.

Use the existing intertwiner/vertex results to classify the relevant
couplings, carrying actual field degrees, representations and full
transitions. Determine whether the scalar channel is already part of the
admitted field roster, a derived composite, or an added field. Keep the
other components and transformed couplings when using a field redefinition.
An extra field or new parent is a separately priced candidate.

Use the actual action to compute induced maps, retaining massive and
boundary modes as required. The neutral-family result is an example of
an interaction derived within one supplied parent; its state amplitudes
remain independent. Do not turn its fourth-order holomorphic coefficient
into an observed pole-mass hierarchy without the physical norms.

**Successful reduction:** a genuinely independent interaction coefficient,
texture or admissibility choice disappears under stated premises.
Uniqueness up to an unselected scalar pays only the relative tensor shape.

**Discriminator:** exhibit two inequivalent allowed couplings or a surviving
coefficient under the exact same hypotheses. A different quadratic
refinement or square-root choice cannot be declared equivalent without
transporting the physical fields and registering observables.

## Third earning duty: normalization and selection on the admitted family

Carry the source kinetic metric, complete physical operator and end law
into normalized interactions. Distinguish a coefficient norm from an
integrated harmonic Gram. A frame change that makes a metric the identity
must also transform the connection, vertices and domain.

Only then ask whether the resulting action or relational consistency law
selects a modulus, vacuum class or distribution. Golden arithmetic and
other AGY formulas are explicit hypotheses to compare with that law;
they are not boundaries to impose in order to recover their own numbers.
Keep one overall energy unit separate from dimensionless empirical ratios.

A successful outcome may be a unique datum, a derived distribution, or
predictions common to an allowed family. If freedom remains, characterize
it rather than force a preferred point. The allowed-word architecture,
not a single convenient root or word, sets the scope of any global claim.
