## 4. Rank deficiency need not prevent a covariance constraint

The leading-slot subspace in (2) has at most five independent coefficients. If an actual Higgs/input family spans all five matrices E13,E23,E31,E32,E33, it can still fix the constant left/right matrix action up to reciprocal scalars.

To prove the stabilizer statement, suppose invertible L,R satisfy L^T Eij R=Eij for those five pairs. Each equality says that L^T e_i is a scalar multiple of e_i and row j of R is the inverse multiple of row j of I. E13 and E23 make the scalars on e1,e2 equal. E31 and E32 fix the other two rows of R with the scalar on e3. E33 equates that scalar with the first two. Thus L=cI and R=c^-1 I.
