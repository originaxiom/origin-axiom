## What the complete parallel-pair test can exclude

Assume the fixed SM embedding remains unbroken, and impose the precise
parallel ansatz A=sH/2+C, qhat=E/2,rhat=G/2, with C flat and commuting
with H,E,G and their compact adjoints. The moment/spin/F equations are

    [H,E]=2E, [H,G]=2G, [E,G]=0,
    [E,E dagger]+[G,G dagger]=H.

SM invariance places the fields in su5_b complexified plus Y. A Y
component of E or G is killed by its grade-two equation; the moment
then has no Y component, so H lies in su5_b. This is not an assumption
that every nilpotent pair is an sl2 triple.

The finite-dimensional compact *-algebra generated by E,G acts on5.
Decompose it into inequivalent isotypic summands. If there are k>=2
types, their scalar orthogonal projectors give k-1 independent traceless
compact U1 generators commuting with the fields and every permitted C.
They commute with the SM but are independent of its Y. Exact SM gauge
algebra is therefore impossible. Equivalent copies may mix under C;
that does NOT remove the projectors onto different isotypic types.
If there is only one type,5=d*m forces either d=5,m=1 or d=1,m=5.
The latter has E=G=H=0 by the grade equations. Its complete flat end
at the old p has the previously established zero-energy spin continuum,
so it is not an isolated phase. No flat core choice changes that end.

In the remaining irreducible five case, Schur forces the structure
part of C to be scalar. The full SM centralizer was derived above;
with the possible Y part included, C lies in an abelian circle modulo
the checked centre identification. Its peripheral commutator is one.
Hence exp(i*pi*H/2) must itself equal the prescribed order-two p.

Irreducibility forces all grades into one connected consecutive integer

integral. Its two negative defining eigenvalues select precisely

A concrete nonproportional stationary pair and its extra U1 are retained
