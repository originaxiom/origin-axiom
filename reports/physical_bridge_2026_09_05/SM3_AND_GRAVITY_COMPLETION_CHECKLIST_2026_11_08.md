## 1. Three different levels of success

1. **A conditional physical model:** specify premises and construct one
   consistent theory with a controlled Standard Model low-energy sector
   containing exactly three chiral families, coupled to physical gravity.
   Supplied inputs are permitted but must be counted.
2. **An OA derivation:** earn the map from distinction and registering
   relations to that theory, remove independent inputs where possible,
   and establish what the principles force, permit or leave independent.
   A supplied E8 field theory is not this derivation by itself.
3. **The full overarching goal:** withstand mathematical, quantum and
   empirical audit, explain the remaining dynamics and scales, and make
   testable predictions. A low-energy SM-plus-Einstein action alone does
   not establish a complete ultraviolet theory or explain existence.

The adopted goal requires all three levels. It does not require pretending
that a dimensional choice of units is a physical prediction. Nor does it
require unique initial conditions unless their uniqueness is claimed.
Residual state or vacuum freedoms must be explicit, and cannot be called
derived observed parameters.

## 2. What SM3 denotes mathematically

Use SU(3)c x SU(2)L x U(1)Y Lie algebra and Q = T3 + Y. The global group,
charge lattice and normalization must be specified and justified; a
particular central quotient is a separate claimed result to audit.

In a convention writing all fermions as left-handed Weyl fields, each
family has:

| Field | Representation (colour, weak) and hypercharge |
| --- | --- |
| Q | (3, 2), +1/6 |
| u^c | (anti-3, 1), -2/3 |
| d^c | (anti-3, 1), +1/3 |
| L | (1, 2), -1/2 |
| e^c | (1, 1), +1 |

There must be exactly three physical family copies of this complete
roster, with the correct chiral weak interactions. The c labels here
describe the usual right-handed particles in left-handed notation;
they are not unwanted mirror families. The Higgs sector must reproduce
the observed electroweak breaking and Higgs interactions; the minimal
doublet has (1,2), +1/2. A realistic completion must also accommodate
neutrino masses and mixing. It must earn its chosen mechanism, rather
than declare a sterile-neutrino slot mandatory or sufficient.

A triplet of discriminant characters, a colour triplet, three coefficients
or three kernel vectors in one charge sector does not establish this
roster. Conversely, a zero index for surviving colour/electromagnetism
in a broken electroweak phase does not by itself exclude SM weak chirality.

In the active candidate the relevant task is to identify the actual
four-dimensional fields from the full weighted fermion operator, its
domain and spectrum, and evaluate the interactions of their normalized
wavefunctions with the actual gauge generators and vector modes.
Representation names must follow this operator/interaction map.
