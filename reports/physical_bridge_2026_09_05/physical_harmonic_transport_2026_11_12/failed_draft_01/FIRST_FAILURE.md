# First failure of the harmonic-transport draft

Preserved on 2026-11-15 while connecting the boundary law to the pending
physical operator map. The failure was found in author self-review
before the draft's full-E identification was committed. The original
acceptance criteria were already committed at `1aa76251f`.

The mode table and full-E identification in the archived draft omitted
the argument/deck action in the global section map. The local coefficient
intertwiner alone does not determine that map.

For equivariant sections `Psi(D_h x)=R_h Psi(x)`, an actual lift must
act as `U_e Psi(x)=B_e Psi(F_e^-1 x)`. For a natural inner lift with
`F_e=D_h` and `B_e=R_h`, the two factors cancel, so `U_e Psi=Psi`.
The proposed physical table retained a coefficient character without
its possible cancellation by this argument factor.

The weave action can retain an inner character because its supplied
intertwiner and complete coefficient local system differ by that
character. The physical register/reference data must decide the actual
lift. The earlier registered root/principal-parts construction already
retains nontrivial normal and argument factors in a different module.
It supplies a route for repair with its explicit field-type duty.

The local weighted-Hodge modes and norm calculations in the draft are
candidates for reuse, subject to their individual hypotheses and review.
They do not support its full-E physical-triplet conclusion as written.
This failure concerns that omitted global transport factor. It gives
no universal obstruction to a properly registered physical lift.

The archived verification brief was prospective and unrun. Its old
mode-table targets must be replaced after the actual field, argument,
spin and reference transport is determined. The custody JSON pins the
unchanged original proof, card, brief and relay.
