## Primary papers personally read

The four complete PDF text extractions, including appendices and

* [Wen, 1305.1045v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.1045v4), seven pages.
  The real ten-vector Yukawa and S9 argument motivate this candidate.
  The paper's gap argument includes a conjectural/expectational step;
  its fixed-configuration mass is not a proof of our interacting phase.
  Appendix B provides Clifford prior. Our conventions and exact
  intertwiner checks are written out rather than copied as a verdict.
* [You--BenTov--Xu, 1402.4151v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.4151v2),
  five pages. Interactions and defect proliferation offer a route
  beyond free doubling. Its symmetry choices, strong-coupling
  assumption and explicit limit on its SM claim are retained.
  Its discussion also makes the anomalous overall fermion U1 relevant;
  preserving that U1 cannot be silently assumed here.
* [Golterman--Shamir, 2603.15985v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15985v1),
  eight pages. The extension of doubling constraints is conditional
  on an effective Hamiltonian and a complete set of interpolating
  fields. It motivates composite-channel, current-response and
  continuum checks. It is NOT used as a universal SMG impossibility
  theorem; the paper itself gives tasks to test its hypotheses.
* [Araki--Fukaya--Onogi--Yamaguchi, 2608.29963v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.29963v1),
  21 pages. This is a two-dimensional Euclidean eight-Majorana FK
  domain-wall simulation, explicitly not a chiral gauge theory.
  Its mirror/physical spectral separation is useful methodological
  evidence, not four-dimensional Spin(10) evidence. Its appendices
  require uniform spectral/locality bounds for correlator decay;
  the main text retains finite-size/residual-mass duties. These
  qualifications inform the next-step acceptance tests.

The papers are used to propose a mathematically explicit interaction
and to set its verification standard. No empirical parameter or
