1. **Which distinctions must survive?** Separate formal word equality,
   gauge/redescription equivalence and operational equivalence. Identify
   the registering tests independently of the desired quotient.
2. **Which operations are native?** Derive or price why all elementary
   Nielsen moves are required at arbitrary rank. A surface's peripheral
   structure restricts its native moves; applying every bare free-group
   move can eliminate objects by a stronger category than they possess.
3. **What is minimized?** Compare local coefficient dimension, generating
   rank, information retained by the full register, number of independent
   relations and physical action. None is a substitute for the others
   without a proved relation under the same preserved data.
4. **What level is selected?** A minimal root/building block, an admissible
   family and an actual physical state/distribution are separate duties.
   A root result does not delete generated covers, sectors or interactions.
5. **Why one fixed sector?** Compare an individually fixed sector with
   a compatible moving family and a jointly irreducible act/register.
   If an additional locality/compression/composition requirement excludes
   the latter, exhibit the exclusion under its exact hypotheses.
