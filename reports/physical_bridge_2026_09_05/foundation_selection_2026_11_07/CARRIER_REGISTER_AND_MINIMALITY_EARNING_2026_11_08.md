## 2. A stronger countercontrol: infinite commutator depth is not free faithfulness

Write F=F(a,b), F'=[F,F], F''=[F',F'] and M=F/F''. This is the free
metabelian quotient; its defining kernel is fully invariant, hence
characteristic. Every
substitution descends, and automorphisms descend with inverse maps.

It is a proper quotient. The fork already gives an explicit nonempty
freely reduced word in F'' (the commutator of [a,b] with [a,b^2]) that
is killed. No new finite search is needed to prove properness. It has
abelianization Z^2, since F'' is contained in F'. The quaternion map
a->i,b->j factors through M because Q8's derived subgroup {I,-I} is
abelian. The pair commutator remains nontrivial by that map, with trace
-2. The actual common Q8 character and its intertwiners are therefore
unchanged by passing from F to M. The peripheral commutator's class under
the descended rank-two moves remains the image of the existing invariant
free-group class.

Despite being metabelian and nonfree, M has nonzero lower-central layers
at **every** depth. Here is a direct proof requiring no nilpotent census.

Let B=Z[t,t^-1] as an additive group and set

    W = B rtimes Z,
    (f,m)(g,n)=(f+t^m g,m+n),
    A=(0,1), B0=(1,0).

A is a shift and B0 is a single lamp; their conjugates generate every
Laurent monomial, so A,B0 generate W=Z wr Z. Its base is abelian and
its quotient Z is abelian, so W is metabelian. Hence a->A,b->B0 gives
a surjection M->W.

Using [x,y]=xyx^-1y^-1 in this note,

    [A,B0]=(t-1,0),
    gamma_k(W)=(t-1)^(k-1) B,       k>=2.

For the second equality, gamma2 is the ideal generated by all t^m-1,
namely (t-1)B. Commutation of a base element with a shift multiplies
it by t^m-1 (up to a unit/sign), and commutation with another base
element vanishes. Induction gives the formula. Cancellation of the
nonzero factor (t-1)^(k-1) in the Laurent integral domain gives

    gamma_k(W)/gamma_(k+1)(W) isomorphic to B/(t-1)B isomorphic to Z.

A surjective homomorphism maps every gamma_k onto gamma_k, so it also
surjects on the successive quotients. Consequently every
gamma_k(M)/gamma_(k+1)(M), k>=2, is nonzero. Gamma1/gamma2 is Z^2.

Thus the proposed conditions 'two generators', 'all descended moves',
'nonzero commutator', 'nonzero lower-central layers at every depth',
'a common irreducible Q8/SU2 coefficient sector' and 'infinitely many
histories' still admit this proper quotient. Equip M with its own
identity/nonidentity contextual register to obtain faithful registration
on M as well. This does NOT retain every distinct free signed word,
because that is exactly the extra requirement under investigation.

The fork's affine-path proof also implies that every positive word stays
distinct in M: its path homomorphism has metabelian image, so factors
through M; sorting positive path edges reconstructs each word. Reuse
that proof rather than declaring that M has lost positive order.

**Scope:** this refutes replacing free faithfulness with the weaker
condition that every lower-central quotient is merely nonzero. It does
not refute a requirement preserving every free-word distinction or
isomorphisms of the entire free lower-central quotients. Those stronger
requirements carry additional information. It also does not establish
the cohomology, metric, end law or physics on M.

This is classical relatively-free/wreath-product mathematics. Relevant
primary source inspected personally: Baumslag, Mikhailov and Orr,
*Localization, metabelian groups, and the isomorphism problem*, sections
1.2, 2.5, 3.1 and 9.1, https://arxiv.org/html/1301.5533v2 . Their
commutator convention is opposite to this note's; no sign-sensitive
formula is copied without adjustment. The Laurent calculation above is
self-contained. No claim of a full review of their telescope theorem
or of an OA discovery of free metabelian groups is made.

## 3. Recovered positive: the algebraic module specifies a measurable carrier duty

The Q8 point surviving M does not mean its doublet cohomology survives.
The fork's proof makes the distinction exact, which prevents both an
overclaim and a premature rejection of every smaller carrier.

Let rho:F->H be a supplied linear representation with solvable image
of derived length at most d. For a rho-cocycle z, the paired map

    w -> (rho(w),z(w)) in V rtimes H

is a homomorphism, since z(uv)=z(u)+rho(u)z(v). The kernel V is abelian,
so V rtimes H has derived length at most d+1. Hence the pair kills
F^(d+1), and every cocycle descends to F/F^(d+1). Conversely every
quotient cocycle pulls back; the coboundaries remain (rho(w)-I)v.
Thus the degree-one cohomology is exactly preserved by inflation. An
automorphism f and a supplied intertwiner g act by

    [z] -> [g^-1 z composed with f].

Characteristic kernels make this operation commute with inflation.
The full lifted move action, including any central phase, is preserved.

For Q8, d=2. Therefore F/F''' suffices for the entire supplied doublet
degree-one cohomology and its move action, not just its dimension. A
bare metabelian carrier does not suffice: normalize z(c)=0 for an
element c in the abelian derived subgroup with rho(c)=-I. For every
n in that subgroup, nc=cn gives z(n)=-z(n), hence z(n)=0. For any h,
hc h^-1 is again in that subgroup, and its cocycle is 2z(h), forcing
z(h)=0. Every cocycle was a coboundary.

The same proof gives the explicit difference: for the source Q8
matrices A=rho(a),B=rho(b), normalize z(a)=0, z(b)=v. Then
z([a,b])=(A+I)v and for r=[[a,b],a[a,b]a^-1],

    z(r)=2(I-A)(A+I)v=4v.

M imposes r=1 and kills every class; F/F''' retains it. This identifies
a **specific extra registering response** that distinguishes these
carriers for that module. It is more informative than asserting that
one must remember all words or that Q8 alone determines the carrier.

For the three supplied nontrivial parity doublets, the same argument
retains the algebraic six on F/F''' and its actual lifted moves. This
was already proved in the fork; it is not a new three-generation count.
Hodge polarization, a physical fermion operator, boundary domains and
interactions do not follow just from the degree-one isomorphism.

**Reduction accounting:** faithful F2 can be weakened to a specified
solvable quotient for these algebraic outputs alone. That is a genuine
conditional dependency reduction already in the record. The coefficient
system and physical carrier remain supplied, and the full architecture
may contain other tests that distinguish the groups. Nothing authorizes
discarding those tests or rewriting the total physical model as M.

## 4. What registration-preserving maps would actually force

There is a useful category-level earning test. Suppose each group G has
a binary register R_G:G->{0,1}, with R_G(identity)=0, and require

    R_H(f(g)) = R_G(g)

for **every** group homomorphism f:G->H. Taking the homomorphism to
the trivial group gives R_G(g)=0 for every g. There can be no nontrivial
register with this unrestricted equality requirement.

This is not a contradiction in existence or in the OA principle. It
shows that 'all legal maps preserve the distinctions' needs a specified
class of legal maps. If R_G is identity detection, a homomorphism f
preserves it in this strict sense exactly when ker f is trivial, i.e.
when f is injective. Isomorphisms and embeddings preserve all such
distinctions; noninjective coarse-grainings do not.

A quotient can still be valid as a less informative register if the
comparison law allows loss rather than claiming equality of all tests.
Alternatively, the full registering family may retain a separate test
for the lost kernel word. A coefficient representation with a kernel
cannot automatically stand in for that full family.

The analytic duty is therefore to derive or justify which maps are
description changes, physical processes, embeddings, coarse-grainings or
deletions. Uniformly calling them all 'allowed interactions' is too weak
to determine their information-preservation obligations.

## 5. The universal-carrier positive and its limitation

The fork already states the relevant sufficient condition. Fix a set of
named primitive operations S. If a group H generated by S has the property
that **every** assignment S->G to **any** group G extends uniquely to a
homomorphism H->G, then H is the free group F(S). The maps H->F(S) and
F(S)->H fixing the generators are inverse by uniqueness. This is a
universal completion theorem, not an inferred result of a few quotients
failing selected tests.

For n=2, it justifies F2 under its exact no-added-relations condition.
It does not derive S, the number of primitive operations, legality of
inverse events, all Nielsen moves or the surface/physical interpretation.
If there are justified native relations, the universal object belongs to
that presented category instead. A free syntax can be retained as a
universal description source while a family of representations realizes
different quotients; choosing one quotient is a separate operation.

The earlier minimum-dimension theorem therefore remains conditional:
minimizing a nonabelian **common fixed finite complex sector** over free
ranks yields dimension two and the Q8 point, and fixed ordinary characters
force determinant one. But the local-sector dimension objective does not
derive the full faithful carrier or justify why that is the cost to
minimize. A minimum building block also need not exhaust its generated
architecture.
