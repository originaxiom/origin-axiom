proof: the relative nine-generator cover register, the first lost order
and its second-order recovery, the native automorphism transport and

## Seven controls, with the mistake each must reject

1. **Cover and basis.** Recover the 8 vertices, 16 labelled positive
   edges and 9 non-tree generators. Rewrite their literal words back
   to themselves. Compare closed-word edge flow with the cycle expansion
   from Schreier exponents. Dropping inverse-edge orientation or treating
   backwards traversal as a second positive edge must disagree.
2. **Lost first-order order, recovered second order.** For alpha=a^4
   and beta=b^4, the endpoint and full edge chains of alpha beta and
   beta alpha agree. Their second-order series differ by the nonzero
   antisymmetric product of their first-order vectors. Independent
   integer Heisenberg matrices must read their commutator with central
   entry one. Removing the mixed product term gives zero and is rejected.
3. **Rank-36 layer.** The second coefficients of all 36 generator
   commutators have exact rational rank 36 in 81 tensor coordinates.
   Symmetrizing instead of antisymmetrizing must not pass the actual
   tensor-coefficient equality. This checks finite degree-two algebra,
   not an all-group dimension-subgroup conjecture.
4. **Higher-order and word controls.** A triple nested loop commutator
   is invisible at order two and nonzero at order three. Finite syllable
   examples with inverse powers have the explicitly derived nonzero
   coefficient. Wrong inverse truncation must fail. This is a finite
   guard for the hand separation proof, not a claim that order three
   is faithful.
5. **Native word transport and composition.** Build induced images of
   the actual nine Schreier words under L,R and compare substitution
   with rewriting after the original F-word automorphism. Include both
   composition orders and an explicit witness on which they differ;
   checking generators alone is insufficient. No new operation alphabet
   is selected by this check.
6. **Retained A5 countercontrol.** In the already-used exact permutation
   images a=(123), b=(12345), the comparison [a^4,b^4] is nonidentity
   although its relative first-order response vanishes. Its next nested
   commutator is nonidentity although its order-two response vanishes.
   This rejects calling those relative layers the whole finite register
   category. It is not a new A5 classification or physical gauge claim.
7. **Finite lifted joint control.** In an explicitly restricted inner-
   quaternion operation example Q8 rtimes Q8, retain the word and lift
   coordinates, their action and central sign. Check the semidirect law,
   its coefficient homomorphism and the nontrivial kernel of that
   coefficient projection. This rejects identifying a full joint state
   with one matrix image. It is an example, not verification of full
   E cofinality or of its 2O image.
