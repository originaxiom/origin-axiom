## Three different mathematical requirements

1. **Closure:** every allowed composition is in the description space.
   A free grammar or a constructed family can satisfy this. It supplies
   available histories, not a physical occurrence law.
2. **Realization:** every admissible state is realized in some compatible
   context. This is a stronger candidate requirement. If 'realized' means
   physically instantiated rather than mathematically represented, that
   bridge must be stated and justified; changing the word does not prove it.
3. **Dynamics and interactions:** occurrences, weights, relative amplitudes,
   and which compatible configurations actually couple. Coexistence does
   not uniquely determine these. In particular a permission to compose
   is not yet an energy, rate, coupling or complete physical action.

The word 'will' can import a time order before time has been derived.
The first two requirements admit a timeless formulation. A chronological
one needs the actual process/clock structure, not just a net group element.

## Why possibility does not uniquely supply frequency

This is a mathematical discriminator, not a proposed cosmological model.
For two states with every transition allowed, let

    P = [[1-p,p],[q,1-q]], 0<p,q<1.

All choices have the same allowed transitions. A stationary distribution
solves pi0 p=pi1 q, hence is (q,p)/(p+q). Both states recur with probability
one in this finite irreducible chain, yet their relative weights vary
continuously with p/q. Even an eventual-occurrence requirement leaves
that parameter unfixed.

Nor does positive probability at each opportunity alone imply eventual
occurrence. If opportunity n has event probability 2^(-n-1), n>=1, the
union bound gives Pr(ever)<=1/2, although every opportunity has positive
event probability. No computation or physical data enters these examples.
They identify the additional uniformity/recurrence assumptions needed
if 'will' is intended as an almost-sure temporal assertion.

A counting measure or uniform distribution is not automatically defined
on an infinite continuous family. Existing finite quotient Haar/cylinder
constructions retain their conditional scope and do not alone determine
a physical distribution over response classes or histories.
