## 1. A retained base point is not an embedded Hilbert-space sector

In the earlier construction, `X=Hom(F2,SU2)` and its quaternion anchor
rho are retained as mathematical data. The based/lifted action fixes
rho, and evaluation on continuous sections at rho recovers the original
coefficient action `f->rho(h)g f`. But Haar measure gives that single
point mass zero. Evaluation at rho is not a well-defined bounded map
on `L2(X,Haar;C2)`: changing a section at one point does not change its
L2 class. Thus the original quaternion fibre has NOT been exhibited
as a normalizable subrepresentation inside that Haar Hilbert space.

This must not be silently used as a physical recovery theorem. To retain
both the Haar family and an actual anchor Hilbert-space summand, one can
conditionally use

    m_t=(1-t)m+t delta_rho,  0<t<1,
    L2(X,m_t;C2) = L2(X,m;C2) plus C2

up to the stated positive rescalings. The measure is invariant because
the anchor is fixed. This is a declared sector completion, not a law
derived from the foundational premise or from covariance. A singular
point evaluation is also not a derived interaction between the two
summands; a physical coupling needs its own well-defined operator and
domain. No generation count follows from the two summands.

## 3. The current joint group action preserves every peripheral-trace leaf

There is a sharp analytical test of the hoped-for law of interaction.
Let B_F be multiplication by any bounded scalar function F(kappa) on
the family Hilbert space. The exact operators in the first note obey

    [B_F,M_h]=0,  [B_F,U_e]=0.                        (1)

The first equality holds because B_F is a scalar fibre multiplication;
the second follows from peripheral-trace invariance. Thus B_F commutes
with the norm-closed and strong-operator-closed algebras generated by
the word and lifted-operation actions. In particular every spectral
projection onto a measurable set of peripheral traces reduces this
entire represented algebra.

Consequently a dynamics restricted to those generated bounded operators
cannot move a state from one kappa range to a disjoint range. The same
statement holds for a self-adjoint Hamiltonian whose spectral projections
belong to that von Neumann algebra. It need not hold for additional
operators outside the algebra. Atoms such as the quaternion anchor
likewise remain separate under the displayed action.

This is not a superselection theorem for the whole physical programme.
It is a theorem about the specified representation and generated operator
algebra. A real field theory may have conjugate variables, end/source
responses or other operators that change kappa. They must be derived or
explicitly supplied and checked against the actual action/domain. Calling
all word and marking moves interactions cannot substitute for that map.

## 4. Why a natural Haar norm does not automatically provide a kinetic law

One might next equip X with an everywhere positive Riemannian metric
and call its Laplacian a canonical dynamics. The full marked action
does not admit such an invariant metric on all of X. This is a bounded
obstruction with an elementary proof, not a ban on physical dynamics.

The trivial representation K0 is fixed by every t_e. In generator
coordinates its tangent space is `su2 plus su2`. For a Nielsen shear,
say `a->a, b->ab`, the tangent map is the corresponding nonidentity
two-slot shear together with the common rotation Ad_g (or its inverse,
depending on the pullback convention). These commute. The quaternion
anchor intertwiners have finite adjoint order, so a suitable power removes
the common rotation while leaving a nontrivial unipotent shear.

An invariant positive metric at K0 would make that shear an isometry.
But its iterates send a vector (X,0) to (X,n m X), with m a nonzero
integer. Their norm is unbounded for every fixed positive-definite
inner product. An isometry has constant orbit norm, a contradiction.
Already at K0 the required metric fails to exist.

The scope is a smooth, nondegenerate, positive, everywhere-defined metric
on the entire based X with the full specified E acting as isometries.
Covariantly transported metrics, additional geometric variables, singular
or degenerate laws and smaller domains are outside this statement. The
old same-parent construction already transports metric and spin data
together; its positivity is not invalidated by this family-space test.
Nor does this prove that every possible invariant nonlocal kinetic
operator is absent.
