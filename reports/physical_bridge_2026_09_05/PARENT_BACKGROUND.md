## Outcome

The specified noncommuting connection solves the entire classical
complex-flatness and real moment-map system of the candidate E8 parent,
not only its central projection. Its positive Higgs norm is finite on
finite-volume hyperbolic quotients. The authored descent argument provides
the global unitary bundle given a chosen SL2 holonomy lift. This earns a
conditional classical parent background; it does not select that parent,
its spacetime, physical scale, source configuration or quantum vacuum.

The same calculation finds its costs: the full unbroken compact algebra
is so(11), not just the initially visible so(10), and its 16/conjugate-16
coefficient operators are unitarily paired on the matched domains.
This particular geometric background is not the desired chiral vacuum.
The result is not a no-go for non-SL2 deformations, sourced or boundary
completions. It neither retracts the exact nonsplit indices nor transfers
those different coefficient systems into this background.

## Global argument versus finite computation

The global descent uses the explicit SL2(C)/SU2 section and its unitary
compensators, not a compact-only existence theorem applied to a cusp.
The chosen holonomy lift is an input. Zero full residuals make the
displayed residual-square action stationary under regular compactly
supported variations in the whole E8 algebra. Boundary variations,
gravity, quantum corrections and a torsion-free G2 completion are not
included in that statement.

The unbroken commuting gauge sections have finite positive norm on a
finite-volume quotient. Complete-space cutoffs put them in the natural
quadratic-form closure; imposed Dirichlet data on a truncation would be
a different problem. J transports compactly supported sections and the
matched minimal/maximal L2 closures isometrically. This proves equality
of the paired spectra on those domains, not the value of their kernels.
These infinite-domain steps remain authored proofs, not independently
accepted theorems merely because the finite tests passed.

## What this changes for the full mission

The positive is a real same-action consistency step beyond representation
matching: a noncommuting candidate-parent background with a positive norm,
complete equations and an explicit physical operator comparison. It is
generic to the hyperbolic geometry, so it is not evidence selecting the
golden row or distinguishing it from the silver row or other quotients.

The next construction must change an identified mechanism, not just count
more algebraic characters. Preserve both paths:

1. For the sourced route, map the invariant subbundle, actual H-central
   source and all parent fields into one globally defined action. Derive
   the required projector balance with its correct sign, all noncentral
   equations and source/end boundary terms. R29's added stationary action
   remains valid on its own specification; this baseline is not its lift.
2. For a non-SL2 deformation, exhibit actual global monodromy/fields and
   a positive admissible metric/domain, then verify that the operator
   pairing and unwanted gauge sector really change. Adding a matrix to
   the invariance equations is insufficient.
3. In the surviving same model, derive the whole light spectrum and
   interactions, including extra gauge/matter/source/end sectors, before
   claiming selective mirror removal or anomaly completion. Three physical
   families, a controlled quantum phase, gravity and empirical predictions
   remain requirements of the full goal, not consequences of this test.
