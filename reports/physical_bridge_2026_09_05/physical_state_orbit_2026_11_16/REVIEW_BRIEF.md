## Physical proof duties

1. In PARALLEL_BACKGROUND_ORTHOGONAL_REDUCTION, check all four J metrics,
   the source factor sqrt(2), every differential sign, parallelness of
   delta and its actual adjoint, and the mixed anticommutators. Verify
   that the projection reduces the complete graph domain and that the
   complementary inverse covers the claimed entire charged block.
2. In CORRELATED_ORBITS_AND_DIRECT_ZERO_MODES, check both triples'
   highest-vector phases on full half-roots. Recompute the pure-spin,
   form and gaugino equations, exact cusp inequalities, global line
   transitions and divisor counts. Verify all three spin/state orbit
   types and both coherent-sign controls. Keep conjugates and continua.
3. In PHYSICAL_SPIN_MODE_TRIPLET_AND_RETAINED_LINE, verify the actual
   stabilizer, both seed characters, induced representation and displayed
   generator matrices. Check their full field inclusion, physical Gram,
   j mu^4 scalar, central sheet and difference from the native triplet.
   Do not assume the three background sectors are one local matter field.
4. In ACTUAL_SPIN_VERTEX_CURRENT_AND_WILSON_CROSSING, verify the dual
   compact phases, opposite delta signs and both terms of the polarized
   vertex. Check its product line, nonlinear integrability, source and
   vector normalizations, operator derivative and exact count near the
   Wilson crossing. Check the full word phases and bilinear finite action.
5. In FINITE_BULK_CONNECTIONS_QUARTIC_AND_REAL_GAUSS, verify fixed-spin
   gluing and the relative bundle argument, the literal common tail,
   residual integrability and each quadratic endpoint identity. Check
   B's coefficients, the real adjoint, the 1/(8 ell^2) gauge Hardy bound,
   complete-form realization and kernel-complement inverse. Retain the
   missing uniform enhancement estimate and transverse interface equations.

Rejecting controls include unequal spin signs, the bare lift, a
nonparallel Higgs, changed one-half connection norm, same-sign charge
in the polarization, erased central sheet, nontrivial nearby Wilson
character, different spin bundles without a transport, a path with
nonzero transverse residual, and treating the gauge Hardy bound as a
gap for the distinct physical spin-fermion operator.
