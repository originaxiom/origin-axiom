## 1. The action and the price of choosing it

Choose compact E6 gauge symmetry on four-dimensional Lorentzian spin spacetime,
with positive kinetic terms, two complex scalar 27s phi_1, phi_2, one real
adjoint A, and N_f left-handed Weyl 27 fermions. This **compact real form and
physical interpretation are inputs**; they are not identified with the banked
noncompact real forms by matching dimensions. N_f is also an input, not the
number of states in an internal representation.

Use Hermitian generators T and `D_mu=partial_mu-i g B_mu^a T_a`. In the 27
matrix realization, `A -> U A U^dagger`. With a positive invariant adjoint
kinetic metric and the usual spinor contraction, the Lagrangian is

```text
L = L_Yang-Mills + sum_a (D_mu phi_a)^dagger D^mu phi_a
    + 1/2 Tr(D_mu A D^mu A) + i sum_i psi_i^dagger barsigma^mu D_mu psi_i
    - V
    - [1/2 sum_a Y^a_ij d_ABC psi_i^A psi_j^B phi_a^C + h.c.].

V = sum_a lambda_a (phi_a^dagger phi_a - v_a^2)^2
    + kappa |phi_1^dagger phi_2|^2
    + eta sum_(a<=b) ||C(phi_a,phi_b)||^2
    + lambda_A (Tr A^2 - w^2)^2
    + zeta sum_a ||A phi_a||^2,

C(u,v)_A = d_ABC u_B v_C.
```

All six displayed quartic coefficients are strictly positive. This is a
particular potential, **not the most general E6-invariant potential**. Other
allowed terms are set to zero at the model's defining scale. Their radiative
generation and the stability of this restriction have not been calculated.
The gauge coupling, three scale parameters and symmetric complex family
matrices Y^1,Y^2 are independent inputs. For three families those matrices
contain 24 real entries before field redefinitions, **not a claim of 24
independent physical observables**. Even the dimensionless scale ratios are
inputs; choosing units removes only one overall unit.

The family symmetry of Y is dictated by the conventional Lorentz/Weyl
contraction with the symmetric cubic. The banked internal E8 family tensor
has not been silently substituted for this physical Yukawa coupling.
No CP, flavor, cosmological or gravitational prediction is being claimed.

## 5. Fermion masses: mix first, label the light states second

For one family, absorb the Yukawa couplings and VEV magnitudes into complex
s,n. Contraction with `s S+n N` gives a symmetric 27-by-27 mass matrix of rank
10 whenever `(s,n)` is nonzero. The extracted color block is 3-by-6 and the
weak block 2-by-4, because both 5bars are included. Exactly,

```text
M_D M_D^dagger = (|s|^2+|n|^2) I3,
M_L M_L^dagger = (|s|^2+|n|^2) I2.
```

There are five equal Dirac masses `sqrt(|s|^2+|n|^2)`: one complete 5+5bar.
The remaining **17 massless Weyl components** are a chiral 10+5bar and two
singlets, at this pre-electroweak stage. The light 5bar is the kernel of the
rectangular block, not necessarily the originally named 5bar in the 16.

matter 5bar, leaving the other 5bar light.

the light 5bars with a positive, orthonormal kinetic metric. The new
