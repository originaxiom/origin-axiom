# Historical domain choices and symmetry requirements

The earlier constructions already separate several apparent walls from
the hypotheses that produce them. Changing a base metric can recover
a finite-energy background; adding a genuine boundary can admit a
nonsplit coefficient; resolving source cores can instead add compensating
states. These are different mathematical problems, not contradictory
answers to a single fixed physical theory. Their supplied choices remain
derivation targets, not a prescription to combine every alternative.

and separately coded reference/control sources of the flat-domain,

FLAT_DOMAIN_AUDIT.md and CS_SECTOR_SCOPE.md were read; the already mapped
PARENT_CUSP_PROOF.md was reread without another coverage row. No scientific
producer was imported or executed. Authored analytic applications and
