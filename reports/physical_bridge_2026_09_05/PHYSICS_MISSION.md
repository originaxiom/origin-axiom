## Research discipline

- Distinguish axioms, conventions, physical inputs, conditional theorems,
  numerical evidence and independently reviewed results at their use.
- Keep membership, equivalence, dynamics and physical realization separate.
  Closure under declared operations is not a proof that all states coexist.
- Transport the whole hypothesis set before reusing either a positive
  result or a no-go: carrier, coefficient, action, metric, background,
  symmetry, source/end law, operator domain and limit.
- Combine results only through demonstrated physical maps. One coherent
  theory need not occupy one internal manifold; an ingredient list is
  not a theory. Cohomology transport alone does not transport an action.
- Distinguish a law's symmetry from the symmetry of a solution. No unique
  invariant representative is not itself a no-chiral-phase theorem.
- Use observations throughout as constraints. Declare calibration inputs
  and reserve consequences not used in construction for predictive tests.
  Do not retrospectively relabel inserted values as predictions.
- Search existing bodies, producers, synonyms, history and relevant
  branches before announcing an absence, new result or universal closure.
  A fetched or read result is not thereby independently verified.
- Follow the pre-compute protocol and seal significant two-outcome probes
  before execution. Preserve raw outputs and failed first attempts.
- Preserve and regularly push work on the audit lane. Do not silently
  edit another seat's branch, allocate its identifiers, or claim a full
  main-bank certificate from focused tests.

Every new research task must reduce a load-bearing physical input,
establish a necessary same-theory connection, or test an observable.
Supporting mathematical and maintenance work must name which duty it serves.

## Completion criteria

These are obligations, not claims that every imaginable alternative has
already failed. Inputs are allowed in a conditional physical theory;
they remain unearned parts of a claimed derivation from the principles.

- [ ] Operational foundation: explicit data, legal moves, equivalences,
  realizations and completeness relative to that grammar. Account for
  information lost by quotients and what is physically observable.
- [ ] Physical interpretation and dynamics: states versus redundancy,
  observables, spacetime/causal interpretation, evolution, positive
  physical norms, quantum probabilities, and action or equivalent laws.
  Derive or explicitly price sources, boundaries and operator domains.
- [ ] Low-energy particle theory: chiral representations, three physical
  generations, hypercharges/global gauge structure, anomaly consistency,
  Higgs dynamics, normalized interactions, masses, mixing, CP violation
  and neutrino physics in one controlled construction. Account for extra
  particles, mirrors, thresholds and the validity of the reduction.
- [ ] Gravity and quantum consistency: actual gravitational dynamics
  coupled to matter, tested classical limits and controlled quantum
  consistency. Spin-two representation content alone does not discharge it.
  Cosmological observations and scales remain constraints, not exemptions.
- [ ] Predictive contact: explicit nonredundant input budget, controlled
  uncertainties, fixed-prescription comparisons and distinctive observable
  consequences not used to select or calibrate the construction.
- [ ] Independent review: load-bearing mathematical/analytic arguments,
  physical identifications and empirical comparisons withstand scrutiny.
  Local tests do not certify a theory of everything or the full repository.
