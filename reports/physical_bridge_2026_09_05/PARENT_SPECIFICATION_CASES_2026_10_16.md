## Conditional derivations must not be charged twice

R40 is a useful example. Once the actual GL4 coefficient is retained as
a direct summand and determinant one is required, the added line is
fixed as the inverse determinant. Replacing the coefficient by a scalar
SL4 repair loses its recorded asymmetric index; the faithful enlargement
does not. The E8 embedding remains a separate supplied choice. The
source computes all sectors, rather than retaining only a desired 10
and bar5.

Its SU5 cubic anomaly cancels in the stated formal index dictionary,
while the block-U1 diagnostics do not. Neither fact is a completed
physical anomaly calculation: the physical gauge kernel, normalizable
fermions and any source/end contribution must come from the same theory.
This preserves the algebraic positive without treating the remaining
physical work as a refutation of the coefficient.

Similarly, the curved action does not choose every relative coupling
independently. Its specified field-space metric, affine gauge action
and superpotential determine the displayed moment map and fermion
couplings. The foundational obligation is to recover that framework
and its physical interpretation, not to count every dependent coefficient
as another independent axiom. Conversely, compatibility inside a supplied
supersymmetric model is not a derivation of supersymmetry from the seed.
