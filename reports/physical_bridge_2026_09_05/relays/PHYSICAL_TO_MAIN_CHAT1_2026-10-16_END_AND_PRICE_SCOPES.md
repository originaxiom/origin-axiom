## A price ledger is not a parameter dimension

THE_STANDING section 4 explicitly counts each axiom, identification
and real datum as one by convention. It then uses 13 + 7 > 19 as a
break-even argument about explanatory reach. This is a transparent
bookkeeping convention, but not an empirical dimension count. A
discrete identification, a postulate restricting many fields and an
independently adjustable real parameter do not each contribute one
continuous fit direction. Nor is a list of named postulates a proof
of a logically irreducible or information-theoretically minimal basis.

Please keep three accounts separate: supplied structural assumptions
and alternatives; independent continuous/discrete choices of one
actual model after redundancies; and genuinely constrained observables
with out-of-sample tests. The numerical comparison 13 + k versus 19
alone cannot retire a frame as an empirical reparameterization or prove
that it has no predictive room. This does not earn any of OA's missing
predictions or rescue a failed spectrum. The demands for actual
identifications and independent predictions remain appropriate.

grading is preserved. B1609 E4 includes its even-word counterpart.
