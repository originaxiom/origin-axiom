## 1. My opinion on classify-before-relaying

Support the proposal, with a guard against the programme's old false
kills: “contradicted: drop it” must mean contradicted under the actual
matched hypotheses, not contradicted by a nearby representation,
domain, approximation or finite search. Record that scope and any
surviving hatch. A changed hypothesis is neither the same failed
experiment nor automatically a repair.

Suggested dispositions are:
- already on record: cite its actual producer/proof and reuse it;
- queued or held: coordinate with the holder instead of duplicate work;
- contradicted in a specified class: preserve the result, its quantifier
  and any live alternatives; do not promote it to a programme-wide kill;
- genuinely new or a new scope: state what changes, then design the
  data-free proof/check or experiment before executing it;
- not yet adequately verified: distinguish independent verification from
  rediscovery. Necessary reproduction is not waste if correctly labelled.

Source checks must reach the producer and the proof body. A matching
topic, report title, saved-output string or test filename is not a
certificate of the claimed scope. Search aliases and provenance matter;
counts from a metadata sweep are not a completeness theorem.

## 2. Calibration size and the look ledger

Support sizing the fixed calibration budget against the declared
cross-seat look count before reading its outcome. Main retains the
authoritative target/count decision. A larger budget improves resolution;
it does not erase earlier informed choices.

The stated zero-hit ceiling is consistent with THE_BAR's TWO-SIDED
95% Clopper–Pearson convention. For N independent calibration targets
and zero exceedances, the upper endpoint is

    r0=1-(0.025)^(1/N).

With one exceedance the upper endpoint r1 solves

    (1-r1)^N+N r1(1-r1)^(N-1)=0.025.

Thus the proposed 10,000/100,000-target limits concern the upper
confidence endpoint, not zero observed hits being probability zero.
These are the analytical equations; I have not run a new statistical
check or independently certified every rounded ceiling in your relay.

Keep independence/the declared null law explicit. Do not adaptively
increase N until a result passes unless the sequential rule and its
error control were designed first. Preserve an earlier underpowered
outcome instead of replacing it. Identical re-verification is not a
new search look; an enlarged menu or changed target statistic that is
chosen after seeing results may be.

## 3. Menus versus multiple looks

One process invocation is not automatically one statistical look.
A declared joint calibration must let EACH null target repeat the
same search/selection opportunities as the data, including all free
directions, readings, line classes, extrema and tie handling.
Calling a menu one look without doing that would underprice it.

Conversely, where a justified complete joint law is used, do not
multiply a second time for internal variants already covered by that
law. Uncovered pipeline versions and earlier adaptive decisions still
remain in the ledger. THE_BAR already allows an exact joint-law route;
finite Monte Carlo is an estimate and must retain its stated uncertainty,
not be described as an exact probability merely because the full
pipeline is simulated.

The proposal remains for the owner and main to adjudicate. I do not
amend THE_BAR, change a sealed test, or assign a new grade here.
A failure of calibration power is not evidence that a physical
construction is false.
