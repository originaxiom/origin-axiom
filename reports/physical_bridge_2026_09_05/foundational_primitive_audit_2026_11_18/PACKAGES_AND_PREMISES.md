# What the proposed primitive requires, before choosing its realization

2026-11-18. Bounded author analytical comparison under scope `6941e2b1b`.
Not independently checked. No scientific code, CAS or test ran. This is
not an amendment to canonical GENESIS or a physical existence theorem.

## The question being decided

Does distinction with registration and compositional consistency force
the present grammar, or is additional selection content required?
The verdict below applies to the explicit packages in this file. It is
not a verdict on every possible meaning of existence or registration.

Canonical GENESIS v1.69 at main `ce0c6ea90` retains PF1-PF3 as postulates
and explicitly states that PF2 has not been derived from PF1. GM1's two
records, the target and its fixed-character conditions retain their
input status. This is the current baseline, not a new absence finding.
The November 7 earning audit already names the register-selection duty.

## 1. Three readings, separated into mathematical questions

| Reading | Minimal mathematical interpretation for this audit | What is additional |
|---|---|---|
| Cancellation | Some processes admit inverses; compare a composite with the identity on its stated domain. A failed cancellation can mean a nonidentity composite. | Which processes are legal, what the full state contains, and why nonidentity means physical existence. |
| Registered distinction | A supplied compositional system has at least one distinction detectable through a stated registering test in an admitted context. | The system, contexts and test family; nontriviality itself; any choice of alphabet, rank or coefficient field. |
| Internal registration | A register is a part of the model's state. Coupled updates can change it in response to another part, and its data can affect subsequent admitted updates. | A state decomposition and coupling law. These need not define a conscious observer, an external reader, an irreversible memory or a selected state. |

These readings are not mutually exclusive theories. The internal models
below realize registered distinctions and failed object-level cancellation
while allowing full cancellation of a complete process. A change of
wording is therefore not, by itself, a change of mathematical predictions.

The phrase "an act of asking" has no separate formal axiom here. A
mathematical question can be represented by a test or family of tests,
but their domain, alternatives and coupling still need specification.
No intentional agent or subjective experience is inferred from that map.

## 2. Precisely declared comparison packages

For an operational illustration, supply a nonempty state set S, admitted
updates and their domains, and a family of readout functions on S. Legal
finite histories compose as functions where defined. This representation
is an explicit comparison setting, not a deduction that every physical
theory must be a classical deterministic state machine.

**Registered-distinction package.** Require at least two admitted
histories or states to be distinguishable by one declared test in one
legal surrounding context. Retain the whole context when composing.
Use the existing AR1/AR2 adequate-quotient rule; do not add that every
formal word difference is physically observable.

**Internal-registration strengthening.** In addition, supply components
within S and an admitted coupling which writes one component in response
to another. Require an admitted context in which this register data can
affect the other component. Readout roles are allowed to move with a
declared interchange of the components. No additional external dynamical
observer is required by these conditions.

**Reversible strengthening.** Require all the named primitive updates
and their inverses to be admitted on the same complete S. This is an
optional stronger package, not a consequence of registration alone.

**Ledger completeness.** In this audit it means every finite legal word
is included in the mathematical account. It assigns no probabilities or
amplitudes. It does not mean that every word is realized in a physical
time sequence or that every infinite history has a limit. A formal ledger
is not automatically an extra physical memory component.

The explicit cyclic-register models in the proof meet all four conditions.
Their readouts are functions of internal coordinates; they contain no
separate dynamical outside reader. This is operational internality only,
not a model of awareness, experience or an autonomous laboratory device.
The proof also carries unbounded integer-register and directly interacting
d-component extensions. Infinite syntax, unbounded registered distinctions
and PF2's philosophical inexhaustibility retain separate meanings.

## 3. Do not identify these five uses of "nothing"

| Mathematical item | Actual meaning here |
|---|---|
| The empty word | No update in that history; the state set and its identity map are still specified. |
| The identity update | No net change of the complete specified state. |
| A zero coordinate | One allowed value in an already specified system. |
| No registered difference | Indistinguishability for stated tests and contexts; it can depend on which data were discarded. |
| Absolute physical nothingness | Not represented or defined by any of the previous four items in this audit. |

Consequently a nonidentity commutator may establish an order-sensitive
relation within a model. It does not, without a further physical argument,
establish why there is a model or a universe. An identity does not imply
the disappearance of its carrier. No contradiction is derived from an
empty physical universe: such an object has not been specified here.

## 5. Outcomes at the declared scope

The companion hand proofs give the following dispositions, pending
non-authoring checking:

1. Registration and complete reversibility are compatible. A record
   surviving only an object undo need not survive the whole inverse.
2. An additional internally interacting register can be necessary for
   an adequate reduced description, with a concrete distinguishing
   context. No infinite tower of external readers is needed for that
   particular example.
3. Binary and nonbinary internal registers, as well as directly interacting
   two- and three-component versions, satisfy these packages. Unbounded
   integer-register extensions remain available. The packages do not
   select the number of outcomes or components. These are not the same
   question as the minimal rank of a supplied complex representation.
4. One such noncommuting internal model has the finite Heisenberg update
   group; in the binary case its two-update subgroup is dihedral, not Q8.
   The Heisenberg alternative was already a November 7 control. Here its
   internal registration/undo implementation is made explicit. This
   does not refute the stronger fixed-SL2-character theorem.
5. Even a unique stationary distribution, complete finite-history
   support and maximal state-relabeling symmetry can leave a family of
   transition laws. This tests stationary closure, not the undefined
   stronger whole-reading functional of GENESIS PF5.

These are non-implications for the explicitly weak packages, not a claim
that the foundational project cannot succeed. A stronger meaning requires
stronger, independently justified hypotheses with their consequences tested.

## 6. The next consequential dependency

The next question is what fixes the **admitted registering tests and
composition law**, rather than only a minimal representation after they
are supplied. No condition may be justified solely by recovering Q8,
E8, a preferred carrier or three families. The fixed-sector and moving-
family alternatives in NEXT_EARNING_DUTY.md remain relevant controls.

For the physical route, the first bridge remains a map from an explicit
registering mechanism to fields and a common action, including allowed
source/end variations and complete fermion domains. The existing E8
operator calculations test such a supplied realization; their fields,
kinetic norm and action cannot serve as an upstream proof of the primitive.

No foundational input is counted removed. No existing conditional
positive or scoped negative is erased, and no canonical wording is changed.
