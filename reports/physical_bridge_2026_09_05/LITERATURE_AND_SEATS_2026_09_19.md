## 1. Friedmann-Witten: rescue the framework's scope, not a prediction

I personally read all 41 pages of v3, including both appendices and
references, and compared the load-bearing v2 passages used by xB029.
The relevant zero-mode treatment is already present in v2.

The source defines a nonzero-spectrum quantity

\[
 K_i=\tfrac32\log\det{}'(\Delta_{0,i}/\Lambda^2)
       -\tfrac12\log\det{}'(\Delta_{1,i}/\Lambda^2).
\]

Its first equality in (3.13) uses this quantity in the KK threshold;
(3.8) separately counts massless states. Appendix B relates torsion
and this determinant through the metric normalization of harmonic
cohomology bases. Finite fundamental group is the paper's sufficient
condition eliminating nontrivial twisted zero modes, not the definition
of the primed determinant. The simple volume-only correction uses a
restricted zero-mode pattern. These facts occur in sections 3.1--3.3
and Appendix B of [Friedmann-Witten, v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0211269v3).

**Our receiving conclusion:** xB029 correctly withdraws a physical
inference based on inserting a homological torsion number alone.
Its addendum already mentions `K_i`; it would be false to claim that
this audit discovered a term that seat had never noticed. But its
blanket exclusion of the threshold framework for hyperbolic Q is
stronger than its evidence. Finding some non-acyclic characters defeats
uniform acyclicity, not every choice of character or the determinant
framework with its massless sector retained.

This does NOT reinstate L225's physical conclusion, a preferred tower
level, a unification scale, or a Standard-Model-only massless spectrum.
The next admissible calculation needs a specified **closed compact** Q,
its physical unitary flat bundle, the actual massless representations,
the nonzero spectrum and its metric data. A G2 realization is another
duty. Finite H1, finite pi1, b1=0 and compactness are distinct conditions.
For cusped m004 or m202 the closed discrete-spectrum determinant is not

## 2. T-branes: a real local mechanism, with its global conditions intact

I personally read all 47 pages and all three figures of
[Barbosa et al., v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.02212v2).
The local PW system admits noncommuting Higgs fields with gauge
curvature balancing their commutators. Section 2 explicitly requires
semisimple monodromy in its Donaldson-Corlette discussion. Section 3
constructs local backgrounds; section 4 treats zero modes and their
normalizability. Section 4.1 states additional analytic assumptions
for noncompact harmonic representatives and does not prove the required
correction exists generally. Section 5 labels its algebraic prescription
conjectural. These are not a global index theorem for our cusp/source
domain, nor a construction of our compact physical vacuum.

**Our inference and use:** a diagonal commuting ansatz cannot exclude
the entire fluxed PW mechanism. Conversely, the word T-brane cannot
turn the nonsemisimple modules in the received tower census into
admissible backgrounds. Nilpotence of a local Higgs component and
nonsemisimplicity of the global flat representation are different
statements. For singular or bounded problems one must state the
appropriate boundary stability/metric theorem rather than invoking
the closed theorem without its hypotheses.

The concrete lead is a noncommuting SU(2)-by-U(1) local core inside the
same charged-source problem: solve both F and D equations, match the
outside solution, retain the actual H-subgroup representations, and
compute the global positive-L2 operator with its domain. This is a
candidate refinement of the source completion, not a reason to discard
R29--R34 or to count a local matter ring as three physical families.

One small source consistency issue illustrates the required standard.
On printed page 24, (4.22) has `(partial_t+4qt) psi_t=0`, while
(4.23)'s displayed Gaussian has a `q t^2` exponent. Direct substitution
leaves `2qt psi_t`; with that ODE fixed, the t exponent should be
`2q t^2`. The PDF page was inspected visually, not just extracted text.
This is an elementary, after-reading check, not a preregistered new
experiment. It does not change the stated sign of normalizability and
does not refute the local mechanism. Preserve this observation as a
prior if a later exact local benchmark is sealed.

## 3. There is a geometric bridge to hyperbolic bases, but not yet our metric

The whole of section 3, plus a selected introduction passage, of
[Gukov-Yau-Zaslow, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0203217v1)

are a different construction. Descent requires compatible integral
period-lattice data, not just an abstract SO(3,1) inclusion.

For our programme this supplies an explicit geometric starting point
for the arithmetic-parent idea: exhibit the integral action and total
fibration, then solve the torsion-free or explicitly sourced equations.

whose stated flat-connection/Ricci-flat correspondence uses compact
