# A one-return flat E8 history with precisely the connected SM gauge group

20 November 2026. Author hand construction. The obstruction at 236900847 applies to holonomy containing the full geometric SL(2,25). Here we keep that information in the register and test a different, honest one-return quotient for the gauge connection. The goal is to compute the whole E8 stabilizer, including generators outside a putative SU(5) sector. This is a conditional gauge-phase construction, not a genesis selection of the vacuum.

## A projective field-of-five return, checked on both handles

Use the B67 fibre generators A,D with return tAt⁻¹=A²D and tDt⁻¹=AD. Over F5 choose

    A = [[4,1],[0,4]],     D = [[0,3],[3,4]],     g = [[0,1],[3,1]].

The first two determinants are 1 and det g=2. Directly A²D=[[4,0],[3,4]], AD=[[3,1],[2,1]], and gA=(A²D)g, gD=(AD)g. Thus t→[g] really extends the two handles to a representation of the entire group in PGL(2,5). Their trace triple (3,4,4) lies on the fixed curve y=z=x/(x−1). The commutator trace is 1 mod five by x²+y²+z²−xyz−2, so this example does not contradict the no-unipotent-puncture result at 4d379d485. The handle group contains the full order-five upper translation subgroup generated by [A]. Conjugating it by [D], which moves ∞ to a finite point, then translating that finite point to zero gives the opposite unipotent subgroup. Elementary operations generate PSL(2,5). Adjoining the nonsquare-determinant g gives all PGL(2,5). The two determinant square classes are exactly the fibre and return cosets.

The choice is inspectable rather than an unmarked search: fixed triples over F5 have x=0,2,3,4 as at 4d379d485. The reported pair exhibits the x=3 point. This proof does not classify or select the active point among all generated carriers, primes or registers. It is also not the five-sheet D5 quotient. If retained together with SL(2,25) as an external record, the two full quotients have product image: Goursat's common quotient must be perfect, and any nontrivial quotient of SL(2,25) maps onto its projective simple group of order 7800, larger than |PGL(2,5)|=120.

## The meridian supplies a 3+2 involution

Let the projective group act by permutations on its six projective lines. Remove the constant line in the real permutation module, obtaining an orthogonal five-space. Twist it by the permutation sign to obtain σ in SU(5)_B: on a five-space that twist cancels the original determinant. On the fibre PSL(2,5) is perfect and the twist is trivial. Its action on the six lines is doubly transitive: it is transitive, and the translations fixing ∞ act transitively on the five finite points. Its permutation commutant has dimension two (diagonal and off-diagonal orbits), hence the five-space is absolutely irreducible and nontrivial. It carries an invariant real symmetric form and no invariant alternating form.

The eigenvalue ratio η of g satisfies η+η⁻¹=tr(g)²/det(g)−2=1 mod five. It has order six; the discriminant of g is 3, a nonsquare. Every nontrivial projective power has the same two eigenlines outside F5, since it is nonscalar. Consequently g acts as a six-cycle. Its cube acts by three transpositions; on the six-space it has three plus and three minus eigenvalues. Removing the positive constant leaves two plus and three minus. Twisting by its odd sign leaves **three plus and two minus**. In the B67 marking t is conjugate to the peripheral base loop a⁻¹t, so the same conjugacy class comes from the meridian. Let P_A be a transported copy of this involution on the defining five of a separate regular SU(5)_A. Transporting its orthonormal frame changes only SU(5)_A conjugacy. This supplies its conjugacy class without entering a splitting angle or manually assigning the eigenspaces. Using that involution in a *second gauge factor* is still a model choice.

## Full gauge stabilizer

In the commuting regular SU(5)_A and SU(5)_B of E8 use the homomorphism

    h ↦ (P_A^ε(h), σ(h)),

where h denotes the PGL(2,5) value and ε its determinant square-class bit. This is a genuine group action because ε is a homomorphism. It gives a flat E8 connection on the knot exterior, and a flat zero-residual solution of the supplied fibre action at Q=R=0. The two SU(5) factors form their usual central product in E8. The SU(5)_A factor itself embeds faithfully by the cocharacter lattice argument in 236900847.

On the fibre, the image lies entirely in B. Under the regular pair, the adjoint branches as (24,1)+(1,24)+(10,5)+(dual 10,dual 5)+(dual 5,10)+(5,dual 10). The 5 of B has no invariant and its 10=Λ²5 has no invariant by the preceding real irreducibility argument. Its traceless endomorphisms have no invariant by Schur's lemma. The only remaining generators are exactly (24,1). This checks every direction, dimensions 24+24+4×50=248. On the full return the invariant Lie algebra is precisely the centralizer of P_A *in that twenty-four*, namely s(u(3)⊕u(2)). The connected subgroup is exactly S(U(3)×U(2)), isomorphic to (SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1))/Z6. There may be disconnected stabilizers; no uniqueness claim about the whole centralizer's components is needed for this connected result. The Lie algebra is unbroken under kinetic parallel transport of gauge vectors. On the complete finite-area fibre the 24 parallel vectors have finite norm; requiring the flat history to return leaves 12. Counting the history-invariant vectors as the physical four-dimensional gauge fields needs a physical return prescription. The untwisted single-fibre parent alone has 24. The regular branching is also recorded in [Karozas et al., E8 decomposition](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-020-08794-y).

This improves the D5 graph control: there the irreducible pieces of dimensions 1,2,2 in the second five left extra block phases. Here the secondary five is irreducible and all mixed E8 coefficients have been checked. The standard hypercharge line is the traceless scalar on each P_A eigenspace, with ratio −2:3. Neither its physical gauge coupling nor the measured charge normalization is fixed by that ratio.

## Fermion and selection controls

Every holonomy lies in the product of real SO(5) subgroups in the two defining fives (in suitable fixed bases). There is a charge-exchanging E8 gauge transformation preserving the entire background: the Weyl element −I on the E8 Cartan preserves the two A4 root subsystems and acts on each SU(5) by complex conjugation up to an inner automorphism. Compensate those two inner automorphisms using the commuting factors; the ambiguity in the common centre does not affect this action. The resulting transformation fixes the real matrices pointwise, exchanges each SM representation with its conjugate and flips hypercharge. Its complex-linear adjoint acts on gauge coefficients without reversing external Weyl chirality and preserves any gauge-natural domain on this zero-matter background. Thus the charged adjoint spectra occur in conjugate pairs in those domains. The presence of the connected SM gauge group has not generated net chiral families.

Growing the holonomy from the fibre to a single marked return, retaining the full arithmetic register elsewhere, and assigning ε to the A involution simultaneously are central decisions of the construction. None is a variational derivation that the universe chooses this history. The model demonstrates that a one-return gauge alternative can preserve the exact connected SM inside the parent. It leaves physical selection, chirality, coupling normalization and dynamical geometry open. Hand proof for independent review; no scientific run or measured input.
