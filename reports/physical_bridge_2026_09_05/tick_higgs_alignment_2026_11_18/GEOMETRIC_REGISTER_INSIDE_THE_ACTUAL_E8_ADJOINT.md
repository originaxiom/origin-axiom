Thus all 64 spin weights are sums of one nonzero vector on each of three distinct lines in k. ## Scope of the selector test

The 156-dimensional centre-odd triplet carrier at 089ed7820 cannot fit inside the 128 centre-odd directions of **this** adjoint. More generally any compact E8 involution has fixed algebra of type D8 or A1E7, unless it is the identity. Thus in any such single-adjoint realization its minus eigenspace has dimension 128, 112 or zero, and equivariant injection of the entire 156 is impossible. The D8 case just exhibited is enough for this calculation; the general classification is independent input for the wider bound. [Burness, Guralnick and Saxl, involution centralizers](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/ws/files/51512668/base_algebraic31.pdf).

The connection and each Q,R slot are single adjoints, and their existing cubic is an adjoint bracket. This rules out identifying the complete 156 with a subspace of any one slot, by dimension in a *fixed central character*. It does not rule out a composite observable, several coupled slots, a smaller eigenspace of an induced operator, a different geometric representation, or a separate matter field with a justified coupling. No claim that every E8 embedding has this one's gauge centralizer or harmonic counts. The mathematical reduction is a concrete allowed flat E8 background without choosing two unrelated handle matrices after the lift.
