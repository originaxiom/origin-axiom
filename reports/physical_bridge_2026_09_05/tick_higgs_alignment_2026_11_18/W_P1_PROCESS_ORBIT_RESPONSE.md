## 2. Averaging is a different operation with different retained data

One can choose to average the rank-one coefficient projector over the
finite orbit. Averaging its six even translates first eliminates the
off-diagonal entry, since exp(2i theta) is a primitive sixth root.
It leaves diag(|z_+|^2,|z_-|^2). The six odd translates exchange its
diagonal entries. Consequently the normalized full average is

    (1/12) sum_(k=0)^11
      |Sigma^k h><Sigma^k h|/N = I_E/2.              (3)

Equation (3) is a mixed coefficient projector, not a pure ray on the
equal-magnitude circle. Its rank is two, whereas every pure state
projector has rank one. Thus the averaging prescription does not give
the proposed pure-state direction reduction. Interpreting (3) as a
physical quantum density matrix would itself require the quantum map.

An especially relevant registering control is the orbit function

Its period-averaged two-time statistic is

c cannot vanish, since an upper triangular unimodular integer matrix

original c=1 axis attains that bound.

sqrt((a+d)^2-4)/|c|. This is standard elementary hyperbolic geometry,
