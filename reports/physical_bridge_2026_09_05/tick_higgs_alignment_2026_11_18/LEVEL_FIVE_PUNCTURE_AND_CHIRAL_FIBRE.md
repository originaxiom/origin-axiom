Here is a short global count that also works for the reducible exterior square. Consequently the ordinary degree downstairs is −dβ, and the extension is semistable, since a destabilizing subbundle would pull back to one in a sum of equal lines. Tensoring by the compact spin or canonical line of degree zero preserves this argument.
