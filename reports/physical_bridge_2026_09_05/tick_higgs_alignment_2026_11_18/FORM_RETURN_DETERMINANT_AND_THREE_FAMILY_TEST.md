# The complete form determinant obstructs three fixed families in the regular five-sheet ansatz

20 November 2026. Hand derivation submitted for independent review. This addresses the physical transport test left at 0f65d4733. It uses the kinetic Hodge connection on the parent connection-form zero modes, fixed flat gauge coefficients along the marked metric path, and the given endpoint identification. The two spin-slot unitaries need not be computed. This proves a conditional exclusion for a specified class of transport and backgrounds; the parent on a single surface alone does not define an extra physical circle or mandate an adiabatic reduction.

## Which connection and which determinant?

Let V be the irreducible five-dimensional unitary coefficient system on the flat punctured torus. First assume its peripheral matrix has no eigenvalue one and its image is finite. Then the cohomology of the peripheral circle with these coefficients vanishes in degrees zero and one, since c−I is invertible. Ordinary, compactly supported (via the natural map), and parabolic first cohomology agree. The physical L² harmonic one-forms represent this cohomology: on the finite cover killing V, holomorphic L² one-forms extend across the filled punctures by their ordinary Laurent expansion, and the antiholomorphic statement is its conjugate. Equivariance gives the downstairs coefficient component. Filling only removes peripheral cohomology; in this component there is none. Hodge theory on the compact cover gives

    H¹(Σ,V) = H^(0,1)(V) ⊕ H^(1,0)(V).

The first summand counts the forward connection fermions and the second is the complex conjugate of the conjugate-representation connection fermions. These are the two charge sectors of the same left form slot.

Along a marked path use the flat Gauss–Manin derivative of cohomology classes, projected onto each of these two summands separately. For smooth harmonic representatives the marked real-form derivative has this cohomology class, and exact terms are orthogonal to harmonic forms. Orthogonal projection in the kinetic one-form norm therefore gives precisely these diagonal connections. The cup pairing with the flat coefficient Hermitian metric is parallel and definite with opposite signs on the two Hodge summands. Each projected connection is consequently unitary in the positive physical norm. Equivalently this is the Hodge connection on the compact cover, restricted equivariantly. No choice of positive spinor metric is substituted for the one-form norm. The full Gauss–Manin connection can mix the two summands even though its diagonal projections are unitary. See Forni, Matheus and Zorich, §2.3, for the projection with the flat polarized pairing on compact surfaces. [Primary paper](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~anton.zorich/Papers/MatheusForniZorich%20Lyapunov%20Spectrum%20ETDS%202012.pdf)

The determinant connection on their sum equals the flat determinant connection exactly: deleting the off-diagonal blocks of a connection matrix leaves its trace unchanged in every splitting frame. Include the actual endpoint sewing in both computations. Denote the unitary returns on H^(0,1)(V) and H^(0,1)(V*) by U and Uc, respectively, before the A-side parity. They obey

    det U · conjugate(det Uc) = det(T on H¹(Σ,V)).

This identity is independent of the marked metric path. It is stronger than unitarity of each multiplicity space alone. A different horizontal interaction acting inside the form spaces must be derived before applying this identity to it.

## Compute the topological side on the two handles

Write X,D for the handle matrices and g∈SU(5) for the return satisfying gXg⁻¹=X²D, gDg⁻¹=XD. The punctured torus retracts to a bouquet of two circles. A left cocycle is a freely chosen pair (x,y) of vectors in V. Its values on the two returned words are ((I+X)x+X²y, x+Xy), by the left cocycle law. To compare at the original coefficient system apply g⁻¹ to each component. The resulting block operator has determinant one: the two factors g⁻¹ have unit determinant and the remaining blocks commute and give (I+X)X−X²=X with det X=1. This can also be read on the five eigendirections of the unitary X. On coboundaries v↦((I−X)v,(I−D)v) the action is v↦g⁻¹v. This embedding is injective by absence of invariants, and its determinant is one. Passing to the quotient leaves det T=1. Reversing the comparison convention inverts the determinant, still one.

The overall central A-side factor allowed by the parity test is a fifth root on the defining five and therefore also on the 10. Multiplying both cohomology blocks by that 10 phase contributes its fifth power, one. The real sign of a common fermionic seam can only change the conclusion det T∈{+1,−1} by a sign. The physical consequence below needs only this reality, and uses the matching conjugate action when the A phase is absorbed. An independently supplied complex chiral seam phase would need its own calculation.

## The k=4 scalar end

There are six forward 10s, of which four are form modes and two are spin modes. There is one conjugate 10 and it is a form mode. Three net Q and three net up antiquarks would require all six forward modes to return, split equally between the two opposite gauge parities. In particular every eigenvalue of the four-dimensional form return would be ±1, using the centered parity convention above. Since det U · conjugate(det Uc) is real and Uc is one-dimensional and unitary, Uc must also be ±1. This conjugate necessarily survives in one of the two parity sectors. The two net multiplicities cannot both reach three. The separate form block used here is part of the quadratic zero-matter parent with metric variation and geometric endpoint sewing; a new coupling mixing forms with spinors is beyond this argument.

## Check the other possible peripheral angles

Consider the whole finite-image monomial family with an irreducible defining five, a transitive cyclic order-five permutation image on the fibre, the B67 return, and the regular A,B assignment. Retain the flat zero-matter parent, extending spin, fibre-kernel reduction and the slot-preserving form transport just specified. At a minus-one spin phase the complete cusp norm admits exponent +1/2 and excludes −1/2 by the divergent integral ∫dr/(r log(1/r)). Thus choose β in (−1/2,1/2], including this endpoint. The absolute count on the degree-five unramified sheet cover still follows from the ordinary line of degree s there: the forward spins each contribute max(s,0) for nonzero s and at most one for s=0. This step does not identify the dual spin extensions. The form formula remains valid at this angle. Since |s|≤2 by integrality, the seven-copy bound of 0f65d4733 extends to these absolute kernel counts. It rules out a generic non-parity A-side split, which needs nine. For the remaining parity split let n count the negative balanced angles, and s=−Σβ_j. A six-copy forward supply is necessary for both the 10 and dual five. It costs nothing to exclude cases by their actual counts before making any assumption on the spin transports.

* If n≤3, there are at most n+2≤5 forward 10s, including the exceptional degree-zero spin line.
* If n=4, s is 0 or 1: four negative angles have magnitude sum below two, and the last nonnegative angle is at most a half. For s=1 there are five forward 10s. At s=0 the fifth angle is strictly positive, so there is no peripheral invariant. Six forward 10s are possible only if the degree-zero spin line upstairs has a section. In that case the form multiplicities are four forward and one conjugate. The determinant argument above forces a returning conjugate whenever all six forward are used. Extra conjugate spin modes cannot improve the signed count.
* If n=5, write β_j=−a_j with 0<a_j<1/2. Their sum is 1 or 2. For sum one the forward 10s again number six with four forward form modes and one conjugate form, and the same determinant applies. For sum two every pair satisfies 1/2<a_i+a_j<1, since the other three terms sum to less than 3/2. Thus all ten exterior-square phases 1−a_i−a_j lie strictly between zero and a half. Their sum is 10−4Σa_j=2. Only two forward dual fives occur, both forms: a forward spin section would have exclusively positive balanced exponents, hence lift to bounded constant coefficients on the compact finite cover, impossible for a sector with no unit puncture eigenvalue. There are no scalar invariants in this sector either.

This exhausts the possible signs and integral sums in the stated class. It preserves the earlier example's three net fibre families and the exact SM gauge-history stabilizer. It excludes getting three complete net *fixed* families there by transporting only those fibre modes with this kinetic form connection. In particular merely varying the two end angles more finely, or changing the geometric path or its spin lift, cannot supply the missing family under these hypotheses.

At a minus-one spin threshold the absolute L² kernel bound above still holds, but zero can meet the continuum. A physical adiabatic projection there requires independent justification; continuum and threshold scattering states were never counted as normalizable fibre modes. Live possibilities include global dynamics coupling nonzero fibre modes, a derived end response carrying additional charged states or phase, and a gauge history outside the flat regular ansatz. The determinant calculation by itself gives no law choosing any of these, no vacuum selection, and no gravitational dynamics. No scientific code executed or data used.
