# Which charged modes can return as three SM families?

20 November 2026. Author proof for independent review. Reuses the marked-family field law, the complete cusp domains and the flat regular SU(5) pair at 068043746. The question is whether a unitary return on the actual four-field left zero modes could preserve three copies of every charged SM representation. This is a necessary test for a physical model in which surviving massless states are parallel sections of the fibre-kernel bundle around the return. It makes no choice of Berry matrix, path in complex structure, or numerical tolerance.

## Exact number available in the cubic-root example

The five in B is irreducible. For the 10 of A its coefficient has balanced cusp weights 0,0,−1/3,−1/3,−1/3. For the conjugate they are all nonnegative. A holomorphic L² spin zero mode in the conjugate sector lifts, in a global parallel coefficient frame on a finite cover, to bounded holomorphic coefficients at every puncture. Here spin is trivialized as the pullback of the compact elliptic spin line: one can first kill its two-torsion character on an unramified cover. The pulled-back fibre frame is for this line, not the square root of the canonical line of the branched cover. Bounded coefficients on the compact cover are constant. Descent would give an invariant in the dual five tensored with a spin character, impossible by irreducibility in dimension five. This proves zero conjugate modes for either spin slot for every complex structure. By Serre duality of the balanced extensions and the degree +1 Riemann–Roch result at 7d0057bd2 the forward slot has exactly one. The scalar has none and the form multiplicities are two and one. The absolute four-slot numbers are therefore **four 10s and one conjugate 10**, rather than just a signed difference of three. The proof also works for the rational three-family wall at 068043746, because the three negative angles add to −1 and the other two are zero.

Under the 3+2 parity, color–color and weak–weak in Λ² are even, while color–weak is odd. These are respectively the up antiquark, charged antilepton and quark doublet of the SM convention used in B1951. Thus three returning net up antiquarks and three net quark doublets require simultaneously

    dim ker(U−I) ≥ 3,    dim ker(U+I) ≥ 3.

The two eigenspaces have zero intersection, so their total dimension is at most four. Any surviving conjugates only increase the forward requirement. The other summands of 248 have no quark doublet or up antiquark of these hypercharges: the off-diagonal A adjoint doublets have |6Y|=5, while the SM quark has |6Y|=1. Consequently **at most two complete net families can survive this type of return on this background**, even granting arbitrary unitary mixing of all four forward copies. Two is an upper bound, with no attainment implied. This obstruction applies to the fixed-state interpretation of the physical return; it leaves the proved three-family fibre index intact. ## Does an arbitrary SM-preserving Wilson angle rescue it?

More generally take meridional gauge matrix diag(a,a,a,b,b) in A, with a³b²=1 and a≠b. This is the defining-five form up to conjugacy when its connected centralizer there is the specified SM group. The three weights on the 10 are a², ab, b². A generic ratio produces three different eigenspaces in U for uᶜ,Q,eᶜ, costing at least nine forward copies. If a²=b² then b=−a and a⁵=1. Up to a central scalar, the previous parity is the **least costly** nontrivial possibility and still needs six.

There is an absolute upper bound of seven forward 10s in the entire finite-image shift ansatz of 068043746 for an irreducible five and no minus-one spin threshold. The permutation action of the two handles is cyclic of order five. The kernel of this sheet permutation gives a connected degree-five unramified cover of the compact elliptic curve, since the commutator acts trivially on the sheets. On its punctured part the five downstairs is the pushforward of one flat line: pick a sheet, restrict to its stabilizer character and use its five cosets as the monomial basis. The five punctures upstairs carry the five phases downstairs. The cusp norms lift locally unchanged; holomorphic spin sections downstairs are sections of this line's balanced extension tensored with the pulled-back spin line upstairs. The compact cover has genus one. Write n for the number of negative balanced angles and s for minus their total together with the other balanced angles, i.e. s=−Σβ_j. Then s is an integer with |s|≤2. The line has degree s. For s≠0 its section count is max(s,0); at s=0 it has at most one section. The form slot has Σγ_j=n−s forward copies and the scalar has none. Thus the total is n+|s| for s≠0 and at most n+2 for s=0, in both cases at most seven. This makes a central multiple of the 3+2 involution necessary for three fixed net families in this class, even if the A-side splitting angle is initially allowed to vary. It is a target-conditioned restriction. The field equations still allow other angles.
