# A harmonic boundary prescription from the bulk equation

C8 author hand proof for independent review. The product filling and conventions are those of 77851f4f0. The previous comparison gave indices −12 and +12 for two domains on the same flat exterior-square twist. Here we compute what the *interior equation* returns if the end is read by bounded continuation on its product cylinder. This is a specified way of obtaining a boundary prescription. Whether the physical theory demands such a cylinder remains an input.

## Which boundary fields actually extend?

Take any unitary flat twist from X on M=X×S¹ with the product metric, flat torus boundary and periodic or antiperiodic spectator spin. On the positive chiral boundary let n be the dimension of the parallel internal multiplicity including the spin-transport sign test, so the zero tangential momentum space has dimension 2n. Work over the complex numbers. For chirality σ=+ or − let Z^σ_< and Z^σ_≤ denote smooth bulk zero solutions with allowed tangential eigenvalues respectively strictly negative or nonpositive, using each chirality's own inward tangential operator. Each finite-dimensional harmonic trace enlargement is Fredholm. Let

    L^σ = image of the harmonic trace projection Z^σ_≤ → ker B^σ.

The kernel of that projection is exactly Z^σ_<. These L spaces depend on the core, including metric and connection, and can be defined before seeing the desired index. Equivalently on the attached outward semi-infinite *product* cylinder, positive inward modes grow, negative modes decay and zero modes remain constant. Bounded harmonic solutions on the extended core thus have limiting values precisely in L. This is a space of asymptotic data; a nonzero constant on the infinite cylinder is not square integrable. Here this cylinder is an analytic prescription for the compact core's finite-dimensional trace choice. It is distinct from the complete shrinking hyperbolic end. For the primary analytic formulation see [Paul Loya, equations (1.2)–(1.3)](https://people.math.binghamton.edu/loya/papers/LoyaCONM.pdf).

The strict positive-chirality APS problem has index −n as in 77851f4f0: on this product the bulk degree-four density vanishes and all nonzero tangential spectral contributions to eta cancel. Its adjoint has *nonpositive* negative-chirality boundary spectrum, by Green's formula and the interchange of signs under inward normal Clifford multiplication c(n). Therefore

    −n = dim Z^+_< − dim Z^−_≤.

The product symmetry J=c_z R_z from 77851f4f0 preserves inward tangential eigenvalues, exchanges Γ signs, and intertwines the bulk kernels. In particular dim Z^+_<=dim Z^−_<. Subtracting the previous identity gives dim L^−=n. Applying J to the harmonic trace map gives dim L^+=n too. This step does not assume absence of L² zero modes. Any such modes disappear in the subtraction.

For completeness the two limiting spaces fit together. For any u in Z^+_≤ and v in Z^−_≤, Green's formula gives zero for their integrated boundary pairing. Their strictly negative boundary pieces make no contribution since c(n) maps those pieces to positive modes; zero is orthogonal to both. Thus c(n)L^+ is orthogonal to L^−. The dimensions just obtained make L^− exactly its orthogonal complement in ker B^−. Consequently the full spectral domain obtained by adjoining L^+ and L^− to the respective strictly negative trace spaces is self-adjoint and elliptic. It is J-invariant. Its chiral index is −n+dim L^+=0, and its actual two kernels are isomorphic under J. The orthogonal-complement prescription on the harmonic blocks also has zero index and is J-invariant. The index cannot distinguish those two physical choices.

All this commutes with every parallel internal projector because the zero equations, spin operation J and harmonic projection preserve each such summand. In particular chirality balances within each parallel gauge sector. Equivalently J commutes with any parallel internal symmetry on the whole kernel, so the two kernels carry isomorphic representations of that symmetry. For Λ²W25 with periodic meridian and spectator, n=12. The positive harmonic trace has dimension 24; **exactly 12 dimensions are realized as limiting values**. For the sheet Fock twists n=0 and nothing needs to be added. The exact subspaces L depend on the core; we derived their ranks, their normal complement, and their effect on chirality, without determining their individual wave functions.

## What was and was not eliminated

With this prescription no independent matrix choosing which spin/charge components extend through the given core is needed. It gives paired chiralities even where the two freely imposed harmonic signs would have yielded opposite nonzero indices. Thus the earlier ±12 does not imply that the core generates a chiral preference. An unsymmetric *physical exterior* with its own Cauchy data could mismatch the core; its action, domains and matching must then be computed independently. A chiral index on a different geometry or with nonflat coupling lies outside this comparison. No additional physical parameter, representation assignment, vacuum, SM family or gravity law follows here. Independent review should especially check the strict/nonpositive adjoint pairing and the distinction between cylindrical continuation and a physical hyperbolic end.
