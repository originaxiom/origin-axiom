## Four exact three-dimensional readings on the end

The harmonic internal coefficients K in Λ²W are fixed by the upper unipotents U(k) and are odd under Z=−I. In the delta basis write w_x=e_x∧e_(2x) for x≠0. The twenty-four w_x are independent oriented edges, since 2²=−1 and x≠−x. K is the span of w_x−w_(−x), dimension twelve. It has an additional arithmetic structure intrinsic to this chosen realization:

* D sends e_x to e_(2x). It is the split torus transport for the multiplier 2 or its inverse depending on the diagonal convention. The quadratic-character scalar of a torus lift disappears on the exterior square. On K, D²=Z=−1. Choosing the generator −2 of the prime subfield's order-four group interchanges D and D⁻¹.
* S sends e_x to e_(x⁵), extended **complex linearly**. This is the field Frobenius on labels, not complex conjugation of wave amplitudes. S²=1 and S commutes with D. It preserves the trace character because Tr(x⁵)=Tr(x). It fixes the geometric meridian and reverses the longitude in the finite model, while D reverses the signed longitudinal element and inverts the meridian. These operations preserve the simultaneous spin-compensating subspace. Normalizing the peripheral data setwise is weaker than commuting with each labelled transport, or extending a symmetry of the full metric and physical fields.

For each D eigenvalue +i or −i and each S sign ±1 the joint spectral projector has **rank three** on K. Proof: the whole multiplicative torus k× permutes the w_x regularly, with no scalar on Λ². The Z-odd part contains each odd character once (exponents j=1,3,...,23 of C24). The element 2 has order four, so each D eigenspace contains six of these characters. Frobenius permutes their exponents by 5, preserves the D eigenvalue, and fixes no odd exponent since 5j=j mod 24 would require 6|j. It acts by three interchanges on each six-dimensional space. Each S sign therefore occurs three times. Complex conjugation in the real coefficient basis exchanges the +i and −i planes at fixed S sign. This supplies four fully specified subspaces, without a distinguished physical choice of one.

## Which boundary selections extend as flat coefficient sectors?

Let B=U(k)⋊k× be the upper triangular Borel of G, and extend a torus character θ to B by killing U(k). In any finite-dimensional complex G-module V the multiplicity of θ in V^U equals the multiplicity pairing of V with Iθ=Ind_B^G θ, by Frobenius reciprocity. There are two double cosets B and BwB, since B fixes one projective line and is transitive on all other lines. In the nontrivial coset the intersection of the two Borels is the diagonal torus and the Weyl representative inverts its parameter. Restricting an induced function to the two orbits therefore gives

    dim Hom_G(Iθ,Iψ) = [θ=ψ]+[θ=ψ⁻¹].

Equivalently an equivariant kernel on G/B × G/B is determined by one scalar on each orbit whose stabilizer characters agree. No odd character θ (θ(−1)=−1) is self-inverse because the order-two character of C24 has even exponent 12. Semisimplicity and the formula imply Iθ is irreducible, isomorphic to Iθ⁻¹, of dimension [G:B]=26. The twelve odd characters group into six inequivalent inverse pairs. Their multiplicities in V^U always agree pairwise. In particular **every** flat complex G-submodule contributes an even number of central-odd U-invariant coefficients, including the possibility of zero.

For the particular Λ²W, the centre-odd sector is W_even ⊗ W_odd of dimension 13×12=156. Each of the six 26-dimensional modules occurs once by the explicit boundary character calculation. Their total already exhausts 156. Consequently the attainable harmonic ranks of full parallel subbundles in this sector are exactly 0,2,4,6,8,10,12. Each inverse pair straddles the two D signs. No single rank-three projector above comes from a parallel summand of the supplied flat geometric bundle. This is stronger than counting boundary dimensions and narrower than excluding an interacting, spatially varying projector. For every such irreducible summand the positive-chiral harmonic trace has rank four and the core-determined limiting trace in 449d43ce8 has rank two. Its net product-filling index is zero separately.

## Check the price on a chiral domain

Suppose one nevertheless uses a single three-plane only at the boundary, keeping the same global Λ²W twist and retaining both tangential spin components in the positive-chirality harmonic trace. The new APS domain enlarges the strict cut by 2×3=6, so its positive chirality index is −12+6=−6 in the previous orientation. More generally a spin-independent harmonic coefficient projector of rank r yields −12+2r. An index +3 on this specific operator would need a fifteen-dimensional selection in the full rank-24 positive-chirality harmonic trace. That would require a different selection involving spin, or changes elsewhere in the physical problem. Simply calling a boundary coefficient subspace three families would skip this calculation. An index of three, if earned elsewhere, still would not determine the actual SM charge content.

The four boundary triplets preserve a lead that an even-rank bulk theorem alone would miss. They identify candidate measuring data and the transformation that exchanges two of the choices. The flat bulk and the product core selection do not promote one of them to three chiral matter families. The missing physics is an action or source that implements the selection on the whole fermion domain with correct currents and independently justified symmetry.
