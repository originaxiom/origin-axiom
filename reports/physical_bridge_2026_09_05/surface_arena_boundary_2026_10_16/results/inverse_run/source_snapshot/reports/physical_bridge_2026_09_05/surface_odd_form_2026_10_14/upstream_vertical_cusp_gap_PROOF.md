# A uniform vertical gap above the three puncture modes

This is an authored analytic argument for the two already prescribed extreme domains. Independent analytic review remains owed. The result concerns the vertical operator, not its coupling to horizontal derivatives in the full total operator.

## Statement and existing inputs

Let tau=x+iy, |x|<=1/2, and E_tau=C/(Z+tau Z) with its zero removed. Retain the extending odd spin, rank-six flat unitary coefficient and flat area-A fibre metric of the previous packets. Let D_V,tau have Lambda=0 or Lambda=C6, including the complementary negative trace. Let P_tau be its orthogonal zero-mode projection.

There are c>0 and Y such that, for y>=Y,

    ||D_V,tau psi|| >= c sqrt(y/A) ||psi||,  psi perpendicular to ker D_V,tau.

The constants may depend on this fixed problem; no numerical value for c is asserted. At each extreme the full vertical kernel has dimension three, by puncture_fredholm's compact-fibre argument. It lies entirely in one vertical chirality. The proof below supplies the uniform complement estimate which pointwise Fredholmness alone did not supply.

Write z=u+is on the Euclidean cylinder. Constant metric rescaling, with its unitary spinor identification, gives D_V,tau=sqrt(y/A) Dhat_tau. The constant factor does not alter either extreme trace subspace. It is enough to prove a uniform gap for Dhat_tau on the long flat punctured tori with metric |dz|^2. The total mixed spin connection is not part of this vertical operator and is not dropped from any total-space claim.

## The actual cylinder restriction

Centre the long fundamental strip at the puncture, -y/2<s<y/2. Its ends are glued with horizontal displacement x and the actual B0 transport. Every fixed neighbourhood |s|<R embeds for y>2R and is exactly the same punctured flat cylinder, including the spin, coefficient and domain. No limiting metric approximation is needed there.

The cylinder's fundamental group is generated by a circle a above the puncture and the puncture loop c. Its restricted transports are A0 and -I6. These commute and can be diagonalized on THIS cylinder; the original A0 and B0 on the punctured torus do not commute. Indeed B0 A0 B0^-1=-A0. The circle below the puncture has transport -A0, and B0 intertwines the two circle transports when the ends are rejoined. This is not replacement by a commuting local system on an unpunctured torus.

Both A0 and -A0 have three i and three -i eigenvalues. The odd spin is periodic in the Cartesian frame. Thus each end has angular frequencies 2 pi(k+alpha), k in Z, with alpha=1/4 or 3/4. Their absolute values are at least delta=pi/2. The based choice of which end has A0 only exchanges the two labels.

For any domain section eta supported away from the puncture, flatness and integration by parts give

    ||Dhat eta||^2 = ||nabla_u eta||^2 + ||nabla_s eta||^2
                  >= delta^2 ||eta||^2.

This holds on infinite ends and on the finite neck after gluing: transition maps are constant unitary, so artificial seam terms cancel. On each puncture-free circle the same lower bound applies regardless of its diagonalization. It is NOT asserted for a section with an allowed critical half-pole at the puncture.

## All zero modes on the limiting punctured cylinder

Use zeta=exp(2 pi i z); the cylinder is C* with zeta=1 deleted. In a character with transport exp(2 pi i alpha) at zeta=0 and -1 at zeta=1, every holomorphic zero mode with at most the admitted half-pole can be written

    f(zeta)=zeta^alpha (zeta-1)^(-1/2) h(zeta),

where h is single-valued and holomorphic on C*, extends across 1, and may initially have isolated singularities at 0 and infinity. This follows by dividing by the displayed nowhere-zero multivalued factor. Square integrability eliminates all but finitely many Laurent coefficients: the Euclidean cylinder measure is |d zeta|^2/(4 pi^2 |zeta|^2), and angular Parseval near the ends applies with a weight comparable to the corresponding radial power. A Laurent monomial zeta^k is integrable at both ends precisely when

    -alpha < k < 1/2-alpha,  k in Z.

These are strict inequalities; equality has a logarithmically divergent norm. There is exactly one integer, k=0, for alpha=1/4 and none for alpha=3/4. Thus the complete holomorphic zero space has dimension three when the plus critical trace is allowed. Its scalar profile is

    f(zeta)=zeta^(1/4)/(zeta-1)^(1/2),
    f^4=zeta/(zeta-1)^2.

At zeta=1 it has the allowed order -1/2, with nonzero critical coefficient. A regular plus trace would require h(1)=0 and therefore kills that entire one-dimensional scalar space. No polynomial of positive degree can evade the end inequalities.

For the negative Dirac equation, complex conjugation gives the same holomorphic calculation with conjugate character. Therefore Lambda=C6 has (dim ker Dhat^+,dim ker Dhat^-)=(3,0); Lambda=0 has (0,3). This classifies the full zero space of each cylinder realization, not just three exhibited solutions.

For the nonzero profile,

    |f(u+is)|^2 = [2 cosh(2 pi s)-2 cos(2 pi u)]^(-1/2).

It is locally integrable at the puncture and decays like exp(-pi |s|) in squared norm density at the ends. This explicitly shows why the end's nontrivial holonomy can coexist with the three puncture modes. The profile is on the cylinder limit, not asserted to be an exact finite-torus theta function.

## Local compactness retains the critical trace

Near the puncture the spin/gauge rotation and radial normalization give the same periodic cone operator and the same finite critical quotient as puncture_fredholm. The graph embedding from Gil--Krainer--Mendoza, Proposition 3.5 and Theorem 3.6(iii), yields local compactness after a cutoff. The finite-dimensional critical traces remain included. Away from the puncture this is ordinary elliptic graph regularity and Rellich compactness. These estimates are uniform on every fixed cylinder neighbourhood because its operator and domain are literally unchanged as y increases. The primary statements were reread at https://arxiv.org/html/math/0410176; no parameter-dependent resolvent or surgery theorem is used.

The infinite-cylinder realization is selfadjoint by the admitted local trace law and scalar exhausting cutoffs on its two complete ends. The local adjoint and deficiency argument is the two-dimensional version of total_domain's cutoff proof. Domain sections localized to a fixed neighbourhood have the same admitted local trace, both before and after taking graph limits. These facts also apply to approximate nullvectors, not only exact eigenvectors.

## No approximate nullvector can escape down the neck

Take a scalar cutoff eta_R which is zero on |s|<=R, one on |s|>=2R, and has |d eta_R|<=C/R. On a finite torus with y>4R it is constant one near the gluing seam, so it is globally smooth and compatible with the unitary transitions. It vanishes near the puncture. The preceding end bound and Dirac commutator yield

    delta ||eta_R psi|| <= ||Dhat(eta_R psi)||
                        <= ||Dhat psi|| + (C/R)||psi||.

Consequently, for unit approximate nullvectors, the mass outside |s|<2R is at most (||Dhat psi||+C/R)^2/delta^2. Taking the small-energy limit first and then R to infinity proves uniform tightness. This estimate does not incorrectly apply a positive lower bound to the three zero modes: their norm is concentrated near the puncture.

Combine this tightness with local compactness. A sequence of unit sections on increasingly long tori with Dhat psi tending to zero has a subsequence converging locally in L2 and with no lost norm to a unit cylinder zero mode. To verify the domain, cut off on any fixed compact cylinder neighbourhood; its derivatives are bounded in graph norm and its closed trace domain is weakly closed. The distributional limit solves Dhat psi=0 and has the same admitted trace. The end norm bound then makes it a global L2 zero mode. Inner products between finitely many such sequences also converge, by the same tail estimate.

## The complement cannot develop a fourth small mode

Suppose the claimed rescaled complement bound failed as y tends to infinity. Choose unit psi_j orthogonal to the three-dimensional exact kernel, with ||Dhat_tau_j psi_j|| tending to zero. Add an orthonormal basis of the exact kernel at each tau_j. This gives FOUR mutually orthogonal approximate nullvectors. The compactness and tightness argument, applied to the four sequences with a common subsequence, gives four mutually orthonormal cylinder zero modes. That contradicts the complete cylinder calculation, whose full zero space has dimension three. Therefore a positive uniform bound exists for all sufficiently large y, uniformly in |x|<=1/2. The proof uses exact finite-fibre kernel dimensions, not an assumption about convergence of chosen bases or projections.

If one extends the bound over a fixed compact smooth range of tau, smooth graph-family dependence and the constant extreme kernel ranks from fibre_family supply a positive minimum there. The stated cusp estimate itself needs only sufficiently large y.

In the original fibre units the conclusion is

    ||(D_V,tau restricted to ker-perp)^(-1)|| <= c^-1 sqrt(A/y).

Thus the nonzero vertical complement becomes heavy while the three exact vertical modes remain. This does not prove that horizontal differentiation preserves their span, or estimate derivatives of P_tau.

## Controls and physical boundaries

With trivial angular holonomy on an unpunctured periodic flat torus, the nonconstant longitudinal mode exp(2 pi i s/y) has Dirac absolute eigenvalue 2 pi/y in Euclidean units and 2 pi/sqrt(A y) in area-A units. It is orthogonal to constant zero modes and tends to zero. That comparator fails precisely the no-escape hypothesis; it is not the actual quaternion coefficient or puncture domain. Removing the allowed half-pole instead erases the explicit cylinder mode; replacing the puncture transport by +I erases its required branch sign. These controls distinguish the ingredients rather than declaring a universal chirality result.

Still to establish on the SAME total operator: estimates for horizontal derivatives of the harmonic projection and the complementary coupling, the effective cusp operator and its actual global spin/coefficient transitions, total Fredholmness and comparison with the claimed index. Intermediate Lambda may have different limiting and finite kernel ranks and is not covered by this proof. The metric, coefficient, extreme choice, physical parent and nonzero sector remain supplied; none is selected by a vertical gap. No Standard-Model parameter or complete theory follows here.
