## The positive survives; the ordinary-Higgs map does not follow

Original q,r are sections of ad(P) tensor S, S^2=K. Their action
contains Dbar q,Dbar r,[q,r] and the full gauge moment map. This is
the generalized spinor-valued system with L1=L2=S. The uniformizing
ordinary Higgs field in W75/Simpson is instead ad(P) tensor K.
W75 can correctly solve its ordinary equation without reproducing our
parent or establishing its flat NAH connection.

Locally a coefficient c y^-k E gives Dbar=-i c(k+2h)y^-k-1 E.
Our k=1/2,h=-1/4 gives zero. The naive conversion sqrt(Omega)q,
k=1, in the SAME gauge connection gives a nonzero residual. Changing
h to-1/2 restores the ordinary holomorphic equation, but changes
curvature and the original scalar moment equation: h+c^2 is then
nonzero for c=1/2. Equal orthonormal field size is not equal dynamics.
No unitary gauge equivalence removes a changed curvature norm.

On frame rescaling dz' = j dz the two coefficients have respective
weights j^-1/2 and j^-1. A natural nonzero linear map S->K requires
an extra section/choice of S. Smooth triviality on the open curve does
not supply a connection-preserving identification: F_S=-i dvol/2,
F_K=2F_S differ. On an elliptic compactification a special trivial
holomorphic spin line is a separate choice, not a uniform map over
all four spin structures and their domains. Thus the ordinary NAH
correspondence cannot be transferred by direct equality of these fields.

There IS a canonical adjoint-valued K map: [q,r]. Hitchin's Prop2
describes this bracket of two spin-valued fields. In OUR action its
F-term equation sets it to zero at every supersymmetric vacuum. Single
field self-bracket is zero as well. This does not exclude other composites,
nonvacuum interactions, defect residues or a changed derived parent.
It identifies the duty instead of declaring all NAH routes dead.

## Finite Q8 restriction does not specify the continuous lift

Actual full E8 SU2_Q character includes dim4 irreps as well as dim2.
All odd-centre irreps restrict to copies of the unique Q8 doublet, so
56 Q8 doublets is compatible with a continuous spin3/2 part. Recompute
the entire continuous character and its dimension/index, rather than
reading continuous SU2 from central parity. The prior registered
embedding ALREADY contains this decomposition; new verification reuses
it, not a claimed discovery. The original condensate root's actual
Cartan weight is tested separately. Odd centre alone still proves it
cannot commute with the entire quaternion SU2; it does not on its own
prove every odd state is a continuous doublet.

## The actual portal opportunity, not an invented cubic

The original bulk parent contains no independent ad(P)-valued scalar
flavon X of internal degree0. It has connection A and spin q,r. A
putative Hdagger Phi_K H still has K degree; replacing Phi_K by a
spin field leaves S degree. A residue/defect scalar can change this,
but its field/kinetic/end map must be supplied or derived. Invariance
under the internal gauge group and puncture centre is necessary, not
sufficient for a bulk physical term.

Do not infer that q and a flavon-direction r never interact. The
existing potential has 2||[q,r]||^2 and the full moment square, with
positive fixed coefficients. On a weight-one root normalized by
kappa(E_alpha^dagger E_alpha)=1, the local comparator has

    V = f^2 +2 f t^2+t^4+2 s^2 t^2.

Here r=sH_Q is locally Hermitian/normal, [H_Q,E_alpha]=E_alpha.
There is no standalone s t^2 coefficient at r=0, but a quartic
interaction is PRESENT. Around r=(v+s)H_Q the same term contains
4v s t^2. Its coefficient/sign follows the chosen background v;
it is not a new free cubic nor evidence of a globally stationary joint.
The term 2f t^2 comes from the curvature moment source, not an
independent flavour scalar. The original derivative-square expansion
cancels its gauge-curvature cross term against the rough kinetic form,
so its isolated sign is not a stability criterion.

The two-field nilpotent stationary backgrounds already banked keep
[q,r]=0 while retaining moment interactions. They are positive prior
examples, not excluded by this normal-direction comparator. Their
gauge completions and physical spectrum are different from the bare
quaternion point and the four-dimensional semi-flat surface operator.

## Physical direction

The point of this audit is to identify the correct same-action bridge:
a spinor/ordinary or defect map with its connection and domain, and an
actual coupled background/vertex. A new effective portal through heavy
