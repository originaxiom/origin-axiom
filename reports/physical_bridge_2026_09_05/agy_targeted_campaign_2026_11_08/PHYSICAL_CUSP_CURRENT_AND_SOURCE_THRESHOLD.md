## 3. Conditional Source Threshold (`E4`), Oscillatory Ward Residual, and Physical Scope (`E5`)

### 3.1 Conditional Fixed-$s>0$ Hilbert Membership and Convergent 4-Generator Green Overlap (`E4`)
At fixed $s=k^2>0$ ($\nu_k=\sqrt{1+4\ell^2 s}>1$, $r_\pm=(1\pm\nu_k)/2$), source membership and Green-exchange convergence are **conditional on a nonvanishing channel/contraction coefficient**:
1. **Cancelled Channel ($\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,\bullet}(k,y)\equiv 0$, including $P_{T,\mu\nu}(k)\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,\nu}^{\text{ext}}(k,y)=0$):** The zero source lies in $\mathcal{H}_v, \mathcal{H}_b, L^2(dy)$ trivially for **every** $\Sigma_{st} > 0$.
2. **Surviving Channel ($\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,T,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k,y) = \mathcal{A}_{st,T,\mu}(k)\Omega(y)(y/Y)^{-\Sigma_{st}+i\ell\Delta\omega_{st}} \neq 0$ or $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,x/y}^{\text{int}}(k,y) \neq 0$ with exponent $\Sigma_{st}$):**
   $$\bigl\|\Omega^{-2}\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,T,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k,\cdot)\bigr\|_{\mathcal{H}_v}^2 = |\mathcal{A}_{st,T,\mu}(k)|^2\int_Y^\infty\Bigl(\frac{y}{Y}\Bigr)^{-2\Sigma_{st}}dy = \frac{Y\,|\mathcal{A}_{st,T,\mu}(k)|^2}{2\Sigma_{st}-1} < \infty \iff \Sigma_{st} > \frac{1}{2}, \tag{13}$$
   and identically $\|\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,x}^{\text{int}}(k,\cdot)\|_{\mathcal{H}_b}^2, \|\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k,\cdot)\|_{L^2(dy)}^2 < \infty \iff \Sigma_{st} > 1/2$. Using $G_{b,s}^D(y,z) = \frac{\ell^2}{y z}G_s(y,z) = \Omega(y)\Omega(z)G_s(y,z)$, for any four-generator witness with nonvanishing $\xi_1\xi_2\xi_3\xi_4$ contraction at $\omega_s=0$, the bulk Green integral converges **iff $\Sigma_{12}+\Sigma_{34} > 1$**:
   $$\int_Y^\infty dy\,dz\,\Bigl(\frac{y}{Y}\Bigr)^{-(1+\Sigma_{12})}G_s(y,z)\Bigl(\frac{z}{Y}\Bigr)^{-(1+\Sigma_{34})} = \frac{Y^2}{\ell^2}\int_Y^\infty dy\,dz\,\Bigl(\frac{y}{Y}\Bigr)^{-\Sigma_{12}}G_{b,s}^D(y,z)\Bigl(\frac{z}{Y}\Bigr)^{-\Sigma_{34}} = \frac{Y^3}{(\Sigma_{12}+\Sigma_{34}-1)(\Sigma_{12}-r_-)(\Sigma_{34}-r_-)}. \tag{14}$$
   For $\Sigma_{12}+\Sigma_{34} \le 1$, `eq. (14)` diverges (its rational continuation is not a convergent exchange integral).

### 3.2 Exact Oscillatory Ward Residual and Three-Level Physical Admission Scope (`E5`)
Keeping external 4D momentum $k_\mu$ ($s=k^2>0$) distinct from radial parameters $z_s=\omega_s+i\epsilon_s\in\mathbb{C}_+$ ($\Delta\omega_{st}:=\omega_t-\omega_s$, $\bar{\omega}_{st}:=\omega_s+\omega_t$) and differentiating $\widehat{B}_{st}(k,y)\propto (y/Y)^{-\Sigma_{st}+i\ell\bar{\omega}_{st}}$ via $\partial_y = \Omega(y)\partial_r$, the pairwise $n=0$ covariant divergence on $T_Y$ is (`E5` repaired):
$$\mathcal{W}_{st,0}^{(\zeta)}(k,y) = i k_\mu\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k,y) + \partial_y\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k,y) = i k_\mu\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k,y) + \Omega(y)\Bigl[\bigl((\epsilon_s+\epsilon_t)-i\bar{\omega}_{st}\bigr)\widehat{B}_{st}(k,y) + \bigl((\epsilon_s+\epsilon_t)+i\bar{\omega}_{st}\bigr)\widehat{\bar{B}}_{st}(k,y)\Bigr]. \tag{15}$$
At equal real frequencies $\omega_s=\omega_t=\omega\neq 0$, the internal terms retain $\bar{\omega}_{st}=2\omega$. Restricting to purely imaginary $z_s=i\epsilon_s$ ($\omega_s=\omega_t=0$), where $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k,y)=i\zeta\Omega(y)(y/Y)^{-\Sigma_{st}}\mathcal{A}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k)$ and $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k,y)=(y/Y)^{-\Sigma_{st}}\mathcal{A}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k)$, `eq. (15)` gives $\mathcal{W}_{st,0}^{(\zeta)}(k,y) = -\Omega(y)(y/Y)^{-\Sigma_{st}}\bigl[\zeta k_\mu\mathcal{A}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k) - (\epsilon_s+\epsilon_t)\mathcal{A}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k)\bigr]$.
- **Three-Level Physical Scope (No Compensating Sector Inserted):** (i) *Arbitrary test coefficients:* `eq. (7)` is an off-shell projected Cauchy family; (ii) *Conserved test currents:* $\mathcal{W}_{st,0}^{(\zeta)}=0$ holds at $\omega_s=0$ iff $\zeta k_\mu\mathcal{A}_{st,\mu}^{\text{ext}}(k) = (\epsilon_s+\epsilon_t)\mathcal{A}_{st,y}^{\text{int}}(k)$, admitting stationary gauge elimination for $\Sigma_{st}>1/2$ without any compensating sector, but without proving $\mathcal{E}_{u,v}^{(\zeta)\dot{\alpha}}=0$; (iii) *Actual on-shell physical fermions (`B5a`/`B5` Open):* solving $\mathcal{E}_{u,v}^{(\zeta)\dot{\alpha}}=0$ on $\Sigma_{1,1}$ remains open.
