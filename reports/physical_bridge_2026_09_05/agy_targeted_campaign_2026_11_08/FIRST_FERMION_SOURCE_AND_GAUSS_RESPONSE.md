## 2. Duty 2: Specialization to Centralizer Spin/Flavour Profiles, Vanishing Orthogonal/Higgs Brackets, Fermionic Radial Gauss Law, and Ward Identities

1. **Specialization to Centralizer Spin/Flavour Profiles and Exact Bracket Selection Rules:**

   - **Vanishing of Matter/Higgs Sources ($J_{q,F}=J_{r,F}\equiv 0$):** Because every term of $J_{q,F}$ and $J_{r,F}$ is bilinear in $(\lambda,u),(\lambda,v),(a,u),(a,v)$, setting $\lambda=a=0$ gives $J_{q,F}\equiv 0$ and $J_{r,F}\equiv 0$ identically across all $248$ $\mathfrak{e}_8$ directions. *(Additional-sector check: even if centralizer gaugino/form components $\lambda,a\in\mathfrak{g}_0$ were retained alongside $u,v\in\mathfrak{g}_0$, since $[\mathfrak{g}_0,\mathfrak{g}_0]\subset\mathfrak{g}_0^{\text{der}}=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\subset\mathfrak{a}_3$ and $\kappa(\mathfrak{a}_3,\mathfrak{d}_5\oplus(\mathbf{16},\mathbf{10})\oplus(\overline{\mathbf{16}},\overline{\mathbf{10}}))=0$, $(J_{q,F},J_{r,F})$ would source only the neutral centralizer matter block $\delta q,\delta r\in\mathfrak{g}_0^{\text{der}}$ and would still vanish identically on all $D_5$ background-Higgs and $128$ charged sectors.)*
   - **Restriction of Gauge Sources to Derived Non-Abelian Colour $\mathfrak{g}_0^{\text{der}}=\mathfrak{su}(3)_c$:** For $u,v\in\mathfrak{g}_0$, all brackets $\llbracket\bar{u}_{\dot{\alpha}},u_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}},\llbracket\bar{v}_{\dot{\alpha}},v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}},\llbracket u^\alpha,v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}}$ lie in $[\mathfrak{g}_0,\mathfrak{g}_0]=\mathfrak{g}_0^{\text{der}}$. By $\operatorname{ad}$-invariance of $\kappa$, $\kappa(\delta Z,[X,Y])=0$ for all $X,Y\in\mathfrak{g}_0$ and $\delta Z\in\mathfrak{g}_0^\perp\oplus Z(\mathfrak{g}_0)$. Thus $(J_{\mu,F},J_{x,F},J_{y,F})$ vanish on all orthogonal $\mathfrak{e}_8$ directions $\mathfrak{g}_0^\perp$ and on the Abelian center $Z(\mathfrak{g}_0)=\mathfrak{u}(1)$, surviving exclusively in $\mathfrak{g}_0^{\text{der}}$:
     $$J_{\mu,F}^{\text{spin}} = i\,\Omega\,\bar{\sigma}_\mu^{\text{Euc}\,\dot{\alpha}\alpha}\Bigl(\llbracket\bar{u}_{\dot{\alpha}},u_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} + \llbracket\bar{v}_{\dot{\alpha}},v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}}\Bigr), \quad J_{x,F}^{\text{spin}} = i\,\Bigl(\llbracket u^\alpha,v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} - \llbracket\bar{u}^{\dot{\alpha}},\bar{v}_{\dot{\alpha}}\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}}\Bigr), \quad J_{y,F}^{\text{spin}} = -\Bigl(\llbracket u^\alpha,v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} + \llbracket\bar{u}^{\dot{\alpha}},\bar{v}_{\dot{\alpha}}\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}}\Bigr).$$
2. **Full Radial Gauss Equation with Fermionic Source:**
   Varying $S_{\text{bos}}(\mathcal{R})+S_F^{\text{Euc}}(\mathcal{R})$ with respect to $A_y$ before gauge fixing adds $-J_{y,F}$ to the radial Gauss law (`FULL_CUT_VARIATION_AND_GREEN_PAIRING.md` §1.3):

   Linearizing in $\mathfrak{g}_0$ ($v_\mu=a_\mu,b=a_x,c=a_y$, $w(y)=\Omega^{-2}=y^2/\ell^2$) gives $-\sum_\mu\partial_\mu(\partial_\mu c-\partial_y v_\mu)-\partial_x\bigl(w(y)(\partial_x c-\partial_y b)\bigr)=J_{y,F}^{\text{spin}}$.
3. **Off-Shell Gauge Ward Identity and On-Shell Current Conservation:**
   Because the integrand of $S_F^{\text{spin}}(\mathcal{R})$ is pointwise gauge-invariant under $\delta_\epsilon(v_\mu,b,c)=(D_\mu\epsilon,D_x\epsilon,D_y\epsilon)$ and $\delta_\epsilon(u,v)=i[\epsilon,(u,v)]$ for any $\epsilon\in\Gamma(\mathfrak{g}_0)$, integrating $\delta_{\epsilon,\text{gauge}}S_F^{\text{spin}}+\delta_{\epsilon,\text{ferm}}S_F^{\text{spin}}\equiv 0$ by parts cancels the $\partial\mathcal{R}$ surface terms $(n_x J_{x,F}^{\text{spin}}+n_y J_{y,F}^{\text{spin}})$ against the normal symbols of $\bar{D}_A,D_z$.    $$D_\mu J_{\mu,F}^{\text{spin}} + D_x J_{x,F}^{\text{spin}} + D_y J_{y,F}^{\text{spin}} = \mathcal{W}_F(u,v) := i\,\Bigl(\llbracket\bar{u}_{\dot{\alpha}},\mathcal{E}_u^{\dot{\alpha}}\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} + \llbracket\bar{v}_{\dot{\alpha}},\mathcal{E}_v^{\dot{\alpha}}\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} - \llbracket u^\alpha,\overline{\mathcal{E}}_u^\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}} + \llbracket\overline{\mathcal{E}}_v^\alpha,v_\alpha\rrbracket_{\mathfrak{g}}\Bigr),$$
