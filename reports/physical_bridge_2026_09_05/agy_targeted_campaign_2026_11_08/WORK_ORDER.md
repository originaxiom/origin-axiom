## Execution and claim guardrails

1. **One declared object per claim.** Record carrier/marking, complete
   metric, coefficient bundles, spin/lifts, holonomies, state, fields,
   action, physical norms, operators, domains, measuring data and maps.
   A change creates a named comparison model, not a silent correction.
2. **No hidden expected physics.** No assigned family tensor I3, tau_plus,
   source offset, negative mass, Robin condition, fitted coefficient or
   radial profile may certify a derivation. Such objects can be legitimate
   definitions or controls when labelled. Derive the physical identification
   and any factorization before using it in a conclusion.
3. **Keep every relevant sector.** Retain all four Weyl slots, all 248
   coefficient directions, compact adjoints, spin/flavour transitions,
   conjugates, gauge constraints, zero modes and relevant continua.
   A decoupling needs an invariant subspace or a controlled effective
   elimination with all induced interactions retained.
4. **Typed evidence.** Separate imported results, definitions, supplied
   choices, hand proofs, numerical checks, exact/interval certificates,
   independent review, admitted execution and empirical claims. Author-
   assigned Boolean flags are not external evidence. A mathematical
   negative must name its hypotheses and quantifiers.
5. **Actual discriminators.** Every producer must return witnesses and
   residuals consumed by the checks. Include deliberate wrong-object,
   wrong-domain, dropped-character and wrong-grading controls as relevant.
   Correct counts, dimensions, singular values or basis invariance alone
   cannot validate the missing physical map.
   A test that only accepts the producer's own success Boolean does not
   independently check its witness. Saved-output hashes establish custody,
   not a fresh recomputation or correctness of the archived result.
6. **Do not execute science early.** Before implementation, seal each
   experiment's design, controls and intended scope. After implementation,
   seal its complete execution specification: exact sources, tests,
   dependencies, environment, commands, outputs and admission machinery.
   Obtain source-complete nonauthoring review of that exact packet,
   separately committed exact main acceptance and strict admission from
   a clean isolated checkout. No scientific import, collection, CAS or
   diagnostic first run before those requirements. Hand/source work may
   continue. This campaign plan is not the required experiment design.
   A change to bound source/specification bytes invalidates the old exact
   binding; do not apply a review to its silently modified successor.
7. **Preserve first failures.** Keep original output and rejected versions;
   repair under a new pin/design as appropriate. Never silently widen
   tolerances, exchange domains or retrofit a preregistration after a peek.
   Software fixtures need their own proper status; they do not certify
   physical mathematics.
8. **Limited active scope.** Finish intake first. Thereafter maintain at
   most two active proof/code packets: full spectrum/current and boundary
   response. Publish a bounded checkpoint before building dependent claims.
   If external acceptance is unavailable, continue independent source/hand
   work; do not fabricate approval or declare an entire theory blocked.

Work in the existing AGY branch/checkout, not main or the physical seat's
working tree. Preserve other work. Commit and push UNREVIEWED checkpoints
