## 1. Duty 1: Finite-Cut Bosonic First Variation, Canonical Outward Momenta, and Radial Gauss Equation

Fix the balanced $h_4, W=1, \eta=\mathcal{O}$ parent on $\Sigma_{1,1}=C\setminus\{p\}$ with orientation $dx\wedge dy$ and metric $ds_\Sigma^2=\Omega^2(dx^2+dy^2)$, where $\Omega(x,y)=\ell/y$ for $x\pmod 1, y\ge Y>0$. Divide $\Sigma_{1,1}=C_Y\cup_{\Gamma_Y}T_Y$ at $\Gamma_Y=\{y=Y,x\in\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}\}$ ($A_c=\operatorname{Area}(C_Y)>0$, $A_t=\operatorname{Area}(T_Y)=\ell^2/Y$, $A=A_c+A_t$). On $\mathcal{R}\in\{C_Y,T_Y\}$ with outward coordinate unit normal $n=(n_x,n_y)$ and line element $ds_0$, $\Gamma_Y$ has $ds_0=dx$ and $n^{(\mathcal{R})}=(0,\sigma_{\mathcal{R}})$ with **opposite outward signs** $\sigma_{C_Y}=+1$ (core outward $+y$, Stokes orientation $-dx$, $\hat{n}^{(c)}=+\Omega^{-1}\partial_y$) and $\sigma_{T_Y}=-1$ (tail outward $-y$, Stokes orientation $+dx$, $\hat{n}^{(t)}=-\Omega^{-1}\partial_y$).

1. **Full Bosonic Action on $\mathbb{R}^4\times\mathcal{R}$ (`weave_curved_action_2026_10_08/PROOF.md` lines 70–125, `SAME_ACTION_GAUGE_BOUNDARY_RESPONSE_AND_CUBIC_2026_11_08.md` eqs. (1), (11)):**
   With external Hermitian gauge field $V_\mu$ ($\mu\in\{0,1,2,3\}$), internal connection $A_x,A_y$ ($A=A_x+i A_y$, $F_{xy}=\partial_x A_y-\partial_y A_x-i[A_x,A_y]$, $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu V_\nu-\partial_\nu V_\mu-i[V_\mu,V_\nu]$, $F_{\mu a}=\partial_\mu A_a-\partial_a V_\mu-i[V_\mu,A_a]$ for $a\in\{x,y\}$), and global spin-flavour fields $(q,r)\in\Gamma(S^1\otimes F\otimes\operatorname{ad}P)$ in a local parallel frame of the flat quaternion flavour bundle $(F,\epsilon_F)$ ($D_\mu q=\partial_\mu q-i[V_\mu,q]$, $R_q:=\bar{D}_A q=(\partial_x+i\partial_y-i\operatorname{ad}A)q$, $R_r:=\bar{D}_A r$, moment map $\mu_D:=F_{xy}+\Omega([q,q^\dagger]+[r,r^\dagger])$), the Wick-rotated bosonic action is
   $$S_{\text{bos}}(\mathcal{R}) = \frac{1}{g_6^2}\int d^4z \int_{\mathcal{R}} dx\,dy\,\kappa\!\left[\frac{\Omega^2}{2}\sum_{\mu<\nu} F_{\mu\nu}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\sum_\mu \bigl(F_{\mu x}^2 + F_{\mu y}^2\bigr) + \Omega\sum_\mu \bigl(|D_\mu q|^2 + |D_\mu r|^2\bigr) + \Omega^{-1}\bigl(|R_q|^2 + |R_r|^2\bigr) + 2\,\bigl|[q,r]\bigr|^2 + \frac{1}{2\Omega^2}\,\mu_D^2\right].$$

   $$\Pi_V^{\mu,(\mathcal{R})} = -\frac{\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2}\,F_{\mu y} = -\frac{\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2}\bigl(\partial_\mu A_y-\partial_y V_\mu-i[V_\mu,A_y]\bigr), \qquad \Pi_{A_x}^{(\mathcal{R})} = -\frac{\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2\,\Omega^2}\,\mu_D = -\frac{\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2\,\Omega^2}\Bigl(F_{xy}+\Omega\bigl([q,q^\dagger]+[r,r^\dagger]\bigr)\Bigr),$$
   $$\Pi_{A_y}^{(\mathcal{R})} \equiv 0, \qquad \Pi_q^{(\mathcal{R})} = -\frac{i\,\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2\,\Omega}\,\bar{D}_A q, \qquad \Pi_r^{(\mathcal{R})} = -\frac{i\,\sigma_{\mathcal{R}}}{g_6^2\,\Omega}\,\bar{D}_A r.$$
