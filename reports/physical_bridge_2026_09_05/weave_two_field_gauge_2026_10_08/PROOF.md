# A stationary two-field completion and its gauge algebra

This proof concerns the particular completion in DESIGN, not all
solutions of the supplied theory. Algebraic identities are checked by
two exact implementations; global analytic statements remain authored
arguments awaiting nonauthor acceptance.

## Local equations and the generated compact algebra

In the same regular SU3 of E8 choose beta=e4+e6 and delta=e5+e6.
Their difference is v=e4-e5. In a defining basis their root matrices
are X=E13 and Y=E23. Set K=diag(1,1,-2), H=2K/3. Then

    [H,X]=2X, [H,Y]=2Y, [X,Y]=0,
    [X,X dagger]+[Y,Y dagger]=K.

Use qhat=X/sqrt(6), rhat=Y/sqrt(6), A=sH/2, with the extending
spin connection nabla_S=d+i s and ds=-dvol/2. In a holomorphic spin
frame q=Omega^(1/2)qhat and similarly r. Spin and gauge connections
cancel since both generators have H weight two. Consequently

    Dbar q=Dbar r=0, [q,r]=0,
    F=-H dvol/4,
    [qhat,qhat dagger]+[rhat,rhat dagger]=K/6=H/4.

These are all residuals of the previously supplied positive-square
potential, with BOTH fields and their existing interactions retained.
The compact algebra generated by X,Y and their adjoints is su3:
[X,Y dagger]=E12, its adjoint gives E21, and the two diagonal
commutators give its Cartan. It is not one compact sl2.

## Global line bundles and finite norms

The complete once-punctured elliptic surface retracts to two circles.
All smooth line bundles on it are topologically trivial. Choose a
unitary trivialization of S and divide the negative spin connection
by three to obtain L with L^3=S^-1 as a line WITH connection. Its nine
flat Z3 twists on the two generators are priced choices. No claim is
made about a third root of a compactified log-canonical line.

The determinant-one bundle L+L+L^-2 induces an SU3 connection A=sK/3.
Both root lines Hom(L^-2,L) equal L^3. Thus qhat,rhat are the two
global identity sections of S tensor L^3, scaled by 1/sqrt(6).
Any commuting flat E6 connection can be added without changing the
residuals or covariant parallelism.

With n=Tr(X dagger X)=Tr(Y dagger Y)>0 the total Higgs squared norm
is n Area(Sigma)/3=2*pi*n/3. Curvature and every polynomial residual
term have bounded orthonormal-frame density and finite area. On the
cusp A differs from its limiting flat connection by -H dx/(4y),
which has finite affine kinetic norm. On the core the data are smooth.
Exhausting cutoffs as in the preceding global packet approximate the
fields in the stated graph norms. Therefore this is an admissible
finite-energy global configuration, not just a formal end solution.
Every auxiliary residual vanishes; the first variation vanishes and
the full bosonic Hessian is the sum of squared linearized residuals.
Kinetic terms remain positive. No fermion Hessian is being substituted
for that bosonic statement.

## Peripheral phase and the actual E8 quotient

The positive x cusp circle is opposite the oriented compact-core
boundary. Area(core_y)=2*pi-2*pi/y therefore gives

    U_spin(y)=exp(i H Area(core_y)/4),
    p0=lim U_spin=exp(i*pi*K/3).

In the defining three p0=diag(exp(i*pi/3),exp(i*pi/3),exp(-2*i*pi/3)).
The old p=exp(i*pi*v)=diag(-1,-1,1), so p/p0=omega I, omega^3=1.
This is NOT a change of the order of an element under a representation.
The spin connection is curved; a separate flat factor compensates it.

Let a1,a2 and b1,b2 be simple coroots of the two A2 components in
the color A2 centralizer inside this E6. All roots have length squared
two. Write tA=(a1+2a2)/3 and tB=(b1+2b2)/3. The root calculation
identifies the adjoint branching into three adjoint eights and two
conjugate (3 or bar3) tensor products. Choose epsilon=+1 or -1 so the
first two factors in a mixed tensor transform as a module and its dual
under the diagonal SU3. Then tA+epsilon*tB exponentiates to a central
element of E6. The eta=+1 or -1 choice is fixed by checking

    alpha.(eta*(tA+epsilon*tB)+K/6-v/2) is integral

for EVERY E8 root alpha. This verifies the required central identification
in (E6 x SU3)/Z3, including the charged mixed summands, rather than
guessing it from the groups' names. The E8 adjoint is faithful.
The 27 has not been discarded as it would be by passing to E6/Z3 alone.

In each root SU3 take D=diag(1,omega,omega^2) and P e_j=e_(j+1 mod3).
They are unitary of determinant one and [D,P]=omega I. The compensator
on a,b is the image of

    C_a=(D^eta,D^(eta*epsilon),I_color),
    C_b=(P,P,I_color).

Its commutator is exactly the E6 element just identified. Since the
surface group is free on a,b, these define a flat connection C.
The spin and flat bundles induce one E8 bundle through the actual
central quotient. Their total limiting peripheral is p on all roots;
the finite-circle correction is p exp(-i*pi*H/(2y)). Flat root choices
do not change a commutator. As before E8 is simply connected, so the
relative bundle identification at the end extends across the core.
This is admission in the same bundle/end class, not gauge equivalence
to the old flat quaternion background.

## The whole unbroken gauge algebra

A compact generator commuting with both Higgs fields also commutes
with their adjoints. Their generated su3 has E6 as its entire compact
centralizer in E8: the root calculation finds 72 orthogonal roots of
rank six and accounts for the remaining (3,27) and conjugate sectors.
No additional gauge direction can be hidden in those nontrivial threes.

The compensator pair has no invariant in either first-factor adjoint
eight: D and P generate the full matrix algebra on the defining three,
whose commutant consists only of scalar matrices. On each mixed tensor
the first two factors are 3 tensor bar3; the unique common invariant
is the identity tensor. Thus the ENTIRE invariant content is the color
adjoint eight plus one color three and one conjugate three.

The surviving Cartan has dimension two. The color roots are six long
roots of squared length two. The six nonzero weights of 3+bar3 have
squared length 2/3. Together these twelve roots are closed under their
reflections and admit a simple system with Cartan off-diagonals -1,-3.
The compact fixed algebra is consequently g2, not an algebra guessed
from its dimension fourteen. Its rank is two, whereas a compact
su3+su2+u1 subalgebra has rank four. Such an embedding is impossible
for this completed background. Extra disconnected symmetries do not
create the missing connected gauge directions.

This does not classify all E6 almost-commuting pairs with the same
central commutator. No maximal-centralizer theorem is assumed here.

## What changed about pairing and what did not follow

Solving J X=-X^T J and J Y=-Y^T J together with the two adjoint
equations gives only J=0 on the defining three. The preceding SO3
three retains its nonzero unitary dual intertwiner as a control.
Thus the previous explicit three-to-dual-three proof cannot simply
be reused for this pair. This neither proves a chiral index nor rules
out other global pairings. The full two-field fermion operator, its
essential gap and actual kernels have NOT been computed in this packet.

The global stationary construction is a positive. Its chosen gauge
completion fails the SM rank requirement, so an expensive spectrum
calculation on this particular phase is not the next SM-directed task.
Preserve both facts without enlarging either quantifier.
