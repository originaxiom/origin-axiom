# Algebraic scope: what the pincer does and does not establish

Authored before execution, October5. Conditional algebra; not independent
proof acceptance or an OA physical construction.

## 1. Cubic and the all-n rank identity

I(Q,Qc,L)=det Q+det Qc+det L+Tr(QLQc). In B1367's slots, the Hessian
with respect to Hu and (Hd,Llepton) at N=L22,nu=L21 is the weak skew
matrix J=[[0,1],[-1,0]] multiplying N, and -J multiplying nu. The triplet
block with D and (Dbar,dc) is the color identity multiplying N and nu.
Polarizing with a symmetric generation tensor gives, after harmless
column signs/permutations, diag(B,B) and diag(B,B,B), B=[G|Gnu]. Thus
rank MD=2 rank B, rank MT=3 rank B for every n and every such tensor,
including exceptional and zero tensors. Light Hu and D generation counts
are both n-rank B. Sampling is not needed for this factorization.

This does not count all physical modes, certify a vacuum, or extend an
operator domain. Adding 27bars and arbitrary higher operators is not
part of the cubic producer. A useful stronger conditional statement:
if the effective Hessian commutes with the same unbroken SU5 on all its
5 copies, Schur's lemma makes each multiplicity block B tensor I5.
Triplet and doublet masses then agree at any order, including conjugate
multiplicity spaces. The crucial assumption is the actual unbroken SU5,
not the fact that fields sit at points. A globally SM-broken background
does not supply it automatically.

## 2. Trace obstruction and the center

A bundle lift covering phi must satisfy C rho(w) C^-1=rho(phi(w)) for
every word w. Equal traces are necessary in every representation; one
exact mismatch proves no such ordinary E6-linear C exists. Conversely,
equal traces on finitely many words do not establish an E6 intertwiner.
We only use mismatches as sufficient obstructions.

E6 roots see the adjoint quotient, while the faithful 27 sees its Z3
center. A root-order-four representative may have g^4=z with z central.
Its three central translates have fourth powers z times zeta^(4j).
Since 4 is coprime to3, exactly one has fourth power1. Check this on
every 27 weight and retain the old defect. Multiplying by a central
element leaves all adjoint breaking data unchanged. The corrected lift
is a declared group-level repair, not an identity of old 27 characters.

The torus commutes with root SU2_beta. Hence its product with a Q8 line
is a genuine representation once the character relations and g^4=1
hold. Full-27 relation checks are required before using traces. The
second implementation uses 27=(Lambda^2 6,1)+(bar6,2), so its trace is
tr(Lambda^2 g^k)+tr(q) tr(bar6 g^k). This checks both center and trace
without importing the SO10-coordinate computation.

## 3. Location-sensitive symmetry selection control

The supplied Z4 comparator has W in SU5, W^4=1. The 5 at one site has
charge alpha=2; the bar5 at the other has triplet charge gamma=2 and
doublet charge delta=3. Combined mass phases are1 and i respectively.
The average P=1/4 sum_{k=0}^3 (-W)^k is diag(1,1,1,0,0).
Every summand is invertible and acts on a whole5, but their sum has
rank3. P is SM-invariant, not SU5-invariant. Independent invertible
changes of basis at the two endpoints cannot change this rank. There
is consequently no general rule reducing a sum of differently lifted
transports to a single removable basis change. The opposite projector
and trivial W are rejection controls.

This algebra implements the type of selection described in Witten,
https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/0201018v2, discrete-charge and fixed-circle
construction. The chosen Z4, location actions, Wilson matrix and fields
are supplied. Bare operator phases are selection rules, not derived
coupling magnitudes or a phenomenologically viable anomaly-free theory.
Its SU5 field content is not B1367's full E6 apex model; no direct
counterexample to the scoped cubic theorem is asserted.

## 4. Physical consequence to test, not presume

An actual OA rescue needs a compatible lifted symmetry of its admitted
background and global coupling tensors with transport dependence. If
the fixed Y3 population has no ordinary deck lift, this comparator
cannot be attached to that population. Neither outcome excludes other
generated carriers, broken-symmetry phases, non-deck symmetries or the
specified silver boundary route. The next physical duty is a common
action with a generated or explicitly supplied response law and full
fermion domain; separate branch successes cannot discharge it by addition.
