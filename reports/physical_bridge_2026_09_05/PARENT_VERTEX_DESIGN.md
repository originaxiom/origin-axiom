## Exclusions and next decision

This does not derive localized matter, a composite condensate,
supersymmetry of R29's tube action, quantum matching, actual global
mode mixing or a gapped mirror sector. An allowed H composite and
an elementary adjoint component are different tests. A zero bare
vertex is not a theorem that every effective vertex vanishes after
mixing or integrating out fields. E8 multiplicity is not generation
selection; changing the embedding is not lifting the original E6/Z3.

The proof, code, tests and frozen input list are sealed, committed,
pushed and remote-confirmed BEFORE the first import/execution.
Native JSON first, new tests second, then the explicitly enumerated
focused population. Preserve failures byte-faithfully; corrections
require a separately named seal. No target weakening. Cumulative
custody and governance are checked, retaining known old debts.

Each numbered assertion can fail independently. A failed embedding
does not invalidate the Grassmann identity; a successful embedding
does not establish a physical action. Report both halves and update
the common-model origin ledger, without a universal missing claim.
