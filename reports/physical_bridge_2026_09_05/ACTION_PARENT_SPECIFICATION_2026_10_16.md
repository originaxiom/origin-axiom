# What the existing action and operator maps supply

The audited constructions contain stationary actions, explicit coefficient maps,
positive geometric backgrounds and interacting operator transports. They are not
one derived physical theory. Their hypotheses differ, and the maps below do not
identify their fields, domains or observables merely by sharing an E8 label.
This is a personal proof-source audit, not a fresh execution or independent
analytic acceptance of the authored arguments.
The earlier [operator-chain crosswalk](OPERATOR_DERIVATION_CHAIN_2026_10_16.md)
retains the closed-fibre/family, critical-trace and total-domain connections;
this action audit supplements rather than replaces it.

## Native action and physical parent are different obligations

[R28's original argument](SOURCE_ACTION_PROOF.md) already supplies an enlarged
action for the entire nonlinear trace map T:

    S = sum p_(n+1) . (x_(n+1) - T(x_n)).

Its multiplier equation is the actual update; variation of x gives the adjoint
costate equation. Every orbit lifts with p=0. Thus the anti-symplectic half-step
does not obstruct every stationary action. R28's separate alternating DEL control
also survives. The excluded class is a regular autonomous real DEL action on the
same state with a fixed preserved area form, not all variational descriptions.
The multiplier construction adds variables and is saddle-type; it does not give
positive physical kinetic terms, spacetime, fermions or a measure.

[R94](REGISTER_ACTION_PROOF.md) has more local structure: a regular nonlinear
relative trace leaf, an orientation register and a signed generating primitive.
[R95](TRACE_PARENT_PROOF.md) carries that primitive to a specified relative
E8 Chern--Simons sector through the actual Sym4 representation. Its central
kernel loses SL2 lift signs. The later faithful
[rank-six registered embedding](weave_registered_embedding_2026_10_13/PROOF.md)
retains those signs but is a different embedding with a different trace index.
Neither a shared Lie-algebra name nor a rescaled primitive identifies these
relative three-dimensional sectors with the complete physical parent actions.
The regular-leaf primitive does not extend automatically to the singular
quaternion character; R28's enlarged action remains available there.

The exact remaining task in this audited chain is to derive or explicitly map
the physical fields, spacetime/time pairing, positive action and allowed end
variations from the native construction. Another supplied parent is not that map.

## The boundary law has several separate layers

An action fixes a boundary variation and current when its integrations by parts
are retained. An allowed variation space, source ensemble, proper gauge group
and singular selfadjoint domain are additional data unless a map derives them.
The full-real-parent and reverse-SUSY sources exhibit the distinction concretely:
the same retained functional has a nontrivial outer flux, and a locally harmonic
gauge-generated direction can survive when its parameter is not proper at an end.
Quotienting it away is not a harmless counting convention.

The fixed-fibre congruence theorem also has an exact scope. For bounded positive
invertible T, B=TAT on T^-1D(A) preserves selfadjointness, compact graph embedding
and the transported zero kernel. If T is not unitary, equality of nonzero masses
or of the fully normalized physical action does not follow. Whole-surface cusp
estimates need their actual uniform bounds and trace space, not a fibrewise name.

The next discriminating scientific question remains the induced symmetry action
on the actual critical trace quotient, including spin, coordinate and coefficient
lifts and the Green pairing. It must distinguish fixed-domain invariance from
covariance of a family. Then test an admissible end law in that same operator and
action. Keep finite puncture and complete cusp separate, and keep domain Lambda,
I-31's field postulate, modular integer n and Higgs cutoff L separate. A restricted
set of possible indices does not select a nonzero index or physical generations.
