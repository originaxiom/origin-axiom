October 2, 2026. Owner-requested implementation plan, version 0.3.

certificate of minimal axioms. It continues the [approved mission](PHYSICS_MISSION.md):

## What we are locking, and what we are not

Lock a versioned specification, its proof dependencies and its current
evidence. Do not lock an unproved universal claim or forbid later
correction. The working formulation is:

> Given explicit starting data, legal operations, admissibility conditions
> and equivalences, study their generated relational architecture. m004
> is a conditional minimum in the positive two-record, mixed,
> once-punctured-torus sector. Which grammar, realization, relations and
> states have physical roles remains to be derived and tested.

This formulation reuses [R57](ROOT_SCOPE_AUDIT.md). It does not assert
that the expanded architecture is physically complete, that all possible
states coexist, or that a larger search space solves the Standard Model.
Observations constrain the construction throughout; consequences not
used to select or calibrate it must be reserved for predictive tests.

The [existing operational contract](OPERATIONAL_CONTRACT.md) is preserved
byte-for-byte: it is part of R58's sealed science. This checkpoint is a
currency wrapper, not a competing contract or an edit of its historical
proof inputs. [The premise register](GENESIS_PREMISE_REGISTER.md) records
the route-qualified reconciliation; [the input manifest](GENESIS_RECONCILIATION_INPUTS.json)
pins the inspected record, not its truth.

## Immediate mathematical work, chosen before outcomes

First reconcile three questions, using existing proofs before new computation:

1. **Carrier versus puncture:** formulate B1380 with its actual finite-type
   surface, faithful F2 and homeomorphism hypotheses. Check which of these
   are premises, consequences or definitional choices. Do not use the
   no-selector theorem to prohibit all natural quotients.
   R79 disposition: faithful F2 recovers its unoriented commutator class;
   topological realization is available. Price physical realization and
   retained boundary markings separately. The same theory must transport
   slopes/holonomies/source and domain, not merely unmarked matrices.
2. **Primitive rule versus minimizer:** separate unit positive shears,
   allowed word lengths, torsion filtering and trace minimization. Legacy
   A5/A6 are alternative routes in the stated family, not evidence of two
   independent necessary axioms. Keep R57's all-positive-word result.
3. **Generated versus realized:** identify the actual generator/equivalence
   specification that admits each signed, swap, cover, filling or arithmetic
   object. A trace field, a common cover or a matrix inverse is not itself
   a proof of native reachability or physical admissibility.

For each proposed new proof, preregister the target implication, retained
premises, countermodels/comparison class and opposite controls. Preserve
first failures. A finite search can refute a universal by a witness; an
empty finite search cannot certify it. Axiom independence requires a
model of the other premises in which the target fails, not a sibling
that merely loses a preferred geometric feature.

## How the checkpoint constrains the next physics probe

The owner's selection-rule handoff is now personally read and scoped in
[the intake](SELECTION_RULE_INTAKE_2026_10_02.md). Adopt its action-level
vacuum question, not its blanket symmetry pre-run veto. A symmetric
ensemble, a fixed individual state and a same-domain operator intertwiner
are different propositions. Before a physical chirality verdict, earn
the full mirror/gauge/spinor/source/domain map, check the same action and
norms, establish attained stationary states modulo actual gauge, and
compute the complete fermion spectrum there. Mixed fixed/paired minima,
nonattained limits and changed domains are explicit outcomes. R76 does
not classify all sourced, nonflat or singular vacua. Structure-duality
covariance in R77 is a partial control, not that physical mirror map.

The current source/core drafts remain unsealed and unrun. A prescribed
current is not a derived source law. Before their execution, expose the
choice of added fields, internal profiles, couplings, gauge redundancy
and admissible variations; check both bulk and source equations. If a
conditional moment-map construction works, audit all extra light modes,
kinetic signs, operator/end domains and anomalies. Free rescue profiles
cannot be counted as parameter-free prediction.

Preserve the canonical paired model, the nonsplit triplet and the certified
partial-filling geometry as distinct controls. Geometric handedness,
CS, cohomological index, normalizable four-dimensional chiral fermions
and a stationary interacting vacuum are different obligations. No such
control is automatically the full physical model.

The next probe must reduce a named physical input, establish an actual
same-theory map, or produce a controlled observable. A foundation finding
may change the probe's premises, not the owner's full goal.

## Freeze, reopen and stop rules

Freeze a reviewed version with open choices visible. Reopen it for a
counterexample, missing dependency, changed legal move, failed physical
identification, recoverable lost evidence, or an observational conflict.
Retain provenance and the superseded version. New results are not silently
absorbed into old proofs.

Stop a particular inference when its required map or premise fails. State
the exact rejected model, coefficient, background, metric/action, source/end
law, operator domain and limit. Do not close the whole programme by that
failure, nor add an unpriced rescue. If the foundations underdetermine a
physical law, that is an open duty, not an achieved parameter-free theory.
