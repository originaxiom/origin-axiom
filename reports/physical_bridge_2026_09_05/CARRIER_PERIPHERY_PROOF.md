## M1. An unoriented peripheral class is recoverable from abstract F2

Choose any free basis (a,b) and let c=[a,b]. Any other free basis is its
image under an automorphism. Nielsen's commutator theorem says that this
automorphism takes c to a conjugate of c or c^-1. Thus the unordered pair
of conjugacy classes, and the conjugacy class of <c>, is independent of
the chosen basis. It is transported by every isomorphism of rank-two
free groups. In particular, a chosen coordinate basis is not a separate
input needed to recover this unoriented datum.

The sign is the determinant of the action on abelianization (Goldman
section 1.2). Thus specifying an orientation in H1 specifies an oriented
commutator class, whereas abstract F2 alone does not select its sign.
This is not a theorem that all Aut-invariant conjugacy data consist of
this class. Nor is c a primitive element of F2: it is a commutator. Here
<c> is maximal cyclic, since c is not a proper power. Its cyclically
reduced four-letter word has no smaller repeated period. The free-group
centralizer theorem therefore gives C_F2(c)=<c>.

Nielsen's criterion also has a converse for endomorphisms of F2, as
Gupta-Shpilrain state on page 1. Unimodular homology is not that criterion.
P5's known map a->a,b->b[a,b] retains identity homology but does not
preserve [c] up to inversion. This check reuses that counterexample and
does not reprove its injectivity. The canonical datum belongs to the
isomorphism category, not every map or quotient.

## M2. Topological recovery and physical realization are separate

The pair (F2,[<c>]) has the standard one-holed-torus realization with
geometric boundary c. For this surface, Goldman propositions 1.1.1-1.1.2
identify the free-boundary mapping class group with Out(F2), and section
1.2 identifies Out(F2) with GL(2,Z). A chosen automorphism therefore has
a topological surface realization up to the specified equivalences.
These classical facts are stronger than a claim that periphery is
arbitrary; a nongeometric graph model does not contradict them.

B1380 separately excludes the other stated finite-type F2 surfaces
when their peripheral classes must be permuted by the golden map:
their nonzero H1 classes would have a finite orbit, but a power of the
golden matrix has no eigenvalue +/-1. The one-holed torus has peripheral
H1 class zero and survives. That result is reused, not a new census.

Neither construction proves that a physical theory must retain faithful
F2 words, restrict its carriers to finite-type surfaces, or interpret this
topological surface as physical spacetime/internal geometry. R57's
characteristic abelianization remains natural and equivariant; it kills
c. One can recover a surface functorially up to equivalence without
proving that nature uses it. A demand to retain a specific observable
may justify fidelity; a no-selector slogan alone does not.

## M3. Outer orientation-positive data lose an integer normalized lift

Let Aut(F2,c) be automorphisms fixing c exactly. By Nielsen, every
orientation-positive outer class has a representative in Aut(F2,c):
conjugate the image of c back to c. Two such representatives in the
same outer class differ by Inner(g). Fixing c requires g in C_F2(c),
so g=c^k. Conversely every Inner(c^k) fixes c. The centre of F2 is
trivial, so these are distinct automorphisms for distinct k. Hence

    1 -> Z=<Inner(c)> -> Aut(F2,c) -> SL(2,Z) -> 1.

This kernel is central within Aut(F2,c), since f(c)=c. If automorphisms
inverting c are included, they invert the kernel; do not call the full
GL extension central. Auroux section 2.2 explicitly distinguishes the
boundary-pointwise-fixed one-holed-torus group B3 and its boundary-twist
kernel from SL(2,Z). Fixing a puncture/free boundary is not fixing every
boundary point. A fibre homology matrix alone retains no preferred
boundary-fixed lift. A supplied based substitution or a chosen generator
lift may add such information; this statement does not erase it or prove
that no further principle can select it.

## M4. Residual lifts transport the same unmarked group, not fixed labels

For f0(c)=c and fk=Inner(c^k)*f0, define

    Gk=<F2,t_k | t_k x t_k^-1=fk(x)>.

The isomorphism Gk->G0 fixes F2 and takes t_k to c^k t. Direct substitution
checks the relations; the inverse sends t to c^-k t_k. Because f0 fixes
c, the subgroup generated by c,t is Z2: c has infinite order in the
embedded free subgroup and t has nonzero base exponent. Thus the
peripheral coordinate transformation is the integer shear

    (p,q) -> (p+kq,q),  c^p t_k^q -> c^(p+kq)t^q.

If rho0 is any representation of G0, rho_k=rho0 composed with this map
has exactly the transported holonomies. R57's filling condition says
rho_k descends through slope (p,q) iff rho0(c^(p+kq)t^q)=1.
Consequently a numeric slope label cannot be held fixed while silently
changing lift. For abelian fibre representations rho0(c)=1, this effect
is invisible in holonomy; they are a deliberately blind control, not
proof of universal invisibility. The explicit nonabelian finite comparator
is checked by the sealed executable, not assumed to be faithful geometry.

Transporting the slope and all theory data together preserves this
description change. This does not prove physically distinct vacua,
force an extra coupling, or make a boundary choice illegal. Conversely
an unmarked manifold/homology identification alone does not transport
specified filling, source, norm or operator-domain data. R58 already
requires those extra data to travel under the actual map.

## M5. Explicit golden lifts connect to the record without a new carrier

Sigma=(ab,a), sigma^-1=(b,Ba), so sigma is an actual automorphism, not
inferred from its determinant. Its square is (aba,ab), with matrix LR.
Writing u=aba gives sigma^2(c)=u c u^-1. Normalize by Inner(u^-1):

    f0=(aba,ba), f0(c)=c.

B1303 uses g=(aab,ab) and normalizes it by Inner(a^-1), obtaining this
same f0. Also sigma^2=Inner(ab)*g. These equations are checked as words;
the closing/index counts based on the other presentation are not
certified by them. All f_k have the same LR abelianization and outer
class. Their differing peripheral transversals illustrate M3-M4, not
a newly selected topology, an extension of legal updates or chirality.
