## Verdict

Not all successful pieces have been shown compatible with each other,
and their union has not been shown compatible with all observations.
There are genuine structural compatibilities, a definite internal
quantum-consistency problem in a specified incomplete model, a retained
coupling mismatch in a restricted approximation, and many physical tests
that are not yet available for a common theory. These are different
statuses; neither "everything agrees" nor "the programme is ruled out"
follows from the evidence.

The retained sourced three/zero kernel is conditional progress. Its
uncancelled extra-U(1) anomaly prevents treating that sector ALONE as a
complete dynamical 4D gauge theory. The added-field R21 EFT cancels it,
so a blanket anomaly no-go would also be wrong. Its source realization
and full observed low-energy theory are still separate requirements.

### The tested mirror wall is not the desired minimal low-energy content

R25's physical free-wall ansatz supplies opposite-index modes and an
opposite anomaly. Their normalized coupling to the unchanged constant
gauge field remains nonzero even when the modes move toward the cusp.
Thus this mechanism does not produce a mirror-free low-energy theory.
[Wall calculation](BOUNDARY_WALL.md).

Do not turn that statement into an exclusion of every mirror model:
an experimental bound requires its actual broken-phase masses,
couplings, production and decays. Conversely, do not hide the additional
states merely by calling them distant. The benchmark remains the
measured chiral charged-current structure, not vector-like doubling.
[PDG electroweak review, (10.2) and (10.6)](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-standard-model.pdf).

## 4. What remains untested in the common physical theory

The audited construction does not yet provide the simultaneous inputs
needed for the following observational checks. This is not evidence of
agreement, a numerical discrepancy, or a claim that the entire corpus
has never studied these topics.

| Requirement | What must be produced before claiming agreement |
|---|---|
| Particle masses and Higgs physics | The actual matched spectrum, symmetry-breaking scales, pole masses and relevant rates in the same model. |
| Quark/lepton flavor and CP | Normalized Yukawa matrices and predicted mixing/CP observables, with the genuine adjustable inputs stated. |
| Neutrino oscillations | A physical mass/mixing mechanism and its parameters. A singlet slot is compatible with several mechanisms but selects none. Oscillations require physics beyond the minimal massless-neutrino SM. [PDG neutrino review, sections 14.2, 14.6 and 14.7](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-neutrino-mixing.pdf). |
| Additional gauge, mirror and scalar states | Their masses, currents, mixing, normalized wavefunctions, production/decay channels and low-energy effects. No universal mass bound can be imported by name. |
| Proton stability | Actual baryon-violating operators, mediator masses and flavor coefficients; an inverse-fit unification scale alone is not a lifetime. |
| Gravity and cosmology | A common dynamical metric/quantum sector and predictions for tested gravitational effects, expansion, abundances and perturbations. |
| Four-dimensional validity | A controlled treatment of neutral/source/continuum modes and a stated energy/regulator range for the effective description. |
