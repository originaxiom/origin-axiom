## The already derived gauge roster matters here

In this SAME regular SU6+SU3_colour+SU2_weak embedding, with
6=rho_Q tensor3_family, the four one-dimensional Q8 multiplicity modules
(trivial and three parities) have no weak doublets. All weak doublets
are in the odd-centre rho_Q module. This is the full branching statement,
not just absence of a zero mode under one puncture condition. The old
packet used it also to analyze its particular zero-spectrum problem;
that separate analytic conclusion is not transported to a new metric.

The matrices used in the new retained embedding are unitary conjugates
of these same three quaternion doublets. That change of basis does not
change the Q8 centre or this weak-module branching. The old reference
has an accepting control: the rho multiplicity module DOES contain weak
doublets. We are not declaring Higgs fields absent from E8.

W60's candidate Higgs instead uses a one-dimensional parity coefficient
chi_p tensor L_(3w). At a matching half-period it becomes fibre-trivial,
as our exact check confirms. But that line has commutator +1, whereas
the weak-doublet sectors of THIS E8 adjoint have commutator -1. A flat
Wilson line cannot change that commutator. A nonzero equivariant map
from the former to the latter would require X=-X; thus the bare parity
line is not the gauge coefficient of this parent's weak-doublet sector.

This is a MAP duty, not a programme-wide Higgs kill. W60 supplied its
field dictionary; it did not establish that dictionary as this E8
parent's full gauge-field roster. Another gauge embedding, additional
fields, a genuinely admitted spin/gauge factor, or another parent may
change the map. Each needs explicit transitions and its own operator;
we will not insert one merely because the count is attractive.

## Consequence for the next physical step

Keep W60's half-period equivalence and parity cancellation as genuine
fibre positives. Do not describe them as the newly solved Higgs of our
existing E8 construction. First identify the actual Higgs carrier and
its puncture/weak/central action in that same physical parent. Then
solve its total-space mode, norm and full interaction vertex. This is
precisely why successes from different constructions cannot simply
be added together. It does not refute W60's separately supplied model,
its numerical potential or possible future alternative completions.
