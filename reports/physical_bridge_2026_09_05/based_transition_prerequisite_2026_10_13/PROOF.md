## Words and coefficient maps

Let L=(a,ab), R=(ab,b), with inverse substitutions L^-1=(a,Ab)
and R^-1=(aB,b). Composition means substitution, right factor first.
Then S=R L^-1 R=(b,BAb), T=L. The two generator images suffice to
check equality of free-group homomorphisms. Direct reduction gives

    S4=Inner(c), c=BabA,       (ST)3=S2.

In homology S is the usual matrix (0,-1;1,0), so S4 is identity
there, but Inner(c) is not identity as a based automorphism. Main
B1641 uses Delta=L R^-1 L and c'=aBAb; its Delta4 relation is the
same kind of peripheral relation, with different conventions. Do not
identify c and c' as literal words or identify stalks and cohomology.

The preceding packet's actual matrices are

    a0=diag(i,-i), b0=(0,1;-1,0),
    A=diag(-a0,a0,-a0), B=diag(b0,-b0,-b0),
    L6=P13 tensor (1+a0)/sqrt(2),
    R6=P23 tensor (1-b0)/sqrt(2).

They obey L6 A L6*=A, L6 B L6*=AB, R6 A R6*=AB,
R6 B R6*=B. Thus the based covariance is checked, not inferred

## The full adjoint cannot be repaired by the same scalar

The retained embedding's complete branching is

    (35,1,1)+(1,8,1)+(1,1,3)+(20,1,2)
    +(15,bar3,1)+(bar15,3,1)+(6,3,2)+(bar6,bar3,2).

Under -I6, exterior degree determines parity. The even population is
35+8+3+45+45=136; the odd population is40+36+36=112. The actual
E8 roots and eight Cartans check the same count. Both conjugate
sectors are present. The fibre commutator sigma=Ad(rho(c)) is therefore
not scalar on this adjoint, even though it is scalar on the defining6.

For the tested prescription, the S4 side is r I248, while the inner
side is sigma, r=-1 for ordinary spin or +1 for canonical Spin-c.
The residual vanishes on112 or136 internal dimensions respectively,
but not on all248. Including the four internal spin components gives
residual ranks544 or448. For any common complex scalar r, equality
on the even part requires r=1 and equality on the odd part r=-1;
both are impossible. A common scalar R/lift rephasing cannot fix this
prescription on the complete adjoint.

Crucial scope: this is an elementary necessary-condition calculation
UNDER the declared factorized prescription, not a proof that its
prescription is the actual tangent/spin transport of the based object.
Connection holonomy is not interchangeable with a transition function.
Point-pushing maps need not have identity tangent/spin lifts merely
because their homology action is identity. A changed lift group or
non-scalar gluing may change the premise. Setting the loop factor to
sigma is a positive algebraic control, not a derived transition on
generators. No companion is discarded to make a scalar match pass.

## W60's half-period positive and its physical duty

For each nontrivial sign pair (sa,sb), conjugation by one of b0,a0,a0b0
sends a0,b0 to sa a0,sb b0. It gives an exact unitary bundle
isomorphism rho_Q tensor chi_p -> rho_Q. This is not an approximation
of W60's lattice eigenvalues. It supports the half-period blindness
on a fibre domain transported by that map; an untransported domain
would require its own check.

At w=p, q_H=3 gives 3w=p modulo the integral period lattice. Hence
chi_p tensor L_(3w) is trivial, and the other two parity copies remain
nontrivial. A flat trivial coefficient on a smooth compact elliptic
fibre has the constant scalar and constant (0,1)-form in its harmonic
complex; both have finite fibre norm. A regular removable puncture
retains them. This does not prove the full coupled total-space
operator kills that mode, does not supply its horizontal wavefunction
or end condition, and does not solve the Higgs potential or backreaction.
W60's supplied content, heat cutoff, finite grid/Fourier fit and numerical
minimum remain a separate conditional calculation, not certified here.
