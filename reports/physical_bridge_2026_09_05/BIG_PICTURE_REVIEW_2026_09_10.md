## Assessment

The strongest pattern is not that the programme repeatedly fails to make
chirality. It is that a successful calculation for one sector is followed
by a difficult test of whether that sector belongs to a complete theory.
The conditional sourced three/zero kernel survives. Its extra gauge
anomaly, the response transported to the ends, and the compensating free
wall spectrum also survive, with their respective hypotheses intact.

The working hypothesis is that we are trying to extract an autonomous
four-dimensional theory from an open, prescribed-source subsystem while
holding much of its completing dynamics fixed. That would explain several
walls together. It is an inference about this construction, not a theorem
that the originating object is a boundary theory, a proof of holography,
or an assurance that an acceptable completion exists.

The alternative deserves equal attention: the useful mathematics may
support a family of conditional models without yet distinguishing the
physical theory we want. An error in the analytic comparison or a bad
low-energy truncation could also change the picture. Progress should test
these alternatives, not make the completion hypothesis unfalsifiable.

## 1. The same assumptions produce both the successes and the walls

| Retained assumption | What it gives | What it also requires or prevents |
|---|---|---|
| One commuting Cartan profile, common-sign strong sources | A computable charged index and the R19 pair-free sector | Chirality follows charge sign; adding more fields with that same response reinforces the abelian anomaly in R22. |
| Finite volume and the unchanged constant gauge mode | Finite gauge normalization | Normalized charged mirrors do not lose that gauge coupling merely by moving toward a cusp. |
| Nontrivial scalar flat transport | Removal of the extra vector-like pair | The relevant holonomy variations are nonnormalizable cusp data, not automatically four-dimensional dynamical moduli. |
| The C3-compatible nontrivial orbit with parent E6/Z3 | A genuine global lift of the sourced adjoint construction | A parent 27 from the earlier simply connected E6 action cannot simply be imported into this parent. |
| The specified singular source and maximal domain | The complete weighted-cohomology comparison | Source activation, strengths and the physical domain still need a variational interpretation. |
| An added free scalar reference-mass wall | An explicit compensating index and anomaly polynomial | The tested physical interface supplies a mirror sector; the scalar interpolation is additional dynamics. |

These are not six independent failures. Several are the other side of
the mechanism that made a positive result possible. The source-sign
calculation is particularly informative: in the stated class, the linear
and cubic abelian traces are proportional to sums of positive
`dim(R_q)*abs(q)` and `dim(R_q)*abs(q)^3`. Merely enlarging the same-source
field list cannot repair them. That is a design constraint, not a reason
to repeat the same enlargement with another representation.
See [R22](GEOMETRIC_COMPLETION.md), [R19](HOLONOMY_SPECTRUM.md),
[R20](HOLONOMY_EQUIVARIANCE.md), [R18](WEIGHTED_COHOMOLOGY.md) and
[R25](BOUNDARY_WALL.md).

There is a precise local-to-global warning in the parent literature.
Pantev--Wijnholt's example uses two Cartan potentials and treats its extra
U(1)s as nondynamical; their becoming massive upon compactification is an
expectation, not a completed solution of our problem. Our R19 already
records why finite gauge normalization changes this obligation. The new
emphasis is to make that distinction organize the next work, rather than
leave it as a caveat after a mode count.
[Pantev--Wijnholt, sections 3.4 and 3.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.1968).

Likewise, the free-wall result must not become a universal mirror theorem.
A reference defining a determinant is not itself an added physical
particle sector. General anomaly-inflow constructions can couple
anomalous boundary fermions to a gapped bulk; that does not provide the
missing regulated action for our singular ends or cancel R23's remaining
gauge-zero-mode variation by assertion.
[Witten--Yonekura, introduction and section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.08775),
[the actual local response](MASS_INFLOW.md).

## 2. Three consistent appearances are not three independent predictions

The interior index, total mass-eigenline flux, and free-wall compensation
are related. Under the declared hypotheses, the records give respectively
`+k`, `+k` for positive charge, and `-k`. Different calculations checking
this relation are valuable: a sign, boundary, or representation mistake
could have made them disagree. But agreement is not three independent
pieces of empirical evidence for three physical generations. They share
the source topology and index data.
[Spectrum](HOLONOMY_SPECTRUM.md),
[total flux](GLOBAL_MASS_FLUX.md), [wall](BOUNDARY_WALL.md).

Nor is it fair to dismiss the three as simply an arbitrary typed number.
The selected m202 symmetry has six cusp fixed ends and hence three proper
fixed arcs. That is real geometric information. The unanswered physical
question in this path is why this locus is activated as the prescribed
source, with the requisite sign, strength and domain. R15 also preserves
possible additional closed fixed components without silently charging
them. Its one- and two-arc controls, and R19's general twisted relative
formula, show which part of the count is generic once those data are
specified. The actual character polynomial and its symmetry-compatible
orbit contain more particular information than the integer alone.
[Source construction](GLOBAL_SINGULAR.md),
[character calculation](HOLONOMY_SPECTRUM.md).

The useful next comparison is therefore not another search for the
number three. It is to hold the physical rules fixed and determine which
normalized observables distinguish the proposed object/symmetry/source
choice from the already available controls. Anomaly cancellation alone
does not select three: R21's enlarged anomaly-free representation works
for any number of complete copies. Conversely, its restricted
parallel-Higgs anomaly does not erase the nonparallel completion.
[R21](ANOMALY_COMPLETION.md).

Protected integers remain assets even when they do not select a theory.
Their stability is also why they cannot, by themselves, determine the
wavefunction normalization, scale and interactions that their defining
index deliberately ignores. This is a limitation of that invariant as a
selector, not a theorem that arithmetic or topology cannot contribute to
physical values through additional analytic structure.

## 3. Several apparent contradictions compare different questions

The history gives concrete reasons to resist both premature celebration
and premature rejection:

- The annular zero and the sourced nonzero index use different cores and
  domains. Drilling source arcs changes the core Euler characteristic;
  each source annulus still has Euler characteristic zero. Neither result
  needs to be retracted to retain the other.
  [R24](GLOBAL_MASS_FLUX.md).
- The older fourteen-dimensional gauge algebra and the verified
  twelve-generator stabilizer belong to different breaking stages. The
  singlet reduction exists; whether its vacuum is selected is a different
  question. These are gauge-algebra dimensions, not a prediction of twelve
  or fourteen spacetime dimensions.
  [Recovered physical steps](RECOVERED_PHYSICAL_STEPS.md).
- Eleven extra tree-level scalar zero modes were not an all-orders
  exclusion. R5's leading one-loop masses are positive on its declared
  common-quartic ray. This is a genuine constructive successor, not a
  certificate for all couplings or all orders.
  [R4](VACUUM_MODEL.md), [R5](QUANTUM_VACUUM.md).
- The withdrawn cosmological shortfall depended on identifying an anyon
  level with a gravitational level. Removing that identification removes
  the alleged prediction; it does not produce a successful cosmological
  prediction. The retraction is already present and must not be forgotten.
  [B980](../../frontier/B980_k3_conflation/FINDINGS.md).
- A symmetry relating two vacua does not put mirror fermions inside each
  vacuum. R20's nontrivial holonomy-conjugate pair has the same charged
  chirality for the same signed source and is not a CP pair. R25's free
  wall actually adds opposite-index modes. Those are different statements.
  [R20](HOLONOMY_EQUIVARIANCE.md), [R25](BOUNDARY_WALL.md).

The same discipline applies to selection. The lack of a canonical
representative is not automatically a physical free parameter. B1232's
banked third-column lemma says that a coupling annihilating the kernel of
a quotient is independent of its splitting; it does not prove that every
actual coupling has this property. First determine the allowed field
redefinitions and the image in normalized observables. Only then ask
whether an observable choice needs dynamics or boundary data.

Symmetry of the action, symmetry of a state, and gauge redundancy must
also be separated. Demanding that every candidate vacuum be fixed by all
symmetries can exclude symmetry-breaking states by construction. This
observation does not select such a state, establish spontaneous breaking,
or authorize an arbitrary asymmetric source.

## 4. The audit's own method needs a change of emphasis

The records have become much better at preserving assumptions and first
failures. That is necessary, but it is not itself evidence for physical
correctness. Multiple symbolic, numerical and topological controls can
all depend on one analytic-domain identification. R18's complete weighted
comparison is a high-leverage dependency: later passing tests do not
substitute for checking its estimates, domains and global gluing.
The same applies to the physical chirality/reality dictionary and the
finite-collar versus complete-end distinction in R23--R25.

There has also been a sequencing risk. We progressively strengthened the
charged calculation while the source action and the full low-energy
spectrum remained explicit inputs or duties. The reports did say this;
the strategic mistake would be to count each strengthening as equivalent
progress on those other duties. A conditional index theorem and a
stationary finite-action background answer different questions.

The finite-volume issue makes this concrete again. It helps normalize
the gauge zero mode, but it does not by itself certify a finite isolated
set of every other light field. The neutral cusp one-form control warns
against borrowing the charged estimate for the full theory. It is a
control on an unpunctured torus channel, not a proof of the entire
punctured neutral spectrum or a universal obstruction to an effective
four-dimensional description. Actual spectral thresholds and couplings
are needed before either verdict.
[Cusp estimate and its control](CUSP_TAIL.md).

The broad R25 receipt has 341 passes, 14 failures and 8 errors, with the
same failed/error IDs as R24. That establishes a scoped non-regression
comparison, not a healthy end-to-end certificate. Historical failed
instruments should remain immutable and discoverable. A future live
reproducibility path can identify their successful successors explicitly,
without deleting failures, relaxing assertions, or hiding which original
check still fails. No such test reorganization is performed here.

Finally, recurring names and invariants in the repository are not a
statistical sample of nature. The recurrence atlas itself distinguishes

## 5. Revised strategy: one simultaneous completion test

The next unit of progress should be a sourced chiral effective theory
whose successful ingredients coexist, not another isolated integer. This
does not replace the full TOE objective or abandon the current path.

1. **Declare one common model.** Specify the geometry, global gauge group,
   bundles and fields, kinetic terms, source and end action, admissible
   variations, operator domains and gauge profiles together. Distinguish
   an action's choices from its stationary solutions and from observable
   parameters. Do not silently combine the earlier E6 parent 27s, the
   later E6/Z3 adjoint spectrum, and a different cone's fields.
2. **Test the physical source/end equations early.** Determine whether
   the prescribed asymptotics can arise from that variational problem,
   with controlled energy and quantum gauge variation. The existing
   proper sources have infinite total length/charge; this creates an
   action and possible renormalization duty, not a proof that no defect
   action can exist. A finite-norm trial Higgs is not a stationary
   solution. Test one explicitly justified interacting/end mechanism
   against R22 and R25's exact assumptions, instead of adding another
   four-dimensional cancellation list.
3. **Retain index stability, within its actual scope.** R26's preparation
   asks about bounded, parity-odd perturbations compactly supported away
   from all ends, or suitably decaying there, on the fixed graph domain.
   First audit the R18 dependency; then prove index stability and
   distinguish it from preservation of the exact three/zero kernel.
   An actual completion mixing charge sectors, changing the domain, or
   changing the source/end asymptotics needs the full new operator, not
   an automatic application of that proposed theorem. R26 is still
   unexecuted, and would not alone solve the anomaly or source problem.
4. **Require the spectrum, anomaly and normalization together.** Include
   neutral and end sectors, the fate of the extra U(1), allowed masses and
   normalized overlaps in the same low-energy limit. A massive anomalous
   gauge boson needs its consistent quantum matching, not just a mass
   term. Moving a mode out of the core needs a coupling calculation, not
   just a localization plot. A surviving chiral sector must be identified
   after these operations, not only before them.
5. **Ask for a discriminating physical output.** After quotienting
   unobservable choices, seek an observable relation that depends on the
   proposed origin data and differs from appropriate controls. Declare
   adjustable inputs and normalization before comparison. Gravity then
   has to join this same construction through physical modes, signs and
   universal coupling; an independently appended Einstein term or a
   spin-two slot is not that join.

The immediate success criterion is not a complete TOE: it is one
well-defined, quantum-consistent sourced low-energy model retaining a
chiral sector without the tested light-mirror cost, with its remaining
inputs honestly listed. Failure of a specified candidate should identify
which of its assumptions failed. Repeated success only after adding
independent choices would instead be evidence that this route remains
model engineering, not the desired derivation.
