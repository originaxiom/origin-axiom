projective family for each fixed q>0, q!=1, on sufficiently small conical

peripheral marking and singular asymptotic class are inputs. Coordinates
x,y have period one and r>0. Hermitian adjoint is the fixed matrix adjoint.

explicitly replaced. Retaining actual holonomy does not retain the old
metric or old fluctuation domain. The existing complete-metric canonical
model is neither invalidated nor proved equivalent by this calculation.
