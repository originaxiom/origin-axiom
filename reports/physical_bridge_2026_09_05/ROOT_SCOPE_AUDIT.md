September 30, 2026. Path-local audit R57.

universal physical root.** Keeping the generated architecture is a useful

exclusion if its operations, carriers and physical relations go unaudited.

not follow. No alternative is thereby proved physical.

## The core result we should keep

For the supplied positive two-record shears,

    L_a R_b = [[1+ab,a],[b,1]],
    trace = 2+ab, det(L_a R_b-I) = -ab.

For positive integers a,b, trace minimization or trivial cokernel selects
a=b=1. More generally, any positive mixed cyclic word can start at an LR
transition. Every additional shear strictly increases the trace of its
positive matrix. Thus LR/RL give the unique minimal mixed positive class
at all word lengths, not just in the old finite grid.

The identification with m004 additionally uses the once-punctured-torus
carrier. These hypotheses matter: two records, positive unit shears, a
specified notion of complexity and a specified geometric realization.
The existing UNIQUENESS theorem explicitly says its axioms are supplied.
Main's B1323 already distinguishes the theorem from the choice of criterion;
we must not describe that distinction as missing or newly discovered.

There is also a small witness correction, not a theorem failure. The
legacy text says SL-conjugate "via the swap", but the swap has determinant
-1. An actual SL witness is L^-1(LR)L=RL. B1384 exchanges the labels L/R
relative to the core; its RP is the core's LP. Neither issue kills m004.

## Where the stronger framing can exclude too much

### A minimum is not the origin of the grammar

The primitive operations are prior to their first mixed product. The
identity and unmixed shears are also grammar states, although they are
not mixed hyperbolic states. Consequently "root" here means a
distinguished minimum of a specified sector, not automatically an
initial object, first physical event or universal generator.

If the record swap P is a legal update, F=LP has determinant -1 and
F^2=LR. Its substitution length is three, against five for the square.
Thus the orientation restriction changes the admissible comparison,
as B1380 already observes. We should retain this fork, not replace m004
by the Gieseking manifold and announce a new universal root.

Likewise, an invertible matrix need not have its inverse in a chosen
positive update monoid. L^-1 has a negative entry and takes (0,1) outside
the nonnegative count cone. If inverse updates are legal on Z^2, the
signed closure is valid and (L^2 R^-1)^2=-I. If not, that is an extension
of the grammar. The original handoff and our September 26 intake already
state this qualification. It must remain at every downstream use.

### Naturality does not prohibit forgetting information

B1380 section 3 argues that the no-unforced-collapse theorem forbids
abelianizing the word group. **That inference is invalid.**

A symmetry with no fixed candidate prevents choosing an invariant
representative. It does not prevent an invariant quotient. Explicitly,

    q:F2 -> Z^2,   q(ab)=q(ba)=(1,1),
    q f = f_ab q.

The last identity holds for every group homomorphism f because it sends
commutators to commutators. The quotient forgets word order naturally,
including under the golden substitution and record swap. The two words
are distinct; neither is chosen in preference to the other. The control
also keeps the free commutator nontrivial before taking its zero image,
so the test does not accidentally abelianize its input.

This does **not** refute the conditional surface result. Given a
finite-type surface with fundamental group F2 and a homeomorphism
realizing the golden substitution, B1380's peripheral-class argument
distinguishes the once-punctured torus among its four stated types.
But faithful word realization and the surface category remain premises.
The no-selector theorem cannot supply them. An explicit prohibition on
information loss would be an additional requirement to justify, not the
same theorem under a new name.

### A cover theorem does not establish cover-only physics

P022's addendum and B1385 correctly require compatible data instead of
adding unrelated ingredient lists. They then narrow the possible join
to cover refinements and one commensurability class. The end-to-end
chain repeats this stronger conclusion. **The exclusivity is unearned.**

Shapiro identifies specified cohomology groups for induction along a
cover. It does not classify all possible data transports, nor does it
automatically transport an action, its nonlinear products, a kinetic
inner product or a normalizable operator domain. Those are physical
obligations on the cover route too.

There is already exact, conditional non-cover transport of flat data:
for a filled slope s,

    pi1(M(s)) = pi1(M)/<<s>>,
    rho descends exactly when rho(s)=1.

This does not preserve every connection or any claimed spectrum. It
does show that "only covers transport data" is too strong at the same
flat-system level used to justify cover transport. A new physical join
still requires a derived action, domain and compatibility laws.

Nor does a partial filling close all ends. Our
[existing partial-filling report](PARTIAL_FILLING.md) retains its named
interval-certified two-cusp example. No geometric certification is
rerun here; the topological distinction itself follows by attaching
solid tori to only a proper subset of boundary tori. The
[official SnapPy documentation](https://snappy.computop.org/manifold.html#snappy.Manifold.dehn_fill)
also specifies cusp-by-cusp filling. This is not a new chirality result,
not a selected slope and not proof the arithmetic/gauge data survive.

The field obstruction can legitimately exclude a common finite cover
between specified manifolds. It cannot exclude every physical interaction
between all generated states. B1390 already corrects the separate
field-equals-class error; this audit does not repeat that correction as
new. A cover-only research lane is legitimate when labelled as such.
It is not the whole architecture by a theorem of commensurability.

### A symmetry constraint must keep the actual domain

The exact potential (x^2-1)^2 is symmetric, with two nonsymmetric minima.
So no unique invariant choice does not mean no asymmetric solution.
This is a logical control, not a proposed chiral vacuum of this model.

B1385's global parity lemma has substantially more hypotheses than a
symmetry of an abstract grammar: an isometry negating the harmonic
class, transported cusp partitions and a summed index in its stated
frame. Nothing here refutes that lemma. To carry such a negative into
another model, transport the coefficient, background, action and end
domain too. The September 30 negative-pattern synthesis is useful
organization, not a new universal physical impossibility theorem.

## A safer formulation of the programme

> Given explicitly specified update rules, admissibility conditions and
> equivalences, construct their generated relational system. In its
> positive two-record once-punctured-torus sector, m004 is the
> distinguished minimal mixed state. Whether that sector, its root,
> or particular relations have physical priority remains to be derived.

This is not permission to treat every imaginable manifold as generated,
every quotient as physically harmless, or every generated state as real.
Keep alternatives in a comparison ledger, with their entry assumptions
and lost/retained data. Membership, equivalence, dynamics and physical
realization are different questions. No argument currently inspected
guarantees that the restricted grammar omits nothing needed by nature.

## Next work and acceptance conditions

1. **Write the operational contract.** Distinguish the positive monoid,
   inverse-closed group and swap-extended grammar; state the count domain,
   carrier category and meaning of "minimal" for each. Do not change
   them silently to rescue a desired physical result.
2. **Audit what equivalence forgets.** LR/RL can represent one unbased
   manifold while different markings, coefficient lifts and boundary
   data remain distinct. Any physical quotient must preserve the data
   actually used by its operator and interactions.
3. **Test relations at the action level.** First reuse B1384's existing
   cover/induction construction and check the nonlinear bracket, kinetic
   pairing and end-domain map, not another generation count. Also retain
   the already existing
   partial-filling descent gate: which actual background kills the slope,
   and what action/end data survive? A failed map is local to its data.
4. **Compare complete candidates on the same obligations.** A related
   collection must supply one consistent account of fields, interactions,
   norms, anomalies and evolution. It need not be assumed in advance
   to live on one selected manifold; neither can disconnected ingredient
   lists be added up and called a theory.
5. **Require an empirical discriminator.** A full architecture is not a
   physical theory until a derived observable distinguishes it from its
   controls and is compared with reality. Mathematical resemblance and
   success at unit tests do not do this.
