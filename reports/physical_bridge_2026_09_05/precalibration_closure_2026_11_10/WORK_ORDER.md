State the exact primitive, registering relation, allowed operations,

restriction to m004, a quaternion register, a cover or E8. Show the
selection rule and quantify over its actual alternatives. The observed

constructions must not be concealed as a derivation from genesis.

Derive the field/action/measurement dictionary or show its remaining
freedom. Preserve registering, lift and measuring data under every
quotient or transport. An operational observer map is distinct from
qualia: consciousness claims need a precise definition and empirical
earning conditions. Neither is to be silently inferred from a register.

Count a reduction only if the independent choice disappears without
returning through a representation, carrier, metric, normalization,
field assignment, boundary condition, state, measure or fitted datum.
Prefer a proof of invariance or a surviving family of inequivalent
solutions over unsupported declarations of uniqueness. Parameter-free
inside a supplied model is distinct from parameter-free from genesis.
