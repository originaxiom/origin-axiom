## The positive connection

For a finite cover p:Y->X with pulled-back base metric and positive bundle
metric h, a rank-r bundle E gives F=p_*E with the direct-sum metric.
Section transport is an L2 isometry, intertwining the transported
connection, its formal adjoint, and minimal/maximal operator domains.
Other closed domains are carried over if explicitly prescribed; the
map does not select them. Genuine boundary and source data must also
be transported. A different metric is outside this claim.

The natural algebra is p_*End(E), locally the sheetwise block diagonal
inside End(F). Products, brackets, metric adjoints and single-trace
local action densities are preserved. With the same admissible fields
and variations, their integrated classical actions and stationarity
problems agree. This is stronger than a cohomology-dimension match.

It is nevertheless a rewriting of a supplied theory, not a derivation
of the action from genesis. It proves no admissible harmonic background,

## The controls identify three places a new assumption enters

**Changing the algebra changes field content.** For a three-sheet cover
and rank-two E, p_*End(E) has complex fibre dimension 12, whereas End(F)
has dimension 36. The additional off-diagonal maps are not automatically
fields of the original theory. Projecting them away is not a Lie-algebra
homomorphism: two projected-to-zero matrices can have a nonzero projected
commutator. A larger parent may be a legitimate candidate, but needs its
own justification and quantum treatment. These dimensions are not
particle counts, nor is this an SL5/E8 embedding obstruction.

**Preserving L2 does not preserve every nonlinear action.** If
x_i=||u_i||^2, the transported sheetwise quartic is sum x_i^2. Replacing
it by (sum x_i)^2 adds cross terms 2 sum_(i<j) x_i*x_j. Both expressions
give equivalent L4 norms at finite cover degree, but different actions.
The exact comparator detects this difference while the properly retained
single-trace quartic passes. Keep the actual interaction tensor, not only
the vector-space dimension or a schematic norm symbol.

**Symmetry does not determine an end law.** The interval boundary control
has distinct Dirichlet and Neumann maximal-isotropic subspaces, both
preserved by the same cyclic sheet permutation. This is an explicit
underdetermination example, not a statement that both are admissible on
the actual nonsplit cusp. That cusp requires its own end action and
analytic domain test.

The nonunitary block-monomial control also confirms that a frame change
must carry the Hermitian metric. Keeping the old metric changes the norm.
Its unipotent cube is a fibre-holonomy check, not a geometric deck no-go.
