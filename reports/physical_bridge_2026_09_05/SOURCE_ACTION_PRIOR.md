Primary sources checked online on September 13 and read directly from

- [Braun et al., arXiv:1812.06072](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.06072),
  printed pages 8--13, especially (2.13), (2.18), (2.22)--(2.24).
  The field is the adjoint ONE-FORM phi, not the auxiliary scalar F.
  The static twisted potential is a sum of F/D residual squares.

These are standard physical frameworks, not newly derived laws of the
originating object. R28 asks what they actually imply for the existing
background, and explicitly does not substitute electrostatic energy
of F for the one-form theory's static potential.
