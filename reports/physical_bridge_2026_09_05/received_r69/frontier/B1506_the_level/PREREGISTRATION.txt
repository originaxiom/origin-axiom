# B1506 PREREGISTRATION — THE LEVEL: is the M₆ triplet natural over the genesis's root, and what does the root's unique 3-fold cover carry in the Standard-Model frame?

**Sealed 2026-09-30, before the complete Standard-Model-frame census of s961 (M₃) and M₆ is run. Seat: cc (the SM-derivation
branch). Occasion: the owner's "lets do 1 first" of 2026-09-30 — sL-5, the number question: is a count taken on a cover or on its
quotient, one family or three?**

## 0. The question

B1384 S3 showed that B1378's M₆ triplet V ⊕ τ²V ⊕ τ⁴V (index −3 in each of the six sectors) is the restriction of one object on M₂,
Ind V (index −1), and said the mathematics does not decide which level is physical (sL-5). This arc asks what the genesis's own
relations fix. The root m004 is the base of every cover the tower generates. The deck of Mₙ over the root is ℤ/n, generated by τ,
conjugation by the root's meridian. So which objects does the root's whole deck fix, and what are their counts?

Notation. Mₙ is the n-fold cyclic cover of m004 (M₃ = s961, M₂ = m206). A background is a flat E₆ connection in B1374's
Standard-Model frame: holonomy in the image G′ of SL(2)_β × U(1)_Y × U(1)_γ in E₆. Its doublet sector s (Q, u^c, e^c, d^c, L, ν^c,
charges (s_Y, s_γ) = (1,3), (−4,3), (6,3), (2,1), (−3,1), (0,−5)) is a rank-2 non-split extension of β_s by α_s, with extension
character λ = α_s/β_s, the same for every sector. B1374/B1375 parametrise backgrounds by a lift (χ, ψ_Y, ψ_γ) with λ = χ².

## 2. Proved now

**T1 (the loci lemma, every level).** Let λ be a finite-order character of π₁(Mₙ). Then h¹(λ) ≥ 1 iff one of:
- λ = 1;
- λ is non-trivial on the fibre and λ(tₙ) = 1.

For λ trivial on the fibre, the only other loci are tₙ ↦ φ^{±2n}, which are not roots of unity. *Proof.* In B1381's presentation, for
σ = λ|_F ≠ 1:
- H¹(Γₙ; λ) is the λ(tₙ)-twisted invariants of φⁿ on H¹(F; σ).
- Restriction H¹(F; σ) → H¹(∂F; ℂ) = ℂ is an isomorphism, since H⁰(F; σ) = 0 and H²(F, ∂F; σ) ≅ H₀(F; σ) = 0.
- φ fixes ∂F pointwise, so φⁿ acts trivially, and the condition is λ(tₙ) = 1.

For σ = 1 the block is s·I − Mⁿ (B1381). ∎ So every finite-order locus is trivial on the cusp.

**T2 (a pullback keeps the index).** Let V′ be a doublet module on M_m that satisfies T5 (B1297: the sub or the quotient character is
trivial on the cusp), and let m | n. Then I(Mₙ, Res V′) = I(M_m, V′). *Proof.* By Shapiro, I(Mₙ, Res V′) = Σ_L I(M_m, V′ ⊗ L) over
the characters L of Γ_m/Γₙ = ℤ/(n/m). Each L ≠ 1 is non-trivial on t_m. By T1 the sub and quotient characters agree on the cusp, and
the twist moves both off 1, so V′ ⊗ L fails T5 and has index 0. ∎ A single background therefore has one count on every level: its
own, by Shapiro, below it, and by T2 above it.

**T3 (lifts).** A G′-background on Mₙ lifts to SL(2)_β × U(1)² iff λ is a square in Hom(H₁(Mₙ), ℂ*).
- H₂(Mₙ; ℤ) = H¹(Mₙ, ∂) = 0 (one cusp, H₁(Mₙ, ∂) finite), so H²(Mₙ; ℂ*) = 0.
- The only obstruction is the square root of λ.

Non-square finite-order loci exist exactly when the fibre torsion has even order. Among n ≤ 6 that is M₃ ((ℤ/4)²) and M₆
(ℤ/8 ⊕ ℤ/40). On M₁, M₂, M₄, M₅ every locus is a square.

**T4 (the kernel).** E₆ ⊃ (SU(2) × SU(6))/⟨(−1, −1)⟩: B1368's 27 = (1,15) + (2,6̄) and 78 = (3,1) + (1,35) + (2,20). In the frame,
U(1)_γ ⊂ SU(6) has e^{iπQ_γ} = −1_{SU(6)}, since Q_γ is odd on the 6 (s_γ odd on every doublet sector). So k₀ = (−1_{SL(2)}, 1,
e^{iπQ_γ}) maps to 1 in E₆. Twisting a lift by a character with values in ⟨k₀⟩ does not change the background.

**T5 (the root's census).** On M₁ = m004 the fibre carries no non-trivial finite character (coker(M − I) = 0). So the only
finite-order locus is λ = 1, and the only T5-candidate doublet module is the unipotent U (the abelian extension class). U lies in
SL(2), so it is self-dual and I(U) = 0. Hence a firing background is never fixed by the whole deck of its level: if it were, it would
descend to m004, and Shapiro would give its index as a sum of m004 indices, all 0.

**T6 (the triplet descends to s961).**
- **(a) The orbit.** By §1, τ³ acts on B1378's seed S = (χ, ψ_Y, ψ_γ) by the character ε ⊗ k₀. By T4, S∘τ³ = S as backgrounds. S∘τ
  and S∘τ² are different backgrounds. So S's root-deck orbit is {S, S∘τ, S∘τ²} = {S, S∘τ², S∘τ⁴}, which is B1378's triplet. **The
  triplet is fixed by the whole deck of M₆ over the root.**
- **(b) Descent.** τ³ generates the deck of M₆ over M₃ (cyclic of order 2), so S extends to a background D on s961.
  - λ extends uniquely: T1 forces λ′(t₃) = 1.
  - The extensions of D are its twists by the order-2 central elements that commute with the frame: −1_{SL(2)} flips every doublet
    sector, and e^{iπQ_Y} flips the sectors with odd s_Y, Q and L.
  - So there are four descents.
- **(c) The meridian.**
  - For the seed, S(t₆) = [(N, 1)] with N a non-trivial unipotent (χ(t₆) = ψ(t₆) = 1; B1384 B).
  - Write D(t₃) = [(u, w)]. Then (u², w²) is (N, 1) or (N, 1)·k₀ = (−N, e^{iπQ_γ}).
  - −N has no square root in SL(2, ℂ): the eigenvalues would be ±i, distinct, so the square would be diagonalisable, and −N is not.
  - Hence u² = N (μ_u = ±1 on the invariant line) and w² = 1.
  - The meridian value of sector s's sub character is α_s(t₃) = σ·y^{s_Y}, with σ = ±1 the same for every sector (every s_γ is
    odd) and y = ±1.
  - Over (Q, u^c, e^c, d^c, L, ν^c) the pattern is σ·(y, 1, 1, 1, y, 1). The four descents realise the four (σ, y), and exactly
    one, D₀, has α_s(t₃) = 1 in all six sectors.
- **(d) The index.** Shapiro gives I(M₆, S_s) = I(M₃, D₀,s) + I(M₃, D₀,s ⊗ L), with L the deck character (L(t₃) = −1). D₀,s ⊗ L has
  α(t₃) = −1 and fails T5. So **I(M₃, D₀,s) = I(M₆, S_s) = −1 in every sector: D₀ is generation-shaped on s961, (−1)⁶.**
- **(e) The orbit on s961.** τD₀ ≅ D₀ would make D₀ descend to m004, against T5. So D₀, τD₀, τ²D₀ are three backgrounds, and they
  pull back to S, S∘τ, S∘τ².
- **(f) No lift.** D₀'s λ′ is not a square on s961. Otherwise D₀ would lie in B1375's lifted census of s961, which is silent. ∎

**T7 (counts by level).**
- The root object R = Ind_{M₃}^{m004} D₀ has I = I(M₃, D₀) = −1 in every sector, by Shapiro.
- Its pullback to Mₙ is a sum over the gcd(n, 3) double cosets, and each summand keeps its index by T2.
- So the count of R on Mₙ is **−gcd(n, 3)**: one on every level prime to 3, and three on M₃, M₆, M₉, ….

**Corollary (sL-5, sharpened).** For one background, the count does not depend on the level (Shapiro and T2). Three is the count of
the root deck's orbit of backgrounds:
- as the sum over s961's three deck translates, if the deck is kept as a global symmetry;
- one on the root, as the induced object, if the deck is gauged.

The genesis fixes both levels, the root and its unique 3-fold cover. The two readings of the deck, gauged or global, are what remain
of the question.

**Corollary (the frame fence).** The triplet's three blocks carry different SL(2)_β holonomies:
- τ² multiplies χ by a character of order 8, so it multiplies the extension character λ = χ² by one of order 4;
- λ is the adjoint character of the SL(2)_β holonomy, which no U(1) twist changes.

So the blocks are three distinct E₆ vacua. No frame whose family index is a tensor factor beside SL(2)_β can hold them as three
families of one vacuum, since such a frame gives every family the same λ. That covers E₆ × SU(3) ⊂ E₈, and the parabolic P_{2,3} of the SL5
commutant, whose pairing census (B1384 E, cited and not re-derived) already excluded this orbit. A physical count of three would
need a parent that carries three different SL(2)_β holonomies as families, or the global-deck reading with three copies of E₆.

## 3. Decided at design time (to be computed as checks; failure would refute a theorem above, and the arc would say which step)

- **D1.** Of the seed's four descents to s961 (on B1384's RS presentation of M₃), exactly one has all six sectors T5-candidates, and
  its six counts are (−1)⁶. None of the other three is generation-shaped.
- **D2.** D₀'s orbit under s961's deck has three members, and their pullbacks to M₆ are the three members of B1378's triplet.
- **D3.** D₀'s extension character is not a square on s961.
- **D4.** s961's lifted census is silent (B1375, reproduced on a second presentation). m004's census is U alone, with I = 0.
- **D5.** The root object R has counts −gcd(n, 3) on M₁…M₆, computed by induction and restriction.
- **D6 (one direction of a bijection).** Every generation-shaped background on s961 pulls back to one on M₆ with the same six counts
  (T2: its charged sectors are T5-candidates), fixed by τ³. The converse is not proved in general, because T6(c) uses the seed's
  non-trivial unipotent meridian; it is P7 below.
- **D7.** On M₂, M₄, M₅ every locus is a square. The complete census there is B1375's, up to its redundancy. B1375's lift data per
  background at its N are |Hom(H₁, ±1)| · |Hom(H₁, μ₅) ∩ μ_N|:
  - 2 · 25 = 50 on M₂ and on M₄ (N = 60);
  - 2 on M₅ (N = 132, which has no fifth roots);
  - 8 · 25 = 200 on M₆ (N = 120).

  The fifth roots are a kernel too: (η, η⁻²) ∈ U(1)_Y × U(1)_γ acts on every weight of the 6 of SU(6) by η^{6Y−2γ} = 1.
- **D8.** No root-deck orbit of a firing background has size 1 (T5). So on M₅, whose deck has prime order, every generation-shaped
  orbit is free.

## 4. Sealed predictions (open)

**Q1 — s961's complete census.** The census covers every locus, square and non-square. Backgrounds are parametrised directly by
(λ, κ, ψ_Y), with α_s = ψ_Y^{s_Y} κ^{s_γ} λ^{(1−s_γ)/2} (κ = χψ_γ on a lift).
- **Read:** the number F₃ of firing doublet modules, the number G₃ of generation-shaped backgrounds (G₃ ≥ 3 and G₃ ≡ 0 mod 3 are
  forced), and their signs and ν^c counts.
- **P1** (prior ~85%): every generation-shaped background on s961 has |count| = 1 in each charged sector.
- **P2** (~80%): the generation-shaped backgrounds on s961 split equally between the two signs.
- **P3** (~60%): all 12 non-square loci of s961 carry firing modules.

**Q2 — M₆'s complete census**, square and non-square loci, with root-deck orbits.
- **P4** (~85%): the lifted generation-shaped backgrounds number 336 = 67 200 / (8 · 25), B1375's lift data divided by its
  redundancy.
- **P5** (~75%): the τ³-fixed ones among them are neither none nor all. At least one is forced (the seed); fewer than 336 is the
  prediction.
- **P6** (~70%): M₆ also carries non-lifted generation-shaped backgrounds.
- **P7** (~75%): the converse of D6 — every τ³-fixed generation-shaped background on M₆ has a descent to s961 whose six sectors are
  all T5-candidates, so it is the pullback of a generation-shaped background there. Then the τ³-fixed ones on M₆ number exactly G₃.
- **P8** (~80%): no generation-shaped orbit of size 2 on M₄ or M₆. Such an orbit descends to M₂; if its descent were all-candidate
  it would be generation-shaped on M₂, which has none. So P8 fails only if the descent is not all-candidate.
- **Read:** the number of orbits of each size on M₄ and M₆, and the pullback check of D6.
