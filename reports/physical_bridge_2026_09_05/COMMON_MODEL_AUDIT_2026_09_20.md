## 2. Benchmark C: a fixed, finite-width common specification

Use this as a calculational benchmark, NOT a selected physical vacuum:

- Internal space: one fixed compact smooth truncation of the R19 core,
  with its chosen metric and three disjoint contractible proper tubes.
  Keep width positive and the outer cutoff fixed during each calculation.
  The separate flat comparator is never substituted for this geometry.
- Gauge group: the actual H=C_(E6/Z3)(u) of R21, with its specified
  central flat bundle. Use the R30 nontrivial pair-free characters when
  invoking its acyclicity result. Do not import a parent fundamental 27.
- Spacetime and fields: supplied four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime;
  R29 gauge field, central h, local tube Q=Phi=1_4; R30 positive-norm
  fermions with the declared odd-left/even-right dictionary. R37 adds
  S=10_2 on the same tube domains and explicitly prices that addition.
- Action: R29 bosonic functional plus R30 quadratic fermions. The
  proposed S kinetic/potential/Yukawa terms are those in the
  [R37 working note](SOURCE_SCALAR_WORKING_2026_09_20.md), not silently
  inferred from gauge invariance. This is an H EFT, not yet a full
  supersymmetric E6 or torsion-free G2 completion.
- Domain: absolute fermion data at the true outer boundary, full
  transmission through artificial tube interfaces; weighted natural
  Neumann data for tube scalars. A scalar defined only on a tube is not
  a smooth whole-space field after zero extension.
- Background: solve the coupled stationary equations with S=0,
  parallel Q on each tube, flat A and h=dF. R37 now checks the added
  variations and preserves this stationary point in the specified EFT.
- Limits: first a calculation at fixed positive width and cutoff.
  Width, complete-cusp, quantum/UV and low-energy limits are separate
  duties; none may be exchanged without uniform estimates.

This specification deliberately keeps the actual resolved pairs. It
does not insert R19's singular three/zero count by hand. Nor does it
silently add R21's spectator fermions to an already paired spectrum.
The complete physical field dictionary and other form/gauge/source/end
sectors still require derivation from an identified parent action.

The ledger is NOT a numerical count of independent free parameters.
Redundancies, scaling conventions and ordinary physical choices must
be quotiented before such a count. A theory can be useful with inputs;
the question is what non-input consequences survive after fixing them.

- [ ] Phase gate: establish selective mirror removal without unwanted
  symmetry breaking, with the remaining anomaly and heavy matching
  terms. A scalar instability or divergent Gaussian coefficient is not
  evidence for the desired phase.
- [ ] Discrimination gate: hold the physical prescription fixed and
  compare an in-class member and an out-of-class control. Class-wide
  invariance is not a failure if the class is the object. Retuning
  couplings/source rules per comparator is not discrimination.
