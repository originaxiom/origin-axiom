## Different ends, different meanings

- The fibre puncture is a deleted marked point in every torus. Across
  the base it gives a puncture section after adjoining that point;
  it is not an enclosing sphere or the modular base's cusp.
- The modular base is noncompact. At level1 it has one cusp, represented
  by Im(tau) tending to infinity. In the stable compactification the
  elliptic fibre degenerates to a nodal cubic, topologically a pinched
  torus; the precise marked compactification of Y also tracks the
  puncture and possible stable components.
- A hyperbolic thread's cusp and its torus cross-sections are ends of
  that three-dimensional mapping torus, not the cusp of the moduli
  space. Our silver calculation uses a supplied regulated compact
  core and a torus boundary with a specified physical response.
  No map identifies that regulator with a universal boundary of Y
  or with a boundary of the observed cosmos.
