P=[[0,1],[1,0]]. Matrix invertibility, legality of an inverse operation,
and the state domain are separate requirements.

These are alternative specifications or layers, not mutually identical
axioms and not a claim that all their states coexist. Positive matrices
can also act on Z^2; requiring the nonnegative count cone is an additional
restriction, not a hidden consequence of using L and R. No selected
carrier is automatically spacetime or an internal compactification.

## Equivalences and retained information

Record what an equivalence preserves BEFORE assigning it a physical role.

- Unbased conjugacy can identify LR and RL while based coordinates differ.
  Retain markings if a proposed observable or boundary map uses them.
- Abelianization is a natural quotient, not an invariant choice of a
  representative. It loses commutators and order; whether that loss is
  physically acceptable requires an observable, not a no-selector slogan.
- Gauge transformations must preserve the specified field/action/domain
  data. A group automorphism or geometric isometry is not automatically
  a gauge redundancy, parity operation or physical time reversal.
- A cover and an induced coefficient system must be named as a pair.
  Changing coefficients while comparing covers changes the problem.
- Holonomy in one model fibre is not the total-space deck action. The
  received deck-descent proof already distinguishes them; do not repeat
  its elementary comparator as a new discovery.

An admissible quotient need not preserve all mathematical information.
It must preserve what the proposed physical theory treats as observable,
or state how those observables are changed. That test remains a duty.

## Required physical specification for each candidate

Before combining two results, record the carrier and coefficients;
background and metric; physical field dictionary and gauge redundancy;
action and couplings; admissible variations; source/end laws and operator
domains; limiting procedure; and normalized observable map. State which
items are derived, conventional, supplied or empirically calibrated.

The relation audit starts with a finite cover supplied as data. Its
transport can preserve a supplied action without selecting that action,
the cover, the physical parent or an end law. This distinction is the
first test in [the sealed design](COVER_ACTION_DESIGN.md).

## Execution order and stop rules

1. Verify finite-cover action transport and its opposite controls.
2. Identify which retained action actually admits the nonsplit candidate
   and the required field embedding. Do not turn a coefficient-space
   inclusion into a parent-theory identification without the map.
3. Compare the latest genesis-side proposal with the end-law requirement.
   A label for a boundary condition is not a derivation of that condition.
4. Only then use that same model's domain to count physical modes and
   compute normalized interactions. Gravity and empirical duties remain.

Stop an inference, not the whole programme, when a required map fails.
Keep its exact hypotheses. A changed premise is a separately declared
candidate, not a repair hidden in the original result. Do not close a
different route by transferring an unverified no-go.
