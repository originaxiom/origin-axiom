follow from the stated premises, and does using it exclude an alternative
before its physical role has been tested? This audit quantifies over the
declared two-record positive grammar and explicit changes of its move,

all realizations of the philosophical principle, or all physical theories.

physical root, completeness of the architecture and cover-only physical

theorem should survive while its use to forbid quotients fails. These
are falsifiable expectations for the finite controls below, not results
of running the instrument. The general deductions have proofs below;
finite checks are safeguards, not proofs of universal classifications.

## The conditional root survives

Use the repository's core convention:

    L = [[1,1],[0,1]], R = [[1,0],[1,1]], P = [[0,1],[1,0]].
    L_a R_b = [[1+ab,a],[b,1]], tr = 2+ab, det = 1,
    det(L_a R_b-I) = -ab.

For positive integers a,b, the first mixed two-block closure has minimal
trace and trivial cokernel exactly at a=b=1. For any longer positive
mixed word, cyclically rotate until its first two letters are LR. Its
matrix is then A=LR followed by more positive shears. For a positive
matrix X=[[x,y],[z,t]], right multiplication by L increases its trace by
z>0 and by R increases it by y>0. Positivity persists. Thus trace three
is the strict mixed positive minimum up to cyclic order. This is a
proof for all word lengths, not an inference from a finite scan.

P conjugates LR to RL but det(P)=-1; the legacy theorem/test's phrase
"SL-conjugate via the swap" has the wrong witness. The SL conjugacy
itself is true: L^-1 (LR) L=RL. The two routes agree, not a root kill.
The B1384 producer exchanges the names L and R relative to the core;
its RP is our LP. This convention difference is not a contradiction.

Calling [LR] the first mixed positive state is justified. Calling it
the origin of everything is not. The identity/empty word and the two
shears precede it as grammar states. Algebraically the moves generate
the closure; m004 is a distinguished output of them. The topological
identification additionally uses the once-punctured-torus realization.
No finite matrix test here certifies a hyperbolic isometry.

## Legal inverses and the orientation fork

All positive products of L and R have nonnegative entries. L^-1 has a
negative entry and is not in that monoid, although det(L)=1. Indeed
L^-1(0,1)=(-1,1), outside nonnegative record counts. Mathematical
invertibility, a total state domain and an inverse-closed set of legal
updates are three separate specifications. If "reversible" explicitly
requires legal inverses on Z^2, the group extension is legitimate; it
must not be inferred merely from embedding a positive monoid in GL.

With inverses admitted, (L^2 R^-1)^2=-I, so -LR is reachable. B1384 says
"once inverses are admitted" and is appropriately conditional here.
With P admitted as an update, F=LP=[[1,1],[1,0]] has det=-1 and F^2=LR.
Its total substitution length is three rather than five for F^2. Thus
orientation is an admissibility restriction, not a consequence of this
unrestricted length comparison. This recovers the existing Gieseking
fork algebra without choosing it as a new universal root.

## The selector theorem does not forbid a quotient

For G acting on X with no fixed point, a G-equivariant map from a
one-point set (with trivial G action) to X cannot exist: its image
would be fixed. This proves the no-preferred-representative statement.
But the quotient map X -> X/G is equivariant and requires no choice of
representative. A two-point orbit under exchange is already a complete
counterexample to the implication "no selector implies no quotient".

The relevant nontrivial example is abelianization q:F2 -> Z^2. For
every group homomorphism f, commutators map to commutators, so f induces
f_ab and q f=f_ab q. In particular this holds for the golden substitution
and the record swap. The distinct reduced words ab and ba have the same
image (1,1), and the nontrivial commutator has image zero. This loss of
information is natural, not an illicit selection of one word.

B1380 section 3 invokes the no-unforced-collapse selector theorem to
forbid this abelianization. That inference does not follow. Requiring
a faithful word realization can instead be declared as a separate
premise. This correction does NOT refute its finite-type surface
theorem: GIVEN pi1(S)=F2 and a homeomorphism realizing sigma, its
peripheral-class argument still singles out the once-punctured torus
among the stated four surface types. It does not derive that surface
category, faithful F2 realization or the physical relevance of either.

## Relations must retain their own hypotheses

P022's useful guard is that ingredients need an actual compatibility map.
The stronger conclusion that only cover refinements can ever supply
physics is not established by Shapiro's cohomology identity. A theorem
for cover transport is not a classification of all possible transport.
Even exact vector-space or cohomology transport is not automatically
transport of a nonlinear action, kinetic pairing or operator domain.

There is elementary non-cover transport for flat systems. For a filling
slope s, van Kampen gives pi1(M(s))=pi1(M)/<<s>>. A representation rho
descends iff rho(s)=1. This is conditional, exact transport, not a
claim that every background descends, that its cohomology is unchanged,
or that a physical action survives. The group-only control uses Z^2,
kills its first generator and tests one nontrivial descending and one
blocked representation; it is NOT a manifold or chiral-spectrum probe.

Nor does every filling close every cusp: attaching solid tori to only
some boundary tori leaves the others. Our existing PARTIAL_FILLING.md
retains a named interval-certified two-cusp witness. Its raw geometry
is not rerun by this audit. The official SnapPy documentation also
explicitly fills individual cusps:
https://snappy.computop.org/manifold.html#snappy.Manifold.dehn_fill
No claim of new filling existence, preserved arithmetic, physical
chirality or a selected slope is made. A cover-only lane is a useful
declared restriction, not the full generated architecture by theorem.

## Symmetry and physics remain separate

The symmetric potential V(x)=(x^2-1)^2 has two symmetry-related minima
at +/-1, neither fixed by x -> -x. It provides a counterexample to
"symmetric equations require every solution to be symmetric", not a
chirality mechanism for this programme. Our earlier intake already
states this distinction. B1385 L3 has additional hypotheses: an actual
isometry negating the harmonic class, a transported cusp partition and
the summed relative index. The toy example does not refute that lemma
or any same-domain pairing theorem. Each application must retain its
background, metric, coefficient and end-domain signature.
