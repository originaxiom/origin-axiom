## Conventions and analytic scope

Letters 1=a, 2=b, negatives inverse; c=abAB.
Function composition f*g means apply g then f. Homology matrices have
image exponent vectors as columns. Inner(g)(x)=g x g^-1.
The canonical datum is the conjugacy class of the maximal cyclic
subgroup <c>, equivalently the unordered conjugacy classes [c],[c^-1].
It is natural under group isomorphisms, not every endomorphism. No
uniqueness claim for all Aut-invariant conjugacy data is tested.

f0(a)=aba, f0(b)=ba; its matrix is LR=[[2,1],[1,1]] and f0(c)=c.
fk=Inner(c^k)*f0. Gk has t_k x t_k^-1=fk(x), and its peripheral pair
is (c,t_k). The declared map to G0 fixes the free generators and sends
t_k to c^k*t. Its slope convention is c^p*t_k^q -> c^(p+kq)*t^q.
Orientation reversal and a change of base-circle convention require
their own transported signs; do not import this orientation-positive
formula unchanged. Compact boundary fixed pointwise, free boundary,
puncture and a chosen cusp basis are distinct categories.

The analytic statements M1-M5 in CARRIER_PERIPHERY_PROOF.md are authored
deductions from the cited classical theorems, not independent review.
Finite safeguards below do not prove the universal theorems or a physical
axiom's independence. A graph realization does not refute a canonical
surface reconstruction. Surface reconstruction does not identify the

2. Opposite controls: the P5 map a->a,b->b[a,b] has identity homology

4. For k=-3..3, verify fk(c)=c, fixed abelianization and both inverse
   maps. Check semidirect-product transport on the two generators;
   check slope transport for p,q=-3..3 and inverse shear. Compare
   marked slopes and transported slopes, not merely homology matrices.
5. In SL(2,F3), supply A=[[0,1],[2,0]], B=[[1,1],[1,2]]. Exhaustively
   enumerate determinant-one matrices to find every order-three T
   implementing f0 on A,B. Choose the lexicographically first T only
   as an explicit comparator convention, not a physical selector.
   Verify group relations and rho(c) nontrivial. Compare the primitive
   numeric slope (1,3) in frames k=0 and k=1, with the latter's actual

   control; no derived gauge group, generation or chirality claim.
