## The map, rather than an identification from a group name

Let Pi be the orbifold fundamental group of the actual punctured universal
elliptic curve with coordinates tau,w=u+tau v. Let F=pi1 of its punctured
fibre. Hain Proposition1.4 supplies 1->F->Pi->SL2Z->1. Define
kappa(g)(h)=g h g^-1 by the actual fibre inclusion. This is Hain's natural
map, not a freely assigned homology representation. On F it is Inner(h).
F is free of rank2 and centreless, so that restriction is injective.
The map on the outer quotient is the oriented monodromy on fibre homology.
In the coordinate deck convention it is gamma->J gamma J; this is an
automorphism of SL2Z, not a lost sign or an unoriented quotient.

The exact sequence 1->Inn(F)->SAut(F)->SL2Z->1 has the SAME kernel and
quotient maps. Thus kappa is injective: an element in its kernel first
has trivial outer image and hence lies in F, where it is trivial. It is
surjective: for an automorphism choose a geometric lift of its quotient;
its residual lies in Inn(F) and is corrected by a unique fibre loop.
This proves the natural map is an isomorphism after the declared marking.
It does not prove the finite gauge representation is faithful on Pi.

In particular each already-tested X,Y,Z is the image of a UNIQUE based
loop class under this map. Point-pushing a,b are not dropped. This is
the explicit natural kernel/quotient dictionary, not a claim that all
based lifts of one modular matrix are the same physical curve.

## Complex critical domains glue locally along the puncture

The puncture is a holomorphic section. In overlapping charts its normal
coordinate has z'=lambda(tau) z+O(z^2), lambda nonzero. In this geometry
lambda=(c tau+d)^-1. The leading unit normal frame thus rotates without
reversing orientation. The already-derived canonical Spin-c transition
acts on the two normal form degrees diagonally; it commutes with the
normal chirality projector. Changes of scale give invertible scalar
weights on leading trace coefficients, not a mixing of those degrees.

Gauge transition is unitary and preserves the puncture's even/odd
eigenspaces: it transports the full fibre representation, hence its
central puncture involution. Terms proportional to z in horizontal
mixing vanish in the leading trace quotient. Accordingly both extremes
(all admitted critical coefficients of one vertical chirality, none of
the other) define complex trace subbundles independently of charts.
The even regular extension is likewise invariant. This proves the
leading complex trace gluing, not a general graph-core density theorem,
global self-adjoint/Fredholm proof or actual kernel computation.

An anti-holomorphic normal map reverses normal chirality and exchanges
the two extremes. A physical reality/charge-conjugation law must therefore
be checked explicitly. Complex gluing does not discharge it or justify
deleting conjugates. Canonical Spin-c is intrinsic; its half-line factors
need not exist separately on the unlifted quotient.
