Hain, Notes on the Universal Elliptic KZB Connection, Section1 and full
Section1.1, https://arxiv.org/html/1309.0580 (v6 displayed), accessed
2026-10-14; personally read. Not a full-paper reading. The needed inputs
are the coordinate quotient, Proposition1.4 exact sequence, Corollary1.5
natural conjugation action and Proposition1.9 framed central quotient.
The framed group fixes the positive puncture loop and identifies its
positive frame loop with that loop. The broader unipotent KZB coefficient
is NOT identified with our finite quaternion/E8 coefficient.

Kassel--Reutenauer, https://arxiv.org/html/math/0507219, Section1 including
Theorem1.8/Corollary1.9 and convention change, personally read in the
antecedent packet. Its SAut(F2) presentation and exact inner kernel are
used with the explicit convention checks in the prior based probe.

Author's conventions: lower-case free generators a,b, uppercase inverses;
functions compose left after right; c=abAB, with the oriented cell's
positive puncture circle. Tangential Z:b->ba and Y:a->aB fix c. The
SL2 coordinate deck element gamma sends (u,v) to J gamma J (u,v), with
J=diag(1,-1). Loop composition may be converted to Hain's path convention
by reversing multiplication consistently. No independent spin sign is
assigned to repair the coefficient. Physical cusp transport on the
projected vertical kernels still needs its actual section map; recording
X and Z is not that map.
