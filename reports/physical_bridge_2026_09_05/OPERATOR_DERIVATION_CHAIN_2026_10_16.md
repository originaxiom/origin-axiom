# Existing operator derivations and their remaining physical maps

The retained work already contains more than a coefficient count. It
constructs a finite-distance closed Dirac realization, a smooth vertical
family with an actual connecting map, a prescribed total selfadjoint
domain and controlled projected cusp dynamics. A later physical-branch
argument estimates the odd complementary sector without differentiating
the singular half-pole twice. These are useful conditional derivations
to reuse, not steps to announce as absent or repeat as new experiments.

## The foundational retention question is already sharper

The fork's positive_carrier proof gives a proper quotient of F2 which
retains every positive word, its order, a nonzero commutator and all
substitution endomorphisms. Thus positive-word fidelity alone does not
force faithful signed F2. Its universal-completion argument identifies
the stronger premise that would force F2; it does not derive why the
principle must demand it.

The cohomology_carrier proof is more constructive than a generic
counterexample. For the supplied quaternion coefficient, degree-one
cocycles descend equivariantly to F2 divided by its third derived
subgroup. A metabelian carrier instead loses that cohomology even
though it retains the quaternion representation point. This is a
retained-information test for the actual algebraic coefficient, not a
replacement of the elliptic surface by a solvable group or a claim
about physical generations.

word_lifts and bundle_transport also remove a redundant choice. The
four coherent positive elementary lift pairs are simultaneously inner
conjugate; the cohomology transport is the parity-sign matrix D_q,
while the local-coefficient transport is a different unitary E_q.
These preserve the corresponding parity projectors and transported
domains. Adjoining the alternative lifts as independent generators
is a different operation and changes the based symmetry hypothesis.
The central lift sign is retained, not erased by abelianization.

The derivation duty is therefore precise: name the signed distinctions,
updates and observables that the principle must retain, and justify the
geometric category and physical field using them. Neither a preserved
parity count nor preference for a useful downstream surface supplies
that justification. The existing ACT_REGISTER criterion is relevant
here, with its declared update/output duties kept explicit.

## The finite distance puncture has an actual operator index

In puncture_fredholm the coefficient is the actual rank-six unitary
parity-twisted quaternion system, with puncture holonomy minus identity,
on a smooth finite-distance deleted point of an elliptic fibre. Including
the spin half-rotation and radial density gives periodic angular modes.
Only angular mode zero is critical. The maximal/minimal graph quotient
is C6-plus plus C6-minus, with the displayed nondegenerate Green form.

A grading-preserving selfadjoint domain is specified by Lambda-plus
and its orthogonal complement. The argument identifies BOTH kernel
and cokernel with the compact holomorphic extensions, then uses
Riemann--Roch to obtain index dim(Lambda-plus)-3. At the extremes it
also gives the individual dimensions (0,3) and (3,0). This is stronger
than inferring a kernel from an Euler characteristic. The smooth
metric, ordinary-spin operator, coefficient and domain are still
hypotheses. A complete hyperbolic fibre cusp is not that Hilbert problem.

puncture_symmetry supplies exact all-matrix commutant and stabilizer
arguments. Joint move invariance plus one appropriate non-scalar
unitary sign suffices to remove the two middle coefficient domains;
the whole continuous flavour group is unnecessary. Its doubled local
model also shows that symmetry alone need not preserve chiral grading:
ungraded invariant Green-null graphs form a continuum. No chiral
sheaf index is assigned to those graphs.

Within the supplied compact E8 embedding the sign is compatible with
the complete displayed interactions at the flat origin. This is a
conditional positive, not a proof that a symmetry-breaking background
or every physical boundary law preserves it. A fixed-modulus symmetry
and covariance over different moduli remain different requirements.

## The smooth family has an explicit trace and connecting map

The critical vertical plus spin section is

    z^(-1/2) (dz)^(1/2) e u.

For w=f(z), f(0)=0 and f'(0) nonzero, the compatible frame ratio is
sqrt(z f'(z)/f(z))=1+O(z). The constant pole and vertical-spin weights
cancel. The correction lies in the minimal domain. Thus the positive
critical quotient is L6 with its actual coefficient transition, not
an additional Hodge half-weight. Coherent square-root signs and bundle
transitions remain required; the coordinate calculation does not
manufacture missing descent data.

fibre_family then constructs common graph charts on compact smooth
base regions. Its map

    delta(u) = [D-plus applied to a cutoff polar lift of u]

takes the critical trace into the canonical cokernel. It represents
the actual vertical index class by the finite complex Lambda-plus
to C, with class [Lambda-plus]-[C]. Under the stated parabolic Hodge
identifications, C=lambda^(1/2) T01 and the full-domain kernel is
lambda^(-1/2) T10. Tensoring by the logarithmic base spin yields
W46's displayed modular weights.

This is an authored operator-to-sheaf map over the smooth base, not a
flat representation sequence or a proof of total Fredholmness across
the degenerating cusp. It should be cited before presenting that map
as a wholly missing step. The fixed-fibre and family positives do not
alone compute the total four-real-dimensional index.

## The cusp extension is more than a lift sign

cusp_spin_extension distinguishes ordinary and logarithmic dualizing
lines on the compactification. Their spin choices differ by lambda^6.
Multiplication by eta^12 matches the open automorphic descriptions,
but a component whose exponent crosses one must lose its leading cusp
coefficient. The resulting elementary-modification correction is part
of the Euler comparison. Testing both overall lift signs at unchanged
weight does not exhaust compactified spin extensions.

theta_cusp derives the classical discriminant from the existing theta
functions and supplies the missing HDelta input of cusp_connection.
That connection realizes each specified integral end unit with a smooth
source-free base curvature and finite action. On the open trivial line,
the same construction admits continuous real flux when the metric/end
class is allowed to change. The canonical compactification extension
is additional data, and the displayed energy favours zero unless a
nonzero sector is imposed.

nodal_transport gives the literal primitive node meridians, their
odd-eighth holonomies and an exact local cochain contraction, robust
to the stated integer framing. Multiplication by q^n changes the
extension and not those local holonomies. Thus this local discriminator
cannot choose n. That is not a no-go for a coupled global end law.

The physical four-dimensional-unit proof adds a useful distinct
positive: for the declared constant-area semi-flat metric, the pulled
back commuting curvature solves the pure bulk Yang--Mills equation
and has finite total action. Its nonzero Kähler moment is not a
zero-moment vacuum of an unconstructed full action. The supplied base
scale, fibre area, embedding and integer sector are not selected.

## Total domain and spectral work must be read in date order

total_metric_dirac retains the changing fibre, actual cusp deck map
and mixed Levi-Civita term c1 c3 c4/(4 ell). The term is fixed by the
supplied metric, not another adjustable coefficient.

total_domain proves the frozen horizontal normal-family estimate for
the prescribed puncture trace, then constructs an authored total
selfadjoint closure. The complete modular cusp adds no further
selfadjoint extension parameter IN THIS fixed norm, line and operator.
Changing that line, metric or end source changes the problem; the
argument does not select n or the puncture subspace.

vertical_cusp_gap retains the three puncture modes and estimates the
orthogonal vertical complement by c sqrt(y/A). horizontal_projection
then differentiates the actual kernel projection, finds bounded but
nonzero mixing, and computes a projected cusp connection and
nonintegral transition phases. Its projected block is confining under
the stated global lift and extreme-domain hypotheses. Rank three or a
vertical gap alone would not imply this.

That projection proof also finds that a mixing profile is L2 but not
in the vertical Dirac domain. This blocks a naive formal square; it
does not prove a complementary total estimate impossible. The later
physical surface_odd_form proof transfers the dangerous derivative to
the regular opposite component and obtains a conditional growing
estimate for the odd complementary TOTAL block. Therefore the older
fork's remaining-duty sentence must not be read as the latest status.

The transfer to the full odd E8 coefficient retains the supplied global
pointed spin/gauge data, zero scalar background and extreme domains.
It does not compute an index or kernel for the full canonical operator.
The ordinary benchmark's even-sector Weyl sequence is not removed by
an odd-sector estimate. These qualifications prevent both loss of the
spectral positive and a false declaration of three physical families.

## The same interaction restricts proposed line assignments

matter_line_compatibility uses the existing adjoint spin pair Q,R,
its positive kinetic metric and fixed antisymmetric superpotential.
Constant mixing preserves both only in SU2 of this TWO-FIELD label.
For a diagonal line action its two charges sum to zero. A common
curved line on both fields does not preserve the unchanged parallel
alternating pairing; opposite twists do.

This removes one independent line-charge choice GIVEN that action.
It neither identifies this SU2 with family SU3 nor supplies a map from
the modular base line into that field role. A transforming coefficient,
different R twist or altered action is a different construction. Scalar
scaling of the whole nonabelian adjoint is also not an existing gauge
derivation, whereas genuine root-space cocharacters remain possible.

spinor_uniformization_joint adds the adjacent field-degree safeguard:
the original spin-valued fields are not an ordinary K-valued Higgs by
a norm rescaling. The original action nevertheless retains real
quartic moment/commutator interactions. The absence of an independently
supplied scalar portal must not be widened to absence of interactions.

## The separate complete flag route has its own positive maps

The fork's noncompact_admission, kernel_transport and
weighted_global_complex concern a different, nonsplit rank-five
coefficient on a complete hyperbolic three-manifold. Their authored
arguments give harmonic admission in a specified positive flag class,
bounded transport of the closed differential complex, and an explicit
whole-torus weighted complex joined to the compact core. The magnitude
of a positive flag amplitude does not change zero-mode multiplicities
under that transport. An explicit global harmonic formula is not a
prerequisite for that particular cohomology calculation.

These are not the elliptic-family operator. The neutral zero-weight
sectors, physical fermion dictionary and full parent still need their
own accounting. Neither their whole-torus boundary rule nor the compact
selective-boundary counts can be transplanted into the weave's cusp.
