## Evidence vocabulary

**Premise**: accepted for a specified conditional construction, not proved
from a weaker principle. **Definition/convention**: specifies what the
construction means; does not demonstrate physical necessity. **Conditional
consequence**: follows only with the named hypotheses. **Read/reported**:
source body inspected, not independently reproduced here. **Checked in
this lane**: the stated R57/R58 proof audit and finite safeguards, not
independent analytic acceptance or universal physics.

Philosophy motivates choices; it does not prove a theorem's premise.
Different A-number systems must not be joined by their numbers. Four
ledger AXIOM labels, two FRAGILE forks and seven motivated premises are
different bookkeeping questions, not competing measurements of logical
minimality. B1003 measured its declared feature-loss forks; B1123 counted
labels in the ledger. Neither instrument tests a formal implication from
all remaining premises after deletion of one.
