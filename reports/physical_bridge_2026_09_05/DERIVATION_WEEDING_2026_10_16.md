## What counts as replacing an assumption

For each proposed link, record the input it replaces, the exact admitted
premises, the map recovering its output, and a rejecting control. Check
that it recovers the full required structure rather than a shared name,
rank or zero set. Follow every auxiliary assumption back through the
same dependency chain: moving an input into a definition is not removing
it. Classify the outcome as recovered given named premises, a proposed
link refuted on its tested domain, or unresolved. A local negative never
withdraws other carriers, branches or boundary laws.

The registering relation remains essential whenever an observable or
variational tensor fails to descend after forgetting it. R94 also retains
the safe orientation-line-valued alternative: a new permanent bit is not
forced for every possible output type. No current result identifies this
register with consciousness or qualia; those remain distinct questions.
