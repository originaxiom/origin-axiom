# Common algebra is not common coefficient bundle

Authored audit, 2026-09-27. Conditional on the declared regular embedding,
adjoint parent fields and unbroken stated SM action. No claim for added
localized matter, another parent or a different physical dictionary.

## 1. The representation map

Write E for the defining rank-five bundle of the structure SL5. With
the gauge factor first and the root orientation in DESIGN, the E8
adjoint decomposes into

    (24,1) + (1,24) + (10,5) + (10*,5*) + (5,10*) + (5*,10).

The instrument compares the weights of all 240 roots and eight Cartans
with independently generated tensor-product weights. This exact
character equality determines the finite-dimensional representation
of the semisimple Lie algebra A4+A4. It is NOT inferred just from 248.

Breaking only the gauge A4 to the specified SM gives:

| Gauge multiplet | 6Y | Structure coefficient |
|---|---:|---|
| Q=(3,2) | 1 | E, rank 5 |
| u^c=(3*,1) | -4 | E, rank 5 |
| e^c=(1,1) | 6 | E, rank 5 |
| d^c=(3*,1) | 2 | exterior-square E, rank 10 |
| L=(1,2) | -3 | exterior-square E, rank 10 |

Opposite gauge charges have dual coefficients. If an additional
hypercharge line L_Y is used in a globally admissible lift, the rows
are tensor-multiplied by L_Y to the listed integer powers. Those line
twists are constrained by the global group and the same background;
they cannot be chosen independently per row. They do not change rank.

The SM-singlet algebra is sl5_structure + C Y, dimension 25. Its two
mixed sixes, relative to a 2+3 Levi split, are background/neutral adjoint
directions. They are not themselves Q,u^c,e^c matter coefficients.
There is also a rank-six mixed subquotient in exterior-square E under
such a split; that has the d^c/L gauge role, not the universal five rows.

Counting all weights with zero colour/weak Cartan eigenvalues and Y=0
would give 28. Three of those Cartans belong to the colour/weak adjoints,
not singlets. This is why centralizer and zero-weight counts differ.
No neutral direction is labelled a right-handed neutrino merely by rank.

## 2. What fails literally, and what remains possible

The marked M6 orbit sum W has rank six in each of the six sector-wise
index computations. W cannot be isomorphic to, or inject as a bundle
into, the rank-five coefficient of Q,u^c,e^c in this adjoint parent.
This already prevents literal simultaneous transfer of those six
index calculations into this one parent by a relabeling of fibres.

This is NOT a bound of five on the number of physical particles and
NOT a prohibition on three generations. A rank-five bundle can have
many independent global modes. A new E with suitable cohomology in E
and exterior-square E remains possible, but its indices, modes and
interactions must be recomputed. The old rank-six values are not that
calculation. Additional elementary or localized matter also changes
the hypothesis and requires an explicit parent/action source.

## 3. Independent necessary tensor-factor test

The M6 W has a filtration with six one-dimensional character factors.
Suppose W were A tensor B with dimensions 2 and 3 over C. Then both
A and B must themselves have only one-dimensional composition factors.

Proof without assuming diagonal holonomy: for simple factors S of A
and T of B, S tensor T is a subquotient of W, hence has a one-dimensional
simple submodule C_chi. The nonzero inclusion is equivalent to a nonzero
map S* -> T tensor chi^-1. Both are simple, so it is an isomorphism and
dim S=dim T. This holds for every pair of simple factors. Their common
dimension therefore divides both 2 and 3; it must be one. Tensoring
filtrations is exact over C, which justifies the subquotient step.

Consequently the six character factors must form a 2-by-3 grid a_i b_j.
There must be a partition into three pairs with the SAME ratio, allowing
pairwise reversals. Exhaustively comparing the 120 oriented matchings
tests this necessary condition, retaining repeated weights. A failure
rules out this tensor factorization independently of extension amplitudes.

For a parabolic SL5 matrix G=[[A,X],[0,B]], conjugation on its positive
mixed adjoint block N(Y)=[[0,Y],[0,0]] is N(A Y B^-1); X cancels.
Moreover the normalizer of this whole six-dimensional matrix space is
the parabolic: its common kernel (also span of all images) is the same
two-plane, so any normalizer preserves that plane, and the block formula
gives the converse. Thus adding the upper extension block cannot rescue
that particular mixed-adjoint realization of W.

For exterior-square E with a preserved rank-two subbundle A and rank-three
quotient B, the natural filtration has factors exterior-square A,
A tensor B, exterior-square B. Its middle rank-six factor faces the
same character-grid test. This does NOT classify every possible invariant
six-space of exterior-square E for every subgroup or exotic background.

## 4. Positive direction and the corrected admission question

The useful target is ONE E (with any explicitly earned extra hypercharge
transport), its exterior square, duals and adjoint on ONE base/domain.
First evaluate that coupled family; do not independently tune six coefficient
systems. At trivial hypercharge transport the three 10-like coefficients
coincide and the two 5*-like coefficients coincide. Nontrivial permitted
transport must be applied consistently through the displayed charges.

A far-away relative SL5 component is still a mathematical candidate, not
yet an embedding of the literal M6 W. Searching for a single index on
the defining five is not a sufficient test of its full matter content.
Nor does reproducing a peripheral invariant dimension transfer an index.

The local mixed BPS core in the handoff remains a positive local construction.
It is already explicit there that its kernels on the 5 and exterior-square
5 do not by themselves give three generations. This audit sharpens that
existing caveat into an exact coefficient-level admission map; it does not
announce the elementary branching or the earlier no-factorization as new.
