# Next gate: one rank-five parent, coupled coefficient systems

This is a task specification, NOT a sealed experiment or a result.

## Fixed question

On the cusped candidate M6, or a precisely stated compatible quotient,
can one SL5 background E and permitted hypercharge transport produce the
required charged modes in E and exterior-square E simultaneously, under
one positive physical norm and one action-derived domain?

The six sector-wise rank-six M6 examples are retained as mathematical
benchmarks. They are not prescribed as literal fibres of E.

## Before a large solve

1. Recover the exact peripheral classes actually intended by the handoff.
   Distinguish its principal-unipotent CONTROL from the desired deck-cubed
   2+3 boundary model. They must not be interchanged.
2. Compute necessary boundary fixed-space/index bounds jointly for E,
   exterior-square E and their duals, including any allowed character twists.
   Reject a boundary class only on the target it actually cannot support.
3. Check those bounds against the existing native Levi seeds and geometric
   controls; do not rerun their large searches to rediscover a known zero.
4. Specify whether cover modes or invariant modes are being tested. A
   physical choice of quotient must not be made just to obtain three.
5. Only then specify a relative representation search with two outcomes,
   positive and negative controls, and an exactness/completeness standard.

## Physical admission is separate

Passing an algebraic index screen does not establish a harmonic background,
normalizable kinetic modes, anomaly cancellation or a chiral physical phase.
The same parent must supply the background equations and end/source terms.
F01's finite-energy obstruction and its scope correction apply only after
their hypotheses are checked; zero static residual potential is not identical
to finite Higgs norm, and neither is a normalizable fluctuation by definition.

If the appropriate common model needs new fields or a different parent,
price that as a changed hypothesis. Do not claim that this route has closed
the generated architecture, and do not quietly broaden it until it succeeds.
