## Analytic and physical fences

convergent integrals. L2 alone is not silently upgraded to L4; the
quadratic Green response additionally requires its source in L2.
The six-coefficient Hodge resolvent is controlled, but a full bosonic
gauge-fixed propagator and four-fermion matching are not derived merely
by renaming that operator. No actual global Codazzi eigenbasis, physical
coupling value, interacting gap, selected condensate or full anomaly
calculation is included. Spontaneous symmetry breaking, different end
data, added fields and interactions outside this channel remain separate.
