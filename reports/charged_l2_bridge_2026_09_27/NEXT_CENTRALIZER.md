This check prevents mislabeling gauge fields as generations and interprets
the surviving normalizable pairs. It will not, by itself, repair the net-three
failure. After it, compare genuinely different chirality candidates using a
single input ledger and stop repeating equivalent monomial/character scans.

Candidate classes remain unverified options: a different globally reductive
representation component, a genuinely nonliftable parent sector, a justified
source/end action, or a specified interacting mirror-removal phase. Review
the other seats' existing examples before introducing a new one. Selection
must be based on an earned operator/action and an explicit discriminator,
not an attractive integer in an unrelated representation.

observables and empirical tests. No gauge-centralizer computation replaces
