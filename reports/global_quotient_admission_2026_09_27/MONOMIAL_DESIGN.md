Their exponent rows form an integer matrix C. The solution group is
Hom(Z^35 / rowspan_Z C, C*). Add the exact phase rows of mu, or mu and
lambda, to impose the declared peripheral choices. This describes ALL
nonzero complex multipliers, not merely roots of a chosen order.

second of seven generators, with zero free coordinate). Verify 40[g1]

and the exact equality, not just diagonal entries. Transform w to w V.
It is in the relation lattice exactly when all torsion coordinates are
divisible by D_ii and free coordinates vanish. Preserve an explicit
integer relation certificate for w when zero, and always for 5w.
