## 1 Common line screen and the surviving reductions

has zero exponent on each marked generator. Thus every honest line has

    I(V)=a0(V)-a0(V*)+t0(V*)-r1(V)

then gives I(Q)=0. This does not depend on unitarity or a sampling grid.

lepton:Lambda2(E)L^-3. A different line for each sector is not allowed.

The fifth-root absorption is checked on EVERY actual M6 generator using

    E_j(zeta5 t)=D_s [chi(g)^k E_j(t)] D_s^-1.

## 2 Explicit bounds outside a finite root set

The rational restriction of E over K has dimension ten. Its relative
cone has degree-one cochains of dimension 80 and cone d0 rank ten.
Hence h1_rel over C=(70-rank_Q(cone1))/2. The exact pair sequence gives
n=h1_rel-3+a0, while a0=(10-rank_Q(d0))/2. Nonzero selected minors
of ranks r and r0 therefore give

    0 <= n <= (74-r-r0)/2

off their roots. Apply this separately to the actual dual. A zero bound
forces zero interior modes there. If both bounds are below three, their
difference cannot be +/-3. The minors need not be maximal generically;
no unsupported generic-rank assertion is used. A larger bound leaves
the corresponding necessary-target decision open.

All coefficients are Laurent polynomials in t. Clearing row powers adds
only the excluded root zero. Factor every nonzero polynomial minor and
take the union of its nonzero root factors on both sides. Those roots
contain every failure point of this particular rank bound, possibly
including spurious candidates. Recompute all cohomology and relative
ranks at each candidate rather than assume every factor is a mode jump.

## 3 Avoiding an average over different roots

A rational irreducible factor f need not remain irreducible over K.
The tensor K tensor_Q Q[t]/f can then be a product of fields. A rational
rank divided by 2 deg(f) would be an average across those factors and
does not determine the answer at a particular parameter. This is forbidden.

Instead factor f over K, certify the product and each irreducible g,
and form F=K[t]/g. In the basis 1,zeta3,t,t zeta3,... the companion T
for t and Omega for zeta3 commute. The producer verifies g(T)=0,
Omega^2+Omega+I=0 and invertibility of T on the nonzero candidate set.
Irreducibility makes F a field of Q degree 2 deg(g). Every complex
embedding with the chosen zeta3 embedding evaluates at one root of g;
field ranks are the same under each of them. Repeating this for all
g factors and both phase components covers every original complex root.

The trace form tr_F/Q(ab) is nondegenerate in characteristic zero. It
identifies restriction of the literal F-dual with the rational dual.
The actual dual inverts BOTH phase and exponent; the same permutation
and +/- signs remain. The producer tests the trace-conjugated inverse
transpose identity at every candidate, then computes both ordinary and
relative results. Normalizing all rational ranks by [F:Q] is justified
only after the field and trace checks, not merely by their divisibility.

The preceding conditional harmonic argument may interpret these classes
