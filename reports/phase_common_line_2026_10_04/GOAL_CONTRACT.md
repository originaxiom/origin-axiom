## What completion would require

The theory must derive its physical structures and consequences from a
declared set of Origin Axiom principles, with every extra input identified.
Recognizing a useful algebra, fitting a coefficient, selecting a favorable
character or supplying an effective action does not discharge that duty.
The completion audit must at least establish the following together:

- A derived spacetime and dynamical interpretation, action and physical
  fields, rather than only representation slots or an externally chosen parent.
- The Standard Model gauge structure, coupled chiral matter with three
  complete generations, electroweak breaking and consistent operator domains.
- Physical scales and dimensionless observable relations, including normalized
  gauge and matter interactions, without independently retuned empirical inputs.
  A choice of measurement units is distinct from an adjustable mass ratio.
- A justified physical state or state-selection law. A continuous family is
  not a derivation of its observed member merely because one can choose it.
- Quantum and anomaly consistency, gravitational dynamics, and empirical
  compatibility with discriminating predictions. A spin-two slot is not gravity.

Neutrino, cosmological and other observed phenomena must be addressed when
claiming a FULL physical theory; successful SM bookkeeping alone is not that
claim. No claim of guaranteed attainability follows from the goal.
