# Reduction and completeness argument, authored before execution

This is a conditional exact algebraic argument. Assertions about the
literal presentation and families are checked by the accompanying producer.
It is not a physical index theorem or an independent specialist review.

## 1. Global dictionary and scope

With gauge factor first the received regular E8 branching is
(24,1)+(1,24)+(10,5)+(10*,5*)+(5,10*)+(5*,10).
On the two centers, the weights are (2,1),(-2,-1),(1,-2),(-1,2)
modulo 5. The common kernel is (z,z^-2). Thus an honest SL5 structure
representation and gauge SU5 representation always map to the parent;
the quotient does not forbid their product. The integral gauge embedding

Q:E L, u:E L^-4, e:E L^6, d:Lambda2(E) L^2, lepton:Lambda2(E) L^-3.
Replacing (E,L) by (E chi^-1,L chi), chi^5=1, is precisely an invisible
central change on these coefficients and all parent summands. It does
not permit separate choices of L for each sector. The normalization 6Y
is a convention, not a derived physical coupling.

We cover globally lifted product backgrounds. The center calculation
does NOT classify nonliftable (SL5 x gauge)/Z5 bundles or extra color/weak
flat transport. Nor does it introduce new elementary representations.

## 2. Why only meridian-trivial lines can meet the common target

In E(t)|M6 the actual meridian is I and the longitude is T or T^-1,
with fixed space dimension 3, for all t != 0. The preferred longitude
has identically zero exponent sum on EACH Schreier generator. Every
one-dimensional character therefore has L(lambda)=1. If L(mu)!=1,
Q=E L and its actual dual have no boundary invariants and no global
invariants. The established duality identity

    I(V) = a0(V)-a0(V*) + t0(V*)-r1(V)

and r1(V)+r1(V*)=t1(V)=t0(V)+t0(V*) force I(Q)=0.
This is independent of any unitary or semisimplicity assumption on E.
Hence a common target with I(Q)=+3 or -3 requires L(mu)=1.

Let A be the integer relation matrix after deleting the meridian column.
The producer checks its Smith factors are (1,1,1,1,8,40). Thus ALL remaining
complex line characters are finite order, forming Z/8 x Z/40, not a
continuous unsearched family. Columns of A^-1 modulo Z generate its
character dual; enumeration and determinant certify that all 320 were
included. Longitude triviality is checked independently, not inferred
from the abstract isomorphism type.

## 3. The necessary u-like coefficient reduces to four families

The fourth-power image on Z/8 x Z/40 is Z/2 x Z/10. Every such character
splits uniquely into a quadratic eta and a fifth-root character. The
mod-5 kernel of the marked presentation is checked to be generated by
the restriction of the M2 character chi with exponents (0,1,-1).
The entire image is also checked directly on all 320 character vectors.

Write the monomial M2 matrices as M_g(t)=diag(t^v_g) P_g, with exponents
indexed by the OUTPUT row. With zeta^5=1, for family j the proposed identity is

    M_g(zeta t) = D_s [chi(g)^k M_g(t)] D_s^-1,

where (k,s)=(2,(0,0,0,2,2)) or (4,(0,0,0,1,4)). It is verified by
v_g[p_g[i]] = k c_g + s[p_g[i]]-s[i] mod 5 on every nonzero matrix
entry. Since k is invertible mod 5, every fifth-root scalar twist is
absorbed by a rotation of t. Restriction to M6 and tensoring eta preserve
the identity. A scalar rescaling of D_s can make its determinant one
without changing the conjugation, if needed; no physical gauge symmetry
is inferred from this coefficient isomorphism.

Consequently E(t)L^-4 is isomorphic to ONE of the four E(t') eta,
with t' in C*, whenever the Q sector can meet the common target. The
quadratic twists here need not individually have determinant one:
they are actual charged coefficient bundles, not newly asserted SL5
structure backgrounds. Their physical embedding is via the original E,L.

## 4. Exact bounds outside explicit polynomial roots

For each signed family and its actual dual the relative cone has C1
dimension 40 and d0 rank 5, so h1_rel=35-rank(cone1). On the verified
peripheral-trivial eta the paired cusp dimension is still t0=3. From
the pair exact sequence n=h1_rel-3+a0. A rank-r0 global minor and rank-r
cone minor give, wherever both are nonzero,

    0 <= n <= 35-r-3+(5-r0).

Use this upper bound on BOTH coefficients. If both bounds are below 3,
then |I|=|n-n*|<3. If both vanish, n=n*=0 on that open set. This argument
requires no extrapolation from a numerical control or generic-rank guess.

Clear Laurent denominators only by powers of t; t=0 is outside the
parameter domain. Roots of the selected nonzero polynomial minors include
every point where the argument might fail, possibly with spurious roots.
Factorization over Q followed by computation over Q[t]/f for EVERY
irreducible factor covers all complex embeddings. At those roots compute
full ranks afresh, including H0 corrections, ordinary restriction ranks
and the direct relative cone for both actual duals. An omitted factor or
an inadequate generic bound leaves the conclusion OPEN.

precludes three complete generations by THIS ordinary-index prescription.

interior cohomology can remain. Mathematical modes are not yet normalized
four-dimensional fields. Other SL5 components, nonsplit positives, sources,
defect domains, nonflat completions and quantum mirror-gapping mechanisms
