## Verdict

The most useful synthesis is not a new numerical coincidence. **Several
successful calculations distinguish data that earlier calculations had
discarded; several failed physical joins silently change the data being
studied.** Both are visible, with examples, in the existing record.

This supports a research strategy: retain the full relevant structure,
then demand a physical action, state, operator and observable that use it.
It does **not** establish that the framework necessarily describes nature.
I cannot honestly certify twenty achieved breakthroughs or twenty routes
guaranteed to succeed. Below are **22 concrete breakthrough opportunities**,
each tied to existing work and a discriminator. Most ingredients and several
proposed joins are already banked; their strategic assembly is the audit's
contribution, not a claim to have discovered their ingredients.

Ranking is by leverage on the physical-theory goal, tempered by how concretely
the next question can be stated. It is not a probability of success or a
promise of short completion. “Now” means a defensible investigation can start
now; solving a global PDE, constructing a quantum phase or obtaining a
laboratory result is not a one-session task.

## 2. Five patterns that change the strategy

### A. Some apparent walls are properties of a compressed description

R27/B1418 give nonsplit and semisimplified systems with the same word
traces but different interior-cohomology indices. B1431 finds information
in the boundary-index collection that its (0,0) series missed. The arithmetic
parent contains structure not acting as a symmetry on every particular cover.
These are three **different mechanisms**, not one proved unifying theorem.
Their common strategic warning is to check what an invariant forgets before
using its silence as a physical no-go.

Crucial counterweight: some forgotten data may be physically inadmissible.
Keeping extension data is not itself a harmonic-metric existence theorem.

### B. Mathematical composition is the missing operation

A chiral kernel in one domain, an anomaly-free EFT with added fields, a
stationary source in another action and a parent with the right weights
do not automatically make one theory. The existing common-model audit
already says this. R38 sharpens it: the actual H embeds in E8, but the
specified pure-SYM action does not transfer the proposed zero-form Yukawa.
“Allowed by the group” and “generated by this action” are different.

### C. A symmetric law need not have only symmetric states

An equivariant deterministic selector cannot choose between indistinguishable
mirror-related points. That is a constraint on that selector, not a theorem
that a theory has no symmetry-breaking states or useful distributions over
them. Conversely, writing a double-well potential by hand would merely move
the input. The meaningful question is whether the actual action produces
the state, and which observable differences survive.

### D. Coincidences are not automatically independent evidence

xB030 explicitly identifies the golden eigenvalue, Alexander root,
reducible-locus condition and torsion-growth rate as connected consequences.
Likewise several ADE appearances reuse arithmetic or representation input.
That mathematical unity is valuable. Counting each consequence as a new
independent confirmation of a TOE would overstate its evidential weight.

The alternative to “accident” is not only “the entire physical universe is
derived.” It can also be “a rich mathematical structure has many related
realizations.” We need a prospectively specified physical discriminator to
tell those hypotheses apart. Internal seat agreement is not that test.

### E. A physical input need not invalidate a theory

Nor is every unselected coordinate a physical input. B1232 gives the right
test: quotient field redefinitions and evaluate normalized invariant
observables. But arbitrary couplings chosen to fit each comparison cannot
be called predictions. Separate a useful conditional model from a uniquely
derived model and measure the predictive content remaining after inputs.

## 3. Ranked breakthrough opportunities

The first seven target the immediate physical bottleneck. Later entries
include distinct mathematical-physics and experimental deliverables. They
are not twenty-two independent votes for the same theory.

### 1. Determine whether the positive nonsplit systems are physical backgrounds

**Asset:** R27 and B1418/B1420 supply exact nonzero cohomological indices;
the trivial/self-inverse-twist and semisimplification controls discriminate.
**Opening:** an ordinary trace-character description can miss the very data
that makes these indices nonzero. Preserve the representation and extension,
not just its semisimple character.
**Next discriminator:** choose one exact witness and a specified parent;
solve or obstruct its harmonic metric/moment-map problem with explicit cusp
or defect boundary conditions, then construct the positive-norm fermion
operator. **Success:** a physically admissible nonzero chiral sector with
identified gauge representation. **Stop:** no admissible background in that
specified class. Neither outcome is a universal verdict on all extensions.
This is the highest-value bridge test, not an invitation to another large census.

### 2. Derive the needed source fields and couplings from one parent action

**Asset:** R38 supplies an actual integral, saturated H-to-E8 map with every
extra sector counted. **Opening:** charge-four parent one-forms might connect
the previously separate sourced and noncommuting constructions.
**Next discriminator:** test them against the full complex-flatness and
moment-map equations, including noncentral variations and charge-three
fields; derive the fermion form degrees and interactions from that action.
**Success:** the source and required coupling occur together without inserting
the desired low-energy vertex. **Stop:** the proposed ansatz fails those
equations or supplies a different interaction. E8 remains a changed input,
not a forced consequence of the original E6. This was already R38's proposed
next test; this fork has not executed or silently resumed it.

### 3. Make the chiral domain a physical output instead of a convention

**Asset:** R19 has a conditional singular three/zero kernel; R30/R31 have
resolved light pairs on a distinct domain. R29 identifies a vanishing-capacity
problem for a bare shrinking line mass.
**Next discriminator:** specify finite-width source/end degrees of freedom
or an explicitly renormalized defect theory and derive its limiting kinetic
domain. Track norm, spectrum and gauge currents as width and cusp cutoff
vary separately. **Success:** a controlled limit selects a chiral operator
without discarding physical partners. **Stop:** this completion retains the
paired spectrum or requires a freely chosen domain. A relative Euler count
alone cannot select the physical limit.

### 4. Establish an interacting mirror gap, not just an allowed quartic

**Asset:** R33/R34 exhibit a nonzero, gauge-invariant Grassmann interaction;
R36/R37 expose false selectivity from ratios and single chosen modes.
**Next discriminator:** derive the *whole* canonically normalized low-energy
coupling matrices in the chosen model, then test for a mirror-gapped phase
with the desired symmetry and ordinary light sector intact. Require absolute
couplings, degenerate-mode control and cutoff/volume stability.
**Success:** selective gapping with anomaly matching. **Stop:** both sectors
gap, both couplings vanish, unwanted symmetry breaks, or extra light sectors
remain. Published symmetric-mass-generation mechanisms motivate the question;
they do not establish it for this model. [Primary research context][smg].

### 5. Close the full anomaly calculation including the ends

**Asset:** R21 has an anomaly-free enlarged H EFT; R22 finds that one same-source
implementation supplies the wrong spectator chirality; R23/R24 compute local
transport and global boundary flux.
**Next discriminator:** calculate the gauge variation of the regulated
fermion determinant plus the actual source/cusp sector for the same action.
Include the global gauge quotient, residual discrete group and heavy matching
terms. **Success:** cancellation with the claimed light spectrum, not simply
with an added free mirror sector. **Stop:** a residual anomaly or unavoidable
light compensator. A massive gauge field does not license ignoring its gauge
consistency. This is a separate quantum gate from the classical parent map.

### 6. Prove that the candidate has a usable four-dimensional limit

**Asset:** R17's charged cusp barrier coexists with a neutral escape channel;
a finite charged kernel is not the complete light spectrum.
**Next discriminator:** compute or bound the neutral, gauge and gravitational
low-energy spectral measures and their couplings to localized matter in the
same metric/domain. **Success:** a controlled 4D effective description, with
any continuum effects explicitly small or physically accounted for.
**Stop:** unsuppressed extra channels contradict that claimed limit. Do not
identify the absence of a strict gap with failure of *every* possible EFT;
the spectral weight and coupling matter too.

### 7. Use the arithmetic parent and its cover correspondences constructively

**Asset:** the Chat1 audit verifies PSL(2,O3) cover indices 12/24/36 for the
specified m004/m202/s959 tessellations and exhibits binary tetrahedral
structure; s958 controls the failure of “same index means same symmetry.”
**Next discriminator:** construct an equivariant bundle/operator under the
parent, and its restriction or correspondence maps through common covers.
Test which finite symmetry actually descends and acts on physical modes.
**Success:** derive structure lost at an individual cover without arbitrary
member-by-member choices. **Stop:** the desired action fails descent or needs
new unpriced sectors. A class, PSL, PGL and one quotient are different objects;
these three indices do not form a demonstrated nested tower.

### 8. Replace impossible canonical point-selection with a dynamical state test

**Asset:** the stabilizer work and B1430 expose genuine unselected directions;
R4–R10 already show vacuum alignment can be computed in a specified action.
**Next discriminator:** obtain a vacuum functional or state prescription from
that action, quotient gauge redundancy, and calculate its minima/distribution
on the actual configuration space. **Success:** physical vacuum properties
follow without choosing the wanted member or sign by hand. **Stop:** arbitrary
weights or adjustable potentials carry all the answer. Mirror-paired vacua
need not be a failure; their cosmology and observable content must be assessed.
This does not derive a probability rule or cosmological selection by naming one.
It does not revive B723's retracted identification of thermal Galois labels
with the object's chirality: B942/B957 already distinguish the relevant groups.

### 9. Select the gauge embedding without inserting the SM branching target

**Asset:** B1430 verifies the regular SU(3)×SU(2) orbit but also thousands of
distinct rational U(1) branchings; the SM-shaped condition is an input.
**Next discriminator:** derive a dynamical or consistency selector on the
permitted charge lattice *before* imposing the observed branching, and test
it on a competing direction. **Success:** the desired embedding survives a
noncircular discriminator. **Stop:** the SM multiset was merely restated as
the selection rule. Direction, global charge lattice and gauge-kinetic
normalization must be reported separately. The finite rational scan is not
an exhaustive classification of all embeddings.

### 10. Solve chiral matter and doublet–triplet splitting in the same geometry

**Asset:** SM B1367's point-local cubic pincer and B1368's bulk splitting
describe complementary strengths and weaknesses. R4–R10 provide conditional
symmetry-breaking dynamics; none by itself joins these mechanisms.
**Next discriminator:** compute the actual local/bulk mode coupling and
E6-breaking action on both Higgs doublets and exotic triplets in one model,
including proton-decay operators. **Success:** light desired Higgs/matter
without equally light dangerous triplets. **Stop:** the same mass-matrix
pincer persists, or its proposed escape eliminates chirality. A Wilson-line
label cannot be assigned to a point field without showing how it acts.

### 11. Reduce the physical input budget using observables, not coordinates

**Asset:** B1232's quotient lemma and B1409's boundary deformation space.
**Next discriminator:** map the actual continuous choices to canonically
normalized masses, coupling invariants and scattering observables; compute
the rank of that map after legitimate field redefinitions.
**Success:** demonstrate that some apparently free directions are unobservable,
or derive a genuine relation among observables. **Stop:** the kinetic metrics
restore dependence that holomorphic tensors concealed. Twenty real deformation
coordinates do not constitute twenty measured SM parameters; conversely, a
missing canonical representative is not proof of physical arbitrariness.

### 12. Turn the existing Yukawa tensors into a falsifiable flavor statement

**Asset:** exact cubic support, the hollow-texture obstruction in its declared
frame, and actual overlap identities are stronger than dimension matching.
**Next discriminator:** calculate the kinetic matrices and complete allowed
corrections in a chosen chiral model, then its basis-invariant singular values
and mixing invariants. Fix parameters on one subset and hold another out.
**Success:** a stable relation not put in by fitting. **Stop:** wrong texture
survives, or enough unconstrained corrections remove every prediction.
Do not assume that a holomorphic texture automatically survives canonical
normalization, or that arbitrary normalization repairs a previously refuted
physical texture. Neutrino masses need their own allowed operators and scales.

### 13. Build a genuinely compatible curved ADE/G2 completion

**Asset:** the corrected maximal-isotropy analysis leaves a point stratum;
the literature supplies local ADE/T-brane mechanisms and ALE-fibration
constructions. Neither an isolated stratum nor a positive G2 form is already
the required vacuum.
**Next discriminator:** exhibit the global integral bundle/fibration and
solve the relevant torsion-free or explicitly sourced equations with the
required singularities and boundary data. **Success:** a geometry supporting
the *same* gauge and chiral operator. **Stop:** only a local cone, wrong
cohomology, or unsatisfied differential equations. The closed-base scope of
[Barbosa's construction][ale] and the distinctions in [R35][r35] matter.

### 14. Replace “spin-two slot” with a gravitational dynamics test

**Asset:** B1140 has representation-theoretic spin-two slots; separate
three-dimensional gravitational constructions are real assets.
**Next discriminator:** derive a quadratic kinetic operator and its gauge
identities, physical norm and coupling to the candidate matter stress tensor
in a single Lorentzian setup. **Success:** the appropriate propagating degrees
of freedom and universal gravitational coupling, followed by controlled
nonlinear consistency. **Stop:** wrong-sign modes, wrong dimension, no
kinetic term or nonuniversal coupling. This is high-impact and long-range;
representation content is not evidence that this calculation will succeed.

### 15. Make the full boundary-index collection a certified discriminator

**Asset:** B1431's lattice distinction and witness coefficients, partially
reproduced here, recover information the (0,0) series lost.
**Next discriminator:** certify the relevant triangulation hypotheses and
summation tails, and prove a bound covering *all* boundary classes that could
match each witness; compare as integral-lattice-labelled objects, not raw
coordinate pairs. **Success:** a portable quantum-topological discriminator
between the siblings. **Stop:** the distinction depended on a marking,
non-topological half-class extension or uncertified finite box.
This is one of the most concrete near-term mathematical deliverables. It is
a protected 3D index, **not** a four-dimensional fermion chirality count.

### 16. Join certified partial filling to its actual boundary quantum theory

**Asset:** the named degree-five partial filling already has a retained
interval certificate: two complete cusps and CS excluding mirror-fixed
classes. Repeating the abandoned grid counts adds little.
**Next discriminator:** apply an appropriate relative-index/filling construction
to that exact marked witness, carrying its peripheral lattice and any gauge
data through the filling map. **Success:** a computed boundary observable and
clear statement of which structures survive surgery. **Stop:** needed
arithmetic/representation data do not survive, or hypotheses fail.
The [Dehn-filling index paper][filling] supplies a concrete literature lead,
already identified in R35. Its abstract was rechecked here; its full proof
was not read in this audit. Nonzero geometric CS does not select a CS level.

### 17. Complete a quantum construction with a physical Hilbert-space test

**Asset:** state integrals, classical actions, gluing data and DGG/class-S
dictionaries already exist; “the project has no action or quantum theory”
is too broad. B8119 expressly completed its **definition** checklist and
recovered B787's previously overlooked state-integral evaluation at b=1.
**Next discriminator:** for one declared construction, specify boundary
states, integration contour, inner product and gluing/adjoint operations;
check positivity/unitarity and determine what time evolution, if any, follows.
**Success:** a coherent quantum theory on that specified dimension and scope.
**Stop:** only a formal invariant or analytic continuation without a physical
state interpretation. The [DGG index][dgg] is an existing 3D construction, not
an automatic 4D E6 theory. A higher-type dictionary requires additional input.

### 18. Compute thresholds with their massless modes retained

**Asset:** xB029 correctly finds non-acyclic characters; R35 explains why
that defeats the simple acyclic shortcut, not all primed-determinant methods.
**Next discriminator:** choose an admissible compact Q and unitary bundle;
compute its massless representations, cohomology norms and nonzero-spectrum
thresholds together, then test whether any coupling relation survives.
**Success:** a controlled physical matching relation from the chosen spectrum.
**Stop:** a homology torsion number is used in place of the spectral calculation,
or the extra massless fields spoil the assumed low-energy model. This is a
conditional use of [Friedmann–Witten][fw], not a reinstatement of L225's
withdrawn prediction or an automatic extension to cusped spaces.

### 19. Test whether tower growth has a physical coarse-graining map

**Asset:** exact tower arithmetic and asymptotics exist. xB030 connects several
golden quantities; it does not produce several independent constants.
**Next discriminator:** exhibit a map from tower level to a physical cutoff
or coarse-graining operation, derive its action on a dimensionless coupling,
and compare held-out levels with a fixed prescription.
**Success:** a beta function, scaling law or scale ratio derived from that map.
**Stop:** cover degree is merely renamed energy, or arbitrary matching can
fit any growth sequence. This is a research hypothesis. It neither violates
dimensional analysis nor generates an absolute unit from a bare shape.

### 20. Complete the cusped spectral/gravitational one-loop calculation

**Asset:** the old one-loop lane explicitly distinguishes a discrete geodesic
product from the missing continuous contribution and points to scattering data.
B739 already supplies the function-level **scalar weight-zero** scattering
transfer from the arithmetic parent to m004 in a common height convention;
the second pass's [recovery](../report_guided_resweep_2026_09_20/SCATTERING_RECOVERY.md)
restores that stronger antecedent. Do not redo it as though B8101's later
functional-equation checks were the entire evidence.
The corrected B8142 retains a Sym-power factorization but withdraws its
reflection formula because the acyclicity hypothesis fails. The next use of
torsion must include cohomology and the appropriate leading Laurent coefficient,
not quietly substitute the withdrawn value-at-zero formula.
**Next discriminator:** prove which scattering matrices belong to the actual
spin-2/ghost/twisted operators. The known scalar determinant on the cover is
not automatically its gravitational determinant. Then include continuous,
zero-mode, ghost and boundary contributions in one regularization.
**Success:** a complete determinant on the specified cusped background.
**Stop:** only the discrete product or a determinant for a different quotient
was computed. This could be mathematical-physics progress even without any
TOE claim; it is not already the 4D graviton test in entry 14.

### 21. Replace the unbounded generation hunt with a mechanism classification

**Asset:** B1427's doublet results and higher-Sym countercontrol distinguish
two mechanisms; xB031's completion remains explicitly unfinished at the
fetched pin. A one-dimensional extension space alone does not bound all
induced-module indices.
**Next discriminator:** characterize the index jump loci and bounds by module
type and peripheral restriction, using the exact |I|=2 witness as a control.
Coordinate with the existing finite scan instead of duplicating it.
**Success:** a scoped existence/bound theorem explaining when larger indices
can occur. **Stop:** another unproved extrapolation from a finite character
grid. Even an exact I=3 must pass entries 1, 5 and 6 before meaning three
physical generations. This is a mathematics opportunity, not a shortcut.

### 22. Obtain a laboratory test of the energy/localization distinction

**Asset:** B1095's finite-chain reversal/isospectrality and localization split;
its three existing tests pass here. The readout gap is already documented
in the edge-spec addendum.
**Next discriminator:** preregister a disorder-robust, per-gap localization
observable at feasible sizes; compare mirror-related and parity-breaking
windows against ordinary bulk-boundary/pumping predictions, then measure it.
**Success:** a reproducible differential in a physical simulator with stated
errors. **Stop:** only generic pumping is measured, or thresholds decide the
count. This is the closest direct experimental lane, but realizing a designed
Hamiltonian does not by itself show that fundamental nature uses this framework.

## 4. Attractive conclusions this audit does NOT license

- **No universal “chirality solved” or “chirality impossible.”** The coefficient
  system, physical operator, parent, domain and full spectrum must be specified.
- **No independent-evidence count from repeated algebraic faces.** xB030's
  identification and the field-to-McKay dependencies expressly prevent it.
- **No “SM embedding uniquely derived” without B1430's conditioning.**
- **No “A5 just excludes orbifold torsion.”** The precise uniqueness document
  defines A5 by first-homology torsion. Finite-order elements of pi1 are
  different. xB012's useful orbifold proposal must not fuse those meanings;
  the distinction was already explicit in the Jorgensen source audit.
- **No 122-order cosmological-constant failure assigned to the object.** That
  earlier cross-dimensional inference was withdrawn; it is not a current
  physical prediction to compare with observation.
- **No resurrection of the old calibrated “16 sigma” gauge-running claim.**
  The corrected, specified-model mismatch and its actual input spectrum are
  the relevant evidence.
- **No selected finite scan called a theorem over all cases.** In B1431,
  `step17_rigor.py` checks finitely many rims and uses a bounded inner search;
  those checks alone are not a global coercive bound. This audit reproduces
  coefficients and finite identities, not universal witness absence. That is
  a verification debt, not a claimed counterexample to the stated result.
- **No use of broad capstone prose to override its own scope.** For example,
  B1336's principal-SL2 squeeze does not cover every E6 embedding; B1368 names
  its matter frames and boundary conventions. Conversely, B1102 already
  limits the naive A2/SU3×SU3 hatch: a charge multiset is not a commuting SM
  product. Repeating that old rank-only discovery would not advance physics.

## 5. Recommended allocation of effort

Do not launch twenty-two projects. Start with three bounded deliverables:

1. **Physical bridge:** entry 1 on one exact positive witness, beginning with
   an action-derived admissibility/end-domain specification as clarified by
   the second pass above. In parallel
   conceptually, assess entry 2's parent equations only if that remains the
   chosen route. Spend effort on an admissibility/compatibility verdict, not
   on a new large bank of candidate integers.
2. **A finishable mathematical-physics result:** entry 15's complete tail and
   lattice certificate, then entry 16 on the already certified partial filling.
   This provides a real deliverable even if the 4D physical bridge fails.
3. **An empirical discriminator:** entry 22's actual measurement/readout
   specification, with a conceded-standard comparator. No collaborator
   contact, apparatus spending or external message is authorized by this audit.

For each, fix the full inputs and stop rule before executing a new scientific
producer. Retain the outcome as positive, negative, conditional or unmeasured;
never turn the last two into zero. Require at least one positive and one
negative control that can distinguish the proposed mechanism.

The strategic shift is **from collecting compatible-looking structures to
demonstrating a small number of compatible physical mechanisms and risky
predictions**. That is a route to finding out what the framework can explain,
not a promise that the complete TOE is already hidden inside it.
