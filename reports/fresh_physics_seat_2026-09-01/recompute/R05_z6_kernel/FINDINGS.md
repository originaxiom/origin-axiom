## Minor finding (outside the banked claim) — the primitivity ADDENDUM's table

The 2026-08-31 addendum's rescaling table claims |kernel| = 6 for k in {5, 7, 11}
(gcd(k,6) = 1). In the honest continuous ambient this is **wrong**: rescaling all
charges by k puts mu_k inside the kernel (every rescaled charge is a multiple of k), so
|kernel| = 6k for every k — verified e.g. (0, 0, 1/5) is in the k = 5 kernel by direct
substitution (all five conditions land in Z). My continuous numbers: k = 2,3,4,6,12 ->
12, 18, 24, 36, 72 (matching the addendum's rows) but k = 5, 7, 9, 10, 11 -> 30, 42, 54,
60, 66 (addendum: 6, 6, 18, 12, 6). The addendum's numbers fit 6*gcd(k,12), i.e. a
12-torsion-truncated root-of-unity search that misses kernel elements of order coprime
to 12. This *strengthens* the addendum's own fenced conclusion — under the honest circle
|kernel| = 6 **only** at k = +-1, so primitivity is even more load-bearing than stated.
The faithful quotient (the global form itself) is unaffected for every k. See
`rescaling_check.txt`.
