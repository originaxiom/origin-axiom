su(4) rank 3 > rank su(3)=2  -> every hom su(4)->su(3)^3 is zero -> su(4) NOT a subalgebra of su(3)^3
su(5) rank 4 > rank su(3)=2  -> every hom su(5)->su(3)^3 is zero -> su(5) NOT a subalgebra of su(3)^3
Hence 'SU(3)^3 -> SU(5)xU(1)' and 'SU(3)^3 -> Pati-Salam' are not subgroup steps.
SU(6)xSU(2) ⊃ SU(5)xU(1) and ⊃ SU(4)xSU(2)xU(1) (Pati-Salam's semisimple part): fine as embeddings, but NO committed menu for parent SU(6)xSU(2) exists.
