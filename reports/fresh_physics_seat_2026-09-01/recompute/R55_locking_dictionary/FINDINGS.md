# R55 — the locking dictionary (built at the owner's request)

**What was built.** `DICTIONARY.md`: the map from the object's closing bits {c, γ5, θ} (B766's table, R54e's symmetry
group) to reality's measured signs, enumerated exhaustively under CPT (`r55.py`), with the predictions and the protocol.

**Results of the computation.**
- Reality's physical discrete-choice space is rank 2 (three world bits mod CPT); the object's is rank 2 on the manifold
  plus the lift sign θ. Measured signs fall into four CPT-even types (EVEN, W handedness, K CP/T asymmetry, E EDM).
- Of six dictionaries, three survive the object's own axis semantics; only c = P, γ5 = T has both rows populated by
  signs already measured. Under it: T4, T6 are handedness signs; T7 = T3 are CP/T rate-asymmetry signs; E-type signs are
  the product of the two rows (the object has no (odd, odd) axis).
- Predictions: P1 all K-type signs one bit (testable with a leptonic CP phase); P2 all W-type signs one bit up to θ
  (needs a physical reading of the chord; none in the record); P3 all EDM signs share one sign pattern (two measured EDMs
  decide); P4 exclusions (no CP asymmetry in the chirality row, no handedness in the time row), decidable now, not
  violated anywhere in the record.

**Provenance.** c and γ5 columns re-derived (R54 §4, R54e); the θ column and the T3 = Out(A5) identification are B766's
and are NOT re-derived by the seat. Physical sign values quoted from memory for sign only; check against PDG before use.

**Bearing.** This is the first place in the seat's work where the program makes a statement reality can refuse. It is
also the honest boundary: in the SM the same lockings hold for a different reason, so the decisive observables are the
ones outside the SM's single-phase sector.
