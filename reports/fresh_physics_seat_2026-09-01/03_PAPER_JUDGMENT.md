# Judgment on the existing papers and spines — fresh physics seat, 2026-09-01

*Read: `papers/PORTFOLIO_2026-08-27.md` (the ratified architecture), `papers/P3_THE_PAPER/SPEC.md`,
`papers/flagship/README.md`, the falsifiability matrix, the frozen/withdrawn history. Judged
against my own Phase-1 map, not against the portfolio's self-description.*

## The one-line verdict

**The portfolio's architecture is right; its headline is wrong.** The anti-dismissal
machinery of P3 (misses in the abstract, non-claims box, recognition table, freedom-ledger
methods, verification-in-minutes) is the best-designed defence against the numerology prior
I have seen in any unpublished programme — keep all of it. But the sentence the whole paper
hangs on — *"a single hyperbolic 3-manifold forces the Standard Model's gauge structure"* —
is the one sentence the record does not support, and a competent referee will find the two
failure points in an afternoon, because the repo's own arcs hand them over:

1. **"Single manifold"** — B1136 (the genericity control) shows the E₆ arithmetic is a
   family entrance; my computation sharpens it to a *commensurability-class* entrance, with
   the (↠2T, ↛2I) selection signature shared by ~30% of small census manifolds. The true
   specificity is: minimal volume + the unique arithmetic knot (Reid). Say that.
2. **"Forces the SM's gauge structure"** — the record's own fences (B1160, B1170, B1033)
   put chirality, frame existence, SM-shaping, and the generation count on the observer
   side, and the cascade landing is Borel–de Siebenthal (conceded at B951/B1210). What is
   forced is: the E₆ skeleton at the class level, the ℤ₆ global form, hypercharge *content*
   given SM-shaping, termination-at-SM among registerable descents, and E₆(−26) given one
   real-structure closing. That is a strong true sentence. It is not the spec's sentence.

## Per-artifact judgment

- **P1 (cc3's foundation, minimal-description → E₆).** Not on this branch; judged only via
  its citations. The chain front (Layer 0 of my map) is axiom-driven; a math-phys venue
  will accept it only if C3/C4 are presented as *definitions of the programme*, not as
  derivations. Risk: the title-thesis problem above, one storey higher.
- **P2 (rigidity crown — e₆ strata, exact hypercharge, the hatch).** **The strongest paper
  in the portfolio and the right one to finish first.** Pure representation theory, exact,
  two-bench-verified in places, zero physics claims. Add B1134 (the simultaneous closing /
  E₆(−26) theorem) — it is the most quotable single theorem the programme owns. One
  caution: a Lie-theory referee will ask what is new relative to Borel–de Siebenthal,
  Dynkin, and the real-forms literature; the honest answer is "the explicit integral/
  Chevalley-level uniqueness and the joint signature-color statement," and the paper
  should say exactly that in the introduction.
- **P3 (the boundary thesis).** Viable and worth writing **after re-headlining** (see
  `04_PAPER_CONCEPT.md` for my version). Its Movement II (WITHHELD) is the genuinely
  novel physics-facing content: a model that *proves its own value-emptiness* under seal
  is new as methodology, and the ten value-negatives + type law + Mostow-scale-freeness
  make a coherent negative theorem-cluster. Movement III (the observer priced at one/two
  bits) should absorb the A6 relabeling (`02_A6_VERDICT.md`): orientation joins the
  closings, the mirror motifs become deck-involution corollaries, and the "priced at one
  bit" bookkeeping gets cleaner, not weaker.
- **P4 (markov stage) / PC23–PC26.** PC23/PC24/PC25 look like publishable mathematical
  letters on their own gates; no objection. PC26 (full chain) is correctly HELD.
- **The flagship preprint.** Fine as a thesis-artifact with its badge discipline; do not
  submit it anywhere — its value is as the companion index P3 points to.
- **P5 monoid (withdrawn), PC13 (refuted), P1_value_theory/P2_trinity (not submittable).**
  The graveyard is in better shape than most programmes' showcases; the withdrawal
  discipline is itself publishable material (P7).
- **P7 (methodology).** Underrated by the portfolio's own ordering. Sealed preregistration
  with two-outcome criteria, adversarial multi-seat verification, freedom ledgers, an
  error ledger with 50+ named classes, gates that fail closed — *no one else in
  mathematical physics is doing this*, and it is the part of the programme other groups
  could adopt tomorrow. I would move it up the queue, jointly with P2.

## Do they claim too much, too little, or the wrong thing?

- **Too much:** only in headlines and connective prose ("forces," "single object,"
  "two-ended," B717's stale "gen COUNT 3"). The arc layer under the headlines is almost
  uniformly more careful than the sentences above it — an unusual and creditable inversion
  (most programmes are the other way up).
- **Too little:** the amphichirality/A6 story (the record undersells its own B605 by not
  drawing the general corollary), the commensurability-class framing (B1136 stopped one
  step short of naming the Bianchi class), and the methodology (P7).
- **The wrong thing:** the portfolio still aims its center of mass at "look how much
  structure is forced." The evidence's center of mass is the *boundary itself* — forced
  skeleton / withheld values with both directions proved — plus the discipline that found
  it. The honest flagship is the boundary paper, and the record has been telling its
  authors this for months (the ratified refinement even says "the thesis beats the
  inventory"; it just kept the inflated thesis).
