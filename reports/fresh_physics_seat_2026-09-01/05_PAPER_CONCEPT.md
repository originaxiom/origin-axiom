## The reframing it rests on (all verified this bench or in-repo)

1. The object is the **commensurability class of the Bianchi group PGL(2,ℤ[ω])** — the
   arithmetic of ℚ(√−3). The Gieseking manifold is its minimal representative; the
   figure-eight complement is *the oriented observer's view of it* (A6 relabeled as the
   first closing); Reid's theorem is what makes that view canonical among knots.
2. The E₆ entrance is the class's McKay signature: **onto 2T, not onto 2I** — real,
   two-sided, and shared by ~30% of small cusped manifolds. Specificity lives one floor
   down (minimality + arithmeticity), not in the door.
3. Everything SM-shaped downstream is exact E₆ representation theory, with four
   non-classical candidates: the registerability-termination rule, the ℤ₆ global form
   derivation, the exact-hypercharge realization inside the object's u(1)³, and the
   E₆(−26) simultaneous-closing theorem.
4. The values are **provably absent**, ten routes, under seal. The scale is Mostow-absent.
   The generation count is obstructed (degree-2 trace field). Chirality is one priced bit.

## What I deliberately removed, and why

- **The origin thesis as a claim.** "Existence from the impossibility of non-existence"
  survives only as one motivating paragraph, labeled as the programme's question, never
  as a result. The chain's front (aperiodic description → golden word → geometric
  realization) enters as *definitions with priced forks*, or not at all.
- **"Three generations," "two-ended E₆/E₈," the compression victory.** Killed by the
  record's own arcs (B1033; B1133; B1028's 1.585-bit margin is stated, not celebrated).
- **The observer metaphysics as vocabulary.** "Closings" survive as the technical term
  for priced choices (it earns its keep in Movement I via A6); "the seam," "the hearing,"
  "the voice" do not appear.
