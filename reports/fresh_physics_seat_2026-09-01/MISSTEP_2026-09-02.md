## The misstep

At step 3 the chain leaves the object and never returns. Every statement after it, E6, the 27, the charge torus, the
measurement ladder, the 14-dimensional algebra, is a function of the number ℚ(√−3), shared by m003 and twelve other
census manifolds and by nothing that distinguishes m004 among them. Step 4's instrument has a one-element image on
hyperbolic manifolds, so "the object selects E6" was never a selection. The program then read the output of a
one-output instrument as a property of the object and built the physics on that reading. The three bits that the
observer supplies (step 7) are the only place the object could re-enter, and they are too small to carry what is missing
(a chiral spectrum, a compact form, two broken U(1)s, three generations are not bits).

The record's own honest lines already point here: B8118 ("E6 is a function of the field"), B282 ("arithmetic, not
geometric"), B1136 (the sole separator inside the family is H1 = ℤ), B1163 (no object-canonical orientation). What the
record did not do is draw the consequence: the object-specific content of the program ends at step 3, and steps 4–7
should not be written in the object's name.

## The rule that prevents the recurrence (proposed as seat rule 3, for the owner's election)

**Before reading an instrument's output as a property of the object, compute the instrument's image over the natural
comparison class. An instrument whose image has one element on that class carries no information about the object; an
instrument whose output is constant on the object's commensurability class carries information about the class only.**
Owner rule 1 (sweep before an absence claim) guards against false absences; this guards against false selections.
