## Quantifier, assumptions and prior

bundle and equivalent positive L2 norms; the application is to F05's
geometric coefficient four and its dual. It is not a statement about all
flat representations, all sources, all metric changes or the full object.

Let d be a closed densely defined flat differential on total forms.
In a reference positive norm suppose Q0=d+d0* is self-adjoint and
||Q0 u||>=delta0||u||. Replace the norms in every degree by

    m ||u||0^2 <= ||u||1^2 <= M ||u||0^2, 0<m<=M<infinity.

Keep the same differential and its graph closure. The new adjoint and
Dirac domain must be obtained from the new norm, not held fixed by fiat.

Expected: the complex remains acyclic with closed range and

    Delta1 >= delta0^2 m/M.

No smallness assumption, derivative bound or isospectrality is claimed.
For F05 delta0=3/2; each regular compact metric/bundle-metric modification
has some finite m,M, but no uniform values across degenerating families
are supplied. Very light positive modes are not excluded.

For the gluing application, replace finitely many embedded closed balls
inside the regular interior of the same three-manifold, keeping the flat
connection unchanged on the exterior/collars and leaving no new boundary
or singularity. Van Kampen preserves pi1 under this specific replacement;
the global holonomy is unchanged. A smooth flat-bundle isomorphism can
be identity outside, so its metric comparison is bounded. A local F06
core satisfying those hypotheses cannot create massless spinor modes,
even if it has pointwise algebraic kernel and nonparallel Higgs fields.
