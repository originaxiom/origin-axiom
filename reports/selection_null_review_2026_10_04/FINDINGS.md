# A match score needs a joint null model

October 4, 2026. The selection bar has a scope gap, not a broken arithmetic
function. Its producer explicitly says independent looks; the prose applies
the formula across frames, levels, ends and variants without establishing
a suitable dependence hypothesis. We verified exact counterexamples to
universal calibration. No actual B1518 grade was recomputed or reversed.

## What the exact controls establish

The independent digit-pair control agrees with the formula: two rate-one-tenth
independent events have union probability 19/100. On a separate uniform
100000-point null space, two disjoint 501-point events have individual rate
501/100000 and actual union probability 0.01002. The independent formula
gives 0.0099948999, below the bar's 0.01 threshold while the actual union
is above it. The associated two-valued null p values are superuniform.
This is not a model of the repository's actual looks or its confidence bounds.

Similarly, sampling two distinct objects from a five-object population
with one hit gives hit probability 2/5; sampling with replacement gives
9/25. The same base rate does not specify the sampling law. Enumeration
of generated states alone does not define a physical chance model.

All 1024 pairs of events on a five-point space obey the union bound.
This supports a dependence-safe bound when valid marginal probabilities
are available; it does not invent those probabilities. Some dependent
classes do satisfy an appropriate Sidak inequality, so we do not say
all dependence makes the formula invalid.

The independent-test restriction is explicit in the
[GraphPad statistics guide](https://www.graphpad.com/guides/prism/latest/statistics/stat_the_method_of_bonferroni.htm)
and the [DiscreteFWER manual](https://stat.ethz.ch/CRAN/web/packages/DiscreteFWER/refman/DiscreteFWER.html).
Our finite counterexamples independently show why a broader contract needs
justification; the underlying probability facts are known, not novel.

## Implication for the physical mission

Keep comparable-object controls and separate fixed selection from scanning.
Specify a joint null or a valid dependence-safe bound before treating a
trials score as calibrated. A census frequency, mathematical derivation,
statistical rarity and physical explanation are four different claims.
A rare feature can remain physically unidentified; a physically useful
mechanism need not be unique among comparison manifolds.

This does not show that the programme's coincidences are accidental or
significant. It prevents either conclusion from being asserted through an
unjustified probability formula. The highest-priority physical test remains
the same-model action and source/domain completion in the
