## Declared inputs and conventions

The physical action, compact gauge group, four-dimensional interpretation,
base metric, carrier and coefficient remain supplied. This is an analysis
of that candidate model, not a derivation of them from OA. Boundary data
are varied freely only in the unrestricted scenario; prescribed Dirichlet
data and a closed join are different scenarios, not contradictions.

A is anti-Hermitian and Psi is Hermitian, with C=A+Psi on columns and
d_C=d_A+[Psi, .] on endomorphisms. Positive trace norms use M dagger M.
The moment residual is mu=d_A^*Psi. Positive complex changes of frame
g=exp(epsilon s), with s Hermitian trace-free, give delta C=d_C s.
Their anti-Hermitian analogues are the physical compact gauge directions.
Outward boundary normal and the nonnegative Laplacian d^*d are used.
The potential is V=c (norm F_C squared + norm mu squared), c>0.
No harmonic-map energy is silently substituted for this functional.

4. Keep the fixed-Dirichlet and closed controls. A nonharmonic deformation must

   This supplied term is not claimed supersymmetric or derived, and the

6. Read the hash-pinned silver profiles and restore H0,H1,H2,H3 by Euler

A successful result is a conditional analytic description of free-boundary
fluctuations, exact controls and a correctly graded silver domain table.

action, a mass spectrum, three generations or the full goal. If the gauge
quotient or positivity argument fails, retain that failure and do not infer
