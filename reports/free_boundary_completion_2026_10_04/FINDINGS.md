## The fixed-monodromy fluctuation calculation

Work on a connected compact three-dimensional core with smooth nonempty
boundary and a smooth positive-metric harmonic flat background C=A+Psi.
For the retained potential V=c integral (|F_C|^2+|d_A^*Psi|^2), c>0,
both residuals vanish. R83 already established that this makes the first
variation zero even when smooth boundary variations are free. We credit
that result; no claim that stationarity itself newly selects a boundary
condition is made.

For a Hermitian trace-free positive complex gauge parameter s, the actual
field variation is delta A=[Psi,s], delta Psi=d_A s. Independently
differentiating both residuals gives

    delta F_C=[F_C,s],
    delta(d_A^*Psi)=L s,
    L=d_A^*d_A+sum_i ad(Psi_i)^2.

L has positive Dirichlet quadratic form. On the compact core it admits a
unique smooth Ls=0 solution for every smooth Hermitian boundary value.
The solution space is infinite-dimensional. These directions preserve
the monodromy to first order and lie in the kernel of the bare Hessian.
At most finitely many become compact gauge: equality with d_C xi for an
anti-Hermitian xi would make s-xi a global parallel endomorphism. The
space of such endomorphisms is finite-dimensional. The invariant regular
embedding gives the same conclusion in the supplied full parent.

This is stronger than counting arbitrary boundary parameters and calling
them particles: it explicitly checks the linearized field equations,
positive norm and gauge quotient. It remains a statement about a classical
Hessian in a specified candidate action. Quantum corrections, a new boundary
action, additional constraints and physical operator domains can change it.
It does not prove infinitely many particles exist in nature.

Fixing the metric boundary value sets s at the boundary to zero and removes
these positive complex directions. A compact closed join also falls outside
the nonempty-boundary argument. Thus this result neither invalidates R81's
Dirichlet positive nor prejudges R85's different joined-background test.

## Constructive boundary comparison

are not removable by compact gauge. With normalized torus area their
finite positive kinetic norms are k coth(kL). Fourier orthogonality
gives an explicit infinite family, independently of the general argument.

Adding the compact-gauge-invariant comparator term
beta/2 integral_boundary |Psi_tangent|^2 penalizes each such nonconstant
direction by beta epsilon^2 k^2/2. Its nonnegative abelian Higgs quadratic
form has the finite relative harmonic kernel. The constant normal one-form
survives, as it must; on this product its dimension is one per Cartan
generator. We checked rather than discarded this residual zero direction.

This is a concrete demonstration that an end term can change the kernel.
It introduces beta and a chosen boundary functional. It is not OA-derived,
not proved supersymmetric, not a proposed complete silver completion and
not a mass calculation. Adding it to a silver background requires solving
its new stationarity conditions; the old admitted background cannot simply
be presumed to survive. The kinetic norm is not a bulk potential, and no
frozen energy-to-norm quotient is labeled a physical mass.
