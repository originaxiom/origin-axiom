## Working rule for the next step

The recent positive results remove a genuine convention ambiguity, but that ambiguity was not the source of Λ or the cusp unit. Do not spend another campaign on equivalent word pairs. The positive-carrier result now also separates preserving descriptions from choosing their full reversible completion: naturality and positive-word fidelity do not distinguish F2 from the exhibited proper quotient. Work on the earliest undecided implication: which admitted reversible contexts or observables justify the stronger carrier, and which separately require its coefficient system? Do not replace this question with a larger census of alternative groups. An answer must say both what is forced and what alternatives remain. A failure of one attempted implication is not a theorem against every realization, and a successful conditional construction is not a derivation of its premises.

The full goal remains to fix the genesis and work upstream toward a full physical theory. These scoped completions are intermediate evidence, not a redefinition of that goal.
