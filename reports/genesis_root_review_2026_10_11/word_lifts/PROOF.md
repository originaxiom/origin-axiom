# Word lifts and the data hidden by abelianization

These statements assume the free group F=F(a,b). They do not derive faithful word data from integer records. Let J_w denote conjugation by w and use f g=f after g. The symbols L and R below denote automorphisms only when their word images are specified.

## Four positive elementary pairs and one conjugacy class

For a positive word, exponent sums equal letter counts. A map with matrix [[1,1],[0,1]] must send a to a and b to a word with one a and one b. Thus it is exactly L0=(a,ab) or L1=(a,ba). For matrix [[1,0],[1,1]], exactly R0=(ab,b) and R1=(ba,b) occur. Their inverses are respectively (a,Ab), (a,bA), (aB,b), (Ba,b), so all are automorphisms. This is a complete classification under positive-word realization, not under nonnegative exponent sums with arbitrary cancellation.

The identity

    J_q f J_q^-1 = J_(q f(q^-1)) f

follows by applying both sides to a word. It gives the complete simultaneous-conjugation table:

| Pair | q such that (Li,Rj)=J_q (L0,R0) J_q^-1 |
|---|---|
| L0,R0 | 1 |
| L1,R0 | b |
| L0,R1 | A |
| L1,R1 | Ab |

Equality on a and b proves equality on every word. Consequently, for any one fixed choice of elementary pair, the entire generated action is conjugate to the standard one by a single change of base point. The insertion-order alternatives introduce no new isomorphism class of this action. Treating this algebraic equivalence as physical equivalence additionally requires transporting every field, boundary datum and observable; it is not a declaration that those data are gauge.

## The peripheral word and the all-word inner intersection

Put c=aBAb and H=<L0,R0> as a group, allowing inverses for this mathematical statement. Direct substitution gives L0(c)=R0(c)=c. Thus every element of H fixes c as an element, not merely its conjugacy class. If J_w belongs to H, then w commutes with c.

Here is the centralizer calculation without invoking the braid representation. The Cayley graph of the free group in the given basis is a tree: a cycle with backtracking removed would be a nonempty reduced word equal to the identity. Since c is cyclically reduced, its successive powers and the paths spelling c between them form a bi-infinite geodesic A. Left multiplication by c translates A by four edges. For any vertex x outside A, the unique path from x to c x goes to A, travels four edges along it, and leaves A; hence d(x,c x)=4+2d(x,A). Therefore A is the unique set of minimal displacement.

An element commuting with c preserves A. Its restriction to this line cannot be a reflection, since a reflection conjugates a nonzero translation to its inverse. It is a translation by an integer number of edges. This integer defines an injective homomorphism from the centralizer into Z: a zero translation fixes a vertex, whereas the free group's left action on its vertices is free. Its image is a nonzero subgroup dZ; choose u realizing its positive generator. Injectivity then gives c=u^m for some nonzero integer m.

The cyclic word c contains each of a,A,b,B exactly once, so it cannot be a proper power. Indeed a cyclically reduced proper power repeats the cyclically reduced root at least twice, making each signed-letter count divisible by that exponent. A non-cyclically-reduced root would leave a nonempty conjugating prefix on its power and could not equal the cyclically reduced word c. Thus |m|=1 and the centralizer is exactly <c>.

For the reverse inclusion set Delta=L0 R0^-1 L0. Substitution gives Delta=(aBA,a), and Delta^4=J_c. This also follows by composing Delta twice to obtain (aBAbA,aBA) and twice again to obtain J_c. Therefore

    H intersect Inn(F) = <J_c>.

This is B1634's existing intersection statement, rederived without its cited braid-kernel theorem. Conjugating the proof by J_q gives the same result for every fixed positive pair, with peripheral element q c q^-1. In particular J_a and J_b do not belong to the standard H: neither a nor b is a power of c (their exponent sums are nonzero, whereas c's are zero).

## Independent variants are not a coordinate change

Let K be the group generated by all four positive elementary automorphisms together. Direct substitution gives

    L1 L0^-1 = J_A,     R1 R0^-1 = J_b.

Since J_a and J_b generate all inner automorphisms, Inn(F) is contained in K. In contrast H meets it only in <J_c>. The difference is between choosing one fixed pair, whose alternatives are simultaneously conjugate, and adjoining the alternative realizations as independent generators. A common incidence matrix does not identify these two operations.

No theorem identifying K with the full orientation-preserving automorphism group is needed or claimed here. Nor is group completion being equated with legal forward evolution.

In fact the monoid generated by any of these positive elementary maps intersects Inn(F) only in the identity. An inner automorphism acts trivially on abelianization. Starting at I, right multiplication of a nonnegative invertible matrix by L adds its first column to the second; by R it adds its second column to the first. The sum of entries strictly increases because neither column vanishes. Thus no nonempty positive product has matrix I. The proof holds for the two-generator and four-generator monoids alike.

## The founding substitution and its double tick

Take sigma=(ab,a) and P=(b,a). Then sigma=L0 P and P L0 P=R1, so

    sigma^2 = L0 R1 = (aba,ab).

But L0 R0=(aab,ab). They are different based automorphisms, although their matrices agree. Direct substitution gives the stronger relation

    sigma^2 = J_ab (L0 R0).

B1607 already records this up-to-inner relation, with the inverse conjugator BA taking sigma^2 to L0 R0. It is important to carry that qualification when using the latest based weave. Since J_ab is not in H and L0 R0 is in H, sigma^2 is not in this particular based H. This is not an obstruction to the root: the coherent pair (L0,R1) contains sigma^2 and is simultaneously conjugate to (L0,R0) by J_A. Specifically J_A (L0 R0) J_a=sigma^2. Transporting the full construction provides an explicit algebraic bridge.

This resolves an apparent mismatch between B1323's dictionary choice R=P L0 P=(ba,b) and B1634's R0=(ab,b) at the level of the based move group. It does not verify that all of their additional representation or boundary data have already been transported consistently.

## Consequence for the genesis dependency chain

The count matrices determine four positive elementary realizations and one simultaneous-inner-conjugacy class of fixed-pair actions, given the stronger positive-word premise. There is consequently no need to pay for their insertion orders as unrelated physical choices if the entire construction is transported.

What still needs an upstream specification is the meaning of a move: a selected coherent realization, an outer equivalence class, or the joint group of all realizations. B1323's abelianization statement cannot by itself choose among these. B1634's intersection is sound for its fixed pair and all its coherent transports. Allowing independent variants changes its hypothesis; it does not refute it.

No Standard-Model parameter, physical handedness, Λ clause, end unit or spacetime action is derived by this algebra alone. Its contribution is to remove a redundant fixed-pair choice while exposing exactly which distinct choice would alter the downstream symmetry argument.
