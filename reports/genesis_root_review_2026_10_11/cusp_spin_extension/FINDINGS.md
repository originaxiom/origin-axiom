Multiplication by eta^12 changes weight k-6 to k and multiplier rho to epsilon rho, where epsilon(S)=epsilon(T)=-1. This removes a potential double count of freedoms: the weight change and opposite sign are related, but only after transporting the cusp condition as well.

## What this changes upstream

The open canonical-bundle identity is not enough to select the cusp spin extension. The two known lift signs do not eliminate that distinction unless their cusp conditions are transported too. Conversely this distinction is not a fresh independent metaplectic obstruction, and it does not invalidate the old table in its stated convention.
