The radial map is load-bearing. At fixed coordinates, changing b produces unbounded relative norms, so identity is not the second comparison map. In a common temporal frame the exact formula is

    K_b(r+c) = K_b'(r) exp(-2c A + ell(b'-b) Xi),

The last factor is constant positive and the translated base metrics are uniformly comparable. Flatness supplies a chain map over the whole base, including the nonsplit core; no global decomposition into five line bundles is presumed.
