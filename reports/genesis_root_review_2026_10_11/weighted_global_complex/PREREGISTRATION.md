# Weighted global flag count design

October 13, 2026. Lifecycle: mechanism. Uses: Gate3 flag end, Gate4 mixing constraint, noncompact admission, kernel transport, and the October 4 silver full boundary restriction. This is a new application of those results, not a claim of an unprecedented cohomology method.

## Quantifier and prior

Compute the fixed supplied m135 nonsplit positive-flag completion and its split control, in W, W dual, exterior-square W and its dual, with every cohomology degree retained. No claim about the whole object, all rows, all ends or all physical boundary mechanisms follows from this one-carrier laboratory.

The analytic prior is that the superexponential weights reduce the end to E_- and hence to a full-torus coefficient-relative cone. Its Euler characteristic must be zero. The nonsplit endpoints are expected to vanish, but neither that nor the four individual Betti numbers is set as a desired answer. Split endpoints may survive. Three generations is not a target imposed on any rank. Unequal degree-one counts with retained endpoints, or a disagreement between finite models, must be reported rather than normalized away.

## Outcome criteria and controls

Reconstruct each representation from candidate inputs, not from saved mode counts. Verify both relators, the literal block-diagonal peripheral representation, induced weights, nonsplitting versus the split control, and the complete Fox restriction. Construct both finite complexes independently and compute all ranks and Betti numbers. Report all eight coefficient cases, not a selected passing member.

Pass requires the exact chain identities, nonnegative Betti numbers, agreement of the two finite models, Euler identity and the degree-reversed duality check. The ordinary absolute core counts in the nonsplit cases must recover the previously recorded (h0,h1) values (0,2), (1,3), (0,4), (0,3); these are detector controls from earlier source, not desired weighted answers. Full-relative versus absolute duality is another control.

Test synthetic missing endpoint classes, their cancellation by an attached endpoint, empty-boundary recovery, a nonzero attached differential and rejection of a broken chain map. Wrong R2 sign must fail on at least one actual representation. Retaining E_- with zero differential must be rejected when its actual differential is nonzero. Check the weight formula and an explicit common positive tail bound; the zero-amplitude control must fail that bound. These controls can pass and fail without reference to a Standard-Model target.

Any failed execution is preserved with unmodified source and logs. Fixes receive a new seal before rerunning. The first execution follows a committed, pushed seal. The wrapper records source hashes before and after, every attempted exit status, raw stdout and stderr, and stops on the first failed job. No science source changes during execution.

## Interpretation boundary

Success establishes the finite cohomology of the specified cone. Identifying it with complete L2 cohomology also depends on PROOF.md and the earlier admission/transport applications. These checks do not independently certify global PDE arguments, identify four-dimensional particles, compute couplings or select the parent/end class. A zero full Euler characteristic is not a universal no-chirality theorem. No canonical B-number or GENESIS adoption is proposed by execution alone.
