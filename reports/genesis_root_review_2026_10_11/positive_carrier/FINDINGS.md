# The extra information required by the free carrier

2026-10-11. Author: originaxiom. Local proof and exact checks; independent analytic acceptance and formal banking pending.

Retaining every positive description, its order and every substitution does not force a faithful free-group carrier. An explicit proper quotient retains all those data and a nontrivial commutator. The distinction is positive-word fidelity versus fidelity to all signed words, not order versus mere counts.

The affirmative result is a fully explicit representation and reconstruction map. The limiting result concerns one implication in the motivation of GENESIS GM4. It does not refute the punctured-torus theorem after faithful F2 and the surface category are supplied, change canonical GENESIS, or rule out a physical realization.

## Proof and exact evidence

The elementary [proof](PROOF.md) constructs affine pairs over Z[X^±1,Y^±1]. Their two polynomial components record signed lattice edges. A positive path contributes exactly one edge at each successive total degree, so all its letters are recoverable in order. This proves injectivity for every length, not just the tested window.

All substitutions act by an explicit semilinear formula. Automorphisms have inverse induced actions, including the golden substitution a→ab,b→a. Meanwhile every commutator in the quotient is a translation, and translations commute. Hence the nontrivial eighteen-letter free word

    abABabbABaBAbbaBBA = [[a,b],[a,b²]] after free reduction

maps to the identity, while [a,b] itself does not. The quotient loses signed-loop distinctions without losing any positive word.

## What this changes in the root strategy

FK5's requested observable of order or the commutator is a useful discriminator against the abelian quotient. It is not by itself a discriminator against every proper quotient. A successful justification of full F2 must require more signed information or an appropriately stated universal property.

The universal group-completion property does force F2: arbitrary pairs in arbitrary groups must extend uniquely. PROOF.md gives the elementary two-map argument. This is an available sufficient premise, not a premise derived from existence, positive-word injectivity, or the observed Standard Model.

The next useful step is not a larger quotient census. It is to trace what reversible contexts and observables the existing principle actually admits, then ask whether their distinguishing power entails that universal requirement. Keep the subsequent geometric and physical realization arrows separate. A selected Q8 coefficient system, a holomorphic grading or a boundary unit must not be assumed to follow simply because the underlying free carrier has been justified.
