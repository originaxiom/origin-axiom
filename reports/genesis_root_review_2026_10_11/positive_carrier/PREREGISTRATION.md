# Testing what faithful positive words require

2026-10-11. Author: originaxiom. Local foundation audit, not a banked B arc.

## Question and scope

Does injectivity on all positive words, together with descent of every free-group substitution, force a faithful F2 carrier? Test an explicit proper quotient. This is a statement about the grammar and all finite words, upstream of the weave or any selected thread. It selects no physical carrier and makes no negative claim about physics, geometry or the conditional surface theorem.

The reference is GENESIS v1.41 at main `6c148ddd495d133213140995976ff08849793d5d`, GM4 and FK5. Main already retains faithful F2 and the surface category as premises. R57's natural abelian quotient and R79's recovery of periphery within faithful F2 are prior results. The narrower addition sought here is a proper quotient which, unlike abelianization, retains every positive word and a nontrivial commutator. No historical or mathematical novelty is claimed.

## Conventions and input audit

The alphabet a,b denotes description letters, not the L,R alphabet of update histories. Uppercase letters are inverses. Products concatenate left to right; [u,v]=uvU V, where U and V mean the inverse words. Endomorphism composition applies the right factor first.

Work over R=Z[X^±1,Y^±1]. An affine pair (m,u) has m=X^rY^s and u in R², with product (m,u)(n,v)=(mn,u+mv). Send a to (X,(1,0)) and b to (Y,(0,1)); G is the subgroup generated by these images. The proof does not assume that G is the full free metabelian group or invoke the Magnus embedding theorem. Ordinary integer and polynomial arithmetic, free reduction, the group laws and the elementary proof in PROOF.md are the inputs. No external surface, spectrum or physical theorem is load-bearing.

## Declared prior and outcomes

The prior is that this quotient supplies the counterexample. The all-length positive-word claim rests on the path-decoding proof, not on a finite search. PASS requires every exact assertion below and the stated analytic argument. A failure is preserved and prevents the proposed conclusion until diagnosed; it is not evidence against the full programme. A finite PASS alone does not prove the universal claims.

Checks, fixed before execution:

1. Independent signed lattice-walk and affine-multiplication evaluators agree on every freely reduced word of length at most five. All signed words of length at most three also check cancellation.
2. Every positive word of length at most ten is recovered exactly by an independent decoder; the encodings have no collisions. The abelian comparator must identify ab and ba, while G must distinguish them.
3. The explicit word [[a,b],[a,b²]] is nonempty after free reduction but evaluates to the identity in G. The first commutator must remain nontrivial. Inverse, associativity and product controls use all freely reduced words of length at most two, with associativity additionally checked against the four generators.
4. For each of the nine preregistered substitutions in verify.py, direct substitution agrees with the semilinear formula on every freely reduced word of length at most four. The list includes Fibonacci, its inverse, positive shears, swap, inversion, a collapsing endomorphism, a commutator-valued endomorphism and an inner automorphism. Both Fibonacci inverse compositions must recover every signed word in that window at the free-word and quotient levels. Kernel images must remain identity under every fixture.
5. Nonvacuity: the decoder rejects the commutator's non-positive flow, abelianization fails positive injectivity, and the nontrivial free kernel word is detected independently of its quotient value.
