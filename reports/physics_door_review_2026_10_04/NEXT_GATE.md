# One candidate and one physical completion

October 4, 2026. This is the proposed next work contract, not a sealed
new calculation, a derived source law or a completed physical theory.
It uses the audit to stop cross-model substitutions and redundant scans.
The goal remains a parameter-free Standard Model and full physical theory.

## Candidate and alternative

Start with an existing ordered nonsplit rank-five positive, not a newly
chosen favorable count. SM B1530's m135 configuration at its recorded
character is a concrete candidate; its exact algebraic route must first
be replayed on its own frozen inputs. Its matching magnitude-one class
differences are a mechanism diagnostic, not three generations.

The m135 coefficient is not R75's M6 coefficient. R81's general compact
Dirichlet theorem may apply only after its hypotheses are checked on m135;
one cannot transfer R75's actual metric or numerical flux to it. If this
candidate fails admission or the required dictionary, retain its algebraic
positive and return to the next justified candidate. Do not replace it
silently with one of this fork's vector-like backgrounds.

## Input budget before calculation

Record separately what OA derives and what the conditional model supplies:
the carrier, metric, spacetime, gauge parent, field content, action,
coupling, source support, boundary functional and fermion boundary domain.
At present important items in that list remain supplied. A borrowed
seven-dimensional gauge model is a useful conditional experiment, not a
parameter-free derivation from the programme's principles.

The first derivation question is whether OA specifies a physical core or
end law at all. Look for an existing principle and its mathematical map
to the law before adding fields or coefficients. If none is found, state
the missing input explicitly. A test with a supplied law must remain
conditional and cannot meet the full goal. No desired index, hand-picked
sign imbalance or target-fit quartic may define the source law.

## Admission and stationarity

- [ ] Replay the marked coefficient, peripheral words and both dual orders.
      Validate transformed representations before any Fox/cohomology rank.
- [ ] Specify a common bulk plus core/end action and derive every field
      equation, connection and Higgs variation, and boundary variation.
      Declare the admissible finite-energy spaces and gauge transformations.
- [ ] Check the exact integrated flag/source balance for each order on this
      carrier. Determine what flux the same action must provide, with its
      sign and magnitude conventions. Satisfying one balance alone is not
      a solution of all equations.
- [ ] Show that the proposed source/end fields themselves are stationary.
      Do not impose a flux as external Dirichlet data and label it generated.
- [ ] Compare split, ordered and mixed configurations in the same domain
      with the same source law. Compute the relaxed effective energy only
      after admitted corrections, metric response and kinetic normalization.
      A frozen quartic is a separately labeled diagnostic.

Stop this admission claim if an essential source or boundary variation is
unpaid. Preserve the conditional Dirichlet solution when it exists; the
failure then concerns physical generation of its boundary data, not that
solution's mathematics. A model that admits both orders without choosing
one is still an admission result, not a derived asymmetry.

## Physical spectrum in the admitted model

- [ ] Derive the charged fermion operator and its domain from the same
      completion, rather than select ordinary, relative or APS cohomology
      by which count is favorable. Establish grading, adjoint and boundary
      conditions; decide whether the problem is Fredholm.
- [ ] Compute all five Standard Model charged sectors, their conjugates,
      gauge and Higgs fields on one background with one hypercharge line.
      Keep global fixed vectors and invariant cusp channels.
- [ ] Check norms, essential spectrum and light-state stability. A finite
      norm is not an isolated particle; a cohomology jump is not a pole mass.
- [ ] Normalize actual interaction integrals and evaluate anomaly matching
      including any boundary, defect or continuum contribution.

No three-generation claim is accepted until these sectors agree in one
vacuum. Three roots, three covers or three chosen copies cannot supply
three generations without a derived common-state construction.

## Decision and remaining mission

A successful bounded result would be an admitted chiral mechanism with
its actual spectrum and an honest input budget. It is stronger than an
index but still not a parameter-free Standard Model or full physical theory.
State selection, electroweak breaking, normalized observable relations,
quantum consistency, gravitational dynamics and empirical tests remain.

If the same-action route has no justified source/end law, report that
specific derivation gap and compare genuinely distinct nonflat, singular
Higgs, nonliftable or interacting proposals by their added inputs and
testable consequences. Do not respond with another unrestricted census
or a universal chirality kill. This is a research priority inferred from
the audited evidence, not proof that it is the only viable door.
