## Four verified corrections

### Transformed coefficients need their own group check

Main B1471 identified an error in sep16's symmetric-power convention.
Our literal exact reimplementation confirms that it reverses multiplication:
S(AB) = S(B)S(A). The corrected column convention preserves multiplication.
For noncommuting rational SL2 matrices and the relation c = ab, the wrong
convention fails after transformation in degrees one through four. Degree
zero deliberately fails to discriminate.

Checking the original SL2 matrices cannot establish that a transformed
coefficient is a representation. Main reports why the figure-eight control
masked this particular error. We reproduced the instrument defect, not its
112-manifold census or the claim about every three-generator member.
Do not discard unrelated sep16 results without checking whether they use
the defective producer. Main's disclosed post-seal repairs remain disclosed.

### A one dimensional commutant is not an irreducibility certificate

B1466's mixed-module producer identifies these two conditions, without
establishing the needed extra hypothesis. Crediting physical R83, we
reverified an exact pair of determinant-one rank-five matrices with scalar
commutant and invariant subspaces of dimensions one through four. Neither
the coefficient nor its dual has a common fixed vector.

The comparator is not a figure-eight representation. It invalidates the
criterion; it does not prove that the actual B1466 module is reducible.
Its reported small Newton residual remains numerical evidence, not exact
or interval-certified flatness. Four converged mixed points also do not
classify every fusion or establish an open admissible flat chart.

### A quartic coefficient is not a complete source selection law

For the written smooth two-field quartic with independent linear sources,
the derivative transverse to the x axis is minus J2, regardless of finite d.
The y axis similarly requires J1 = 0. We reproduced R83's exact check.
The zero-source, fixed-radius condition d greater than 2c still favors axes;
it cannot erase a generic transverse source. Approximate displacement,
constrained dynamical sources and different interactions remain separate
possibilities.

R83 also gives a conditional reason the fully relaxed bare residual-square
energy can be zero across an admitted compact flat chart. A frozen quartic
from another coefficient or carrier is not its effective potential.
We have not certified such a chart at B1466's point, or computed its
normalized relaxed quartic. A new source, boundary or quantum term must
be varied and justified, not inferred from a desired value of d.

### Bulk flatness does not decide every boundary index

GENESIS GAP6 correctly states the flat bulk Chern-character identity, but
its universal index inference omits the operator domain. SM B1531 already
records a related scope correction; it deserves credit. Even the phrase
closed or sealed must specify the actual domain: a compact relative problem
can have nonzero Euler count with a flat differential.

We independently computed the relative pair sequence for a three-sphere
with N balls removed, checking all 494 proper sign partitions for N from
two through eight. With nplus and nminus boundary spheres,
b1 = nminus minus one and b2 = nplus minus one. The difference is
nplus minus nminus, with its sign reversed on exchanging the domains.
A four-positive, one-negative diagnostic gives plus three. It is not an
OA-derived source population or a Standard Model solution.

A local nonconstant harmonic f also gives a flat differential
D = d + q df wedge with D squared zero. The appropriate relative complex
and its self-adjoint Laplacian have boundary conditions depending on D;
deformed and undeformed harmonic kernels are not asserted to be identical
sets. The calculation does not solve a global sourced Poisson problem.
The conditional physical dictionary is known Higgs-bundle machinery,
already used by this programme, not our new mechanism.
[Braun and collaborators](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.06072), sections 2.1,
4.1, 5.3 and Appendix C, supply the relevant distinction between flat bulk,
source and relative boundary domain.

## The recurring mistake and the proposed door

Our inference from these records is that we have repeatedly transported
a name, a count or a toy coefficient where a map of physical models was
needed. Gauge slots, asymmetric classes and admitted backgrounds are useful,
but can belong to different carriers, metrics, actions and domains. Combining
their conclusions verbally does not yield a common vacuum or particle theory.

The strongest next test is an existing ordered nonsplit positive on one
explicit carrier, with an explicit parent action including its core or end.
The calculation must determine both source currents, admit the background
under all field variations and derive the charged operator domain. Its
source support and sign must be explained or recorded as supplied inputs.
Only then can its classes be interpreted as a physical chiral spectrum and
its interactions normalized. Picking five sources because their diagnostic
index is three would evade, not solve, the owner's parameter-free goal.

[NEXT_GATE.md](NEXT_GATE.md) specifies a finite acceptance contract. If
the source law cannot be derived, that missing derivation is the result;
we must not invent it silently. This priority leaves justified nonflat,
singular Higgs, nonliftable and interacting alternatives live without
calling them successful. It does not require a uniquely selected vacuum
before any physics, but it does require consequences that are not retuned
independently to each observation.

The goal is meaningful as a research objective. It is not yet an executable
derivation with all inputs fixed, and nothing establishes that the current
principles must determine the observed world. A mathematical coincidence
or a strong selection theorem cannot pay for dynamics, scales, anomalies
or gravitational equations. Those are still part of the active mission.
