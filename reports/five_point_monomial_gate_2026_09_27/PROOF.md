# Exact certificates used by this gate

The finite subgroup is enumerated from literal permutations; no abstract
group name is inferred from order. Any two distinct commuting involutions
generate {1,a,b,ab}, a Klein-four group. Enumerating these pairs therefore
enumerates all V4 subgroups. Conjugation permutes them. The producer checks
this action on every group element and all generator products, its kernel,
evenness and image size. An injective image of size 60 consisting of even
permutations on five letters is precisely A5.

For (p,v) representing D(t^v)P_p, multiplication is
(p,v)(q,w)=(pq,v+P_p w). Inversion is (p^-1,-P_p^-1 v).
Word exponents are therefore exact linear integer functions of the generator
exponents. Identity permutation and zero word exponent imply a relator for
ALL nonzero complex t. The determinant exponent is sum v; even permutations
then give determinant one. This is stronger than testing a few rational t.

A diagonal conjugation with exponent s sends v_g to v_g+s-P_g s.
Keeping T pure requires s=P_T s. The producer verifies every such gauge
column satisfies all fixed-relator/determinant/longitude constraints.
Nullspace and rank selection compute the rational quotient exactly.

The algebra generated by the five-by-five matrices is built by adjoining
all right-generator products until its vector-space dimension stabilizes.
At most 25 independent matrices can occur. If the result is 25, the complex
algebra is all M5(C), which cannot preserve a proper nonzero subspace.
This certifies absolute irreducibility; a smaller dimension is reported,
not automatically converted into a claimed classification.

Solving G^t Q G=Q for every generator tests invariant complex bilinear forms
by rational linear equations. Nullity zero proves absence of any such form.
The self-dual permutation point is an explicit contrasting control.

The off-diagonal matrix units E_ij (i != j) form a 20-dimensional module:
Ad(D(t^v)P_p)E_ij=t^(v_p(i)-v_p(j)) E_p(i),p(j).
The implementation verifies these actions against actual matrix conjugation.
Diagonal and off-diagonal modules split as representations at a monomial
point. H1 in the latter is transverse to monomial variations modulo gauge,
but tangent vectors need not integrate. All global and cusp cohomology
uses the already sealed exact Fox/chain/restriction engine.

The number of cusps in the associated permutation cover is the number of
orbits of the peripheral subgroup on the five sheets; this is only a
topological covering statement. An invariant dimension of three does not
derive three modes, a chirality index, or a physical generation count.
