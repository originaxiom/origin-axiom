## Honest prior and decision rule

A nontrivial monomial family and an irreducible non-self-dual rational point
seem plausible. Three chiral generations do not: these induced-line-type
families are expected to have zero ordinary indices, and off-monomial
relative directions may be absent. The latter two predictions are tested,
not asserted as theorems or encoded as mandatory passing outcomes.

- No nongauge exponent: close only this fixed-pair rational monomial ansatz.
- Wrong cusp capacity: do not prioritize local continuation for index three.
- Capacity three plus non-self-duality: a better algebraic seed, not chirality.
- Zero indices: preserve family and scope; do not call a physical no-go.
- Zero off-monomial tangent: local first-order statement, not a distant
  component classification. A positive tangent still owes integrability.
- Even joint index three would still owe the parent action, admissible
  harmonic background, physical operator/norm/domain, full spectrum,
  anomalies and an observable.
