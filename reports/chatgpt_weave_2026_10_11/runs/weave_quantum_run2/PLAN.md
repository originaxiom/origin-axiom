# Weave quantum realization — run 2 working plan

Date: 2026-10-10. This plan is a local research log, not an externally timestamped preregistration.
It was written after reading Run 1 and identifying the quaternion cover in the literature,
and before running the new verification code. Exploratory reasoning already informed these tests.

Scope: the faithful eight-sheeted Q8 cover of the punctured torus, its ordinary and relative
cohomology, spin structures, minimal parabolic Dolbeault problems, and exact representation comparison.
No four-dimensional or gravitational claim; no observed particle inputs; no repository edits.

1. Reconstruct Q8 cover from the generators a->i, b->j, using graph chains, cap boundaries.
   Independently derive genus and Q8 isotypic multiplicities.
2. Identify the known quaternion origami and its smooth plane-quartic family; verify differential basis.
3. Compute actual L,R/deck chain action; use ribbon-graph intersection form and enumerate all
   64 compact spin structures. Do not presume an invariant spin or three fermion zero modes.
4. Test an analytic argument that no theta characteristic is invariant under all Q8 deck transformations.
5. Determine minimal rank-two parabolic Dolbeault kernel/cokernel dimensions with weights 1/2,1/2.
   Distinguish boundary-domain choices, one bundle, and the already postulated three labelled copies.
6. Compare natural triplets (boundary, adjoint/bilinear) with the target theta multiplier including scalars.
   A correct projective action is not a full quantum block or determinant construction.
7. Include positive and negative controls and exact calculations; state unperformed infinite-dimensional
   determinant, connection and sewing computations explicitly.

Potential outcomes: a faithful candidate, a representation/phase obstruction, spin-domain nonselection,
or a reduction to known geometry without a physical selection rule. No outcome will be promoted by its dimension alone.
