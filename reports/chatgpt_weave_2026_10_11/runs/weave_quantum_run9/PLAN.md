# Run 9 — endpoint transport, without imposing colour/flavour identification

This plan is written after reading Run 8 and exploratory reasoning, before the formal scripts. It is not a blind or independently timestamped preregistration.

Fixed model: the equal-velocity SO(5)_1 coset boundary condition of Run 8; original Q8 is a colour-gauge subgroup; no copy/flavour background, no colour–flavour locking field, no new determinant prefactor. Source L=T and R=ST^{-1}S^{-1} act on the marked geometry and colour background, not on the new copy labels. Local coordinates and Wilson paths are transported with the surface. Standard conformal-block framing is kept.

Questions:
1. Does gauge compensation induce nontrivial transport on the neutral flavour triplet? Compute the singlet projection and its global-flavour commutant, not only dimensions.
2. Compute torus two-vector endpoint blocks from five free Majoranas. Distinguish three handle/spin channels from three flavour components.
3. Keep a residual central F puncture as well: count channels and construct a nonzero H5-inserted paired endpoint block. Do not quietly replace the central-puncture problem by the pair-only problem.
4. Compare both S and T, with weights and endpoint coordinates retained. Test relative eigenphases; scalar line twists cannot change those.
5. Generalize only the analytic fusion/eigenphase obstruction to ordinary SO(2m+1)_1 primary-puncture spaces. Do not claim a classification of arbitrary spin/Q8-crossed defects, subspaces, or other theories.

Evidence: analytic derivations; exact linear algebra/fusion/phase arithmetic; independent finite Clifford–Fock trace for the odd-spin insertion; numerical theta-series versus oscillator products and kernel mode sums at two precisions; explicit negative controls. Report actual counts and any failures without turning check counts into confidence scores.

Success would require source T on a physically defined triplet or complete rank-three block without assigning source matrices to new labels. A mismatch rejects this dictionary, not the consistency of the known coset theory or all possible completions.
