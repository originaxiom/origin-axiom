# Run 6 working plan

Written after reading Run 5 and the source manuscript, primary-source checks, and exploratory exact cohomology calculations. This is NOT blind preregistration or an independently timestamped prediction.

## Fixed question
Can the manuscript's actual three-dimensional lifted cohomology representation be mapped to a protected operator/defect sector of the already specified interacting spin-CFT family?

## Tasks
1. Rebuild H^1(F2; chi rho_Q) from crossed homomorphisms modulo coboundaries, for all four parity characters. Compose the specified quaternion normalizer lifts; retain contravariance. Reconstruct the six-dimensional nontrivial-parity direct sum. Separate the two conjugate three-dimensional subrepresentations and compare both modular generators exactly with the manuscript. Do not infer an independent Hodge-Riemann signature calculation from the match.
2. Identify the scalar character from the untwisted two-dimensional cohomology, not by adding an eta factor. Construct the exact line-valued-current intertwiner. Distinguish that geometric coefficient line from a physically realized quantum sector.
3. Determine the complete intersection of local holomorphic operator spaces across an open interval of the Run 5 charge deformation, not just conformal weight one. Compute a graded character as a check. Test whether the internal normalizer action can contain the full target.
4. Test invariant current OPEs, including the vacuum coefficient, before allowing a scalar twist of local currents. Calculate neutral endomorphism transport separately.
5. Examine the ordinary su(2)_1 spinor-module alternative. Verify its pointed fusion/associator/braiding and ordinary conformal-block rank, with no claim to have calculated Q8-crossed puncture blocks.
6. Run independent numerical theta/character/connection controls at 60 and 90 digits. No prior run's verification code is imported.

## Outcomes and limits
A representation-level tensor factorization is not a quantum determinant derivation. A local-operator obstruction is not an obstruction to conformal-block transport, charged matter modules, or additional fields. Primary WZW blocks do not exhaust nonabelian Q8 defects. Actual singular-operator Quillen transport, defect junctions, 4d chirality, gravity, and empirical parameters are not calculated here.
