# Run 5 working plan — deformation versus faithful puncture

Date: 2026-10-10. Written after reading Run 4, primary-source scouting, and analytic exploration, before the formal verification scripts. Not a blind preregistration or an externally timestamped prediction.

## Input and boundary
Continue the one-parameter I_3 Ibar_3 deformation identified in Run 4. Retain two complex fermions (four real per chirality), a common spin structure, the Q8 action on the flavour doublet, and the original equal-sign half-charge puncture. No observed physical inputs. No changes to previous reports or repository.

## Intended calculations
1. Construct the finite current-current family as an O(1,1) boost of the charge sector, with the flavour sector unchanged. Keep the original odd charge lattice and its parity gluing; do not replace the fermionic theory by an unconstrained bosonic tensor product.
2. Transport the fermion, original half-charge puncture, alternative Ramond spinors, and fused field. Compute weights and operator-product exponents exactly. Distinguish chiral defect components from full nonchiral endpoints.
3. Prove or reject rigidity of ANY local NS holomorphic weight-1/2 fermion in the family, not only the named field. Finite scans are checks, not a proof.
4. Evaluate all four torus spin-sector partition functions using a parity-resummed charge lattice. Verify the free theta limit and S/T covariance, retain a direct four-dimensional lattice implementation as an independent comparison.
5. Test whether the free odd-spin zero persists. Derive the leading response analytically and check it independently.
6. Establish only the scope supported for Q8 defects: invariance of the perturbation and preservation of symmetry lines do not amount to transport of the exact chiral E_+ state or evaluation of every nonabelian junction.

## Possible outcomes
- Holonomy/defect symmetry survives continuously, but exact chiral E_+ data select t=0.
- A different radius produces replacement holomorphic fermions.
- Spin/gluing or modular checks invalidate the proposed continuation.
- Insufficient control of nonabelian sewing; state it without treating local checks as all-genus calculations.

## Deliverables
A technical report, executable exact and numerical tests, machine-readable results and terminal logs. No PDF or repository mutation. No gravitational or Standard Model claim. Numerical truncation checks are not interval proofs unless a certified bound is explicitly implemented.
