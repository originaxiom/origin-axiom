# Post-cover extension: parity-twist conjugacy

Formulated after the cover check, before the script below. This is an analytically predicted verification, not a blind discovery.

For rho_Q(A)=i, rho_Q(B)=j, each character-twist chi*rho_Q is conjugate to rho_Q: conjugate by i, j, or ij to change the three sign pairs. Check this in an independent complex 2x2 representation. Consequence under explicitly stated assumptions: covariantly defined free Dirac operators on these bundles are unitarily conjugate, hence gauge-invariant vacuum determinant magnitudes are equal. They cannot directly be the three theta/eta blocks in the fixed parity-labelled basis, whose norm ratios vary with tau.

Scope exclusions: does not rule out cohomology triplets, operator insertions, puncture-state amplitudes, non-equivariant boundary prescriptions, or a larger theory. No full punctured determinant is numerically computed.
