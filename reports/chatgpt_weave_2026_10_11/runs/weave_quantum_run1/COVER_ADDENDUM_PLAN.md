This extension was formulated after the main 34-check run. It is not part of the initial local PLAN hash. Expected counts below were derived analytically before this new script ran.

The faithful Q8 monodromy defines an eight-sheeted cover of the once-punctured torus. Its peripheral permutation should have four 2-cycles. Filling the four lifted punctures produces a genus-three branched cover of the compact torus. Check the count and verify that the two grammar automorphisms preserve the Q8 quotient, hence its kernel.

This constructs a geometric domain for the NEXT twisted-fermion calculation. It does not calculate a determinant, Pfaffian, spin structure, chiral index, quantum amplitude, or three generations. No extra physical sectors are introduced by this covering-space construction.
