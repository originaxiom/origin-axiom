# Run 10 plan — a dynamical projective record field

Written after reading Run 9 and exploratory mathematical reasoning and literature search, before the verification scripts below. Not a blind or externally timestamped preregistration.

Source gap: Run 9 excludes a parallel colour–flavour frame with trivial flavour holonomy and leaves a spatially varying reference field as a possible new hypothesis.

1. Test a stronger topological obstruction to a smooth everywhere-invertible REAL adjoint frame on the compact smooth adjoint extension. Calculate w1 and w2 from its three sign lines. Distinguish the punctured surface and complex frames.
2. Instead of pretending a single direction is a full frame, declare a weaker model: CP1/O(3) sigma field in the original FIXED projective quaternion background. SU(2) record orientation is a background global-symmetry datum, not a new gauge field integrated over. The local U(1) homogeneous-coordinate redundancy is separate.
3. Test the explicit holomorphic projective field w=(theta3(z|2tau),theta2(z|2tau)). Calculate monodromies, equation of motion, topological charge, action and BPS stability. Relate it to the Run 3 profile only projectively, not as an equality of quantum theories.
4. Calculate bosonic tangent zero modes from the deformation operator and topology; test translation modes numerically. Track modular S,T and their center in the actual projective field.
5. Independently integrate the charge and energy and calculate the charge from spherical triangles with the twisted boundary gluing. Compare mesh resolutions. Use no target-dependent eta factor.
6. Determine what this does NOT select: coupling, modulus, location, handedness, full metaplectic phase and physical fermions. A half-charge response to a theta-angle shift is not the earlier metaplectic anomaly.
7. Optional next-dimension control: distinguish known twisted 4d Yang–Mills constructions from an unperformed lift of this exact CP1 solution. Do not claim an uplift from a shared flux alone.

Known prior art: Belavin–Polyakov energy bound; Hayashi et al. arXiv:2507.12802, explicit twisted CP(N-1) torus lumps and their moduli. No novelty assertion. Our task is an independent specialization and an explicit comparison to the supplied record geometry.
