Chat-only wording, unsaved console output and historical temporary files not included in an attachment or package cannot be certified as captured. No missing original is fabricated from memory. Hidden internal reasoning is not included.

ZIPs are the authoritative record of what each run exported. Byte integrity does not establish scientific correctness, complete historical source coverage or a fresh execution of the scientific tests. All original evidence grades remain unchanged.
