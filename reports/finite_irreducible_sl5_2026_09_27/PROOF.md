# What the finite seed search establishes

Let a permutation p act on Q6 by e_i -> e_p(i). On the invariant
augmentation subspace sum x_i=0, use f_i=e_i-e_5, i=0,...,4. The matrix
entry is delta_(i,p(j))-delta_(i,p(5)). This is a representation because
it is a restriction of the literal permutation action. Its determinant
is sign(p); thus even permutations give SL5 matrices. The standard
Euclidean metric restricts to Gram matrix Q=I5+ones, positive definite
(eigenvalues 1,1,1,1,6), and G^T Q G=Q. This supplies an explicit
self-duality, not only a numerical character coincidence.

For a finite transitive group on six letters, endomorphisms commuting
with its permutation representation have dimension equal to its number
of orbits on ordered pairs. Double transitivity gives two such orbits.
The permutation module is the direct sum of one trivial line and the
augmentation module, with no additional invariant line. By complete
reducibility of finite complex representations and the sum-of-squares
formula for the commutant dimension, the augmentation module is
irreducible. We verify the group/action by full finite closure; no group
name is inferred from a few element orders. This does not make a finite
group into a selected physical symmetry.

Conjugating (T,B,C) by an element commuting with T preserves the fixed
marked-meridian enumeration. The centralizer in S6, not only in A6, is
used to remove those obvious equivalent permutation triples. This
enumeration exhausts exactly the declared A6-pair ansatz; it is not a
completeness theorem for arbitrary SL5 representations. The index-two
base-group extension is never assumed: the M2 relators are solved directly.

The invariant Gram form implies E and its exterior square are self-dual,
so their ordinary interior indices are zero on every cover. These are
forced-zero controls. Their full cohomological dimensions and boundary
maps can nevertheless inform a deformation search. Finite-image unitary
holonomy admits a parallel positive bundle metric; choosing Phi=0 is a
flat-bundle control, not a derivation of spacetime dynamics or matter.

At this orthogonal point sl5 splits as so(Q) plus p(Q), where

    p(Q) = { X : X^T Q = Q X, tr X=0 }, dimension 14.

Conjugation by every G preserving Q preserves these conditions. The
producer solves the rational linear equations for a basis and derives
the 14x14 action using selected independent rows, then verifies the
full action without projection error. H1(M2;p(Q)) is the first-order
space transverse to orthogonal deformations modulo gauge. Its kernel
under restriction to the meridian circle is the corresponding
meridian-relative first-order space. It need NOT be integrable, and it
does not guarantee a nonzero matter index or an admissible physical mode.

If the E cusp invariant bound on M6 is below three at the seed, a
nonzero matrix minor remains nonzero in a neighborhood, so that fixed
space cannot increase to three sufficiently nearby. For irreducible
nearby E with balanced H0, this prevents an ordinary interior index
three in that neighborhood. This is an upper-semicontinuity argument
about this specific route, not an exclusion of all physical indices.

All representation equations, ranks and quotient maps used here are
exact. A first-order positive would require a subsequent nonlinear
integrability test, followed by the joint E/wedge-square target and the
action/domain/normalizable-spectrum obligations. Mathematical existence
of a finite-image seed is not physical success.
