## What changes about the route to physics

The earlier partial gauge screen's failure was not a universal obstruction:
a declared alternative closes algebraically without removing every
interaction. Conversely, closure alone does not preserve the wanted
spectrum. Both facts must be carried forward.

The next construction must transport the background, action, gauge
transformations and physical fermion domain together. A cohomological
subalgebra has not supplied a smooth, real, supersymmetric or Fredholm
boundary problem. Ghost and antifield degrees cannot simply be renamed
physical fermion degrees. A lift must preserve the relevant gauge quotient
and higher operations, not only dimensions.

Our priority remains one justified source or end law that admits the
nonsplit background AND determines a compatible charged operator and
consistent interactions. This is a research hypothesis, not proof that
it is the only door. The compact closed kinetic model is a useful
comparator; its admission cannot be combined verbally with this different
model's charged spectrum.

The parent, action, metric, polarization and complements remain supplied
here. Their algebraic consistency does not derive them from OA. Even a
successful conditional chiral model would leave parameter-free observable
relations, quantum consistency, gravity and empirical tests to establish.
