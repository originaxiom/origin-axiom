## Integration verdict

Asymmetric classes, conditional admission, a full-parent detector and
authored compact kinetics are useful positives. They do not yet concern
one common model with the same carrier, domain and supplied inputs.
The next step is an exhibited physical map or one common construction,
not another numerical coincidence. The new boundary result advances
algebraic compatibility while exposing its spectrum cost. None of this
establishes that the framework must explain reality, or rules it out.
