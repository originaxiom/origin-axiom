# Why these minors delimit possible first-order exits

With T fixed, an infinitesimal left cocycle is (0,z_B,z_C). Both relator
equations say J z=0. Infinitesimal conjugation preserving T comes from
s in ker(rho(T)-I); its image is G s. The Fox identity gives JG=0.
Hence the fixed-meridian tangent modulo conjugation has dimension

    40 - rank(J) - rank(G).

Here ker(rho(T)-I) has constant dimension eight because T is the same
pure order-three permutation for all t. The rank of G can drop where
global invariants appear; ignoring that would give false negatives.
This quotient is the kernel of the map H1(M2;V) -> H1(mu;V): a class
restricting trivially on mu can be gauged to z_T=0, and residual gauge
is exactly the indicated fixed space.

At t=2 the actual ranks are 32 and 8. Choose a nonzero 32-minor of J
and an 8-minor of G there, and compute them as Laurent polynomials.
Where both minors are nonzero, rank(J)>=32 and rank(G)>=8.
Since rank(G)<=8 and JG=0 in a 40-dimensional middle space, these
ranks must be exactly 32 and 8. Thus the relative tangent vanishes
outside the union of their roots. Clearing row powers of t introduces
only the excluded point t=0. The roots are a SUPERSET until all ranks
are recomputed there; different minors may remove spurious roots.

For an irreducible rational factor f of degree d, its companion matrix
represents multiplication by t in K=Q[t]/(f). All representation,
cochain, boundary and bilinear equations are K-linear. Restricting
scalars to Q multiplies their dimensions/ranks by d. The producer
checks divisibility before normalizing. The rational transpose-inverse
dual is isomorphic to the K-dual through the nondegenerate field trace
pairing, so this dual computation has the correct normalized ranks.

To test the generated complex matrix algebra, each new word is expanded
also by the d scalar basis multipliers. A rational span of dimension 25d
is all M5(K), hence all M5(C) after any embedding: absolute irreducibility.
For bilinear forms, equations G^t Q G=Q are solved over K using the
literal monomial action on all twenty-five coordinates. Nullity zero
certifies non-self-duality. A positive nullity at a reducible point does
not by itself certify a nondegenerate invariant form.

The exterior square is constructed with the actual permutation signs
and product monomials on wedge basis vectors, not as the exterior
square of the larger restriction-of-scalars rational representation.

No statement here integrates a tangent or identifies cohomology with a
physical fermion. A complete classification is only for this prescribed
first-order question on these two families when every candidate factor
is analysed.
