# Invariant boundary channels and exterior dimensions

This is an explicitly post-native follow-up to the tensor screen, not a
blinded prediction. The first native capture is sealed as input. Its chosen
complements fail closure, but that by itself says nothing about all other
complements. This addendum tests a basis-independent necessary condition
over the same four marked silver coefficients and their split controls.
It neither enlarges the population nor closes the physical chirality route.

## The conditional argument

Suppose the boundary coefficient splits as E=V plus a trivial line, with
dim V=4 and det V trivial. Write epsilon and epsilon dual for its invariant
last basis vector and covector. The already tested volume tensors define

    J:  F -> F dual,     J(a)(b)=vol(epsilon wedge a wedge b),
    Jd: F dual -> F,     the corresponding dual-volume tensor.

In the fixed exterior basis their composites are the projector onto
Lambda2(V); their kernel is V wedge epsilon, or its dual. This follows
by evaluating on the six pairs with indices less than four: complementary
pairs are exchanged with the four-dimensional volume sign, twice giving
sign +1. Every pair containing index four is killed. Equivariance makes
these chain maps on torus cochains.

If H*(T;V)=0, the kernel contributes no cohomology. Therefore J and Jd
induce inverse isomorphisms on H1(T;F) and H1(T;F dual). If a graded trace
algebra admits both invariant degree-zero channels and is closed under
these two products, its subspaces must obey

    J L_F subset L_Fdual,       Jd L_Fdual subset L_F.

The two injections force equal dimensions. If the spaces are also full
cup annihilators, each has half the common H1 dimension. More generally,
for any induced map Jd with kernel dimension k, the escape rank from a
source subspace of dimension d into a target of dimension e is at least
max(0,d-k-e). This is the rank-nullity theorem, independent of a metric
complement. It is only a necessary condition, not a construction of a
closed trace algebra or a physical elliptic domain.

## Planned computation and controls

Before first execution of this follow-up, seal this argument, its script,
tests, and inputs. Reconstruct the actual peripheral matrices, check the
literal splitting and invariant epsilon and epsilon dual, and compute
V and F torus cohomology directly. Check tensor equivariance, chain maps,
their induced ranks and both composition defects modulo exacts. Use the
first native capture only for the previously computed L dimensions and
compare its H0 and H1 dimensions against this reconstruction. Recompute
global F and F dual H0 directly from their generator matrices.

The honest expectation, informed by the first native run, is V Betti
(0,0,0) for both m136 characters, versus (1,2,1) for both m135 characters.
The expected inverse isomorphisms in the first pair would make nonsplit
L dimensions 1 and 3 impossible in this trace-algebra category, whatever
complement is chosen. With global H0 zero on both sides and boundary H0
two, the B1509 paired degree-one formula dim L_F minus two would then be
zero for an admissible equal-dimensional pair. This is a conditional
count, not a computed physical spectrum. The formula was independently
reproduced in the preceding global-complement packet.

In the second pair, an expected kernel of dimension two gives bound
max(0,4-2-2)=0. No all-subspaces obstruction follows there. Do not promote
the chosen-complement failure into that stronger claim.

Controls: identity maps on dimension four allow a common dimension-two
space, but the dimension-one versus dimension-three nested pair has a
reverse escape of rank two. Conversely the rank-four projector on a
six-dimensional space allows dimensions two and four in both directions
when the extra two dimensions are its kernel. This opposite control
guards precisely against extending the acyclic argument to the second
pair. Check the exterior projector identity at coefficient level as well.

## Physical scope and next decision

The assumptions A0=full boundary H0, A1=L, A2=0, no extra fields and
tensor-closed cohomology traces are declared choices, not consequences
of the OA principles. Restricting degree-zero channels, adding end fields,
or using nonlinear boundary relations changes this test. Such a change
must be derived from an action and followed through the full spectrum;
it cannot be made just to save a preferred count. A failure here does not
retract the established linear interior positive or rule out other ends,
coefficients, rows or physical realizations.
