## Problems that should not guide the programme

Main's current GENESIS GAP6 says no index built on a flat bundle can
distinguish 27 from 27-bar, and says no curved/singular frame is on the record.
Those sentences are too broad. This fork's exact nonsplit class counts
distinguish bundles with the same rank and flat Chern character; they are
not closed Dirac indices. The SM seat has already supplied the scope

its limitations. This is a pending propagation issue on main, not a newly
discovered missing idea or permission to identify a class count with
physical chirality. Curvature alone does not settle the physical map.

chirality. This is a defect of the proposed diagnostic, not a refutation
of spin-dependent information.

## The integration target

My research inference is that the next most informative door is a common
physical boundary or defect completion, with one action determining both
background admission and the charged operator. It is not established as
the only possible route. The [new exact test](FINDINGS.md) makes the issue
concrete: the same pure cusp rule keeps W's difference but cancels its
exterior-square partner. The desired interior pair remains mathematical
data, not automatically the spectrum of that completion.

There is a sharper discriminator. The split and nonsplit exterior-square
systems have the same peripheral data and global invariant difference.
B1509 Proposition E therefore makes a paired cohomological end rule based
only on those peripheral data insensitive to their distinction. The proof
and its limits are in FINDINGS. A completion meant to realize the
extension-dependent count must carry additional relevant information, such
as a response determined by the global solution, actual end fields, or an
analytic domain not captured by this finite rule. Those are hypotheses to
derive and test, not inputs silently granted.

The constructive sequence is:

1. Keep one silver candidate fixed and enumerate every supplied input.
2. Derive an end or interface functional from an identified OA construction,
   or label it a supplied conditional model if that map is not earned. Do
   not choose its sign or coefficient from the desired count.
3. Check the full variation, background and end-field equations, not only
   the necessary integrated flux balance. Compare split and both orders.
4. Derive the charged operator/domain from the same model; include full
   degrees, duals, end states and continuum. Then test all SM sectors,
   interactions, anomalies and stability together.
5. Use spin data only after exhibiting their action on that same model.
   Use the cover test only when an actual outcome is published.

## Verdict toward the goal

physical constructions. The reviewed pieces do not yet constitute one
parameter-free physical theory. Their apparent fit motivates research; it
does not establish that a final breakthrough is guaranteed or close.
The efficient next step is a compatibility calculation with explicit
success/failure conditions, not accumulating another favorable integer.

Success at the next gate would mean one admitted completion with an actual
charged spectrum and honest inputs. It would still leave three families,
state selection, normalized observable relations, quantum consistency,
gravity and empirical tests as separate duties. No foreign branch or
shared numbering was modified. The write-page discipline separates source
