## Conventions and calculation

Generators are ordered (a,b,t), uppercase means inverse, products act on
column vectors on the left. A cocycle satisfies c(gh)=c(g)+rho(g)c(h).
The four is the stated Hermitian-matrix action divided by positive |det g|.
The character multiplies each four-vector generator by the supplied sign.
For x=a+b sqrt(2), restriction of scalars is [[a,2b],[b,a]], with adjacent
coefficient pairs per vector entry. Ranks over Q must be even. The K-dual
is the inverse transpose over K, implemented using the trace Gram matrix
diag(2,4) per coordinate; a bare rational inverse transpose is not silently
identified with that dual.

Derive the two relators and corrected peripheral word from free L/R maps.
Validate representation relators, commuting cusp words, determinant one,
the four's Lorentz form, Fox chain identities and dual involutivity. Use the
mapping cone to compute relative H1, and cross-check it with the exact pair
sequence. Recover one nonzero interior cocycle from the cone, gauge its
two peripheral evaluations to literal zero, and test non-exactness. If the
interior space is one-dimensional, all nonzero classes give isomorphic
extensions; this is proved in PROOF.md rather than inferred from a sample.

Construct W=(V,c;0,1), its exterior square over K and actual duals. Compute
the same objects with c=0. Report all H0 and peripheral invariant dimensions;
do not discard global or boundary fixed channels. Degree one differences
are dimensions E minus E dual, not a physical anomaly or index assertion.

do not, record the discrepancy without changing inputs to recover it. Either
way compute the other domains. Equality across domains would show this
particular count insensitive to those choices; disagreement would make a
domain-selection derivation necessary for this interpretation. Neither
outcome proves or disproves the theory.
