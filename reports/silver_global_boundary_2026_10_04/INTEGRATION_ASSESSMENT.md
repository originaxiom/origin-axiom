## The pattern behind repeated apparent near successes

The reviewed results pay different obligations:

1. The algebraic calculation supplies a candidate asymmetric class space.
2. A compact-boundary theorem admits a background with chosen boundary data.
3. A global complement recovers the desired interior count.
4. A smooth closed completion may improve admission and spectral control,
   but under its own hypotheses returns a balanced charged count.

These statements are compatible. They do not automatically combine into
one physical model. The missing step in THIS proposed assembly is the
derived map from one action and its end or interface equations to the
charged domain. It is not another count to substitute from a different
carrier. The cited packets explicitly leave this duty open; this is not
an assertion that every possible construction in the repository was absent
under every name.

The new exact table makes this diagnosis testable: on the same silver
coefficient, changing only the cohomological prescription changes the
candidate charged differences. Peripheral matrices alone cannot see the
split/nonsplit exterior distinction in this finite screen. Global data can,
but a physically justified global response must be constructed rather than
chosen to match the desired answer. These are constraints on the next model,
not a universal prohibition on local field theories.

## The proposed door and its acceptance test

Freeze one recorded silver candidate, while retaining its dual, split
comparator and the other character as controls. Do not repeat the SM
seat's ongoing cover census. The next model must declare its carrier,
spacetime, parent, metric, fields, action, couplings and end data, with
each marked derived or supplied.

The constructive possibility is an end or partner whose equations generate
the required response. Eliminating a solved region can in principle yield
a boundary response depending on global data. That is a proposed strategy,
not evidence that OA already chooses that region, map or action. Smooth
closed gluing alone must face its known balanced-count result. Any retained
defect, boundary fields or alternate charged grading needs an explicit
derivation, not a name assigned after the count.

The next bounded success criterion is one model in which:

- the background, source or end fields, and every boundary variation are
  stationary under the same action;
- the charged operator and admissible boundary data follow from that action,
  including the linear dual and exterior sector rather than sectorwise choices;
- the split limit is tracked with the full modes, so any projected rank
  change has an explained spectral or domain mechanism;
- candidate charged states have controlled norms and continuum coupling,
  with all relevant anomaly and interaction contributions retained.

If this succeeds it would connect an actual background to an actual
spectrum. It would still not supply three families, physical gauge breaking,
normalized observed parameters, quantum consistency or gravity by itself.
If it fails, record the failed equation or hypothesis and preserve the
mathematical positive. Do not substitute a favorable domain or declare every
physical completion impossible.
