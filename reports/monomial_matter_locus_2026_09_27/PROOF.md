# From finite minors to a complete family decision

Let the coefficient dimension be five. The verified M6 presentation has
seven generators. For any representation, the relative cochain cone has

    C_rel^0 = C_M^0,
    C_rel^1 = C_M^1 + C_boundary^0,

with d_rel^0=(d_M^0, I). Its rank is always five. The next differential is

    [ d_M^1      0      ]
    [ res^1   -d_bdy^0  ].

Thus h1_relative=40-rank(d_rel^1)-5=35-rank(d_rel^1).
The exact sequence of the pair also gives

    h1_relative = n + t0 - a0,

where n is the dimension of the image of relative H1 in ordinary H1,
equivalently the kernel of ordinary H1 restriction to the boundary.

In the declared families the actual M6 peripheral pair is (I,T) or
(I,T^-1), with T a three-cycle and two fixed coordinates, for EVERY
nonzero parameter. Therefore t0=3 for both E and E*. A nonzero five-minor
of global d0 proves a0=0. A nonzero thirty-two-minor of d_rel^1 gives
h1_relative<=3. Nonnegativity of n then forces h1_relative=3 and n=0.
This needs no unjustified assertion that a rank at one sample is generic
everywhere; the explicit polynomial minors specify where the bound holds.

Clearing row powers of t adds only roots at the excluded t=0.
Factor roots of all four relevant minors per family contain EVERY point
where this particular proof of n(E)=n(E*)=0 could fail. They need not
be actual cohomology jumps. At every candidate root, exact specialization
and the earlier ordinary/cochain engine compute the result anew.

For each irreducible factor f over Q, companion arithmetic realizes
Q[t]/f. All ranks normalized by degree(f) hold under every complex
embedding of that field, so one exact calculation covers all its roots.
A degree cap is explicit and never licenses discarding an unanalysed
factor. If all factors are processed, combining them with the open-set
minor certificate gives a result for ALL nonzero complex t in this
particular family.

For a signed monomial generator M=D(t^v)P, M^-transpose=D(t^-v)P with
the same row signs (which are +/-1). Thus the dual is constructed as an
actual dual representation, not a separately chosen parameter system.
The producer compares this with inverse transpose at the control point.

Even a complete I(E)=0 result would only rule out the declared common
ordinary-index target within these families. It would neither retract
the existing nonsplit positives nor prove absence of physical chirality.
The family parameter, parent, metric, source/end law and physical operator
are still different kinds of data.
