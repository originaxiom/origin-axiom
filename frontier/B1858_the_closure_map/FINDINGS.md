## 1. The answer first

- **What is there.** On the spine from the principle to the goal the map holds 155 claims, premises and choices. Of
  them 12 are proved on main, 9 are banked negatives, and 17 are proved given named premises.
- **What is conditional.** Everything that reads as physics. No result of the programme is upstream-clean:
  - the root rests on three postulates and one choice;
  - the structural theorem rests on those, on five typed external data and on thirteen identifications;
  - the chiral three is an index only given two tagged postulates and one open fork;
  - the six mass ratios are a reading on 13 borrowed joints, and nothing is predicted while the targets are held;
  - the modulus under the read-out rule rests on four choices and is one seat's estimate past the second decimal;
  - the lanes' three coefficients rest on seven inputs their own ledger prices as supplied or unselected.
- **Parameter-free?** Not yet, and the map says by how much: 0 of 19 earned; 11 postulates, 41 choices, 2 tagged
  postulates, 2 fitted inputs, 1 ansatz and 6 unstated commitments on the spine; 36 open items.
- **Bugs.** The register carries 78 open debts, 18 of them false or stale statements in foundation or front documents.
  Two were found and fixed in this session (P-751, P-604). One front-page claim is known false and uncorrected (the
  golden grammar's uniqueness, P-318).
- **One finding the map makes by putting the lanes side by side.** Their open items are not many things. They are ten,
  each under several names (§ 3). And every branch of the programme meets the same one, the hand (§ 4).

## 4. Every branch meets the hand

Sixteen nodes are the one question of what makes a count chiral:

- **The chiral three** exists only as a conditional index, given Λ and the cusp's unit, on the double-tick weave.
- **The root** is amphichiral by a choice (SE2), and eight walls pass through that.
- **The object's own 2+1 matter** is vector-like by theorem (B1089).
- **The lanes' model**, by its own claim graph, has a kernel that splits equally between conjugate representations and

- **So the lanes' newest result is the programme's oldest wall, reached from the other side.** That is a convergence,
  not a refutation of either: a model built from a supplied parent on the same curve meets the same bit.

## 5. The one next unowned derivation

**The register-to-field join: the lanes' three against the weave's triplet.**
- **The two sides.** The lanes count three coefficient modes per representation on the punctured elliptic curve, by
  their account as a split of two and one. Main's weave has a triplet at the quaternion point, on which the moves act
  through a group of order 96.
- **The computation.** How the moves act on the lanes' three modes as the modulus varies, decomposed against the
  weave's triplet.
- **Three outcomes, stated before computing:** it is the triplet; it is two plus one under the moves; or the moves do
  not act on it, and the model has selected a thread.
- **Why this one.** It is the one place where main's banked mathematics and the lanes' model can be joined by a
  computation. It is data-free. It decides whether the lanes' count may be called a family count. Only in the first
  outcome does the weave's conditional chirality reach the lanes' operator.
- **Owner.** Unowned: the lanes list it as a missing join. Proposed for main. The agy lane's current ticket is not
  duplicated.

## 6. What this map is not

- **Not a census of the record.** Main has about 1,600 arcs; the map cites 75 of them and 118 register rows. It is the
  dependency spine, read from the foundation and front documents. The verdict ledger and the Ledger Desk hold the
  census.
- **Not a review of the lanes' mathematics.** Their nodes carry the grade their own input ledger and claim graph give.
  The run tickets and review receipts issued after that ledger are not reconciled node by node (P-750).
- **Not complete on the lanes' side.** The physical lane's hand proofs since 2026-11-05 enter only through its summary
  and the ledger's twelve entries (P-749).
- **Not verified beyond its pins.** A pin shows that the record says a thing, not that the thing is true. The grades
  are the record's.
- **Not accepted.** Main does not accept its own map. The physical lane is asked to review it as non-author.
