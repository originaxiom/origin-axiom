# B1983 — listening card 1 — the moves acting together on the fibre's characters over every finite field: where does the whole leave structure?

Written 2026-11-25, before any run, at the author's word: listen to the interactions' natural unfolding, choose
nothing, and do not sculpt the Standard Model. Data-free. 0 of 19.

## The system (nothing chosen)

- **The seed** (GENESIS §§ 1–2):
  - two records a and b;
  - the grammar's moves L and R, acting on the fibre's characters (x, y, z) = (tr a, tr b, tr ab) as the Fricke maps
    L: (x, y, z) ↦ (x, z, xz − y) and R: (x, y, z) ↦ (z, y, yz − x) (B1600, B1601);
  - the frustration κ = x² + y² + z² − xyz − 2, conserved by every move (B497's stratum 1);
  - κ = 2 is "nothing" (B309);
  - κ = −2 is the cusp's leaf, the parabolic Markov surface, with the common point (0, 0, 0) (W2).
- **The levels.** The characters are read modulo every prime p ≤ 47: all of them, each leaf κ ∈ 𝔽_p, none chosen.
- **The unfolding.** Everything allowed happens: the whole group generated by L and R acts. Its orbits are what the
  whole cannot tell apart, and its fixed points are what it forces.
- **The open moves, carried both ways.** The swap P (GM5c, open) is a second run with P: (x, y, z) ↦ (y, x, z) added.
  Inverses act within the same orbits, since the maps are bijections of the finite set, so they need no separate run.

## What is watched (described, not targeted)

- **For each p and each κ:**
  - the number of points;
  - the orbits and their sizes;
  - the points every move fixes;
  - the leaves where the whole leaves more than one orbit apart from its fixed points.
- **No pass/fail cell is shaped like the Standard Model.** The output is a table of where the whole leaves structure.

## Controls

- **C1:** on every leaf of every p, the run reproduces the point count. The counts are counted twice, and each orbit is
  checked to be invariant under L, R and their inverses.
- **C2:** on κ = −2 the origin is fixed by every move at every p (W2).
- **C3, the literature.** Bourgain, Gamburd and Sarnak (2016) and W. Chen (2024), cited from memory and to be checked at
  source before anything is banked: on the Markov surface mod p, the nonzero points form one orbit under the moves for
  all primes outside a small exceptional set, and Chen's result covers every large p. A run that splits them for most
  p > 3 means a broken instrument.

## What it means

- **Behaviour only.** Where the whole connects everything, it carries no structure at that level and leaf. Where it
  leaves several orbits or forced points, it does. A level or leaf the whole singles out is a candidate for what the
  record has been choosing by hand: the level (P-845), and frustration levels other than the cusp's.
- **Nothing is compared with data.**
