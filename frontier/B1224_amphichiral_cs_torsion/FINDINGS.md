## What happened

B1222 proposed that the programme's ~65 proved vanishings share one cause — a **symmetry** of the
object — and named three kill conditions. The decisive test was run as *symmetry-group order versus
H₁ torsion*, returned **r = +0.50** against a predicted negative, and the thesis was banked NEGATIVE.

**That test was invalid, and the invalidity is mine.** The thesis is about **physical** vanishing —
Chern–Simons, modular flow, values. **H₁ torsion is not a quantity the thesis mentions.** Refuting a
claim on an observable it never made is not a refutation; it is a category error wearing a
correlation coefficient.

## And the sharper statement, which is the actual finding

The 3-of-6 is not noise, and it is not a partial result. Amphichirality gives CS ≡ −CS, so
**2·CS ≡ 0** — CS is **2-torsion**, not zero. In SnapPy's normalisation (CS defined mod ½) the
2-torsion elements are exactly $\{0, \tfrac14\}$, and that is precisely what the census returns:

| manifold | CS |
|---|---|
| **m004** | **0** |
| m136 | 0 |
| m206 | 0 |
| **m003** | **1/4** |
| m135 | 1/4 |
| m207 | 1/4 |

**Six of six, exact, no exceptions, two values only.**

> **So the banked statement "CS = 0, forced by amphichirality" is the special case.** The theorem is
> **CS ∈ {0, ¼}**, and m004 happens to sit at 0. B303's conclusion is right for our object and its
> stated *reason* is incomplete — amphichirality alone forces 2-torsion, not vanishing.

## And it separates the sisters

The paper says m003 and m004 share field, volume and amphichirality, and that *"what separates them

## What this says about our criteria — the owner's actual question

**Yes, a gate killed a positive, and the failure mode is nameable:** a kill condition stated in
advance is only as good as its *observable*. B1222's falsifier was pre-registered, run honestly,
and reported faithfully — and it was still wrong, because the observable did not belong to the
claim. **Pre-registration protects against moving the goalposts; it does not protect against
choosing the wrong goal.**

The corrective is not to weaken the gates. It is one added question at design time: **"is this
quantity one the hypothesis actually predicts?"** — asked *before* the test is run, since afterwards
it is indistinguishable from special pleading.

**B1222's NEGATIVE is superseded**, not deleted: its two other tests (7/10 and 6/10 with real
counterexamples — the regulator is Dirichlet, ℓ and σ are missing data) stand, so the thesis is
**partially supported and not established**. What is retracted is the claim that the decisive test
refuted it. It did not test it.
