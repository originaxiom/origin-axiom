## The target

B821 decomposed the blind set and found **exactly one genuine lexicon gap**: `B537`, whose subject
is the **Markov-type surface** `x² + y² + z² − xyz = c` and **SL(2,ℤ) trace triples**
`(tr A, tr B, tr AB)` — a real object topic that none of the 18 motifs covers. It is the sole
`GAP` row in `docs/atlas/BLIND_ARCS.md`.

## What will be added

**One motif** for the character-variety / trace-triple surface: `conserved="structural"` (an
invariant of a transform, not our method and not a first integral), `domain="topology"`.

## Two-outcome criteria, fixed here and not adjustable

**SUCCESS** — all three must hold:

1. **Hits the target.** `B537` matches.
2. **Not vacuous.** Matches **≤ 15 %** of the corpus. Tighter than B821's 25 % because this is a
   *specific* topic, not a register; if a specific topic matches a seventh of everything, it is not
   specific.
3. **Not redundant.** The set it matches must not be **≥ 90 % contained** in any single existing
   motif's set. A motif that is `trace_map` under a new name adds a label, not information.

**FAILURE** — any of the three fails → **revert**, and `B537` stays a recorded `GAP` rather than a
closed one. **A gap honestly left open is better than one closed by a label that carries nothing** —
which is precisely what B821 established when its 46 %-matching motif was reverted.

## Declared in advance

- I expect **criterion 3 to be the dangerous one.** `trace_map` already carries `Fricke.Vogt`, and
  the Markov surface *is* the Fricke identity's level set, so the two topics are genuinely adjacent.
  **If the new motif is ≥ 90 % inside `trace_map`, the honest conclusion is that B537's blindness is
  a `trace_map` pattern gap, not a missing motif** — and the fix would be a pattern, not a concept.
- I expect SUCCESS overall. Recorded so the outcome cannot be softened.

## What this does not do

It does not re-ground the lexicon in K023–K025, and it does not touch the other 18 motifs. It closes
**one** named gap. If it succeeds, `docs/atlas/BLIND_ARCS.md` drops to **zero open `GAP` rows** —
which must be read as *"no known uncovered object topic among substantial blind arcs"*, **not** as
*"the lexicon is complete."*
