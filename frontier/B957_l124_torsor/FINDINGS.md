## The verdict

**NO.** And the gap is not marginal — it is a category difference.

| | **B700's torsor** | **CMR's torsor** |
|---|---|---|
| structure group | **ℤ/2** | **Gal(K^ab/K)** = the idèle class group |
| order | **2** | **infinite (profinite)** |
| field | **quadratic** — ℚ(√5), ℚ(√−7) | the abelian closure of **ℚ(√−3)** |

B700 does not merely have a ℤ/2 torsor in one case. Its own summary generalises it:

> *"at each stage the hearing = a choice among exactly TWO D-dim shadow irreps, with a
> **QUADRATIC** character field, swapped simply-transitively by **its Galois ℤ/2** — a
> fiber-functor torsor."*

**Every B700 torsor has structure group ℤ/2.** A finite group of order two is not the
idèle class group of ℚ(√−3). The identification B723 relied on is therefore **refuted, not
merely unearned.**

## The fields are not just different — they are mutually blind

Computed here rather than cited: with the Legendre symbol,

- **(−3 | 5) = −1** ⟹ **5 is inert in ℚ(√−3)**
- **(5 | 3) = −1** ⟹ **3 is inert in ℚ(√5)**

So the two fields are **mutually blind** (the banked B857 fact, re-derived). The torsor
B700 exhibits lives over a field that the CMR system's field cannot see, and vice versa.
There is no near-miss to rescue here.

This is the part worth keeping. B723's observer construction had two clauses, and both have

| clause | B723 placed it in | where it actually lives | order |
|---|---|---|---|
| **chirality** | Gal(K^ab/K) | **Gal(K/ℚ)** — B942 | **2** |
| **values** | Gal(K^ab/K) | **a quadratic Galois ℤ/2** — B700, this arc | **2** |

That is a more useful diagnosis than two unrelated errors, and it retro-explains B942's own
finding: the free-transitive action B723 was reaching for is real, but its group is small
and finite, not large and profinite. **Right object, wrong level** — for the third time in
this corpus, and now with the pattern named across two clauses at once.

## What this does and does not settle

**Settles:** the values clause is not supported by the CMR identification, and that
identification is dead. L124 is **CLOSED, negative.**

**Does NOT settle:** whether values are a torsor phenomenon *at all*. **B700 establishes
that they are** — concretely, with its own base-rate gate passed (it checked that "the value
lives in ℚ(√5) so the group is ℤ/2" is trivial and does *not* count, and verified the three
non-trivial facts: exactly two irreps, character field exactly ℚ(√5), simply-transitive with
no fixed point). So the values clause is **not** rehabilitated by this arc, but it is no
longer floating: it has a **concrete, banked, small torsor** of its own, and the thing that
was wrong was gluing it to CMR.

**Consequence for B723:** its correction banner should note that *both* clauses were
misplaced, not only chirality.
