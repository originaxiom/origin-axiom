## 4. The tree-level vacuum, and the neutrino obstruction

**The sign theorem.** In the design the E₆-charged fields are the three apex 27s and the bulk modes of the 78. The Standard-Model
singlets among them are N_i, ν^c_i (γ = −5/3 each) and the three moduli (γ = 0; the only SM-singlet roots of E₆ are ±β, with γ = 0).
The U(1)_η D-term restricted to SM-preserving VEVs is −(5/3)g′²Σ_i(|N_i|² + |ν^c_i|²) and vanishes only at N_i = ν^c_i = 0. Hence no
SM-preserving flat direction breaks U(1)_η; the tree-level SM-preserving moduli space is the tripod of §3, on which U(1)_η and the
apex U(1)² are unbroken. This is the exact opposite of B1283's Y₉ closing, whose nine paired branches ⟨N N̄⟩, ⟨ν^c ν̄^c⟩ needed the
27̄s of h¹ — the apex design has none. (The theorem holds for any flat connection commuting with the Standard Model, since every
such connection's centraliser contains γ — the double centraliser of B1269 — and every 27 has the same two singlets.)

**The consequence.** U(1)_η is therefore exact above the soft-SUSY-breaking scale, where the soft masses of N and ν^c can drive VEVs
against the D-term (the E₆SSM's mechanism for its U(1)_N). But U(1)_η, unlike U(1)_N, charges ν^c (Z′ = 10): the Majorana mass
ν^c ν^c has Z′ = 20 and is forbidden above the soft scale (the lowest neutral completion is ν^c ν^c (H_u H_d)²/M³). No seesaw. The
Dirac coupling L ν^c H_u sits in the 16·16·10 of the E₆ cubic together with Q u^c H_u, with B1276's single coupling and B1361's
hollow texture: two neutrinos at λ⟨H_u⟩ — the up-quark scale — and one massless at tree level, with B1362's order-one corrections
changing the texture, not the scale. **The three-apex design with the η line predicts neutrinos at the up-quark masses and is
excluded.** Closed loopholes: a Fayet–Iliopoulos term needs an anomalous U(1) (three complete 27s and a vector-like bulk pair are
anomaly-free); membrane instantons generate gauge-invariant terms only; every other sector of the design (the companion loci's
moduli, the C-field, the apex-U(1)²-breaking fields, the E₇ apex singlets) is E₆-neutral; SM-charged VEVs (H_u, H_d, D, D̄ carry
negative Z′) break the Standard Model at their scale.

## 5. What it means

1. **The Z′ is named and family-universal.** The design's extra U(1) is the η model's; LHC bounds apply directly; its mass is at the
   soft scale by the sign theorem.
2. **The bulk is not free.** Three exactly flat moduli (their lifting is the instanton superpotential's job, L201) and one vector-like
   triplet pair without a tree-level mass — a fourth TeV-scale triplet pair beside the 27s' three, all four at the soft scale.
3. **The pure three-27 design is excluded by neutrinos**, by a one-line sign argument that no tuning within its field content evades.
   This is the honest outcome of B1364's resolution: the level mismatch is resolved, and the resolved design fails at the next step.
   The seat's next arc is L215.
4. **The deck is broken by the line alone**: no combined line is E₆-conjugate to its deck image. B1362's requirement is met
   structurally; the three apexes' inequivalence is the character χ₄'s.
5. The three faces: the torus's 4-torsion is arithmetic (the coweight lattice in the plane of Y and γ, basis (6, 0), (−6/5, 1/5)); the
   −1 roots' type is the root system's geometry (one involution, triplet-type, under all four SM-reaching elements); the D-term's sign
   and the Fox-calculus zero modes are the quantum theory's.

## 6. Caveats

1. Tree level throughout; soft terms and non-perturbative effects shift vacua below their own scale but cannot violate U(1)_η above it.
2. H¹(Y₃; 78_ρ) is the standard reduction of the 7d theory on Y₃ (the 4d chiral multiplets of the E₆ locus); the compact closing is
   still a design (B1355–B1360), and the loci other than Y₃ contribute E₆-neutral fields only.
3. The Z′ charges are family-universal because the apex 27s are E₆-identical; the apex U(1)² is a separate gauge factor with its own
   (unbroken at tree level) D-terms.
4. One-loop unification with the extra content (three 5 ⊕ 5̄ from the 27s, the bulk pair) is not computed.
