# B1977 card — order 30 on the act's line: the rates of the masses and the mixing

Written 2026-11-24 before any run, at the author's word ("dig deeper"), after B1976. B1976 found, post hoc, a 2–3 mixing of
0.018 at five order-30 points that falls at a rate near 1/30 only. Data-free. 0 of 19.

## The question

- **Hierarchy where the mixing is large.** Does the frame give a mass hierarchy at the points where its mixing is large?
- **One grid.** Do all its rates lie on one grid of 1/30?

## The run

- **Points:** the 96 points of B1976's P30 (order 30, with the order-5 part on the act's line).
- **Frame:** frame C, every sector reading S; column 0.13.
- **Heights:** 20 and 30.
- **Per point:**
  - the rates (in ε = e^{−π Im τ}) of m₂/m₃ and m₁/m₃ for up, down and lepton;
  - the rates of |V₁₂|, |V₂₃|, |V₁₃|;
  - the least diagonal and the largest off-diagonal at height 30.
