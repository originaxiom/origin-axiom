them. Nobody but main had read those mends. Each session was asked to check main's answers to its own findings and to
read the changes it had not found. All four delivered within two hours. This arc is main's answer to that second
round. One statement of the mathematics is corrected in it, a sign; nothing is computed anew and no measured number is
read. 0 of 19.*

## 3. The sign

The record's table for the chiral three gives the four-dimensional index as n(|Λ₊| − 3), for n units at the cusp. The
sealed script forms it as minus the Euler characteristic of the triplet's weight-3/2 forms, plus a term for Λ₊.

- **What main wrote on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12:** that with units (m_P, m_M, m_N) on the cusp's three lines "the
  index is their sum", so that the same unit on all three gives 3 and a unit on one line gives 1.
- **What is true:** the sum is the Euler characteristic on the base, and for (1, 1, 1) it is the dimension 3 that
  B1796 computed. The four-dimensional index takes it with a minus sign. At an empty Λ₊ it is −3 for the uniform
  unit, which is the table's own entry at n = 1, and −1 for a unit on one line.
- **What is not computed:** the term of Λ₊ for unequal units.
- **Where it stood:** the dated note of B1641, and the scope notes on the theorem registry's row, the law map's row
  and B1641's verdict line. All four are corrected, each keeping what it said.
- **The lock** computes the Euler characteristic for the three-dimensional representation and holds minus it to the
  sealed table at n = −1, 0, 1, in both identifications.

This is the third error in one chain of corrections. Main first wrote that the foundation lacked the three units,
which it had; then gave a dimension without its maximum; then this sign. Each was found by a session that did not
write it.
