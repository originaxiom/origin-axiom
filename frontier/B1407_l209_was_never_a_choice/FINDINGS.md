## 6. THE ERROR — E79, AND IT IS THIS BENCH'S

Framing a settled question as the owner's preference is its own failure class,
and it is the one this arc corrects. **E79, the FALSE OWNER-CALL class:** a
question the record already answers is escalated to the owner as a judgement.
It is expensive in a specific way — an owner cannot *know* it is decidable
without redoing the work, so the question stalls indefinitely and looks like
patience rather than error. Here the spec had been written down for weeks, in
three places, one of them the very table the row lives in.

**The rule:** before calling anything an owner's call, grep the record for the
thing's **specification**. Genuine owner-calls are about *resources and
exposure* — what to spend effort on, what to publish, what to risk. Anything a
document already answers is not one.
