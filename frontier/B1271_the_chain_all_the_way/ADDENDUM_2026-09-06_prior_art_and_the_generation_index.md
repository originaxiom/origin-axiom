  T_{αβγ} = ε_ijk ∫_Q ω^i_α ∧ ω^j_β ∧ ω^k_γ,  α, β, γ ∈ H¹(Q; 3_ρ),

symmetric one, as it must be (E₆'s d_abc is symmetric).
