# B1474 — ADDENDUM (2026-10-07): the coset rule is withdrawn as a law — the audit lane's R92, taken in full

**What B1474 §3 says.** "The mirror genuinely swaps the geometric spin structure iff 1 ∉ η₀K — iff no orientation-reversing
isometry fixes it", with K the group generated by the sign characters of the orientation-preserving isometries a bounded
word search found.

**What is wrong (R92, 2026-10-04; credited).** Isometries act on the spin structures of M *affinely*: T_g(x) = A_g x + b_g
on the torsor over H¹(M; ℤ/2), with b_{gh} = b_g + A_g b_h. The orbit of the geometric spin structure under the preserving
isometries is {b_g}, which need not be closed under addition; a *span* of found characters is not that orbit, and a
bounded search is not coverage of the isometry group. R92 exhibits a complete synthetic action on which the span rule
reports FIX falsely and a missing-generator sample on which it reports SWAP falsely. Nothing in R92 says a row of
B1474's table is wrong; the justification was not a certificate.

**What each verdict rests on now.**
- **The seven SWAP verdicts** (m003, m207, s955, s957, s960, t12838, o10_150695) do not rest on the search: no spin
  structure of any of them has a real odd torsion (B1476 §2b: 0 of 2, 2, 4, 4, 8, 2, 2), and all seven are among the 75
  one-cusped census manifolds on which B1477's Theorem A forbids every mirror-invariant spin structure by proof.
- **The six FIX verdicts** are existence claims and rest on exhibited witnesses; no coverage is needed.
- **The coset statement** is kept only as a description: on every member the η's found do lie in one coset of the span
  of the preserving characters found.
