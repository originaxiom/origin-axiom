## 2. The frame, and what a generation needs

1. **The sectors.** With the SM unbroken the holonomy lies in c(SM) = SL(2)_β × ℂ*² (B1368); the SL(2)_β part is ρ_χ (main's
   locus), the ℂ*² part a pair of characters (ψ_Y, ψ_γ), and the weight w sees χ_w = ψ_Y^{6Y(w)} ψ_γ^{3γ(w)} (6Y and 3γ integers on
   every weight; B1372's typing, re-read here: in the 78 Q, u^c, e^c are doublets with γ = 1 and Q̄, u, e doublets with γ = −1; in the
   27 the 10 and the 5 are spin 0 with γ = −⅔ and 4/3, d^c/D̄ and L/H_d doublets with γ = ⅓, ν^c/N a doublet with γ = −5/3). The number
   of copies of an SM representation R in the zero modes is n of its sector module and its net chirality is I of that module (B1297's
   definition); for the self-conjugate 78 the net count n(V_w) − n(V_{−w}) equals I(V_w) when ρ_χ is self-dual, which holds when
   h¹(χ²) = 1 (the two extensions of χ⁻¹ by χ are then proportional) — true at every locus used. The six doublet counts: I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_Y ψ_γ³)
   for Q, I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_Y⁻⁴ ψ_γ³) for u^c, I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_Y⁶ ψ_γ³) for e^c, I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_Y² ψ_γ) for the (3̄,1)_{1/3} states (d^c and D̄ share the
   doublet), I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_Y⁻³ ψ_γ) for the (1,2)_{−1/2} states (L and H_d), I(ρ_χ ⊗ ψ_γ⁻⁵) for the singlets. The spin-0 sectors are rank-one
   modules: **I = 0 by T4**. So no net 10 can come from the 27; the only 10 available is the 78's, and a generation in this frame is the
   78's 10 with the 27's 5̄ — door 2's mixed frame (B1372), now with a non-split SL(2)_β holonomy.
2. **T5 at the cusp.** I ≠ 0 needs t₀ ≠ 0 (B1297's T5; finite-order diagonal, so t₀ = t₀*). An invariant vector of ρ_χ ⊗ χ_w on the
   torus exists iff χ_w = χ⁻¹ on ⟨μ, λ⟩, or χ_w = χ there and the cocycle restricted to the torus is a coboundary of χ²|_T.
3. **A generation.** Five charged sectors firing at once, in the generic case: ψ_Y ψ_γ³ = ψ_Y⁻⁴ ψ_γ³ = ψ_Y⁶ ψ_γ³ = χ⁻¹ on T gives
   ψ_Y⁵ = 1 on T; ψ_Y² ψ_γ = ψ_Y⁻³ ψ_γ = χ⁻¹ gives the same; and (ψ_Y² ψ_γ)³ = ψ_Y⁶ ψ_γ³ gives **χ² = 1 on the peripheral subgroup**. So
   a generation-shaped count needs the SL(2)_β holonomy of order ≤ 2 on the cusp — ρ_χ(μ) = ±[[1, c], [0, 1]], parabolic in kind — with
   ψ_Y of order dividing 5 there and ψ_γ = χ⁻¹ ψ_Y⁻² there. The census searches every pair and does not use the derivation; the
   derivation explains the census: on t12839 the peripheral curve SnapPy calls μ is a commutator (bcBC), so every character is trivial
   on it, and the other curve carries only ±1 at every non-split locus (44 cusp-trivial, 45 with −1) — the condition holds at every
   locus, and the ψ_Y found are exactly trivial or of order 5; on the siblings χ(μ) runs over twelfth roots (s958: 5 of 13 loci have
   χ² = 1 on the cusp; v2873 23 of 31; t12835 23 of 55; o10_150701 31 of 119 at N = 48) and no surviving pair completes the five counts.
4. **What the count is.** In every sampled background the net spectrum is one 10 from the 78 and one 5̄ from the 27 (or their
   conjugates) and nothing else — no net singlet, no net Higgs, no net exotic triplet, the spin-0 sectors all zero. One generation, not
   three; the two signs are equally frequent (conjugate backgrounds).
