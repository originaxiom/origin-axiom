**Verdict: PROVED** (computed, with the mechanism exact). cc (main), 2026-10-22. No data enters §§1–2. **0 of 19.**

## 0. Why

B1660 introduced three readings of the Higgs in the up sector: S, S̃ = εS*εᵀ (the Standard Model's H̃) and S*. Every
census since used all three: B1712, B1713, B1720, B1723, and the web seat's census on main's frame. The controls of B1720
and B1723 tested covariance with S only. The question surfaced while the web seat's thread reductions and main's
replication design both assumed T-covariance for every reading. A reading that is not covariant gives predictions that
depend on which representative of τ in its SL(2, ℤ) orbit was used.

## 1. The mechanism (exact)

S = a_i·i + a_j·j + a_k·k in B1644's quaternion units, the a's being B1691's triplet at τ.
- **T acts on the components by ρ(T) = e^{iπ/4}(1 ⊕ R₋₉₀°):** a_i → e^{iπ/4}a_i and (a_j, a_k) → e^{iπ/4}(a_k, −a_j). The
  2 × 2 block was solved from two τ and checked on a third (1.8 × 10⁻¹⁵); the i-phase is 1/8 exactly at three τ; the
  eigenphases are 1/8, 3/8 and 7/8.
- **S̃** conjugates the components: with ε = j, εXεᵀ = jXj⁻¹ flips i and k, so εS*εᵀ = a_i*·i + a_j*·j + a_k*·k. Since
  ρ(T) is real up to its phase, S̃ transforms by ρ(T) times the global phase e^{−iπ/2}, which no singular value sees.
  **S̃ is covariant.**
- **S*** = −a_i*·i + a_j*·j − a_k*·k also flips the i and k components. Conjugating 1 ⊕ R₋₉₀° by diag(−1, 1, −1) gives
  1 ⊕ R₊₉₀° = 1 ⊕ (−R₋₉₀°): a relative sign between the i component and the (j, k) block, not a global phase. **S* is not
  covariant under T.** Under T², R₋₉₀°² = −1 = R₊₉₀°², so the sign disappears.
- **The general rule.** S̃ is covariant under every transformation (§2), so conj ρ(γ) equals ρ(γ) up to a phase for all γ.
  S*'s components are D·ā with D = diag(−1, 1, −1), so its effective representation is D ρ(γ) D up to a phase. **S* is
  covariant exactly where ρ(γ) commutes with D up to a phase.** That set is a subgroup. §2 finds it contains R
  (τ → τ/(τ + 1), the record's move [[1, 0], [1, 1]]) and T², and not T, S or TS. Its image in SL(2, ℤ)/Γ(2) ≅ S₃ is
  therefore the order-2 subgroup generated by R's image, so the group is Γ⁰(2) = ⟨R, T², −1⟩, the matrices with even
  upper-right entry, of index 3. S* respects one of the record's two moves and only the square of the other.

**So S and S̃ are covariant under SL(2, ℤ), and S* exactly under Γ⁰(2) (index 3).** S*'s rows at τ and τ + 1, or at τ and
−1/τ, are different predictions; a census of S* sees one of three sheets unless it samples all three. At τ = i, which S
fixes, every reading passes the S comparison (3 × 10⁻¹² for S*), because there the test is weaker: the components are the
same vector.

**An instrument limit, disclosed.** A first S T² S check composed from the four τ above reached Im τ = 0.09 at its last
step, and there every reading failed (S included, up to 8). The frame's series are not reliable that close to the real
axis. No sealed test went there (B1720's apexes ≥ 1.1, B1723's valley ≥ 1, the censuses ≥ 1.5). The check in the table
keeps every chain at Im τ ≥ 0.31.
