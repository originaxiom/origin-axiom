## 1. The mechanism: how the spectral action turns spectra into couplings

**The principle** ([Chamseddine-Connes 1996, hep-th/9606001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9606001); CMP 186 (1997) 731). For a spectral triple (A, H, D), the bosonic action is the universal formula

    S = Tr f(D/Lambda)

with Lambda a cutoff scale and f a positive (cutoff) function, plus the fermionic term (psi, D psi). Physics is required to depend only on the spectrum of D.

**Where numbers come from.** The heat-kernel / Seeley-DeWitt expansion gives, on a 4d base,

    Tr f(D/Lambda) ~ f_4 Lambda^4 a_0(D^2) + f_2 Lambda^2 a_2(D^2) + f_0 a_4(D^2) + O(Lambda^-2),

with f_4 = Int u^3 f(u) du, f_2 = Int u f(u) du, f_0 = f(0). Three points matter for B793:

1. **Structure sits in the a_k** (curvature invariants tensored with the representation content of the internal space): a_0 -> cosmological term, a_2 -> Einstein-Hilbert + Higgs mass term, a_4 -> gauge kinetic terms, Higgs kinetic + quartic, Weyl gravity.
2. **Values sit in (f_0, f_2, f_4, Lambda)** — three free moments plus a scale. Perturbatively *nothing else about f matters*.
3. **Relations, not values, are the honest output**: because all gauge kinetic terms arise from one trace, one gets g3^2 = g2^2 = (5/3) g1^2 at Lambda (SU(5)-like normalization) and the Higgs quartic tied to Yukawas. Measured values then require *imposing* these as boundary conditions at a unification scale and running down with RG.

**The NCG Standard Model** ([Chamseddine-Connes-Marcolli 2006, hep-th/0610241](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0610241); ATMP 11 (2007) 991). Spacetime is almost-commutative: M4 x F with F the finite space of algebra C + H + M3(C); H_F is the 96-dim fermion space; D_F contains Yukawas and the Majorana seesaw. The spectral action then *derives* the full SM Lagrangian with neutrino mixing minimally coupled to gravity. Predictions at unification: gauge relations (as in GUTs), Sum y^2 = 4 g^2 constraint (top mass ~ right), and the Higgs quartic — which under the "big desert" RG gave **m_H ~ 170 GeV**.

**Hyperbolic side (no spectral action, but the toolkit exists):**
- Compact H3/Gamma heat trace via Selberg: [Cognola-Vanzo, hep-th/9303028](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9303028) (JMP 35 (1994) 3109): hyperbolic elements contribute only exponentially small corrections; all polynomial coefficients are the identity-term (volume) ones.
- Cusped, rigorous: [Park, math/0111175](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111175) — eta invariants and regularized determinants of Dirac Laplacians on cusped hyperbolic manifolds via relative traces, tied to Selberg zetas of even/odd type; [Friedman, DOI 10.1007/s00220-007-0330-3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-007-0330-3) — explicit det(Laplacian)-to-Selberg-zeta formula for cofinite Kleinian groups (i.e., exactly our class of objects); Ruelle/Selberg zetas with cusps: [DOI 10.1007/s00208-009-0408-7](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-009-0408-7).

**The volume-rigidity theorem (exact, checkable in one line).** The diagonal heat kernel of H^3 for the scalar Laplacian (spectrum offset: lambda = 1 + r^2) is *exactly* (4 pi t)^{-3/2} e^{-t}. Hence for any hyperbolic 3-manifold the local part of the heat trace is Vol * (4 pi t)^{-3/2} e^{-t}, i.e.

    a_0 = Vol,  a_2 = -Vol,  a_4 = +Vol/2,  a_{2k} = (-1)^k Vol / k!

Cross-check in standard Seeley-DeWitt form: a_4 = (1/360) Int (5R^2 - 2|Ric|^2 + 2|Riem|^2) with R = -6, |Ric|^2 = 12, |Riem|^2 = 12 gives (180/360) Vol = Vol/2. Consistent with Cognola-Vanzo: geodesic terms are exponentially small in 1/t, cusp terms are non-local. **Consequences:**
- The perturbative spectral action of m004 is a function of Vol = 2*Cl2(pi/3) = 2.0298832128... alone (`FINDINGS.md:213-218`) — one number, transcendental, already exhaustively tested against SM values in earlier campaigns (null).

Everything m004-specific — mod-4 trace laws (`FINDINGS.md:169-197`), H1, cusp lattice — enters only via (i) geodesic terms ~ e^{-l Lambda}, (ii) cusp/scattering terms including a non-Seeley-DeWitt Lambda-log sector (the -(T/pi) ln T parabolic behavior already observed at `FINDINGS.md:129-131`), (iii) the coupling function f itself.
- Therefore: **any claimed SM-value derivation from m004 that uses only perturbative spectral-action data is volume numerology by construction, and the m003 sister is a free, exact control for every coupling model.**

**The cusp is the interesting part.** On m004, Tr f(D/Lambda) diverges (continuous spectrum [1, infinity)); the correct object is the *relative/regularized* trace (Muller-Park-Friedman), whose extra terms are built from the scattering matrix — and for m004 the scattering is **exactly** phi(s) = Lambda_K(s-1)/Lambda_K(s) (banked B737/B739; used at `FINDINGS.md:86-88` and `frontier/B792_maass_m004_eigenvalues/weyl_scattering_check.py`, cf. `FINDINGS.md:123-132`).
