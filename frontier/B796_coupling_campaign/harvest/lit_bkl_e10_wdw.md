## 1. The mechanism

Near a generic spacelike singularity, the Einstein equations ultralocalize (BKL): each spatial point evolves as an independent Bianchi-type system, and in the strict limit the dynamics of the logarithmic scale factors beta^i becomes a relativistic billiard in beta-space, confined by potential walls (curvature, symmetry, p-form) to the fundamental Weyl chamber of a hyperbolic Kac-Moody algebra [Damour-Henneaux-Nicolai, hep-th/0212256]. For pure gravity in D spacetime dimensions the algebra is AE_{D-1} = A_{D-3}^{++}; chaos exists iff the algebra is hyperbolic, i.e. for 3 < D < 11 [Damour-Henneaux-Julia-Nicolai, hep-th/0103094; Demaret-Henneaux-Spindel, PLB 164 (1985) 27].

Wheeler-DeWitt quantization of the billiard (minisuperspace, one spatial point) separates Psi(rho, z) = R(rho) F(z) on the unit hyperboloid of beta-space, giving (Kleinschmidt-Nicolai, arXiv:0912.0854, eqs. (12)-(13)):

    -Delta_LB F = E F,   R_pm(rho) = rho^{-(d-2)/2} exp( +/- i sqrt(E - ((d-2)/2)^2) log rho )

with rho -> infinity the singularity, d = number of spatial dimensions. Sharp walls impose **Dirichlet** conditions on the chamber; oddness under the full Weyl group W = W^+ . 2 plus invariance under W^+ makes F an **odd Maass form** of the arithmetic group W^+. Consequences (0912.0854; Kleinschmidt-Koehn-Nicolai, PRD 80 (2009) 061701): E >= ((d-2)/2)^2, the wavefunction is generically complex, oscillating, and **tends to zero at the singularity**; for 11d supergravity W^+(E10) = PSL(2,O) (octavians), for 4d pure gravity W(AE3) = PGL(2,Z), eigenfunctions automorphic under PSL(2,Z) (first noted by Forte, arXiv:0812.4382).

## 2. CONFIRMED: which theory owns PSL(2,O_3)

The question "for which (super)gravity does the billiard group become the Eisenstein Bianchi group" has an exact, published answer, and it is **five-dimensional**, not 11d:

- **Feingold-Kleinschmidt-Nicolai, J. Algebra 322 (2009) 1295, arXiv:0805.3018** (PDF extracted): **Proposition 5: W^+(A2^{++}) ≅ PSL_2(E)**, "where PSL2(E) denotes the 'Eisenstein modular subgroup' of PSL2(C) obtained by restricting all entries to be Eisenstein integers." **Proposition 7: W^+(G2^{++}) = W^+(A2^{++}) ⋊ 2 = PSL_2(E) ⋊ 2.** Their summary table also gives C2^{++} -> PSL_2(G) ⋊ 2 (Gaussian), A1^{++} -> PSL_2(Z), D8/E10 -> PSL_2(O) (octavians).
- A2^{++} = AE4 is the billiard of **pure gravity in D=5** (hep-th/0103094; hep-th/0212256). G2^{++} is the billiard of **5d Einstein-Maxwell, unchanged by the Chern-Simons term, hence of the bosonic sector of minimal D=5 supergravity** (Damour-de Buyl-Henneaux-Schomblond, JHEP 08 (2002) 030, hep-th/0206125; sigma-model confirmation Mizoguchi-Mohri-Yamada, hep-th/0512092; fermionic sector Damour-Spindel, PRD 105 (2022) 125006, arXiv:2202.03794).
- **De Clerck-Hartnoll, "Wheeler-DeWitt wavefunctions for 5d BKL dynamics, automorphic L-functions and complex primon gases", JHEP 11 (2025) 160, arXiv:2507.08788** (PDF extracted) is the full quantum treatment: §2.1 5d pure gravity -> equilateral-triangle billiard in H^3, wavefunctions (their eq. (56)) = functions on H^3 invariant under PSL(2,Z[omega]) and **odd under z -> -z̄**, decaying at the cusp: **odd Maass cusp forms of PSL(2,Z[omega])**. §2.2 5d Einstein-Maxwell/minimal sugra -> hemi-equilateral (G2) domain, odd Maass forms of PSL(2,Z[omega]) ⋊ 2. §2.3 4d Einstein-Maxwell-axion-dilaton (N=4 sugra truncation) -> Gaussian integers PSL(2,Z[i]). They prove the billiard spectrum is **purely discrete**: the Eisenstein series is even under z -> -z̄ and fails the Dirichlet conditions (dch extraction, §3.4 end). Each wavefunction is then Mellin/Fourier-transformed into Hecke L-functions evaluated on the critical axis, with an Euler product over complex primes ('complex primon gas'); convention -nabla^2 psi = (1 + eps^2) psi — **identical to our lambda = 1 + r^2**.

Bookkeeping for m004 (vol 2.029883, index 12 over the Bianchi orbifold, B792 FINDINGS.md:209-229): the A2^{++} chamber is half the Bianchi orbifold, so **m004 tiles the 5d pure-gravity billiard exactly 24 times** (48 times for the G2^{++} chamber). ## 5. Exit from the billiard phase / isotropization

- Chaos requires hyperbolicity: pure gravity D >= 11 is monotone Kasner (Demaret-Henneaux-Spindel 1985; hep-th/0103094).
- A massless scalar / stiff fluid quenches oscillations: rigorous AVTD singularities (Andersson-Rendall, CMP 218 (2001) 479, gr-qc/0001047).
- Quantum side: the wavefunction vanishes at the singularity under Dirichlet conditions (0907.3048, 0912.0854); wavepackets spread and redshift (Koehn, PRD 85 (2012) 063501). De Clerck-Hartnoll's Discussion lists the physically decisive open problem for any exit story: **re-coupling of spatial points away from the strict BKL limit** (perturbation around ultralocality; many-body billiards), plus black-hole-interior applications where the exit connects to an exterior observer (De Clerck-Hartnoll-Santos, JHEP 07 (2024) 202).
- No paper derives isotropization or symmetry breaking FROM billiard spectral data; that gap is exactly where B793's observer-object coupling models can be genuinely novel rather than duplicative.

## FAILURE MODES
Six distinct traps. (1) Dimension mismatch: the Eisenstein billiard is 5d pure gravity / minimal 5d supergravity, not the 4d Standard-Model world; a coupling model must either motivate the 5d->4d descent (Kaluza-Klein) or accept that the established physics contact is with a non-realistic theory. (2) Parity trap: physical billiard states are ODD Maass CUSP forms of PSL(2,Z[omega]); generic Gamma_41 newforms (16 of our 17) are NOT billiard states — they live on the 12-fold congruence cover — and even the parent form at 51.0132 is a billiard state only if it passes the z->-z̄ parity test, which is currently UNKNOWN (Grunewald-Huntebrinker's Table gives no parity). Claiming 'our spectrum = wavefunctions of the universe' without the parity/sector census would be wrong. (3) No literature meaning for Gamma_41: no paper assigns physics to any congruence subgroup of a billiard Weyl group; the 'level-(4) observer sector' reading is a B793 construction to be tested, not an established mechanism. (4) Asymptotic validity: the sharp-wall billiard is exact only in the strict BKL limit; finite wall height and inter-point couplings (the explicit open problem in De Clerck-Hartnoll's Discussion) deform the spectral problem, so low-eigenvalue conclusions are limit statements, not statements about finite-time cosmology. (5) Exit erasure: the billiard phase ends (stiff scalar/quiescence per Andersson-Rendall; D>=11 per Demaret-Henneaux-Spindel; isotropization thereafter), and nothing in the literature says the arithmetic data survives the exit — a coupling model must specify the carrier (e.g. which spectral functionals imprint on exit distributions). (6) Numerology pressure: the mechanism now supplies a large zoo of legitimate numbers (eigenvalues, Hecke coefficients, L-values, time delays, primon-gas data); every SM comparison must stay behind the B743 base-rate/surrogate protocol that produced the clean nulls, or the campaign recreates exactly the pathology it was built to kill.
