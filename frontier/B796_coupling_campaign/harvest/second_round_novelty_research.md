# Adversarial re-search report

## CLAIM 1 — spectral action / heat coefficients on cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds

**Verdict: NARROWED** (refuted as written; a much weaker statement survives).

The claim as phrased conflates two things, and one of them is well-trodden. Small-time asymptotics of the **regularized heat trace** on finite-volume hyperbolic manifolds — which is exactly the input a spectral-action expansion consumes — is established Müller-school technology, and **zeta-regularized determinants** (built from the same heat-trace data via ζ'(0)) have been computed for cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds/orbifolds since the mid-2000s:

- **W. Müller, "On the analytic torsion of hyperbolic manifolds of finite volume" (arXiv:1501.07851, 2015)** [VERIFIED] — abstract states verbatim: "This requires the definition of a regularized trace of heat operators. We use the Selberg trace formula to study the asymptotic behavior of the regularized trace for small time." That IS a heat-coefficient computation for cusped hyperbolic manifolds, including the weighted (unipotent) orbital integrals that produce the cusp terms. Companion: Müller–Pfaff, arXiv:1110.4065 [VERIFIED].
- **Jinsung Park, "Eta invariants and regularized determinants for odd dimensional hyperbolic manifolds with cusps" (arXiv:math/0111175; Amer. J. Math. 2005)** [VERIFIED] — relative traces via the Selberg trace formula with detailed unipotent-orbital-integral analysis; eta invariants and regularized determinants of Dirac Laplacians on cusped hyperbolic manifolds, tied to Selberg zeta functions of even/odd type.
- **J. S. Friedman, "Regularized determinants of the Laplacian for cofinite Kleinian groups with finite-dimensional unitary representations" (arXiv:math/0605288, 2006)** [VERIFIED] — regularized det(Δ) for finite-volume hyperbolic 3-orbifolds (cofinite Kleinian groups, i.e. the PSL(2,C) setting containing the Bianchi groups), via Müller's regularization, with an explicit Selberg-zeta formula. The Elstrodt–Grunewald–Mennicke monograph (Springer 1998) [VERIFIED as scope] supplies the explicit cofinite trace formula and the PSL(2, O_K) arithmetic examples underlying all of this.

What I could NOT find, anywhere: a computation packaged as the **Chamseddine–Connes spectral action** Tr f(D/Λ) with its Λ-expansion for any finite-volume non-compact hyperbolic 3-manifold, and nothing of this kind for m004. The NCG spectral-action computational literature covers spheres/tori/Bieberbach, Robertson–Walker, and Bianchi IX — and the **name collision must be flagged**: Fan–Fathizadeh–Marcolli "Spectral action for Bianchi type-IX cosmological models" (arXiv:1506.06779, JHEP 2015) and "Modular forms in the spectral action of Bianchi IX gravitational instantons" (arXiv:1511.05321) [VERIFIED] concern the **Bianchi classification of homogeneous cosmologies** (SU(2)-invariant 4d metrics), not Bianchi groups PSL(2,O_K); citing them as prior art "on Bianchi groups" would be wrong, and conversely they are no defense against Müller/Park/Friedman. On the physics side the one-loop AdS3 literature also stops at cusps: Bourne–Fliss–Knighton, "A spool for every quotient" (arXiv:2507.05364, 2025) [VERIFIED] is explicitly restricted to "smooth, cusp-free" quotients.

**Narrowed statement that survives:** "No Chamseddine–Connes spectral-action expansion (Λ-expansion of Tr f(D/Λ) organized in Seeley–deWitt-type coefficients) has been published for any cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold, and no spectral-theoretic heat-coefficient computation of any kind has been published for m004 specifically. However, regularized heat-trace small-time asymptotics (Müller 1501.07851) and zeta-regularized determinants (Park math/0111175, Friedman math/0605288) on cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds are established; the novelty is assembling this into the spectral action for m004, not the regularized heat analysis itself."

**Nearest misses:** Müller 1501.07851 computes precisely the t→0 expansion of the regularized heat trace (identity, hyperbolic, and unipotent contributions, the last carrying the cusp-specific terms, including the non-classical ones) but deploys it only to define analytic torsion — the coefficients are never read off as gravitational-action terms (volume/curvature/cutoff-dependent cusp corrections), no Dirac-operator spectral triple is invoked, and no specific manifold is evaluated. Park math/0111175 and Friedman math/0605288 compute ζ'(0)-type quantities (determinants, eta) — single spectral values, not the tower of heat coefficients the spectral action expands in — and work at the level of general cofinite groups rather than m004. The Marcolli-school Bianchi IX papers do compute full Seeley–deWitt towers with remarkable arithmetic structure (modular forms, mixed Tate motives), but on cosmological 4-metrics unrelated to hyperbolic 3-manifolds.

## CLAIM 2 — no E6 T[M3]; no Z-hat for m004

**Verdict: REFUTED** (both prongs fail as stated; a narrower E6-specific statement survives).

**Prong "no Ẑ/homological block for m004" — REFUTED outright:**
- **Gukov–Manolescu, "A two-variable series for knot complements" (arXiv:1904.06057, 2019; Quantum Topology 2021)** [VERIFIED] — defines F_K(x,q) = Ẑ of the knot complement and, per the abstract, "find[s] numerically the first terms in the series for the figure-eight knot, up to any desired order." Ẑ for m004 is not merely defined — 4_1 is the paper's flagship hyperbolic example. The "not a negative-definite plumbing" argument attacks only the 2017-era plumbing definition, not the state of the art.
- **S. Park, "Large color R-matrix for knot complements and strange identities" (arXiv:2004.02087, 2020)** [VERIFIED] — R-matrix definition/computations of F_K beyond plumbings; **Willetts, "A unification of the ADO and colored Jones polynomials of a knot" (arXiv:2003.09854, 2020)** [VERIFIED] — a Habiro-ring invariant for **all** knots unifying ADO and colored Jones (the ADO route the claim ignores is fully classical for 4_1).
- **Chun–Gukov–Park–Sopenko, "3d-3d correspondence for mapping tori" (arXiv:1911.08456, 2019)** [VERIFIED] — T[M3] and Ẑ-type invariants for b1>0 manifolds with genus-1 mapping tori as the case study; m004 is the simplest hyperbolic once-punctured-torus bundle, i.e. inside the case-study class. **Ekholm–Gruen–Gukov–Kucharski–Park–Sułkowski (arXiv:2005.13349, CMP 2022)** [VERIFIED] push F_K = Ẑ(M_K) to large N / a-deformation (with follow-up arXiv:2110.13768 giving branch expansions for "several simple knots"; 4_1's inclusion there I could not confirm from the abstract — UNVERIFIED).

**Prong "machinery is type-A only" — REFUTED:**
- **S. Park, "Higher rank Ẑ and F_K" (arXiv:1909.13002; SIGMA 2020)** [VERIFIED] — abstract: defines "Ẑ^G for negative definite plumbed 3-manifolds" for **any root system G** (E6 included by definition) and F_K^G for torus knot complements.
- **Murakami–Terashima, "Homological blocks with simple Lie algebras and Witten–Reshetikhin–Turaev invariants" (arXiv:2308.04010, 2023)** [VERIFIED] — "for any Seifert fibered homology 3-sphere, we introduce homological blocks with simple Lie algebra and prove that its radial limits are identified with the WRT invariants." "Any simple Lie algebra" includes E6, with a theorem, not a conjecture.
- Non-type-A directions are an active industry: Chung's Ẑ for SO(3)/OSp(1|2) (arXiv:2209.00095) and Ferrari–Putrov's supergroup Ẑ (Ann. Henri Poincaré 2024) [VERIFIED as existing; contents beyond abstracts unverified]. Also note T[M3, g] is defined for **all ADE g** (E6 is ADE) via 6d (2,0) compactification — the standard formulation of the 3d-3d correspondence is ADE-labeled (nLab/review level, [VERIFIED as framing]) — so "no T[M3] exists for E6" is false as a statement about definitions.

**Narrowed statement that survives:** "No **explicit** T[M3, E6] computation (DGG-style Lagrangian, sphere partition function, index, or state-integral) has been published for any specific 3-manifold, cusped or closed; no exceptional-group Ẑ or F_K has been computed for m004 or any non-plumbed/non-Seifert manifold; higher-rank F_K computations exist only for torus knots (Park 1909.13002) plus the a-deformed (symmetric-representation, type-A) tower for simple knots (EGGKPS); general-G homological blocks exist only for negative-definite plumbings (Park) and Seifert homology spheres (Murakami–Terashima). For m004 at rank > 1 the physics side has type-A only (e.g. SL(N,C) Chern–Simons on cusped manifolds via wrapped M5-branes, Gang–Kim–Yoon arXiv:1911.10718 [VERIFIED as existing])."

**Nearest misses:** Park 1909.13002 has the E6 case of Ẑ^G formally in hand — the definition is uniform in the root system, Weyl-group sums over the plumbing lattice — but computes F_K^G only for torus knots, whose complements are not hyperbolic; nothing there touches m004. Murakami–Terashima 2308.04010 prove the strongest general-G statement in the literature (radial limits = WRT for any simple Lie algebra) but strictly for Seifert fibered homology spheres, again disjoint from cusped hyperbolic geometry. Chun–Gukov–Park–Sopenko 1911.08456 get T[M3] and torsion/WRT-from-index statements for the exact topological class containing m004, but with type-A (in practice rank-1) machinery. So the honest novelty statement is the intersection — exceptional gauge algebra AND a cusped hyperbolic target — not either factor alone, and "E6 T[M3] does not exist" should be recast as "T[M3, E6] exists by the ADE (2,0) construction but has never been made explicit or evaluated."
