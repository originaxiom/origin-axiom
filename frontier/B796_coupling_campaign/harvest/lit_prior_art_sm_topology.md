# B793 prior-art survey: the "Standard Model from topology / discrete objects" graveyard

**Scope.** Literature reconnaissance for the origin-axiom programme (m004 = figure-eight knot complement as structural substrate; surviving hypothesis H0: object supplies structure, values arise from observer-object coupling + dynamics). Question set: what did each prior programme claim, what did it actually derive (structure vs numbers), why did it fail or stall, what is the one lesson for a coupling-first campaign, and did anyone attempt an explicit observer/measurement coupling. All citations below were found via live web search on 2026-07-28 unless explicitly marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Braid/preon topology: Bilson-Thompson and the LQG embedding

**Claimed.** Bilson-Thompson's helon model ([arXiv:hep-ph/0503213](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2005hep.ph....3213B/abstract), 2005) maps the Harari-Shupe preon phenomenology onto framed 3-strand ribbon braids (braid group B_3): twist = electric charge (quantized in thirds), crossing handedness = chirality; one generation of SM fermions plus gauge-boson slots emerge as braid equivalence classes.

**Actually derived.** Structure only: charge quantization, a chirality label, one generation's quantum numbers. No masses, no coupling constants, no mixing angles, no dynamics.

**The LQG embedding (Smolin-Wan).** Wan ([arXiv:0710.1312](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.1312)), then Smolin-Wan and collaborators ([arXiv:0803.3203](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.3203), [arXiv:0805.0453](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.0453), [arXiv:0805.1265](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.1265)) embedded braids as local excitations of embedded 4-valent spin networks, with evolution moves giving propagation and interaction constrained by topological conservation laws, plus C/P/T actions on braids. The capstone review is Bilson-Thompson, Hackett, Kauffman, Wan, *Emergent Braided Matter of Quantum Geometry*, SIGMA 8 (2012) 014 ([ADS 2012SIGMA...8..014B](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2012SIGMA...8..014B/abstract)).

**Why it stalled.** (i) No Hamiltonian: "propagation" was a set of allowed moves, never a dynamics with a spectrum; no mass or coupling ever emitted. (ii) The braid-state/spin-foam amplitude connection was never built; conservation laws did not become interaction strengths. (iii) Generations: higher braids give a zoo of exotica, not three copies; contemporaneous discussion already noted the paper "did not provide definitive rules for the behavior of braid configurations acting as particles" ([Physics Forums thread](https://www.physicsforums.com/threads/long-awaited-braid-matter-paper-bilson-thompson-hackett-kauffman-smolin.225876/)). (iv) The principals dispersed after 2012. A 2025 revival ([arXiv:2501.03260](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03260), physics.gen-ph) maps helon states to Lorentz-group representation theory — still structure only.

**Lesson for coupling-first.** A perfect structural dictionary with no coupling layer emits nothing measurable and cannot be falsified; it stalls rather than fails. Build the coupling layer first, or the grammar is permanently inert.

## 2. Division algebras: Furey, Gresnigt, and the value-claiming fringe branch

**Claimed.** Furey's programme (thesis *Standard model physics from an algebra?*, [arXiv:1611.09182](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09182); key result [arXiv:1910.08395](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08395); ongoing, e.g. [Annalen der Physik 2025, DOI:10.1002/andp.202500229](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/andp.202500229)) derives SM gauge-group fragments and particle content from R⊗C⊗H⊗O acting on itself: charge quantization, three colours, SU(3)c×U(1)em ideals, and — the strongest result — 48 states behaving as exactly three generations under the two unbroken symmetries. Popular account: [Quanta 2018](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-octonion-math-that-could-underpin-physics-20180720/).

**Actually derived.** Structure: charge lattice, colour, a three-generation pattern under the *unbroken* subgroup only. Not derived: the full chiral SU(2)_L action, electroweak breaking, any mass, any coupling, anomaly analysis.

**Why it is considered stalled.** Documented criticisms ([Are octonions necessary to the Standard Model?](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333977233_Are_octonions_necessary_to_the_Standard_Model)): the constructions are "weakest where algebraic procedures are selected to reproduce experimental results rather than because the mathematical structure uniquely demands it"; the objects used are left/right multiplication algebras, not the octonions proper; the weak sector and anomalies remain open after a decade.

**Gresnigt** ([arXiv:1803.02202](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02202), Phys. Lett. B 783 (2018) 212; also [arXiv:2004.11140](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.11140)) showed the Bilson-Thompson framed braids coincide with the basis states of Furey's minimal ideals (B_2, B_3^c represented inside C and H) — unifying two structure-only programmes. The union still emits no numbers.

**The value-claiming branch.** T. P. Singh and collaborators graft trace dynamics + exceptional Jordan algebra onto the octonion programme and claim actual numbers: low-energy alpha and mass ratios ([arXiv:2205.06614](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.06614), [arXiv:2108.05787](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.05787)), CKM parameters ([arXiv:2305.00668](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.00668)). These claims have no mainstream acceptance; they are the live specimen of "values located in the object", with characteristic marks: exact algebraic numbers matched to scheme- and scale-dependent measured quantities.

**Lesson.** Structure results in this family are real but chronically incomplete at exactly the chiral/broken sector — the same gate origin-axiom's grammar must pass. And when a branch of the programme starts emitting exact values without a scale/scheme declaration, that is the numerology failure mode reappearing inside an otherwise serious programme.

## 3. Geometric alpha: Atiyah, Wyler, Eddington, Parker-Rhodes — the pure value graveyard

- **Atiyah (2018).** Claimed alpha = 1/Ж from a "Todd function" tied to the Riemann hypothesis ([manuscript PDF](https://empslocal.ex.ac.uk/people/staff/mrwatkin/zeta/atiyah_FSC.pdf); analyses by [Carroll](https://www.preposterousuniverse.com/blog/2018/09/25/atiyah-and-the-fine-structure-constant/), [Cook](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2018/09/24/riemann-hypothesis-the-fine-structure-constant-and-the-todd-function/)). Rejected: the Todd function was never well-defined, the numerics irreproducible, the manuscript kept off arXiv; colleagues advised against publication. **Failure mode:** value-in-object with no mechanism, plus irreproducible computation.
- **Wyler (1971).** alpha = 1/137.03608 from volumes of bounded complex domains; demolished in PRL by Robertson ([DOI:10.1103/PhysRevLett.27.1545](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.27.1545)): the match requires arbitrarily setting a domain radius to 1, and one coefficient was miscomputed ([Springer critique](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01807299), [historical account](https://physicalgeometry.wordpress.com/2016/08/30/armand-wyler/)). Dyson invited him to IAS; he could not supply a physical context; dismissed as numerology. **Failure mode:** un-selected normalization ("radius = 1") — every normalization must be dynamically chosen.
- **Eddington (1929-1944).** 1/alpha = 136, then 137, "by pure deduction", inside an explicitly observer-based epistemology ("selective subjectivism": what we comprehend is what we put in to make it comprehensible) — see Kragh ([arXiv:1510.04046](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.04046)) and the historical review of alpha attempts ([arXiv:1411.4673](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.4673)). Failed: experiment drifted away from the integer; the a-priori scheme could absorb any correction. **This is the canonical explicit observer-coupling attempt — see section 8.**
- **Parker-Rhodes / Bastin / Noyes combinatorial hierarchy & bit-string physics.** The 1961 construction yields 3, 10, 137, 2^127+136 and terminates ([Cameron's account](https://cameroncounts.wordpress.com/2015/02/22/the-combinatorial-hierarchy/); [Bastin-Noyes-Amson-Kilmister, DOI:10.1007/BF00670503](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00670503); Noyes, *Physical numerology?*, [SLAC-PUB-4929](https://inspirehep.net/files/7f24bff119b8967b596c080bd1cf8511)). Values were tied to counter-firing (measurement-act) combinatorics — a second explicit measurement-based value programme. Failed: it derives the **integer** 137 where physics has a running alpha(mu) = 1/137.035999...; no SM structure; the ANPA community closed in on itself. **Failure mode:** integer-exactness is a red flag; measured constants are scale/scheme-dependent.

**Lesson (jointly).** Any B793 model whose output is a bare, scale-free algebraic number matched to a measured constant repeats this graveyard. Protocol should *ban* matches that do not declare mu and scheme.

## 4. Kaluza-Klein: structure succeeded, values failed at two distinct gates

- **Structure success:** KK charge quantization from compactness; internal isometries as gauge groups ([Overduin-Wesson review, arXiv:gr-qc/9805018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9805018)).
- **Gate 1 — chirality no-go.** Witten showed no even-dimensional homogeneous internal space yields the SM's chiral fermion representations (index-theoretic, Atiyah-Hirzebruch; robust under deformation) — confirmed in modern treatments ([arXiv:2306.01049](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.01049), [arXiv:2506.09126](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09126)). Primary source is Witten's 1981 *Search for a realistic Kaluza-Klein theory* — details from memory, UNVERIFIED.
- **Gate 2 — value derivation.** Candelas-Weinberg, Nucl. Phys. B237 (1984) 397: Casimir energy self-consistency fixes the compactification size and hence the gauge coupling. Mechanically the *right kind* of move — the scale is dynamically selected, not read off — but numerically unrealistic (requires enormous field content). 
- **Hyperbolic internal spaces in mainstream physics:** Kaloper-March-Russell-Starkman-Trodden ([arXiv:hep-ph/0002001](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0002001v1), PRL 85 (2000) 928) used compact hyperbolic manifolds for an exponential hierarchy and altered KK spectra. Hyperbolicity buys **scales**, never SM values. This is the closest mainstream physics has come to origin-axiom's substrate class, and it never touched values.

**Lesson.** Chirality is the first gate (a representation-theoretic audit precedes all numerics), and the Candelas-Weinberg *shape* of value derivation — dynamics selects the scale — is the correct template even though its numbers failed.

## 5. String/Calabi-Yau: generation counting from topology, values displaced into moduli

The standard embedding gives generations = |chi|/2; the three-generation hunt (Tian-Yau chi = -6, and successors) is documented by the Oxford school ([overview](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/groups/mathematical-physics/research-areas/calabi-yau-manifolds-and-particle-physics), [three-generation models, arXiv:hep-th/9501131](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9501131)). **Structure from topology genuinely worked** (generation count, gauge group). But Yukawas and couplings depend on h^{1,1}+h^{2,1} moduli fields; the value problem was not solved, it was *renamed* vacuum selection, and became the landscape. **Lesson:** deriving structure from topology is achievable and respectable; the values migrate into a dynamical layer — exactly H0's claim — and the programme that refuses to model that layer concretely ends in a landscape, not a prediction.

## 6. Other "fermions from topology" lines

- **Topological geons.** Friedman-Sorkin: half-integral spin from spatial topology ([Semantic Scholar record](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0aa5f04447175d0a578a0ca80ff4f3c519edf7bf); year 1980 from memory, UNVERIFIED); geons can even violate spin-statistics ([arXiv:gr-qc/0101042](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0101042)). Structure only; kinematics, never values.
- **Hadley 4-geons.** Spinor transformation properties from non-time-orientable spacetime regions ([arXiv:gr-qc/0004029](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0004029)); claims QM itself emerges from 4-geon causal structure — a measurement-flavoured claim, but no constant was ever derived.
- **Lisi E8 (structure claim, killed).** Distler-Garibaldi ([arXiv:0905.2658](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2658), Commun. Math. Phys. 2010): no chiral theory — not even one SM generation — inside any real or complex form of E8. The clean model of a *pre-numerics structural audit*.
- **Skyrme model (the success).** Topology (baryon number as winding) plus an effective Lagrangian with two fitted constants; Adkins-Nappi-Witten, Nucl. Phys. B228 (1983) 552 ([ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1983NuPhB.228..552A)): static nucleon properties within ~30% after fitting f_pi and e. Everyone knew which numbers were fit and which predicted. **This is the only fully healthy specimen in the survey, and it is coupling-first by construction.**
- *(Historical frame, UNVERIFIED from memory: Kelvin's 1867 vortex-atom programme — atoms as knotted vortices — is the original "particles from knots" failure; it produced taxonomy, no dynamics, and died with the aether, while accidentally founding knot theory.)*

## 7. Hyperbolic 3-manifolds specifically — and the figure-eight knot

**Direct prior art: Asselmeyer-Maluga.** *Braids, 3-manifolds, elementary particles* ([arXiv:1910.09966](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09966), Symmetry 11(10):1298, 2019) and the exotic-smoothness line with Brans ([arXiv:1401.4816](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.4816)): via Thurston geometrization and 3-fold branched covers, **fermions are modeled as hyperbolic knot complements** and bosons as torus bundles; electric charge spectrum claimed from topology; the paper explicitly invokes the Bloch group, algebraic K-theory, and quaternionic trace fields — a striking overlap with origin-axiom's toolkit. Status: structure only (a WebFetch of the abstract confirms no masses/couplings/mixings are derived); the figure-eight knot is not treated specifically; published in a low-visibility venue; essentially zero community engagement, positive or negative. It is prior art for the *dictionary*, not for values, and it has never been subjected to (nor passed) a chirality/anomaly audit.

**Hard search for figure-eight-knot physics beyond Chern-Simons: null.** Targeted searches ("figure-eight knot" + Standard Model / fermion / gauge group / fine structure / dark matter / cosmology, minus Chern-Simons) return only the mathematics literature (e.g. [complex hyperbolic structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.7096), [geometric bounding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.08684)) and the quantum-gravity/quantum-invariant line ([volume conjecture QHI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16077), [3d gravity statistics from knots](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.10864), [Gukov's A-polynomial/CS work](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0306165)). **No published attempt to extract SM data from m004 or from any specific hyperbolic 3-manifold was found.** Origin-axiom's exhaustive internal nulls (B742-B792) therefore have no published competitor — and no published precedent to warn it.

## 8. Who attempted an explicit OBSERVER or MEASUREMENT coupling?

This is the sharpest question for H0, and the answer is: **almost nobody, and never in modern falsifiable form.**

| Programme | Observer/measurement element | Emitted values? | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eddington (1930s-40s) | Explicit: "selective subjectivism" — constants arise from the conditions of observation ([Kragh, arXiv:1510.04046](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.04046)) | Claimed 1/alpha = 137 exactly | Failed: apriorist, unfalsifiable, integer forced |
| Bit-string physics (Parker-Rhodes/Noyes) | Explicit: constants from combinatorics of discrete measurement acts ("counter firings") ([DOI:10.1007/BF00670503](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00670503)) | Integer 137, 2^127+136 | Dead: integers vs running constants; no SM structure |
| Brannen (2000s) | Explicit: Schwinger's **measurement algebra** — particles as idempotent measurement processes; circulant density operators give sqrt-mass spectra ([talk PDF](https://indico.phys.hawaii.edu/event/3/contributions/1662/attachments/943/1138/jpp06_DensMat.pdf); Koide context [HandWiki](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Physics:Koide_formula), [arXiv:1201.2067](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1201.2067)) | Yes: Koide relation (tau mass predicted 1981, holds to ~7e-6), neutrino-mass extension; but exact eta^2 = 1/2 and delta = 2/9 are simultaneously in tension with data | Never mainstream; mechanism unexplained; the single most interesting data point for H0 |
| Wheeler "participatory universe / it from bit" | Programmatic only (from memory, UNVERIFIED citation) | No | Slogan, not a mechanism |
| Hadley 4-geons | QM/measurement-context emergence claimed | No | Structure only |
| Bilson-Thompson, Furey, Gresnigt, Lisi, Asselmeyer-Maluga | **None** — all locate everything in the object | No | Stalled at structure |
| NCG spectral SM (Connes-Chamseddine) | Not an observer, but an explicit **coupling functional**: spectral action Tr f(D/Lambda) + RG running is where numbers enter ([arXiv:1208.1030](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.1030)) | Yes: Higgs 170 GeV (falsified), then 125 GeV after post-hoc sigma-field rescue | The pivotal precedent: a coupling layer CAN emit falsifiable numbers; the rescue cost it its credibility |

**Finding.** The two historical *explicit* observer-coupling programmes (Eddington, bit-string) are the worst failures in the survey — but they failed for identifiable, avoidable reasons (apriorism, integer-exactness, no scale), not because the coupling idea was tested and refuted. The two *mechanical* coupling layers (Skyrme's fitted effective couplings; NCG's spectral action + RG) are the only places in this entire literature where defensible numbers ever appeared. **H0's specific move — structure in the object, values in a declared, falsifiable observer-object coupling with scale/RG dynamics — has never actually been executed.** The graveyard constrains *how* to do it, not *whether* it can be done.

## 9. Design rules for B793 extracted from the graveyard

1. **Chirality gate first** (Witten; Distler-Garibaldi): a representation-theoretic audit — chiral, three generations, anomaly-free — precedes any numeric claim.
2. **Declare scale and scheme** (Eddington/Wyler/Atiyah/bit-string): every value is emitted at a stated mu in a stated scheme; matches to bare constants are protocol violations. Integer-exactness is a red flag, not a triumph.
3. **No radius = 1** (Wyler): every normalization must be dynamically selected (Candelas-Weinberg-style self-consistency is the template shape, even though its numbers failed).
4. **No post-hoc rescues** (NCG sigma field): pre-register the coupling ansatz family; a falsified ansatz dies. Count all degrees of freedom honestly.
5. **Score overdetermination, not hits** (Adkins-Nappi-Witten): fit k parameters to k observables, be judged only on the rest.
6. **Sibling negative controls** (origin-axiom's own B792 practice, absent from every failed programme surveyed): run everything on m003 and other census manifolds; base-rate calibrate.
7. **Reproducibility of numerics** (Atiyah): every computed number ships with the computation.
8. **Avoid isolation** (Asselmeyer-Maluga, Brannen, ANPA): structure-only dictionaries in low-visibility venues attract zero engagement and cannot fail honestly.

## FAILURE MODES
Eight recurring failure modes define the graveyard. (1) Structure without dynamics: Bilson-Thompson/Smolin-Wan derived quantum numbers and chirality labels but never a Hamiltonian, mass spectrum, or interaction strength; the programme's own 2012 review lists these as open and they were never closed. (2) Algebra selected to fit: Furey/Gresnigt results are weakest exactly where algebraic procedures are chosen to reproduce known physics rather than being forced by the structure (documented criticism); the weak SU(2) sector and anomaly cancellation remain unfinished after a decade. (3) Numerology without mechanism: Wyler (arbitrary radius=1), Eddington (forced integers 136→137), Parker-Rhodes/Noyes (integer 137 vs running alpha(mu)), Atiyah (undefined Todd function, irreproducible numerics) — all located values in a mathematical object with no coupling to measurement scale or RG scheme, and all are dead. (4) No-go collisions discovered too late: Witten's chirality theorem killed pure KK value-derivation; Distler-Garibaldi's representation-theoretic audit killed E8 — structure claims must pass the chirality/generation/anomaly gate before any numerics. (5) Value problem displaced, not solved: Calabi-Yau compactification turned 'derive the Yukawas' into 'select the vacuum/moduli' — the landscape is the value problem renamed. (6) Post-hoc rescue after falsification: the NCG spectral model's 170 GeV Higgs was falsified, then repaired by un-truncating a sigma field to hit 125 GeV; the mechanism survived but its predictive credibility did not. (7) Sociological isolation: Asselmeyer-Maluga (low-visibility venues, zero engagement), Brannen (never mainstream-published), ANPA bit-string (closed community) — single-author programmes without external audit converge on unfalsifiable elaboration. (8) The mirror success clarifies all of these: the Skyrme model survives because it never claimed values from topology — topology fixed discrete structure, two fitted couplings then overdetermined many observables at ~30%, and everyone knew which numbers were fit and which were predicted.
