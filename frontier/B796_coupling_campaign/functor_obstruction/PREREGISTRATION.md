### C4 — THE DIMENSIONLESS SECTOR: theorem, or missing mechanism?

This is where θ_QCD lived, and B1009 concedes it is *"the one sector the weight
ledger lets the object speak to."* **B813** refuted the CS↔θ_QCD dictionary on
three type mismatches — **kind** (a computed invariant cannot be a free

**The question this cell must answer and must not dodge:** is *"a computed
invariant cannot be a free coupling"* a **theorem**, or a **demand for a
mechanism**? If it is a theorem it forbids every framework that determines a
free parameter — i.e. it forbids any theory of everything, which would be a very
strong claim requiring very strong support. If it is a demand, the refusal is
contingent.
