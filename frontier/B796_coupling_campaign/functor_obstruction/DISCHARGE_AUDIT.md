# THE DISCHARGE AUDIT — which hypotheses does the derivation theorem actually consume?

**cc3, 2026-08-11.** Gate 5-Q. **No new mathematics; this is bookkeeping against
banked text.** For cc to adjudicate and number as it sees fit.

## The question nobody had asked

`THE_CLAIM.md` states the derivation theorem as **hypotheses → conclusions**:

> *"The six axioms A1–A6 and one bit A7 … plus **five** typed external data: two
> 𝔽₂ bits (time's arrow; chirality), one scale, one Lie type, and one
> rank-closing VEV direction."*

and then tables **nine conclusions**. **The two lists were assembled
independently.** No document asks, hypothesis by hypothesis: **which conclusion
discharges this?**

## Method

For each hypothesis, search all fifteen arcs behind the nine conclusions
(B892 · B992 · B862 · B864 · B991 · B863 · B994 · B978 · B884 · B987 · B897 ·
B928 · B303 · B811 · B1012) for a genuine **consumption** — the hypothesis used
as an input — distinguished from a **mention of its absence** and from
**list-membership**. Checked against **full FINDINGS**, not one-line claims.

### The scale — every apparent hit is a statement of its ABSENCE

- **B991**: *"the NORMALISATION is **not derivable** BY THE FORM OF THE EQUATIONS,
  since a homogeneous system **cannot single out a scale** on its solution cone."*
- **B1012**: *"One geometric property **deletes the only term that could carry the
  scale**."*
- **B987**: merely **lists** it — *"an external input exactly like chirality, rank
  reduction and scale."*

**Not one conclusion takes a length as input.** Every conclusion is weight-0, and
one of them (B811 · B1012) is the theorem that **no dimensionful quantity can be
produced at all.**

### Time's arrow — both apparent hits are FALSE POSITIVES

- **B864**'s match is *"the derivation **arrow**"* — a derivation step.
- **B978**'s match is *"three **times**, in one day"*.

**No conclusion consumes a time direction.** None of the nine is about dynamics,
irreversibility, or an arrow.

## What this means, stated carefully

> **The nine stated conclusions consume THREE of the five declared external data.**
> **The structural theorem costs three, not five.**

**And the mismatch is structural, not sloppy.** THE_CLAIM's hypothesis list is the
**full closing budget** — what B1000's five closings require to turn the object
into a *physical description*. Its conclusion list is **only the structural
sector**. The document prices the whole budget against the structural result.

**The honest form:**

> *Three inputs buy the SM gauge structure and one generation's multiplet
> content. Five closings buy a physical description — and the remaining two, the
> scale and time's arrow, have **no stated conclusion yet**.*

**That is a reduction, and it is obtained by bookkeeping.** It is also, read the
other way, an **honest debt**: two declared inputs are paid for outputs the
programme has not yet stated.

## Scope, and the ways this could be wrong

- **Method is search-plus-read, not proof.** An implicit consumption that names
  neither the hypothesis nor a synonym would be missed. **The table is a
  falsifiable claim: name a conclusion that consumes the scale or the arrow, and
  the row is wrong.**
- **INERT ≠ unnecessary.** Time's arrow is one of B1000's five closings and is
  needed for a physical description. It is inert **for the nine conclusions as
  stated**, which is a claim about *this theorem*, not about the programme.
- **The A7 row is the softest.** A7 is the φ bit and the object is selected by the
  golden word; that is a chain, not a cited consumption.
