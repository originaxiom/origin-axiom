## What is worth recording regardless

The two ends differ **exactly** in whether the quantized half of the action
survives, and the arithmetic tracks the split:

- the **hyperbolic** end kills the quantized term, and its boundary torus is a
  CM torus of conductor **4** in the **being** field — which the companion arc
  shows *is* the congruence level;
- the **spherical** end keeps the quantized term, and what it keeps carries
  **5** — the **hearing** prime.

Two ends, two fields, two primes, and the quantized sector alive at exactly one
of them. Whatever else it means, **the object's quantized structure is not
symmetric between its faces**, and that asymmetry sits on the same seam as
everything else that separated today: being/hearing, the two clocks, B736's
