# PHASE 3 — THE EXOTICS ARE NOT A FALSIFICATION RISK

cc3, 2026-08-10. Gate 5-Q. Structure only; no measured quantity.

## The question

`27 = 16 + 10 + 1` leaves **twelve exotic states per generation** (L134), and
they are not observed. Phase 2b showed this question also carries the **rank**
question and the **re-anchoring**, so it is the campaign's last and heaviest item.
A framework predicting twelve unobserved states must make them heavy or be
refuted.

## The cubic, decomposed

The E₆-invariant cubic on the 27, with `27 = 16(+1) + 10(−2) + 1(+4)`. Requiring
U(1) neutrality over all ten unordered triples leaves exactly two:

```
        16·16·10        charge  0    ALLOWED   — the SM Yukawa
        10·10·1         charge  0    ALLOWED   — the exotics' mass term
        16·16·1         charge +6    forbidden
        1·1·1           charge +12   forbidden
        (six others)                 forbidden
```

**The cubic is `16·16·10 + 10·10·1`** — matching B884's exact computation.

## The sort — and a correction to my own first pass

**My first pass was wrong and the error is worth stating**, because it is the
one that makes this question look fatal when it is not:

> **The 10 of SO(10) is a REAL representation.** As *fermions* it is therefore
> **vector-like** — it can be given a mass without breaking any Standard Model
> symmetry. Only the **16** is chiral and protected. I initially treated the 10's
> doublets as "the Higgs, which must stay light", conflating a **fermion** in the
> matter 27 with a **scalar** in a separate 27. Doublet–triplet splitting is a
> question about the scalar, not about these fermions.

Sorted correctly:

| states | what | mechanism | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **10** | the fermionic **10** (3 + 3̄ + 2 + 2) | real rep ⟹ vector-like; lifted by **⟨1⟩·10·10**, the cubic's own term | **LIFTABLE — mechanism internal** |
| 1 | the E₆ singlet **1** | `1·1·1` forbidden (charge +12) | no cubic mass, but an **SM singlet** — no gauge charge |
| 1 | **ν_R** inside the 16 | `16·16·1` forbidden (charge +6) | no cubic mass, but an **SM singlet**; Majorana mass needs extra structure |

## The verdict

> **Ten of the twelve are liftable by the framework's own cubic. The remaining
> two are Standard Model singlets** — uncharged under SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), so
> "unobserved" carries none of the weight it would for an unobserved coloured
> state.

**The exotics are not a live falsification risk.** What survives is the **ν_R
Majorana mass**, and that is unsolved in *every* E₆ model — the 27 has no
126-equivalent, so ν_R mass always requires higher representations or
non-renormalisable operators. By the campaign's own test that is a **(b)**: a
problem no framework solves, not a wall of this one.

## What this closes across the campaign

Phase 2b unified rank, the re-anchoring, and the exotics into one problem. That
problem now resolves as:

- **the fermionic 10 is liftable** — the truncation to the chiral core has a
  mechanism, internal, in the cubic B884 computed exactly;
- **therefore the re-anchoring has a candidate** — B864 said it *"does not derive
  the re-anchoring"*, and this is what a derivation would run through;
- **therefore the rank question resolves** — chiral matter follows from lifting
  the 10, and rank 4 follows from chiral matter.

**One mechanism, three ledger items.** It is not a proof: `⟨1⟩ ≠ 0` is an
assumption, and no arc computes the resulting spectrum. But it converts three
open items with five impossibility proofs between them into **one assumption with
a named term**.

## What remains, stated exactly

1. **`⟨1⟩ ≠ 0` is assumed**, not derived. Whether the E₆ singlet takes a VEV is a
   dynamical question the framework does not address.
2. **ν_R Majorana mass** — standard E₆ problem, standard solutions all external.
3. **Doublet–triplet splitting** — still real, still external, but it is a
   question about the **scalar** sector and does **not** bear on the fermionic
   exotics, which is what this phase was about.
4. **No mechanism removes an anomalous gauge U(1)** (Phase 2b) — unchanged, and
   it is the sharper of the two remaining gaps.

**Not claimed:** that the framework predicts the exotics' masses, or that ⟨1⟩ ≠ 0
is motivated. Only that the twelve unobserved states are **not** an internal
contradiction: ten have a mechanism and two are invisible to the gauge group.
