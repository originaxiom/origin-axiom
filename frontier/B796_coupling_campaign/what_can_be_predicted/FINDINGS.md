This is a theorem, not a shortage, and it traces to the axioms: **C4** puts the
word on a geometric carrier, geometrisation gives a metric defined only up to
the curvature normalisation, and hyperbolic geometry is **exactly
scale-covariant**. So every quantity the object owns is weight-0 or carries a

content in the level `k`, which the object cannot even see (CS = 0).

> **The framework cannot predict a number with units. Ever. By theorem.**

**The programme's shortage of predictions is not a limit of the principle. It is
