## The mechanism (cc3's, own-verified)

**The orbit theorem:** a G-invariant is constant on G-orbits, so it cannot select a point of one — and
exactly **two escapes**, exhaustive (any successful selector either fails G-invariance or is invariant for a
smaller group): **(1) shrink the group** — pass to H < G whose orbits break up; **(2) add non-invariant
structure** — supply the point from outside (= observer-paid, definitionally).

**The two halves of the adelic observer are the two escapes:**

- **Archimedean (orientation):** G = ℤ/2 generated by the mirror. **Escape (1) is unavailable** — B1163
  proves the mirror is a *genuine automorphism* (amphichirality), not a frame artifact, so there is no
  smaller group to descend to. Only escape (2) remains. **Cost: a full bit, unavoidable.**
- **Finite (the rank-closing VEV):** G = F₄, transitive on frames over ℝ. **Escape (1) IS available** —
  pass from F₄(ℝ) to F₄(ℤ), whose orbits are **finite** (Krutelevich, **cited** — the L162-gated integral
  theory; not re-derived here). **Cost: a finite label, not a continuum point.**

> **The observer is adelic because escape (1) is available at the finite places and unavailable at the
> archimedean one.** This upgrades the B1164 price-reconciliation from a *description* (where the closings
> happened to land) to a *derivation* (why they must land there).
