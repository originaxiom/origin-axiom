**T-05** — *Uncertainty model.* Per quantity sigma = sqrt(rel_err^2 + ALLOW^2) in natural-log space, ALLOW = 0.10 ("a 10% theory allowance"), ratios combine both masses' relative errors in quadrature. Status: CHOSEN (B1741; unchanged B1811, B1814, B1825). Where: stepb_core.py ALLOW = 0.10, sigma_from_relative; SIGMA_ONLY.json "allow": 0.1; B1825 prereg §6 item 3. **T-08** — *Phase and sign content.* |J| is scored; its sign is never scored; no other CP phase, Majorana phase or mixing phase is graded. Status: CHOSEN. ## B. Thresholds and the statistic

**T-09** — *Chi-square thresholds.* Support: chi2_ten < 23.21 (chi2_10's 0.99 quantile); chi2_nine < 21.67; chi2_six < 16.75 (B1735's precision bar); CKM-leg clause chi2_4 < 13.28 (0.99 of chi2_4); the leg's top-posterior window "within 13.86 of the least" (posterior density within 2^-10). Excludes: a true answer sitting outside the 99% ball; with it any frame whose best address is farther than that. Consequence: S is a yes/no on a fixed ball; with 10 numbers the ball already allows about 3 sigma per number combined. Alternatives: chi2_nine < 21.67 read-out; the 16.75 six-only bar of earlier arcs.

**T-10** — *Support is a minimum over all discrete choices and continuous tau.* S asks whether any single row (line, direction, radius, reading, tau anywhere) reaches the ball at c = 3. Status: CHOSEN. Where: B1825 prereg §2 "S, support". Excludes: nothing by itself (it is generous); the price is paid in X2/K (T-19, T-21). Consequence: the more the menu is widened, the easier S and the harder X2 (Bar-FA S0 "A larger, finer space makes support easier to find and specificity harder to earn").

**T-11** — *The frame on continuous tau is DEFINED as an interpolation of the census.* Biquadratic Lagrange polynomial in the natural logs of the nine positive outputs (J signed) through a 3x3 stencil; nodes with undefined mixing or non-finite value scored at the node alone. Status: CHOSEN (definition). Where: B1825 prereg §2 "τ is continuous (the frame on continuous τ is DEFINED as the census interpolated …)". Excludes: sharp features narrower than a cell: isolated zeros and J's zero curves "where interpolation fails"; node-only scoring misses 9.2% of planted worlds vs 0.4% interpolated. Consequence: resolution = planted miss rate 0.21% (certification), gate 1%.

**T-12** — *Structural-zero floor.* An output below 1e-12 at a point or stencil node is a "structural zero" (m1 = 0 for rank-2 coupling; J = 0 for real CKM) and the world is planted on its remaining outputs or dropped; null worlds with such entries yield "no world of our kind". Status: CHOSEN ("The floor 10⁻¹² is CHOSEN"). Where: B1825 prereg §2 X2, §4 floor rule; B1814 card "resolved when all six … exceed 10⁻¹²". Excludes: a true answer containing a genuinely tiny but nonzero hierarchy below the floor (e.g. 1e-13 ratio) and any answer where a zero of one of the ten is exact. Consequence: R and t = -5/288 are "structurally dead for our world" (B1825 §4 census structure); the physical-choice restriction raises p2 by 1/(1-pi), pi = 0.214.

## The five conventions that most plausibly make a true answer unrecognisable

1. **T-20 (one rigid address):** all ten numbers must be hit at a single line, direction, radius, reading and tau; the record's decisive miss is exactly that the six and the four fit separately (1.36, 0.35) but not together (291.4), so a true answer with the up and down sectors at different loci cannot register (P-555 untested).
