# B1443 — ADDENDUM 1 (2026-10-01): the cubic of an orbit's Higgs classes has no group to live in; a Higgs value is a position on a curve

This arc left, as the first candidate for what fixes the Higgs values, a cubic joining an orbit's three Higgs
classes: character-allowed on the root's tower, value not computed.

**It is not a bulk product** (B1444 §8, `no_bulk_cubic.py`). On a level h²(M; k) = 0 and h³ = 0; the pairwise
products of an orbit's Higgs classes vanish, because each lies in a line read on the boundary torus by the
difference of two slopes and the slopes of an orbit are equal (checked with the cocycles on the 16 orbits of the
root's three-fold cover, the 16 of −LLLR's and the 80 of the root's five-fold cover, up and down couplings); the
triple product is then defined and lands in H²(M; k) = 0. So the characters allow the term and the bulk has no
group for it.

**What a Higgs value is has an object instead** (B1444): the extension class of a background is the tangent of a
curve of flat connections through it, and a value is a position on that curve. On the root's three-fold cover the
curve is rational, with κ − 2 = w(w − 1) and m + 1/m − 2 = −w(w² − w + 4); the three members of a deck orbit lie on
three curves permuted by the deck transformation, with the same functions on each. Nothing computed selects a
point, and nothing computed distinguishes the three members.

This arc's tensor is unchanged. Lead L235 (a) is closed; the lead is restated there and continued as L236.
