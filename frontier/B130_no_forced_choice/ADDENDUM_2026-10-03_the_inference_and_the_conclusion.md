# B130 — Addendum (2026-10-03, B1463): the inference was invalid; the conclusion holds by a better argument

"Empty κ-elimination ideal ⟹ κ free ⟹ no discrete value" is not a valid inference: V(x(x − 1), xk) is a line with k
free and an isolated regular point, and its k-elimination is the zero ideal (the audit lane's ACT_REGISTER AR4; the SM
seat's sm:B1521 C3; B1463 on main). The conclusion of this arc — no forced choice in the trace-ring invariants, no
isolated fixed point with a discrete κ — **holds at m = 2, 3, 4 by a valid argument**: at m = 2 the fixed ideal is
generated by two polynomials in three variables (Krull), and at m = 2, 3, 4 its primary decomposition has every
component one-dimensional (2, 2 and 4 curve components; Sage/Singular, recorded in B1463). The tombstone's "isolated
points" with κ = −6, −158/21 are singular points of the curve components, as the tombstone judged by a different tell.
For m ≥ 5 the claim rests on this arc's numerics and is scoped so; the method note's "empty ⟹ continuous ⟹ no choice"
should read "empty elimination is necessary, not sufficient; decide by components".
