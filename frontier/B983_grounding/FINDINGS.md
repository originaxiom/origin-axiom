## The rule that does the work

> **Before writing "the object does not supply X", find X on the ladder. If X is not there, X has
> not been checked, and the honest sentence is "not checked" — not "absent".**

That converts *"we don't have X"* from an impression into **a claim with a citation**, which is
the same standard the programme already applies to positives. The corpus's own rule — *an unearned
negative costs as much as an overclaim* — finally has an instrument.

## Scope, honestly

This is **governance, not mathematics**. It proves nothing about the object. What it does is make
the next five errors of this class cost a lookup instead of a session — and the day's evidence is
that the class is the dominant one: **five in one day from one seat, one from the audit seat, one
from a gate.**

**The ladder's 30 rungs are a snapshot, not a theorem.** Its BLIND grades in particular are claims
about *the repo's coverage*, measured by marker scan and reading, and cc3's own recall control
(49% on known pairs) says such scans under-count. A rung graded BLIND may yet have an arc nobody
found — which is exactly why the grade means **"not checked"** and never **"absent."**
