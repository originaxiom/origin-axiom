## The chain — five links

1. **`t² = t − 1`.** m004's holonomy parameter is a primitive **sixth** root of unity, so
   `ω := t²` is a primitive **cube** root.
2. **`Φ₃(ω) = Φ₃(ω²) = 0`.** B1200's polynomial is reached **from the holonomy**, not from the
   partition function where B1200 met it. Two independent routes to the same `Φ₃`.
3. **`u ↦ u²` swaps the two roots** and is an involution on them — B1200's linking map.
4. **That swap is complex conjugation** on `ℚ(√−3)`: `ω² = conj(ω)`.
5. **Conjugation is orientation reversal.** `conj(t) = 1 − t`, which satisfies the **same relator**,
   so Galois acts on the character variety and conjugating a representation reverses orientation.

**⟹ MIRROR = complex conjugation = the `Φ₃`-root swap = `c`.**

**CONTROLS.** `u ↦ u³` is **not** the swap (it is the identity on cube roots), and `c` fixes `ℚ`
but not `ω`. The test therefore distinguishes maps rather than passing everything.

## Link 6 — the consequence for Paper IV's escape dichotomy

Identifying `G` is not decoration: it makes Escape (i) — *shrink the group* — **testable rather
than assumed available**, and it fails.

> If `G` acts transitively on `Ω` with `|Ω| > 1` and **`|G|` is prime**, then every subgroup is
> `G` (orbit unchanged) or trivial (every function is invariant, so no invariance is retained).

The mirror is that situation at `|G| = 2`. **So for this datum the two escapes are not
alternatives: a selector must fail to be invariant under complex conjugation outright** — it must
know *which* root, and no invariant of the manifold supplies that.

**This STRENGTHENS the no-go** by removing a route the paper previously listed as open.

**CONTROL:** vacuity tracks **primality**, not the number 2 — `|G| = 4, 6, 8, 9` each admit a
proper nontrivial subgroup with strictly smaller orbits, so Escape (i) there is a genuine
alternative and the check distinguishes rather than passing on every group.
