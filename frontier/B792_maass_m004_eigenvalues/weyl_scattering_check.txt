Reading: 'residual' = N_disc - (Weyl + scattering correction).
If eigenvalues were MISSING, the residual would step DOWNWARD by
an integer each miss and drift increasingly negative. A residual
that stays small and smooth (absorbing the O(T log T) cusp terms)
supports completeness of the found spectrum.
