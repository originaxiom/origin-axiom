- **GAP1 gains a [v1.60] rule.** An identification is EARNED BY PREDICTION within a frame F when six conditions hold:
  1. *fixed before*: F, every choice, every fitted input and the identification are on a sealed frame card;
  2. *held out*: each predicted quantity is on THE_FINAL_ACTS_BAR's V list, untouched by F's building, its prior looks in
     m_V;
  3. *passed*: each is PASSED by V (inside the stated 68% band; p_V < 0.01/m_V against F's pipeline re-run over its whole
     menu);
  4. *two at least*: two held-out quantities, not functions of one another, pass on the same card;
  5. *verified*: main recomputes; a hostile referee and a third opinion find no fault;
  6. *scoped and revocable*: "EARNED BY PREDICTION within F, by arc X", reverting to UNEARNED on a later NEGATIVE of the
     same frame and identification.
- **The grade.** A value matched through an earned identification is **PREDICTED**, not a READING, and counts as
  predicted; values used to build F stay FITTED or CHOSEN and count as inputs (P-418's vector); DERIVED stays separate.
- **What stands.** GAP1's sentence (no frame is derived from §1–§4); FK11's unearnability **by derivation** within F-HE and
  F-CI (FK11's status cell gains a pointer). No other status cell changes.
- **Drafting.** The six conditions are main's drafting of the owner's YES — strict on purpose (two passes, scoped,
  revocable); the owner may revise them.
