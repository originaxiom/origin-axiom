## B21's mechanism, made exact

B21 says the half-step is anti-Poisson *"because it is orientation-reversing"*. Since a map
preserving the invariant transforms the bracket by `det(DF)`, **the sign of the determinant IS the
statement**:

    det DT  = −1   →  the half-step is ANTI-Poisson
    det DT² = +1   →  the A-level map is Poisson
    det DS  = +1   →  for all four sign pairs — which is WHY the quotient descends at all

All three verified directly on every generator pair of the bracket the invariant determines,
`{x,y} = 2z − 2xy` and cyclic — not inferred from the determinant rule alone.

not the Markov form `x² + y² + z² − xyz`.** A first pass used the latter and the `T`-invariance check
failed immediately. *Two normalisations of one name — the `E1` convention collision the corpus
