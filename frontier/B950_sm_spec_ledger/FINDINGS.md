## 2. THE FIRST FRUIT — an overstatement in our own record

Writing the gauge row surfaced it immediately.

**B892 banks:** *"z(x₁, y*) = su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)³ EXACTLY. Two measurements of the
object's own superselection charges take E₆ to the Standard Model algebra, skipping SU(5)."*

**Its own arithmetic:** dim **14**, derived **11**, centre **3**.

> **su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)³ has dimension 14. The Standard Model gauge algebra has
> dimension 12: su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1), one abelian factor, not three.**

**The mathematics is right and is not disturbed.** 8 + 3 + 3 = 14 is exactly what B892
computed and locked. **The sentence wrapped around it overstates by two abelian factors.**

**Why this is not a refutation.** Extra U(1)s are completely standard in GUT descent —
SO(10) → SM leaves U(1)_{B−L}, and rank-6 E₆ reaching rank-4 SM *must* shed rank somewhere.
The result stands as a real structural fact. What changes is its distance from the target:
**the chain is two steps from the SM, not zero**, and both steps are now named:

1. **which u(1), or which combination, is hypercharge** (the `gauge_dict` obstacle, 48/79
   banked — B949's table); and
2. **what breaks the other two**, and at what cost.

**This is exactly the failure mode the directive predicted**: not knowing the target
precisely enough to notice that "lands on the Standard Model algebra" had been banked for
something that is the SM algebra ⊕ u(1)².

Section B of the ledger separates what accepted physics **forces** from what it merely
**measures**. One entry stands out:

> **The hypercharges are the SM's only computed value-level structure** — fixed (up to
> normalisation) by anomaly cancellation as a Diophantine system, given the group and the
> rep structure.

The object supplies a **rank-3 abelian sector**. So the question *"do the object's own
charges satisfy the SM's anomaly conditions, and does Y fall out?"* is

- **value-level but not value-matching** — anomaly cancellation is a set of integer
  equations, not a measured number — so **the firewall permits it**;
- aimed at the one row where accepted physics *derives* rather than measures, so a result
  there is intelligible to a physicist without any crossing; and
- **the natural successor to B892**, whose §4 already reports a hypercharge fit
  reproducing "at rank 3" without deriving anything from it.
