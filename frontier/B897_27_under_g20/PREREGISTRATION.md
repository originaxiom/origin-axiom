## The question

G₂₀ = su(3)_c ⊕ su(3)′ ⊕ su(2)′ ⊕ u(1) is the algebra generated by all three
frames' breakings together (banked; verified on this build in B892 — closure in
one round, dim 20, derived 19, center 1). The coexistence question: when one
observer holds all three perspectives at once, does the resulting algebra
organize the 27's matter with **generation-shaped** multiplet structure —
su(3)′ acting as a native flavor symmetry replicating SM-type multiplets — or
does it not?

## The operation (non-trivial, MB12-checked)

Decompose the 27 (B883's exact integer build, rep27) into joint isotypic
blocks under the commuting pair (su(3)_c, su(3)′) ⊂ G₂₀, using the exact
Casimir operators of each factor on the 27 (the su(3)_c Casimir is banked with
spectrum {0×9, 4/9×18} — B895; the su(3)′ Casimir is computed the same way
from the verified G₂₀ generators). Blocks are labeled by the pair of Casimir
eigenvalues with their joint multiplicities; where needed, the su(2)′ Casimir
refines the third label. The operation is non-trivial: it can produce many
inequivalent block tables (the 27 could split with su(3)′ acting only by
singlets, only by mixed non-replicating pairs, or by flavor-triplet blocks).

## The two-outcome criteria (each can pass; each can fail)

Let the block table be the list of (c, f, m) = (su(3)_c Casimir label,
su(3)′ Casimir label, total dimension) joint blocks.

- **OUTCOME A — GENERATION-SHAPED**: there exists at least one joint block
  where a *nontrivial* su(3)′ action (f ≠ 0, i.e. the su(3)′ factor acts by a
  3-dimensional irrep on the flavor index) multiplies a fixed su(3)_c type —
  concretely, a block of dimension m = 3·d where d is the dimension of a
  single su(3)_c multiplet (d ∈ {1, 3}) appearing as ONE joint isotypic (three
  copies of the same color type bound into one su(3)′ triplet). This is the
  replication signature: three generations of one matter type as a single
  flavor multiplet.
- **OUTCOME B — NOT GENERATION-SHAPED**: no such block — every joint block
  with f ≠ 0 pairs the flavor action with *distinct* color types (no
  replication), or su(3)′ acts trivially on all matter blocks.
- **UNSTABLE**: Casimir eigenvalue gaps < 10⁻⁶ at working precision (mod-p
  cross-check at two primes disagrees), or the joint multiplicities do not
  tile 27 — recompute exactly before any verdict.

Both outcomes are live: A fails if su(3)′ never replicates a color type
(e.g. purely off-diagonal trichotomy pairing); B fails exactly when A holds.
The criterion references only computed structure — no SM values, no
magnitudes. Gate 5 untouched either way.

## The disclosed prior (B890's lesson: state it, then let the cell decide)

From the E₆ ⊃ SU(3)³ trichotomy (27 = (3,3̄,1) ⊕ (1,3,3̄) ⊕ (3̄,1,3)) and the
suspicion that su(3)_c and su(3)′ sit conjugate to two of the three SU(3)³

(1,3)-with-multiplicity-3, (3̄,1)-with-multiplicity-3 — in which case the
9-dim (1,3)-type block (three color-singlet su(3)′-triplet copies) would
decide A vs B on the su(2)′ refinement: if the three copies carry the SAME
su(2)′ type, that block IS generation-shaped (A); if they carry distinct
su(2)′ types, it is not (B). **The prior leans A via the singlet sector**
(confidence low — B890 taught us the prior can lose).
