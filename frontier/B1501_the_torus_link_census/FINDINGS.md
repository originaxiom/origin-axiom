## 1. What is sealed

- **The question.** B1500 made each cusp point's chirality a local datum that only the local physics at the point can supply. In
  M-theory that physics is a G₂ conical singularity whose ADE locus, for a cusp point, is a cone over the cusp torus. Which such
  singularities exist among the cones over S⁶, S³ × S³, ℂP³ and the flag manifold F₁,₂, modulo finite groups of automorphisms? Of what
  ADE type, and what torus shape?
- **The reduction, proved at seal.**
  - A non-trivial automorphism fixes points or surfaces of the link.
  - Single elements find every torus locus.
  - On the equal-rank links (S⁶, ℂP³, F₁,₂) no left translation fixes a torus, because every fixed component has positive Euler
    characteristic.
  - So tori can come only from S³ × S³ and from the flag manifold's order-3 twisted automorphisms.
- **The predictions.**
  - P1: every torus locus is A-type. Prior YES, about 90%.
  - P2: every torus is hexagonal. Prior YES, about 80%.
  - P3: per-link read-outs. Prior that F₁,₂ carries a torus locus: about 70%.
