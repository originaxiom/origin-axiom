## What this says about the lift question the owner asked

**3d-3d is not a lift and cannot become one.** It takes a **3-manifold** to a **3d** theory — that is
what the name means. Obtaining a **4d** theory from the same 6d `(2,0)` theory requires a **Riemann
surface** (class S), not a 3-manifold. **The dimensional question is untouched by this route**, so
"use 3d-3d to reach 3+1" is not a strategy that can succeed as posed.
