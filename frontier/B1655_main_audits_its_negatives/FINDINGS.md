## 1. The answer

**The arithmetic is not the problem.** Every sealed instrument the auditors re-ran reproduced byte for byte, B1609–B1630
already had (B1631), and the independent routes agree (B1654's D(τ) by a third quadrature, its cusp constant, B1645's
half-bound algebra on 20,000 random isotropic triplets, B1651's group theory, B1611's character table). **What was wrong
is what the record said about the numbers:**

1. **Five features were booked as failures.** B1644's NEGATIVE is a failed |q|-suppression guess; what it found, an
   exactly massless first generation forced by Jacobi, is the pattern a light first generation needs (its own §3 says
   so). B1642's "the zero modes give no flavour structure" was its addendum's widening of a failed symmetric assumption;
   the coupling is rank two with an exact massless direction, the base of B1644–B1653. B1635's failed cell is the
   golden-spectrum discovery. B1628's NEGATIVE is main's failed expectation that data exclude TM2; TM1 passes at 0.85σ.
   B1603's failed cells say generation-shaped readings are widespread.
2. **τ = ω was retired on a clause with no cell.** B1617's "near ω the degeneracy splits by powers of ε … quasi-degenerate"
   computed no spectrum near ω. Its order-48 residual included the inner automorphisms, which on the owner's ruled branch
   are not moves; there the residual at ω is cyclic of order 12 with three distinct charges (B1634), which is the input
   the near-fixed-point mechanism needs, not its refutation. The six public refresh blocks and the outside-review page
   (`docs/THE_DERIVED_STRUCTURE_FOR_REVIEW.md`) still said "degenerate at every weight … and quasi-degenerate near it".
   And the fibre frame's Higgs directions tested as vacua were all real (B1644's 400, B1646's units); the isotropic ones
   were never tested as vacua.
3. **Restrictions were worded as walls.** B1646's "never between" is a statement about a quartic potential: one octic
   invariant puts all 40 minima at isotropy defect 0.447, and the zero modes' own one-loop term (λ₂ = −0.02, g = 0.1)
   puts all 40 at m = 0.323. It had risen into GENESIS FK9 as "every 'which' comes out free". B1615's "out of reach" and
   B1616's "fixes no neutrino mass" hold for one irreducible at a time along four chosen elements. B1620's "reduces none
   of the 13" means none compatible with the data (2916 of 3249 pairs do reduce the count). B1651's "the puncture is not
   forced" holds for a point or density Higgs, and its own addendum shows a (0, 1) Higgs carrying a parity is forced
   there. B1652's "no frame survives" means none is *shown* to satisfy every demand, which D3, D4 and D8 — computed
   positively for no frame — made foregone.
4. **Corrections reached the claim line and stopped.** GENESIS FK14 still read B1602's −LLRLRLRR carrier, withdrawn by
   B1604 and contradicted by B1605 (GENESIS never cited B1605). B1637's addendum never reached THEOREM_REGISTRY. B1653's

   B1642 and B1644–B1646 carry its conditions and never wrote them into their verdict files. CLOSED_DOORS printed
   B1632's refuted door.
5. **Cells that could not fail were shown as tests** (most disclosed in their preregistrations; the "every cell holds"
   stamps gave them test weight): B1600's lemma; B1607 C3b, C3c; B1610 H1; B1615 C3 and C4's clause 2; B1616 N3; B1618's

   B1642 R1; B1645's four unreachable tolerances (the pass direction); B1646 D1–D5; B1649 K1, K2; B1651 U3a; B1653 U2–U5;
