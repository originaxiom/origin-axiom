## What this document is, and the limitation it does not hide

The prediction register was written with its **status column already filled in** —
by a seat that knew the experimental record. That is post-hoc, and post-hoc
wording is the failure mode where a falsifier gets quietly shaped until it can
absorb whatever nature did.

**A literal blind test is not available to this seat.** I know that neutrinos
oscillate and that 0νββ has not been seen. **No seal can make me not know it,
and this document does not pretend otherwise.**

What a seal *can* do is fix the **wording** before the verdicts exist, so the
wording cannot be tuned afterwards. And there is one judgement here that is
**genuinely record-independent**:

> **Is the falsifier mechanically applicable by a hostile reader who does not
> know what I meant?**

That is a property of the sentence, not of nature. It can be audited blind, and
it is what this document commits to.

**Split:** Phase A (this file) = falsifier + **sharpness**. Phase B = **status**.
Phase B may not reword Phase A; it cites this file's hash.

## The sharpness scale, fixed here so Phase B cannot move it

| grade | meaning |
|---|---|
| **S1** | Names a **physical observation** with a stated threshold. A reader with the data applies it without asking me anything. |
| **S2** | Names a physical observation, **contingent on a discovery that may never occur**. Sharp if the thing appears; silent otherwise. |
| **S3** | Names a **state of theory or of the literature**, not an observation. Cannot be settled by a detector. |
| **S4** | **Defective** — contains an undefined term, a missing scale, or a clause that retracts it. Cannot be applied as written. |

## The eight

### P1 — θ_QCD = 0

> **REFUTED IF:** a nonzero neutron electric dipole moment is measured, at a
> value implying the strong CP phase is not zero.

**Sharpness: S4 — defective.** The framework predicts `θ_QCD = 0` (via CS = 0).
The *measurable* quantity is **θ̄ = θ_QCD + arg det M_q** — the QCD angle plus the
quark mass matrix phase. **A nonzero nEDM does not refute `θ_QCD = 0`**; it is
consistent with `θ_QCD = 0` and `arg det M_q ≠ 0`. The falsifier as written
targets a quantity the framework does not predict.

**This is a structural feature of the observable, not an experimental fact, and
it is why the grade is fixed here rather than in Phase B.** To become S1 the
claim must either add "and the quark mass phase vanishes" (which the framework
does not derive — masses are weight ≠ 0 and forbidden) or be restated as a claim
about θ_QCD alone, which is not directly measurable.

---

### P2 — charge quantisation

> **REFUTED IF:** a free particle is observed whose colour triality *t*, weak
> duality *d* and hypercharge *Y* satisfy `t/3 + d/2 + Y ∉ ℤ`.

**Sharpness: S1.** Fully mechanical. "Free" carries its ordinary meaning —
an unconfined asymptotic state — so the non-observation of free quarks is not a
violation, and no reader need ask.

---

### P3 — the global form is `[SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)]/ℤ₆`

> **REFUTED IF (magnetic):** a magnetic monopole is observed and (F1) carries no
> colour flux at minimal hypercharge magnetic charge, or (F2) carries no weak
> flux at minimal hypercharge magnetic charge, or (F3) carries a
> (colour, weak, hypercharge) flux triple outside the six rows of the ℤ₆ table,
> or (F4) is a pure-hypercharge monopole below hypercharge magnetic charge 1.

**Sharpness: S2.** Mechanical *given a monopole*. No monopole has ever been
seen and none is guaranteed to be, so the falsifier may never be applicable —
but it is not defective, and if a monopole appears it decides immediately.

> **REFUTED IF (electric):** the theory's admissible line spectrum is found to
> contain a bare colour-triplet line `(3,1)₀` (F5), a bare weak-doublet line
> `(1,2)₀` (F6), or an isolated hypercharge-1/6 colour singlet (F7).

**Sharpness: S3.** Line operators are **probes, not particles**. Settling F5–F7
means a lattice or theoretical determination of the spectrum, not a detector
event. Real, but not an observation, and the register should not present F5–F7
in the same breath as F1–F4.

### P4 — right-handed neutrinos exist

> **REFUTED IF:** all neutrinos are massless.

**Sharpness: S1 as a falsifier — but the CONVERSE IS UNEARNED, and that is the
defect.** The claim is that **ν_R exists**. Neutrino mass **does not require
ν_R**: a Majorana mass from the dimension-5 Weinberg operator gives massive
neutrinos with no right-handed state at all. So massless neutrinos would refute,
but **massive neutrinos do not confirm ν_R** — they are consistent with its
absence.

**Recorded here, blind, because it is a logical property of the implication and
not an experimental result:** whatever Phase B writes in P4's status column, it
must not read as support for ν_R.

---

### P5 — neutrino masses are Dirac at the renormalisable level

> **REFUTED IF:** neutrinoless double beta decay is observed.

**Sharpness: S4 — defective, and self-cancelling.** The register states the
falsifier and then **retracts it in the same entry**:

> *"a 0νββ signal would not refute the framework; it would establish that
> structure beyond the 27 is required."*

That retraction is **correct physics** — Majorana masses can come from
non-renormalisable operators, and the 27 has no **126**-equivalent, as in every
E₆ model. But a falsifier that the same entry declines to honour **is not a
falsifier**. P5 as written cannot be lost.

**What P5 could honestly say instead** (not adopted here — Phase A does not
rewrite claims, only grades them): *the renormalisable cubic contains no
`16·16·1`* is a **theorem about the 27**, S3 at best, and it is not a prediction
about the world.

---

### P6 — the exotics' quantum numbers

> **REFUTED IF:** a vector-like exotic is discovered whose quantum numbers are
> not a colour-triplet pair `3 + 3̄` with `|Y| = 1/3` or a weak-doublet pair
> `2 + 2` with `|Y| = 1/2`.

**Sharpness: S2.** Fully mechanical *given a discovery*; silent without one.
**This is the cleanest conditional in the register** — the exclusion list is
explicit and a single wrong-quantum-number discovery kills it.

---

### P7 — exactly two extra neutral gauge bosons

> **REFUTED IF:** exactly one, or three or more, additional neutral gauge bosons
> are found.

**Sharpness: S4 — defective for want of a scale.** The register states plainly
that *"two, and heavy"* is sayable but *"two, at 10¹⁶ GeV"* is **not, ever** —
every mass is weight ≠ 0 and forbidden by theorem.

**Without a scale, no finite search can refute this.** Finding zero Z′ at any
energy is always answerable with "they are heavier." The claim is unfalsifiable
**in practice**, not by sloppiness but **by the framework's own theorem**.

**This is the sharpest thing in the sharpness audit**: P7 is the entry where the
weight ledger's own prohibition destroys the prediction's testability. It is not
a wording defect and cannot be repaired by rewording.

---

### P8 — the gauge group terminates

> **REFUTED IF:** a new **low-energy** gauge interaction is discovered.

**Sharpness: S4 — defective, undefined term.** **"Low-energy" is not defined.**
Any discovery can be declared not-low-enough after the fact. To reach S1 or S2
the entry must name an energy, and by the weight ledger **it cannot**.

Same failure mode as P7, from the same cause.
