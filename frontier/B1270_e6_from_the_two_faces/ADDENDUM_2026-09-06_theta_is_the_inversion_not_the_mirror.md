# Addendum (2026-09-06, B1272) — CORRECTION: the "θ" of this arc is the inversion, not the object's mirror (E69)

This arc called **quaternion conjugation** x ↦ x̄ on the icosians "θ — the Eisenstein conjugation, B576's mirror
axis made exact", on the strength of two facts: it sends g ↦ ḡ = g⁻¹ and it swaps the two triples (27,3) ↔ (27̄,3̄).
Both facts are true, and every use this arc and B1271 make of θ — the triplet swap, the chirality bit as the
choice ω vs ω̄ — stands. **The identification with the mirror is withdrawn.** Quaternion conjugation is the
*inversion* x ↦ x⁻¹ on the unit group, an anti-automorphism, invisible on characters. The object's mirror is
the amphichirality of A = LR, conjugation by P = diag(1, −1) (R ↦ R⁻¹, L ↦ L⁻¹), which reduces mod 5 to an
**inner** automorphism of 2I (det P = −1 = 2² is a square) — Ad(h) for an order-4 unit icosian h — and the
representative Ad(h′), h′ = L_q h, preserves ℤ[g] with g ↦ g⁻¹ (B1272 §1). The two maps agree on the Eisenstein
plane and on the triplet swap and differ by the inner involution x ↦ h′ x̄ h̄′; on E₆ they have different Cartan
signatures (mirror 3/3, inversion 1/5) — both split-type (B1272 §2–3).

**The error, typed** (`docs/ERROR_LEDGER.md` E69, IDENTIFICATION-BY-RESTRICTION): an involution of the whole
lattice was identified with the mirror because it agreed with the mirror on a sub-lattice (the Eisenstein plane);
the extension to the whole lattice was not derived from the object. Rule: derive the mirror from the object's own
amphichirality (P) and reduce it through the same isomorphism the arc constructs.
