1.  THE PROPOSAL UNDER TEST
========================================================================

P16.3 proposes: the "level distinction" -- reading the word at the
LETTER level vs reading the substitution RULE -- corresponds to c
(complex conjugation, sqrt(-3) -> -sqrt(-3)) in the F_2^3 torsor.

The motivation (K021): RL (product, trace 3, Q(sqrt(5))) represents

========================================================================
2.  THE NEGATIVES (three independent failure modes)
========================================================================

--- 2a: The level distinction has NO formal definition as a Z/2 ---

For c to BE the level distinction, "level" must be a well-defined
binary operation (an involution) on some space where c also acts.
Unlike theta = tracking choice (which has a clean definition: at
each 'a', follow offset or offset+1) and unlike gamma_5 = reading
direction (L-to-R vs R-to-L), the "level distinction" has no clean
formal realization.

Candidate formalizations TESTED AND FAILED:

(i)  BLOCK TRACKING vs LETTER TRACKING.  The most natural candidate:
     at rule level, the pointer tracks which sigma-block it belongs
     to rather than which letter it sits on.  COMPUTED: when 'a'
     maps to 'ab', parent (pos 0 in block) and child (pos 1 in
     block) are ALWAYS in the SAME block.  The within-block position
     IS the parent/child distinction = theta.  Block membership adds
     no independent binary information.  At every 'a' position in
     F_3 through F_9 (verified exhaustively), parent lands at
     within-block-pos 0, child lands at within-block-pos 1.
     Block tracking collapses to theta, not c.

(ii) SIGMA^2 vs SIGMA TRACKING.  "Rule level" as composing the
     substitution: track through sigma^2 instead of sigma.  COMPUTED:
     the sigma^2 trajectory is a SUBSEQUENCE of the sigma^1 trajectory
     (every other step).  This is not a binary choice (Z/2 involution);
     it is a different sampling rate.  No involution structure.

(iii) META-READING (reading the rule itself).  "The pointer reads the
     rule a->ab, b->a instead of the letters a, b."  But the rule IS
     defined on the letters.  You cannot read the rule without reading
     letters.  The rule is not a separate object the pointer can point
     at; it is the MAP between successive words.  There is no second
     "word of rules" for the pointer to scan.

All three formalizations fail.  The level distinction is NOT a
well-defined Z/2 involution on the pointer's state space.

--- 2b: c's signature profile DOES NOT MATCH any word-level operation ---

From A2 (the rank-onset discriminator, S1/S2/S3):

  S1: c is non-trivial at SL(2)
  S2: c acts DIAGONALLY (coordinate-wise conjugation on traces)
  S3: c has a SOLO axis (T4, chirality -- flipped by c alone)

Any word-level formalization of "level" would:
  - Act by PERMUTATION (relabeling what the pointer reads), not
    diagonally.  This is S2-type = theta, not c.
  - Have no natural connection to T4 (chirality).  Chirality is
    the distinction between the two geometric points (rho_geom vs
    rho_bar_geom in Q(sqrt(-3))).  "Letter vs rule" does not flip
    handedness.
  - Be trivial or undefined at SL(2), since the level distinction
    is a parsing convention, not a coordinate conjugation.

The signature mismatch (S2 especially) is structural: c acts by
field automorphism (diagonal), not by permutation (spatial).  No
word-level parsing distinction can reproduce diagonal action on
trace coordinates.

--- 2c: B749 F8 FORBIDS a word-level c ---

The key constraint: Q(sqrt(-3)) does NOT exist at the combinatorial
level (B749 F8).  It is born at hyperbolization (axiom A5).  The
combinatorial carrier sees ONLY Q(sqrt(5)).

If "level distinction" were definable at the word level (as all three
candidate formalizations attempt), then c would be visible at the word
level.  But B749 says c is NOT visible at the word level.

The proposal tries to escape via: "the substitution rule is meta-level,
requiring geometry."  But this is false.  The rule sigma: a->ab, b->a
is PURELY COMBINATORIAL.  It requires no geometry to state, no
hyperbolization to apply.  The rule is as combinatorial as the letters
it acts on.  The passage to geometry is hyperbolization (A5), which is
a separate, non-trivial step that has nothing to do with parsing at
the letter level vs the rule level.

3.  THE ONE HONEST POSITIVE
========================================================================

The K021 analogy is real at the ALGEBRAIC level:

  RL    = product  = trace 3  = Q(sqrt(5))  = the word's content
  g     = ratio    = trace -1 = Q(sqrt(-3)) = the rule's structure

The ratio g = -RL^{-1} does encode "the relationship between the two
generators" (the rule's structure in an algebraic sense).  And c does
conjugate Q(sqrt(-3)), which is where g lives.

But this analogy operates at the ALGEBRAIC level of SL(2,Z) generators,
not at the POINTER level of the Fibonacci word.  The pointer has no
operation that maps to this algebraic-level distinction.  The analogy
motivates the proposal but does not instantiate it.
