## The core question (one sentence)

When σ stretches the word and 'a' becomes 'ab', does the pointer follow
the parent (a) or the child (b)? That choice is NOT determined by σ.
It is the observer's choice. It is binary. It might be θ.

## Preregistered predictions

### P16.1: Parent/child tracking choice IS θ

The tracking choice breaks word-reversal symmetry because parent-tracking
and child-tracking trace different paths through the stretching word.
θ (word reversal) acts on the spatial relationship within the pair (a,b):
reversing the word swaps the spatial ordering of the pair, hence swaps
which tracking choice corresponds to which spatial direction.

### P16.2: Reading direction IS γ₅

Left-to-right and right-to-left define two pointer orientations. These
should correspond to the two golden branches (φ vs φ̄) because the
Fibonacci word's asymptotic structure is governed by the golden ratio.

### P16.3: Case level IS c

The pointer reading the word is the observer at object level. The pointer
reading the rule (substitution law) is the observer at meta-level. The
level distinction corresponds to c (complex conjugation, Q(√-3) Galois),
because Q(√-3) is born at the geometry/rule level, not at the
combinatorial/letter level (B749 F8).

### P16.4: Pointer state has NO F₂³ fixed point

The full pointer state (position, direction, tracking) makes three
independent binary choices. C22 says no equivariant section exists on
the F₂³ torsor. If the pointer breaks all three generators:
co-emergence satisfies C22.

### P16.5: Co-emergence closes 2 of 3 bits, θ remains

The pointer closes γ₅ (direction) and c (level) but not θ (the reading
act itself). The tracking choice USES θ but IS NOT θ — or alternatively,
the tracking choice IS θ, in which case all three are closed but the
pointer IS the reading act, not something that has a reading act.
