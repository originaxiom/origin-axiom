B1 -- DOES THE TRACKING CHOICE CORRESPOND TO THETA?

Source data: A1 (tracking computation), A2 (torsor construction).
Independent verification: scratchpad/theta_verify2.py, theta_verify3.py.

========================================================================
1.  WHAT THE COMPUTATION FOUND
========================================================================

A1 Section 7 tests the relationship between tracking and reversal by
constructing sigma_mirror = R . sigma . R (the reversal-conjugated
substitution):

  sigma(a) = ab,  sigma(b) = a
  sigma_mirror(a) = ba,  sigma_mirror(b) = a

Result (Section 7a): For every 'a' position in F_k (tested k=3..8,
all a-positions at each level -- 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34 positions):

  parent(sigma, i) == child(sigma_mirror, i)   for ALL i
  child(sigma, i)  == parent(sigma_mirror, i)  for ALL i

Zero mismatches. Perfect swap at every position, every level.

Result (Section 7b): Multi-step trajectories (12 steps from F_3 to
F_15, all 5 starting positions):

  parent-trajectory(sigma) == child-trajectory(sigma_mirror)
  child-trajectory(sigma)  == parent-trajectory(sigma_mirror)

All 10 comparisons match exactly.

========================================================================
2.  THE CONTENT vs POSITION DISTINCTION
========================================================================

The tracking choice can be interpreted two ways:

  CONTENT-BASED:  parent = "follow the original letter 'a'"
                  child  = "follow the new letter 'b'"

  POSITION-BASED: left  = "follow offset+0 in the expansion pair"
                  right = "follow offset+1 in the expansion pair"

Under sigma:  a -> 'ab'.  original 'a' = LEFT,  new 'b' = RIGHT.
Under mirror: a -> 'ba'.  original 'a' = RIGHT, new 'b' = LEFT.

The two descriptions COINCIDE under sigma (parent = left, child = right)
but DIVERGE under sigma_mirror (parent = right, child = left).

Independent computation (theta_verify3.py) confirms:

  left(sigma) == left(sigma_mirror)    for all start positions  [TRUE]
  right(sigma) == right(sigma_mirror)  for all start positions  [TRUE]
  parent(sigma) == parent(sigma_mirror)                         [FALSE]
  parent(sigma) == child(sigma_mirror)                          [TRUE]

POSITION labels (left/right) are theta-INVARIANT.
CONTENT labels (parent/child) are theta-SWAPPED.

========================================================================
3.  WHAT THE TRACKING CHOICE IS
========================================================================

The A1 computation defines tracking as CONTENT-BASED: parent means
"follow the letter that was already there," child means "follow the
letter that is new."  This is the biologically natural definition
(parent = progenitor, child = offspring).

Under this definition:
  - Theta (conjugating sigma by reversal R) SWAPS the tracking choice.
  - parent(sigma) becomes child(sigma_mirror), and vice versa.
  - The swap is exact, verified at every position, every level.

The POSITION-BASED labels (left/right) do NOT swap.  Both sigma and
sigma_mirror expand 'a' into two letters starting at the same offset.
"Follow the left one" gives the same trajectory regardless of whether
the left letter is 'a' or 'b'.

Therefore: the tracking choice is the CONTENT label, which is the
RELATIONAL property between position-in-pair and letter-identity.
This relational property IS what theta acts on.

========================================================================
4.  THE THIRD OPTION: ALIGNMENT WITH SIGMA'S DIRECTION
========================================================================

The task poses a third interpretive frame: the tracking choice is about
alignment with the substitution's preferred direction.

  sigma writes 'ab' (preferred: a-left, b-right).
  sigma_mirror writes 'ba' (reversed: b-left, a-right).

  "Aligned tracking" = follow the letter at the position that sigma
  puts first.  Under sigma this is LEFT='a'=parent.  Under sigma_mirror
  this is LEFT='b'=child.

  "Anti-aligned tracking" = follow the other.

This IS the correct geometric description.  The tracking choice measures
the relative orientation between:
  (i)  the observer's tracking direction (which letter to follow), and
  (ii) the substitution's intrinsic direction (how it writes the pair).

Theta reverses (ii) while leaving (i) in place.  The relative orientation
flips.  Equivalently: theta swaps which content label (parent/child)
sits at which position (left/right).

The tracking choice is neither purely content nor purely position.  It is
the ALIGNMENT between them -- a relational, geometric quantity.  Theta
acts on precisely this alignment.

========================================================================
5.  CONSISTENCY WITH THE TORSOR STRUCTURE
========================================================================

From A2 Section 2, theta's flip-vector:

  theta flips: T6 (chord) alone.
  T6 is composite: T6 = T4 xor theta (the chord's sign is the XOR of
  conjugation and reversal choices).

The tracking choice, being swapped by theta, is a theta-ODD quantity.
It changes sign under reversal.  This is consistent with the flip-table:
the tracking bit lives on the theta-sensitive axis T6.

From A2 Section 4 (onset discriminator): theta is TRIVIAL at SL(2),
NON-TRIVIAL at SL(3).  The tracking choice on the Fibonacci word is a
pre-geometric (combinatorial) operation.  But its non-trivial geometric
CONTENT -- the fact that it corresponds to an involution that couples
theta-even and theta-odd sectors with norm sqrt(3) -- requires the
SL(3) = Sym^2 level to manifest.  This matches the onset signature.

From A2 Section 5 (visibility): theta is listed as "AMBIGUOUS" at the
word level -- word reversal exists as an operation on the substitution,
but its full non-trivial action requires geometry.  The tracking choice
demonstrates the word-level SHADOW of theta: the swap is detectable
purely combinatorially, but the geometric weight (coupling fraction
15/32, discriminant law sqrt(|disc(K)|)) only appears at SL(3).

========================================================================
6.  WHAT THE BIOLOGICAL METAPHOR GETS RIGHT AND WRONG
========================================================================

RIGHT: The parent/child distinction IS a genuine binary choice.  It IS
swapped by theta.  The metaphor correctly identifies the choice as
involving an "original" vs "new" distinction, which is the content
distinction that theta acts on.

WRONG: The metaphor suggests the choice is about IDENTITY (which letter
is the "same one").  In fact it is about ALIGNMENT (which direction the
observer tracks relative to the substitution's direction).  The word
"parent" imports a notion of temporal precedence that obscures the
spatial (directional) nature of the choice.

More precisely:
  "parent-tracking" = tracking aligned with sigma's writing direction
  "child-tracking"  = tracking opposed to sigma's writing direction
  theta = reversal of sigma's writing direction

The biological metaphor is a useful label but a misleading analogy.

========================================================================
7.  VERDICT
========================================================================

CONFIRMED.  The parent/child tracking choice corresponds to theta
(word reversal) in the F_2^3 torsor.

Mechanism: theta acts by conjugating the substitution sigma -> R.sigma.R
= sigma_mirror, which reverses the pair orientation within the expansion
rule (ab -> ba).  This swaps the content labels (parent <-> child) while
leaving position labels (left/right) fixed.  The tracking choice, being
defined as a content label (follow the original vs follow the new), is
thereby swapped by theta.

Confidence: HIGH.

Evidence:
  (a) Perfect position-by-position swap at all levels tested (k=3..8,
      up to 34 a-positions per level): zero mismatches.
  (b) Perfect multi-step trajectory match (12 steps, all 5 start
      positions): parent(sigma) == child(sigma_mirror) and
      child(sigma) == parent(sigma_mirror) for every trajectory.
  (c) Independent verification reproduces all A1 results exactly.
  (d) Position-based tracking (left/right) independently confirmed as
      theta-invariant -- the swap is specifically of the content labels.
  (e) Consistent with the torsor flip-table (theta flips T6 alone).
  (f) Consistent with the onset discriminator (theta trivial at SL(2),
      non-trivial at SL(3); tracking is combinatorially visible but
      geometrically weighted only at Sym^2).

Caveats:
  (1) The correspondence is via conjugation of sigma, not direct action
      on words.  "Theta swaps tracking" means "conjugating sigma by
      reversal swaps which content label sits at which position."
  (2) The tracking choice is more precisely described as an alignment
      (relative orientation between observer direction and substitution
      direction), not as a content identity (original vs new).
  (3) The biological metaphor (parent/child) imports associations that
      are absent from the geometry.  "Aligned/anti-aligned" or
      "left/right-of-sigma" would be structurally more faithful labels.
