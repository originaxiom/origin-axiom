WHAT REMAINS
========================================================================

8.  THE THETA RESULT IS BANKED.

    tracking = theta is established.  It does not depend on the other
    identifications.  It is a standalone fact about the Fibonacci
    substitution and its reversal-conjugate.

9.  THE gamma_5 SLOT IS OPEN.

    gamma_5's combinatorial avatar is the COMPLEMENT operation (a<->b
    letter swap).  This IS a well-defined Z/2 involution on the Fibonacci
    word.  But it is NOT a current pointer state component.  The pointer
    has position, direction, and tracking -- not complement.

    Options for the observer program:
    (a) Expand the pointer to include a complement bit (letter-relabeling
        choice: does the observer read 'a' as 'a', or 'a' as 'b'?).
        This would be a fourth state component.
    (b) Recognize that complement acts on the word's CONTENT, not on the
        pointer's PARSING.  The pointer reads whatever letters are there.
        Complement changes what letters are there.  This is an operation
        on the object (the word), not on the observer (the pointer).
        If so, gamma_5 is an object-side symmetry, not an observer-side
        choice, and the pointer cannot close it by co-emergence.
    (c) Revisit the trajectory-level degeneracy.  Since direction and
        tracking are indistinguishable on trajectories, one of them is
        redundant.  Could direction actually carry gamma_5*theta (not
        pure theta)?  B2's frequency test says no: direction preserves
        frequencies, gamma_5*theta would swap them.  But this test
        operates at the frequency level, not the trajectory level.  If
        the trajectory representation is the physically relevant one,
        the assignment is ambiguous.

10. THE c SLOT IS STRUCTURAL.

    c requires geometry (Q(sqrt(-3)) born at hyperbolization).  The
    pointer is combinatorial.  This gap is not a deficiency -- it is
    axiom A5 ("geometry buys being").  The c-bit is not closable by
    any combinatorial mechanism.  It requires the hyperbolization step.

    The observer program's next question: what is the geometric
    counterpart of the pointer?  The pointer is the combinatorial
    observer.  The geometric observer would need a state component
    that lives in Q(sqrt(-3)) -- the chirality choice (T4, rho_geom vs
    rho_bar_geom).  This is not a word-level operation.  It is a
    choice of which geometric point to sit on.
