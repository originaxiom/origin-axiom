VERDICT: NOT CONFIRMED.  Reading direction is not gamma_5.
========================================================================

The proposal (A2 Section 10, P16.2) that reading direction on the
Fibonacci word corresponds to gamma_5 (the Galois automorphism
sqrt(5) -> -sqrt(5), phi -> phi_bar) FAILS three decisive tests
and survives only one -- and the surviving one is incomplete.

========================================================================
1.  THE ROTATION-ANGLE RESULT (the one thing that works, partially)
========================================================================

The Fibonacci word is the cutting sequence of the circle rotation
R_alpha, where alpha = 1/phi^2 = (3-sqrt(5))/2.

Under gamma_5:  alpha -> (3+sqrt(5))/2

  gamma_5(alpha) mod 1 = (3+sqrt(5))/2 - 2 = (sqrt(5)-1)/2 = 1/phi
  -alpha mod 1 = 1 - alpha = (sqrt(5)-1)/2 = 1/phi

  EXACT MATCH:  gamma_5(alpha) = -alpha  (mod Z)

So at the rotation-angle level, gamma_5 reverses the direction of
rotation.  Reversed rotation produces the reversed cutting sequence.
This is exact arithmetic and it is the strongest argument for the
proposal.

BUT gamma_5 also acts on the PARTITION of the circle:

  Original partition: [0, 1-alpha) = [0, 1/phi) for 'a'
  gamma_5 partition:  [0, alpha) = [0, 1/phi^2) for 'a'

  The 'a' interval shrinks from 1/phi to 1/phi^2.
  Letter frequencies swap: d(a) = 1/phi -> d(a) = 1/phi^2.

Therefore:  gamma_5 = reversal + letter swap (complement a<->b)

This was verified computationally: the cutting sequence under
gamma_5(alpha) with the gamma_5-conjugated partition boundary
is EXACTLY the complement of the original Fibonacci word
(Test 4: 50/50 position match).  It is NOT the reversed word.

Conclusion: the rotation-angle argument gives gamma_5 = reversal
composed with complement, not pure reversal.  Reading direction
is pure reversal without letter swap.

========================================================================
2.  THE FREQUENCY TEST (decisive FAILURE)
========================================================================

The Fibonacci word has letter frequencies:
  d(a) = 1/phi,   d(b) = 1/phi^2

Under gamma_5 (phi -> phi_bar = -1/phi):
  d(a) -> 1/phi_bar = -phi (not a probability)

In the positive-cone interpretation: gamma_5 swaps the two real
embeddings of Z[phi], taking d(a) = 1/phi to d(a) = 1/phi^2
(the complement frequency).

Under word reversal:
  d(a) -> d(a) (frequencies are INVARIANT under reversal)

This was verified for F_2 through F_11: d(a) in the forward and
reversed words are IDENTICAL to all digits.

Conclusion: reversal preserves frequencies.  gamma_5 swaps them.
Reading direction is not gamma_5 on the frequency vector.

========================================================================
3.  THE SECTION 7 CONSTRAINT (decisive structural FAILURE)
========================================================================

A1 Section 7 established a perfect algebraic result:

  sigma_mirror (the RTL substitution) EXACTLY exchanges
  parent-tracking and child-tracking at every tested level
  (k=3 through k=8, 0 mismatches in all cases).

  Equivalently:
    parent(sigma) = child(sigma_mirror)     [EXACT]
    child(sigma) = parent(sigma_mirror)     [EXACT]

  Verified on multi-step trajectories for all 5 starting positions
  (Section 7b): MATCH = True for every case.

Now apply the proposed correspondence:
  reading direction = gamma_5
  tracking choice   = theta

The Section 7 result says: switching reading direction has the SAME
effect as switching tracking choice.  Algebraically: gamma_5 and
theta have IDENTICAL actions on the trajectory representation.

  gamma_5 * theta = identity on trajectory space
  => gamma_5 * theta is in the kernel of the representation
  => gamma_5 and theta are INDISTINGUISHABLE at the trajectory level

This means: no trajectory-level observable can separate gamma_5 from
theta.  The assignment "direction = gamma_5" vs "direction = theta"
is arbitrary at this level.  There is no computational test within
substitution-word tracking that distinguishes the two.

In the flip table (A2 Section 2): gamma_5 * theta flips T6, T7, T3.
So these three axes are invisible at the trajectory level.  The
trajectory representation has rank at most 2 (seeing only T4, T1)
out of the torsor's rank 3.

========================================================================
4.  THE EIGENVALUE TEST (failure)
========================================================================

The substitution matrix M = [[1,1],[1,0]] has eigenvalues phi and
phi_bar.  Both parent-tracking and child-tracking position ratios
converge to phi (A1 Section 5).  Neither direction of reading
produces growth at rate phi_bar.

The phi_bar eigenvalue is NEGATIVE (-0.618...).  It has no role as
a growth rate.  Its effect appears only in the alternating sign of
the convergence error: n_{k+1}/n_k - phi ~ C * (phi_bar/phi)^k.
But this alternation is the SAME for both reading directions.

The K-theory positive cone K_0^+ in Z[phi] is determined by the
phi-embedding, not by reading direction.  Both LTR and RTL readers
inhabit the same positive cone.  gamma_5 would switch to the
phi_bar-embedding, producing a different ordered group -- but
reading direction does not do this.

========================================================================
5.  WHAT READING DIRECTION ACTUALLY IS
========================================================================

Reading direction = word reversal.  Word reversal acts on the
SPATIAL ORDER of letters, not on their algebraic identities or
frequencies.

At the substitution level:
  sigma(a) = ab     sigma_mirror(a) = ba
  sigma(b) = a      sigma_mirror(b) = a

  sigma_mirror = R . sigma . R  (conjugation by reversal)

At the trajectory level (Section 7):
  sigma_mirror exchanges parent and child tracking.

At the 2-gram level:
  Reversal swaps #ab <-> #ba (verified for k = 5,7,9,11).
  Reversal preserves #aa, #bb.
  Reversal preserves all 1-gram frequencies.

These are all ORDER operations, not ALGEBRAIC ones.

In contrast, gamma_5 acts algebraically (phi -> phi_bar) on the
FIELD Q(sqrt(5)), not spatially on the word's letter sequence.
Its word-level avatar is the COMPLEMENT operation (a<->b swap),
which changes letter identities without changing order.

The complement operation:
  C . sigma . C: a->b, b->ba     (NOT sigma_mirror)
  sigma . C:     a->a, b->ab     (NOT C . sigma)
  Complement does not commute with sigma.

So: reversal and complement are DIFFERENT operations on the word,
producing different substitution conjugates, corresponding to
different torsor generators.  Reading direction is reversal (a
spatial/order operation), not complement (an algebraic/label
operation).  gamma_5 is complement, not reversal.

========================================================================
6.  THE CORRECT IDENTIFICATION (if any)
========================================================================

The data supports:

  word reversal (reading direction) = theta  (or gamma_5*theta;
     indistinguishable at trajectory level)

  letter complement (a<->b swap) = gamma_5

  parent/child tracking = theta  (or gamma_5*theta;
     indistinguishable at trajectory level)

Since reversal = parent/child swap on trajectories, and both are
proposed to be different generators, there is a DEGENERACY: the
trajectory representation cannot separate them.  The two operations
that were supposed to be gamma_5 and theta respectively turn out to
be the SAME operation at the observable level.

This is not a paradox for the torsor (different generators can have
the same action on a quotient representation), but it means P16.2
(direction = gamma_5) is not testable within the trajectory framework
and fails when tested against the cutting-sequence framework.

7.  RELATION TO B749 F8 AND THE COMBINATORIAL CARRIER
========================================================================

B749 F8 established: Q(sqrt(5)) is combinatorial -- it exists at

This means gamma_5 (acting on Q(sqrt(5))) should have a combinatorial

The complement operation swaps letter frequencies:
  d(a) = 1/phi  <->  d(a) = 1/phi^2

This correctly implements phi -> phi_bar on frequencies.  It is
a genuine combinatorial gamma_5.  But it is not reading direction.

The proposal P16.2 is REFUTED at the combinatorial level.

cutting-sequence decomposition (gamma_5 = reversal + complement)
