# B1222 — "THE SELECTION IS THE OBSTRUCTION": proposed with kill conditions, and killed by them

**Status: banked (frontier). Verdict NEGATIVE.** A unifying thesis was proposed for the programme's
sixty-five proved vanishings, with three falsifiers named in advance. All three were run. **One
refutes it outright; the other two return partial support with real counterexamples.** The thesis
does not survive. Gate 5 clean.

## The thesis, as proposed

> The programme keeps *proving zero* — Δ = I, CS = 0, ∂S/∂k ≡ 0, θ trivial, the mirror trivial,
> invariant content zero, symmetric support empty. Each is forced by a **symmetry** of the object.
> And the object was selected for **canonicity** — minimal complexity, extremal, smallest volume,
> unique. Canonicity *is* symmetry, and symmetry is what makes things vanish. **So the selection is
> the obstruction**: we did not fail to find values despite choosing the best object, but *because*
> we chose it. Corollary: the freedom ledger is the object's symmetry group itemised — the observer
> costs exactly what canonicity forbids.

## TEST 3 — the decisive one. REFUTED.

**Prediction:** a less symmetric sibling should carry *more*.

**First finding: the premise was wrong.** m003 is not less symmetric than m004 — both have symmetry
group of order 8, both amphichiral, both volume 2.0298832128. What differs is the cusp shape, and
**m003's is exactly ω = e^{iπ/3}, the hexagonal torus — the *most* symmetric one** — while m004's is
2√3·i, rectangular. m003 also carries **ℤ/5 torsion** in H₁ that m004 lacks. So on this pair, *more*
cusp symmetry accompanies *more* homological content.

**Then the population test**, which is what a thesis of this shape actually predicts. Over the first
400 one-cusped orientable census manifolds:

| \|Sym\| | count | fraction with H₁ torsion |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 261 | **0.195** |
| 4 | 133 | **0.872** |

**Pearson r(\|Sym\|, has-torsion) = +0.50.** The thesis predicts **negative**. More symmetry goes
with **more** carried content, by a wide margin and in the wrong direction.

**The general law is dead.** What the data says instead is narrower and more interesting: symmetry
does not destroy content, it **redistributes** it — removing Chern–Simons, modular flow and
invariant pairings while *adding* homological torsion. "Symmetry kills content" is simply false.

## TEST 2 — is every vanishing symmetry-forced? **7 of 10.**

Adjudicated by hand on the named object-level vanishings, because a keyword sweep cannot separate
an object vanishing from a bookkeeping arc containing the word "zero" (294 raw hits, ~half noise).

Symmetry-forced: CS = 0 and ∂S/∂k ≡ 0 (amphichirality) · Δ = I (traciality) · θ trivial · the mirror
trivial · invariant content zero · symmetric support empty.

**Not symmetry-forced — the counterexamples:**
- **the regulator is identically 1**, because the relevant **unit rank is 0**. That is Dirichlet's
  unit theorem for an imaginary quadratic field: r₁ + r₂ − 1 = 0 + 1 − 1 = 0. **A signature fact
  about the field, not a symmetry of the object.**
- **the h = 0 locus** is a *word* property — the diagonal-free weld matrices (B1110) — not a
  symmetry statement.
- **designer freedom 0.000 bits** is a meta-accounting over the chain, not an object vanishing at
  all; including it in the pattern was sloppy.

## TEST 1 — is every ledger row a broken symmetry? **about 6 of 10.**

Map cleanly: the chirality bit (amphichirality — and B717 already said it, *"the observer is what
breaks the c-swap by choosing a side"*) · λ (traciality → type III_λ) · the real-structure closing
(conjugation) · the arrow label (time reversal) · family/VEV/filling (*"an invariant selector cannot
pick a point of its own orbit"* — the ledger's own words are a symmetry statement) · the colour
frame (which A₂ is colour — a triality orbit).

**Do not map:** ℓ and σ are *missing data*, not broken symmetries — the object has no scale to break;
ℙ³'s Higgs line is not obviously an orbit; the SM-shaping is an input, not a symmetry.

## Verdict

**NEGATIVE.** The unification does not hold. Seven of ten vanishings and six of ten ledger rows fit,
which is a real and non-obvious *tendency* — but a thesis that fails its own decisive test by
correlating **+0.50 where it predicted negative** is refuted, not "partially confirmed."

**What survives, and it was never in doubt:** the individual implications are theorems.
Amphichirality *does* force CS = 0. Traciality *does* force Δ = I. Those stand on their own arcs.
What is refuted is that they are **instances of one law**.

## Why this was worth doing anyway

The thesis was proposed with three kill conditions stated **before** any test ran, and the first
test executed killed it. The alternative — writing it up as "the essence of the programme" on the
strength of the seven cases that fit — is exactly the failure mode this corpus has spent two years
building instruments against. **Sixty-five proved vanishings are a real pattern; they are not
evidence for the first story that explains them.**
