Methodology note on the "MAIN coverage" columns: computed by
`git grep -l -E "sm:B12nn|sB12nn|seat's B12nn" -- docs frontier` on MAIN, plus a manual read of
`docs/HARVEST_LEDGER.md` for rows naming the seat and the arc. This literal-substring method
under-counts arc numbers that only appear **inside an en-dash range** written in main's prose
(e.g. text reading `sB1267–sB1276` contains the literal substrings `sB1267` and `sB1276` — its two
endpoints — but not `sB1268`, `sB1270`, etc., even though those numbers fall inside the stated
range). Where this happened it is flagged in the row. Separately, `docs/SM_SEAT_ALIAS_TABLE.md`
names every one of the seat's B1267–B1276 arcs individually **by directory name** in its collision
table (not by an `sB…`-prefixed token), which the regex also does not catch; that table is the
subject of part (c) below and its per-row naming is noted in part (a)'s coverage column as
"(also individually named, not by sB-token, in `docs/SM_SEAT_ALIAS_TABLE.md`)" wherever it applies.
