> *"the 𝔽₂ bits and the Lie type are discrete (no continuous anchor needed); the scale slot is

- **"Zero dimensionless inputs" is false as stated** — four of the five are dimensionless. What

  dials. A discrete datum cannot be tuned to a measured number, which is why B724's C5 could
  compute that *"2 bits = measure-zero in ℝ¹⁹"*.
- **"Zero *continuous* dimensionless inputs" is not proven either — the count is exactly one**,
  A2, and it is **declared, sealed and priced as an anchor** rather than derived. **L154 is the
  single open cell that would take it to zero**: if the gravitational σ is the stage's σ, then

  dimensionless input converts from anchor to OUTPUT — the input list shrinks."*
- **The whole-programme grade agrees.** `WHAT_WOULD_COUNT` §4: **"Tier 4 — parameter-free …
  STATUS: NOT DONE"**, and its rule 2: **"No arc claims 'parameter-free', 'ToE', or 'crosses the
  physics wall'."** `THE_CLAIM` §2 keeps the banked meaning — *zero **free** dimensionless
  parameters* — under which a declared, priced, subtracted anchor is not free; **that is a
  scoring rule, not a proof that the anchor is unnecessary.**

**The honest one-liner: the claim is one open lead away from being sayable, and is not sayable
today.**

**Consequences, stated at the size they are:**
