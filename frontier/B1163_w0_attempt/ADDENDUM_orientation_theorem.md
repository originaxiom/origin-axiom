2. **"Object-canonical" = fixed by the automorphism group** — including the mirror. (That is what canonical

3. The **orientation** (+Vol vs −Vol) is **swapped** by the mirror (Bloch–Wigner is odd under conjugation,

nor a mirror image is canonical: the object is maximally symmetric under the two-element group that acts on
its orientations, and fixes neither choice.
