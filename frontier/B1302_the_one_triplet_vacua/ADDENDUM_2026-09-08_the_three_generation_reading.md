# B1302 addendum (2026-09-08) — the three-generation reading: with ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ alone, the one-triplet lines carry the MSSM's field content in three generations, vector-like, with no exotic and one Higgs pair

B1302 chose its configurations by the smallest number of light colour-triplet pairs, which singled out ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ with the
flavon pair {g₀g₁}: one light generation. `verification/three_generation_reading.py` (record
`three_generation_reading_run.txt`) evaluates, on the same six patterns of the 768 one-triplet lines, the sub-branch
**⟨N_{g₂}⟩ alone** — no flavon VEV, no ν^c VEV; D- and F-flat by B1283's rule — with B1302's mass matrices:

- colour-triplet sector: exactly massless are the three d^c and their mirrors, and nothing else — N_{g₂} D_{g₀} D̄_{g₁}
  gives the two surviving triplets of the 27s their Dirac mass; **no exotic colour triplet is light**;
- doublet sector: exactly massless are L₁, L₂, L₃ with their mirrors and **one Higgs pair, H_u,g₂ and H_d,g₂** (the other
  Higgs doublets are paired by N_{g₂} H_u,g₀ H_d,g₁ and N_{g₂} H_u,g₁ H_d,g₀);
- Q, u^c, e^c (and ν^c, which no VEV touches): all three generations light.

So the light spectrum at tree level is **three generations of Q, u^c, d^c, L, e^c, ν^c, one Higgs pair and no exotic —
the MSSM's field content with right-handed neutrinos, every field with its mirror** (the closing is vector-like, B1260),
on 768 lines of Y₁₂ (and Y₂₄), for each of the six generation assignments. The flavon VEVs of B1302's one-generation
configuration are what pair generations g₀, g₁ with their mirrors; at zero flavon VEV all three stay.

**The Yukawa structure.** The light Higgs pair is generation g₂'s, and every Yukawa carries the family tensor |ε_ijk|
(B1273, B1271): Q_i u^c_j H_u,g₂ has coefficient |ε_{i j g₂}|, the matrix [[0,0,1],[0,0,0],[1,0,0]] for g₂ = 2 — **rank 2,
generation g₂ massless, generations g₀ and g₁ paired off-diagonally with equal entries** — and the same for the down,
charged-lepton and Dirac-neutrino Yukawas. The tree-level flavour structure of the three-generation vacuum is therefore
one massless generation and two degenerate ones; the hierarchy, as everywhere in the corpus, is the exponential sector's
(L201). Only N_{g₂} has a VEV, so three of the four extra U(1)s survive at tree level (SM × U(1)³; B1302's charges
addendum gives the two that survive the flavon VEVs and the one that survives the ν^c VEV).

This corrects the emphasis of B1302 §4 and of the ledger's §4h: the tower's best configuration is not "one light
generation" by necessity but by the choice of flavon VEVs; **the three-generation, exotic-free, one-Higgs-pair content
exists on the same 768 lines at ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ alone.** Vector-like throughout; 0 of 19; price unchanged.
