# B1302 addendum (2026-09-08) — the structure of the 768 one-triplet lines

`verification/structure_of_the_768.py` (run record `structure_of_the_768_run.txt`) identifies the 768 lines of Y₁₂ on
which B1302's solved configuration exists — the six patterns D in generation g₀, D̄ in g₁, N only in g₂, every other
multiplet complete — and finds:

- **all three determining characters have order 16**: (ord ψ_Q, ord ψ_u, ord ψ_L) = (16, 16, 16) on every one of the 768;
- **the line's image is the whole 2-part of the character group**, ℤ/16 ⊕ ℤ/16 of order 256, on every one (Y₉'s SM lines
  had images ℤ/19, ℤ/19 × ℤ/2, ℤ/19 × V₄ of orders 19, 38, 76, B1278);
- ψ_u and ψ_L are never a power of ψ_Q times a family character — the three letters are independent;
- the deck transformation preserves the set and permutes the six patterns as its 3-cycle on the generations: **64 deck
  orbits of 12 lines each**; each of the six (g₀, g₁, g₂) assignments carries 128 lines.

So the one-triplet vacua are not a corner of the SM lines but a class of their own — the lines whose image is the full
2-torsion of H₁(Y₁₂)^∧ — and there are 64 of them up to the deck group. Whether the closing's isometries identify more
(L206, L210(iii)) is not computed here.
