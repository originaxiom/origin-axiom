# B1302 — THE ONE-TRIPLET VACUA: on 768 of Y₁₂'s 34 752 Standard-Model lines a tree-level flat direction leaves exactly one light colour-triplet pair — the light generation's own d^c — and its three light doublet pairs, one vector-like generation with two Higgs doublets and no exotic triplet; on Y₉ no flat direction of any line does this (every one keeps at least two light triplet pairs — B1283's and B1300's light D pair, confirmed by the same code)

**Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Seat:** cc (the SM-derivation branch) · **Status:** POSITIVE (exact: every D- and F-flat VEV configuration of every survival pattern enumerated; the generic ranks of the holomorphic tree-level mass matrices computed exactly at random rational points, twice; the massless fields identified) · **Price: unchanged** · **Numbering:** B1302.

## Why this arc — L210(ii)

B1283 found that on Y₉'s Standard-Model closing every maximal tree-level branch leaves generation g's light Higgs pair
together with generation g's light colour-triplet pair D_g, D̄_g: the VEVs ⟨N_g⟩, ⟨ν^c_g⟩, ⟨S_hk⟩, ⟨S_kh⟩ never touch
generation g's 27, and B1300 proved that Y₉'s Wilson lines keep D in every generation. B1301 then found the tower's
second Standard-Model closing, Y₁₂, whose lines keep D in one generation only. The question left there: on such a line,
is there a flat direction on which the surviving triplet is paired off while a Higgs pair stays light — the
doublet–triplet problem solved at tree level, in the closing's vector-like sense?

## 1. The survival patterns ((a))

Every SM line of a closing is characterised, for the tree-level mass problem, by its survival pattern: which of the
eleven multiplets of the 27 survive in which of the three generations (a mirror component of the 27̄ survives iff its
partner does, since h¹(ψ⁻¹) = h¹(ψ)). From the support files of B1301: **Y₉'s 706 464 lines fall into 67 patterns, Y₁₂'s
34 752 into 265.**

## 2. The tree-level mass matrices ((b))

On a pattern, the D- and F-flat VEV configurations of the SM-singlet sector are the unions of conjugate pairs {N_g, N̄_g},
{ν^c_g, ν̄^c_g}, {S_hk, S_kh} allowed by B1283's squarefree-cubic rule — at most one N generation, one ν^c generation, one
flavon pair, and the pair {hk} only with the third generation's N and ν^c — restricted to fields that exist on the line
(a VEV needs its field). For each configuration the holomorphic mass matrices are built from every term of the
one-coupling cubic that contains a VEV'd field — N_i H_u,j H_d,k and N_i D_j D̄_k, ν^c_i L_j H_u,k and ν^c_i d^c_j D_k, with
the family tensor |ε_ijk| (B1273's zero diagonal); the mirror cubic with the conjugate VEVs; the flavon couplings
S_ij 27_j · 27̄_i pairing every component of 27_j with its mirror in 27̄_i — in three sectors:

| sector | fields of charge q | fields of charge −q |
|---|---|---|
| **T**, (3, −1/3) × (3̄, +1/3) | D_g, the mirrors of d^c_g and of D̄_g | d^c_g, D̄_g, the mirror of D_g |
| **H**, (1, 2, +1/2) × (1, 2, −1/2) | H_u,g, the mirrors of L_g and of H_d,g | L_g, H_d,g, the mirror of H_u,g |
| Q, u^c, e^c (flavon-only) | the multiplet | its mirror |

with independent symbols for every VEV and every coupling; the generic rank is computed exactly at random rational
points (twice, the maximum taken); **light pairs in a sector = dimension − rank**, and the fields whose row or column
vanishes identically are the exactly massless ones. The sectors are square on every line (the closing is vector-like).

## 3. The census ((c))

| | Y₉ (control) | **Y₁₂** |
|---|---|---|
| patterns · lines | 67 · 706 464 | 265 · 34 752 |
| best light-triplet count n_T per line (its best configuration with a light doublet pair) | **2** on 29 376, 3 on 677 088 | **1 on 768**, 2 on 14 880, 3 on 19 104 |
| with two light generations' worth of doublets (n_H ≥ 6): best n_T | 4 (288 lines) | **2** on 2 496 lines |

**Y₉ never goes below two light triplet pairs** — generation g's d^c pair and its D pair, exactly B1283's and B1300's
light D pair, now from an independent construction of the full mass matrices with mirrors and flavons included.

**Y₁₂ reaches one.** The 768 lines are six patterns × 128: D surviving in one generation g₀, D̄ in another g₁, N only in
the third g₂ (every other multiplet in all three generations), and the configuration is ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ with the flavon pair
{g₀ g₁} — S_{g₀g₁}, S_{g₁g₀} — and no ν^c VEV. Then

- ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ D_{g₀} D̄_{g₁} (|ε| = 1, the three indices distinct) gives the only surviving triplet and the only surviving
  anti-triplet of the 27s a Dirac mass, and ⟨N̄_{g₂}⟩ does the same for their mirrors;
- ⟨S_{g₀g₁}⟩, ⟨S_{g₁g₀}⟩ pair generations g₀ and g₁ with each other's mirrors — d̄^c_{g₀} with d^c_{g₁} and d̄^c_{g₁} with
  d^c_{g₀}, and the same for every doublet, Q, u^c, e^c;
- generation g₂ has no D and no D̄ on the line, and nothing touches its 27.

So the **exactly massless fields** are generation g₂'s d^c and its mirror (n_T = 1), its L, H_u, H_d and their mirrors
(n_H = 3), its Q, u^c, e^c and their mirrors (the flavon-only sectors, 1 each), with N_{g₂} the branch's modulus: **one
vector-like generation with two Higgs doublets and no exotic colour triplet.** The kernel has no directions beyond these
zero rows (checked). This is the doublet–triplet problem solved at tree level on the closing — by the Wilson line's
thinning of the triplets (B1301) and the vacuum's pairing of what it left, the two halves neither could do alone: on Y₉
the line keeps every D (B1300) and the vacuum cannot pair generation g's; on Y₁₂ without the vacuum the surviving D and
D̄ are light.

**The other mechanism, two generations deep.** With six light doublet pairs the best Y₁₂ configurations (2 496 lines,
e.g. the pattern H_u in two generations, D in one, no D̄, on 256 lines) use a single ν^c VEV and no N or flavon VEV:
⟨ν^c_2⟩ d^c_3 D_1 marries the one surviving triplet to a d^c — E₆'s classic ν^c mechanism — leaving three generations of
Q, u^c, e^c, two of d^c, six doublet pairs and again no exotic triplet, at the price of one d^c. On Y₉ the same mechanism
would have to marry three D's to three d^c's; the best two-generation configuration there keeps four light triplet
pairs.

## 4. What this settles, and what it does not

- **L210(ii): yes.** The tower's Standard-Model closings contain lines and flat directions whose tree-level light
  spectrum has no exotic colour triplet and a light Higgs pair: 768 of Y₁₂'s lines (one light generation) and 2 496
  (two generations' worth of doublets, three of Q, u^c, e^c). B1283's obstruction is Y₉'s.
- **Vector-like, and one generation.** Every light field comes with its mirror (B1260), and the one-triplet vacua make
  generation g₂ light alone — the flavon VEVs that pair g₀ with g₁ are what removes their triplets' partners. Three
  light chiral generations are not produced by any tree-level configuration of any line (the census's largest n_H is 7
  of a possible 9). The chirality wall (B1280, B1290) stands where it stood.
- **What the numbers are.** Tree-level, generic couplings and VEVs, holomorphic masses only (no D-term or soft masses);
  the E₆ Clebsches enter as independent symbols, so the ranks are generic. A non-generic Clebsch cancellation could only
  lower a rank, i.e. add light fields, never remove the solved cases' zero rows — those are exact.
- 0 of 19 values; price unchanged.

## Controls (MB12)

- **Y₉ control:** the mass matrices with mirrors and flavons reproduce B1283's light Higgs/D pair on every branch of every
  line (min n_T = 2 over 67 patterns × all configurations), and B1300's "the light D pair cannot be removed".
- **Exactness:** ranks over ℚ at random rational points with all VEVs and couplings independent, twice; the zero rows
  are identities of the coupling structure, not numerics; the sectors are square on every pattern (assertion).
- **Existence of the VEV'd fields** enforced per pattern; the configuration rule is B1283's (its nine maximal branches
  are the maximal configurations of the full pattern).
- **Coupling completeness:** the terms with a VEV'd field are exactly those of the E₆ cubic's zero-sum triples that contain
  N or ν^c (B1300's table: N H_u H_d, N D D̄, ν^c L H_u, ν^c d^c D) — no other triple contains an SM singlet.
- **E70-class:** no representation matrices; an index bookkeeping only.
