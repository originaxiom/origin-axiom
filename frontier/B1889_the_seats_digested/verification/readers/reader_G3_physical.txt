TRACK RECORD
The seat's reproducible work held up: every verified row is an agreement or a partial verification, and none is a refutation (rows 25, 55-64, 72, 73, 75, 536, 704, 739, 795, 1154, 1273, 1484, 1492, 1645). It found a real defect in main's B915 (row 55) and predicted the failure of main's B1642 before main's results were out (row 1111). Its overstatements are mostly labels: "VERIFIED" was relabelled "REPRODUCED" (rows 56-62), and its independent audit and word-architecture claims needed later qualifiers (rows 1273, 1512, 1538). It is careful about grades, with "unreviewed, unrun" stated on its own proofs (rows 1393-1394, 1465, 1522). Roughly 66% of its rows are registered only, so most of its analytic content has never been checked by main.
