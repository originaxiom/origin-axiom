## 2. The work order: its purpose, and what the programme keeps

The work order is the physical lane's (`WORK_ORDER.md` at `521e6f241`, read in full through the binding-minimum mask).

- **Its purpose** is to stop the programme from reaching "parameter-free" by the adjective. It asks for one dependency
  map from the principle to one physical construction, for every independent input to be removed by derivation or
  shown, and for the comparison with measurement to come strictly after a seal.
- **It is a careful document, and most of the programme is the work order carried out.** Kept as written:
  - three statuses kept apart: the map complete, the construction verified, the predictions ready;
  - two constructions share a result only through an exhibited map; a count or a matching constant is not one. The
    programme's three joins (WP-2.3, WP-3.2, WP-3.4) each end in an exhibited map or a proof that there is none;
  - a reduction counts only if the choice does not come back through a representation, a metric, a normalisation, a
    boundary condition, a state, a measure or a fit;
  - a demonstrated free parameter is a result, and a new obstruction leads to a revised derivation or an explicit
    surviving freedom. Every package that the mathematics can settle states both ends;
  - parameter-free inside a supplied model is not parameter-free from the foundation; a unit is a convention, and a
    free ratio of scales is not; a sealed, unfitted prediction is not a blind discovery.
