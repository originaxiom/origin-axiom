# Citation verification (2026-09-01) — the σ result's "robust core" is weaker than banked

## 1. Anderson–Moore / Vafa — "c is rational in any RCFT" → **DEFLATES**

**Banked as (B1229):** *"the robust core — Anderson–Moore/Vafa: in ANY rational CFT the central
charge and every conformal weight are rational. A Chern–Simons boundary IS rational. So σ is
rational."*

**What verification found:**

- **Neither primary paper could be read** — both 1988, pre-arXiv, paywalled. The check was built
  from abstracts plus secondary sources that reproduce the mechanism (Lewellen SLAC-PUB-4740;
  Bantay–Gannon math/9909080; Gaberdiel hep-th/9910156). *The actual proofs were not read.*
- **Anderson–Moore's real statement** (abstract, corroborated): *"if the one-loop partition function
  of a modular invariant conformal field theory can be expressed as a **finite sum of holomorphically
  factorized terms** then c and all values of h are rational."*
- **Hypotheses:** modular invariance **and finitely many primaries**. Mechanically this forces the
  T-matrix to have finite order.
- **GRADE: PHYSICS-ARGUMENT, not theorem.** Rigorous derivation from CFT postulates taken as given.
  The fully rigorous mathematical theorem is a *different, later* result — **Dong–Li–Mason (2000)**,
  after Zhu (1996), for VOAs with Zhu-rationality **plus C₂-cofiniteness**.
- **The decisive caveat:** *"there is no uniform definition of rationality"* — physics' "finitely
  many primaries" and mathematics' C₂-cofiniteness are **provably inequivalent** (Gaberdiel–Kausch).
  And explicitly: **a general Chern–Simons-boundary chiral algebra is not automatically covered;
  finiteness of primaries must be independently established, not assumed.**

**Consequence, stated plainly.** B1229's "robust core" **is not robust**. The chain runs:

> *σ is rational* ⟸ *c is rational* ⟸ **the boundary has finitely many primaries** ⟸ **NOT ESTABLISHED**

I asserted "a Chern–Simons boundary is rational" as motivation and then used it as a premise. The
verification names exactly that step as the one requiring independent proof. **σ ∈ ℚ is therefore
CONDITIONAL, on an unestablished finiteness, and at physics-argument grade even when granted.**

## 2. The MMS two-character classification → **HOLDS, and sharpens**

- **Complete, and now genuinely proved** — MMS's 1988 methods were *"mathematically incomplete"*;
  **Mason (arXiv:1803.11281, 2018)** closed it with a VOA-theoretic proof, confirmed independently
  by Das–Gowdigere–Mukhi (arXiv:2012.14939). **GRADE: PROVED-CLASSIFICATION.**
- **Every central charge confirmed exactly**: A₁ 1, A₂ 2, G₂ 14/5, D₄ 4, F₄ 26/5, **E₆ 6**, E₇ 7,
  plus Lee-Yang −22/5 (non-unitary). Eight solutions.
- **E₆ two-character: CONFIRMED, with a better reason than the one banked.** I wrote that 27 and 27̄
  share h = 2/3 so their characters coincide. The sharper fact: they are exchanged by E₆'s **outer
  charge-conjugation automorphism** and have **identical full characters** χ₂₇(τ) = χ₂₇̄(τ). The
  character space is 2-dimensional despite **three modules**. Same mechanism for A₂ and D₄.
- **E₈ excluded structurally**, not by elimination: (E₈)₁ is the E₈ lattice VOA — holomorphic, a
  **single** primary, a one-character theory that never enters the n = 2 case.
- **NEW LIMIT, not previously recorded:** the seven-value list is **ℓ = 0 only**. Two-character RCFTs
  with **nonzero Wronskian index** (ℓ = 2, 4, and ℓ ≥ 6 for c < 25) are *separate additional
  classifications*. **So the σ menu of 7 is not the whole menu even within two-character theories.**
- Caveat carried: a stricter reading — VOAs with *exactly two simple modules* — gives only
  {A₁, G₂, F₄, E₇, Lee-Yang}; A₂/D₄/E₆ have 3/4/3 modules collapsing to 2 characters. Our C-5b
  arithmetic used **module** counts (55/7/4/3), which is the correct quantity for that cut.
