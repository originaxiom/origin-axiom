# B1446 — Addendum (2026-10-03): the 24 zeros are a theorem (B1459)

The class index zero at this arc's 24 doubly parabolic points on the root is a consequence of B1297's three lines and
the fibre's elliptic involution (B1459 §1): ι̃*V ≅ V* ⊗ ε with ε a meridian sign, hence I(V) = −I(V ⊗ ε), zero in both
branches at a doubly parabolic point. B1459 ran the computation on B1451's 188 points on 16 levels, not on these 24;
the argument covers them as stated, with the same hypotheses (SL(2) factors irreducible on the fibre, a fibre character,
a unipotent meridian). "Observed zero" here reads "zero by B1459".
