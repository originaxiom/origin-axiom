## 2. CLAIMS

1. A Z₃(deck)-symmetric complex symmetric 3×3 Yukawa is a symmetric circulant circ(x,y,y), with eigenvalues x+2y (mult. 1) and x−y (mult. 2 — a degenerate pair). — **COMPUTED**, `hierarchy_bound.py` §1, sympy `Matrix.eigenvals()` on the explicit circulant; confirmed in run output.
2. Hence the deck symmetry must be broken to get three distinct fermion masses. — **derived/ASSERTED** immediately and validly from claim 1 (trivial logical step, not separately "computed" but essentially forced).
3. Weyl's inequality (Mirsky form): for M = M₀ + E with M₀ hollow-symmetric (σ₁(M₀)=σ₂(M₀)+σ₃(M₀), from B1361), |σᵢ(M) − σᵢ(M₀)| ≤ ‖E‖_op for each i, giving m₃ − m₂ − m₁ ≤ 3‖E‖_op. — **CITED** (a standard linear-algebra theorem, Weyl/Mirsky singular value perturbation bound; not itself re-derived from scratch, but a well-known, easily verifiable inequality) applied to the specific data.
4. Numeric bound values: ‖E‖_op ≥ 57.29 GeV = 0.332 m_t (up); ≥ 0.9341 GeV = 0.327 m_b (down); ≥ 0.5569 GeV = 0.313 m_τ (leptons). — **COMPUTED**, `hierarchy_bound.py` §2, direct arithmetic (m₃−m₂−m₁)/3 on the same hard-coded mass table as B1361 (so ultimately **CITED** mass inputs + **COMPUTED** arithmetic on them).
5. The bound is (numerically) nearly attained: minimizing max_i|(UΣUᵀ)ᵢᵢ| over unitaries U (Takagi frames) gives 0.3321 m_t, 0.3272 m_b, 0.3135 m_τ — matching the Weyl bound to ~4 digits (up) / within 0.1–0.2% (down, leptons). — **COMPUTED**, `hierarchy_bound.py` §3, a custom numerical optimizer (60 random restarts × 4000-iteration adaptive random search over a 9-parameter unitary parametrization via matrix exponential of a Hermitian generator). This is a heuristic local-search minimization, not a certified global minimum — flagged by the arc itself as "numerical... matching the bound to four digits."
6. Conclusion: the sector breaking the apex U(1)s must supply "at least a third" of the third-generation Yukawas — not a small correction. — **derived** from claims 4+5, a direct reading of the numbers.
7. Consequence for model-building: "a Froggatt–Nielsen structure with the deck's charges can therefore not be the whole story unless its expansion parameter is not small, or unless the tree-level 27₁27₂27₃ is itself absent... in which case its texture is set by that sector, not by the apex charges alone." — **ASSERTED**, an interpretive/model-building conclusion, not computed.
