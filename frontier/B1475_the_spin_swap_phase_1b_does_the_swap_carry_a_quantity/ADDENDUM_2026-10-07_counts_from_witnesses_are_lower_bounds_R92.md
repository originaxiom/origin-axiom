# B1475 — ADDENDUM (2026-10-07): a count read from witnesses is a lower bound

B1475 reports the fix controls' mirror-invariant spin structures as "m004 2 of 2, t12839 2 of 2, s961 4 of 8". The four
on s961 are those fixed by the reversing automorphisms a bounded word search found. The search does not cover the
isometry group (the audit lane's R92; see B1474's addendum of this date), and all eight spin structures of s961 have a
real odd torsion (B1476, `verification/r92_certificate_family.json`). **"4 of 8" is to be read "at least 4 of 8"; the
exact number is not on the record.** The same holds for o10_150707 (four exhibited of eight; four torsion-real). The
"none" verdicts on the seven swap members are unaffected: they are certified without enumeration (B1476 §2b) and proved
by B1477's Theorem A.
