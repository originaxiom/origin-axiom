## Why this arc — L209(ii), the Wilson-line option

B1283 found that on the SM closing (Y₉ with an SM Wilson line, B1278) every maximal SM-preserving tree-level branch carries
exactly one light Higgs pair and, from the same μ-matrix μ_jk = λ|ε_ijk|⟨N_i⟩, one light colour-triplet pair D_g, D̄_g — the
doublet–triplet problem in its E₆ form. L209(ii) named three candidate splitters: the instanton sector, a ν^c-VEV-induced
mass, and the Wilson line's action on the 10 of SO(10). The third is the classical one — in orbifold and Calabi–Yau model
building the doublet–triplet problem is solved by a Wilson line that acts differently on the doublet and the triplet of the
same 5 — and it is decidable here by exhaustion: the SM-commuting line W lies in the torus generated by Y, β and γ; D and
H_u share (β, γ) = (0, 4/3) but have Y = −1/3 and 1/2, so W's hypercharge component can, in principle, give them different
characters and project the triplet while keeping the doublet. B1278 counted the lines and their spectra by label; it did
not ask which lines lose which component in which generation. This arc asks exactly that — and finds that the question has
an answer of a different kind than a count.

## 2. The structural model ((b), exact, seventeen seconds)

C₂ are two cyclic subgroups of order 19 of the 19-part (ℤ/19)² of Ĥ₁ = (ℤ/76)² and V₄ = {1, χ₁, χ₂, χ₃} is the group of the
family characters; every other character has h¹ = 0. So the multiplicity of a component with character ψ is a function
of ψ alone:

| ψ | m(ψ) = Σ_g h¹(χ_g ψ) |
|---|---|
| 19-part a non-trivial element of C₁ ∪ C₂ (any 2-part) | 3 |
| ψ = 1 | 3 |
| ψ = χ_g, a family character | 2 — generation g projected |
| 19-part outside C₁ ∪ C₂ | 0 |

## 3. The theorem — the triplet's protection

> **Theorem.** On Y₉, every SM-commuting Wilson line with three generations of the quark doublet Q keeps the colour
> triplet D in all three generations. Hence no Wilson line of Y₉ splits the doublets from the triplets: the 0 of §1 is
> not a count but a consequence.
>
> *Proof.* w_D ≡ −2 w_Q modulo the SM roots (the diquark coupling Q Q D of the 27), so ψ_D = ψ_Q⁻². Three generations of
> Q means ψ_Q ∈ K3 = {1} ∪ ((C₁ ∪ C₂) ∖ 1) × V₄, whose elements have order 1, 19 or 38. The square of such a character
> has odd order: its 19-part is doubled — still a non-trivial element of the same C_a, or zero if it was zero — and its
> 2-part is killed. So ψ_D = 1 or ψ_D ∈ (C_a ∖ 1) × {1}, and in both cases m(D) = 3. The only characters that project
> anything are the family characters (§2), of order 2; no square of a letter of the alphabet is one. ∎

The two ingredients are of different natures. **w_D = −2 w_Q is an E₆ fact**, independent of the closing: D is the *only*
component of the 27 whose weight modulo the SM roots is twice a weight (its row is the only one with all coefficients
even), so D is invisible to every 2-torsion character of the SM-commutant torus. **That the projecting characters are
2-torsion** — the family group V₄, which is also the whole 2-part of the alphabet — **is Y₉'s**: B1278's support theorem.
Any closing whose support has the form (C × V) ∖ 1 with C of odd order and V the family group inherits the theorem
verbatim; on Y₃ and Y₆ the alphabet is trivial and there are no SM lines to begin with. The hypothesis is Y₉'s, not the
tower's: B1301 computes the support of Y₁₂, whose alphabet is 2-adic (letters of order 16), and there the conclusion
fails in a controlled way — ψ_D = ψ_Q⁻² still governs, but the squares of the letters land in a part of the support
that carries one generation, so D is thinned to one generation, never to none.

The complement is a theorem of the same table: a multiplet can lose a generation only if its character can be a family
character, i.e. only if its row has an odd coefficient — every row but D's — and the SM conditions then forbid it for
Q, u^c, d^c, L, e^c. So the multiplets a Wilson line of Y₉ can ever thin are **D̄, H_u, H_d, N, ν^c**, always by exactly one
generation per multiplet (the 2-part of a character is a single family character), and the histograms of §1 are the
census of that. The doublet and the triplet of the same 5 are separated by the Wilson line in the wrong direction: the
doublet H_u can be projected, the triplet D never.

*Remark (the prime, by hand).* Δ_{4₁}(t) = t² − 3t + 1 has the roots 6 and 16 = 6⁻¹ in 𝔽₁₉ — Δ(6) = 19, Δ(16) = 11 · 19 —
both primitive 9th roots of unity (6³ ≡ 7, 6⁹ ≡ 1), which is why 19 divides |H₁(Y₉)| = ∏_{ζ⁹=1, ζ≠1} |Δ(ζ)| = 76² and why
its 19-part is (ℤ/19)² (the norm of Δ(ζ₉) is 19²; the 4² comes from Δ(ω) = −4ω at the cube roots).

## 4. What this settles, and what it means for the SM closing

- **L209(ii)'s Wilson-line option is closed, negatively and by theorem.** The light D_g, D̄_g pair of B1283 cannot be
  removed by the closing's discrete data: every one of the 706 464 SM lines is an SM line *with* three generations of the
  colour triplet.
- **The two remaining options of L209(ii) are the μ-term's own.** On the maximal branch g the tree-level VEVs pair the
  other two generations with each other (⟨N_g⟩, ⟨ν^c_g⟩ through |ε_gjk|) and with the mirrors (⟨S_hk⟩ pairs 27_k with 27̄_h,
  ⟨S_kh⟩ pairs 27_h with 27̄_k) and leave generation g's Higgs pair and D pair untouched (B1283); the Wilson line, by the
  theorem, leaves the D pair untouched too. Whatever gives generation g's Higgs pair its μ gives its D pair a mass through
  the same E₆ Clebsch at tree level (N h_u h_d and N D D̄ are one coupling of 27³). **If the instanton sector generates μ,
  it generates m_D at the same scale up to the running between the closing and the weak scale** — a vector-like
  colour-triplet pair with Y = ∓1/3 near μ, the E₆SSM's exotic D quarks in the object's clothing. This is a consequence
  under a stated assumption (that the non-perturbative term respects the E₆ Clebsch — a Wilson-line-dependent instanton
  action need not, since D and H_u carry different characters), not a banked prediction; it goes to L209(ii) as the shape
  its answer must take.
- **B1278 verified by a second method.** The structural model is an independent derivation of B1278's Y₉ enumeration from
  its support theorem and the weights alone; every count agrees.
- Not a chirality statement: the closing is vector-like (B1260) and the 27̄s mirror the 27s; "SM line" names the gauge
  group and the count, not the chiral spectrum. 0 of 19 values; price unchanged.
