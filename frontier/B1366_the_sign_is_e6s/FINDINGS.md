## 0. Seen from above

B1365 proved that on B1364's line no tree-level VEV breaks U(1)_η, and the two exclusions followed — no seesaw, light exotic triplets.
The proof used the standard chain SU(5) ⊂ SO(10) ⊂ E₆ to name the 27's singlets N and ν^c. One could ask whether a different
embedding of the Standard Model in E₆ — a flipped hypercharge, a different SU(2)_L — would put the singlets in different places and
give them opposite charges. It cannot. su(3)_c must be a root A₂ (its 27 branching 3·3 + 3·3̄ + 9·1 has embedding index one) and
su(2)_L a root A₁ commuting with it (six doublets: index one); every A₂ of E₆ is Weyl-conjugate to every other, every root A₁ in the
centraliser A₂ × A₂ gives the right doublet count, and every commuting pair is conjugate to every other. Hypercharge then lives in a
three-dimensional Cartan subspace and must give the 27 the Standard Model's multiset of representations; exactly three vectors do,
one for each of the three colour-weak singlets chosen as e^c, and the Weyl group of the residual su(3)_R — the A₂ commuting with
su(3)_c ⊕ su(2)_L — permutes them. So the Standard Model is in E₆ once, up to conjugacy, and every "flipped" reading is the same
embedding with the singlets renamed. In each the roots commuting with the Standard Model and Y-neutral are one pair ±β, the two
singlets differ by β (an SU(2)_β doublet) and carry the same charge under the remaining direction γ, and the 78's Standard-Model
singlets (±β and the Cartan) are γ-neutral. The sign is E₆'s.

## 2. The theorem

*In any E₆ theory whose E₆-charged fields are 27s and modes of the 78, and for any embedding of the Standard Model in E₆ under which a
27 is one generation with a Higgs pair, one exotic triplet pair and two singlets, the extra U(1)′ that commutes with the Standard Model
and is orthogonal to the SM's centraliser SU(2)_β charges every Standard-Model-singlet field with one sign or zero. Its D-term
restricted to SM-preserving VEVs therefore vanishes only at the origin, and U(1)′ is unbroken at tree level by any such vacuum.*

Because su(3)_c and su(2)_L are regular of index one and the pairs are one orbit, and the hypercharges are one orbit, the statement
is checked on the standard chain and holds for all. The U(1)′ is U(1)_η in the standard names (B1365); the E₆SSM's U(1)_N mixes β into
it and is unbroken only when the line leaves U(1)_β too, in which case the D-terms of β and γ are separate and γ's still has one sign.

## 3. What it means

1. **The wall is the object's, not the design's.** E₆ is the object's (B1268), the 27 and its one coupling are the object's (B1276), the
   line is the Standard Model's own (B1364); with the embedding unique, the sign theorem and the two exclusions — no seesaw with
   y_ν = y_u, exotic triplets at the soft scale with both diquark and leptoquark couplings — hold for every E₆ closing of the object
   whose matter is in 27s, whatever the line and whatever the reading of hypercharge. Only 27̄s (anti-apexes, L215) change the sign.
2. **B1269's numbers are universal**: c(SM) = 5, the double centraliser 13, the U(1)′ — computed there on one chain — are properties of
   the unique embedding.
3. **B1283's (β, γ) table is universal**, and with it the Z′ = Z_η identification of B1365.
4. The three faces: the uniqueness is the root system's (one orbit of A₂'s, one of pairs); the three hypercharges are the arithmetic of
   the SU(3)_R triplet of singlets (one weight at 1, two at 0); the sign is the quantum theory's D-term.

## 4. Caveats

1. The 27 content assumed is the Standard Model's: a generation, one Higgs pair, one exotic triplet pair, two singlets. Hypercharges
   giving the 27 a different content are not the Standard Model in E₆ and are not considered.
2. The regularity of su(3)_c and su(2)_L is the index-one argument (a subalgebra of embedding index one in a simply-laced algebra is a
   root subalgebra); the computation enumerates root subsystems.
3. Tree level; the theorem is about D-flatness. Fayet–Iliopoulos terms need an anomalous U(1) (U(1)′ ⊂ E₆ is not), and Witten's
   axionic terms belong to the C-field U(1)s, which are E₆-neutral.
4. Matter in other E₆ representations (351, 351′) has no source in the design and is not covered.
