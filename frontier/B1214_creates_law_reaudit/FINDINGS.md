## 0. Why before the disposition pass

B1213 established that the field is absent on **89%** of settled arcs, and that the paper's claim
base must therefore not depend on it. That fixed the *base*. It did not fix the *field* — and the
disposition column is about to be filled against a corpus whose law-metadata is mostly missing. The
owner's sequencing is right: audit the field first, or the disposition inherits the gap.

## 1. The rule, fixed before adjudicating

> **LAW** — the arc states a **new general proposition proved here**: a law, theorem, no-go, exact
> characterization, or uniqueness over a family.
> **NOT LAW** — a verification, harvest, audit, census, sealed-cell decision, ledger-write, status
> report, or correction — **even when it uses theorem vocabulary**, which most of them do.

The second clause is what keeps the registry from flooding, which is the failure its designers
avoided when they rejected gating on verdict `PROVED` (~600 rows of audit and census work).
