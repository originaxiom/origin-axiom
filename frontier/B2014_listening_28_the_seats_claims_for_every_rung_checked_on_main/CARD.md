## Derived by hand before the run (main's own)

- **The mirror is not a move at any rung.** Reduction mod 3^{k−1} carries the permutation of a word at mod 3ᵏ to the same
  word's permutation at mod 3^{k−1}, and τ to τ. So τ ∈ G_k implies τ ∈ G_{k−1}. B2008 found τ ∉ G₃, so τ ∉ G_k for every
  k ≥ 3, and ⟨G_k, τ⟩ has order 2|G_k|.
- **With the mirror the abelianisation is ℤ₂ × ℤ₂ for every k ≥ 3.** ⟨G_k, τ⟩ maps onto ⟨G₃, τ⟩, whose abelianisation is
  ℤ₂ × ℤ₂ (B2008). Conversely, τCτ = C⁻¹ kills C's image (its order divides 2 and 3), so the abelianisation is generated by
  the images of the involutions σ and τ.
- **The induced characters:** Ind_S^G 1 has character |Fix(g)|, and Ind_S^G(ρ|_S) = ρ ⊗ Ind_S^G 1 has character
  ρ(g)|Fix(g)|. So their difference is (1 − ρ(g))|Fix(g)|, as agy-002's L1 states.
