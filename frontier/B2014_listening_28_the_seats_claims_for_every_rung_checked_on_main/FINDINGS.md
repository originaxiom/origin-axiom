## What the whole does, read in its own terms

- **The six moves are two walks, each the orbit of one move under ω's exchange.**
  - W₊ is the orbit of L and W₋ the orbit of R. They commute; each is the other's transpose; the mirror swaps them.
  - In each charge sector the walk W₊ is 3ω^{−q} times σ's compression, and W₋ is 3ω^{q} times it. Their sum, the whole's
    unfolding, is 6σ on the neutral sector and −3σ on each charged one (B2004).
  - An oriented combination, W₊ alone or a flux e^{iθ}W₊ + e^{−iθ}W₋, would weight the two charged sectors differently.
    Choosing it is choosing L over R, and the whole does not choose: its unfolding is the mirror-symmetric sum. This is
    B2008's lock seen from inside, and the seats' "chiral walk" is the ingredient that would break it.

  one of them, with no fixed point of C, the halving holds with nothing left out. So the left-out modes are exactly the trace
  of C's three fixed points.
