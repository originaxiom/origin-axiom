## THE ANSWER — yes, and it settles the novelty question negatively

**ℤ₆ IS path-independent.** The computation uses only the 27's decomposition under
$\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)_Y$; **$\mathrm{SU}(5)$ appears nowhere
in it.** Any chain landing the SM algebra acting on the 27 yields $\Z_6$ — through trinification,
through Pati–Salam, or through $\mathrm{SU}(5)$. B862's claim is therefore **true and stronger than
it was stated**: the global form does not depend on the descent path.

**And that is precisely why it is not new.** The kernel is fixed by the *matter representation*, and
"the SM's matter content is $\Z_6$-invariant" **is** the standard fact — the same one that makes
$\mathrm{SU}(5)$ quantise hypercharge. Path-independence does not rescue novelty; it *explains* the
1980 result, by showing the SU(5) embedding was never what was doing the work. **The mechanism is
representation-theoretic and classical.**
