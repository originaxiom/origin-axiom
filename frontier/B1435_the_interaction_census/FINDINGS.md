## What was computed

For each of the 8 800 generation-shaped backgrounds of B1434's census (30 levels of 19 signed word states) and each
pair A, B of its firing doublet sectors: the value of (a, e) ∪ b ∪ h on the relative fundamental class, through
E₆'s SL(2)_β-invariant ε and the spin-0 character η the pair couples to, at three primes.

- **The chain.** z = [t|λ] − [λ|t]; C = prism(σ) + filling(Φ_*σ − σ), σ the fibre's relative 2-chain. ∂C = z is
  asserted as an identity of integer chains on every level (up to 1 557 cells).
- **Each class is a line**, so a single coupling's vanishing is invariant and its value is a normalisation. (B1438
  finds the normalisation in which it is a number.)
- **Asserted on every background:** the firing class is interior; Y(A, B) = Y(B, A); h¹(η) = 1; the three primes
  agree. **No disagreement on any of the 8 800.**

fifteen allowed pairs fall into five blocks — ten·ten (the 78's H_u and D), ten·five (the 27̄'s H_d and D̄),
five·five (the 27's ten), ten·ν^c, five·ν^c — and on those backgrounds a block is one number. On the other
752 the sectors are distinct modules and a block can be mixed.

## What it means, and the fence

- **The frame's zero modes do interact, and not uniformly.** The cubic that would be an up-type coupling exists on
  most backgrounds and on every own-level one; the cubic that would be a down or lepton coupling is absent at the
  own level. Whether that is a hierarchy or an obstruction depends on a physics reading this arc does not have.
- **The web seat's withdrawn pattern is real on a proper chain** — on the own-level states of the architecture. It
  is not the pattern of the root's tower: on s961 everything couples.
- **Unchanged:** main's class index on reducible non-split modules; non-semisimple backgrounds; an index is not a
  generation count; the frame is carried to every state as an instrument. **Added:** "allowed" means
  charge-allowed, E₆'s Clebsch–Gordan constants are not computed; which spin-0 sector is a physical Higgs is not
  asked; a coupling here is not a Yukawa coupling of a four-dimensional field. 0 of 19.
