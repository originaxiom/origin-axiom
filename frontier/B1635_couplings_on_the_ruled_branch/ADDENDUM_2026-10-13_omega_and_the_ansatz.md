# B1635 — Addendum, 2026-10-13: "every coupling is degenerate at ω" scoped; the count's assumptions named (B1640)

**§3's sentence overstated the sealed cell.** K5(i) tested the single pieces of T ⊗ T (D, O⁺, O⁻ alone), and those are
degenerate at ω. This arc's own JSON lists nine T̄ ⊗ T members as non-degenerate at ω (`K5_nondegenerate_at_omega`), and
B1640 finds T ⊗ T couplings that mix the symmetric and antisymmetric pieces non-degenerate there too. The sentence should
read: "Every single piece of T ⊗ T is degenerate at ω; T̄ ⊗ T and mixed T ⊗ T couplings are not." The independent
archive audit of the owner's earlier weave runs found the same with one example (a weight-3 symmetric plus twice the
antisymmetric coupling, masses 3 : 2 : 1).

**The count's assumptions.** 2 + Σ_s (2 d(k_s) − 1) counts holomorphic vector-valued modular forms with every field in
T and one Higgs per sector. Holomorphy and the minimal Kähler term are the supersymmetric modular-flavour ansatz
(Feruglio 2017), not derived from the principle; without them the count is larger (non-holomorphic modular forms;
non-minimal Kähler terms). The count is a lower bound under those assumptions.

**The hand.** The pieces and dimensions here hold for the record's multiplier (ρ = lift⁻¹, B1634). Under its conjugate
the weight-1 symmetric space is empty (B1640, cell B).
