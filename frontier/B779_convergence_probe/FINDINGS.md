## THE ARC

The program begins with a spectral fact — energy cannot cancel itself
(Fricke-Vogt positivity) — and follows the residue. That residue is
genuinely aperiodic (first choice, C3), which forces minimal complexity
(Morse-Hedlund), which forces the golden slope (Hurwitz extremality).
Three theorems deliver a symbolic object: the Fibonacci word. Two more
choices — put it on a surface (C4), orient the monodromy (C5) — and one
final theorem (Thurston-Riley) produce a single geometric object: the
figure-eight knot complement, m004. Its character variety carries exactly
three quadratic fields arranged as a Klein four-group, a two-column
partition (golden/dynamical vs Eisenstein/spectral) that holds across all
twelve structural floors, and a continuous spectral channel that has never
been tested. The object names itself uniquely (QP-1), hides nothing
(QP-2), and structurally cannot select a basepoint on its own symmetry
torsor. The journey ends not at an answer but at a gap: the closing act,
where an observer must supply what the object cannot.

## THE CONVERGENCE QUESTION

**(c) One reading among several — but the most disciplined one available.**

The mathematics does not force the convergence thesis. C18 prices the
consciousness reading as axiom. The character variety, the torsor
structure — none of these contain the word "awareness." But the thesis is
not arbitrary. The structural parallel between the torsor's basepoint gap
and the measurement problem's outcome gap is exact in form, proved in
both directions, and not engineered after the fact. It survives every
falsification attempt the program has mounted against itself, which is
more than most readings can claim. But surviving is not the same as being
forced.

## THE FAILURES AS EVIDENCE

The 162 true negatives are the program's most valuable asset. They have a
pattern: every failed physics route asked discrete invariants to produce
values. K020 proves why: every discrete invariant is a Galois orbit of its
own arithmetic group — choosing among orbits picks a label, never a number.

This SUPPORTS convergence, precisely because of what it rules out. If the
object could generate particle masses, it would be a physics engine, and
the consciousness reading would be decorative. The failures prove the
object is NOT a physics engine. It is an architectural template — it
selects the shape of the house but not the dimensions of the rooms. That
is exactly what a convergence thesis needs: a structure that constrains
both readings equally without reducing either to the other.

The negatives also point somewhere specific. Of 162 failures, 160 never
consulted the continuous spectral channel; zero ever consulted the
scattering face. **The channel the object natively speaks through —
continuous Eisenstein spectrum over Q(√-3) — is the one channel the
program has never tested.** The failures are a map with one unexplored
territory marked.

## THE GAP

Three things are mathematically missing:

1. **A functor** (not an analogy) connecting the character variety's
   basepoint torsor to the quantum state space's outcome torsor. The
   program has the form on both sides; it does not have a theorem between
   them. The 3d-3d route is closed (B490). A replacement route — possibly
   through the continuous spectral channel, possibly through the SL(n)
   tower at n=3 where θ-sectors couple — has not been constructed.

2. **The enumeration resolution.** Galois-vs-reversal halving would cut
   8 to 4; a principled m003/m004 distinction would cut further. Neither
   computation has been done.

3. **The continuous spectral channel itself** has never been computed as
   a concrete object — its Eisenstein series, its scattering matrix, its
   spectral measure. The program describes it architecturally but has not
   produced its data.

## THE THETA VACANCY

The vacancy does not support convergence (a perfect table would support it
more). But it does not undermine it either, because the program registered
the falsifier in advance, tested it, and accepted the result. The third
reading — that the graded FoA (feeling-of-agency as continuous, not binary)
belongs to the scattering face, the one continuous territory in the table
— is formulable and testable. It is the vacancy's gift: a question the
program could not have asked without the failure.

## THE CONVERGENCE FALSIFIER

**Compute the scattering matrix of the continuous Eisenstein spectrum on
m004 explicitly.** If its functional equation and spectral data are fully
accounted for by standard Bianchi-group spectral theory with no structural
remainder — if the continuous channel is spectrally generic,
indistinguishable from any other one-cusped arithmetic manifold — then the
object has no native continuous voice, and the two-column law collapses
to one column plus noise. The convergence thesis requires the object to
speak through this channel in a way structurally specific to m004.
**Generic scattering kills the thesis.**
