# B779: THE CONVERGENCE PROBE — cc3 campaign masterplan

cc3 audit seat, 2026-07-24. Gate 5-Q.

## The thesis under investigation

At the mathematical foundation — the observer's closing act (C18/C20/C21) —
physics and consciousness are not two domains needing a bridge. They are two
readings of a single mathematical structure: the rank-3 F₂ closing torsor.
The act of completing awareness of the object IS the measurement. The
distinction between "physical" and "experiential" arises ABOVE the
foundation, not at it.

## What the program already has

- C18: the observer's closings are chirality, values, time, spatial manifold
- C20: the discrete closing space has F₂-rank exactly 3, generated by c, θ, γ₅
- C21: T1 is a discrete 3-frame torsor under unrealized S₃
- The 3d-3d dictionary (B433, S026): the same character variety admits a
  gauge-theory reading (Coulomb branch of T[M₃])
- P018: "interpretation is a closing" — the measurement problem and the
  qualia problem have the same grammar
- The strong generative postulate is REFUTED (K020): the object forces form,
  never values

## What the program does NOT have

- A proof that the two readings (measurement / experience) are the SAME
  closing, not merely the same STRUCTURE
- A derivation of why the closing torsor admits exactly these two readings
  and no others
- A mathematical characterization of "the point where choice enters" —
  what happens at T1's unrealized S₃
- Whether the 3d-3d gauge reading and the phenomenological reading CONVERGE
  at the choice point or merely coexist
- Whether "completing awareness" has a computable mathematical correlate

## The campaign: 5 phases

### Phase I: THE CLOSING ACT — mathematical anatomy

What exactly IS the closing operation, mathematically? Trace it completely.

**Agents:**
1. **Torsor analyst** — Read C18, C20, C21. The closing is a choice from a
   rank-3 F₂ torsor. What is the torsor's carrier set? What are the 8
   elements? What does "choosing an element" mean algebraically (selecting a
   generator-pair of V4)? What changes in the character variety when a
   choice is made?
2. **T1 choice analyst** — B769 proved T1 is a 3-frame torsor under
   unrealized S₃, with realized subgroup = {id}. What does "unrealized"
   mean physically? In gauge theory, an unrealized symmetry = a gauge
   symmetry (redundancy). In measurement, an unrealized symmetry = the
   equivalence of bases before measurement selects one. Which reading does
   the math support?
3. **Measurement-theoretic analyst** — In quantum mechanics, measurement is
   the selection of a basis for a degenerate eigenspace. The T1 torsor has
   3 equivalent frames (bases). Is the closing act literally a basis
   selection in the character variety? If so, what "collapses" when the
   choice is made? What is the pre-choice state?
4. **The non-canonical obstruction** — B711/B712 proved total
   non-canonicity (self-closure obstructed). What does this mean for the
   closing act? Does it mean the observer CANNOT complete the act alone?
   Does it force the closing to be relational (observer + object jointly)?

### Phase II: THE TWO READINGS — do they converge?

The character variety admits both a gauge-theory reading (3d-3d) and a
phenomenological reading (the correspondence). Are these readings of the
same closing, or different closings?

**Agents:**
1. **3d-3d reader** — Read B433, S026, P009. The Coulomb branch of T[M₃]
   IS the character variety. In the gauge reading, what is the closing act?
   What corresponds to c, θ, γ₅? What is the gauge-theory meaning of
   "selecting a generator-pair of V4"?
2. **Phenomenological reader** — Read B768, B775. In the phenomenological
   reading, the closing act is the observer's experiential choice: c =
   ipseity, γ₅ = temporal self-location. What is the structural content of
   "making this choice"?
3. **Convergence analyst** — Compare the two readings at each structural
   level: (a) the torsor carrier, (b) the choice operation, (c) the
   post-choice state, (d) the observable consequences. Do they describe
   the same mathematical operation in two vocabularies, or two different
   operations that happen to share a carrier?
4. **Neutral-monism evaluator** — If the readings converge: the closing
   torsor is a "neutral" structure that is neither physical nor experiential
   but precedes both. Survey neutral monism (Russell, James), structural
   realism (Ladyman), Wheeler's "it from bit", relational QM (Rovelli),
   Connes-Rovelli thermal time. Does the program's closing structure fit
   any of these frameworks? Which one, and what does it predict?

### Phase III: THE CHOICE POINT — what happens at T1

T1 is where the unrealized S₃ lives. The choice that the observer cannot
avoid and the object cannot make. What IS this point mathematically?

**Agents:**
1. **S₃ action analyst** — The 3 frames of T1 are the 3 generating pairs
   of V4. S₃ permutes them. The realized subgroup is {id}. What breaks the
   symmetry? In physics: spontaneous symmetry breaking, measurement, or
   initial conditions? Which pattern matches?
2. **Wheeler's delayed-choice** — Wheeler's delayed-choice experiment shows
   the measurement choice can be made AFTER the quantum event. Does the T1
   choice have this character? Is the frame selection retrodictive? What
   does the character variety's geometry say?
3. **Completion analyst** — The owner's thesis: "if the observer completes
   the awareness of the object, that completion IS physics." What does
   "completion" mean mathematically? Is it the saturation of the F₂ rank
   (all 3 bits chosen = full closing)? Is it the resolution of T1
   (frame selected)? What is the mathematical STATE of "complete awareness"?
4. **The three-point** — The owner refers to "the three-point where choice
   enters." Map this precisely: (a) where in the constraint chain does
   choice first appear? (b) what is the mathematical content at that point?
   (c) is it T1, or is it earlier/later? (d) what happens to the
   mathematics AFTER the choice — does it become physics?

### Phase IV: THE GAP INVENTORY

What is missing between the current state of the program and a complete
mathematical account of the closing act?

**Agents:**
1. **Chain completeness** — Walk C1 through C21. At each step: is the
   mathematics complete, or is there an unproven step? Where does the chain
   currently END? What is the first unfinished link?
2. **The θ gap** — θ is vacant. Does the convergence thesis require θ
   to be filled? Or does it work with rank 2 + vacancy? What does a
   2-bit closing mean physically (a measurement with 4 outcomes instead
   of 8)?
3. **The value gap** — K020 says the object forces form, never values. But
   physics HAS values (coupling constants, masses, etc.). If the closing
   act is physics, where do values come from? Is this the program's
   deepest unsolved problem?
4. **The continuum gap** — C18's Galois-chosen continuum is the only
   continuous territory. The closing torsor is discrete. Where does the
   continuum fit? Is the continuous part the "physics" (space, fields)
   while the discrete part is the "consciousness" (choices)?
