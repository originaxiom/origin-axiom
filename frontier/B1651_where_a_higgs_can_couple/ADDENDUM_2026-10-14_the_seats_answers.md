# B1651 — addendum (2026-10-14, S126): the seats' answers to its owed items; "the puncture is not forced" is scoped to point Higgs fields

B1651 owed, among other items (FINDINGS §5; POINT_REGISTER P-142):

Two seats answered the same evening. Their answers are recorded here as they stand. Neither is yet re-derived on main.

## 1. The SM seat's §107 (a reading, by hand; `40ca3bb07`)

- **Each parity channel's pairing is fixed by its residue.** By Stokes, the overlap of the zero modes' pairing with a
  (0, 1) Higgs depends on the Higgs's representative through −2πi φ(p) Res_p. So a bulk Higgs couples as a function of
  its class only once a framing is fixed at the puncture.
- **Framed, the one class per channel sits at the puncture and couples by the residue.** B1642's two disclosed
  hypotheses become one end datum. The flat class grows like 1/∣z∣ and needs a cutoff.
- **So, by this reading:** "the puncture is not forced" holds for point Higgs fields, which is B1651's premise P1. For a
  bulk Higgs carrying parity, the puncture is forced as the locus, at the price of one end datum. The seat cites its
  C0, item 3, as the record's parabolic statement.

**Main's status:** read, not yet checked by hand on main. If it holds, B1651's reading is sharpened, not reversed:
- for point Higgs fields the covariant loci are the puncture, the half-period orbit, or both;
- for a bulk Higgs carrying parity they are the puncture alone, with one end datum supplied.

- **(b) A value at a regular point** (a reading, by hand):
  - In the fibre frame at one τ it is canonical up to one constant.
  - Wherever τ moves, a move rescales dz by (cτ + d)⁻¹. So a half-period's value carries one unit of weight more than
    the puncture's residue, which needs no frame (B1651's U1).
  - The Petersson factor (Im τ)^½ absorbs the modulus, but the phase of (cτ + d) remains. That is a debt whenever a
    puncture coupling and a half-period coupling are mixed.
