# B1986 — listening card 4 — the unweighted unfolding on functions over the common point's structure: what persists and what decays

Written 2026-11-25, before any run, unattended, on the listening line. Data-free. 0 of 19.

## Why

- **B1984 and B1985:** the one structure the whole forces is the common point. Its neighbourhood carries the cube's
  rotation group at every scale and opens only along the body diagonals.
- **GENESIS's law:** "everything that is allowed happens … it does not weigh them." The unfolding of all histories is
  then the sum of the moves acting on functions. Its spectrum says how fast the whole forgets each kind of structure
  per move, and which kinds it never forgets.
- **This is the first place a field (FK15) can be read from behaviour.** A field is what the whole carries on
  functions, and it should not be put in by hand.

## The system (nothing chosen)

- **Functions on** the cusp's leaf mod N over the common point, for N = 9, 25, 49 (the cube's level at each prime), and
  on the whole cusp's leaf mod p for p = 5, 7, 11, 13.
- **The unfolding,** carried both ways because GM5b (inverse moves) is open in the grammar:
  - W₊ = P_L + P_R, the positive words;
  - W± = P_L + P_L⁻¹ + P_R + P_R⁻¹, with inverses.

  P_g is the permutation the move g induces. Every history of length n is counted once (Wⁿ).

## What is watched (described)

- **On each orbit:** the eigenvalues of W₊ and W± and their moduli relative to the largest (the decay per move), and the
  eigenvalues of largest modulus other than the constant.
- **On the cube's orbits** (mod 9): the decomposition by the cube group's irreducible representations (1, sign, 2, 3,
  3′), and each irreducible's eigenvalues under W₊ and W±.
- **What persists:** the non-constant modes with modulus equal to the constant's, which never decay.
