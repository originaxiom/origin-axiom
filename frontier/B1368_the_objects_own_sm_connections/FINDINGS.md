## 0. Seen from above

B1367 left one door: chiral bulk matter on the cusped object with the Standard Model unbroken. Such a connection commutes with the
Standard Model, so its image lies in c(SM) = SL(2)_β × ℂ*² (B1364, B1366): a representation of the knot group into SL(2)_β — the
geometric one or any other point of the character variety — times a character on the two Cartan directions Y and γ. Every sector of
the bulk matter is then a spin of SL(2)_β times a character z_w, and B1351's index lives only on cusp-fixed non-trivial sectors. Two
facts close the door. First, the spin-0 sectors carry a character alone: they are cusp-fixed only when z_w = 1, and then the local
system is trivial, the Higgs field vanishes on the weight and the index is −χ(∅) = 0 — for every flat connection of the family,
whatever its SL(2)_β part. Second, the two halves of a generation never share a spin: in the 27 the 10 of SU(5) (Q, u^c, e^c) has β = 0
and the 5̄ (d^c, L) sits in the doublets; in the 78 the 10 sits in the (2,20) and the 5̄ in the (1,35). So in either frame one half of
every generation is never chiral. For the geometric representation the other half fails too: the longitude lifts with trace −2
(Calegari), so ρ(λ) = −(unipotent) has no fixed vector on V₂ ⊗ ℂ_z for any z, and no spin-½ sector is ever cusp-fixed. The only
cusp-fixed non-trivial sector is the SL(2)_β adjoint — the hyperbolic deformation, Standard-Model-neutral. What the object does have
is a golden spectrum: the Alexander polynomial z² − 3z + 1 and the geometric representation's twisted Alexander polynomial
z² − 4z + 1, whose roots φ^{±2} and 2 ± √3 are the non-unitary characters at which Standard-Model-charged bulk pairs become massless
— vector-like, and with two complex parameters a doublet can be brought to a root while every triplet stays off it: the object's own
doublet–triplet split, B1302's mechanism on m004 itself. With B1351 (closed closings vector-like), B1367 (apexes cannot split) and
this arc (bulk matter on the object cannot be chiral with the Standard Model unbroken), the E₆ route from m004 has no chirality
mechanism compatible with the Standard Model in any frame the record has — save the disc-convention question the bridge lane is
adjudicating.

## 2. The two theorems

**(i) The geometric representation.** ρ(λ) has trace −2 (Calegari, for the holonomy of a hyperbolic knot complement; computed here
exactly), so on V₂ ⊗ ℂ_z the longitude acts as −(unipotent) whatever the character (the character is trivial on λ ∈ [π, π]) and
has no fixed vector: no spin-½ sector is cusp-fixed. Spin-0 sectors are cusp-fixed only for z = 1, the trivial local system. Hence
every Standard-Model-charged sector of the 78 and of the 27 has N = 0 under B1351 (ii), and the single cusp-fixed non-trivial sector
is the neutral adjoint of SL(2)_β.

**(ii) Every flat connection of the family.** For any representation of the knot group into SL(2)_β and any character, the spin-0
sectors depend on the character alone, are cusp-fixed only when trivial, and carry no index. In the 27 the 10 of SU(5) is spin 0
(β = 0 on Q, u^c, e^c) and in the 78 the 5̄ is spin 0 (it lies in the adjoint of SU(6)); so in each frame one half of a generation is
never chiral, and a chiral generation — a 10 with its 5̄ — cannot arise from bulk matter with the Standard Model unbroken. The
spin-½ half could be cusp-fixed only at the eight λ-parabolic points: four are the SU(2) dihedral representations (unitary: no Higgs
field, vector-like by duality) and four the golden reducible points m = ±φ^{±1}, where a spin-½ sector with z = m^{∓1} is cusp-fixed
and non-unitary — half a generation at most, and in the 78-frame a chiral 10 without its 5̄ would be anomalous.

## 3. What it means

1. **L216 is closed in the seat's frame.** With B1351 (every closed closing vector-like), B1367 (point-localised matter cannot split
   doublets from triplets) and this arc (bulk matter on the cusped object cannot be chiral with the Standard Model unbroken), the E₆
   route from the object has no chirality mechanism compatible with the Standard Model in any frame the record has. What the object
   fixes — E₆, the 27 and its one coupling, the unique Standard-Model embedding, the Standard Model's own line, U(1)_η, the hollow
   texture — stands; what it does not supply is a chiral generation that survives the Higgs sector.
2. **The disc conventions.** B1351 (ii)'s "torus acyclic ⇒ N = 0" holds under the whole-torus and annular conventions of ∂⁺M (rows
   A–C of the I-26 table) and is scoped by the audit lane's R23 for the disc conventions (rows D–F), where the physical-bridge lane
   reads a conditional net three from the ends. This arc's theorems (i) and (ii) are stated in the seat's frame; the frame theorem's
   first half — the spin-0 sectors are trivial when cusp-fixed and have no Higgs field on the weight — holds in every convention, so
   even there the spin-0 half of a generation has no Morse structure to partition.
3. **The object is golden.** The Alexander polynomial's roots φ^{±2} and the twisted Alexander polynomial's 2 ± √3 are the characters
   at which the Standard-Model-charged bulk sectors have zero modes; both are non-unitary (|z| ≠ 1), so the massless pairs sit on
   non-unitary characters — the object's Higgs field is what makes them massless. With two complex parameters the family can bring a
   doublet weight to φ² while every triplet weight stays off the roots: the doublet–triplet split that B1302 found on Y₁₂ exists on
   the object itself, vector-like. The twisted Alexander polynomial z² − 4z + 1 agrees with the hyperbolic torsion polynomial of the
   figure-eight knot in the literature.
4. The three faces: Calegari's −2 is the hyperbolic geometry's (the longitude's lift); the spin split of the 10 and the 5̄ is the
   root system's (β vanishes on the 10 of the 27 and on the adjoint of SU(6)); the index is the quantum theory's.

## 4. Caveats

1. The frame is B1351's (Pantev–Wijnholt on the cusped 3-manifold, abelian Higgs data per sector); the conventions caveat is §3.2.
2. The placement in SL(2)_β is forced by the Standard Model's centraliser (B1366); a connection leaving a larger group unbroken has an
   image in a smaller centraliser, inside the same SL(2)_β × ℂ*², and the sector argument applies unchanged.
3. The Galois conjugate of the geometric representation (u ↦ ū) has the same longitude trace; the unitary points are vector-like by
   duality without appeal to the index; the golden reducible points' partitions are not computed (they could carry at most half a
   generation).
4. Both matter frames of the record — the 27 twisted by the E₆ connection (I-26) and the 78 of the 7d theory — are covered; a frame
   in which the 10 and the 5̄ shared a spin would need a matter representation of E₆ other than the 27 and the 78, and the object
   supplies none.
