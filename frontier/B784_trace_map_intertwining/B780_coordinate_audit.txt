B780 MISLABELS iota (inversion) as theta (reversal).

  theta (word reversal): IDENTITY on all 8 Lawton coords, ALL ranks.
  iota (inversion):      permutation (1 4)(2 5)(3 8)(6 7) at SL(3),
                         trivial at SL(2), trivial on V0 (Sym^2 locus).

  The mislabeling arises because theta and iota coincide at SL(2)
  (tr(M) = tr(M^-1) and tr(XY) = tr(YX)). They separate at SL(3).

  The 'rank-onset at SL(3)' signature in B780 is REAL but belongs to
  iota, not theta. theta has NO rank-onset (trivial at all ranks on traces).
  theta's non-triviality is matrix-level (Sym^2(AB) != Sym^2(BA)), not trace-level.
