## The one structural fact that makes this lane work

These experiments **adiabatically vary the phason** — *"the relative phase between the modulation
and the underlying lattice"* — and pump states across the sample. **That phase is the Sturmian
intercept.**

B8091 (today) showed the founding rule forces the **slope** (golden) and never supplies the
**intercept**; the intercept is the circle-torsor the object leaves free. **The experimental
community's standard control parameter is precisely that free coordinate.** So the banked prediction
shape — the *function* `ρ ↦ edge content` — is not merely falsifiable in principle: **the knob is
already on the apparatus.**

## THE DISCIPLINE THIS LANE NEEDS — stated before anyone gets excited

**Gap labeling is standard, mature, and already measured.** The integers labelling spectral gaps of a
quasiperiodic chain are given by the **gap-labeling theorem**, and edge-mode counts follow from
bulk-boundary correspondence. **Therefore:**

> **A prediction of the form "`N` edge modes at intercept `ρ`" is NOT automatically novel. If it
> coincides with what gap labeling already forces, it is a re-derivation, not a prediction.**

**The prereg must state, before any comparison, what the gap-labeling theorem predicts for the same
configuration, and where our claim DIFFERS.** A claim that agrees everywhere is confirmation of
standard theory and must be banked as such. **This is the B724 look-elsewhere discipline transposed
to an experimental lane**, and it is the single most likely way this lane produces a false positive.
