1. carry the moves as symmetries with a vacuum selected by a run dynamics (the D1 criterion), with a gauge completion
   over 𝕎 that keeps the weak roots and a hypercharge, or a proof that none exists (P-140). In the audit lane's frame,

6. fix the kinetic normalisation (D5; P-134);
7. **split the down quarks from the charged leptons:** no E₆ cubic with 27 Higgs content alone can (W55, W56; the SM
   seat's clause).

**And above all of them, the selection principle** (GENESIS FK9; P-139). Every "which" is still free: τ, the
