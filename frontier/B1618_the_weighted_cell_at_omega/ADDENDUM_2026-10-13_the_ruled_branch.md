# B1618 — Addendum, 2026-10-13: on the ruled branch the Dirac masses at ω are not degenerate (B1640)

This arc's argument is that the inner automorphisms make every Dirac coupling diagonal in T's three parity lines at every
τ, and U cycles the lines, so the masses are degenerate at ω for every weight. B1634 has since shown that on the ruled
branch (GENESIS v1.33) the inner automorphisms are not moves: ⟨L, R⟩ meets Inn(F₂) only in ⟨Δ⁴⟩. Without them the
premise fails. B1635's own sealed read-outs, and B1640's live members, give **distinct masses at ω under T̄ ⊗ T**, this
arc's own tensor (e.g. 1 : 0.674 : 0.448 at k = 4).

**What stands.** The computation, and the statement where the inner automorphisms act (the sign or swap branches, or if the
records' base point is given moves of its own). **What changes.** On the ruled branch, τ = ω's retirement (GENESIS v1.35)
stands as "no hierarchy found in any sample", not as "degenerate for every weight". The near-fixed-point regime is not
computed. GENESIS v1.42 records this.
