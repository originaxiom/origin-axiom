## 3. What it means (the pre-written readings, applied, with the referee's corrections)

- **D2 fails, both typical.** The pre-written reading for this outcome: the measured depths do not separate the clocks,
  and the clock is not decided by these numbers. It holds as written, and the power figure says why.
  - The test could not have decided the clock: a world from the critical clock fails D2 about 81% of the time.
  - The 45% prior was above the instrument's power.
  - The p_nn read-out goes the other way, as it does for 67% of random hierarchies.
  - **The direction of the marginals is B1813's but generic.** The data are deep, as stage 2a disclosed before the
    seal, and every random hierarchy A0 rejects is rejected on the deep side. It carries nothing beyond "the data are
    deep". The clock question goes to the theory side (P-438, P-439).
- **D1 and D1h hold.** The pre-written reading: CONSISTENT, NOT SPECIFIC; the two ingredients are not needed for
  typicality at this resolution.
  - **With the referee's corrections, what this means:** the measured six are not atypical at the 5% level among the
    geometric clock's resolved worlds, at the chosen floor 10⁻¹².
  - **It does not mean the model comes close:**
    - the nearest unforced world is 11σ away;
    - the draws as isolated as the data are mostly cusp-deep worlds where the radii are held;
    - the hold fails at the floor 10⁻⁸;
    - 73% of random hierarchies pass too.
  - **The resolution.** D1's q95 (13.4σ) is coarser than the lepton shift log 3/s6 = 11.0σ, so nothing about c follows
    from comparing D1 with D1h.
  - **D1h's nearest draw** sits on the line and in the region of B1735's and B1749's best rows. That is expected,
    since the by-hand configuration was informed by them, and it adds no independent support.
- **With the mixings (the nine)** the by-hand world is atypical (p_nn 0.0017); the unforced one is not (0.085).
- **Against W57** (the SM seat's, harvest row 1099). W57 found the world atypical of the seed's own measure in the
