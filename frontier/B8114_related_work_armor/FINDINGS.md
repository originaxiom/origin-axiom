## 1. Lisi's E8 unification, and the Distler-Garibaldi no-go

**Cites:** `arXiv:0711.0770` · `arXiv:0905.2658` · `Comm. Math. Phys. 298 (2010) 419`

Lisi (arXiv:0711.0770) proposed embedding gravity and the Standard Model in a single real form of E8. Distler-Garibaldi (arXiv:0905.2658; Comm. Math. Phys. 298 (2010) 419) proved this cannot work. THEOREM 1.3, verbatim: 'There are no ToE subgroups in (the transfer of) the complex E8 nor in any real form of E8.' A ToE subgroup must satisfy (ToE1) a compact G centralizing SL(2,C), (ToE2) no irreps m⊗n with m+n>4, and (ToE3) that V_{2,1} be a COMPLEX representation of G -- i.e. admitting no antilinear J with J^4 = 1 commuting with the action. ToE3 IS CHIRALITY. The obstruction is that the centralizer of any admissible SL(2,C) forces a self-conjugate structure, so the theory is non-chiral.

> **What we do NOT claim that they did.** We never embed the gravitational SL(2,C) into the same exceptional algebra as the internal symmetry -- the gravity sector here is a separate, parameter-free 2+1 theory (B8099-B8113) -- and our landing is on E6, not E8, so Theorem 1.3's hypotheses are not our setup twice over. More importantly we make NO CLAIM THAT THE OBJECT SUPPLIES CHIRALITY: B713/B760 prove it does not, B1028 prices it as a declared input, and B582 constructs it only under an inserted closing. THE PROPERTY DISTLER-GARIBALDI PROVED UNATTAINABLE IS THE ONE WE INDEPENDENTLY BANK AS A NEGATIVE. Their no-go kills a hypothesis we decline to assume.
