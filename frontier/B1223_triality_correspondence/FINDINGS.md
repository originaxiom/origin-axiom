## The correspondence, as assembled

The object supplies two arithmetic data, both banked:

- **V₄** — the three forced quadratic faces ℚ(√−3) · ℚ(√5) · ℚ(√−15), a Klein four-group
  (**B730**; re-verified here: {1,−3,5,−15} is closed under squarefree product and every element
  is an involution).
- **S₃** — the classifying cubic field K = ℚ[x]/(x³−12x−5), disc 6237, totally real, Gal = S₃
  (cloud seat).

And the dimension cascade lands on **D₄**: E₆ (78) on the 27 with one invariant → 52 = dim F₄;
F₄ (52) on the 26 with two invariants → **28 = dim Spin(8) = dim tri(𝕆)** — which **B904** built as
an *exact nullspace over ℚ*, not hypothesised.

Now the algebra side. **Z(Spin(8)) = V₄**, whose three nontrivial elements are exactly the three
8-dimensional representations (vector, spinor⁺, spinor⁻). **Out(D₄) = S₃**, triality, permuting
exactly those three. Together: **V₄ ⋊ S₃ = S₄**, the full automorphism data of D₄ modulo inner.

**So the object's arithmetic appears to *be* D₄'s automorphism data.** The cloud seat had one half
("an S₃-torsor is exactly a full trialitarian twist of D₄; the classifying cubic field is the
twisting datum such a form requires"). Nobody had joined the halves.

## Why it had to be tested rather than written up

Both groups are small and generic. **Any** three quadratic fields whose discriminants multiply to a
square form a V₄ — that is genus theory, not a distinction. And **almost every** cubic field has
Gal = S₃. So the two isomorphisms are, on their own, exactly the kind of short-catalogue coincidence
this programme's method section refuses to accept as evidence — the same species as the N=4 order
coincidence corrected today, and as the E₆ genericity §2 exists to defuse.

**The discriminating question is the ACTION.** Triality is not merely "an S₃ next to a V₄"; it is S₃
permuting V₄'s three involutions **faithfully** — a semidirect product V₄ ⋊ S₃ = S₄. So: does the
object's S₃ act on the object's three faces?

## THE COMPUTATION, AND THE REFUTATION

The S₃'s own quadratic resolvent is the field generated by √disc:

> disc K = 6237 = 3⁴·7·11 → squarefree part **77** → the resolvent is **ℚ(√77)**.

**ℚ(√77) is not among {ℚ(√−3), ℚ(√5), ℚ(√−15)}.** The S₃'s quadratic data is **arithmetically
disjoint** from the V₄ of faces. Therefore the compositum has Galois group **V₄ × S₃ — a DIRECT
product** — and the S₃ acts on the faces **trivially**.

Triality requires the **semidirect** V₄ ⋊ S₃ = S₄. Direct ≠ semidirect.

> **VERDICT: the correspondence is an isomorphism of small groups, not a structural
> correspondence.** The pieces are individually real — B730's V₄ is proved, cloud's S₃ is proved,
> B904's 28-dimensional triality algebra is banked over ℚ, and the dimension cascade is exact. What
> is false is that they compose into D₄'s automorphism data. **The action does not match, and the
> action is the whole content of triality.**
