## 1. The three senses, separated

**The test (the SM seat's, adopted here with credit; its reading of 2026-10-24, harvest row 1379):** a choice is a
**convention** when the joint action's symmetry carries every option to every other (a move, the mirror, or a relabelling
the moves permute); a choice that fails the test is information — forced by the joint action, or a free number counted
against the 19. "The observer's" is neither, so the word hid which of the two it was. A convention is one choice for the
whole: once two are made, the relation between them is physics unless the symmetry carries the pairs jointly.
