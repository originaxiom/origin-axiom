> **Lemma (region swap).** Let σ be an involution of the cusp torus T² and g a σ-odd field (g∘σ = −g) whose zero set Z is a closed 1-manifold (transverse zeros). Then σ maps {g > 0} onto {g < 0}, so χ({g > 0}) = χ({g < 0}); and χ({g > 0}) + χ({g < 0}) = χ(T²) − χ(Z) = 0 − 0 (compactly supported Euler characteristics add over the decomposition, and a closed 1-manifold has χ = 0). Hence **χ(∂⁺M) = χ(∂⁻M) = 0 for every θ-odd field with transverse zeros.**

On m004's cusp θ is z ↦ −z (R62/R71's table; the four fixed points are the corners, on which every θ-odd field vanishes). The SM seat's caveat — "if the (±2,0) coefficient vanished, the (±2,±1) mode would lead and cut the torus into eight rectangles, χ(∂⁺M) = ±4" — concerns a **non-transverse** zero set (the exact product mode has eight crossings; the four open rectangles give +4, their closures glued at the crossings give −4): with crossings ∂⁺M is not a surface and χ(∂⁺M) is undefined, not non-zero; any subleading mode resolves the crossings and the count is 0 again.
