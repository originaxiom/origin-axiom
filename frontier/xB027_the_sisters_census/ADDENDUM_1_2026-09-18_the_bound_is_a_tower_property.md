## What this does and does not do to xB027

**It does not refute anything.** xB027's scope line already reads *"levels 2–4 on both towers"*, and
its claim is about those towers. **What it corrects is the REACH a reader might give the headline**:
*"the generation count is stabiliser-fixed"* is established **on the towers**, and the family is
where the index is known to go higher.

doublet module**. The **generation count** requires **five sectors firing together**. **B1418 reports
no generation-shaped background on `t12835`.** Concluding "the family has a generation count above
one" from `|I| = 2` would be the identification error this record names most often — **two numbers
in different places with no map between them — and that conclusion is NOT drawn here.**
