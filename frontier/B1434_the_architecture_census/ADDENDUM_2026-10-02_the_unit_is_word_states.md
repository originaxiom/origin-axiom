# B1434 ADDENDUM (2026-10-02, from B1456): the census counts word states, and a word and its reverse are one manifold

This arc takes states up to rotation and the swap of the two letters. It does not divide out reversal, and a word and
its reverse realise the same oriented manifold (found by the SM seat's sm:B1517; re-derived on main, B1456 H1, H2: the
identity M(reverse w) = D·M(w)⁻¹·D⁻¹ on every word to length twelve, and SnapPy's isometry signatures). So the 758
states to length twelve are 536 manifolds, the first pair at length seven; the 24 states to length six are 24
manifolds. Read by manifold, the 95 states that carry a background at their own level are 87, and no pair is split
(B1456 H3). No number of this arc changes; its unit is word states, and a rate quoted from it should say so.
