- **PATHS EXIST** — at least one named target scores (S,V,C,M) = (no,no,no,yes). It is named, and

  no, and *the pattern of which axis kills what* is the arc's result.

**This is not rigged toward NO.** The criteria are the programme's own proved walls, and a target
