## The result is the pattern, not the count

**The dominant wall is M — no computed mechanism — and M is not a proof of impossibility. It is an
absence.**

`S` (scale) and `C` (class-level) are **proved** walls: K018 reached "the scale is external" by three
independent modes, and B803 verified that m003 shares m004's invariant trace field at identical
volume. Those close their targets permanently.

**`M` closes nothing.** It records that **no derived map exists** between the object and the target —
that the connection was postulated rather than computed. Five of ten targets die there, and for all
five the mathematics on the object's side is intact.

**CORRECTED at review, same day.** A first draft of this section named `L91` as "the blocking wall
for the majority of the physics surface." **That over-reaches, and checking it matters more than the
tidier sentence.** L91 is the **stage-selection** gate (why κ = 5, why the SU(3) modular family) —
and its obligation **(4), the typed functorial monodromy-equivariant map, is already DISCHARGED**
(B650 types + B644 group functor + the equivariance wall). Obligations (1)–(3) remain, reduced to
one hypothesis plus a branch lemma.

The **cusp↔gauge dictionary** is a *different* item — `OPEN_LEADS` Campaign 2, *"ground or demote
the cusp↔gauge dictionary"*, whose heavy part is open. And **no registered lead at all covers a
`CS → θ_QCD` map.**

So the accurate statement is narrower and more useful: **the M-blocked targets are blocked by
several distinct missing derivations, not one gate** — L91 for the stage reading, Campaign 2 for the
cusp↔gauge dictionary, and **nothing registered** for the CS↔θ_QCD map that this arc's most advanced
target would need.

**That an obligation of exactly this type has already been discharged (L91's (4)) is the useful
precedent: a typed, functorial, equivariant map was specified and built. It is the template for what
the others would require.**

## Two targets whose classification changed on inspection

**The quasicrystal bridge — killed by C, and more sharply than expected.** The prereg predicted it
would survive S and V (the *material* supplies the scale, which is right) and fail C as
*family-level*. The truth is sharper: **m004 sits at `κ = −2`** (the parabolic cusp, K001) while the
**Fibonacci Hamiltonian sits at `κ = 2 + λ² > 2`** (K010). They are **different points of the same
trace-map family.** K010 says it outright: *"κ = 2+λ² is the coupling of a tight-binding chain, not
a vacuum or a constant of nature."*

So the corpus's only `PERMITTED` physics reading is an adjacency **of the trace map — our tool** —
not of the object. That is precisely the selection effect the atlas warns about (`trace_map` recurs
in 46 % of probes *because it is our method*), appearing here at the physics boundary.

**θ_QCD = 0 — the one target that nearly cleared.** It is dimensionless (S = no), it supplies the
*symmetric point* rather than a fitted value (V = no), and **amphichirality is genuinely
manifold-level** — m003 is not a knot, so it fails amphichirality where m004 holds it (C = no).
`CS = 0` for the cusped object **is computed and banked** (B286/B289/B303).

**It fails M, and only M.** The identification `CS ↔ θ_QCD` is a **postulated dictionary**
(K021: *"the physics dictionary is **postulated**, not derived"*; K022; B143), and **no arc computes
that map.** Axis M exists to forbid exactly this: an adjacency read as a path.

**This is the sharpest entry on the map.** Three of four axes clear, on a manifold-level property
with a computed invariant. What is missing is not a fact about the object — it is a derived
dictionary.

## What this does and does not license

**It does not license optimism.** Zero paths is zero paths, and B811 has just shown how a tight
σ-agreement evaporates against a proper null.

**It does not license "physics is closed" either** — and that phrasing should stop. Five of ten
targets are blocked by a **missing derivation**, not by a wall. The honest sentence is:

> **No path exists today, and for the majority of targets the blocker is the absent typed
> dictionary (L91), not a proved impossibility.**

**The two permanent walls are worth stating plainly**, because they narrow all future work: the
object cannot supply a **scale** (K018, three modes), and it cannot supply anything **class-level**
that distinguishes it from a sister sharing its trace field (B803).
