## 1. The convention (adopted 2026-09-27; the handoff's wording, with its fences)

**Generated-state-space convention.** The genesis update laws define X_gen = (reachable states, native moves, equivalences,
root). The root x₀ = [LR] = m004 is the distinguished minimal positive mixed persistent state. Later states generated by the same
legal operations remain part of the mathematical architecture unless an equivalence identifies them or a *derived* selector
removes them. **This does not assert that all states are simultaneously physically realized.**

Two theorems underlie it; they must not be conflated.

1. **Naturality (invariant selection) on a free orbit.** If the prior data has automorphism group G and the candidate outputs
   form a free G-orbit, no natural selector can choose one point. This is the precise form of "no unforced collapse" on the
   LR/RL torsor at the based level (B1379 verified the restricted form).
2. **Generated closure.** When primitive operations and composition are part of the supplied structure, the smallest
   operation-closed reachable space is part of the object. This places the signed carrier states and the m010 ladder inside
   the architecture. It does **not** make them equally minimal or equally physical.

What each generated item is, in the new wording:
- **m004** is the root.
- **m003** (−LR) is a generated signed state at the reversible-carrier layer: −I = (L²R⁻¹)² once inverses are admitted (S0).
  The torsion-free criterion *distinguishes* the positive root; "separates" is kept only inside the conditional uniqueness
  theorem, whose job is point selection.
- **m010** is a later generated state, distinguished by a new intrinsic property: the first rung that self-hosts the cubic
  character δ.
- **The Gieseking/orientation fork** is represented explicitly. The m004 chain is conditional on the orientation axiom until
  that axiom is derived. Whether the fork is a naturality torsor is a separate theorem, and the handoff does not claim it.
- **Covers** are refinement or sector relations, **decks** are symmetries, **fillings** are closure/surgery relations, and
  **word extension** is reachability.

**Transition-semantics fence.** None of the generated arrows is presently physical time: not substitution depth, cover degree,
deck position, filling order, or genesis reachability. The only equation-of-motion flow in hand is ws:R40's conditional field
flow from an adopted action. The architecture is kinematic. Physical history needs an action, a domain, internally measurable
change, clock semantics and a trajectory rule, all still open.

**Two order-three mechanisms, kept apart.** m010's internal cubic character δ (duality defect, determinant repair, Sym³
minimality) and M6's deck C₃ (a multiplicity-three orbit) are different elements. They meet through boundary/non-split
structure (§2, item B), but they are not identified.
