# B1438, addendum 1 (2026-10-01, the same day): "the frame cannot split generations" sharpened — it does not split them by a number inside a background, and it does give each member of an orbit its own Higgs class

**What B1438 said.** Under "What it means": *"The frame cannot split generations. By B and E a background carries one
generation and a deck orbit carries it three times with the same couplings … Whatever distinguishes three
generations is not in this frame."* This seat then reported it to the owner as a limit to start strategy from.

**The owner's question.** *"why is this a blocker? or we just dont understand it properly, and we have
missexpectations on how should reality / sm emerge."*

**What was wrong.** Not the theorems. The sentence carried an expectation that is nowhere proved and that the record
itself fences: that one background is the world and that the generations' differences must be numbers inside it.
Theorems B and E say where the difference is **not**. They do not say it is nowhere.

**What is computed instead (B1443).** Member i of a deck orbit couples to the spin-0 character of its own data, and a
different character admits no coupling on it. On the root's three-fold cover every orbit's three members have three
different Higgs characters, for every coupling type. The tensor is y·[i = j]·[η_i = η_k]; a mass matrix on such an
orbit is y·diag(v_k). So the frame supplies identical copies, no mixing at the cubic order, and a separate Higgs
class per copy. What fixes the values v_k has not been asked: no invariant singles out a member, but the unordered
pattern of the v_k is itself an invariant, and a deck-invariant function with unsymmetric minima is not excluded by
anything proved (B1443, checked at the owner's instruction).

**The sentence as it should read.** *The frame does not distinguish generations by any number inside one background,
and the copies along a deck orbit have equal couplings and do not mix. On most orbits, and on every orbit of the
root's three-fold cover, each copy couples to its own Higgs class; what would distinguish the copies is the pattern of
values those classes carry, and that has not been computed.*

The theorems, their proofs and every number of B1438 stand. ERROR_LEDGER: an instance of reading a proved boundary
as terminal (the owner's standing rule, WORKING_RULES §0), caught by the owner.
