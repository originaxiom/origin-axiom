## The five questions (each with what the record expects, so the answer can surprise)

Q1 DEFINITION. In the established physics, a generation is a chiral zero mode of the internal Dirac operator: the number
   of generations is an INDEX of the internal space (Atiyah–Singer) — in heterotic E6 models |χ(X)|/2 = h^{2,1} − h^{1,1}
   (27's minus 27-bar's); for an SU(n) bundle V, the index ∫ ch_3(V). Expected: the count is topological (closed-cycle
   homology), equal copies of ONE representation, with the vector-like pairs (27 + 27-bar) massive and not counted.
   Record analogue: the class index n(E) − n(E*) on a cusped 3-manifold; the room = closed-cycle classes (B1493–B1494).
Q2 CONSTRUCTION. How do three-generation models get three? Expected: a simply-connected cover with 3·|G| (an Euler number
   or ch_3 = 3k, k | |G|), a FREE group G acting, the quotient dividing the count, and WILSON LINES — flat bundles =
   characters of π_1(X/G) = G — breaking the gauge group and selecting the surviving modes (Tian–Yau/Z_3, 1986;
   the quintic/Z_5×Z_5; the Z_3 quotients of CICYs; "triadophilia"). Record analogue: companion = cover with deck group T;
   members = characters; the count on the state at four ⊗ Ind ν ⊗ χ (B1494–B1495). To verify: is the generation count
   on X/G literally the χ-isotypic part of the cover's cohomology (the deck orbit's eigenvalues 1, ω, ω²)?
Q3 SHAPE. The 10 and 5-bar (or 16 of SO(10)) counts must be EQUAL and no chiral exotics may survive. Expected: for SU(5)
   bundles with c_1 = 0 the identity ch_3(Λ²V) = (n − 4) ch_3(V) makes the 5-bar count equal the 10 count automatically
   (n = 5) — an index-theorem identity with NO analogue for the class index of flat bundles on 3-manifolds (B1495's (3, 9)).
   To verify the identity and its hypotheses; and what "no exotics" requires beyond the index (h^1(V*) = 0 etc.).
   Consequence for the record: generation shape must be checked, never assumed; and three generations = ONE rank-5 module
   with index three, never three rank-5 modules (the direct sum is outside the E8 dictionary).
Q4 WHY THREE. Does any framework DERIVE three? Expected: none — every construction SELECTS a space/bundle/flux whose index is
   three; the literature on "why three" (anomaly arguments, asymptotic freedom, Z-width = 3 light neutrinos as the
   empirical fact, family symmetries A_4/S_3/Δ(27)/Z_3 for mixing) constrains but does not derive. THE_BAR's baseline:
   a selection is the state of the art; a derivation would be new.
Q5 THE BLIND SPOTS. Given Q1–Q4, what can the record's two frames NOT see? Expected: (a) a one-ended fibred state has no
   closed cycles — it cannot carry an index of three by any module (B1487, T-COMPANION-NO-ROOM: the family's objects are
   "blind" to three the way a CY with h^{2,1} = h^{1,1} is); (b) the F-CI count (fixed points of an isometry on a cusp) has
   no analogue in the physics literature — research whether any construction counts generations by fixed points of a
   symmetry (orbifold fixed points DO carry twisted-sector generations: Z_3 orbifolds of T^6 give 27 fixed points → 27
   generations, reduced by Wilson lines to 3 — a fixed-point count of an order-3 symmetry on a torus: F-CI's three IS an
   orbifold-style count); (c) what the family would need: an object the genesis determines with closed cycles (room) at
   ends with trivial character — or an orbifold reading where the three fixed points of the act (the ends) ARE the
   twisted sectors.
