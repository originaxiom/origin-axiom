# B1631 — ADDENDUM (2026-10-14, Review 63, B1648): what main's recommendation of the unit's tagging rested on

**A correction of one sentence; the owner's ruling stands.**

§1 says: "Main recommended it on the math: B1629 shows the principle's dynamics cannot supply the unit." B1629's addendum
of the same day reads that sentence in its scope:
- the fraction of time deep in the cusp tends to (k + 1)/2^k, and is not equal to it at every length;
- "not dynamical" holds for the forced data and the uniform measure on threads, for observables in ever-deeper
  horoballs, and other measures and dynamics are not excluded.

It adds that the owner's tagging "does not rest on this sentence". The two pages disagree on what the recommendation
rested on.

**The correction:** main's recommendation rested on the scoped statement. Neither the tick nor the clock reaches the
cusp, and the uniform measure gives it no atom. That leaves a postulate or a physical end mechanism as the routes to the
unit, and it does not exclude other dynamics. The owner's tagging of the unit (GENESIS v1.39) is the owner's ruling, and
it stands in either reading. GENESIS v1.44 records the scope (B1648). Found by Review 63's premise audit (P-119).
