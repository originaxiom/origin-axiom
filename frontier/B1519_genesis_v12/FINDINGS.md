- **K1, B14's square roots, completely.** By Cayley–Hamilton any X ∈ GL(2, ℤ) with X² = A has A = tr(X)X − det(X)I, so
  X = (A + det(X)I)/tr(X) with tr(X)² = tr(A) + 2det(X). For a hyperbolic A this lists every integer square root, not a box of
  them. LR's roots are ±LP and nothing else (a brute force over entries in [−6, 6] finds the same two). On LᵃRᵇ, a, b = 1..8, a
  determinant −1 root exists exactly when a = b, and it is ±LᵃP; no determinant +1 root exists. The proof is two lines: for
  det X = −1, tr(X)² = ab and X = (A − I)/tr X is integral only if tr X divides a and b, which forces a = b; for det X = +1,
  tr(X)² = ab + 4 and tr X must divide 2, impossible for ab ≥ 1. Main re-derived B14 too (its B1454, G11).

## 3. The signed powers, placed

**Setting.** Every hyperbolic class of SL(2, ℤ), up to conjugacy in GL(2, ℤ), is ±A(w) for a positive word w with both letters,
unique up to rotation and the L↔R swap (B1516 C9 constructs w; Salepci §7 gives uniqueness). Writing w = uᵏ with u primitive, every
such monodromy is exactly one triple (u, k, ε) ↦ εA(u)ᵏ. A **state** of GENESIS §3 is a triple with k = 1. The n-fold cyclic cover of
the bundle of (u, k, ε) is the bundle of (u, kn, εⁿ).

**Which triples are levels of states.** (u, k, ε) is a level of the state (u′, 1, ε′) when (ε′A(u′))ⁿ is conjugate to εA(u)ᵏ. The
trace of a positive hyperbolic matrix is positive, so ε′ⁿ = ε, and A(u′)ⁿ is conjugate to A(u)ᵏ; by the uniqueness of the cyclic
word, u′ = u and n = k. So (u, k, +1) is the k-fold level of (u, 1, +1) and, when k is even, also of (u, 1, −1); (u, k, −1) is a level
of a state exactly when k is odd, the k-fold level of (u, 1, −1). For k even, (u, k, −1) is a level only of (u, k′, −1) with k/k′
odd: the signed powers have their own seeds, **−u², −u⁴, −u⁸, …**, one tower above each primitive u.

**The first one is in the record.** −(LR)² is m207, H₁ = ℤ ⊕ ℤ/3 ⊕ ℤ/3: B1385 §2 S4 (±(LR)², with ±(LR)³ and ±(LR)⁴), main's B1418
class census and B1224's CS census; the audit lane's R78 recovered it and proved it is no level of any signed seed by a matrix-root
argument and a finite trace-seven exclusion (K5 reproduces both). It lies in m004's commensurability class: its double cover is the
bundle of (LR)⁴ = t12839, which is also m004's fourth level.

**What does not change.** The census of 758 states (536 manifolds) counts states, k = 1. Every result banked on states or their
levels stands; a count that claims the full grammar must say whether it includes the signed seeds. Whether the signed powers are
legal is GM5b's question (GENESIS FK4); B1385's T1 (one cusp, b₁ = 1, no free cusp) covers ±φ for every hyperbolic φ, so the
free-cusp frame closes them as it closes the states. R78's representation, (u, k, ε) with covers (u, kn, εⁿ), is adopted.
