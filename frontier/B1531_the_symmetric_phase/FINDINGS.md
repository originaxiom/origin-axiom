### 4.2 The orbit sum is a free gas, and its β_c is a Hagedorn point

The Euler product makes the zeta function the grand partition function of an ideal gas of periodic orbits: each orbit of
period m contributes a factor (1 − e^{−βm})⁻¹, with z = e^{−β}. Since c_m grows like λ^m/m, the gas's partition function
diverges as β decreases to log λ.
- That is Hagedorn's mechanism: exponential growth of the number of states against Boltzmann suppression (Redlich–Satz,
  eq. (14)).
- Redlich–Satz also record what the divergence alone cannot decide. In Hagedorn's case, whether T_H is a limiting
  temperature or a transition depended on the next correction to the spectrum and, beyond that, on "more information: we
  need QCD".
- Here the orbit gas is free, and its zeta is rational with a simple pole at z = 1/λ. Chat 1's critical point is the
  limiting point of a free gas. A transition there would need an interaction between orbits that the object has not been
  shown to supply.

### 4.3 The lemma: no broken mirror phase in the monodromy's own thermodynamics

**Lemma.** Let f = ±A, with A ∈ SL(2, ℤ) and tr A > 2, act on the torus. Let K ∈ SL(2, ℤ) satisfy K f K⁻¹ = f⁻¹. Then for
every Hölder potential φ with φ ∘ K = φ, the equilibrium state μ_φ of f is unique and K-invariant.

**Proof.**
1. f is a hyperbolic toral automorphism, hence Anosov and measure-mixing for Lebesgue (Springham §1.1).
2. Lebesgue has full support, so f is topologically mixing: for open U and V, Leb(f⁻ⁿU ∩ V) → Leb(U)Leb(V) > 0.
3. By Sinai–Ruelle–Bowen, f has exactly one equilibrium state μ_φ for each Hölder φ.
4. The equilibrium states of f⁻¹ for φ are those of f, because h_μ(f⁻¹) = h_μ(f) and ∫φ dμ is unchanged.
5. K_* carries the equilibrium states of (f, φ) to those of (K f K⁻¹, φ ∘ K⁻¹) = (f⁻¹, φ), hence to those of (f, φ).
6. By uniqueness, K_*μ_φ = μ_φ. ∎

**C6 checks the hypothesis.** The quarter turn K = [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] conjugates each of ±LR and ±LLRR to its inverse, so
(x, t) ↦ (Kx, −t) is an orientation-reversing self-map of the bundle. SnapPy reads m004, m003, m136 and m135 as amphicheiral,
each isometric to its bundle b+±word.

**So the monodromy's own thermodynamic formalism has no broken mirror phase at any β, β_c included.**
- For a mirror-odd potential, the family βφ moves the equilibrium state analytically through β = 0. The pressure is
  analytic on Hölder potentials for mixing subshifts of finite type, and Bowen's Markov coding carries this to Anosov maps
  (Kucherenko–Quas, §1). There is no spontaneous magnetization.
- This is the "symmetric phase is exactly symmetric" statement of chat 1, proved for this system.
- It closes the thread as stated: "whether symmetry breaks there" is answered, for the object's own orbit thermodynamics,
  by no.
