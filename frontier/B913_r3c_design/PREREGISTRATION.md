# B913 PREREGISTRATION — R3c: the cone-respecting colored magnitude (design seal, before any computation)

**Date sealed:** 2026-08-05 · **Seat:** cc (banking) · **Register:** ROADMAP

## The question

B912 proved the six colored atoms carry Lorentzian (1,2,0) Grams under the
canonical H. R3′'s colored ratios need a magnitude that respects the cone
rather than pretending definiteness. This design cell CHOOSES the magnitude —
sealed before any colored ratio is computed — so the choice can never be
retrofitted to a wanted answer.

## The candidates, weighed at design time

1. **|det Gram|^{1/3}** — already computed in B912 (1867.69, 702.46, 451.72,
   each ×2). Pro: basis-invariant up to the declared normalization, already
   banked, treats the cone as a volume. Con: blind to the (1,2) split — a
   definite and an indefinite Gram of equal |det| read the same.
2. **The compact-direction restriction** — H restricted to the atom's
   positive line (the "timelike" axis). Pro: reads the cone's structure.
   Con: requires selecting the positive line canonically (the charge
   eigenstructure inside the atom would have to pin it — an extra
   construction whose canonicity is itself unproven).
3. **The polar/singular spectrum** — the three singular values of the Gram.
   Pro: complete invariant data. Con: three numbers per atom is not a
   magnitude; ratios would need a further choice, reintroducing arbitrariness
   one level down.

## THE SEALED CHOICE

**Candidate 1: |det Gram|^{1/3}, with the (1,2,0) signature carried alongside
as a mandatory tag** — every colored ratio in R3′ is reported as
(magnitude ratio, signature pair). Rationale, fixed now: it is the unique
candidate that is already fully computed, basis-invariant, and requires ZERO
further construction (each further construction would be a fresh place for
unintended freedom — the drift rule's spirit applied to mathematics itself).
Candidates 2 and 3 are NOT discarded as mathematics: they are registered as
future refinement cells, gated behind R4 (they may not be introduced between
now and the crossing, so the crossing cannot be tuned by magnitude-shopping).

## The binding consequence

R3′'s table = the nine colorless scales (banked) + the three colored
magnitude pairs (banked values, |det|^{1/3}) + all ratios thereof, each
colored entry signature-tagged. NO alternative magnitude may enter R3′ or R4;
any future refinement (candidates 2/3) happens AFTER the crossing, as its own
sealed cell, compared against — never substituted into — the R4 verdict.
