## The finding

C20/B766 names the torsor's third generator "θ (reversal)". But **reversal is trace-trivial
at every rank** (tr(w^R)=tr(w) always), so it is invisible to the character variety. The
involution that is genuinely active on the SL(3) character variety is **ι = inversion**
(contragredient, g↦g⁻¹): at genuine SL(3), tr(w⁻¹) ≠ tr(w).

| involution | character variety (traces) | where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| c (conjugation) | active (ℚ(√−3)) | trace |
| **ι (inversion)** | **active at SL(3)** (tr(g⁻¹)≠tr(g)) | **trace — the true 3rd generator** |
| γ₅ (Galois ℚ(√5)) | active | trace |
| θ (reversal) | **trivial at all ranks** | matrix level only — **the chord** |

So the **character-variety measurement rank is 3, generated by {c, ι, γ₅}** — ι, not θ. θ
(reversal) is the matrix-level chord (Sym²(AB)−Sym²(BA), invisible to tr), the c⊕θ observable.

## Why C20's rank-3 still stands for the object

The object is the figure-eight = **Sym²(SL(2))**, a **self-dual** rep. There tr(g⁻¹)=tr(g) too,
so ι and θ **both collapse to trivial on traces and coincide**. The object's measurement rank
is therefore 3 — {c, (θ~ι), γ₅} — exactly C20. The theorem is unchanged; the refinement is
that the third generator is properly **ι** on the character variety, with θ its self-dual
shadow (the chord).

## The open door (back to cc3)

On the **full, non-self-dual** SL(3) character variety (cc3's W1/W2 components), θ and ι are
distinct — potentially a **rank-4** torsor {c, ι, γ₅, θ}. cc3's N8 asserts this via
ι = θ·inner(S), but its S = diag(1,−1,1) does not intertwine (I flagged it). The rank-4 claim
is CONDITIONAL on cc3's corrected S; the object's rank 3 is unconditional.
