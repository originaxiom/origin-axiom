## The reduction being tested

For an Anosov foliation, the type is read off the **ratio set** of the Radon–Nikodym cocycle,
generated by the transverse expansion factors — which for a hyperbolic manifold are `e^{ℓ}` over
the closed geodesic lengths ℓ. So:

| the additive group generated by the length spectrum | ratio set | type |
|---|---|---|
| **discrete** (⊂ cℤ) | λ^ℤ ∪ {0} | **III_λ** |
| **dense** in ℝ | [0, ∞) | **III₁** |

**This reduction is a DECLARED CITATION and is NOT verified in this sandbox**, exactly as B849's
Cell 4 was. The cusped case is the weak point and is named here in advance: m004's flow is **not
uniformly hyperbolic** (cusp excursions) and the foliation carries an **infinite** invariant
measure, which is precisely why the compact-Anosov ⟹ III_λ result does not transfer. **Any verdict
resting on the reduction carries CONDITIONAL or is not banked.**
