**The structure this suggests:**
- **The orbit:** on the 3-tower the orbit is a coset space G_k/H, with H = ⟨C⟩ of order 3, the stabiliser of (3,3,3).
- **Point-fixing elements:** an element that fixes a point is conjugate into H. It is therefore 1 or a conjugate of C^{±1}, which
  is B2004's finding.
- **Where the halving should hold:** if ρ factors through G_k, the twist argument of B2004 applies. ρ fails to factor at mod 9
  (R⁴ acts trivially with ρ ≠ 1), and that is where the halving fails.
