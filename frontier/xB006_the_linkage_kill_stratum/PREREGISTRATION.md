## Conventions

"Kill" = an arc whose `verdict` is `NEGATIVE`, or whose `claim_one_line` asserts a refutation,
closure or non-result. "Link exhibited" = B1223's discriminator, applied to the kill's own

An order match, a name match or a shared numeral is **not** a link.
