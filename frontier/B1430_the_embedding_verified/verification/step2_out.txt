  {+-a,+-b,+-(a+b)}, closed under negation and under addition inside
  E6, of rank 2, all six of equal length.  Two different generating
  pairs that span the same 6-element set are ONE subsystem.

CONVENTION D: sets of 3 POSITIVE roots (A2 up to sign) -- same objects
  as convention A, so COUNT = 120
