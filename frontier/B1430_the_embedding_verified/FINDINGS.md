## 3. THE QUALIFICATION — AND WHY IT VINDICATES THE PAPER RATHER THAN THE REVIEWER

The A₂ orbit fact alone does **not** carry the SM's uniqueness. The u(1) is a **free line in a 3-dimensional
space**, and the residual symmetry acting on it is finite (order 72), so there are **uncountably many** W-orbits of
su(3) + su(2) + u(1). Measured: scanning 28 513 primitive rational directions gives **8 177 distinct branchings of
the 27**, i.e. thousands of pairwise non-conjugate embeddings.

**Uniqueness returns only when the 27 is required to branch into one Standard-Model generation plus a 10 and a 1.**
Then exactly three directions survive, in one orbit — one conjugacy class.

Now read the paper. Its hedge says the selection *"rests on the colour frame and the Standard-Model shaping, which
§ledger lists as inputs on the weakest reading; what is determined is the quotient **given** that frame."* And
B1366's own claim line carries the same condition — *"exactly three hypercharges … give the 27 the Standard
Model's multiset"*.

> **The arc and the paper agree. The reviewer's charge was a lossy compression of the arc, not a finding about the
> paper.** The paper is right to call the Standard-Model shaping an input, because without it the embedding is not
> unique — it is a three-parameter family, and this arc measures how big it is.

**B1366 itself is not guilty of the gap either.** Its claim line states the branching condition explicitly; it is
the paraphrase that dropped it.

## 4. ONE THING THE ARC DOES NOT CLAIM AND IS TRUE ANYWAY

The **su(3) + su(2)** pair alone, before any hypercharge, is already unique up to conjugacy — 720 commuting pairs
in a single orbit. That is computed here and is a slightly stronger statement than the A₂ fact by itself.

## 6. WHAT IS NOT COVERED

Regular embeddings only — subalgebras generated by root vectors. Non-regular (S-subalgebra) embeddings of su(3) or
su(2) were **not** tested. The u(1) scan is a box of 28 513 rational directions, not an exhaustive proof over all
of them. The branching target is external physics input and is kept separate in the code. The standard reduction
from G-conjugacy to W-conjugacy is assumed, not machine-verified, so these are literally Weyl- and
automorphism-conjugacy results.
