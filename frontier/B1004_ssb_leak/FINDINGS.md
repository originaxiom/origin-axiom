## Why the sweep was blind — and the reasoning was good

**B967 declined to register the phrase on purpose**, and said why: registering it *"would fire on
B723's own correction banner, which is the correct treatment."* **The intent was right. The effect
was not.**

> **Leaving it unregistered kept the sweep quiet — and left three LAW_MAP rows asserting a retracted
> clause, one of them inheriting it AS AN AXIOM.**

The mention-cue machinery already handles correction banners; the phrase belonged in the registry.
**Registered now as row 7.** The sweep immediately found **two further live instances** that had been
invisible for as long as the phrase was unregistered — including **B725's**, which the arc's own
"deepest caveat" section had flagged as an *unproved* premise. **It is not unproved. It is false.**

**All five surfaces corrected; violations now 0.**

## The second gate, widened — cc3's finding, adopted

cc3 reported that the **one artifact that went unadopted today** (`README_ARC_PROPOSAL.md`) was
**invisible to the relay-debt gate**, because the pattern matched only `CC3_TO_CC_<date>_*`. **The
mechanism written to prevent exactly that loss could not see it.**

Widened here to `*_PROPOSAL`, `PROPOSAL_*`, `*_HANDOFF`, `HANDOFF_*`, dated from first commit since
those carry no date in the name. **It surfaced nine more invisible artifacts immediately** —
`BIFOCAL_STRUCTURE_HANDOFF`, `DARK_SECTOR_WAVE1_HANDOFF`, `HANDOFF_ADJUDICATION`,
`HANDOFF_CC_SELECTION_COCHAIN`, `HANDOFF_COMPARATOR_WEBSEAT`, `HANDOFF_MANIFEST`,
`HANDOFF_PREDICTOR_CC2`, `HANDOFF_README_CC`, `METALLIC_LANDSCAPE_HANDOFF` — **four of them already
past the 21-day escalation threshold.** All seeded OPEN.

*The reason is recorded next to the regex, not only in the commit message, so whoever narrows it
later knows what the narrow version cost.*

## The pattern, stated once

**Three gates were told to look away today, each for a defensible reason, and each exclusion had a
cost:**

1. **B982** — seven file-drawer exemptions cited an audit that never named them.
2. **B967** — a retracted phrase left unregistered to avoid a false positive, at the price of five
   live uses.
3. **B999** — a relay pattern narrow enough to miss the day's one unadopted artifact.

> **An exclusion is a claim, and a claim needs a check. The rule that comes out of this: any gate
> exemption must name what it would cost if wrong — and that estimate is itself checkable.**

debt.**
