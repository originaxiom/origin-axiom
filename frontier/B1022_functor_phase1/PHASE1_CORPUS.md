  is exactly scale-covariant (the ledger; B413/B811 upgraded from observation to mechanism).

**Sector P — dimensionless, pair channel. A CONDITIONAL THEOREM.**

- **C5.** The condition, stated: the obstruction is proved *for the banked classes*; the named
  door is non-invariant structure (B990's route 2). Absence here is a theorem **with a scope
  clause**, not an unconditional one.

**Sector C — dimensionless, coupling channel. CONTINGENT, AND THE CORPUS SAYS UNOBSTRUCTED.**
