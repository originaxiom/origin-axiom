## 0. Seen from above

The level mismatch (L214) asked whether the object's own closing — the ℤ/3 descent of Y₃, whose orbifold group P2₁3 has order-3
characters the cover lacks — could carry a Standard-Model line. Its first homology is ℤ/3: the descent is the figure-eight orbifold
with cone angle 2π/3, its orbifold group is π₁(m004)/⟨⟨μ³⟩⟩, and H₁(m004) = ℤ is generated by the meridian. So its abelian Wilson
lines are the three characters of order dividing 3, and the question is which subgroups of E₆ are commutants of an element of order 3.
Kac's classification answers by the affine Dynkin diagram: six classes up to symmetry, with fixed subalgebras E₆ (the central element),
A₅ + u(1), A₂ + A₂ + A₂, A₁ + A₄ + u(1), D₅ + u(1), D₄ + 2u(1). None is A₂ + A₁ + 2u(1). The descent has trinification, SU(6) × U(1),
SU(5) × SU(2) × U(1), SO(10) × U(1) and SO(8) × U(1)² on offer, and not the Standard Model — the same verdict B1277 and B1300 gave
the cover's (ℤ/4)² (for which no single order-4 element has the Standard-Model commutant either: eleven Kac classes, none SM).

## 2. What it means

1. The descent's abelian Wilson lines cannot break E₆ to the Standard Model; with the cover's negative (B1277: the E₈ theory on Y₃ never
   reaches the Standard Model with or without Wilson lines; B1300: no SM lines on Y₃ and Y₆), neither level of the ℤ/3 carries an
   abelian SM line. The order-4 list shows the same for single elements on the cover; pairs are B1277's.
2. Non-abelian lines of P2₁3 (image not abelian) would need the translation image, a subgroup of the centraliser of the Standard Model
   — whose identity component is a three-torus — to carry the point group's action through that centraliser's finite part; that is
   registered, not decided (L214 (i), non-abelian half).
3. So the reconciliation of the levels cannot come from the descent's characters. What remains of L214: a non-Wilson-line breaking
   on Y₃ (ii) or a non-deck triple on Y₉ (iii).

## 3. Caveats

1. Kac's classification is used for inner automorphisms; outer automorphisms of E₆ (order 2 composed with inner) are not Wilson lines
   of a connected gauge group and are not considered.
2. The fixed subalgebras are read from the subdiagram of the nodes with s_i = 0 plus one u(1) per additional non-zero node; the
   S₃ symmetry of the affine E₆ diagram is quotiented.
