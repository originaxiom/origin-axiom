## 0. Seen from above

- **Where the 10 lives.** A generation needs a 10 of SU(5). In the 78 every 10-type weight is an SL(2)_β doublet: 40 of 40. The frame's
  rule gives doublets nothing (B1372's Lemma A: the geometric connection makes their partitions whole or empty at every cusp).
- **What the frame's count carries.** It lives on the 78's spin-0 sectors: the X,Y partners and a 5 and 5̄. Those form three
  direction classes, and their anomaly matrix has full rank. So the count is anomaly-free only when it is zero, whatever the rank of
  the Higgs field.
- **Caps are the only source of 10s,** two per unit of γ-flux, so always an even number. A bulk flux sums to zero over the cusps
  (B1397 §7).
- **Three is therefore impossible in the frame.**
- **The escape.** A frame with 27 matter, where the 27's spin-0 part holds a 10, together with a Higgs field of rank two. B1389's
  rank-one analysis left that case open.

- The Higgs field is abelian: φ = Y ⊗ ω₁ + γ ⊗ ω₂, with harmonic 1-forms ω₁, ω₂. When they are proportional this is B1389's rank-one
  H = aY + bγ.

**The menu.** Every completion the record has contributes in one of three forms.

  - A spin-0 sector μ gets N(θ_μ), where θ_μ is the direction of (Y(μ), γ(μ)) and N is an odd integer function of the direction: the
    signed zero count of cos θ·ω₁ + sin θ·ω₂. For rank one, N = N₀·sign(cos(θ − θ_H)).
  - Every SL(2)_β doublet gets 0 (Lemma A).
- **Caps (B1395–B1397).** Every weight μ, doublets included, gets ⟨F, μ⟩·n, linear in the charges.
- **Charge-blind ends and Dehn fillings** give 0 (B1393, B1351).

## 2. The statements

**(1) The 78's 10s are doublets.** Every weight of the 78 of type Q, u^c, e^c, or conjugate, has |⟨w, β⟩| = 1: 40 of 40. The 78's
spin-0 non-Standard-Model roots are the X,Y partners (3, 2)₋₅/₆ and the 5 and 5̄ at γ = ∓2. So the frame's rule never gives the 78 a
10, for any Higgs field.

**(2) The frame's count in F78 is anomaly-free only when it vanishes.**
- The 78's spin-0 non-SM sectors (one per ± pair) fall into three direction classes of (Y, γ):
  - (1, 0), the X,Y partners;
  - (1, 6), the 5's triplet;
  - (1, −4), the 5's doublet.
- With an arbitrary integer count on each class (any odd function N, so any rank), the five perturbative Standard-Model anomalies
  form a 5 × 3 matrix of rank 3. The only anomaly-free count is zero.

**(3) Caps give 10s in pairs.** A cap with unit γ-flux gives exactly two generations in F78. Its 10s come from the doublet (2, 20) at
γ = ±1, and its 5̄s from the 5̄ at γ = +2.

**(4) Every combination.**
- Take frame-rule counts on the three classes plus caps with any flux cY + dγ in total.
- Impose exotic-free, anomaly-free and g generations. The unique solution is c = 0, all frame-rule counts zero, and g = 2d.
- So the number of generations is twice the caps' total γ-flux: always even. For a bulk flux it is zero, since the degrees sum to
  zero over the cusps (B1397 §7).
- With cap fields independent of the bulk it is still even. ∎

**The verdict.** In the seat's frame no completion on the record's menu, alone or combined, gives an anomaly-free chiral spectrum with
three generations. The anomaly-free spectra it does give have an even number of generations, and zero unless some cap carries a field
independent of the bulk. This holds for an abelian Higgs field of any rank, on every member of the class.

## 3. The escape, located

**(5) With 27 matter the frame's rule is not closed.**
- The setting is F27+78, or the E₇ frame F133 with the Higgs field in the (Y, γ) plane. There the 27's t-charge does not enter the
  directions, so the classes are the same.
- The spin-0 sectors, the 27's 15 and the 78's X,Y partners, 5 and 5̄, fall into six direction classes. There are two parallels:
  - Q(27) ∥ H_u(78);
  - D(27) ∥ L(78).
  They tie the 27's D to its Q.
- The anomaly matrix has rank 5, so there is exactly one anomaly-free family, and it is g complete generations:

| class (Y, γ) | sectors | count at g = 3 |
|---|---|---|
| (1, −4) | Q(27), D(27), L(78) | 3 |
| (1, 0) | X,Y | 0 |
| (1, 1) | u^c(27) | −3 (i.e. +3 u^c) |
| (1, 6) | D(78), d^c(78) | 0 |
| (3, −2) | e^c(27) | 3 |
| (3, 8) | H_u(27) | −6 |

Its net content is three of each of Q, u^c, e^c, d^c and L, with no exotic, and it is anomaly-free.

**(6) Rank one cannot give it.** The pattern takes two absolute values, 3 and 6. A rank-one count takes one: |N₀| on every class it does
not kill. B1389's sealed F27+78 FAIL agrees.

**(5′) Caps widen the family.** In F27+78, frame-rule counts plus caps with flux (c, d) give a three-parameter family (c, d, g). The
frame-rule counts can then cancel a hypercharge cap's X,Y chirality. With a bulk flux the caps sum to zero and the family is (5)'s
pattern.

**Why this escape is the smallest.**
- **It adds only 27 matter,** already one of the record's frames (B1389's F27+78 and F133; the E₇ points of B1353 and B1360). It keeps
  the frame's own rule and an abelian Higgs field, and relaxes only B1389's rank-one assumption.
- **The other escapes need more.**
  - Non-abelian Higgs data commuting with the SM lie in SL(2)_β, the only non-abelian factor of c(SM) (B1368). That is B1372 and B1373's
    "door 2", closed except for a narrow residual, and its generation assignment also takes its 5̄ from the 27 (B1372). So it needs the
    27 and non-abelian data.
  - Caps with independent fields are even in every E₆ frame (B1397), so they would need E₇ as well as walls.

**The test.** B1399, sealed with this arc: does a member of m004's class carry two cuspidal classes whose count function realises the
pattern?
