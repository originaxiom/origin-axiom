  surface, and the weave's threads equidistribute without an atom (the seat's §41.4). The arc computes these facts on
  the weave's own words so that FK10's question can be stated without assuming them.
- **C1's second half is a reading.** The fixed-point word is a word in the records' letters a, b, not in the moves. Read
  letter for letter as moves (a → L, b → R) it is a choice, reported for its run lengths only. The principle's own orbit

  commutator trace κ, which is −2 at a cusped geometric point. The arc does not claim that the cusp is where "cancellation
  completes".

compact region of the weave's surface: the tick is the lowest closed geodesic, and the clock's word has bounded runs.

thread. **So nothing the principle forces visits the cusp.** A unit of end data there, which is what W45 and W46 need

condition supplied from outside: a physical end mechanism, which is the audit lane's open duty, or a new postulate,
which the owner declined. FK10 stays open, but sharper: the forcing, if any, is not dynamical.
