Also verified the equivalence step: (s1+s2+s3)(-s1+s2+s3)(s1-s2+s3)(s1+s2-s3) = 4 e2 - e1^2 for e_i built from s_i^2 (residual 0), so e1^2 = 4 e2 iff one singular value is the sum of the other two; with s1 the largest this is s1 = s2 + s3 (the other branches force s3 = 0 and then also s1 = s2 + s3). ## (d) Scope: over-reaches and conditions
1. "The sum rule for every off-diagonal coupling" (FINDINGS bullet 1, and the arc's own wording) is false as stated. It holds for the symmetric zero-diagonal sector (O+), and trivially for the antisymmetric sector (s = (s,s,0) gives s1 = s2 + s3 only because s3 = 0, floor ratio 1). It FAILS for a general zero-diagonal matrix, e.g. weight 3 symmetric + r antisymmetric: my_verify4.log, 50 000 of 50 000 random samples violate it, max |s1-s2-s3|/s1 = 0.83, min s2/s1 = 0.128 (below 1/2); likewise for a symmetric matrix with a diagonal. B1636 says "O+ coupling", correct. So the corollary "no hierarchy" covers the parameter-free sectors only (the weight-1 O+ form and the identity), exactly as the arc's section 2 words it ("two parameter-free sectors"), and is not a statement about any weight-3-or-higher sector, where the arc itself relies on fits (N10c) instead.
2. "Parameter-free" means one form per sector (dim 1) AND a single modulus tau shared by u and d, AND both sectors drawn from these two rep types. Hierarchy is excluded for T x T weight 1 (m2/m1 >= 1/2) and T-bar x T weight 0 (identity, ratio 1). Mixed assignments (u in T x T, d in T-bar x T) are not "V = 1" but also have a degenerate d sector; the arc does not discuss them.
3. "No Cabibbo angle" is N8b (dim 1 -> M_u proportional to M_d -> V = 1), not the sum rule (c.3). It needs the same tau and the same representation in both sectors.
4. "On the ruled branch the strong CP angle is pi Re tau": the determinant identity is exact for any generic T x T coupling (c.5) but theta_bar = pi Re tau holds only if arg(c_u c_d f_u f_d) = 0 (real coefficients), and only to O(e^{-2 pi Im tau}) (error 2e-2 at Im tau = 1); the FINDINGS section 2 states the full formula theta_bar - theta_QCD = arg(c_u c_d) + 12 arg eta + arg(f_u f_d), the headline and the title drop the first and third terms. Further: tau -> tau + 1 shifts it by pi, so the statement is for tau in the strip |Re tau| <= 1/2 with the triplet basis fixed (it is not modular invariant; the compensating field redefinition is anomalous and is not discussed). "theta_bar < 1e-10 puts tau on the imaginary axis" additionally assumes real coefficients and theta_QCD = 0 and no other contribution (e.g. the Higgs sector or lepton rotations are irrelevant; but the usual loop and threshold corrections are not considered). "Tree level" is stated in the findings.
5. N10c is a Nelder-Mead local minimisation (100 starts, 3000 iterations, 6 parameters, fixed seed) of a hand-chosen chi2 with hand-chosen relative uncertainties on quark data (Huang-Zhou M_Z masses). It is a lower-bound-free statement: chi2 > 500 is the best FOUND value, not a certified global minimum. It is platform-dependent (b above: 3.4e4 became 2.9e4 and 3.9e4), and one of the three rerun minima sits on the domain boundary with coefficients ~3e3, a sign of unconverged search. The prose "miss by three orders of magnitude in chi2" and "the triplet begins to fit at weight 5" (the latter is not computed in this arc at all) are not established by it; "still misses by orders of magnitude" is supported to the extent of 4e3 vs the 500 threshold at best, i.e. a factor 8 to 80 over the preregistered bar.
6. The "Zee texture" and angle read-outs (theta13 ~ 1e-10, theta23 = 45 deg, theta12 ~ 44.5) are numerical read-outs with charged leptons assumed diagonal; theta13 values differ between runs at the 1e-10 level (zero in exact arithmetic on Re tau = 1/2), so only "< 1e-8" is meaningful.
7. "Dimension table reproduces B1635's by piece": confirmed (c.4), for both T x T (k <= 11) and T-bar x T (k <= 6); the statement depends on the cusp condition (order >= 3 in q^{1/4}) which I implemented independently and which matched.
8. Provenance: every threshold was set after the referee's own computations (disclosed). The seal certifies statements as asserted, not a blind prediction; FINDINGS says this. "PROVED" for N8 is a genuine symbolic proof; for N10 it is an identity checked numerically to 1e-9 plus a character argument I find sound; N10c is numerical heuristics.
