## What was proposed, and by whom

An outside AI, relayed by the owner, proposed that the VEV-direction gap closes like this: the
closing's torsion gives a finite **ℤ/5 Wilson-line menu** over `E₆`; *generic collapse* (the modal
row) gives the left-right group at 27.6%; *exceptional collapse* (the rarest row — the programme's
own stated method) gives `so(10)⊕u(1)` at 0.7% with **108 = 27×4** directions; and either lands two
already-priced VEVs from the SM. **The gap would compress to one binary bit.**

## What is wrong

**The mode is a tie.** Two rows share 4320 (27.65%): `A₁+A₁+A₂+u(1)²` (left-right) **and**
`A₁+A₃+u(1)²`. So *"the most probable row is the left-right group"* is false — **generic collapse
does not select at all.** This sharpens rather than weakens their preferred half: only the extremal
rule returns a unique row.

## Why the mechanism is dead anyway — twice

**(1) The menu is not this object's.** The paper computes `H₁(M_m) ≅ ℤ ⊕ (ℤ/m)²`, so the golden
member `m = 1` has **`H₁ = ℤ`, torsion-free**. There is no ℤ/5. That menu belongs to `m = 5`, a
different manifold.

**(2) Even granting it, every row has rank 6** — all nine, computed here exhaustively. **The SM has
rank 4.** `so(10)⊕u(1)` is rank 6 as well, so the "exceptional collapse" lands on another rank-6
row and *still* needs `⟨1⟩` and `⟨ν^c⟩` — the very step the proposal set out to source.

## The second route, and why two are better than one

**This independently re-derives B955.** That arc proved rank-preservation *structurally*: a knot
complement has `H₁ = ℤ`, **cyclic**, so every abelian flat connection has cyclic image generated by
one element, whose centraliser in simply-connected `E₆` contains a maximal torus — *"abelian
holonomy preserves rank **necessarily**, not generically."*

This arc reaches the same wall by **exhaustive enumeration**: all 15,624 elements, all nine
resulting rows, every one rank 6. **No shared step with B955's argument.** A structural proof and a
brute-force census agreeing is worth more than either alone — and it means the wall is not an
artefact of how B955 chose to argue.
