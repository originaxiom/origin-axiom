# B1634 — Addendum, 2026-10-13: "no choice is made" is scoped — the multiplier carries a hand (B1640)

§3 says no choice is made beyond the convention H₁ fixes, and P6 that ρ(γ) = lift(γ)⁻¹ is forced because the lifts
compose in reverse. Reversed composition is satisfied by two homomorphisms: lift⁻¹ and the transpose. For these unitary
lifts the transpose is the complex conjugate of lift⁻¹. Which of V's two pieces is called T (the sign of W21's form) is a
second binary choice. The mathematical-chain referee of the frame audit ran all four combinations on the W10 move
matrices: two give the theta constants' multiplier and two its conjugate, each a genuine Mp2(ℤ) representation.

**What stands.** Every computation of this arc, for the choice it made. **What changes.** The choice is a convention,
recorded as one. The record's choice allows W45's weight 3/2 and B1636's symmetric weight-1 coupling; the conjugate allows
neither. So the index and the golden coupling come with the same hand. Which hand the matter carries is not derived; that
agrees with B1607 ("neither hand is derived on the weave"). A derivation from the zero modes' period map is owed
(B1640 §3.1). Flipping the 2O lift signs (η²¹ → η⁹) is a further convention; by the referee's check, not re-run on main, it does not change the couplings.
