## The answer

**The owner is right, but the leads are rarely lost at the moment they are spoken. Most are lost after they are written
down.** About 530 distinct ideas were traced across five sources. About 340 were worked, often as negatives. About 80
were partly worked and about 45 were rowed and then stalled. About 65 had nothing carrying them: no register row, no arc
and no tracked document. Main confirmed each of those 65 against the whole tracked record.

The losses are not random. Twelve mechanisms account for them (§ 2). Five matter most:
- **Leads never come due.** Since the 2026-11-03 typing (P-587), [LEAD] and [PROCESS] rows never age. Only debts are worked
  in order, and an idea nothing yet rests on is by definition a lead.
- **The decadal review ranks leads and then carries none of them.**
- **An item merged into a host row dies when the host closes on its own narrower ask.**
- **Whole places are never read by the register or the retrieval tools:** campaign side-cells, "owed" lines inside
  FINDINGS, the hint ledger, the audit room, the handoffs and the speculations.
- **Ideas come back months later under new names**, uncredited and unjoined.

Two of the recovered leads bear on today's top debts:
- **U_e and the PMNS matrix** (P-638) is a candidate for the second held-out quantity whose absence leaves the candidate's
  confirmation one target short (P-597).
- **A loop-level lift of the rank-≤2 Yukawa** (P-637) acts by sector (QCD acts on quarks only). That is exactly where the
  candidate's lepton–down relation fails (P-355).

The central direction, what holds τ and selects the point, was named at least six times in six vocabularies. It was split
across eleven rows with no owner, and its umbrella row had not moved since 2026-10-14. It now has one row (P-629) with
four never-computed handles (P-630–P-633).

## 5. The mechanism proposed (P-659, the owner's ruling; the rules change what is worked first, so they are the owner's)

- **M1. Direction leads age.** A [LEAD] row that comes from the owner, a brainstorm, a meditation, a framing ledger or a
  review's ranked list gets a 21-day clock, as a debt does. By then its first step must be run, declined with a reason, or
  put to the owner. The register gate would enforce this as rule 7.
- **M2. One owner row per central question.** Its children are listed, and it cannot close while a child is open. P-629 is
  the first.
- **M3. A merged item keeps its own acceptance cell.** The host cannot close DONE while a merged cell is unticked.
- **M4. Brainstorm intake.**
  - Every brainstorm, meditation, framing pass and review ends with a carry list, and each item is rowed the same day.
  - A FINDINGS line saying "owed", "carried forward", "not computed" or "next" must have a register row citing its arc. A
    scanner would fail the push otherwise.
- **M5. A review's ranked leads become action items.** The carry-age rule then fires on them.
- **M6. OWNER rows and drafts.**
  - OWNER rows are printed in every report to the owner, which a gate would list.
  - An audit-room draft cited by a row lands or is declined within seven days.
- **M7. Retired frames and frame cards.**
  - When a frame is retired, its open ideas are listed, and each is translated or declined.
  - A frame card whose free continuous data outnumber the object-fixed invariants that could fix them is graded
    fit-capacity up front. It cannot become the main line without the owner's word (Meditation 3's consequence clause).
