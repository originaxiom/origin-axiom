## Why

- **The law** (THE HIGGS SINGLET'S LAW; check fdaecb4f7 → 317e233f7). In B1656's frame with the record's triplet DR, a
  singlet keeps the six, |V| and J functions of the point only if:
  - it gains e^{iπ/4} under the T step;
  - it is unchanged under w → w + 1, w + τ and −w.

  It keeps the mirror's CP map only if σ → −σ̄.
- **What the law admits.** With the mirror kept, a constant-coefficient singlet lies on σ = i r η³/2. There the natural
  size r = 1 is the null Higgs.
- **What is untested on record.** The admissible axis away from the natural size. Main's robustness scan used the phases
  k = 0, 2, 5 and 8 of twelve, never ±i, and only |σ|²/|h·h| = ¼, 1 and 4.
- **The candidate, with no free number.** One singlet:
  - sized by the identity kernel's |a₀|, which is the same in every sector;
  - on the mirror's axis, with η³'s multiplier;
  - with the sector's sign from the adjugate reading (S291). P-583 ties that reading to the frame's own down–lepton
    relation on order-5 lines.
- **How much of RI-1 is left.** Only |a₀|, which B1656 already uses as |a₀|². RI-1's sign does not survive the law. The
  leptons here carry up's sign; RI-1 gives them down's.
- **The level.** This is a level-1 screen (THE_BAR step 6, GENESIS v1.61): p < 0.01 against the matched null, with every
  within-arc choice priced. A pass makes a CANDIDATE, which is frozen and earns no credit. The four CKM numbers are not
  untouched (S298), and no clean level-2 target is named yet: y_b/y_t carries the agy lane's prior look (S298), and θ̄
  depends on the labelling (the strong-angle note).

## The quantities (fixed now)

- **The CKM.** V = U_up† U_down, with U each sector's left singular vectors of its Yukawa, lightest first.
  - The corner order is |V_ub| < |V_td|, that is V[0, 2] against V[2, 0].
  - A corner modulus below 10⁻¹² is zero. If exactly one of the two is zero, the order is decided: zero is the smaller.
    If both are zero, the point is a tie, which is not nature's order.
  - The joint coarse fact (P-566's) is the corner order together with |V_ud| > 0.9 (V[0, 0]).
- **Degenerate points.** Let s₀ ≤ s₁ ≤ s₂ be a sector's singular values. If (s_{k+1} − s_k)/s₂ < 10⁻¹⁰ for k = 0 or 1, in up
  or in down, the point is excluded under that variant and counted, before the filter.
- **The filter**, main's, as in singlet_higgs_scan2.py, on each variant's own spectra:
  - both quark sectors hierarchical: s₂ > 0, 10⁻¹² < s₀/s₂ < 0.01 and s₁/s₂ < 0.1;
  - up steeper than down: s₁/s₂ (up) < ½ s₁/s₂ (down), and s₀/s₂ (up) < s₀/s₂ (down).
- **The rates.** For variant v and line ℓ, F_v(ℓ) counts the line's filtered points (of its six τ) and J_v(ℓ) those
  where the joint coarse fact holds. Then rate_v = ΣJ_v/ΣF_v, and Δ_v = rate_v − rate_none, in percentage points.
- **The test** (the matched null, fixed now): an exact paired sign-flip test on the points filtered under both variants.
  For a variant v against a reference (none unless stated):
  - per line ℓ, B(ℓ) counts the line's points filtered under both, and d(ℓ) = J_v(ℓ) − J_ref(ℓ) on those points;
  - Δ_both = Σd/ΣB;
  - p = P(Σ_ℓ s_ℓ d(ℓ) ≥ Σ_ℓ d(ℓ)) for independent signs s_ℓ = ±1, each with probability ½. It is computed exactly, by
    convolution over the lines (the d(ℓ) are integers).

  Under "no lift" each line's d is symmetric about 0. The unpaired Δ_v (each variant on its own filtered points) is
  reported beside it.
- **The price.** b = 11.58 bits, so the screen needs p·2^11.58 < 0.01, that is p < 3.27 × 10⁻⁶. The choices:
  - the size: the bare modulus or the natural one, null on the axis (1 bit);
  - r's sign, +i against −i, or which quark sector takes the adjugate (1 bit);
  - the reading, among the four relation families S290 scanned before the adjugate was named (2 bits);
  - the mirror condition (1 bit);
  - the radius rule and its end, L_max against L₂ (1 bit);
  - the band (1 bit);
  - Re τ (1 bit);
  - the direction DR, among B1824's twelve (log₂ 12 = 3.58 bits).

  DR is also the owner's directive's direction (P-284's C1), and the law's axis is defined by DR's own invariant. It is
  priced anyway, because P-520 lists the directions among the choices that entered through fits.

  P-555's 3.63 bits is a K budget at B1825's address. This screen claims no K; main rules whether P-566 Q1 applies. The
  read-outs report p·2⁷ beside p·2^11.58.
- **The power** (for the negative branch, fixed now). There are 500 simulated data sets, with seeds 1–500:
  - B(ℓ) and none's J(ℓ) on the common points are as realised;
  - the candidate's J(ℓ) are drawn with full line clustering: with probability q = min(1, r_both + 0.25), all of a
    line's common points hold the joint fact, otherwise none do. Here r_both is none's rate on the common points.
  - Each data set gets the exact test.

  The power is the fraction with Δ_both ≥ 10 points and p·2^11.58 < 0.01. The paired test has little power when the
  candidate keeps few of none's filtered points, so the likeliest failing outcome is UNDECIDED.
- **Degeneracy** includes s₂ = 0. The per-τ read-outs are counts and rates only.

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **K0 fails:** the instrument is not as stated. The values are withheld, the failure is recorded, and the card is VOID.
- **K2 holds (with K0): CANDIDATE**, level 1. It is frozen, never cited as support, and owed a level-2 confirmation by
  held-out prediction: two targets at p_V < 0.01/m_V, none of them the four, none clean yet. The ledger notes the
  0.01 expected by chance.
  - The sector sign is credited only if the candidate also beats even in the same paired test, at p < 0.01 with
    Δ_both > 0. Otherwise the candidate is "a singlet of size |a₀|".
  - If up-only passes K2's test, the candidate is "a sector-different singlet of size |a₀|", not an odd one.
  - If K4 fails, the pass is scoped to the lines that keep their hierarchy.
- **K2 fails** with at least 30 points filtered under both, and power at least 80%: **NOT FOUND within the reach.** The
  reach is:
  - the 230 lines at the six τ;
  - the small-cut L_max, H = I and DR;
  - up on S, down on adj S, leptons on S;
  - the size |a₀| and the phase +i.

  Nothing is closed. A singlet of another size, sign rule or construction is untouched.
- **K2 fails otherwise: UNDECIDED.**
- **K1 or K3 fails:** the controls do not behave as main's scans did. This is recorded, and K2 is read against the
  controls as computed.
- **A crash.** The failure is logged and its cause fixed in a disclosed commit, and the run starts again once, from the
  beginning. A second crash voids the card.
