## 2. What the read-out's framing presupposes

- **The framing.** B1846 poses the read-out "using only the object's own hyperbolic metric (Mostow-rigid; no chosen
  kinetic term)", and asks "whether the frame's couplings are read at a point of the fibration circle or as an
  average".
- **The circle is fixed.** "The metric fixes, with no choice, the harmonic map θ: M → ℝ/ℤ in that class" (B1846 § 1),
  up to a rotation of the circle, which the read-out's point removes.
- **The step that selects the reading.** The frame's τ is a fibre's conformal modulus: P-723's Q1 writes "the fibre's
  conformal modulus (the frame's τ)".
  - Three of the four readings are fibre moduli: τ(Σ_s), the Teichmüller history's points, and the pleated layers,
    which "are also fibre moduli" (c6b813450).
  - The tetrahedron's shape is not one: it "would need a dictionary from shapes to the fibre's modulus" (c6b813450 § 4).
  - What the framing adds is "at a point of the fibration circle". Over a level s of the metric's circle the fibre is
    the level set Σ_s, and the metric induces its conformal structure, τ(Σ_s). The map s ↦ τ(Σ_s) is P-727's "bridge
    between the circle's clock (harmonic time) and the moduli-space clocks".
- **So, read in the framing's own words, the point read-out's τ is τ(Σ_s)**: at Review 76's point, the systole, τ(Σ₀).
  This reduces P-714's question to the framing's words; it does not derive the framing. Main's disposition of harvest
  row 1607 states the same conditional: "the level surface's own modulus would replace (N2)'s Teichmüller modulus if
  the frame read it (P-727)".
- **With a weight, no single τ results.** The couplings at τ(Σ_s) are averaged against ρ, or uniformly for couplings
  built from dθ (B1846 § 6: "A coupling that is a scalar density on M averages with ρ. One built from dθ averages
  uniformly (T1)"); c6b813450 § 4: "An average returns no τ". P-729's C5 lifts ρ through exactly this loop.
- **"The metric alone" does not exclude the layers by itself.** For a hyperbolic once-punctured torus bundle the
  layered (Floyd–Hatcher) triangulation is the canonical decomposition (Lackenby, Comment. Math. Helv. 78 (2003)
  363–384), so the metric fixes it too, and B1846 § 3 places its tetrahedra in harmonic time. What excludes the layer
  readings is the step above: a tetrahedron or a pleated surface is not the fibre over s.

## 3. Why the other three are read from other structures

- **The layer readings are indexed by letters.** The letters can be placed on the circle by θ itself: B1846 § 3 gives
  "The layers in harmonic time. By tetrahedron, with θ as a circular mean", tetrahedron 3 (the run's middle) at
  θ ≈ 0.0003 and the pairs at ±θ. But what is read there is a tetrahedron or a pleated surface, not the fibre over s.
- **The Teichmüller apex lives on the monodromy's axis.** It is placed on M's circle by symmetry, not by a clock:
  B1847 § 4 reads it "at the same symmetric point", and c6b813450 § 1 fixes it by symmetry, given (N1) and (N3).
- **Location does not discriminate; the structure does.**
  - On the cyclic palindromes σ is an isometry. It preserves θ up to θ ↦ c − θ. It carries the layered triangulation
    to itself, because that triangulation is canonical and an isometry preserves the canonical decomposition (B1846
    § 3's ±θ table agrees). On Teichmüller space it acts by the reflection J that conjugates the monodromy to its
    inverse (B1852 § 1), which maps the monodromy's axis to itself.
  - σ fixes two levels of the circle, the tube and the anti-apex's level (B1847 § 4). At the tube every structure has
    a σ-fixed reading: the level set Σ₀; tetrahedron 3, the σ-fixed one, with the pair of pleated layers σ swaps
    around it; and the axis's σ-fixed top, the apex.
  - Each structure's own selection picks the tube: the systole's level (Review 76; B1846 § 2 calls it "the same place
    as ρ's mode"), the deepest layers at the run's middle (P-723), and the apex (B1846 § 6: "That is where P-717
    places the apex").
  - At k = 1 the four readings at the tube are τ(Σ₀) 6.149, the apex 3.65, the pleated pair 2.87 and the tetrahedron
    2.58 (§ 1). Which structure is read decides the value, not where.

## 4. It is not the clock

  chooses which path is read (the Teichmüller geodesic rather than pleated surfaces or tetrahedra). It does not choose
  that path's law." (3d32077ea § 1).
- **The clock is a different choice, the law over histories.**
  - Harvest row 1597 (VERIFIED S306b): "The read-out's premise (N2) presupposes one history, not its law, and one
    history carries either clock".
  - B1846 § 6: "B1815's 'the clock stays CHOSEN' stands for clocks over histories. The circle's own clock is not
    chosen."
  - befdd5ffc: "τ*₆ = i√5φ needs no clock".
- **So P-714's reading does not fold into P-439.** It is a choice within one history, beside the clock, which stays
  CHOSEN over histories (B1815; door C, P-645).
