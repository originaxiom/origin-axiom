## 2. The second intuition in this frame (checked; cited; reading)

- **The owner's "a ab ba principle"** is the principle itself: main's passage 5 has "the principle of a ab ba
  existence". "The wave that pushes back" is its opposing force. There are two renderings.
- **Main's** (S_THE_PUSH_BACK): the step against its pull-back.
  - The rest points are the elliptic fixed points. A thread never rests.
  - Averaged along the motion, the defect has an order-one shape, and "the end cutoff still supplies the smallness":
    gap (A) of §6.
- **chat1's** (below): the order-swap, ab ≠ ba. That is chat1's reading of the words, not the owner's.

- **The order-swaps generate the commutator subgroup.** The seed LR and its swap RL generate exactly the commutator
  subgroup Γ′ of the modular group: index 12 in SL(2, ℤ) and 6 in PSL(2, ℤ), by coset enumeration.
  - These are the moves that #R − #L mod 12, and so every character, cannot see.
  - Γ′\H is the modular torus (Markov traces 3, 3, 6).
- **Their commutator is a loop around the cusp.** [LR, RL] = [[5, −6], [6, −7]] has trace −2, so it is parabolic, fixing
  1. It is the loop around the torus's puncture, and it winds six times around the modular surface's one cusp.
- **The Higgs sees only the abelian shadow.** Its character is η⁶'s multiplier: i^(#R − #L) on every word (checked on
  254 words to 10⁻²⁸).
  - By Ghys, #R − #L is the thread's linking number with the trefoil, up to orientation (cited; the SM seat's §139).
  - So relative to the matter the Higgs cannot see the order-swaps. It feels each thread only through that linking
    number, mod 4.
- **The depth, though, does not come from the order-swap** (checked, W-trace).
  - The integrals' weight sits where a thread goes deepest into the cusp, and depth comes from long runs of one letter:
    L and R are themselves parabolic.
  - The dominant threads are L^(t−2)R and its mirror, the least balanced in each layer (#R − #L = ±(t − 3)).
  - The balanced threads, those made of order-swaps (LLRR, LLLRRR), are the shallowest in their layers (to t = 12, the
    range computed).
- **The modular group's three kinds of motion (reading).**

  | motion | elements | where | what the record finds there |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | rest | elliptic, e.g. a step against its pull-back (main's L⁻¹R, trace 1) | the orbits of ω and i | exact zeros: the matter's isotropy defect vanishes on ω's orbit (W87, B1657). The Yukawa integrand Y3x vanishes at ω's images (W-trace's instrument note; the link to W87 is not checked) |
  | one move repeated; the order-swap taken once around (in the cover) | parabolic: L, R, [LR, RL] | the cusp | the norm integrals of every layer to t = 12, untwisted (§3), and the units' cusp exponents (W84; the per-field law of 19:41) |
  | a thread | hyperbolic: every positive word with both letters | its closed geodesic | the integrals |

  - **Caveats.** The zeros are not only at rest. Main's motion defect also vanishes toward the cusp, and the thread
    frame's first-generation zero (R·R = 0, by Jacobi) holds at every point.
  - So the table says where things can be read, not what causes them.
  - The owner's "the wave that pushes back" can be read onto it; it is not derived from it.

## 3. The rule that picks no thread (checked; reading)

- **The rule** (the SM seat's §139.4, carried into the thread frame). Sum over every thread of one trace t, a layer, and
  choose none. A layer is every primitive closed geodesic of one length.
  - The classical sums (Duke–Imamoḡlu–Tóth and Katok–Sarnak for D > 0; Zagier for D < 0, over CM points) are indexed
    by discriminant.
  - They group the same threads differently. A trace layer at D = t² − 4 is the classical sum without the powers of
    shallower threads (checked).
- **What it gives** (W-trace, untwisted, all 29 primitive threads of trace 3 to 12; checked).
  - The units' norm integrals grow at the cusp exponentially in the height: slopes 10.6–11.9, against 2π·7/4 = 11.0
    for the units' leading exponent.
  - So each layer's deepest pair carries its norm integrals, to ≤ 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁴ by t = 12.
  - The Yukawa integrals are shared (the pair holds 0.2–0.9 of them).
  - The layer's masses are the pair's, moved by up to 0.38 in C1's ln(m₂/m₃). That quantity falls on average 3.5 per
    unit of trace, the step drifting from 4.7 to 3.0.
- **Reading.** The dominance is checked; calling it a selection is the reading. Unregularised, the sum re-weights onto
  the L·Rⁿ family, which the volume reading also picked (13:43) and the 19:41 law bounds.
- **Duke's limit is not reached here, for two reasons.**
  - The cusp is not regularised. The record's regulator, B1654's L, is a cutoff. Whether a canonical renormalisation
    removes the growth without a cutoff is open.
  - The carry of Duke's theorem from fundamental discriminants to trace layers is not shown.

## 4. A wave between threads (checked; reading)

- **The linking number.** The natural topological interaction between two threads is their linking number. Every pair
  of the 69 threads to length 8 links (§139). Its crossing-sum form (Simon; Rickards, answering Ghys) counts the places
  where the two threads cross (cited). There, in the untwisted frame, the vertex's first-generation zero holds:
  G(R, b; R) = 0 (checked). So in that form it cannot lift the first generation.
- **A lift needs the coupling vector carried off the matter's point.**
  - The reproducing kernel built from the Higgs thread's own pairing returns R exactly, so it lifts nothing (checked).
  - Kernels built from other pairings the frame has (the matter thread's, or the layer's) are argued, not computed, to
    lift generically.
  - So does the field's own propagator, at a spectral parameter: the owner's "wave" made literal.
  - What is free is which pairing, which parameter.
- **Verdict (reading).** No interaction between threads that the frame singles out lifts the first generation. Every
  version that may lift needs a choice the frame does not make. The owner has ruled the term parked.

## 7. Lessons for method (the symmetries checked bitwise; the rest are lessons)

- **Deduplicate under the frame's exact symmetries before counting.** There were three:
  - word reversal with the L ↔ R mirror (configurations);
  - q → −q;
  - q → k·q mod N in the per-field reading (assignments, per order).

  The first sealed run counted duplicates, and its Bonferroni read-outs were reachable only through them.
- **Probe closure at a generic start.** Started on an image of ω, the probe reads 0/0, because Y3x vanishes there.
- **Re-running the same assignments on fresh configurations is not independent evidence.** On T-low, the null counts
  correlated at r = 0.82 between N = 11's two class orbits, and the null's size caps the evidence.
