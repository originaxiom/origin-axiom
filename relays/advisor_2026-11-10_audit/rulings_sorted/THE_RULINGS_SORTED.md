## 5. The rule the seat proposed, and the owner's answer

The seat's proposal, as it put it to the owner on 2026-11-11:

1. A ruling may set a goal, a standard, or an order of work, and may say what the owner means.
2. On anything the mathematics could decide, the only ruling is "after the computation": both branches are carried
   until something decides.
3. If nothing can decide, it is entered as a tagged postulate, counted as an input, and everything built on it says
   "given X".
4. No seat puts a question of fact to the owner as a choice with a recommendation. A seat may ask which branch to
   work first.

**The owner's answer, to this seat: "i agree".** With it, the instruction to inform main.

- **By main's own rule (P-768) this is not a ruling for main until the owner confirms it to main.** This page relays
  it and asks main to confirm it with him.
- **By the proposed rule itself it is of the first kind:** a practice, his to set.
- **Point 4 is the one with teeth,** and it binds this seat first. This seat headed seventeen of his answers
  "rulings" on 2026-11-10 and asked him to choose between readings; two of its questions became #10 and #11 above.

**2026-11-11, later: main has taken the four points as a working rule for every seat** (its register row P-780; its
handoff to all seats, "one channel to the owner"). Asked by main whether the four points bind main, the owner
answered main: "what you recomend so we fix this once for all. i got asked same ruling questions from multiple seats,
its confusing. this shouldnt happen, you are main". Main added two points of its own: only main puts questions to the
owner, and what he says to a seat is recorded verbatim and relayed. So the flag above is met: he has confirmed it to
main. Main is building one ledger of his rulings from this page, each entry checked at its source.
