## What the gap is

Laid end to end, what the record holds is a chain that runs one way:

> the principle → the grammar → the golden background → its level → a premise at that level → a departure → its
> geometry → the numbers read from it → the couplings.

**Nothing computed at the end constrains anything chosen at the start.** Every link is a choice or a computation
from the link before it; no link is an equation that an earlier link must satisfy. That is why the conditions on any
value stand in series and none can be closed alone (`dry_run/DRY_RUN_VALUE_CHAIN_B1841.md`, §6), and why the owner's
claim of one self-consistent outcome is not yet a statement the mathematics can decide
(`THE_CLAIM_STATED_THE_CLOSED_LOOP.md`).

A line can be extended for ever and stays as free as its first choice. Only a loop can fix its own beginning.

## Proposed words

- **A line result** is anything that follows from choices upstream and constrains none of them.
- **A back-edge** is a relation in which something computed downstream must agree with something chosen upstream:
  an equation the choice has to satisfy.
- **The loop** is closed when every upstream choice on a value's chain is the target of a back-edge, so that the
  whole is a fixed point and not a sequence.

## Proposed test, before any arc: line or loop?

1. **Name the upstream choices** the arc stands on: everything chosen, fitted or postulated on its chain.
2. **Name what it computes downstream.**
3. **Does anything in 2 constrain anything in 1?** If yes, state the equation: that is a back-edge, and the arc says
   which choice it closes. If no, the arc is a line result. It is labelled so, and it is not presented as closing
   anything.

A line result is still a result. What the rule forbids is counting line results as progress toward a derived value:
on a line the distance to the beginning never shortens.

- The weave rule and this rule are two halves of one discipline: the weave rule says a result must be about all the
  threads together; this rule says the chain of results must return on itself.
