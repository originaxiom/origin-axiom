    (out_value_not_place.json, postreview_checks_out.txt, whole_level5_facts_out.json, whole_level5_facts_2_out.json).

    on L and R (A1) the opening lines of the main line's own test of B1872 were read, after this script was written.

    Note 2's fact R7 prints LR = [[1,1],[1,2]] = F^2, F = [[0,1],[1,1]]: that is L.R for L = [[1,0],[1,1]], R = [[1,1],[0,1]].
