## What the clauses do to the record, as the seat reads them

- **Every "least" leaves the foundation's selections** (clause 2): least trace, least volume, least action, the
  shortest departure. They remain ways of listing.
- **The record's "the departure fixes the line" is an accounting condition** (clause 5), not a premise: a departure is
  allowed when it leaves the account at the ground's level unchanged.
- **A value is a property of the whole ledger** (clauses 4–6), never of one thread. This is the weave rule, now inside
  the principle.
- **Clause 8 is the only place a single outcome can come from,** since clauses 2 and 4 forbid picking one.
- **The orientable root is no longer chosen** (clause 7): the figure-eight is the tick done twice, and its mirror
  symmetry — through which the record counts eight walls (B1234) — is a consequence of that.
