# PREREGISTRATION — attack A: ambiguity

**From:** the advisor seat. **Written and committed before any reader is asked.** No mathematics is computed by the
seat in this attack and nothing is compared with measurement.
**The owner's word it follows:** 2026-11-10, "go" (R12).

## 1. The question

Do the owner's words determine the mathematics? If two readers, each working from the words alone and each judged
faithful to them, arrive at different mathematics, then at the point where they differ the principle does not
decide — and what decides there is a choice that should be written as one.

## 2. The design

Five readers and one judge. Every reader is a fresh instance that has seen none of this session, none of the
repository and none of the seat's own formal statement (v0, `../THE_PRINCIPLE_TOP_DOWN.md` §2). Each receives one of
the prompts in this folder, with `OWNERS_WORDS.md` inserted, and nothing else.

| Reader | Prompt | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| F1, F2 | `PROMPT_A1_free.md` | given only the words, which mathematics does a reader reach for? |
| F3, F4 | `PROMPT_A2_arena.md` | given the words and the bare arena (two counts, two updates; the target hidden), what exists first, and what does "depart further" become? |
| F5 | `PROMPT_A3_adversary.md` | can a reading at least as faithful make something else win? (This reader is told what the other reading selects; it is not blind by design.) |
| J | `PROMPT_JUDGE.md` | tabulates the five against a fixed checklist, rates each for faithfulness to the words, and lists where faithful readings differ. Blind to how each was produced and to v0. |

The readers' answers are saved as they are written and committed unedited.

## 3. Disclosures

- **The readers are instances of one model family**, of more than one tier. Their agreement is weaker evidence than
  agreement among independent readers; their disagreement is not weakened by this. The same prompts are left here so
  that the owner's other seats, which are built differently, can be given them.
- **The seat is not blind.** It wrote v0 and knows what the record selects. So the seat does not rate faithfulness:
  the judge does, and the final rating of what is faithful to the owner's words is the owner's.
- **Prompt A2 gives the arena.** Two counts and two updates are `GENESIS.md`'s GM1 and GM2. Whether the words alone
  lead there is what A1 tests; A2 tests what happens once they are granted.
- **W5 was said after the seat's analysis**, in the seat's terms ("line", "loop"). It is included because the owner
  says it is what he has been telling the seats; its gloss is marked as the seat's.

## 4. The checklist (fixed)

C1 the setting · C2 what cancellation means: absolute, relative to a ground, or both · C3 the first existent ·
C4 the second and third · C5 does order matter · C6 the exchange of the two sides and the mirror · C7 selection: a
minimum under an ordering, or persistence with no ordering · C8 does what persists become the ground, and by what
rule · C9 what interaction means · C10 what is forbidden · C11 the choices each reader declares.

## 5. What counts as what (fixed)

- **A choice point at item X:** two readers the judge rates 4 or 5 for faithfulness give different answers at X.
- **The attack lands on the words** if there is a choice point at C1 between F1 and F2: the words alone do not fix
  the mathematics, and the arena is a postulate, as `GENESIS.md` already grades it.
- **The attack lands inside the arena** if there is a choice point at C3 or C4 among F3, F4 and F5, with F5 rated 4
  or 5.
- **The principle holds inside the arena** if F3 and F4 agree with each other at C3 and on the form of C4, and F5 is
  rated 3 or below.
- **Agreement with v0** is read by the seat only after the judge's table exists, on these seven points: (a) the first
  existent is the class of LR; (b) the criterion is hyperbolicity, or equivalent; (c) positive words as the normal
  form of what never cancels; (d) no fixed character, or equivalent, as a criterion; (e) a cancellation relative to
  a ground; (f) a level attached to the ground; (g) a single lengthened run as the next thing.

## 6. The seat's expectations (written first)

| | Expectation |
|---|---|
| F1 and F2 reach the same setting | unlikely (about 30%). Readers of these words usually reach for a calculus of marks, for sets and ordinals, or for symmetry breaking; a pair of counts under two updates is not the obvious first choice. |
| F3 and F4 both give the class of LR as the first existent | likely (about 80%): once the arena is given, "both updates, and it must not cancel" leaves little else |
| F3 and F4 both state a criterion equivalent to hyperbolicity | likely (about 70%) |
| either of F3, F4 invents a cancellation relative to a ground, at a level | unlikely (about 20%) |
| either of F3, F4 arrives at a single lengthened run as the next step | possible (about 35%), by "the smallest change", not by a level |
| either arrives at the level 5 or the run of 6 | very unlikely (about 5%) |
| F5 is rated 4 or 5 | about 50%. The likely routes: the single tick with the exchange (the non-orientable sibling), a self-similar rule (a → ab, b → a) in place of a departure, or a different sense of "first". |
| the judge finds at least three choice points in C2–C8 | likely (about 75%) |

If the expectations about (e), (f) and (g) hold, then the part of v0 that selects the departure is the seat's
construction and the record's, not something the words lead a reader to. That would not make it wrong; it would make
it a clause to be written into the principle and ruled on by the owner.

## 7. What the attack produces, whatever happens

A list of choice points, each with the two readings and the phrase of the owner's words that fails to decide between
them, for the owner to rule on. Each ruling turns an ambiguity into an explicit clause of the principle.
