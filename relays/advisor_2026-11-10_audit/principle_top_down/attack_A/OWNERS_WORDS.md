> Existence is what remains when cancelling to nothing cannot complete. That remainder is a description that cannot
> be exhausted.

**W2:**

> the law that causes existence as i understand from our work is "if something is allowed to happen it will happen"

**W3:**

> interaction between objects in relationship should produce reality, not single objects alone

> take every object the principle allows or forces, none chosen by hand, none emerging from another, and let the
> forced interaction on all of them provide what is needed

**W4:**

> id like for you to understand also the intuition behind it, to understand the vision of the existence in
> relationship to inexistence, the very in-between act of disctintion (the disctintion between question and answer,
> between being and notbeing, between here and there, is and not, on and on feel free to imagine rest yourself) that
> is the act, selection and dynamics of existence. the difference between distinction gets selected to depart
> further, their interaction matters

**W5** (said of the research programme built on the principle; the bracketed gloss is not the author's):

> a real gap exists in the program because the entire programme is an open one-way line, not a closed loop

*[Gloss: the chain of derivation so far runs one way — from the principle to its consequences — and nothing at the
