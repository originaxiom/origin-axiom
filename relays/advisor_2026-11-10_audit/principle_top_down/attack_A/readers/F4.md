## 1. Definitions in the arena

**Setting (decided).** Counts are nonnegative. The only primitive acts are L and R. Inverses are not acts. The swap of the two counts is not an act. A history is a word in L and R. Its matrix M(w) is the product of the letters.

**Distinction.** A distinction is an ordered inequality between the two counts, i.e. the direction of (a,b) in the projective line, the slope b/a. A step (L or R) makes one distinction by making one count exceed the other. Equal counts (1,1) is the undistinguished seed, and (0,0) is nothing.

**Cancellation.** I find three senses in "cancelling to nothing".
- (c1) *Count cancellation* is Euclid's subtraction. From (a,b) subtract the smaller from the larger. It undoes L when a>b and R when b>a. It is only a partial inverse, and it is allowed only where it stays nonnegative.
- (c2) *Word cancellation* is the deletion of a letter against its inverse, X X⁻¹. It is available only if inverses are acts, and I refuse that. In the positive monoid only the empty word cancels to the identity.
- (c3) *Complete cancellation* means that c1 run on a direction reaches (0,0) after finitely many steps. This holds exactly for rational directions. For example L^n applied to (1,0) cancels completely.

**Existence of a history.** A history w exists iff the periodic history w^∞ has a direction whose cancellation cannot complete. Concretely, the attractor of w^∞ must be irrational. This holds iff M(w) is hyperbolic (trace > 2), iff w contains both L and R. Existence is a property of the cyclic class: rotations of w are the same description restarted, and powers w^k are the same description repeated. So existents are *primitive cyclic words* containing both letters. "Remainder that cannot be exhausted" means the infinite non-terminating continued fraction of the attractor.

**Interaction.** Interaction means that both L and R occur in one history, in an order that matters.
- L^∞ and R^∞ are parabolic, with trace 2 and a rational fixed direction. Their cancellation completes, so they do not exist.
- A single update alone is therefore always cancellable. Only the pair, in relationship, leaves a remainder (W3).

## 2. What exists first

The criterion is the trace of M(w), which is the size of the smallest nonterminating remainder. Length gives the same order on these words. Ties are not broken, because nothing is chosen by hand (W3). Tied words exist together.

1. **LR**, with M = [[2,1],[1,1]] and trace 3. Its attractor is the golden ratio. It is unique at its level and is the first.
2. **LLR**, with M = [[3,2],[1,1]] and trace 4.
3. **LRR**, with M = [[3,1],[2,1]] and trace 4.

LLR and LRR are tied, so the second and third exist simultaneously. They are a mirror pair, and mirror here is a relation, not an act. The next levels are LLLR and LRRR (trace 5), then LLRR (trace 6). (LR)² is excluded as a repetition of the first.

## 3. Order and mirror

- **LR versus RL.** As matrices they differ: RL = [[1,1],[1,2]]. As existents they are the same cyclic class, since RL is a rotation of LR. So *order matters for the matrix and for the attractor*, but not for the existent class. This is the one place where I let rotation, but not inverse, count as the same.
- **Mirror (swap S).** S L S = R, so S sends the word w to its letter-exchange. I refuse S as an act. Allowing it would identify here with there and L with R, which means choosing an origin by hand. It would collapse LLR and LRR into one existent, and it would turn "two simultaneous existents" into "one".
- **What follows from refusing.** Chirality survives. LLR and LRR are distinct. Only self-mirror classes (LR, LLRR, ...) are achiral. Allowing S would halve the chiral pairs, and the list of existents would then be a quotient that I decided, not a forced one.

## 4. Depart further

**Ground.** G = LR, so M(G) = [[2,1],[1,1]].

**Difference between distinctions.** For two existents G and w, the difference is the commutator trace K(G,w) = tr(G w G⁻¹ w⁻¹) − 2. This is an integer. It is 0 iff G and w share a fixed direction, which means they are not different distinctions. A larger K means a larger departure. (G⁻¹ is used only to define the number, not as an act in any history.)

**Rule.** Among existents w that are not a rotation or power of G, select those with the *least positive* K(G,w). Ties are broken by trace, and then tied words are all selected. Subsequent layers have the next K value.

**Computation** (primitive cyclic words up to length 7, run in Python):
- K = 1: LLR, LRR (trace 4), then LLRLR, LRLRR (trace 10), then LLRLRLR, LRLRLRR (trace 26), and so on.
- K = 4: LLLR, LRRR (trace 5), LLRR (trace 6), then LLLRLR, LRLRRR (trace 13), and so on.

The first selection is **LLR and LRR together**. This agrees with the trace order of section 2. The rule is honest about a problem: the K=1 layer appears to be infinite, so under strict lexicographic order (K, trace) the K=4 layer is reached only after an infinite one.

**Minimum or persistence.** This rule is a *minimum under an ordering* (K, then trace). It is not persistence with no ordering. W2 says everything allowed happens, and W2 as stated has no ordering. So my ordering is only a way of listing what exists, not of making it exist. I have not shown that K=1 is forced by the words. A "maximum departure" reading is rejected, because it has no first element.

## 5. What is forbidden

- Any history with only L or only R. It is parabolic and its cancellation completes.
- The empty history, which is nothing.
- Non-primitive histories, as separate existents: w^k for k>1 is a repetition, not a new description.
- Rotations, as separate existents.
- Negative counts and counts exchanged by an act.
- Histories whose attractor is rational (these are exactly the finitely cancellable ones). This also forbids any "final" existent, because every level has a next one.
- A last element in the order: there are infinitely many primitive classes at growing traces.
- Chiral identification: LLR = LRR is forbidden, because swap is refused.

## 6. Choices (not forced by the words)

| Choice | Rejected alternative |
|---|---|
| Counts nonnegative | Allow negative counts (full SL2(Z), where L⁻¹ makes everything cancel) |
| Inverses not acts; c1 is a partial inverse only | Free group, where all words cancel via X X⁻¹ |
| Swap refused | Swap allowed (quotient by mirror) |
| Distinction = slope of (a,b) | A distinction = the pair of counts as such |
| Existence = irrational attractor of w^∞ (trace > 2) | Existence = any nonempty word; or = fixed integer vector |
| Rotation and power identify histories | Distinct words are distinct existents; or all powers separate |
| Order by trace, ties coexist | Order by length alone; or break ties by lexicographic order |
| Seed (1,1) | Seed (1,0) or (0,1) |
| Commutator trace as "difference between distinctions" | Distance between attractors (Farey distance, Hilbert metric), or cross ratio |
| Least positive K selects | Greatest K, or all K>0 at once |
| Tie-break inside a K-layer by trace | Tie-break by length, or no tie-break |
| Existence refers to the cyclic class | Existence refers to individual matrices |

W5 is not formalised: nothing in my answer closes the loop. K depends on G, but nothing returns to constrain G. Note that G itself is selected by the unordered trace criterion, not by K, so the one-way line is still present.
