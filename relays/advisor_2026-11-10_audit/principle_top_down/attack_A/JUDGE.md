## 2. Faithfulness

- **F1 — 4.** Takes "the difference between distinction gets selected to depart further" literally (the commutator, bracketed again), and ties "not single objects alone" to W1: one generator is exhausted at level 1. Strain: each remainder is finite, so "a description that cannot be exhausted" holds only of the whole tower; its "closed loop" is one arbitrary self-embedding.
- **F2 — 3.** Same commutator reading, but "cannot be exhausted" becomes torsion-freeness, which a single generator already has. A single object therefore satisfies the principle with no interaction, against "not single objects alone", and a is put before b by hand.
- **F3 — 4 (borderline).** The most literal W1: "cancelling to nothing cannot complete" is a subtraction that never ends, the remainder is an unending expansion, and its period gives a "closed loop". Strain: "depart further" becomes least Lagrange departure, a measure the words never name, and splitting a tied mirror pair alphabetically sits badly with "none chosen by hand".
- **F4 — 4.** Same literal W1; keeps ties because "none chosen by hand" and concedes that "if something is allowed to happen it will happen" gives no ordering. Strain: "the difference between distinction" becomes a commutator trace whose least value is selected, which it admits is unforced; the "closed loop" is left open.
- **F5 — 3.** Literal on "what remains" and on interaction that "should produce reality". But the remainder becomes a number, not "a description"; by its own account its existents arise from earlier ones, against "none emerging from another", and each later one holds less, a poor fit for "depart further".
