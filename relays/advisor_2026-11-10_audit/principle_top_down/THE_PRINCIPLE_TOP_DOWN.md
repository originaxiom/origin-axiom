**It stands (in a stated form) when:** one formal statement with no chosen ingredient reproduces the foundation's
items as consequences, survives the alternatives of attack A, gives at least one sealed new consequence that checks,
and has a recursion that is defined at every step.
**It is flawed (in that form) when:** every faithful statement needs a choice at the same place — then that choice is
the real postulate and should be written as one — or the recursion branches without end, or a theorem refutes it.

**The seat's expectation, written before any of this is run:** it will stand as a generator of the foundation's
structure, and it will be found one clause short at exactly one place — what counts as detection, that is, who the
ground is and at which level it looks. It will not by itself give a continuous value; that is the closed loop's work.
