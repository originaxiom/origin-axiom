# THE CLAIM, STATED — one self-consistent outcome as a closed loop

**From:** the advisor seat. **To:** the owner and cc. **Kind:** `propose:` — a statement of the owner's claim in a
form that can be proved or refuted. Data-free: no measured number appears and none is compared.
**The owner's rulings it states:** 2026-11-10, "the claim is one self-consistent outcome" (R2), and, to the seat's
question which of four meanings of "self-consistent" is meant, "yes the closed loop" (R8).
**Status of everything below:** the claim is POSTULATED. Nothing here is proved, and section 4 says what the record
already holds against the easy versions.

## 1. The claim in words

> What exists is fixed by the requirement that it reproduce itself: the configuration determines its geometry, the
> geometry determines the numbers read from it, those numbers determine which configurations are allowed, and the
> configuration is the one that its own numbers allow. There is exactly one such configuration.

"Everything allowed happens" (plenitude) says what takes part. The closed loop says what "allowed" finally means: a
configuration is allowed when it survives its own consequences.

## 2. The claim as a statement

Four maps, composed into one:

| Link | From → to | In the record's terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Geom** | a history H → its geometry g(H) | the hyperbolic metric of the manifold H builds |
| **Read** | the geometry → the numbers r read from it | τ and whatever else a frame evaluates |
| **Data** | the numbers → what is conserved or visible, d(r) | levels, charges, the line, the couplings |
| **Adm** | d → the histories that d allows, and the one it selects | the premise a departure must satisfy; the rule that picks among those that do |

**Φ = Adm ∘ Data ∘ Read ∘ Geom** maps histories to histories.

> **(CL)** Each of the four maps is forced — it contains no chosen or fitted ingredient — and Φ has exactly one
> fixed point, Φ(H\*) = H\*, up to the conventions the record already grants (one unit, one orientation).

Three things have to be true, separately:

- **(CL-1) the maps are forced;**
- **(CL-2) a fixed point exists;**
- **(CL-3) it is the only one.**

The values of the programme are then the numbers Read gives at H\*. Which of them is which physical quantity is the
dictionary (the identification ledger), a separate debt that (CL) does not pay.

### The finding of this note: the record holds a line, not a loop

Laid end to end, what the record has is

> grammar → the golden background → level 5 → a premise at level 5 → the run of 6 → its geometry → τ read out → couplings.

**Nothing computed at the end flows back to constrain anything chosen at the start.** The level comes from the
background's discriminant, not from τ. The premise is posited, not produced by the couplings. So today (CL) is not
false; it is not yet a statement, because Data → Adm does not depend on Read. The construction the claim needs is a
**back-edge**: a quantity computed downstream that an upstream choice must agree with.

This replaces "the weighting rule" as the name of the open problem. A weight on threads was a way to turn many into
one number; the loop needs instead one equation that the many must satisfy.

## 5. How (CL) is tested, data-free

Each test is written and sealed before it runs; none compares anything with measurement.

| Test | What it does | What refutes |
|---|---|---|
| **T0 — the loop is a loop** | with a candidate back-edge written down, show that Adm really depends on Read: change τ in the definition and the allowed set changes | the allowed set does not move: there is no back-edge |
| **T1 — existence** | compute Φ on the candidate history and check Φ(H) = H | Φ(H) ≠ H |
| **T2 — uniqueness** | enumerate a declared family (every history with at most two departures to a stated length; every level to a stated bound) and compute Φ on all of it | two distinct fixed points, or none |
| **T3 — it can fail** | name in advance a history that must not be a fixed point, and show it is not | the control is also a fixed point: the condition is vacuous |
| **T4 — the second thread** | run the same loop on the other metallic backgrounds (silver, bronze) | the loop closes on every background: it does not select the golden one, and SE1 and SE2 stay chosen |
| **T5 — a second reader** | an independent implementation by a seat that has not seen the first result | the two disagree |

T2 is the programme-level falsifier ruling R2 asked for: two self-consistent outcomes exhibited refute the claim.
T4 is the weave discipline's second-thread control applied to the claim itself.

## 6. The two ways this ends

- **(CL) holds.** One history, all four links forced. The values are then the invariants of one rigid manifold —
  definite numbers with nothing to adjust — and the remaining debt is the dictionary.
- **(CL) fails.** No back-edge can be forced, or Φ has several fixed points, or none. Then "one self-consistent
  outcome" is not what this grammar gives, and the record's other picture — an address among many — is what remains.
  That would be a result, banked as a negative with what would reopen it.

The seat's view of the odds, for what it is worth: (CL-1) is the hard part. Every forward link has a choice in it
today, and four earlier routes to fix τ each brought a free number with them. The claim is worth stating because it
turns "derive the values" into one construction with a pass and a fail.
