## 1. In one sentence

**A read-out on the whole is accepted as a selection only if it places something that its own symmetries do not
already place, that does not move when its chosen ingredients are changed, and that is not what the level would
give at any level.** Everything else it may still be: a result, typed by what it rests on.

## 3. The eight conditions

Each is to be met **in the design, before any number exists.** A design that cannot meet one says so and is typed
accordingly; that is not a failure of the design.

| # | condition | accepted when | what it guards against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **every ingredient is listed and typed** | the level, the mark, the grammar, the clock, the operator, the rule for "the" state, and what of the state is read, each marked forced, postulated or chosen, with its alternatives and their number | an ingredient that is found only when the result depends on it |
| **R2** | **it is a state of the whole** | it is defined on the quotient by the allowed subweave and never evaluates a quantity of one history (an apex, a height, a departure) | fact 1: the admission form of the first back-edge |
| **R3** | **existence and uniqueness come first** | the space in which "the" state lives is shown to have dimension one, or the design says which member is taken and counts that as a choice | fact 4: a "first state" that does not exist, or is one of several |
| **R4** | **symmetry is computed first, and separately** | before any spectrum, the design lists the symmetries of its own construction and the points fixed by all of them, and states what those alone force | fact 3: reporting as a result what symmetry gave |
| **R5** | **what counts as a place** | written in advance: a single point; or an orbit, with what a value on an orbit means; and the margin by which a peak must stand out, set from the instrument's error | a peak chosen after seeing the profile |
| **R6** | **the result survives its own choices** | for each ingredient marked chosen, the run is repeated over the alternatives R1 lists (at the least: the record's three clocks). The place must not move by more than the stated margin | fact 5: a result that is a function of the clock |
| **R7** | **the vacuity control** | the same construction is run at levels other than 5, on the golden ground, and on another ground at its own level. If the place is the same kind of point at every level, the result is the level restated | fact 7 |
| **R8** | **no measured number, in the design or in the run** | the card compares its place with no value of the modulus on record. Any comparison is a later card under the owner's hold | a look that was not counted |
