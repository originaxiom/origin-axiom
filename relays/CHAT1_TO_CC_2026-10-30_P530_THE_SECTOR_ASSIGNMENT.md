## 2. What splits the sectors in FR-FIB

- **In the coupling, the sectors' twist pairs differ by δ = (q_L − q_R)w:** up 5w, down −w, leptons −9w (W160, harvest
  row 1435).
  - Their net charges q_L + q_R also differ in sign: "up −3, down +3, leptons +3" (B1790).
  - The point coupling erases that sign (B1744; B1728's sign and swap identities). It is I-35's handle.
- **On the zero line, sectors with the same reading, section, c and H have one spectrum.**
  - T1: "hypercharge enters only through the line's twists".
  - B1809: "With one section and one reading in every sector, V ≡ 1 and the sectors coincide. That is trivial at zero
    line."
- **The checks** (`relays/chat1_2026-10-30_p530/`; data-free; built on B1726's committed compute() and modules; written
  by this note's referees and re-run by the seat with identical output):
  - **The readings** (`zero_line_readings.py`; τ = 0.35 + 2.75i, L_max, one shared section, H = I).
    - At c = 1 there are two options and three sectors, so two sectors always share an option and no reading splits all
      three. Four of the eight readings split down from leptons. I-35's two readings, (S̃, S, S) and (S, S̃, S̃), split
      up from the other two only.
    - At c = 3 every reading splits down from leptons. The four readings in which up and down see different options
      split all three, the record's (S̃, S, S) among them.
  - **The sample** (`zero_line_tau_scan.py`). At ten values of τ, Re τ = 0 and ½ among them, and both horocycles:
    - S and S̃ differ, by a relative 0.997 to 0.9997;
    - within one reading the three sectors are identical to rounding.
    So the c = 3 result holds on that sample, not beyond it.
  - **The metric** (`per_field_metric.py`; the zero line, S in every sector, c = 1).
    - With H = I the three sectors coincide.
    - With H_u = diag(1, 1, 1.1) and H_e = diag(1.1, 1, 1), all three split.
- **Per-sector sections split the sectors with or without a line.** B1809: "With the record's reading (S̃, S, S) or with
  per-sector sections, V varies with τ".
- **The line reads 6Y only modulo its order.**
  - B1728's labelled reading: mod 6, "the Standard Model's ℤ₆ centre, which does not tell u^c from d^c up to sign"; mod 5,
    five-ality (down = leptons); mod 4, the Pati–Salam pairing.
  - B1703's law: "d = e iff N ∣ 4 or N ∣ 5; u = d iff N ∣ 6; u = e iff N ∣ 2 or N ∣ 7 … every order from 8 on splits all
    three sectors".
- **So, without a line, splitting all three takes the reading with c ≠ 1, per-sector sections, or unequal metrics H.**
