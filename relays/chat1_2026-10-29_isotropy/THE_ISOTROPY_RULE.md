**The claim under test (grammar level).** A once-punctured-torus bundle (GM4's carrier), orientable, with monodromy M
in SL(2, ℤ), can be the space of a locally isotropic universe exactly when M is hyperbolic (GM3). "Can be" means its
interior admits a complete Riemannian metric of constant curvature.

**The classical theorems relied on, cited not proved:**
- Thurston's hyperbolisation of fibred 3-manifolds, and Jørgensen for punctured-torus bundles: hyperbolic M gives a
  complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric.
- In a torsion-free Kleinian group (any complete hyperbolic 3-manifold), the centraliser of a non-trivial element is
  abelian. So a non-abelian group with a non-trivial centre is not one.
- A rank-2 abelian subgroup of the fundamental group of a complete hyperbolic manifold homeomorphic to the interior of
  a compact M with torus boundary is conjugate into a boundary subgroup.
- Bieberbach: a complete flat manifold has a virtually abelian fundamental group.
- A complete spherical manifold has a finite fundamental group.
- A closed hyperbolic 3-manifold's group has no ℤ².
