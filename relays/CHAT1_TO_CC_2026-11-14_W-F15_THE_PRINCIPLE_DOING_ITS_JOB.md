**The principle, read in the chain.**
- "Existence is what survives cancellation" has a counterpart in the frame: the index I(V) = n(V) − n(V*), what
  survives pairing against the dual.
- The chain shows the principle at work in four places filed as inputs (W-F10: chosen, conditional, irreducible, or
  once conditional), and silent in one.

**E1. The scar is the principle; the common point is where it completes.**
- In the free product of the distinctions the scar [x, y] satisfies no relation. On Fricke's cubic (line 3) it has
  infinite order everywhere off the origin: a cancellation that never completes. This is W-F5's, by hand, computed
  once on 2,000 points.
- At the origin, the common point, the cancellation completes in two steps: [x, y]² = I. There:
  - nothing grows, since every move fixes the point (the record's W2; W-C8 part 4, line 3);
  - nothing computed there alone tells the puncture from an even cone point, or, in PSL(2, ℂ), from the closed torus.
  - What survives is the hand: s₁s₂s₃ = ±iI, by the triple's order (W-F1, A2).
- So the puncture is not a separate choice. W-F5 derived it from line 1, and from line 3 off the common point (by hand,
  not independently checked).

**E2. The even-tick condition is the principle, granted P-846.** At the odd ticks computed, the class index cancels,
and what does not cancel is the double cover's:
- the class index is zero on every doublet computed: 51,506 doublets at golden levels 1, 3, 5 and 7 (W-C8 part 2a,
  W-F12). That is computed on those levels and not proved;
- the characters' h¹ gaps do not vanish ("h¹ gaps exist", W-C8 part 2a). They come in equal and opposite counts, |T|
  of each sign (W-F6: by hand, characters of finite order, characteristic 0). W-F4 shows those characters are the
  double cover's;
- every class at an odd tick is the double cover's, sorted differently (Shapiro, W-F4: by hand, p odd, not
  independently checked).

At the even ticks computed, individual modules fail to cancel: I = ±1 on 4 + 4, 36 + 36 and 244 + 244 record modules
at golden levels 4, 6 and 8 (W-C8 part 2a, W-F9).
- By the principle, and granted P-846's condition that counting respects covers (which W-F4 leaves a choice), an odd
  tick has no existence of its own. That is the even-tick condition GENESIS asks a weight to deliver.
- On that reading, and granted P-846, SE2 is not chosen: the non-orientable member carries nothing that is not its
  double cover's. W-F10 types SE2 as conditional on exactly this.

**E3. SE1 is the principle with one further premise.**
- The premise: the start carries nothing that survives (GENESIS: "the root is the emptiest state"). It is not the
  principle, and on the root it is SE1 in the frame's terms (W-C8 part 5).
- With it, existence appears only along the history. Among the metallic acts only the golden root is torsion-free:
  - its class-index frame is empty (levels 1 to 3; F-HE and F-AP not computed);
  - bronze's root already carries 56 record modules, 16 of them with I = ±1.
- So "SE1: chosen" reads as the principle, with that premise, applied to the root.
- The principle does not pick golden among all acts: W-F11's five-distinction root (one history, H₅) is torsion-free
  too.

**E4. The order and the naming of the sides are the principle's relativity.**
- Duality pairs the record's modules (V* at orientable levels, V* ⊗ w at the others) and negates the index (W-F9). So
  every duality-closed census sums to zero; that follows from the definition of I and is not separate evidence.
- What carries content is that individual modules fail to cancel. Which member of a pair bears the name is the side
  one stands on.
- No symmetry of the manifold fixes it (W-F9). No construction from the charges alone does either, given M25's
  premise (W-F14).
- Read this way, it is not a missing input but the frame of reference of "survives". W-F9 types it irreducible. W-F14
  calls it a mere convention only if nothing else in the frame sees which sector reads S, which is main's to judge.
- The same would hold for S against S̃, and so for P-847's global bit, if W-F13's criterion 3 holds (hypercharge
  assigned through the matter modules; a hypothesis for main to test).
- "Matter" here is the frame's label for the degree-1 side (W-F9); nothing is said about η_B or its sign.
