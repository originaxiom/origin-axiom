## 1. What can be said without the overlap

1. **The global flip is a symmetry of every charge-based construction.**
   - z ↦ z⁻¹ is an automorphism of U(1). It is the only non-trivial continuous one, though the argument needs only
     that it is one.
   - Applied to every field at once, matter and Higgs together, it reverses every hypercharge. A coupling that is
     gauge-invariant (hypercharge 0) stays gauge-invariant, so the set of allowed couplings is unchanged.
   - Making the Higgs's twist part of the overlap, if the result is gauge-invariant, is therefore invariant under the
     flip too.
   - Non-abelian factors do not change this: the flip acts on U(1)_Y only, and S and S̃ share their SU(2) action
     (W-F13 § 1).
2. **So including the Higgs's twist cannot decide the global bit by the charges alone.**
   - **Read as charges,** M25's two surviving assignments are exchanged by this flip. It sends the matter's charges to
     their negatives, which M25 states leaves the frame unchanged. It sends the Higgs's charge to that of the other
     reading (W-F13 § 1: S and S̃ carry opposite hypercharge).
   - **As fields,** the flip leaves S as S. The exchange also needs S ↔ S̃, which is complex conjugation of the
     coefficients, and which the charges do not see.
   - Built from the charges alone, then, "the reading is relative, one global bit left" holds. The conclusion is
     conditional on M25's premise. If the frame tied the matter's hypercharge sign to S's (for example through
     W-F13's criterion 3), the charges would decide the bit.
   - This is the expected outcome of main's proposed computation, if the overlap uses only the charges. It is a
     prediction by hand, for main to test. The frame's overlap is built from modular forms of τ, so it is not purely
     charge-based. This settles only the charge bookkeeping.
3. **What could fix it is a structure that S ↔ S̃ does not preserve.**
   - In the frame, S is holomorphic in τ and S̃ antiholomorphic (M24). The flip itself does not touch τ. The step
     S ↔ S̃ that completes the exchange does, and it exchanges the holomorphic reading with the antiholomorphic one.
   - So the bit is fixed inside the mathematics only if the frame requires a named sector's reading to be holomorphic,
     or adopts another principle that tells S from S̃ absolutely.
   - Requiring every sector's reading to be holomorphic would not choose between the two assignments. It would exclude
     both (W-F13 § 2, the structural remark).
   - Otherwise the frame does not fix it. It is then a free datum in W-F13 § 3's sense (W-F9's order bit, if W-F13's
     criterion 3 holds). It is a mere convention only if nothing else in the frame sees which sector reads S, and that
     is for main to judge.
   - W-F13's question to main stands: is analyticity (B1927's principle) a line of the page?
4. **The overlap construction itself** stays with main, or with a seat without this firewall: it is Yukawa-overlap work.
