## The entries

### E1 — GENESIS FK12 (i): at which step is the order dropped?
- **What is computed:** the instrument lead L241 (b) asks for, built and run step by step.
  - Define a based module: a module V that is a non-split extension 0 → A → V → B → 0, kept with its marking (which
    piece is the sub).
  - Define its based count: the class count of B1438's slope law, read on one term of the two, by the marking.
  - For each word w, and for its reverse, carry the based count along the steps: word → manifold → module → vacuum →
    the read-out on the whole.
  - A step drops the order when the based counts of w and its reverse agree after the step and differed before it.
  - Then the audit lane's criterion, re-derived on main: a reduction keeps the register exactly when both the updates
    and the declared outputs descend. It is applied to each step, and the two answers are compared.
- **On which object:** two parts.
  - Word → manifold is about the weave: every word to length 12, the 8 190 words and their 758 states.
  - Module → vacuum is about threads: B1459's 188 complete doubly parabolic points on 16 levels, and B1455's family on
    m004.
  - The read-out step is about the whole, at level 5, as in B1881 and B1895.
- **Outcomes:**
  - The based count survives the module step and vanishes at the vacuum step → the order is dropped exactly where the
    record says. The register is a marking that no class invariant keeps, so it must come from outside the state, or
    from a relation (E3).
  - The based count survives to the read-out on the whole → the order is kept by the state at the read-out. The
    register is part of the state at that level, and B1895's four marks that do not respect reversal are its trace.
  - The based count of w and its reverse already agree at the module step → the order is dropped at the manifold
    alone. Drop 2 is empty, and B1455's reading needs a new statement.
- **Rests on:**
  - B1456 (the reversal identity);
  - B1438 (the slope law; one term per order);
  - B1455, which holds under R76's hypothesis that minima are reductive;
  - B1459 and B1465;
  - B1297 (the involution reverses the order with a sign);
  - B1881 and B1895, for the read-out.
  - **Chosen, not derived:** the marking is an input to the instrument. The test asks whether a step's output depends
    on the marking, never whether the marking is there, so the answer is not assumed.
- **On record:**
  - Drop 1 is decided: a word and its reverse are one manifold (B1456, all 8 190 words; the identity is algebraic).
    Cited here, not redone.
  - Drop 2 is proved on one family (B1455, a thread) and for a class of modules at complete points (B1459; B1465 for a
    meridian twist).
  - The instrument of L241 (b) was never built; P-654 corrected a label only.
  - The audit lane's criterion is read on main and not re-derived.
  - The read-out results of B1881 and B1895 are not tied to the fork.
- **Can it run now:** yes. The populations exist on main, and the instrument is new code over B1459's points. Nothing
  waits.
- **Firewall:** data-free.

### E2 — GENESIS FK12 (ii): is the choice binary or richer?
- **What is computed:** two things.
  - **The count's values.** At every point of E1's thread population, and at the four silver members of B1486, take
    the flat configurations near the split point: the two orders, side by side, and every fused module in the mixed
    direction. Compute the count of each. The question is whether any count lies outside {−1, 0, +1} times the unit.
  - **The bits' independence.** Apply the convention test of B1750 (P-374) to the three binary signs the record names:
    the mirror bit c, the exchange of particle and antiparticle, and the order. For each, compute whether the joint
    action's symmetry carries every option to every other. Then compute the rank over 𝔽₂ of the three bits as
    functions on the states: rank 3 is three bits; rank below 3 means one is the others' product, which is B1327's
    over-count.
- **On which object:**
  - The count's values are about threads: B1459's points and B1486's members.
  - The convention test and the rank are about the weave, since the moves act on all word states.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Every count is in {−1, 0, +1} and the rank is 3 → the choice is binary, three times over, and independent. B1466's
    "the count is a bit" extends to the population.
  - Some count lies outside {−1, 0, +1} → the choice is richer than a bit on that population, and B1466's reach is
    single.
  - Every count is in {−1, 0, +1} but the rank is below 3 → the bits are fewer than the record counts. The order is
    identified with c or with the exchange, and B1327's over-count holds.
- **Rests on:**
  - B1466 and its addendum: the irreducibility certificate is numerical at 60 digits, and R83's third point stands;
  - B1486;
  - B1750's convention test, with P-374;
  - B1327 and its addendum (the bit of its § 8 is not the mirror; B1248);
  - B868, B871 and B599, for the bits at the group layer.
- **On record:**
  - At the counted point (B1466) and at the four silver members (B1486), the configurations are richer than a bit, and
    the count is a bit. Both are single objects; cited, not redone.
  - The sign of a word state needs the move −I (B1482, general).
  - P-374, the recount of the typed bits under the convention test, is QUEUED; no arc applies the test to the order.
- **Can it run now:** yes, data-free.

### E3 — GENESIS FK12 (iii): both outcomes at once, or at different relations?

  - A relation supplies the order when P's count depends on Q's order, while P alone sums to zero over its two orders.
  - **And the converse, the proof that none can:** compute whether the symmetry that fixes the count's sign (the
    inversion followed by dualising, B1455 and B1459) commutes with the map. If it commutes with every map of the
    population, the joined count also sums to zero, and no partner on that population supplies the order.

    landed.

    question there.

  - Neither: no dependence is found, but the symmetry does not commute with some map → this population does not decide.

### E6 — GENESIS FK8: the join, which maps carry physical data between states
- **What is computed:** filling descent, the one candidate the foundation names beyond covers, tested in three steps.

     index is not an index (B1604), compare the interior ranks instead, as B1604 defines them.
  3. **The action, the domain and the anomaly.** Compute whether the physical lane's operator, with its complete domain
     on M, restricts to a self-adjoint operator on M(s). Then account for the anomalies of the spectrum carried

  while B1858 types the fork as a weave.
- **Outcomes:**
