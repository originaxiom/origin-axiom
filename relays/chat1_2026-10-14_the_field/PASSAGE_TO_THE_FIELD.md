## The two passages the record already has

Both passages run on one engine: an impossibility, then the refusal to add what no reason supplies.

## The passage to the field, in five steps

1. **What every thread shares is forced.** Every move fixes exactly one point of the fibre's characters: the common
   point a ↦ i, b ↦ j (W2). Every odd thread receives the same 2T there (W3). Shared by all, it is law: the symmetry.
2. **The shared point is the only still point.** On the surface the principle's remainder defines — κ = −2, the cusp —
   the common point is the one unitary point (B1235; the SM seat's W28 I5; checked again here). It is also the
   surface's node (W24). Unitary means nothing in it has left the compact, symmetric locus.
3. **Every thread has moved.** Each thread's own geometry is another point of the same surface: a genuine cusp, with
   κ = −2 and a puncture that is parabolic rather than central. This difference is not optional. If the threads did
   not differ from the common point there would be no threads, only the point. The weave would collapse back into one
   thing that nobody chose.
4. **Moving while keeping the remainder cannot be done unitarily.** The common point is the only unitary point at
   κ = −2 (step 2), so a departure that keeps the remainder must leave the unitary locus. On a surface with a complex
   structure, that part has a name. Under the non-abelian Hodge correspondence a flat connection is a unitary
   connection plus Φ + Φ*, and the non-unitary part Φ + Φ* is carried by Hitchin's Higgs field Φ. Φ is the adjoint
   scalar that a gauge field leaves behind when dimensions are reduced, the kind of field gauge–Higgs unification calls
   the Higgs.
5. **So the field cannot not exist.** The threads cannot coincide with the common point (step 3), and the remainder
   cannot change (κ stays at the cusp). What carries the difference is a Higgs field. Plurality forces it to exist.
   The remainder forces its form.

**In one line:** the impossibility of nothing forces the seed; the plurality of the threads forces a departure; the
conservation of the remainder makes the departure a Higgs field.

## What the remainder does to the field

On the Higgs side, keeping κ at the cusp exactly means keeping the field's value at the puncture nilpotent (O3,
Simpson's correspondence). In the record's own coordinates that is the isotropic cone a·a = 0 (O2). B1645 found that
every hierarchy needs a Higgs on or near that cone, and B1646 found that nothing on the record drives it there. On the
cone the coupling has rank one: one heavy generation and two massless, at leading order (O4). Read this way, the cone
the record has been looking for (P-115; the SM seat's W63–W69) is the shadow of the principle's own invariant.

One thing the record already had turns out to fit. The residue triplet R makes the first generation massless (B1644,
B1645). It is the value at the puncture of the one Higgs field that is nilpotent at every point of the fibre (O6).
Jacobi's identity is the puncture's half of that statement.

## What the principle does not fix: the field's state

- **Where on the cone.** A null direction is a point of a curve, and τ parametrises the same curve (O7).
- **How large.** The size of the Higgs, which is the scale, stays outside, as B151 and P010 found.
- **How far off the cone.** This is the puncture's opening κ + 2: the cusp widening into a short closed geodesic, or a
  weight at the puncture (O5). It is where the second generation's mass would live. The record's P012 already says
  that closing is where symmetry breaks.

Forced to exist, forced in form, free in state: that is what a field is.

## Against the record's earlier stance

P010 and S037 placed Φ on the Hodge side of the object and called its scale external (B151, B167). S037 judged Φ
"something more primitive" than the Higgs boson. This note keeps the scale external. Two things have changed:

- **The setting.** The weave now supplies a shared point and many threads, and the departure between them is Φ. P010
  spoke about one object, where "a selected point" was absent.
- **The Higgs.** The Higgs that B1644 couples to the matter turns out to be Φ's value at the puncture (O1). So the
  "primitive" field and the record's Higgs are one object at the puncture, given the hypothesis H-RES (relay §3).

The seed and the field also meet in the owner's terms of 2026-10-13: "a field of maximality compared to the seed of
minimality" (the SM seat's §64). The seed sits at the node: the minimal, symmetric, unitary point. The field ranges
over the whole surface of departures, every thread's geometry included. They touch at the node.
