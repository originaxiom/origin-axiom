## What went wrong in how we looked

All day the field was treated as a thing to find inside the seed's mathematics: a vector in M, a condensate at the end.
Both exist, and the condensate test showed they are different things. Neither is the field in the owner's sense. In
the owner's words the field is the seed's *counterpart*: "a field of maximality compared to the seed of minimality, a
sea of potential where anything is allowed". The SM seat read that sentence statistically (§64, W57). Here it is read
literally, and in this programme the literal reading is exact mathematics.

## 1. The seed is minimal, so its field is maximal

- **The seed** is two letters with no relation between them: the free group on a and b (GENESIS §2). A description
  cannot be smaller and still not cancel. **[MATH + the record]**
- **A realisation of the seed** assigns to a and b two actual transformations. Because the seed has no relations,
  *every* pair is allowed. Up to conjugation the realisations are exactly the points (x, y, z) = (tr a, tr b, tr ab) of
  all of ℂ³, with no constraint at all (Fricke–Vogt). **[MATH]**
- **So minimal syntax gives maximal semantics.** The least possible description has the largest possible space of
  things it can be. "A sea where anything is allowed" is not a metaphor here. It is the character variety of the seed.
  **[READING of the owner's words; the space is MATH]**

## 2. Why the field cannot not exist

- PF1 says existence is what remains when the cancellation cannot complete. A remainder that is never realised would
  still describe nothing. To *be*, the remainder must be realised, and its realisations form the field. **[READING]**
- **Seed and field are one thing seen twice:** what is said, and everything it can be. Neither comes first. The field
  is not added to the seed. It is the seed's own space of being. **[READING]**
- PF1's own invariant, κ = tr[a,b], is a function on the field. The seed's realisations where κ = 2, where the
  cancellation completes, are the ones PF1 removes. **[MATH: κ = 2 is the reducible locus, where a and b share an
  eigenvector; for unitary realisations, where they commute]**

## 3. The seed acts on its field, and the remainder is conserved

- The seed's moves, L and R, act on the field by polynomial maps, the trace maps. **They preserve κ.** The commutator
  goes to a conjugate of itself or of its inverse (Nielsen), so the remainder is the conserved quantity of the seed's
  action. **[MATH]**
- The field therefore splits into shells of fixed κ, and the seed moves every realisation around inside its shell.
  Goldman: on the compact (unitary) part of each shell with −2 < κ < 2 the action is ergodic; at κ = −2 that part is a
  single point, the common point. **[MATH]**
- **PF2 says the remainder is "a description that cannot be exhausted".** Its counterpart here: the seed never stops
  moving its field, and on the unitary shells it visits everything. **[READING]**

## 4. Only one realisation stands still, and it has no extension

- Exactly two realisations are fixed by every move: (2, 2, 2), where the cancellation completes (removed by PF1), and
  the common point (0, 0, 0), a ↦ i, b ↦ j. **[MATH, checked here]**
- **The common point has a finite holonomy group, the quaternion group Q₈.** A finite group of isometries of hyperbolic
  space has a fixed point (Cartan). So the "space" the common point builds collapses to a single point: it has no
  extension. **[MATH]**
- **Every thread has moved, and its geometry is extended.** A thread's realisation is fixed by its own word, not by
  every move: a periodic point of the seed's action. Its holonomy is non-unitary and acts freely on hyperbolic space,
  building a whole 3-dimensional hyperbolic space with a cusp. **[MATH]**
- **In three dimensions that geometry is a gravitational field.** A flat PSL(2,ℂ) connection is a solution of Euclidean
  3D gravity with negative cosmological constant (Witten 1988), with A = ω + i e:
  - its unitary part ω is the spin connection;
  - its non-unitary part e is the dreibein, the metric.

  The record already reads the object's native gauge system this way (B715: "exactly what it should be as 3d Euclidean
  quantum gravity"). **[MATH; B715]**
- **On the fibre the same thing has Hitchin's name.** The Higgs field Φ of the uniformising Higgs bundle is the frame of
  the surface's hyperbolic metric (Hitchin 1987). **[MATH]**

## 5. So the first field that cannot not exist is space

- **The plurality of threads forbids the collapse.** If every thread sat at the common point there would be one
  realisation and no weave.
- **The remainder forces the non-collapsed realisations to be non-unitary.** On κ = −2 the common point is the only
  unitary point (C2).
- **And non-unitary means extended.** It has a metric. **[READING built on the MATH above]**

**In one line:** the seed is what is said; the field is everything it can be; the only thing that can stand still is
the shared point, and it is a point with no room in it; to differ from it, a thread must have extension, and extension
is a metric. The first field is space itself.

## 6. What this changes

- **The record has had the field all along.** Every thread's geometry (traces, holonomies, volumes, Chern–Simons
  classes) is a stationary configuration of the field under that thread's own path. **[READING]**
- **What is missing is not the field's existence but its locality.** **[READING; the facts are MATH]**
  - A physical field varies from place to place, and its waves are particles.
  - Here each thread is one rigid space (Mostow), and Euclidean 3D gravity has no local degrees of freedom, no waves.
  - The record found the same absence by other routes: P010's "fields / second quantization: absent"; B715's "no
    dynamics".
  - So the sharp form of "we lack the field" is: **we lack local degrees of freedom**, something that lets the
    realisation vary from point to point of a space.
- **The particle-physics fields come at the second level.** They are fields *on* space: the condensate at the end
