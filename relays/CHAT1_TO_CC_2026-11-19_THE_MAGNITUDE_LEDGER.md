## 4. The routes out, and what each must supply

**(a) An extremum.**
- **What it needs:** a functional of τ that the weave fixes, with a minimum at finite height.
- **[E] What the record has:**
  - The invariant functionals computed have their minima at isotropy points (ingredients map § 0 item 3; B1684:
    critical only at i and ω).
  - A content holds τ off the cusp only if its total cusp coefficient is negative, and every content on record is
    positive (B1704).
- **[J; arithmetic] The form that would work:**
  - Two terms with exponents E₁ > E₂ and opposite signs, V = A ε^E₁ − B ε^E₂ with A, B > 0, have one minimum, at
    ε*^(E₁−E₂) = B E₂ / (A E₁).
  - When E₁ − E₂ = 1/10, the coefficients' ratio is raised to the tenth power.
  - This is the racetrack form (Krasnikov 1987; Casas, Lalak, Muñoz and Ross 1990). It meets B1704's condition in
    form: its leading term at the cusp, −B ε^E₂, is negative.
  - The exponents of such a functional need not be the table's. The table only shows that exponents of the frame's own
    kind can differ by 1/10.
  - It needs two coefficients that the weave forces. Nothing on record supplies them.

**(b) Arithmetic.**
- **What it needs:** a rule from the programme's own arithmetic that picks τ among the CM points with no free integer.
- **[E; standard]** At a CM point of discriminant D < 0 with reduced form (a, b, c), τ = (−b + √D)/(2a). So
  Im τ = √|D|/(2a), and the scale becomes a pair of integers.
- **[E] The record:**
  - In the holomorphic flux frame, LR-invariant fluxes force isolated quartic CM points in ℚ(√5, √−d), chosen by flux
    integers (B1804).
  - That is an infinite menu nothing on record selects (B1809 (S2)). FR-FIB's values at CM points are not computed
    (B1809 § 7).
  - The group wall (B990, B1327, B1881): an orbit invariant cannot pick a point of its own orbit. So a selection must
    pick orbits by arithmetic.
- **[E; this page's enumeration] One condition gives a finite list.**
  - The condition: the torus has an endomorphism of degree 5, that is, an element of norm 5 in its order.
  - The norms of the order of discriminant D are the values of x² + bxy + cy², with b ∈ {0, 1} and c = (b − D)/4. When
    y ≠ 0 this is at least (c − ¼)y², so c ≤ 5. When y = 0 it is x², which is never 5.
  - That leaves D ∈ {−4, −11, −16, −19, −20}, six classes in all.
  - Im τ is 1, √11/2, 2, √19/2, and √5 or √5/2 (D = −20 has two classes). So ε runs from 4.3e-2 down to 8.9e-4.
  - Every reduced representative has Re τ ∈ {0, ±1/2}, on the mirror lines. The frame's configurations are images of
    these under the order-5 structure and need not lie there.
  - `chat1_2026-11-19_ledger/norm5.py` checks this to |D| = 20000.
- **[J] What the card must still settle, before any output is printed:**
  - whether this condition is the programme's own, or one more choice;
  - how many configurations each class gives once the order-5 structure is kept. The reduced representatives are not
    the frame's configurations;
  - whether the frame is in its exponent regime at these heights. W-F25 fitted from height 2 upward, so at Im τ ≤ √5
    the outputs must be evaluated directly.

**(c) A count.**
- [J; W-P1d's run, the rate not derived] Under the length clock, the height averaged over the fibre grows like
  (3/π) log X. If the frame keeps falling above height 6, log G_read ≈ const − 3Ē log X, and ε is about X^(−3) (W-P1d).
- [E] X is a choice (W-P1d), and the clock stays CHOSEN (P-439; B1815). So the count moves the open number from ε to X.
- [J] A count fixes the scale only if the process supplies its own stopping point.

**(d) One input.**
- [J] Take ε from one ratio. The other five are then fixed up to their prefactors, which are definite numbers once
  Re τ and the counted choices are fixed. Their scale-free content is § 2's ratios of exponents.
- The menu of exponent vectors is 16 without the charge rule. Under the charge rule, with the leptons read as the down
  sector, it is 4: two Higgs worlds, (S̃, S, S) and (S, S̃, S̃), times mark or twin. A sealed test would declare that
  menu as options (W-F25 § 2).
- [E] It reaches the six mass ratios only. The mixing is § 0 item 4's wall.
