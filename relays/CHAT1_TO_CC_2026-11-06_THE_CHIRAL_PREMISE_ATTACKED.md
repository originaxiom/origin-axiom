## 1. The forms used, and which k is which

**Dimofte–Gukov–Lenells–Zagier (DGLZ), arXiv:0903.2472** (arXiv numbering; named, not re-derived):
- the action (1.1), with t and t̄ on the two halves;
- "if we write t = k + σ and t̄ = k − σ, then consistency of the quantum theory requires the 'level' k to be an integer,
  k ∈ ℤ, whereas unitarity requires σ to be either real, σ ∈ ℝ, or purely imaginary, σ ∈ iℝ";
- the perturbative factorization Z^{(ρ)}(M) = Z^{(ρ)}(M; t)Z^{(ρ)}(M; t̄) (1.5);
- ħ = 2πi/t (1.7);
- q = e^{2ħ} ((2.42); footnote 19: "Note that in our notations e^{2ħ} = q = e^{2πi/N}");
- the geometric S₀ = (i/2)[Vol + i·CS] at u = 0 ((2.53));
- the colored Jones invariant at ħ = iπ/N, normalized by the solid torus ((4.4));
- footnote 1, for a compact level: "ħ = iπ/(k + h∨)".

**Dimofte–Garoufalidis, arXiv:1202.6268:**
- the Kashaev invariant ⟨K⟩_N ~ Z_M(2πi/N), with ħ = 2πi/N ((1.2), (1.3));
- S_{M,0} = i(Vol + i·CS) (the bullet after their (1.3); their (1.4) is the torsion relation);
- q = e^ħ ((5.22)).

**Two units, a factor 2 apart (U1, U4).**
- In DGLZ's, k ∈ ℤ, and a block of Kashaev order N has coupling t = 2N: q's order is t/2.
- The first check (Q1–Q8) used Dimofte–Garoufalidis's S₀. Its units make every coupling half of DGLZ's, so there
  DGLZ's integrality reads 2k ∈ ℤ.
- B1012 § 2's I = σ·Vol/π is the modulus in those halved units (Q1). The gravity dictionary in § 4 is in them too.

**The product's classical exponent.** In the action (1.1) the antiholomorphic half is the conjugate connection's, so its
classical value is the conjugate S̄₀:
- E = (t/2πi)·S₀ + (t̄/2πi)·S̄₀;
- in DGLZ's units this is σ·Vol/2π + i·k·CS/2π (U2).

**Which k is which.**
- **DGLZ's integer level** k = (t + t̄)/2, which is Witten's ℓ in t = ℓ + is, t̃ = ℓ − is (arXiv:1001.2933 (2.4)).
- **S305w's compact level** in ħ = 2π/(k + h∨). Witten's (2.28) gives the compact level as t/2 − h, and notes "there is
  no such shift for a complex Lie group". So q's order is t/2 (U5).
- **§ 4's gravitational Chern–Simons coupling,** which is DGLZ's k in the record's halved units.
- σ is TERMINOLOGY's first sense, the gravitational level.

**A reading note on B1012's display.** B1012 writes t = k + iσ, which is Witten's form, with s for σ.
- Witten: "This makes sense straightforwardly only if s is real, for then I is real and the integrand exp(iI) of the
  path integral is bounded".
- With σ real in that form, the leading term is a phase on every manifold (Q2).
- Witten's introduction: "Euclidean quantum gravity is obtained by analytic continuation to imaginary values of the
  coupling parameter s".
- So the record's use of σ, as the coupling whose product with Vol is the modulus, is DGLZ's real σ.

## 2. What the checks show

**(b) The hand acts on the couplings as k → −k, with σ fixed (Q3).**

**(c) "One hand" is one value of σ (Q4, U3).**
- The product's classical exponent equals one factor's exactly when the other coupling vanishes: t̄ = 0, i.e. σ = k,
  where t = 2k. The mirror point is t = 0, i.e. σ = −k.
- So, inside the theory, the chiral reading as main words it ("one hand") is not a second theory. It is σ pinned to
  ±k: the pin of σ by the integer level that this seat's R2/R4 note flagged.
- **Witten places a singularity at that point.**

  - There, "one should expect singularities at s = ±iℓ, where the action (2.3) becomes holomorphic or antiholomorphic",
    and the continuation may need "the points at s = ±iℓ (and ∞) omitted" (arXiv:1001.2933 § 2.4).
  - His footnote 1: "the singularity at s = ±iℓ that we will discuss in section 2.4 is at least a cousin of the 'chiral
    point' of three-dimensional gravity", citing Li–Song–Strominger.
- **So the product equals one factor at t̄ = 0 in its classical exponent only.** The partner's ħ̄ = 2πi/t̄ diverges
  there.

**(d) The one-hand point exists at every order (U1, U3).**

**(f) The knot reading: one factor without the pin.**
- **The setting.** Witten, arXiv:1001.2933 § 2.6.1 ("Knots Again"), takes the complex-group theory with the knot's
  Wilson operator in a holomorphic representation. The action of the compact group on an irreducible representation
  "can be extended to an action of G = H_ℂ in two ways; in one case the matrix elements of the representations are
  holomorphic functions on G and in the other case they are antiholomorphic". For a holomorphic one, "the trace of the
  holonomy around K is a function only of 𝒜 and not of 𝒜̃".
- **The S³ step.** "as S³ is simply-connected, this means that up to a gauge transformation we can take 𝒜̃ = 0". So
  "the integral over 𝒜̃ does not depend on the knot K. So the general form of Z_G(K; t, t̃) is

- **Under the knot reading, R1 fixes the read factor's exponent with no pin of σ.** Witten identifies B(K; t) with the

## 5. Verdict

**Not derived, but relocated.**
- **The premise is not where the ruling put it.**
  - The hand's sign is a c-odd bit.
  - That c is broken is met by the theory at k ≠ 0.
  - The premise is the c-even statement that one factor is read.
- **At the order main evaluated, inside the SL(2,ℂ) theory, one factor is read in one of three ways:**
  - **(i-0) the one-hand point.** σ is pinned to k, the pin of B980's kind, at a point where Witten expects the
    continued theory to be singular. The partner's classical exponent vanishes there, but its ħ̄ = 2πi/t̄ diverges.
  - **(i-a) the knot 4₁ in S³ in a holomorphic representation** (Witten's (2.30)).
    - The partner is present and does not depend on the knot.
    - The unknot-normalized invariant is main's quantity (in perturbation theory; exactly with Witten's choice of
      cycle). B̃(t̃) cancels, and it depends on k and σ only through t.
    - So R1 fixes its exponent with no pin, away from the singular point. The volume pairs there with t/2, not σ;
      σ = t − k waits on the integer level, and B1012 § 2's dictionary takes no σ from R2 unless k is fixed too.
    - It needs the S³ filling, which is unique for m004 (Gordon–Luecke), and a holomorphic representation of
      dimension t/2.
    - It is unavailable on the excursion members.
  - **(i-b) P-725's two sectors.**
- **So the premise is one of these readings, rather than the manifold's two factors at free σ.** For the quantity main
  evaluated, (i-a) needs no pin and avoids the singular point, but it gives no σ. If R2 is to feed B1012 § 2's
  dictionary, (i-a) needs k as well, and (i-0) pins σ to k. (i-a) replaces "one factor is read" by "the object as the
  knot in S³"; which factor is still the hand's sign (§ 3).
- **Outside the SL(2,ℂ) theory, the continued compact block carries no σ.**
