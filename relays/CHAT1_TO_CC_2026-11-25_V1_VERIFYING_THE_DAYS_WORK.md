## 6. Riddles for main

1. [J] **What the whole selects carries no values by itself.** The whole selects the common point (B1988) and, with
   integrality, ω (B1993): both are its points of most symmetry. B1988 says the common point carries no values and
   values can live only in the flow away from it. At ω the record found no 2–3 hierarchy (B1691), and agy's C59 finds
   |S₁| = |S₂| = |S₃| there, as ω's stabiliser would force under the covariance of § 4 (not checked here). So hierarchy
   needs a departure from the selected point, and what fixes the departure is the old height hole in a new place. The
   listening line has one exact number of flow, λ*, on one chosen walk (riddle 2), and main reads no rate as physical.
   The bottleneck: is there a forced map from the whole's flow to a departure from ω, or does the departure need an
   input?
2. [J] **λ* belongs to a chosen walk.** W+ = P_L + P_R (no inverses, unweighted) is one walk on the moves. W+ with
   inverses is another, with a different spectrum (B1986 reports both), and adding the swap P or weights changes it
   again. The grammar gives the moves; on record it does not give the walk. λ* is the whole's number only if the walk is
   forced. This is the one-hole pattern again, unless GENESIS fixes the walk.
3. [J] **The act is a symmetry of the pair, not of a fixed τ.** By § 4, frame C at (τ, w) equals frame C at (Mτ, w M⁻¹).
   Frame C sees w only mod 30, so the act would be a symmetry at a fixed τ in H only through some γ ≡ ±M^k (mod 30)
   with γτ = τ, an elliptic γ. By C8 no M^k (k < 60) has trace 0 or ±1 mod 30, so there is none. (M itself fixes only
   the golden points (1 ± √5)/2 on the real line.) So no τ in H has the act relating readings at the same τ.
   - On order 30's 96 points of B1976 the act's orbits have twelve points, so such an orbit read at fixed τ is one
     point seen from twelve cusps, as agy-002 says. At order 10 the orbits have 6 or 30 points, and the other order-30
     points lie in orbits of 60. Inside H only the images of i and ω have fixed-τ symmetries (S, and the order-3 move
     of B1994).
   - For this seat: W-LV1 § 8 named the act's orbits as the object's own grouping. That holds for the pair (τ, w), not
     at fixed τ. W-LV2 as owed (per-orbit rates at fixed τ at orders 10 and 30) would average one point over several
     heights, a forced aggregation, and is withdrawn in that form.
   - The lepton–down question at fixed τ then reduces to the content's charges and to τ's stabiliser. By hand
     (unsealed): at ω the order-6 lift of the order-3 move has characteristic polynomial x² − x + 1, with no root mod
     5, so 3w is never in the ±orbit of w when w's 5-part is nonzero. At i, S has eigenvalues 2 and 3 mod 5. Post hoc,
     of the 72 points of order 10 exactly 8 have 3w an S-image of w (up to sign): 2-part (1, 1) and 5-part on either
     eigenline of S. A re-posed W-LV2 would test what frame C does there, sealed first, at the author's word.
4. [J] **ω returns after its retirement.** B1993 notes the record retired τ = ω (GENESIS v1.35) after B1617 and B1618
   found no hierarchy there, and leaves open what the whole does at ω before any frame is laid on it. agy's C59
   already builds static τ = ω into frame claims. That is early: B1993's ω is the shape of the Fuchsian torus of the
   integer readings; frame C's τ is the modulus of the curve carrying E[30]. Equating them is an identification

5. [J] **A question, unchecked.** The common point is the character of the quaternion group (a = i, b = j: traces 0,
