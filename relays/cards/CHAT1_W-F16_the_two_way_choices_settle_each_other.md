# Card W-F16: the two-way choices settle each other. The bits, the involutions, and the count of what is left free

**Seat:** the web seat (chat1). **Kind:** `result:` (data-free), with a reading. **Committed before any work of it.**
Approved by the author on 2026-11-14 (status file).

## The thesis

- **Two-way choices.** Every choice that survived the foundations is a pair exchanged by an involution of the
  structure, a ℤ/2-torsor.
- **A bit flipped by a symmetry** of everything the structure computes is a convention, not a choice.
- **Two bits flipped together** by one involution have a fixed relative sign: they settle each other.
- **The claim.** On the current page, the number of bits left free after relations and symmetries is zero.

## The bits and the involutions

- **Bits:**
  - the hand h at the mark;
  - the tick parity o;
  - the undoing e;
  - the order r (V against V*);
  - the Higgs reading q (S against S̃);
  - the hypercharge sign y.
- **Involutions:**
  - J, the tick (flips o and h);
  - K, the undoing (flips e and h);
  - D, duality (flips r; W-F9);
  - C, charge conjugation with S ↔ S̃ (flips q and y; W-F13, W-F14);
  - M, the mirror (an orientation-reversing automorphism at even levels).
- **Relations:**
  - h = o + e, the hand law (W-C8 part 1);
  - q = y + const, neutrality (M25, W-F14);
  - in one variant, r = y + const (W-F13's criterion 3, a hypothesis).

## What is computed

- **H1.** The hand law over F₂ for every odd m up to 41.
- **H2.** The mirror is a homomorphism on every record module at golden levels 4, 6 and 8 and bronze level 2, and it
  preserves the index I on every one.
- **H3.** The F₂ count in four variants:
  - with C a symmetry, and without;
  - with criterion 3 and D∘C a symmetry, and without.
