## Why this card

- W-F2 compared the two pure faces of W₃ = ℤ/2 ∗ ℤ/2 ∗ ℤ/2: all mirrors (the ideal triangle) and all half-turns
  (S²(2, 2, 2, ∞)). Only the half-turns let an aperiodic history run. The two mixed faces were left open.
- Each mixed face is a 2-orbifold whose fundamental group is again W₃:
  - two mirrors p, q and a half-turn r: a disc with one cone point and two ideal corners, ⟨p, q⟩ and ⟨q, rpr⟩;
  - one mirror p and two half-turns q, r: a disc with two cone points and one ideal corner, ⟨p, (qr)p(qr)⁻¹⟩.

## The question

For each mixed face and each labelling: which re-descriptions are history steps of the face, and do any of them act
hyperbolically on the records' H₁? A history step sends mirrors to conjugates of mirrors and half-turns to conjugates of
half-turns, and sends the face's corners to its corners. The tick is hyperbolic (line 5 of W-C8 part 4's page).
