## 3. How it could come out wrong, written before any run

1. **The five words may not divide the arcs.** One arc can be a theorem in one part and an estimate in another. The
   field may have to name the weakest support the claim line rests on, or be a list. The card does not decide it;
   the fourteen will show it.
2. **"Theorem" cannot be held to a proof.** What the commit holds is a lock, and a lock is a test. The gate can hold
   the word only to this: a lock is named, it is in the commit, and an assert in it can fail. Whether the lock proves
   the claim is not a gate's. The note must say so in the gate's own sentence.
3. **A grade is a judgement, and this seat gives no verdict on main's arcs.** The proposal for the fourteen states
   only what was read mechanically (a lock that can fail, a script and its committed output, sealed cells and how
   many held) and what word that would allow. Main decides each.
4. **"The arcs the candidate runs on" needs a definition that is a function of the commit.** Main's script for it is
   in an arc's own folder. Until WP-0.12 builds the graph, the fourteen may have to be held by name as found, and the
   gate must then say that the list is typed.
5. **PROVED with a grade of process, or of numerical estimate, is the audit's M1.** The gate does not forbid it: the
   meaning of the verdict word is main's and the owner's. It prints it.
6. **The schema's lock is main's test.** If the field is checked there, the change carries a dated note; if it makes
   that test longer than it should be, the check stands in a lock of its own.
7. **A generated page that prints by grade changes many pages at once.** The views are regenerated by main at
   landing; the patch must leave every view gate as it found it.
8. **Two lists must not be taken for one.** The five words say what kind of support an arc has. The nine labels of
   the protocol's § 12 say how a result was checked. The field's name and the gate's sentence must keep them apart.

## 4. What it rests on, and what it must not touch

- It rests on main's head with series 2, 3 and 4 of WP-0.7 as they land. If main changes one, it is rebuilt.
- It changes no sealed file and no file that a hash list names.
- It changes a verdict file only by adding the field, and only in the proposal's own patch.
- It changes none of main's tests without a dated note in the test.
- It writes to this seat's branch only.

## 5. One control that must fail if the work is broken

A verdict file of the candidate's chain with its grade taken out must turn the gate red. The suite's five controls
must be caught on the end of the series, and each new case must be not caught on the commit before its mend.

## 6. What is needed from main, or from another seat

Nothing to start. From main, when the series arrives: its word on what evidence each of the five words asks, and on
the fourteen grades, row by row. From the two sessions that M5 says will send specifications of checks: nothing is
waited for; what arrives is read against this card and the next.
