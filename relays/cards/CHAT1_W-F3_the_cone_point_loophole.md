## 1. The question

W-F1 and W-F2 left one choice of the distinction layer open by the opposites test: the puncture against a cone point of
order k ≥ 3. A cone point closes the fibre, and so closes the 3-dimensional object. The agy-exp lane asks a question.
By duality on a closed space (Selberg's lemma gives a closed manifold cover), is h¹(V) = h¹(V*) forced for every finite
k? Then a non-zero gap, a chiral count, would need the puncture, k = ∞.

- **The groups.** It takes W-C8 part 2a's sealed helpers by text and adds the relation λᵏ = 1 for k = 2 to 6. It
  compares these with k = ∞ on the tick's levels 2 to 6, both orientable and non-orientable.
- **The modules.** Every character, and every non-split module that exists.

## 3. How it could come out wrong

- The cone relation changes which classes exist. The script reports that, along with the gaps.
- A prime field that divides k would fake classes. The prime is checked to be prime to every k.
