## The two frames (P-Q1's D1 and D2, M29's two constructions)

- **Frame C.**
  - Matter carries its charge q as a twist of the fibre: its kernel's theta characteristic is q times the line.
  - The Higgs carries its charge only as the choice of S or S̃ = εS*εᵀ (complex conjugation).
- **D1.** Every field, the Higgs included, carries its charge as a twist. Charge conjugation acts by negating the
  characteristic: N: θ[a, b](u, T) ↦ θ[−a, −b](u, T).
- **D2.** Every field carries its charge as complex conjugation, like the Higgs. Charge conjugation acts by K: f ↦ f̄.

## What is checked (expectations in the script's header, sealed before the run)

1. **The two conjugations, on the frame's own theta functions** (this seat's implementation of B1656's formula):
   - N keeps holomorphy, since θ[−a, −b](u, T) = θ[a, b](−u, T);
   - K is the mirror, since the complex conjugate of θ[a, b](u, T) is θ[a, −b](−ū, −T̄).
2. **S's level-5 class.** The triplet R that builds S (B1691: R = (−θ₂², −θ₄², iθ₃²)/(θ₂θ₃θ₄), and S from its Serre
   derivative) transforms under τ ↦ τ + 1 by a matrix whose eigenvalues are eighth roots of unity. So S carries no
   level-5 class of its own (W-F20's R2, made exact).
3. **D1.**
   - Every object is holomorphic, both Higgs charges included.
   - The up Higgs has class 1 (odd) and matter is even. So the holomorphic objects meet both parity classes, and
     B1936's premise is false in D1.
   - The hypercharge sign acts by u ↦ −u, the fibre's own involution.
4. **D2.**
   - Holomorphy flips with charge conjugation. Matter's frame fields are even, and their conjugates are odd.
   - The premise holds exactly when the holomorphic Higgs has class 4, and charge then gives W₋. If it has class 1, the
     world is W₊. So in D2 the premise is P-847's global bit restated.
5. **Whether D1 and D2 agree.** They represent charge conjugation by N and by K, which differ by the mirror (1). So they
   agree object by object only where the mirror acts trivially. Whether their read-outs agree is a question about
   values, which is main's and agy-exp's.

## What would refute this card's reading

- A construction of D1 in which some field's charge-conjugate is not holomorphic.
- A level-5 class for S coming from its forms (2).
- A definition of D2 under which the frame's matter fields are not all of one parity.
