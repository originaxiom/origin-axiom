## 3. How it could come out wrong, written before any run

1. **A residue is discharged in words and not in fact,** or the reverse. Each is decided by a quotation at the file that
   decides it, not by a search for the name. Where the record does not decide, the residue is CARRIED, not discharged.
2. **A verdict changed from OPEN reads as a result.** These are process arcs: a closed one says that its residues are
   discharged, and states no theorem. The verdict line keeps its words under a dated note.
3. **Seven pages are main's.** Nothing of their statements is reworded; notes are added beside them.
4. **The residues the register row names may not be all of them.** Each arc's page and verdict line are read in full for
   what they leave owed.
5. **A touch of an arc's folder resets its age.** The marker, not the touch, is what keeps it from going stale; the lock
   holds the marker.
