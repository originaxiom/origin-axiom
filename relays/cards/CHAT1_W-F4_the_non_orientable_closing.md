# Card W-F4: the non-orientable closing, and the odd tick's matter as the double cover's

**Seat:** the web seat (chat1). **Kind:** `result:` (data-free). **Committed before any work of it.**
**Goal it serves:** the foundations' goal (status file, ruling of 2026-11-14): each input of the candidate system ends

## Why this card

- The agy lane's relay of 2026-11-14, § 3, reads two ways for chirality to survive a closing: an open cusp, or a
  non-orientable closing. agy-exp's card X-SM exhibits a closed non-orientable manifold (N₃ × S¹) with h¹(V) ≠ h¹(V*).
- Main's B1926 addendum (P-846, main's reading) reads the twisted classes at odd ticks as the double cover's matter.
- W-F3 tested the cone point with the twist only. The untwisted gap on closed odd-level objects was not tested.
- If the non-orientable closing carries the record's matter on the tick's own family, the puncture loses its
  strengthening from W-F3. If it carries none, the puncture is strengthened further.

## The questions

1. **Transfer.** At odd level n, with π the orientation double cover (level 2n), does h^i(G_2n; π*χ) =
   h^i(G_n; χ) + h^i(G_n; χ ⊗ w) hold, and the same for the interior classes?
2. **Main's B1926 addendum, a second computation.** Does every fibre-non-trivial character χ with χ(μ) = −1 at odd n
   pull back to a level-2n character trivial on that level's meridian, with a class non-zero on the scar?
3. **The whole accounting.** At level 2n, is every piece of the record's data (a fibre-non-trivial ψ, trivial on
   μ_2n, with a class non-zero on λ) seen at level n, either as a twisted character's class or as a class of the
   induced module Ind ψ (Shapiro)?
4. **The non-orientable closing of the tick's own family.** On the closed objects at odd levels (the fibre's puncture
   filled, λ = 1, or replaced by a cone point of order k = 2 to 6): is there any character, non-split abelian
   doublet or induced module with h¹(V) ≠ h¹(V*)?
5. **A control.** On agy-exp's N₃ × S¹ example, does this seat's code reproduce the non-zero untwisted gap?
