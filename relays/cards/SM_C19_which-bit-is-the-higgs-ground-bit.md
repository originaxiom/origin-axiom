# SM-C19 — which bit is frame D1's Higgs ground bit: the lift, the parity, or the hand?

*Card of the SM seat, 2026-11-18, following SM-C18. Committed and pushed before any check is run. Data-free; no coupling
or mass value. It uses main's own B1957 instrument (`checks/level6_higgs.py`, `checks/cim_overlap.py`), imported
unchanged.*

## 0. What the record already says (read before writing)

- B1957 (H6) averages the Higgs-mode bubble over the four lifts of each point. The result is a multiple of the identity.
- So M_H² = K − c·B is K up to a constant, with K = (θ/L)² on the eigenvectors of U_M, the six modes' transport along
  the golden geodesic.
- Its ground space is the two modes with eigenphases ±π/6: "one bit".
- **That bit is the sign of U_M's eigenphase. It is not the lift itself:** the lift group {1, Z³, X³, Z³X³} has
  already been averaged away.
- SM-C18 found the lift acting on the coupling by diagonal signs on matter, and by k → k + 3 on the Higgs modes.

## 1. The questions, and what is derived before any check

- **B1, the lift.** Does an element of the lift group map the +π/6 eigenvector into the −π/6 eigenspace?
  - Derived: the lift group is generated by Z³ and X³, of order two. The transport U_M is built from T₆ and S₆.
  - Z³ commutes with T₆, and X³ with S₆ up to the relation of the Weil representation. So the lift group normalises
    the image of SL(2, ℤ). Whether it centralises U_M decides B1.
  - Expected: the lift commutes with U_M and preserves each eigenspace, so it is not the ground bit.
- **B2, the parity.** Does the parity P: k → −k mod 6 (the Weil representation's ρ(−I)) exchange the two eigenspaces?
  - Derived: ρ(−I) is central in the image of SL(2, ℤ), so it commutes with U_M and preserves each eigenspace.
  - Expected: not the ground bit.
- **B3, the hand.** Does complex conjugation, composed with a unitary on the modes, exchange them? That is, is conj(U_M)
  conjugate to U_M by a unitary that sends the +π/6 eigenvector to the −π/6 one?
  - Derived: conj(U_M) has eigenphases −θ. U_M's spectrum is symmetric under θ → −θ (H1:
    −5/6, −1/3, −1/6, 1/6, 1/3, 5/6). So the antiunitary "reverse the hand" exchanges the two ground modes.
  - Expected: **the ground bit is the hand.**
- **B4, frame C's reading.** Frame C's switch S → S̃ acts on the generations high in the cusp as a permutation of order
  3 (B1946). The lift acts on matter by diagonal signs of order 2 (SM-C18). No identification can carry an order-3
  action to an order-2 one, so the reading switch is not the lift bit. This is derived, and it is not computed here.

## 2. What is computed

- U_M as B1957 builds it, its eigenvectors, and the transport's own direction: B1957's H3 is rerun to see whether U_M
  or conj U_M is the modes' transport.
- **For B1:** the commutator of each lift element with U_M, and the overlap of each lift element's image of the
  +π/6 eigenvector with the −π/6 eigenspace.
- **For B2:** the same with P.
- **For B3:** the unitary A with A·conj(U_M)·A⁻¹ = U_M, if one exists among the record's operators (P, the lift
  elements, T₆, S₆ and their products), and the overlap of A·conj(v₊) with v₋.

## 3. Expectations, written before any check

| | expectation | prior |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | the lift commutes with U_M and preserves the ±π/6 eigenspaces: not the ground bit | 60% |
| B2 | the parity preserves them: not the ground bit | 80% |
| B3 | an antiunitary built from the record's operators exchanges them: the ground bit is the hand | 65% |

## 4. What it means

- **If B3 holds and B1 and B2 hold:** frame D1's one undetermined Higgs bit is the hand, the orientation. That is the
  same bit the record already carries as the tick's orientation (B1926) and as the hand's invariant (SM-C9, B1929).
  It would then be set wherever the hand is set, not chosen separately.
- **If B1 fails:** the bit is the lift's, the double cover of SM-C18.
- Neither outcome says which sign is realised.
