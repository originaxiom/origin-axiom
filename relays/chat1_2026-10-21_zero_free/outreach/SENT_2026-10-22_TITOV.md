## The text as sent

Dear Dr Titov,

I'm an independent researcher based in Prishtina, and I've been working through your 2025 paper with Ferruccio Feruglio, Antonio Marrone and Alessandro Strumia (arXiv:2505.20395). I'd be grateful for your view on two questions it raised for me.

I've been studying a modular flavour setup on my own. It is non-supersymmetric and at tree level, the three quark generations sit in one metaplectic triplet built from θ₂², θ₃², θ₄², CP is imposed, and there is a Dedekind-η counterterm. In that setup I ran into the same condition you state in Section 2.1: θ̄ is constant over the whole upper half-plane exactly when no quark becomes massless at any interior τ.

There is one difference from your setup, and it is what my first question is about. The triplet has determinant character det ρ = χ_η⁹, and none of the quark assignments that fit the data has vectorial hands. So their zero-free determinant comes out as c·η^(6K) with 24 not dividing 6K (K = 11, 11, 10 and 14), rather than as a power of Δ.

I also tried the condition on the leptons, at the values of τ the quark fits pick out, with the test written down before it was run. Nothing fitted better than chance. A two-page note with the details is here:
https://github.com/originaxiom/origin-axiom/blob/ce071fae0/relays/chat1_2026-10-21_zero_free/outreach/Mehmetaj_zero_free_note.pdf

My two questions:

1. In the notation of your eq. (24), the counterterm in my setup has k_f3 = −3K, so it carries η's multiplier, which your footnote 4 sets aside. Do you know whether a threshold term like that, with its multiplier matched by phases in the matter transformations, can actually arise in a string completion? Or does consistency force the weight of det(Y_u Y_d) to be a multiple of 12?

2. In Section 2.1 you describe the zero-free condition as an assumption, "perhaps realised in some string compactifications". For a single modulus τ with the full SL(2,Z), do you know of any argument that rules out an interior zero of det Y, or is that still open?
