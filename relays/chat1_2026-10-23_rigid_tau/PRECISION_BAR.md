# The precision bar, fixed before any further ingredient is built

chat1, 2026-10-23. **A standing commitment, committed before the tests it governs.** At the owner's "lets do it
properly and informed". 0 of 19.

## Why

- Main's coarse measure (the worst factor over the ratios) with its random-as-good calibration asks whether a model's
  pattern of hierarchies is non-generic. It does not ask whether the values are right.
- At TICK_LINE_HIGGS's best nine-row, where all nine are within a factor 2.043 and only 0.11% of random targets do as
  well, the values are far off: in the measurements' own errors m_μ/m_τ is off by 280σ, m_e/m_τ by 567σ, |V_us| by
  236σ, m_c/m_t by 19σ and m_d/m_b by 9σ; with a 10% allowance alone the χ² over the nine is 178
  (`relays/chat1_2026-10-23_tick_line/precision_check.py`, post hoc).
- Main's own sealed arcs carry a χ² cell for this reason (B1698's H3, B1720's T3: the minimum χ² below 16.75), and every
  one has failed.

## The bar

- **χ²_six**, main's definition (B1698 and B1720 `compare.py`): over the six ratios m_c/m_t, m_u/m_t, m_s/m_b, m_d/m_b,
  m_μ/m_τ, m_e/m_τ, χ² = Σ (ln model − ln measured)² / σ², with σ² = (the ratio's relative error from the pinned
  Huang–Zhou 2021 file)² + 0.10² (B1637's 10% allowance). The bar: the minimum over a model's rows below **16.75**.
- **χ²_nine**: the same with |V_us|, |V_cb| and |V_ub| added, their relative errors from the pinned PDG file, each with
  the same 0.10 in quadrature. The bar: the minimum over a model's rows below **21.67** (the 99% point for nine degrees
  of freedom).
- No fitted parameter is subtracted from the degrees of freedom: a model's continuous parameters are counted by
  reporting them beside the cell.

## The commitment

- Every sealed test chat1 writes from now on carries these two cells, with priors, beside the coarse cells.
- No ingredient is described as reaching the values unless a sealed test finds it below both bars on rows nobody had
  computed. Meeting the coarse cells alone is described as a pattern match.
