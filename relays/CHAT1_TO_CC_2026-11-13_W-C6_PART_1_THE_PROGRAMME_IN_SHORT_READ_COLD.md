## 1. The findings

1. **Lines 3–4, kind 3 (a claim wider than its source).** The page: "checked line by line by three readers". The source,
   `docs/THE_PROGRAMME_SO_FAR.md` line 874: "Three readers then checked it in three parts: 158, 133 and 332 sentences and
   table rows against their sources". Each line had one reader, not three. The page inherits this from the source's own header
   (lines 3–4: "three readers checked every sentence and table row against its source"), which Appendix B makes
   precise. Suggested: "checked line by line, in three parts, by three readers".
2. **Lines 17–18, kind 3.** The page states the grammar as fact: "A description is a pair of whole-number counts. Two
   elementary moves, L and R, each add one count to the other". The source it names, § 2 (lines 137–138), adds: "Each
   rule of the grammar has a status: most are assumed, one was adopted by a ruling, one was chosen, one stays open
   (GENESIS §2)." Line 12 of the page marks the principle as a postulate. Nothing on the page marks the grammar as assumed.
3. **Lines 19–21, kind 3.** The page: "Each word acts on a torus with one point removed and builds a three-dimensional
   space of constant negative curvature. Each such space is called a thread". The sources say "Every allowed word"
   (§ 3, line 179) and "A **thread** is one object the principle allows" (`docs/THE_WEAVE.md` line 13). § 3.1 (line 189)
   restricts states to primitive words "using both letters". § 2 itself (lines 136–137) says "each word", so the page
   partly follows its source there. Suggested: "Each allowed word".
4. **Lines 22–23, kind 3, as an omission.** The page: "One thread is picked out by two stated selectors, one assumed and
   one chosen". That restates § 3.3's own plain-words summary (lines 237 and 240). The same section says the root needs four inputs (lines 249–251): "Given GM3, GM4, SE1 and SE2, the root is
   m004 … Each of the four inputs is needed". It also records (lines 258–260) that the object preceded the axioms, and
   that the genesis is "a conditional reconstruction of a chosen sector". The page leaves out GM3 and GM4 and both
   caveats.
5. **Lines 39–41, kinds 3 and 7 (a label stronger than its evidence).** The page: "only with inputs chosen by hand".
   The source, § 7 (line 519): "only after choosing several inputs by hand or by looking at the data". The page drops
   "or by looking at the data". It also drops § 7's sentence on the quark mixing (lines 521–522): "The quark mixing is
   either missed by the rigid versions of the frame or fitted by the free ones without being specific." The page's list
   of what is not reached ("the mass scales, the gauge couplings, the Higgs sector or θ") then leaves the mixing
   unmentioned. The page does not say that the mixing is not reached.
6. **Lines 29–30, kind 3; minor.** The page: "three generations of chiral matter are not". The source, § 5.1
   (line 443): "three *gauge* generations of chiral matter are not". It also records the owner's question answered as
   "the gauge three is a selection" (line 451). The page drops "gauge" and "selection". The sentence stays true, but it
   loses the distinction the source draws.
