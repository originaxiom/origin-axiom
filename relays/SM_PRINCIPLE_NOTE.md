## The answer

**The open end.** We have carried it as a premise of the grammar: the puncture, the carrier axiom, and GENESIS FK14's
open question whether the genesis makes ends. Read through the principle, it is not a premise. It is the principle
doing its job.
- On any closed orientable object, twisted duality makes every sector cancel exactly: h¹(V) = h¹(V*). That is a
  theorem, checked on 46 618 fillings.
- So if existence is what survives cancellation, a world with anything in it must have an end.
- The surviving amount is a quantity of the end alone: I = h⁰(∂M; V) − dim A_V. On all 46 618 modules it equals the
  record's index.

**The scar.** The end is not arbitrary. Do a, do b, undo both: the history does not close, since [a, b] ≠ 1. At the
one point every act fixes, its phase is −1. The puncture is where that smallest non-cancelling history sits, and the
remainder lives on its torus.

**The realised sign.** We have recorded the sign of the remainder as a free bit, supplied by a partner if at all.
- Every such binary in the chain comes as two members that cancel exactly: V against V*, the partner's two lines, the
  act against its undoing, 3 against 3̄.
- A world that realised both members of every pair would contain nothing. So existence does not leave the sign free.
  It requires one member to be realised wherever anything survives, and it leaves only the global relabelling, the
  mirror of everything at once.
- SM-C8 shows the mechanism. The relation through an open end carries the order (100 846 leaking joins), and its two
  orders cancel in ± pairs. The relation is where the sign lives, and existence is why one is realised.

**How many signs.** One per independent pairing left unbroken.
- At determinant +1 there are two: E2 found the hand and the order independent.
- At an odd tick, duality pairs V with V* ⊗ w, so order and orientation fuse (SM-C10; the web seat's W-C8; main's
  proof in M25).
- Read this way, the arrow, the hand and the remainder are not three inputs. They are what a remainder needs in order
  to survive cancellation:
  - an end, through which the count can leave its pairing;
  - an orientation-odd invariant (η_A = −L_odd(1, χ_c)/π², SM-C9, post hoc);
  - a direction of the act, under which that invariant flips.

**What it does not explain.** m003's companion carrying the three, the golden act and the mark's line stay
selections. Cancellation does not pick them.
