## 2. What opens

1. **One hypothesis becomes a finite read-out:** the root, with the cells computed. FK9, the selection, stays open. §3's
   Stage 1b reads the result off directly.
2. **The first generation has a candidate mechanism; not every link is computed.**
   - Rank-2 cells give m₁ = 0 at leading order: the (−½, 7/2) cell under every completion, and the (3/2, 7/2) cell
     under E4·F. That is B1649's zero, in the thread frame.
   - **Main's B1654** lifts the zero by the boundary class's end condition, at a point on the fibre:
     - under the canonical end condition (P4), m₁/m₃ ≈ 0.15/L in the bulk;
     - under C0's "one normalisation serves all three" (P4′), the zero stays at every τ;
     - which end condition is physical is open (B1654: "the parent's clauses 4 and 6").
   - **Carrying B1654's lift into the thread frame's cells is not computed.** Where R is a matter mode, the τ-integral
     alone cannot lift the zero (B1653's dated note). B1654's lift changes the Higgs's triplet, from R to u with
     u·u = D, and that could.
   - **Main's next arc** gives each sector its own D through the twisted channels. "The ratios of their first-generation
     lifts do not depend on L. They are the first place this frame could give a structural number" (B1654).
3. **The down quarks and the charged leptons can be split, if their weights differ** (the parent's clause 7). Sectors in
   different cells get different hierarchies, with no E₆ tie. But the assignment of weights to sectors is free, not
   forced. Main's twisted channels bear on the same clause.
4. **CP: where it can come from, if the vacuum is the root.**
   - On the root, step 3a's stored couplings are real in all three cells: K to 2.2·10⁻¹⁸ and Y to 6.7·10⁻¹⁶. Elsewhere
     Y's imaginary part reaches 1.2·10⁻⁵ on some reflection-symmetric threads, and its origin is not shown.
   - So CP violation needs a source beyond those couplings: the phase of the hypercharge line in the twisted channels,
     or the lift (B1654's D is complex at generic τ, 0.9956 − 0.0289i in |R|² units at one point).
   - **Strong CP is an open question, not a result.**
     - For a rank-3 assignment with real matrices, arg det(M_u M_d) ∈ {0, π}.
     - In rank-2 cells det = 0 at leading order, so θ̄ is set by the lift's phase.
     - Linking the bit to θ̄ needs I-18, which is unearned.
5. **Lepton mixing is open again.** The first row's gap (nature's 0.675) is reached by four routes:
   - couplings at different fixed points;
   - two moduli;
   - the one-leg Reading-B placement (a fit, with free parameters);
   - T-NORM.
6. **The scale might be quantised. These are pointers, not results.**
   - The audit found that "the weave does not fix the hierarchy" was computed with k and σ independent. The locked case
     is untested.
   - Frameworks where the levels lock exist: chiral gravity (c_L = 0, c_R = 24k), and Witten's conjectured
     holomorphically factorised gravity (c = 24k).
   - At c = 24 the classical root shares are 56%, 71% and 83%, with no one-loop.
