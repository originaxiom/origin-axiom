# Answers of the cold reader (P-653)

Read: p653_questions.md, GENESIS_masked.txt (all 833 lines), README_masked.txt. Line numbers below are GENESIS's unless marked README.

**1. The principle and its status.**
The principle is GENESIS §1's single wording (lines 85–88): existence is what remains when cancelling to nothing cannot complete (PF1); that remainder is a description that cannot be exhausted (PF2); the programme reads that description as mathematical structure and asks what it forces (PF3). These are "three faces of one commitment, not three principles" (96–97). It is **assumed**: PF1, PF2 and PF3 are each POSTULATED (92–94). PF2 does not follow from PF1 by any derivation in the record (98–99), and PF1's mathematical form (non-cancellation as the commutator trace κ = tr[a, b] ≠ 2) is "a reading, not a derivation" (104–111). The owner confirmed the *wording* (FK1, CONFIRMED; lines 40, 85, 478), and that confirmation does not make it derived. README line 10 says it outright: the principle "is a postulate: it is stated, not derived". The owner's statements of what he means (113–123) count as wording and settle no status.

**2. The root, its criterion, and the further choice.**
The root is m004, the figure-eight knot complement: the once-punctured-torus bundle of LR (README 7–8; GENESIS 220, 224). The criterion is **SE1: the root's first homology is torsion-free**. Its status is **POSTULATED** (219). It is motivated by topology, and its alternatives are kept: minimal trace would select ±LR (m004 and m003), and minimal volume would select m000. What SE1 admits is the derived theorem T-ROOT (DERIVED, 220): exactly two classes, LR (m004) and the golden matrix LP (the Gieseking manifold m000). The choice between them is **SE2: take the orientable one, m004, the orientation double cover of m000**. SE2 is **CHOSEN** (221) and is called "the most fragile link of the chain" (fork FK2, 479). Minimality points the other way, since m000 is the smaller manifold. What the choice costs has been computed (B1234). What dropping it would break has not. Since v1.65, SE2 is also a "named condition to derive": the orientable root would be what an observer at one member of a mirror pair would see. GENESIS states that this is not derived and is not counted as derived, so the status stays CHOSEN and the input budget keeps its bit. The root needs four inputs (GM3, GM4, SE1, SE2), and each of them is needed (224–233). Reading SE1 as "the least remainder" links it to PF1, but that is "an interpretation, not a derivation" (261).

**4. A state, and the census to length 12.**
A state is a signed cyclic word s = (ε, w), where w is a primitive word in L and R using both letters, taken up to rotation and the L↔R swap, and ε = ±1 (§3, 156–157). It is realised as the once-punctured-torus bundle with monodromy εw, a hyperbolic 3-manifold with one cusp (157–158). The census is DERIVED (161–163): **758 states** at word lengths 2 to 12, realising **536 distinct manifolds**. The two numbers differ because a word and its reverse are two states realised by one manifold (158). 222 states pair off this way, and the first such pair occurs at length 7. Levels (cyclic covers) and signed even powers −uᵏ are neither of them states, and the census counts states only (169–184).

**5. A frame, and whether any is derived.**
A frame is a dictionary from geometry to particles: it turns flat bundles on a state into particle counts (§5, 272–274). F-MC is of a different kind, going from the root's arithmetic to a gauge algebra (275–276). Five frames are named: F-FC, F-CI, F-MC, F-HE and F-AP (280–284). They are thread instruments, each built on one thread's holonomy (48). **No frame is derived from §1–§4.** The transport of any frame to physics (identification I-26) is UNEARNED, and each frame is "a named hypothesis" (274–275). GAP1 repeats this: no frame is derived, and until one is, a count is mathematics about a flat bundle (349). The v1.60 route for earning a dictionary by prediction leaves that sentence standing (350–351, 377–379). FK11 has the dictionary UNEARNED and unearnable by derivation within F-HE and F-CI on any thread or cover (488). Results are statements about a frame applied to an object, never about the architecture as such, and the frames disagree on the same objects (286–289). §7 lists "that any frame is physics" as not claimed (345).

**7. The six gaps (§7, 348–451).**
- **GAP1, the dictionary:** no frame is derived (349).
- **GAP2, the ends:** chiral counts live at cusps and depend on an end condition that is chosen, not derived (388).
- **GAP3, the source:** chirality sits on non-split bundles, which need a source to carry a harmonic metric. A count needs an order of the pieces, an open end, and a source or flux of the right sign (397–411).
- **GAP4, selection and coincidence:** with many states, frames, levels and end conditions, a Standard-Model-like feature is expected somewhere by chance. A selection rule and a null model must be fixed first, and the selection rule (FK9) is still open (412–418).
- **GAP5, dynamics:** topology yields integers and discrete structure. Values need moduli, symmetry breaking and running, and nothing has yet been turned into a potential, a breaking or a running that fixes a value (419–425).
- **GAP6, the flatness:** every frame is a frame of flat bundles (426).

The heading was corrected from "five" to six at v1.15. GENESIS says none of the gaps can be closed by work on one state (348).

**8. What flat frames can and cannot reach.**
GAP6 (426–451) answers this. Through the boundary, a flat frame reaches **discrete data**: counts, bits and signs, such as the boundary's η term, the class index, and the mirror's action on the spin structure. It **cannot reach continuous moduli**: a mass, or a coupling that the construction leaves free (432–433). On a closed bulk, no characteristic-class index can tell a representation from its conjugate, because a flat bundle has ch = rk (426–428). A rigid flat point does fix continuous numbers exactly, for example m004's volume, but these are numbers of a member, and none is a Standard-Model parameter (435–438). On an open end a flat count can tell a module from its dual, but it cannot see the hand, because the class index is mirror-even for every module (448). The record's value-negatives therefore bound the frame, not physics (433–434). The remedy is a frame with curvature, which would have to sit on a bulk of dimension ≡ 2 (mod 4); none is on the record (442–451, 510–511).

**9. Earning a dictionary, and the grade it gives.**
This is GAP1's v1.60 rule (the owner's ruling of 2026-11-03, amended at v1.62 and v1.64; lines 350–379). An identification is **EARNED BY PREDICTION within a frame F** when all seven conditions hold:
1. **Fixed before:** F, every discrete choice, every fitted input and the identification itself are on a sealed frame card before any predicted value is compared.
2. **Held out:** each predicted quantity is on the held-out list, no function of it was used to build, choose or tune F, and its prior looks are counted.
3. **Passed, graded jointly:** each measured value lies inside its prediction's stated band, and a joint p-value against a matched null, corrected for every recorded prior look across seats, falls below the threshold.
4. **Two at least:** two or more held-out quantities that are not functions of one another pass jointly on the same card. The claim reads "jointly confirmed, each within its band".
5. **Verified:** main recomputes the result with its own code, and a hostile referee and a third opinion find no fault.
6. **Scoped and revocable:** the status reads "EARNED BY PREDICTION within F, by arc X", and it reverts to UNEARNED if a later sealed prediction is graded NEGATIVE.
7. **Worth spending:** the card's power is computed beforehand from the null alone. If it is too low, the targets stay unspent, and each target is spent only once.

A value matched through an identification earned this way is graded **PREDICTED, not a READING**, and counts on the board as predicted (375–376). The values used to build F stay FITTED or CHOSEN and count as inputs. DERIVED (from §1–§4, with no frame choice) remains a separate and higher grade: this route earns a frame's *use* of a dictionary and does not derive the dictionary (376–379). Under FK9's two-level bar, a screen pass alone gives only a CANDIDATE, and values count as PREDICTED only after this confirmation (486).

**10. PF5 and its status.**
PF5 was added at v1.65 (line 129): **the claim that the whole has one self-consistent outcome**. "Self-consistent" means the loop closes: what is read from the whole is itself among what takes part, and reading it again returns it. "One outcome" means one orbit of the whole. It does not mean one history picked, nor a point the symmetries happen to fix. Its status is **OPEN**. It is what the programme sets out to establish, and it is not a face of the principle; it is neither assumed nor derived. Nothing in GENESIS is derived from it, and no result yet returns to fix an input. It would be refuted by two distinct self-consistent outcomes of the joint action, exhibited, or by a proof that the allowed histories admit no read-out that closes. Neither symmetry nor "the greatest" can supply the outcome. What is left is a weight on the histories, and that is open (FK9). §7 repeats that the claim is open (342).

**11. Thread result against weave result.**
These are defined in §0, line 48. A *thread* is one object the principle allows (m000, m003, m004, +LLLR, …): one closed path of the moves through the shared fibre, the two records. *The weave* is the joint action of every allowed move on the records, and everything that action forces.
- A **weave result** takes every thread under a rule stated in advance, with none hand-picked. It is defined by the joint action and makes a claim about all threads at once.
- A **thread result** is anything read on one thread or its covers. It is labelled as such (the `weave_or_thread` field in verdict files) and is never presented as the answer to a weave question.

Within GENESIS, §1–§2 are weave statements, SE1, SE2 and T-ROOT are thread statements, and the frames are thread instruments. So the root's selection and every frame count are thread results.

**12. The statuses, and who sets them.**
The five statuses are listed in §0, lines 50–55. Each item has an ID and one of them:
- **DERIVED:** proved, with the proof or check cited.
- **CONDITIONAL:** proved given named premises.
- **POSTULATED:** assumed.
- **CHOSEN:** a fork settled by choice, with the alternative kept.
- **OPEN:** not settled.

Who sets a status is fixed by the working rule of 2026-11-11 (56–63). The owner rules on a goal, a standard, an order of work and what he means, and **no status is set by a ruling on a fact**. An item the mathematics could decide is OPEN, with both branches carried and the deciding computation named. An item nothing can decide is POSTULATED or CHOSEN as main's entry: it is tagged, counted in the input budget, everything built on it says "given" it, and the owner may strike or replace it. Only an arc may change the file. That arc names each item it touches, its old and new status and the evidence, and adds a line to the version log (36–38). On statuses GENESIS overrides every other document, and GOVERNANCE.md delegates the statuses to it (29–34).
