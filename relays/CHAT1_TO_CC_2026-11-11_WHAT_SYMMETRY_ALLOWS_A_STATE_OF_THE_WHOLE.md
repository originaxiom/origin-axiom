## 4. What the quotient's symmetries force

**The statement.** Let A be a finite group of symmetries of the quotient, and F a C¹ function on it that every
element of A leaves unchanged. Then:
1. the set where F is greatest is carried to itself by A;
2. F is stationary at every isolated fixed point of an orientation-preserving element of A, and along the fixed
   line of a reflection its gradient is tangent to the line;
3. so F is greatest at exactly one point only if every element of A fixes that point.

*Proof.* (1) is immediate and gives (3). (2) is § 3 of 4f7af67b6 with the symmetry in place of the elliptic element:
a rotation other than the identity fixes no nonzero linear form, and a reflection fixes only those along its line. ∎

**Which F it covers.** "Built from the quotient alone" means: from the hyperbolic orbifold, forgetting the tori.
- Covered: the density of an eigenspace of an operator the symmetries keep; the zeros and the size of a section
  that is alone in a space the symmetries carry to itself.
- Covered as a pair: the two weight-1 sections. The symmetries exchange their spaces, so only the two together are
  carried to themselves.
- **Not covered:** a function of the torus alone, such as j or the fibre's own determinant. It is a function on the
  quotient, and W does not keep it. Nor a function built from the fibres with their mark, as the record's line
  potentials are; that is § 6 of the inventory, and it needs a content.

**The symmetries at level 5** (classical; R1 to R4 and Q3 to Q6 check them).

| the symmetry | what it does to a torus with its mark | in the coordinate |
|---|---|---|
| **the twin exchange δ** | v goes to 2v | on X±: s ↦ −1/s. On X_line: the identity |
| **the dual involution W** | the torus goes to its quotient by the marked line, marked by the dual line | on X_line: t ↦ 125/t |
| **the mirror** | τ ↦ −τ̄, with a move that restores the mark | t ↦ t̄, s ↦ s̄ |

- Here t = (η(τ)/η(5τ))⁶ in the standard frame. s is the coordinate of X±, the fifth power of the Rogers–Ramanujan
  continued fraction, with t = 1/s − 11 − s (R3). W is an integer matrix of determinant 5 with W² = −5. It is not a
  move. On X± "W" is one of two lifts; which of them is called W depends on the matrix chosen.
- On X_line the symmetries are W, the mirror and their product, and no others (the referee, by computation; it
  agrees with Atkin and Lehner's normaliser). On X± they form a group of order 8, generated by δ, W and the mirror.
- **W has exactly two fixed points on X_line: t = +5√5 and t = −5√5** (Q7, R2). They are the two classes of
  discriminant −20, the tori τ = i√5 and τ = (1 + i√5)/2. At each, the fixing matrix is an endomorphism of the torus
  with square −5 whose kernel on the points of order 5 is the mark. No symmetry exchanges them, and they differ in
  themselves: one class is principal and the other is not. The mirror fixes each.
- **W with the mirror is t ↦ 125/t̄. It fixes the circle ∣t∣² = 125,** which holds both of those points and both
  elliptic points, t = −11 ± 2i (R6). Modulo the mirror the circle is an arc. It is the set of tori whose quotient

## 5. Two rules the symmetries offer, named and not adopted
