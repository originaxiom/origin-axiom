## The claim

1. **The open end is not a choice; it is the principle at work.**
   - Twisted duality cancels every sector exactly on a closed orientable object: h¹(V) = h¹(V*) whenever there are no
     invariants.
   - So a world in which something survives cancellation must have an end. The remainder is a quantity of that end
     alone: I = h⁰(∂M; V) − dim A_V.
   - The record carries the end as a premise of the grammar: the puncture, the carrier axiom, and GENESIS FK14's
     question whether the genesis generates ends. The claim is that existence, read through the principle, forces it.
2. **The scar is the smallest history that does not cancel.** Do a, do b, undo both: [a, b] ≠ 1. At the one point
   every act fixes, its phase is −1. The puncture is where that history closes up, and the remainder lives on its
   torus.
3. **The sign of the remainder is not a free input per bit.**
   - Every binary that the chain records as a choice comes in two members that cancel exactly: matter against
     antimatter (V against V*), the partner's two lines, the act against its undoing, 3 against 3̄.
   - A world that realised both members of every pair would have nothing left. So existence requires exactly one
     member to be realised wherever something survives. Only one global relabelling, the mirror of everything at
     once, stays a convention.
   - How many independent signs remain is the number of independent cancellation pairings left unbroken. That is two
     at determinant +1 (E2: hand and order) and one at an odd tick, where duality fuses order with orientation.
4. **Not claimed.** The record's selections (m003's companion carrying the three, the golden act, the mark's line)
   are not explained by the principle. They stay selections.
