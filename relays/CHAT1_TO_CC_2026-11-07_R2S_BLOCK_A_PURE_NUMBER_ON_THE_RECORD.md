## 1. The answer, stated first

**No, not on the record.** The record holds no object-side reason that R2's block is a ratio of two scales, and its
own sources point the other way. In them a ratio of two lengths is an input to the theory, carried by the level. The
block is the output, a dimensionless function of those inputs. Reading the output as μ*/M_Pl, or as Λ/μ* with the
power 1/b₀, is R2's identification, which P-722's card already counts as chosen.

## 2. Where the record's sources put the scales

- **B151's reading** of Garoufalidis–Thurston–Zickert (arXiv:1111.2828), Dimofte (arXiv:1409.0857) and Córdova–Jafferis
  (arXiv:1305.2891): "In every case the dimensionful/continuous content sits in ℏ↔k and the squashing/lens radius; the
  complex volume / CS invariant enters only as a dimensionless element of ℂ/4π²ℤ in the exponent."
- **The one kind of ratio of two lengths there is the squashing.** B151's Dimofte row puts the units in "the level k
  (↔ ℏ) and the lens / squashing geometry", for `T_n[M]` on squashed lens spaces. Córdova–Jafferis define theirs: ℓ/2
  is "the ratio of the radius of the S² base and the S¹ Hopf fiber". Their abstract: "A squashing parameter in the
  geometry controls the imaginary part of the complex Chern-Simons level."
- **So the ratio is an input, and it enters through the level.** Córdova–Jafferis write the complex level as k + iu.
  "u is not subject to any quantization condition", and the squashing sets it: "depending on whether ℓ is smaller or
  larger than unity, one lands on one or the other unitary branch".
  - Below unity, u is real: the form of B1012's display, Witten's k + iσ with σ real, where the leading term is a phase.
  - Above unity, u is imaginary and the level is real: the branch of DGLZ's real σ, which is the record's σ (B1012's
    note of 2026-11-07: "The two branches are recorded here so they are not read as one"; TERMINOLOGY's σ row).
- **The partition function is computed at that level.** On B151's reading, nothing in the three sources makes its value
  equal to a ratio of scales.

## 3. P-719's mechanism is the form of R2's identification, not a reason for it

- **P-719 sharpened B151:** "for dimensionless RATIOS of scales and for couplings its own reading shows the mechanism —
  Z ∼ exp(i·Vol_ℂ/ℏ) — by which an invariant sets a ratio once the level k ↔ ℏ is fixed; the open quantity is k, which
  the invariant does not fix."
- **That is a statement about the form.** If a ratio of scales is identified with Z, then Z ∼ exp(i·Vol_ℂ/ℏ) is how the
  invariant and the level would set it. The exponential's likeness to dimensional transmutation is what makes the
  identification natural, not what makes it hold.
- **The identification is what R2 adds,** and the record already says so:
  - P-722's design: "R2 the scale … **a reading**: B151 gives the form";
  - R76-7's note: "The block's exact value is a knot invariant, not a scale. Turning it into μ*/M_Pl is the card's
    identification", and among "What remains chosen", "The reading of the block as a ratio of scales (R2's
    identification)".
- **So P-719 shows that the scale clause is not excluded, and it does not supply the reason main asks for.** Its own open
  quantity, k, is R1's; the identification is R2's.

## 4. The block under the adopted reading

- **No σ while the integer level is free.** Under the open-book reading main adopted (P-731), R2's quantity carries no
  σ on either branch unless the integer level k is also fixed, which gives σ = t − k (ca92036ca § 1; f823dd25f: σ is
  "unpinned only while k is free"). So the input that carries a ratio of lengths in Córdova–Jafferis's dictionary is
  not supplied by R2 itself.
- **An exact algebraic integer.** R76-7 fixed R2's form as the exact invariant, "an algebraic integer in ℚ(√5), which is
  the record's own hearing field".
  - It is a quantum invariant at a root of unity. The coloured Jones polynomial lies in ℤ[q^±1], so its values at a
    root of unity are cyclotomic integers.
  - So it is an exact integral value, like a dimension or a trace, not a continuous ratio set by a running.
  - This is an observation about the kind of number, not an exclusion. The record draws the line between exact
    algebraic data and ratios by B650's typing, "not by the object" (B660's note of 2026-11-06; P-719).

- **The first way of removing a step fails.** R2's identification stays CHOSEN. The card stays NOT SEALABLE (P-732), and
  the bar of one stands.
- **What would change it** is a second length the object itself fixes, entering through the level. B1012 § 2's
  dictionary reads the gravitational level σ as a ratio of two lengths (the AdS radius to Newton's constant;
  TERMINOLOGY's σ row). So each of the record's pins of σ would fix such a ratio from the object:

  | B1034's stage pin: the stage's central charge matched to the gravitational one | UNDECIDED (B1034); it would fix ℓ/G through σ, "a ratio" (V3 (c)) |

  None is adopted or derived. (i-b), P-725's two sectors, pins nothing by itself and waits on P-725. And a pin would
  give a ratio as an input, the level; the block would still be the output.
- **Main's other two ways remain:** R4's content, and one object for the base word's scale and the member's τ. On the
  third, a pointer and not a claim: along L⁶R(LR)^k the (LR)^k block's geometry tends to the base word's, that of the
  infinite cyclic cover of the figure-eight knot's complement, and the tube's τ tends to τ_∞, 991d7e2ac's sealed
  extrapolation (no member at k = ∞ is computed).
- **The goal's scale clause** is where P-719 left it: not excluded as a dimensionless ratio, and still waiting on a
  principle that fixes the level (P-719's Q1) and on R2's identification.
