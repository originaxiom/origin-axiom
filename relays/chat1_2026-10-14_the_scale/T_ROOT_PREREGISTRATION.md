# T-ROOT: how much of the action-weighted weave is the root, at c = 6 and as c grows (chat1, 2026-10-14). Sealed before any code that computes it.

**Why.**
- `THE_SCALE.md` §5 states a tension in words. If L154 lands (c = 6), the weave is not dominated by its root and sees
  the level k at order one. If c ≫ 1, the weave concentrates on its amphichiral root.
- The owner said "continue" after chat1 proposed this cheap check to put numbers on that tension.
- The weighting is a READING: the classical saddle weight of Euclidean 3D gravity, e^{−I} with I = c·Vol/6π (B1012's
  closure; B1088). At c of order one, quantum corrections are of order one, so the numbers there are indicators, not
  quantum predictions.
- The word weight is not fixed by the record. Its uniform measure on threads (B1629) is per length, so three weights
  are run.

**Seen first.**
- `the_scale_check.json`: 745 threads to length 12, with Vol and CS.
- From it: the root LR is the unique volume minimum (2.0299), and LLR, LRR come next (2.6667).
- The hand estimates in `THE_SCALE.md` §3 and §5.
- No sum over threads has been computed, at any c.

**Objects and instrument.**
- **Threads.** Lyndon words in L < R of length 2 to 18, generated by Duval's algorithm: 31,040 of them.
  - Bundle: SnapPy 3.3.2 `Manifold('b++' + word)`.
  - Vol = `volume()`. CS = Im `complex_volume()` mod π², in (−π²/2, π²/2].
  - Chiral means |CS| > 1e-6.
- **Word weights.**
  - (A) w = 2^{−n}, where n = |W|;
  - (B) w = 3^{−n};
  - (C) w = 1/(n(n−1)·L(n)), with L(n) the number of threads of length n, so each length carries 1/(n(n−1)) and the
    total is 1.
- **Action factor.** e^{−c·Vol/6π}.
- **Quantities**, on the truncated weave (n ≤ 18):
  - root share p(c) = w(LR)·e^{−c·Vol(LR)/6π}/Z(c);
  - chiral share f(c);
  - effective number of threads N_eff(c) = e^{entropy};
  - the thresholds c_{0.5}, c_{0.9} and c_{0.99} where p crosses each level, found by bisection;
  - per-length sums S_n(c).
- **Tail beyond length 18.** A rigorous upper bound from Guéritaud–Futer.
  - For (A) and (B): Vol ≥ 2k·v₃ for k syllable pairs, and at most C(n−1, 2k−1)/k threads of length n have k syllable
    pairs. Summed to n = 4000, with a geometric remainder.
  - For (C): (1/18)·e^{−c·2v₃/6π}.
- **c grid.** 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 192, 256, 384, 512. B1229's menu σ ∈ {1/3, 1}
  gives c ∈ {2, 6}.
