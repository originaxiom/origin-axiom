### 4.2 On the cone and off it

**[MATH, model; post hoc where marked]**

    (post hoc), whatever the residue's normalisation.

    of order one at the cusp. The exact limit ½ is the model's. (Cited, not re-derived.)
- **Off the cone** (eigenvalues ±λ ≠ 0, a·a ≠ 0): the residue's norm stays |λ|, so the pointwise norm grows like
