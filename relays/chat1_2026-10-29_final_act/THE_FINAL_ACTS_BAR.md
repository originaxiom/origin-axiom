## 2. The order of work, and what is sealed when

  - the priced address lengths (K's framework side);
  - the exclusions, with their proofs or computations;
  - the measure's Im τ marginal;

  The rules for Stage B:
  - Each script is committed before its run.
  - Its outputs are committed and pushed **before any comparison script exists.** They may be read when committed,
    since they are data-free, but nothing in them may change the card.
  - Each CHOSEN item's alternatives are run in the same script: every clock in C4's menu, and each constant at both ends
    of its band.

## 3. The cells and their grades

**S — SUPPORT (P-485 (a)).**
- **S0, reach and resolution** (the owner's refinement: the search must see "all the levels where our math could
  spread"). The card states, for every axis of the declared space, its range and its grid. The axes include:
  - the height, up to the cusp;
  - Re τ;
  - the line orders and offsets;
  - the thread lengths;
  - the rungs of the moves' ladder;
  - the stages of the towers;
  - the CM points, if used (enumerated by discriminant, as P-495 does);
  - the Higgs directions, the contents, the readings and the clocks.
  - **Reach.** Each axis ends either where the framework itself ends, or at a computational cutoff.
    - The framework's own ends: the ladder at N = 5 (W153), the tower chain at its last stage, the height at the cusp
      through the frame's limits there, as B1817 computed them.
    - A computational cutoff is labelled CHOSEN. Beyond it a negative is NOT FOUND, unless an argument covers the axis
      past the cutoff (a proof, or a certified bound on the outputs there).
  - **Resolution, checked at Stage B without data.** Across each grid cell every output changes by less than half of its
    target's 1σ uncertainty (INPUT_COMPLETENESS row 3). The uncertainties are used, never the measured values. This is
    certified by a bound on the outputs' derivatives over the cell, or else checked on the doubled grid, whose values must
    agree within the same tolerance. Where it fails, the grid is refined there before any comparison script exists.
  - **Convergence.** Stage B also computes the outputs on an extended range (the next orders, a larger height). Any change
    in where the outputs fall is reported. At Stage C, a change in the support between the declared and the extended
    range is reported as well.
  - **The price.** The nulls of K and X2 get exactly the same reach and resolution. A larger, finer space makes support
    easier to find and specificity harder to earn (GAP4). Under THE_BAR's 2026-10-27 clause, an enlarged menu chosen after
    seeing results "may be" a new look; this file counts it as one.
- **S1.** On Stage B's grid over the declared space, this cell reports:
  - the least value of the support statistic (C6) against the seen targets;
  - the set of configurations below the threshold (the support at our world), with its size in the declared space's own
    measure;
  - its range in Im τ.
- **Its grades:**
  - **IN SUPPORT:** some configuration lies below the threshold, and its location is reported.
  - **EXCLUDED within the declared space:** none does, the grid passes S0's resolution check (or the space is finite and
    enumerated exactly), and every axis's reach ends where the framework ends or is covered past its cutoff by an
    argument. This is EXCLUDED "within a stated scope, by an argument" (WORKING_RULES item 4).
  - **NOT FOUND:** none does, and the conditions for EXCLUDED do not hold. This is a declared search's null.
- **What S earns.**
  - IN SUPPORT alone earns no credit: in a large space a match somewhere is expected (GENESIS GAP4). It is a READING.
  - EXCLUDED is a NEGATIVE for the framework as declared. It is scoped to the space, the statistic, its threshold and the
    frame's dictionary.

**T — TYPICALITY (P-485 (c)); a screen, CONDITIONAL on § 0's premise.**
- **T1, the depth's tail.**
  - D is C4's measure on the declared space, computed at Stage B. The card states, before Stage B, whether T1 reads D on
    the whole space or conditioned on the worlds that share our world's discrete data. Both are reported.
  - y₀ is our world's depth as the attempt finds it: the Im τ of S1's best configuration, with the support's Im τ range
    reported beside it. For FR-FIB under the strip's five conditions, y₀ = 3.03.
  - The statistic is t = 2·min(P_D(Im τ ≤ y₀), P_D(Im τ ≥ y₀)), the two-sided tail.
  - **NEGATIVE** if t < 0.01: D puts our depth in its 1% tails. **CONSISTENT** otherwise.
  - **The forms D can take:**
    - No highest-density region is used, since it would need a base measure.
    - A D given as a point with a band is read as the distribution the card names. The default is normal, with the band
