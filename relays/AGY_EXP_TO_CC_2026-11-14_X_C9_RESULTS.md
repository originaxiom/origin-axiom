## Unconditional Proof of $\eta_A(T^2_M) = -L_{\mathrm{odd}}(1, \chi_c) / \pi^2$

1. **Exact cancellation of all three untwisted terms:** Adding Bohn's Equation `(67)` (`chap_IV.tex` line `3588`) and Theorem `12` (`chap_IV.tex` untwisted $\eta(T^2_M)$) gives

   because the three untwisted pieces $-\frac{a+d}{3c} + \frac{a+d}{3c}$, $+4\operatorname{sgn}(c)\,s(a, c) - 4\operatorname{sgn}(c)\,s(a, c)$, and $+\operatorname{sgn}(c(a+d)) - \operatorname{sgn}(c(a+d))$ cancel identically (`2` times Bohn's Proposition `18`, $\int_0^1 \widehat{\eta}_A$).
2. **Identification with the Siegel–Dieter transformation law:** Under $Jc_{\mathrm{char}} \equiv (-c_2, c_1) \equiv (\nu_2, -\nu_1) \pmod{\mathbb{Z}^2}$, the weight-two odd Eisenstein series $G_{c_{\mathrm{char}}}(z)$ satisfies $\frac{d}{dz}\log g_{Jc_{\mathrm{char}}}(z) = -\frac{1}{2\pi i} G_{c_{\mathrm{char}}}(z)$, where $g_{Jc_{\mathrm{char}}}(z)$ is Siegel's function (`chap_IV.tex` Definition `14` and Lemma `17`, matching $\boldsymbol{\eta}_{\nu_1, -\nu_2}$ up to a constant phase and half-exponent). ## What the Wall Teaches (`E4` — Symplectic Two-Dimensional Fourier Duality and Higher-Cover Telescoping)

Why does $\rho_A(T^2_M)$ fail to be an affine function of $\sqrt{D}\,Z(0, \chi_c)$ on `12` of the `13` multi-value levels, while succeeding on `+LR 3`? Both quantities are periods of weight-two Eisenstein series along the **same** closed geodesic of $\gamma = M^T$, yet they live on **symplectically dual bases** of the $N$-torsion group $((1/N)\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^2$:

1. **Position basis vs character-Fourier basis:**
   - $Z(0, \chi_c)$ is the geodesic period of the **position-shifted** Kronecker–Eisenstein form $G^{(Jc)*}(z)$ (localized at the single coset $Jc \in ((1/N)\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^2$).
   - $G_c(z)$ is the **character-weighted** two-dimensional finite Fourier transform over all $N^2$ cosets $u \in ((1/N)\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^2$:
     $$G_c(z) = \frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{u \in ((1/N)\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^2} e^{2\pi i N (c \cdot u)}\,G^{(u)*}(z),$$
     where the non-holomorphic term $-\pi / \operatorname{Im} z$ cancels identically for every $c \neq 0$ by character orthogonality.
2. **Exact symplectic DFT on principal congruence levels (`+LR 3` vs `+LR 5`):**
   - Whenever $M \equiv I \pmod{N}$ (so all $N^2 - 1$ non-zero $N$-torsion characters are fixed by $M$, which holds on `+LR 3` with $N = 4$ and `+LR 5` with $N = 11$), integrating along $z_0 \to \gamma z_0$ gives the exact finite Fourier bridge
     $$-\frac{1}{4}\eta_A(c) = \frac{L_{\mathrm{odd}}(1, \chi_c)}{4\pi^2} = \frac{1}{N^2}\left(-\frac{\Psi(M)}{12} + \sum_{c' \neq 0} \cos\bigl(2\pi N (c_2 c'_1 - c_1 c'_2)\bigr)\,Z(0, \chi_{c'})\right),$$
     verified numerically to below three parts in ten to the fifteenth across all `15` characters of `+LR 3` and all `120` characters of `+LR 5`.
   - A finite symplectic Fourier transform $F_{\mathrm{symp}} v$ is affine in $v$ **if and only if** $v$ lies in at most a single non-constant eigenspace of $F_{\mathrm{symp}}$! On `+LR 3` ($N = 4$), $Z(0, \chi_c)$ takes only three symmetric values $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ and happens to be an exact eigenvector of the $(\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^2$ symplectic DFT ($\rho_A = -Z(0, \chi_c)$ identically). Already on the next congruence level `+LR 5` ($N = 11$, `11` distinct values across multiple $M$-orbits), the symplectic DFT mixes distinct $M$-orbits and destroys any pointwise function $\rho_A = f(Z(0, \chi_c))$.
3. **Higher-cover telescoping on the `14` non-congruence levels:**
   - Even deeper: when $M \not\equiv I \pmod{N}$ (`14` of the `16` levels), the sum over $u \in ((1/N)\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z})^2$ includes torsion cosets **not** fixed by $M$. Grouping those cosets into $M$-orbits of length $r > 1$ telescopes $\sum_{j=0}^{r-1} \int_{z_0}^{\gamma z_0} G^{(\gamma^j u)*}(z)\,dz = \int_{z_0}^{\gamma^r z_0} G^{(u)*}(z)\,dz$, which is the Shintani slope $Z_{M^r}(0, \chi)$ on the **$r$-fold cyclic cover $T^2_{M^r}$**, not on $T^2_M$! Thus on non-congruence levels $\eta_A(T^2_M)$ is not even determined by the fixed-character $Z(0)$ table of $T^2_M$ alone: the flat unitary spectral asymmetry $\eta_A(T^2_M)$ is genuinely a **new invariant** in the record, Fourier-dual to the Shintani slopes across the cyclic cover tower rather than a rescaling of `B1438`'s $Z(0, \chi_c)$.
