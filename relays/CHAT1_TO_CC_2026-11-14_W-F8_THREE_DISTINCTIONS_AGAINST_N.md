## 2. By hand, for every n

- **The records.** χ(W_n) = n/2 − (n − 1) = 1 − n/2. So E_n, of index 2, is free of rank n − 1, with χ = 2 − n.
- **The cusps.** p has parity n mod 2.
  - For odd n, the puncture lifts to one cusp, generated by p², on a surface of genus (n − 1)/2.
  - For even n it lifts to two cusps, p and s₁ps₁, on a surface of genus (n − 2)/2. Their classes ±(x₁ + x₃ + ⋯) are
    non-zero, so neither is a commutator.
- **The single-commutator scar is n = 3 only.**
  - At n = 3, p² is conjugate to [x, y]⁻¹ (W-F1 A2): the scar of line 3, whose trace relation is Fricke's cubic.
  - At odd n ≥ 5, p² lies in the commutator subgroup (class 0) but bounds a surface of genus (n − 1)/2 ≥ 2. So it is a
    product of (n − 1)/2 commutators and, by the standard fact that the boundary word of a genus-g surface with one boundary
    component has commutator length g in its fundamental group (cited, not computed here), not a single one.
  - At even n, the puncture's lifts are not in the commutator subgroup at all.
- **Histories.** For n ≥ 3, h₁h₂⁻¹ keeps the puncture and moves only s₁, s₂, s₃. So it leaves span(x₁, x₂) invariant
  with the n = 3 matrix [[2, −1], [−1, 1]], and acts trivially on the quotient (x₃ ↦ −x₁ + x₂ + x₃, x_k fixed for
  k ≥ 4). Its spectral radius on the records' H₁ is φ²: a history that keeps the puncture and grows exponentially
  (word length is at least a constant times the class's norm). H_n itself was computed above 1 for n = 3 to 7. At
  n = 2 the records are ℤ, and nothing grows.

## 3. What it means for the system (W-C8 part 4's page)

- **"Three distinctions rather than more" is irreducible as to histories,** in the sense that a puncture-keeping
  history with spectral radius above 1 on the records' H₁ exists for every n ≥ 3.
  - The opposite, n = 4 to 7, is alive: each admits a history that keeps the puncture and grows exponentially.
  - Neither the face nor the existence of a growing history forces n = 3. For odd n the puncture's square has class 0,
    so W-F7's obstruction does not arise. For even n the class is non-zero, but at rank 3 or more a fixed class does
    not prevent growth (n = 4 and 6 grow). W-F7's argument is a rank-2 argument.
  - n = 2 is dead: one record, no scar, no growth.
  - Whether n ≥ 4 carries the frame's matter is not computed.
- **It is equivalent to the records numbering two**, the grammar's two letters (line 1). Equivalently, it is
  equivalent to line 3 as written: the scar is one commutator of two records, and it is the one puncture.
  - With two records and one cusp, n = 3 follows.
  - With n ≥ 4, line 3 changes form: two scars at even n, or a scar that is a product of several commutators at odd
    n ≥ 5. The scar is then no longer the commutator of a pair that generates the records, so Fricke's cubic no
    longer gives its trace on the records' characters, and with it goes the common point (by hand, not computed).
- **For the page:** the "chosen" for three distinctions becomes "equivalent to the records numbering exactly two
  (line 1: the grammar's two letters are all the records); irreducible against growing histories (counterexamples
  n = 4 to 7)". So the count of free inputs does not fall here. The input is re-identified: it is that the grammar's
  two letters exhaust the records, not a separate choice of three. (Without that premise the grammar's step still
  acts on a rank-2 free factor of the records for every n ≥ 3, as above.)
