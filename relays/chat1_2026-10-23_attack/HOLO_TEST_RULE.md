## The question

In THE READING TEST (sealed; `READING_TEST_RULE.md`, `READING_TEST_RESULT.md`) the declared search found nothing below
χ²_nine 1 045 for the Standard Model's one-doublet reading — the up quarks seeing h̄, the down quarks and leptons h — on
4 256 fresh rows over every line. Under main's identification of S̃ with H̃ that points to two Higgs directions with
equal (holomorphic) readings. The simplest such structure is one direction h read S in every sector (THE_NEXT_ATTACK's
A1; in two-doublet language H_u and H_d with the same direction in the holomorphic convention). Reading S̃ in every
sector is the same structure with h → h̄, which the free direction absorbs.

**Does one holomorphic Higgs direction bring the nine below the bars on some row nobody has computed, and is a fit as
good as the data's rare for random hierarchies through the same search — including random hierarchies that share the
lepton–down relation c = 3 puts in by hand?**

## The model

- **The frame:** main's fibre frame (B1720's instrument at 3412bf09d, unchanged, through chat1's `channel_store.py`
  wrappers): the four channel weights per sector and horocycle; B1736's closed form (C1 against `coupling()` at five
  points on lines of different orders); B1736's scalar part zero (a pure Higgs direction).
- **The reading:** up Y(u_up; h), down Y(u_down; h), leptons Y(u_lep; h), all S; the lepton factor c = 3 by hand
  (m_μ/m_τ × 3, m_e/m_τ ÷ 3), as in every value test since B1726.
- **Free:** h ∈ CP² (four real numbers per row).
- **Objective:** PRECISION_BAR's χ²_nine and χ²_six (σ² = the data's relative error² + 0.1² on natural logs; the pinned
  files at 3412bf09d as in `inverse_higgs.py`: Huang–Zhou 2021 at M_Z and PDG 2025's moduli; the scale is a framing
  choice, THE_FRAMING_CHECK §4a); bars 16.75 (six) and 21.67 (nine).
- **D**, the double ratio (m_μ/m_s)(m_d/m_e) with c = 3, is built from four of the nine (ln D = (L₄ − L₂) + (L₃ − L₅)):
  the nine's errors allow it about 23%, P-094 measures it to 1% (10.58 ± 0.11). Under A1 with c = 3, D = 9 for every h
  on the 16 lines of order 1, 2 and 5 (the leptons copy the down quarks there; the referee checked 200 random h at every
  stored row of the reading test's grid, |D − 9| ≤ 6 × 10⁻¹¹; every other line departs from 9 by at least 6.6
  somewhere). D is read at each row's optimum only.

## The calibration (a pre-registered price for choosing the row and the direction)

- **Targets.** Main's generator (`two_higgs_compare.py`'s `r_masses` and `r_ckm`, the same code; Python
  `random.Random(1743)`): per sector r₂ = 10^U(−3, −1), r₁ = 10^U(−6, −2.5) with r₁ < r₂; the CKM a = 10^U(−1.5, −0.2),
  b = 10^U(−2.5, −0.7), c = 10^U(−3.5, −1.5) with c < b < a. **Set 1:** the first 300 draws as they are. **Set 2
  (lepton-tied):** the first 1 200 draws with each target's lepton ratios set from its own down ratios by the data's
  factors (m_μ/m_τ = 3.144 m_s/m_b, m_e/m_τ = 0.2972 m_d/m_b), so that random targets share the relation c = 3 builds
  into the model. Only the generator is TWO_HIGGS's: there the comparison was a worst-factor distance; here it is the χ²
  with the data's σ, for every target.
- **The statistic, identical for the data and every target:** on every test row of the 264 lines (not (2/5, 0) or
  (2/5, 3/5), not the mirror set, not degenerate) the target's minimum over the calibration grid (every fourth direction of the search grid; one matrix product per
  chunk, `chi2_many`, with the data's target as column 0); the target's single best test row by that minimum, refined by
  the declared search. **Random as good** = the fraction of a set's targets whose statistic is at or below the data's,
  with the upper end of its exact (Clopper–Pearson) 95% interval, two-sided as THE_BAR reads it.
- **What it prices and what it does not.** It prices the choice of row (line among the 264, τ, horocycle) and of
  direction for this structure, through the identical procedure. It does not price the choice of A1 (made after the 17.26 look and the
  reading test's negative), c = 3 (set 2 prices the lepton–down relation it builds in), the frame and its scalar part,
  M_Z, the 10% allowance in σ, the record's directions used as starting points, or the grid. **THE_BAR's look
  correction:** A1 was chosen after the data were compared under three structures (A2, A1 and the Standard Model's
  reading), so H3′_BAR multiplies the upper end of set 2's interval by 3 and grades it at THE_BAR's p < 0.01; with 1 200
  targets that can pass only with no hit (3 × 0.307% = 0.92%; one hit gives 1.39%; with 1 000 even no hit would give
  1.10%).
- **Cost:** the store about 25 minutes; the scan about 3–3.5 hours on two workers, which outruns the sandbox's two-hour
  limit on one job — the scan writes chunks of 200 rows atomically, refuses chunks from another version of the script,
  and resumes from the completed chunks (a resume will be disclosed); the calibration's 1 501 refinements about
  50 minutes.

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **H2′, H3′ and H3′_BAR hold, with C2 and C3, and the data's calibration statistic is below both bars.** One holomorphic
  Higgs direction brings the nine below both bars on rows nobody had computed, away from the look's lines, and random
  hierarchies that share the lepton–down relation do as well through the same search in no case of 1 200, under THE_BAR's
  look correction: PRECISION_BAR's necessary condition for describing an ingredient as reaching the values is met — for
  this structure with h fitted (four real numbers per row), the line, τ and horocycle chosen from 5 320 rows (the
  calibration prices that choice), c = 3 by hand, at M_Z. **Not yet a number of the 19:** the direction is fitted; the
  next step would be a rule that fixes h, sealed on fresh rows. If H4 fails, add: at those optima D lies outside P-094's
  1% window (the nine hold it only to about 23%).
- **The same with H3′ but not H3′_BAR:** below both bars and specific at the point estimate, not passed under THE_BAR's
  look correction (as B1741's P2); reported as such.
- **H3 holds, H3′ fails:** specific only through the lepton–down relation set by hand; reported, not read as a selection.
- **H2 holds, H2′ fails:** the look reproduced at nearby τ, not a new row.
- **H3 or H3′ holds, H1 fails:** pattern-level specificity, described as a pattern match (PRECISION_BAR), no value claim.
- **H1 or H2 holds, H3 fails:** below the bar, but random hierarchies are fitted as well in 1% of cases or more (3 or
  more of 300);
  reported, not read as a selection.
- **H1 holds, H2 fails:** the nine's χ² dips below its bar only where the six's share stays above 16.75; reported with the
  rows.
- **H1 fails.** The declared search, with C2's measured power, found no direction bringing the nine below the bar on
  these rows; THE_NEXT_ATTACK's step 3 (two directions from a stated rule) is the remaining road.
- **If the data's calibration statistic is not below both bars while H2′ holds,** H3 and H3′ are reported as pricing
  that statistic only, not H2′'s row.
- **C2 fails:** a failure of H1 is not read as a negative. **C3 or another control fails:** nothing is read until it is
  resolved, post hoc and labelled.
- **Reach.** Conditional on the frame as main built it (B1736's scalar part zero), the 266 lines, the ten τ, the two
  horocycles, c = 3, the comparison at M_Z, the reading of S as holomorphic (P-295 records another reading) and the
  declared search with C2's measured power. Between the sampled τ nothing is claimed.
