- B1736: the triplet (a_i, a_j, a_k) transforms under ρ′, whose image at k = 21 is the cube's rotation group (order 24, ≅ S₄,

  i, j, k label family directions; ε = j is a unit of that same algebra.

  H̃ = iσ₂H* is that j and iσ₂ are the same matrix in the 2 × 2 representation of the quaternions. Whether the frame's

## Two readings of the Higgs direction h, and what each makes of the sealed tests

| what h is | the up sector's Higgs under one electroweak doublet | THE READING TEST (S̃ up, S down) | THE HOLOMORPHIC READING TEST (S everywhere) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (i) a family direction (a flavon-like vacuum direction, electroweak singlet) times one Higgs doublet | the same h: H̃ conjugates the doublet, not the family direction | tests a structure no single doublet produces: "conjugating the family direction for the up quarks fails" | **the Standard Model's single doublet** |
| (ii) the vacuum direction of a family triplet of Higgs doublets H_a (three doublets, ⟨H_a⟩ ∝ h_a) | h̄: H̃_a = iσ₂H_a* conjugates each component | **the Standard Model's coupling structure for that triplet: it fails** (S1 holds) | a second, independent triplet with an aligned direction (holomorphic, MSSM-like): H_u and H_d triplets |

Main's identification of S̃ with H̃ is reading (ii). Under (i) the reading test's pointer to two doublets falls away and
an A1 pass would mean one doublet suffices; under (ii) it stands and an A1 pass means two aligned Higgs triplets.
