## 1. On a closed member the choice stands

On L⁶RLR, where P-726 states the choice, ρ sees the excursion: B1846 § 6's "an average that sees the excursion while
the history is closed and short" (and row 1601: ρ is blind only in the limit). So a read-out by the point and a
read-out by ρ both register the departure there.
- Neither Π3's registration nor the goal's criterion picks one, so main's "remaining choice" stands on every closed
  member.
- c6b813450 § 4's "An average returns no τ" holds at every k and would settle it. But that is a commitment of this
  seat's, not a principle.

## 2. In the one-departure limit, within the family, the read-out that registers is a point

- ρ is blind in the limit (B1846 § 5; row 1601), so a read-out by ρ registers no departure there.
- P-717 records the consequence: "a departure that is seen at all needs a point read-out". A point read-out gives its
  point unit weight. That is the read-out's kind, and it is P-717's premise, not something the metric supplies.
- So within c609cf9ab's family the point follows from three premises:
  - Π3 read as registration (c609cf9ab's reading of befdd5ffc's Π3, which selects histories);
  - the goal's criterion, which excludes growing weights and windows of chosen width;
  - the limit itself.
- **Open:** windows the metric fixes with no chosen width, such as the maximal embedded tube about the systole pushed to
  the circle, would also register the departure in the limit. This note does not address them.

- P-727's value, on L⁶RLR, is the k = 1 term. B1847 found the level surface folding near the tube there, and P-727
  stays open.
