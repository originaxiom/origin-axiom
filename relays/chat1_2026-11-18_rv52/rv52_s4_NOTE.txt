rv52_s4.py's docstring says the group 'acts faithfully on its 4 cosets of a subgroup of order 6'. That is a slip, written before the run: the script acts on the 8 cosets of <R>, as its code and output say. It computes the order (24), the order of the image on those cosets (24) and the order of T's image (4); that the group of order 24 is S_4 is the standard identification of the (2, 3, 4) triangle group with the octahedral rotation group, not computed here.
