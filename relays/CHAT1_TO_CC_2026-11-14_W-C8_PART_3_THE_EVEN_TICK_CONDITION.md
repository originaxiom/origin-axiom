## 2. What follows for the condition (a reading, under the identification above)

- **On the doublets of levels 1, 3 and 5, the class index reads nothing at odd ticks.**
  - It is zero on every doublet there.
  - The record's recipe cannot be formed at any odd level (by hand).

  So, in that frame, on those levels the condition holds whatever the weight: the index cannot see odd ticks. It does
  not hold because a weight delivers it. On those levels, the index is no test of the condition. For the ℓ(μ) = −1
  doublets the zero is computed, not proved. Level 7's doublets were not computed. And the index is zero at level 2
  too, so on this tower its silence is not tied to odd ticks alone.
- **Two quantities computed here do not vanish at odd ticks:**
  - **The characters' h¹ gaps.** They are non-zero only on odd levels.
  - **The hand at the mark.** Part 1 found it flips with each tick and with each undoing: (−1)ⁿ⁺ᵉ.
- **For the pair branch of (c)**, where the even ticks are what is observed at either member, a weight delivering the
  condition would have to give the characters' odd-level h¹ gaps no weight. The hand at the mark would stay constant
  along the observed histories without undoing.
- **At a fixed point (c's other branch)** every tick is a symmetry and there is no hand, and nothing above forces
  either requirement.
- **A candidate for the "pair the mirror exchanges".** At every odd metallic mark (part 1, P4), J_m acts by λ and K by
  −λ. Both exchange the pairs {±v} and {±λv}, and JK fixes them. At the golden mark (N = 5) these two pairs are the whole
  line. At the bronze mark (m = 3, N = 13; λ = 8, −λ = 5) they are two of the line's six ±pairs. That this is the
  criterion's pair is not shown. The identification is open.

## 3. What the wall teaches

A frame that cannot see a thing cannot be evidence that the thing is not observed. A reading: if the even-tick
condition is to be derived and not built in, the derivation has to run through an observable that does not vanish at
odd ticks. The ones found here are the characters' h¹ gaps and the hand at the mark.
