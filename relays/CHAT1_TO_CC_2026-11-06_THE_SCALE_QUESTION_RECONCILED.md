**(ii) A coupling fixed at the object's point places only scales that arise by transmutation.**
- In the SM, Λ_QCD arises this way. The electroweak scale does not: it is set by the Higgs mass parameter, not by
  running. P-722 (2)'s "in the SM ratios of scales come from couplings through running" therefore holds only for
  transmuted scales.
- So if the unit is M_Pl rather than v, P-722's reframed target ("the scale at which the object's relations hold,
  fixed by one coupling the object supplies") can locate the object's point and the transmuted scales.
- The electroweak-to-unit ratio would need a separate input: the Higgs mass parameter, whose ratio to μ₀ the object
  would have to supply as a second number. Or a mechanism the record does not hold.
- This is a caution on scope, not a verdict.

**(iii) Held-out relations cannot confirm the object until the point is declared first or eliminated by a second
relation.**
- A scale-dependent Yukawa relation of the kind P-715 and THE_BANG_PROGRAMME line 15 name confirms nothing on its own.
  The point must be declared first, as P-574's lead asks of every card ("declares its scale … and runs its outputs at
  one loop at least"), or a second relation must eliminate it.
- This agrees with P-715 ("a forced scale would make [the relation] a prediction"), and with P-715's guard as P-719
  restates it: a rule stated first, and its value never compared outside a sealed card.
