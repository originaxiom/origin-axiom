### 2.2 The dropping rule

- The rule leaves out a component whose ratio falls below 1e-10 at any node, for either object of a pair. It was meant
  for exact zeros (B1716), and only the log average needed it.
- It also leaves out components that are not zeros. The cause is a long run of one letter: such a word's thread climbs
  far toward the cusp.
  - Per the referee's recomputation, in the sealed X3 at level 7 one n = 16 word with a 9-letter run reaches reduced
    Im τ ≈ 5.1 to 5.3, where five ratios fall to between 1e-10 and 1e-11.5.
  - Leaving out exactly those five reproduces 8 of 43 (median 1.5378).
  - The follow-up's first 40 words, all n = 12, still lose 11; m004's n = 12 words include a 10-letter run.
- So the sets compared differ between rows. With nothing left out (recomputed here from the json):
