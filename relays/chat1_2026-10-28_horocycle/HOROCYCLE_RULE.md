## 1. The question

- **The input.** The six reach their best rows with the horocycle set by hand to L_max (main's plan: "two that do
  fitting work without a plan holding them … the horocycle (L_max or L₂; the six's best rows are on L_max, the nine's
  one-Higgs optimum on L₂)"). B1667 found two canonical horocycles in the fibre's own geometry, the maximal embedded
  horoball (L_max) and the horocycle of length 2 (L₂). It left open "which canonical horocycle, if either, is the physical
  end".
- **Two ways it could leave the six's count.**
  - A switch of horocycle could be a shift of τ in disguise. Then it adds no freedom to the six, since Im τ is already
    counted.
  - It could be a freedom the six do not need, if both horocycles carry them.

  Otherwise it stays: a discrete input chosen by the data, as the slope is.
- **This rule asks, on the 40 lines of slope 2/5:**
  1. how the horocycle acts on each of the six (data-free);
  2. whether a shift of τ absorbs it (data-free; THE DECISIVE CELL);
  3. whether its action on the first generation is common to the three sectors (data-free);
  4. whether the six need L_max when τ is free, on the 30 lines of orders 25 and 30. L_max's passing there was known
     from B1786; L₂'s clause is the new result;
  5. PRECISION_BAR's second cell, the nine, per horocycle.

## 5. The measures (fixed now)

- **v(ℓ, τ, h) ∈ ℝ⁶:** the six's logs on line ℓ at τ and horocycle h. A ratio equal to 0 (a clamped or vanishing
  generation) makes its point non-finite. Such points are counted and excluded from every cell.
- **The lever:** Λ = v(ℓ, τ, L_max) − v(ℓ, τ, L₂), component by component.
- **The absorption distance** at a base point p = (ℓ, τ_p):
  - A(p) = min over Δτ in the window W of rms_i [v_i(ℓ, τ_p + Δτ, L₂) − v_i(ℓ, τ_p, L_max)], with the rms over the six;
  - W: |Δ Re τ| ≤ 0.25 and −1.4 ≤ Δ Im τ ≤ +0.3;
  - **the search:**
    - start at the G point of the same line inside τ_p + W with the least rms;
    - then Gauss–Newton: forward differences of step 0.005 in Re τ and Im τ (backward at W's upper edges), the normal
      equations with a 10⁻¹² ridge, every step clamped to W;
    - a step that does not lower the rms is halved, at most five times, and the search stops if none does;
    - at most six iterations, stopping when the rms changes by less than 10⁻⁵;
  - a search that ends on any edge of W, or finds no finite start, is flagged;
  - **a found distance is attained inside W, so it bounds A(p) from above.** A small found distance cannot come from a
    failed search; a large one can.
- **The first-generation lever difference:** Δ₁(ud) = Λ(up₁) − Λ(down₁) and Δ₁(ℓd) = Λ(lep₁) − Λ(down₁). The second
  generation's are read out.
- **χ²_six and χ²_nine:** B1741's measure exactly, per horocycle separately:
  - its `sigmas()`, with the 10% allowance;
  - its seat menu: the configurations (S̃, S, S) and (S̃, S̃, S̃), with c ∈ {1, 3};
  - its data files: running masses at M_Z, and the CKM moduli for the nine.
- **The free-τ minimum per (line, horocycle):** start at the G point with the least χ²_six. Then Nelder–Mead in (Re τ,
  Im τ), clamped to Re τ ∈ [0, 1] and Im τ ∈ [1.5, 3.6] (which W's reach matches). The initial simplex steps 0.05 in each
  direction. It stops after 80 evaluations or when the simplex's χ² values agree to 10⁻³ (no tolerance in τ).

- A failure of (b) leaves NOT ABSORBED unreadable. ABSORBED can still be read, because a found distance is attained.

## 7. What each outcome means (written now)

- **H2 ABSORBED:** in (S̃, S, S), at the base points, L₂ reproduces L_max's six after a shift of τ, to 5% rms over the
  six. A single ratio can still be off by about 0.12 in log.
  - Main's ledger could drop the horocycle from the six's inputs only if, in addition, H4's L₂ clause fails: the
    free-τ χ²_six at L₂ is below 16.75 on the 30 lines. The reduction is main's call.
  - If H4's L₂ clause holds, the horocycle still does fitting work, and nothing is dropped.
- **H2 NOT ABSORBED:** within W, the horocycle is a separate freedom of the six in (S̃, S, S).
  - With H4 holding, the six need L_max on the 30 lines, and L₂'s failing there is the new result. The horocycle is one
    discrete input chosen by the data, counted, as the slope is.
  - With L₂ also below 16.75, the six do not need it at the χ²_six bar, and it is not counted as an input of that
    match. No value is claimed: PRECISION_BAR needs both bars on rows nobody had computed.
- **H2 UNDETERMINED:** neither statement. The distances and the flagged searches are listed.
- **H1:**
  - if it holds, the horocycle acts mainly on the first generation: the first generation's values depend on the choice
    of horocycle more than the second's;
  - if it fails, the first generation does not dominate everywhere.
- **H3:**
  - if it holds, the first-generation lever is common to the sectors. So (m_u/m_t)/(m_d/m_b) and (m_e/m_τ)/(m_d/m_b) are
    the same at both canonical horocycles to 10%: candidates for a forced-relations ledger, not tested against data
    here;
  - if it fails, no such horocycle-independent first-generation combination exists among these.
- **H5:** a pass at either horocycle would be the first χ²_nine pass on record and would be taken to a sealed test on
  fresh rows. Failing is the expectation.
- **No value is claimed.** No input is removed by H1, H3, H4 or H5. 0 of 19.
