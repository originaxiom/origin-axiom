## 0. In short

1. **[J] The row.** The chain (the substitution, its monodromy LR, the bundle m004, the fibre's frame, the read-out;
   and, beside it, the golden category) is read as an account of the Standard Model's parameters. No stage is derived
   as a process that runs in a natural system: a growth, a decay, an evolution in time, a transition (§ 1).
2. **[E] What is on record about process.**
   - **Order matches, not mechanisms.**
     - B1082 compares the chain's forcing order with the early universe's gauge-breaking order. It is consistent from
       E₆ onward, conditional on the GUT model: a match of shape, not a mechanism.
     - B1115 (T-SPENDING-DAG) matches the chain's spending order to the cosmological freezing order with zero
       inversions.
   - **Preconditions.** B867 finds the three Sakharov preconditions for baryogenesis in banked structure. P-712 found
     its "barrier" a roll, not a double well.
   - **The programme for the process.**
     - P-694 (opened 2026-11-05) asks for the object's own process. The owner's stated motive is a process that
       creates what is observed, rather than an account of parameters.
     - Its first wave found the Farey transition, which places nothing (P-713, NEGATIVE).
     - Main's answer (P-729): the whole cosmological process is downstream, a check, because it needs a dynamics the
       frame does not derive (FL-05).
   - **The first stage's realisations, known and partly tested.**
     - B107 found the metallic tower to be Kohmoto, Kadanoff and Tang's quasicrystal trace map. B417 found the object's
       symbolic face to be the Sturmian subshift, with gap group ℤ + ℤφ.
     - B82 and B812 closed the physics bridges.
     - The record also holds a crossing campaign (L161; P-335 ruled no experiment), and a laboratory falsifier for
       photonic and polariton Fibonacci chains, sealed (L173, B1106), whose count is queued for re-grading (P-609).
     - B519's "no external crossing" headline is retracted (B525): gap labelling is not gap opening, and the mixed
       chain's gap-opening cell is live again.
3. **[J] The map, first version (§ 2).** Five named open problems or families are touched, none advanced:
   - **Markov's uniqueness conjecture** (Frobenius, 1913). T-CHAIN, T-TWOTEETH, T-UNIQ, T-COMM-UNIFIED and T-FAMILY
     work on the Markov surface and its tree; none bears on uniqueness.
   - **The spectral-gap conjecture for congruence subgroups of the Bianchi groups** (λ₁ ≥ 1). T-CONGRUENCE makes m004's
     group congruence in PSL(2, O₃) at level (4) (corrected 2026-11-11; B734). T-RIGID-SPEC and T-LANE-C describe its
     continuous spectrum.
   - **The Riemann hypothesis, GRH for L(s, χ₋₃) and Montgomery's pair correlation.** T-LANE-C and T-RIGID-SPEC place
     the scattering resonances at ζ_K's zeros and test GUE spacing numerically (generic, B1142).
   - **Special values and regulators** (Zagier's polylogarithm conjecture and Beilinson's). T-VALUE-REGULATOR, T-ADELIC,
     T-CANDZERO and T-ACTION compute the object's regulators and volume. The volume is the n = 2 case, a theorem.
   - **The Kashaev invariant's arithmeticity and modularity.** T-EULER-STRUCTURE, T-ALLORDERS and T-ASYMPTOTIC compute
     instances for 4₁. For 4₁ the volume conjecture is proved, and Zagier's modularity conjecture is proved (Bettin and
     Drappeau), reduced there to arithmeticity.
   - The other rows touch no named open problem found here. Most concern the record's own constructions (E₆ and
     trinification, the frame, the weave); the rest are known theorems (Minsky, Lackenby, Cohn, Reid).
4. **[J] Where the object is the only instance.**
   - The logic: a congruence subgroup is arithmetic, and the figure-eight is the only arithmetic knot (Reid, 1991; cited
     in T-ATOM-SPLIT). So among knot complements in S³, the congruence spectral-gap conjecture is a statement about
     m004 alone.
   - Its scope is narrow. m004 is one congruence instance among many: m003 is congruence too, at level (2) (B734), and
     so are arithmetic link complements and congruence covers. "Knot complement" is the record's own selector
     (T-M004-UNIQUENESS, H₁ = ℤ). Settling it would be a single-instance verification, of the kind already proved for
     PSL(2, ℤ[i]) and for small-level Γ₀(N).
   - The record has computed m004's cuspidal spectrum numerically:
     - seventeen eigenvalues, cross-validated (B795, B797);
     - 43 to r = 13.5 (B878);
     - the second cusp form to 25 decimal places (B922, with B943's correction of its priority sentence);
     - the parent eigenvalue at 31 figures (B1021).
     These are unpublished and not rigorously certified: the solver cannot count roots (B793), and "certified" in B797
     means cross-validated. P-651's "no paper" refers to the record's unwritten spectral paper, not to an absence of
     computation.
   - The first computable step for λ₁ ≥ 1 is a completeness certificate: no eigenvalue in (0, 1), and none missed below
     the first computed one, by the trace-formula route.
   - It is a contribution to mathematics, not physics.
