### What is forced, what is chosen, and what is not claimed

**Forced.** C1, C2, C6 and every step from the trace field to the Standard
Model gauge group are theorems. The ℤ₆ global form is forced, not chosen.
Hypercharge is the unique gaugeable U(1) in the chain's abelian sector. A
control run confirms the descent does **not** produce the Standard Model from
arbitrary starting groups, so the result is not vacuous.

**Chosen — and disclosed.** Three axioms, C3/C4/C5, each priced: the forks were
computed and banked. The most expensive is **orientation** (C5), graded
FRAGILE: taking it the other way gives the **Gieseking manifold**, m004's own
orientation double-cover parent. There is a road not taken and it has a name.

**Not claimed — and this is the programme's own position, not a critic's.**
From `philosophy/P000_what_is_not_nothing.md`:

> *"The family is determined; the member is contingent."*

m = 1 is singled out by three independent criteria — the systole (metric), the
expansion threshold (dynamical), and arithmeticity — two of which are unique to
it. That is *most-selected*, **not** *forced as a theorem*. P000 goes further
and argues a unique seed would be the **suspicious** outcome: fixed-point
phenomena generically give sets, not points, so *"we end up with a family" is
the intended shape, not a gap to be closed.*

### The honest limits, stated with the claim

- **The chain reaches a *shape*, never a *number*.** m004 is scale-free: every
  quantity it owns is a ratio to its own curvature radius, and no internal
  relation can fix that radius on any face. One dimensionful input from outside
  determines all the rest; it cannot come from inside. So the arc can deliver
  the gauge group and its global form, and **cannot** deliver a mass, a
  coupling, or a scale.
- **The 2T step is not a fingerprint of m004.** Measured over the SnapPy
  census: ~35 % of one-cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds and 36 % of knot
  complements admit the same surjection. The chain is forward-forced but not
  backward-unique — reaching E₆ is therefore **not evidence that the genesis
  choices were right**.
- **What *is* unique to m004** — it is the only member of its commensurability
  class with H₁ = ℤ, which is why it alone is a knot (Reid: the unique
  arithmetic knot complement in S³, re-verified here from census data). **The
  derivation does not consume that condition.** Closing that gap is the
  programme's sharpest open structural question.
- **No physical constant has been derived.** The sealed comparison against
  measured values placed the object at the **51st percentile** of random
  spectra — dead centre of chance — at 8 digits, a depth with essentially no
  exclusion power. A 25-digit test is now possible and has not yet been run.
