## 3. THE TWO GAPS — can they be closed?

### Gap 1: `⟨1⟩ ≠ 0` — does the E₆ singlet take a VEV?

**Not closable inside the framework, and it is already counted.**

A VEV is the minimum of a potential; a potential is dynamics; **the framework has
no dynamics** (Gate 5, and the object is timeless by construction — A2's
invertibility). So this cannot be derived from the object.

**But it is not a new debt.** It is the **rank closing** — one of B1000's five —
now carrying a *name* instead of being an unlabelled hole. That is the whole
progress: the closing went from *"something must reduce the rank and nothing
can"* to *"the rank closing is the E₆ singlet's VEV, and the mass term is
`10·10·1`."*

**Worth checking, and cheap:** S4 typed the rank closing as drawing on the **τ**
bit. If `⟨1⟩` and τ are the same closing seen twice, the budget is unchanged; if
they are two, the input count rises from four to five. **That is a bounded
question and it is unrun.**

**Research verdict:** repo — no (dynamics is out of scope by construction).
Literature — no (it would only confirm that a VEV is an input in every GUT).

### Gap 2: no mechanism removes an anomalous gauge U(1)

**This one is closable, and it is the campaign's sharpest remaining item.**

The framework uses *"an anomalous U(1) cannot be gauged"* as a **selection
principle** — it says which subgroup *is* the gauge group. Physics has a
**mechanism** for the same fact: **Green–Schwarz**, under which an anomalous U(1)
is cancelled by an axionic shift and the gauge boson becomes massive. **The
corpus contains no Green–Schwarz, Stückelberg, or anomalous-U(1)-mass material
anywhere** — I searched.

**Research verdict: BOTH would help, and here is why each is non-trivial.**

- **Literature — yes, and it is standard.** GS for anomalous U(1)s in string and
  GUT constructions is textbook. What is *not* standard is whether this
  framework has the field GS requires (a 2-form / axion with the right coupling).
  So the literature supplies the mechanism's requirements; it cannot supply the
  answer.
- **Repo — yes, and there is a specific reason to expect a hit.** GS needs an
  axion coupling to `F ∧ F` — a **Chern–Simons** coupling. **This corpus is full
  of Chern–Simons work** (B1, B250, the Gukov split, the complex volume, L15).
  The material may already exist under different vocabulary, which is exactly
  the failure mode the loss audit and the hedge-drop detector were built for.

**And there is a live tension worth naming before anyone searches:** the object
has **CS = 0 exactly**, by amphichirality. If the GS mechanism needs a
non-vanishing CS coupling, then **the same property that gives θ_QCD = 0 may
forbid the mechanism that removes the anomalous U(1)s** — which would be
amphichirality's *fifth* consequence, and the first unwelcome one. **I am not
claiming that. I am saying it is the first thing to check**, because if true it
converts gap 2 from "unsearched" to "structurally obstructed", and if false it
tells you where the mechanism lives.
