### B897 — REAL, and the only one

The arc: *"Mechanism-hood of generations: this is replication **at the algebra

mechanism-hood."*

The summaries: *"three generations, structurally"* (×2, one marked **"banked"**),

### B863 — MINOR, and worth a one-line fix

The arc: *"**Not exhaustive** over exotic conformal embeddings at the SM levels
beyond the enumerated structural descents + su(2)₄; **the menu import (P5)
covers this arc** exactly as it covers B861."*

`THE_FRAMEWORK:119` states the termination theorem with **no conditionality on
its own row.** P5 does appear two rows below, so a careful reader can find it —
but the theorem's row should carry its own dependency, especially now that the
confluence result made P5 the cascade's **only** remaining import.

### B864 — FALSE POSITIVE, and the reason is instructive

The arc's hedge is *"it does not derive the re-anchoring itself … what it derives
is the dial/gauge split **given** the chiral matter."*

Every flagged summary **does** carry the relevant qualifier — *"over chiral
matter"*, *"direction only"*, *"normalisation is homogeneous and not
derivable"*. **My detector's vocabulary simply did not contain those phrases.**
The summaries are faithful; the tool was under-informed.

### B790 — FALSE POSITIVE

`LAW_MAP:73` carries *"IN THE SL-KERNEL CONVENTION"*, which is exactly the scope
qualifier the arc asks for. Faithful.

# CORRECTION — I adjudicated a list I partly reconstructed, and understated the tool

The detector's full strength-≥6 output arrived after the above was written.
**Two errors in my adjudication, and they push in opposite directions.**

## Error 1 — I adjudicated an arc that is not on the list

I adjudicated **B790** (`LAW_MAP:73`). **B790 is not in the strength-≥6 set** —
it came from the earlier strength-≥2 run, and I carried it across from memory
rather than from the actual output. Its adjudication (false positive) may well be
right; **it is not part of this worklist and is withdrawn from the count.**

## Error 2 — I missed one, and it is the most interesting of the twelve

The actual twelve are: **B897 ×4, B864 ×5, B863 ×2, B850 ×1.** I reported B863 as
×1 (it is ×2, the second at `THE_SM_VERDICT:49`, the same omission) and **did not
adjudicate B850 at all.**

**B850 — BORDERLINE, and a distinct failure mode.**

The arc: *"**A specialist is still owed the question.** What this arc changes is
the question's cost: it is no longer 'what is the type?' but 'does the standard
ratio-set argument survive the cusp?'"*

`CAMPAIGN_STATUS:118`: *"**B850 — the reframe's last architecture-changing lead
closes.**"*

The **headline verb overstates** — the arc explicitly leaves the type question
owed to a specialist. But the **body immediately qualifies**: *"It has a
**computable shadow**: length-spectrum density → type."* So a skimming reader
gets *closed*; a continuing reader gets the qualification.

That is a **different defect from B897**, where nothing anywhere corrected the
claim. Worth recording as its own pattern: **headline overstates, body repairs.**

## The corrected calibration — the tool is better than I said

| | by flag | by arc |
|---|---|---|
| **real overstatement** | 4 (B897) | 1 of 4 |
| minor (conditionality dropped) | 2 (B863) | 1 of 4 |
| borderline (headline/body split) | 1 (B850) | 1 of 4 |
| **false positive** | 5 (B864) | 1 of 4 |
| **actionable** | **7 / 12 = 58 %** | **3 / 4 arcs** |

**I reported "~8 % precision". That was wrong** — I counted B897's four instances
as one defect and divided by twelve flags, mixing two denominators. **By flag,
58 % are actionable. By arc, three of the four flagged arcs had something.**

**So the instrument is substantially better than my own write-up of it**, and the
error was mine in both directions: I under-counted its hits and adjudicated a
candidate it never raised.

Recorded rather than silently amended, because a calibration figure that is
wrong by 7× would have been quoted downstream — and the whole point of this
exercise is that summaries of arcs get quoted without their qualifications.
