### B897 — REAL, and the only one

The arc: *"Mechanism-hood of generations: this is replication **at the algebra
level**. The solo seat's §5 fence stands."* / *"NOT decided (pre-stated):
mechanism-hood."*

The summaries: *"three generations, structurally"* (×2, one marked **"banked"**),
plus two more in the masterplan. **The arc refused the claim; four summary lines
make it**, and a CLOSED tombstone (P13, *"the wrong 3 … never as generation
multiplicity"*) forbids it. Already relayed in full.

### B863 — MINOR, and worth a one-line fix

The arc: *"**Not exhaustive** over exotic conformal embeddings at the SM levels
beyond the enumerated structural descents + su(2)₄; **the menu import (P5)
covers this arc** exactly as it covers B861."*

`THE_FRAMEWORK:119` states the termination theorem with **no conditionality on
its own row.** P5 does appear two rows below, so a careful reader can find it —
but the theorem's row should carry its own dependency, especially now that the
confluence result made P5 the cascade's **only** remaining import.

### B864 — FALSE POSITIVE, and the reason is instructive

The arc's hedge is *"it does not derive the re-anchoring itself … what it derives
is the dial/gauge split **given** the chiral matter."*

Every flagged summary **does** carry the relevant qualifier — *"over chiral
matter"*, *"direction only"*, *"normalisation is homogeneous and not
derivable"*. **My detector's vocabulary simply did not contain those phrases.**
The summaries are faithful; the tool was under-informed.

### B790 — FALSE POSITIVE

`LAW_MAP:73` carries *"IN THE SL-KERNEL CONVENTION"*, which is exactly the scope
qualifier the arc asks for. Faithful.

## The tool's honest calibration

| | |
|---|---|
| candidates at strength ≥ 6 | 12 |
| **real defects found** | **1** (in 4 places) |
| minor | 1 |
| false positives | 10 |
| **precision** | **~8 %** |
| **recall on the known defect** | **100 %** — it found B897 independently, and in 3 more places than were known |

**Precision is poor and that is acceptable for this tool**, because adjudication
costs one arc-read each and the alternative is discovery by luck. But it should
be stated plainly rather than presented as a clean instrument: **eleven of twelve
flags were noise.**

**The fix for the next run** is in the false positives themselves: add *"given"*,
*"over chiral matter"*, *"direction only"*, and convention-scope phrases to the
summary-hedge vocabulary. Each addition is a false positive retired, and the
source already records that pattern from the first calibration pass.

## What this discharges, and what it does not

**Discharges:** Phase 1's audit half. The summary layer of the eight flagship
documents has been checked against the arcs' own refusals, and **exactly one
substantive overstatement exists**, already relayed.

**Does not discharge:** Phase 1's *ledger* half — the eleven-row content table
still has to be written. But it can now be written knowing which summary lines
are trustworthy, which is the harder half of the job.

**And it lowers the temperature on the campaign's headline finding.** The
generation overstatement is not a symptom of a document layer riddled with
inflation. It is, on this evidence, **the only one of its kind** — which makes
the programme's summary discipline better than the finding alone suggested, and
makes the single defect more conspicuous rather than less.
