## 1. The map in one paragraph

The principle fixes structure: three generations, alike; odd chirality; the flavour triplet; the common point. It does
not yet fix a number. Every Standard Model number breaks a symmetry, and the record keeps finding that the moves'
symmetry forbids exactly those numbers at every point it can select (§2). So the numbers live in **choices**: which
frame, which τ, which line, which end condition, which Higgs direction (§3). There are two directions to travel:
- **forward**, from the principle to the choices. This waits on a selection dynamics, which needs the dictionary
  (§4).
- **inverse**, from reality to the choices. Data already rule out some of them, and each exclusion narrows what the
  forward route must produce (§4).

The breakthrough, if there is one, is where the two meet: a choice the principle selects that the data also demand.

## 4. The two directions

### Forward: from the principle to the choices (blocked at selection)

The chain is dictionary → content → potential → a selected vacuum (τ, line, end, direction) → numbers. B1652 already
says it: no value is derivable until the parent is built and its vacuum selected (the selection principle, GENESIS FK9;
P-139).
- **Where it stops (COMPUTED).** With a content that treats the parities alike, the potential is N·log(y|η|⁴) + const.
  Its only interior stationary points are i and ω, so τ goes to ω, the cusp or a flat direction (W59). At ω every
  lift vanishes (W87).
- **What would move it (OPEN).** Competing sectors, bosons against fermions with different twists, could put a minimum
  off the symmetric points. Their balance is set by the content, which is the dictionary's (GENESIS FK11).

### Inverse: from reality to the choices (open now, and cheap)

Each statement below is conditional on its frame (READING, by hand, from the cited arcs and the data's qualitative
facts):
1. **The electron is massive, so τ ≠ ω** in B1654's frame (a boundary-class Higgs, any channel-blind end condition;
   W87). With W59 that leaves the cusp or a flat direction. At the cusp the lift falls as q·(8π² Im τ + K), so a
   small first-generation mass is exponentially small there, not tuned.
2. **The quarks mix (V ≠ 1), so a hypercharge line of order dividing 6 is excluded** (§115). That includes both of the
   record's selected lines.
3. **m_s/m_b ≠ m_μ/m_τ, so a line of order dividing 4 is excluded** (§115). Together with item 2: N ∉ {1, 2, 3, 4, 6},
   and the smallest order left is 5.
4. **J ≠ 0, so whatever splits the sectors must break CP** (W74). Any line class kept by items 2 and 3 must also fail
   to be fixed by W74's involution.
5. **θ̄ ≈ 0 is consistent** with the record's 0, given the dictionary and positivity (W76, W74; the count by kind).
   It is the nearest number, not earned.

**What the inverse map says, read together** (READING). If the parent's frame is B1654's, reality points to:
- τ at the cusp, where the hierarchy is a power of q, a Froggatt–Nielsen texture whose exponents are the units'
  charges (W84);
- a hypercharge line of order at least 5, and not 6;
- a CP-odd line class.

None of this is selected by the principle yet. It is the target the forward route must hit.

## 6. The route, in order

1. **Now: relations before values** (this seat, W88). Some combinations of the 19 do not depend on the free size of a
   breaking: log-ratios of mass ratios at the cusp (W84's 1 : 3/2), equalities a line order forces (§115), D's shape
   (W87). Census every such relation the record's frames make, each with its premises. Compare them with data once,
   sealed. A relation that holds is the first earned piece; one that fails excludes its frame or its class.
2. **Next: the per-sector numbers at an allowed line** (main's twisted channels; this seat's blind check after main's
   seal). Only lines with N ∤ 4 and N ∤ 6 can give V ≠ 1 with d ≠ e. Main's arc is where the first structural numbers
   can appear.
3. **Then: the cusp texture with a twisted line** (this seat's W73 per sector, or chat1's thread frame). A twist shifts
   the q-exponents by amounts fixed by the hypercharge and the line's holonomy. At the cusp the sectors' log-ratios
   then become pure numbers, with one common q.
4. **In parallel: the parent and the dictionary** (main and the audit lane). The lane's latest packets (the compact
   g₂ ⊕ u(1); the cusp section map) are steps on that road.
5. **The fork at the end.**
   - **Either** a selection dynamics lands in the class the inverse map allows. That is the breakthrough, and the
     numbers follow.
   - **Or** every allowed class fails a sealed relation. Then a no-go theorem is the honest result: under the
     principle alone, no Standard Model value is derivable without a vacuum it cannot select. That would be progress
     too.

## 7. What would count as the breakthrough

- **A relation among the 19,** derived with no free input and confirmed by data under a sealed comparison with
  look-elsewhere accounting. It would not be a fit.

  requires.
- **Not:** a frame read at a symmetric point; a thread, line or normalisation chosen after the data; a fit with free
  coefficients.
