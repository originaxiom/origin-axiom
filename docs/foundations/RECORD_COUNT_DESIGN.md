# THE RECORD COUNT — a weave of n records, n = 2, 3, 4 (design note for the seats; not a preregistration)

P-201 (the owner, 2026-10-20): "are we sure we end up with a weave of two records instead of a weave of four or more?"

## What the record holds

- GM1 (two records, ℤ²) is POSTULATED, and not independent of GM4 (ℤ² is the first homology of the punctured torus).
- Fork F9 (B749, B1323) compared one, two and three records on carriers. One record has no hyperbolic element; three
  records on T³ have no carrier (χ = 0); three on S₁,₂ and S₀,₄ looked worse than two to word length 3 and were FRAGILE
  at 4–5 (m412 keeps ℚ(√−3) and is chiral). Four or more: never computed.
- What hangs on two: the three parities (2² − 1), the modular machinery (SL(2, ℤ), Γ(4), the theta triplet), and the
  punctured-torus carrier.

## The weave principle applied to the count

The weave says: not one thread, every thread acting together. Applied to the records, it says: not one count, every
count, joined by the inclusions ℤⁿ ⊂ ℤⁿ⁺¹ (SL(n, ℤ) ⊂ SL(n+1, ℤ); F_n ⊂ F_{n+1}). The question is then whether rank two is
(a) a forced restriction of the larger weaves, (b) one sub-weave among many that carry the same content, or (c) a choice.

## Computable first facts (proposed)

1. **The parities.** On 𝔽₂ⁿ \ 0 (2ⁿ − 1 points) SL(n, 𝔽₂) acts transitively. For n = 3 that is GL(3, 𝔽₂), order 168, on
   the Fano plane's 7 points; each of its 7 lines is a copy of the rank-two parity triplet. **Does the weave of three
   records carry the triplet as its lines, and does every positive word of SL(3, ℤ) act on some line as a 3-cycle?**
   (n = 4: 15 points, 35 lines.)
2. **The common point.** For n = 2 every odd-trace thread meets the quaternion point of the Fricke surface (Q₈, 2T, the
   triplet). **Is there a common point of the SU(2) character variety of F₃ fixed in character by the rank-three moves,
   and which finite group does it carry?**
3. **The carriers.** H₁ = ℤ³: S₁,₂ and S₀,₄ (F9's); H₁ = ℤ⁴: S₂,₁, S₁,₃, S₀,₅. **The torsion-free selection (SE1) and
   the minimal hyperbolic bundles at each rank, to a stated word length**, extending F9 to n = 4.
4. **Rank two inside rank three.** Restricted to a coordinate ℤ² ⊂ ℤ³, do the rank-three moves induce exactly the
   rank-two weave (the Fano line's stabiliser acting on its 3 points as S₃)?

## The decision it would inform

If (1)–(4) show rank two as the sub-weave every larger weave restricts to, with the triplet as the lines, the count of
two is a consequence of the weave rather than a postulate. If a larger weave carries content rank two lacks (m412's
handedness), the count is a choice, and the paper's GM1 row stays priced as it is now.
