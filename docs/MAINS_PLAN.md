## What counts (the reduction-first mission, P-392)

A step counts when a free input disappears and does not come back as a hidden metric, boundary, normalisation, measure,
selection rule or fitted datum. Each removal is stated before its check, sealed, and tested where no record has looked.
Fits and scans are support work. The score is inputs removed, not fits improved; a fit that needs a new input counts
against it.

## Main's steps, in order

1. **The slope α = 2/5 and its offset rule** (P-384). B1755 showed that the lines carrying the six have slope 2/5 at every
   order tested (10, 15, 20), at selected offsets (the odd tenths at 10; four of ten at 15 and at 20). First, data-free:
   what the slope does to each sector's hierarchy — **done, B1785:** the slope alone sets the leading exponents, the offset
   only prefactors; on α = 2/5 the second-generation pair is (up, down) = (3/10, 1/5), the measured 3 : 2, and on many slopes
   the pair is {x, ½ − x} with x the Higgs's twist ‖3α‖. **The closed form — done, B1788:** e(m₂/m₃) =
   ½ − (‖2q_Lα‖ + ‖2q_Rα‖)/2 in every sector and both readings (the slope acts through the channel norms; W160 verified
   and generalised); given five-ality alignment (5α ∈ ℤ), "the Higgs at the unit twist" is the measured up exponent 3/10
   restated. **The principle out of sample — B1786 (sealed):** on the lines of order 25 and 30 the slope 2/5 carries the
   six specifically (8 of 160 000) and no other slope carries them at all, the slope 1/10 included — not PASSED under the class-choosing null (on THE_BAR's gate); with W162 (orders ≤ 12,
   Im τ 1.2–12) the six select 2/5 uniquely at every order tested. **Status: the slope is selected by the data, stably
   out of sample, with one number's worth of input (e_up) — not removed.** Still open in step 1: a data-free reason for
   ‖3α‖ = 1/5 (the Higgs at the generator of SU(5)'s centre), and the offset rule (12 of 30 lines at orders 25 and 30, in
   mirror pairs; the SM seat's W164 finds the carriers recurring under b → b + 1/3 across orders; B1792 at order 60: a
   necessary condition, not a rule — the leptons are shift-invariant, the quarks are not, and m_u/m_t breaks one pair). Inputs it would remove: the slope and the offset (two).
2. **The lepton factor c = 3** (P-294). **Done, B1789 (NEGATIVE):** not derived in the frame as built. On the slope-2/5
   family the frame's second-generation lepton/down ratio is 1 at leading order, and a net fibre flux is excluded. The
   band's first-generation spread means exact c = 3 does not force D = 9. The route left is an insertion that sees the
   integer hypercharge (zero-net-degree curvature on the channel norms, or a 24-type vev): main's design task, P-397.
   Input it would remove: c.
3. **The reading rule** (P-394, with P-295 and P-365). **Done, B1790 (NEGATIVE, frame-scoped):** not derived. The coupling
   is complex-bilinear in holomorphic Higgs and matter, so no map of (τ, w) conjugates one relative to the other. The eight
   reading assignments are physically distinct. The hypercharge sign rule would cut the reading to one genuine bit, but
   it is an unearned identification (I-35). A derivation needs an antiholomorphic or Hermitian Higgs slot; that question
   is asked of the SM seat. Input it would remove: the reading.
4. **Im τ** (P-373). **Done, B1791 (NEGATIVE):** not found forced in the frame. The special points, cusp shapes and thread
   apexes are closed, and the strength-type functionals rise to the cusp. The one line-independent height, the ρ′ Higgs's
   Petersson peak at 2.8406, is set by the η-power convention and lies outside the strip (P-398). The one-loop potential
   trades Im τ for a free ratio. Im τ stays a counted continuous input.
5. **Named, not yet planned:** the horocycle and the comparison scale (no reduction may keep them hidden; the web seat may propose the horocycle as its own reduction). **Later:** the three mass scales, the gauge couplings, the Higgs sector and θ (θ̄ in the mass frame, P-323, handed to the web seat at its offer).
