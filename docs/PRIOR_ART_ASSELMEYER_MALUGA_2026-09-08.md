## 1. Overlapping — cite it (KNOWN-ADJACENT for the frame)

From the abstract, verbatim where quoted: fermions are modelled as **"hyperbolic knot complements (a complement
C(K) = S³∖(K × D²) of a knot K carrying a hyperbolic geometry)"**; bosons by **torus bundles**; the work exploits **"a close
connection to number theory (Bloch group, algebraic K-theory, quaternionic trace fields)"** of hyperbolic 3-manifolds;
3-manifolds are handled through **3-fold branched covers of S³**, fermions characterised by 3-braids; Drinfeld–Turaev
quantisation; electric charge given a topological interpretation with a spectrum; connections to Furey, Stoica, Gresnigt
and the Bilson-Thompson braid model. *As relayed:* Mostow rigidity as the justification, the figure-eight named as an
arithmetic example, Maclachlan–Reid as the source.

This is the programme's territory — one hyperbolic knot complement, its rigidity, its arithmetic — and the corpus must cite
it wherever it says "a hyperbolic knot complement as the carrier of matter" (THE_ASSEMBLY, THE_VIEW_FROM_ABOVE, the
entrance arcs B997/B1002 and their dossier).

## 2. Not overlapping — the route (the load-bearing half)

- **The gauge group.** His comes from **torus bundles** (as relayed: isotopy classes counted 1 + 3 + 8 for U(1), SU(2),
  SU(3)); ours from the **McKay correspondence** applied to the residue image of the trace field — 2T → E₆ — and then
  Wilson lines on the closings Y_n (B1278–B1304). Different objects, different mechanisms, different groups (his is the
  Standard Model's directly; ours is E₆ broken to it).
- **The selection.** As relayed, he holds the which-knot question to be *"meaningless"*; the corpus's entrance theorem
  (B997/B1002: among the metallic grammars only the golden one has a binary polyhedral shadow) is a selection principle.
- **The arithmetic route.** The abstract names quaternionic trace fields, the Bloch group and K-theory as the connection;
  as relayed, §7 closes with *"we hope to use this relation in the future"* — **the arithmetic route is flagged and not
  taken.** The corpus's route (trace field → its residue field → 2T → E₆ → the 27 → the cubic → the closings' lines) is
  not in this source.

## 3. What he has that the programme does not (stated plainly)

As relayed: a **4-dimensional construction** (Piergallini branched covers of S⁴ whose branch-set singularities are knot
and link complements), a **charge spectrum** from the Hirzebruch defect, and **numbers** — Ω_Λ, m_H, m_ν — resting on exotic
smoothness and Akbulut corks. **His programme has more physics contact than this one**: it reaches numbers by
constructions the corpus has not made; the corpus has 0 of 19. The record carries his **constructions as tools** and his
**numbers as his claims** (legitimate machinery, contested application; he calls some steps "hand-waving" as relayed) —
that separation is exactly what the status labels are for, and nothing of his enters a banked value here.

## 4. The three follow-ups, with this seat's answers

**(i) The gate question, run.** *Has anyone done trace field → McKay → E₆ for a hyperbolic knot complement?* This seat's
sweep — its own reading, the abstract above, and the corpus's literature files (`LITERATURE_GAUGE_SM_2026-07-13.md`,
`LITERATURE_SWEEP2_2026-07-13.md`, `LITERATURE_CONTEXT_2026-07-15.md`, the 2026-09-06 Higgs-bundle sweep) — finds: the
McKay correspondence 2T ↔ E₆ is classical; the 3d–3d correspondence (Dimofte–Gaiotto–Gukov; Terashima–Yamazaki) attaches
3d N = 2 theories to hyperbolic 3-manifolds through the same Bloch-group and trace-field data, with abelian gauge groups
and Chern–Simons levels from the triangulation, not E₆; Asselmeyer-Maluga names the arithmetic as a tool and takes the
torus-bundle route to the gauge group. **No source found for the conjunction.** Verdict for the route:
**NOVEL-CANDIDATE** — sharpened by the fact that the nearest neighbour flagged the route and left it — with B659's guard:
not a certificate, the specialist bar stands, and the outside-bench letter to authors should ask this question by name.

**(ii) The two threes, adjudicated.** His three generations come (as relayed) from Donaldson's theorem on the K3 surface —
the three hyperbolic summands of its intersection form 3H ⊕ 2(−E₈), b₂⁺ = 3 — a 4-manifold invariant. The corpus's three
come from the object: det(A − I) = 3 for its order-3 isometry (main's B1294 count), h¹(Y₃; 3_ρ) = 3 on the 3-fold closing
(B1273), the (27, 3) of the golden E₈ (B1271). **No shared derivation exists in either record**: a K3 surface appears
nowhere in the corpus's chain, and the object's order-3 isometry appears nowhere in his. Two coincident integers with no
map between them are two coincidences until a map is exhibited (a K3 whose order-3 automorphism has the object's closing
as its fixed-locus data would be that map, and none is known here). Registered as a fenced hint (`docs/HINT_LEDGER.md`,
H-PRIORART-THREES), **not as a convergence**, exactly as the ledger's guard on order matches requires (B1223).

**(iii) Piergallini covers at the 4d-lift wall — registered (L211).** The corpus's wall: every closed 3-manifold bounds
(Ω₃^SO = 0), so the object's closings have no canonical filling and the 4d lift is a selector problem. Piergallini's theorem
(every closed orientable PL 4-manifold is a 4-fold simple branched cover of S⁴ over a transversally immersed surface) is
an **existence** theorem for all 4-manifolds at once; Asselmeyer-Maluga's use of it — branch-set singularities that are knot
and link complements — is a construction with a chosen branch surface. **Expected verdict: the selector problem survives**
(existence is not selection); **what would overturn it:** a theorem that the object's closings force the branch surface,
or a branched cover of S⁴ whose singular set is exactly one of the tower's closings with no residual choice. Priced as a
reading (Piergallini 1995; Iori–Piergallini; the author's construction) — no computation in the corpus's toolkit decides
it. Nobody in the corpus had asked it; now it is asked, with its expected answer written down first.
