## 2. WHAT "PARAMETER-FREE" MEANS, EXACTLY

**It does NOT mean zero inputs.** One **dimensionful anchor** is required of *every* theory —
established here and universal: SI itself *defines* c, h, k_B by fiat and derives the metre. Nobody
derives the metre. **Taking one anchor is the correct end state, not a failure.**

> **Parameter-free means: the number of free DIMENSIONLESS parameters is zero.**

That makes the target **finite and countable**: the minimal Standard Model has **19** dimensionless
parameters (26 with Dirac neutrinos, 28 with Majorana phases). A parameter-free theory outputs all
of them from structure plus one anchor.
