## How THE_BAR uses it

For a result graded after 2026-10-24, THE_BAR's Bonferroni factor counts the looks in this ledger at the same target that
came before the result's seal (sealed decisive cells and the post hoc scans that informed it), across all seats. The
ledger is completed at each decadal review; a seat adds its own looks when it seals one. Its first full count (with the
rows above checked against each seat's record) is owed at the next review (P-376).
