## Part II — the one cause (the information lens)

- **The object is a finite datum.** It can fix a continuous number only as one of its own invariants: a volume, a torsion, a
  period, an L-value, a spectral number.
- **The frames add continuous moduli the object does not fix** (τ, w, the Higgs direction). So they can fit, and they cannot
  derive.
- **The record's history shows the swing:**
  - the rigid route was tried first, as numerology (object periods against ratios; the value campaign; V-3);
  - it then moved to flexible frames (the observer-coupling reorientation; modular flavour).
  - It never tried the rigid route as physics: the object as a space, with couplings as its invariants.
- **The walls are the literature's walls** (report C):
  - no published mechanism derives the Georgi–Jarlskog 3 without a hand choice ("We are unable to explain exactly the
    factors of 3", Dudas–Palti);
  - none fixes a large Im τ without a chosen ratio;
  - the target is itself soft: at the GUT scale y_μ/y_s ≈ 4.4 and D ≈ 10.6.

## Part IV — the reading

**The pattern holds on every route tried, the rigid one included.** The object (and m004's fillings) fix discrete structure
and relations among scales; they have not fixed a dimensionless coupling. Two readings remain, and the record cannot yet tell them apart:
1. **The values need a principle the record does not have** — a selection among the object's closings: FK15, the measurer,
   L241. Then every frame will fit and none will derive, until that principle is found.
2. **The values are not in the object.** Then the honest result is a theorem: the object fixes the Standard Model's
   discrete data (the group and its ℤ₆, the hypercharges, three, the hand) and leaves its
   continuous data free. The count of what is forced is itself the deliverable.
