## NOT FALSIFIABLE, AND WHY — the section that keeps the register honest

**P5, P7 and P8 are true statements about the framework and are kept — outside the
status column, here, so no reader mistakes "unfalsified so far" for evidence.** P7 and
P8 fail for one structural reason, and it is creditable rather than careless: **the
weight ledger forbids scales as a theorem** (no dimensionful prediction exists — B811,
B1012), so a prediction that needs a scale to be testable *cannot be made testable by
wording*. This is the type law (B1032) read from the falsifiability side: the object
emits relations and finite labels; a Z′ *mass window* is neither. The tension between
the programme's sharpest theorem and the testability of two of its predictions is real,
recorded, and unresolved.
