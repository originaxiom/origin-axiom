## 2. The three theorems

1. **Closed closings are vector-like (B1351).** χ(Q; L) = 0 for every local system on a closed 3-manifold, and h¹(ψ) = h¹(ψ̄) by
   Poincaré duality: every Wilson-line vacuum of the tower's closings pairs each weight of the 27 with its mirror in the 27̄.
2. **Point-localised matter cannot split doublets from triplets (B1367).** In trinification form the E₆ cubic pairs N with (H_u, H_d)
   exactly as with (D, D̄), and ν^c with (L, H_u) exactly as with (D, d^c); for any number of 27s and any coupling tensor the doublet
   and triplet mass blocks share their generation matrix, so light up-Higgs doublets equal light exotic triplets in every vacuum
   (9 300 configurations, no exception), and the light D decays the proton (B1276 §3). Only E₆-breaking acting on the matter splits
   them — a Wilson line on bulk modes does (B1302) — and fields at points never see any. Every apex design of the object is excluded,
   with or without 27̄s, whatever the line; the neutrino and proton-decay walls of B1365–B1366 are its corollaries.
3. **Bulk matter on the object is never a chiral generation with the Standard Model unbroken (B1368).** Such a connection puts its
   non-abelian part in SL(2)_β; spin-0 sectors carry a character alone, are cusp-fixed only when trivial and carry no index; the 10
   and the 5̄ of SU(5) never share a spin (27: the 10 is spin 0; 78: the 5̄ is), so one half of every generation is never chiral; and
   for the geometric representation Calegari's trace −2 of the longitude removes the spin-½ half as well. The object's twisted
   cohomology is golden — z² − 3z + 1 and z² − 4z + 1 — and its massless charged pairs sit at those roots, vector-like.

**m004 fixes E₆, the 27 and its coupling, the embedding of the Standard Model, its Wilson line, its Z′ and the shape of its textures,

## 5. What the negative teaches

Chirality in the record comes only from points (apexes) or from ends (cusps); the doublet–triplet split comes only from a Wilson line
acting on bulk matter. Points and lines never meet; on one cusp the ends and the line meet but the two halves of a generation never
share the spin that the ends can make chiral. A closing that gave the Standard Model would need chiral bulk matter on a manifold whose
ends can make both halves chiral — two cusps at least (B1291's parity theorem points the same way from the count of three).
