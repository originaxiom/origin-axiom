# THE FIFTH-FORCE QUESTION — what the record says, item by item

**What this page is.** On 2026-10-06 the owner sent the transcript of a popular-science video on a possible fifth
force and asked: "how does our program fit or answers on this … what does our math say? analyze it properly, skip
nothing." A reader swept the record topic by topic and returned verbatim quotations; main verified the load-bearing
ones and wrote this page. It banks nothing; every statement points at the arc that carries it. Items the reader
reported and main did not re-open are marked (reader).

## The answer in three sentences

The record neither predicts nor excludes a fifth force. It contains the ingredients for two specific kinds — extra
abelian gauge bosons, and one continuous scaling that may or may not be a field — and fixes no mass, coupling or range
for either. Its one worked example of a new gauge boson is, by the record's own later computation, incompatible with
the observed chirality as it stands.

## 1. Against the video's three criteria (a new field, a new carrier, effects the four forces cannot explain)

- **A new field and carrier: structurally present.** The cascade ends on su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)³, of dimension 14 against the
  Standard Model's 12: "It is the SM algebra plus two extra abelian factors" (`docs/SM_SPECIFICATION_LEDGER.md` §D1), the
  three abelian directions being exactly Y, χ, ψ (B992, reader). This is classical E₆ descent; the landing is a Levi
  subalgebra catalogued long ago (B951, reader: "not a discovery").
- **On vacua the record has two different extra bosons**, named apart in GENESIS v1.12 (B1478):
  **U1-η** on the flat closing Y₃ — the η direction, family-universal (the paper); and **U1-Z′₉** on the ninth closing —
  E₆ part (5ψ − 3χ)/2 with charges 4 on Q, u^c, e^c; −2 on d^c, L; 10 on ν^c, N; −8 on H_u, D; −2 on H_d, D̄, plus a family
  part (−10, 5, 5), family-non-universal (sm:B1283, verified in B1303). A third route reaches rank four with no extra
  abelian factor at all, as a priced choice (B1098, reader).
- **Effects the four forces cannot explain: none derivable.** The falsifier register grades "exactly two extra neutral
  gauge bosons" (P7) as untestable — "no scale can be named" — and "a new low-energy gauge interaction" (P8) as vacuous
  (`docs/FALSIFIER_REGISTER.md`, reader).
- **The worked example dies under chirality.** On full 27s every Standard-Model-commuting direction is anomaly-free, so
  that check "carries zero bits"; what can fail is the cube of the family part, and for (−10, 5, 5) it is −750 (B1340;
  recomputed in B1478). The register's own words (P9): "The programme cannot hold both the observed chirality and this
  Z′." **What chirality would force instead** is a family part of the shape (0, t, −t) — one generation neutral, two
  opposite (B1340). *Main's remark, not on the record and not checked: that is the pattern of the L_μ − L_τ type of new
  force in the literature; a sealed look is owed before anything is said about it.*

## 2. Range and strength

B1303 gives "a regime, not a value — and a fork, not a number": M_Z′/g ≳ 154 TeV from the kaon mass difference and
1,720 TeV from kaon CP violation if the generation that takes the expectation value is a light one; about 2 TeV if it

option — a collider and flavour object — not a long-range force. Kinetic mixing and running were out of that arc's
scope (reader).

## 3. A long-range, dark-energy-type force

- **The only candidate is one continuous freedom the record leaves the observer: a scaling** (B1166, OPEN). Whether it
  is a field or merely a unit is undecided (the web seat's correction of its own "B1166 verified a dilaton"). If it
  were a light field it would be exactly the video's weak cosmic-scale force, and equivalence-principle and
  solar-system tests bound such a field tightly. Registered as lead L249 (f).
- **No light scalar with a mass, range or coupling is on the record.** B1409 finds twenty real parameters on one
  five-cusped cover, "every one of them … cusp data; … zero interior moduli" (reader).
- **Dark energy.** "The object is a 3d Einstein solution with Λ = −1, exactly"; "The observed 4d value is permanently out
  of reach as a value" (`docs/THE_LAMBDA_POSITION.md`, reader). The one dynamic-dark-energy claim ever made here was
  killed ("ruled out at ~30σ", `CLAIMS.md` D3, reader). **Scope, under the owner's rule:** "out of reach" is a statement
  about the flat frame and about m004; GENESIS v1.12 says the flat frame cannot reach continuous *moduli*, and that
  the record's value-negatives "bound the frame, not physics".

## 4. Dark matter and a dark sector

- The neutral singlets are the natural candidates. Their stability was probed on the cloud seat's lane (memo 122):

  m004** (GENESIS v1.12 §8; `docs/COSMOLOGY_LEDGER.md` row 4, updated 2026-10-06).
- "Hidden sector", "dark sector", "dark photon", a force acting only on dark matter: **not found** anywhere on main or
  on the seats' lanes. The extra bosons of §1 couple to ordinary matter.
