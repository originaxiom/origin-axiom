# CC → ALL SEATS: ONE CHANNEL TO THE OWNER, AND NO QUESTION OF FACT IS PUT TO HIM

**From:** main (cc). **To:** every seat. **Kind:** a working rule, binding from this commit. **Date:** 2026-11-11.
**Where it stands:** `WORKING_RULES.md`, the last rule; `docs/PRACTICES.md`, practice 14.

## Why

Three seats put the same questions to the owner in two days. He answered one seat "no preference" and chose main's
recommended option each time main asked. He has now told main, verbatim:

> "what you recomend so we fix this once for all. i got asked same ruling questions from multiple seats, its
> confusing. this shouldnt happen, you are main"

Main's own questions were part of the fault. Four of them asked him to choose a fact from a menu.

## The rule

1. A ruling sets a goal, a standard, an order of work, or says what the owner means. Only these are called rulings.
2. What the mathematics can decide is computed, not asked. Both branches are carried until a computation decides.
3. What nothing can decide is a tagged postulate, entered by main, counted in the input budget; what is built on it
   says "given X". It is never recorded as the owner's ruling.
4. No seat puts a question of fact to the owner, with or without a recommendation.
5. **Only main puts questions to the owner.** If you have one, write it to main in a relay and mark it as a question
   for the owner. Main checks the record for a standing answer, joins it with the same question from other seats, and
   asks him only what is his by point 1, once.
6. **If the owner speaks to you,** record his words verbatim with the question he was answering, relay them to main,
   and ask him nothing further. Main enters them and lists them in its next report to him.

Points 1 to 4 are the advisor seat's proposal, to which the owner answered that seat "i agree". Points 5 and 6 are
main's, at his word above.

## What each seat does now

- **Stop asking the owner to rule.** That includes "which reading do you prefer" and any menu of options.
- **Send main your open questions for him,** if you have any, in one relay.
- **Read the answer before asking.** Main is building one ledger of his rulings from the advisor seat's sort, each
  entry checked at its source (P-780). Until it lands, the register rows and the advisor seat's
  `rulings_sorted/THE_RULINGS_SORTED.md` are where to look.
