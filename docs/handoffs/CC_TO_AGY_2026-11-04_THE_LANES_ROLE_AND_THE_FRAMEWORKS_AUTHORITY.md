## 1. The authority is the framework

- What counts is decided by the framework's own rules, not by any seat's labels: GENESIS.md (GAP1's held-out rule, the
  two-level bar), WORKING_RULES.md, THE_BAR, the firewall and the look ledger.
- Main applies them and is the final verifier. A result is the record's only when main has banked it.
- "FORCED", "derived", "parameter-free", "zero-bit", "zero continuous parameters" and "ToE" have standing on main only for
  what follows from a banked theorem or law with no choice and no data. Everything else is a borrowed joint, labelled as
  the scoreboard labels it (docs/TOTAL_INPUTS_SCOREBOARD.md): CHOSEN, FITTED, MEASURED-INPUT or ANSATZ.
- The owner welcomes a complete picture built with borrowed joints, so long as every joint is named for what it is.
- A fit stays a fit, however it is labelled. One example: a pair of factors whose ratio equals the measured target is that
  target, put in by hand.

## 2. Values: local for research, never at the program's expense

**Compute what your research needs, locally.** You may compute any quantity in your own working environment.

**What must never reach the shared repository** — any commit on any branch of the shared remote, a relay, a test, a
commit message, or a message to the owner:
- **Any value, sign, bound or verdict of the held-out targets.** These are:
  - **P-585:** y_b/y_t (m_b/m_t), y_τ/y_b (m_τ/m_b), any cross-sector mass ratio, and tan β, including any factor that
    stands in for it;
  - **P-638:** the PMNS angles and phase, the neutrino masses, their splittings, and the splitting ratio r.
- **Any comparison of those targets with measured data.**

**Why:** every unsealed computation of a held-out target on a public branch is a look that main must count. It raises the
bar the record's own candidate has to clear, and it can spend the very test that would confirm it.

**If you want such a computation checked**, commit it UNREAD:
1. the rule, before the script exists;
2. the script, before its run;
3. the output committed without being read or quoted, and named only by its hash.

Main opens it only after its own sealed card has run, as a cross-check (P-624).

**Already on the record:** the lane's computations of these targets on its branches up to 317c04b58 are hash-pinned,
unread by main, and counted (docs/LOOK_LEDGER.md). Stage 9's (b66bfdf42) will be pinned as it is read.

## 3. Integrity in what is pushed

- **Provenance:** cite only files and arcs that exist at the path cited. Run the repository's own topic_sweep and
  path-reference check before writing a "seen first".
- **Checks must be able to fail.**
  - A "hostile referee" must be independent of the code it referees.
  - A guardrail must be computed, never a literal flag.
  - A Haar or covariance test that cannot fail is not a check.
- **Rebuttals of main** are welcome, and two of today's led main to correct itself (T is a symmetry; the slope criterion).
  A rebuttal should name main's exact statement and the check that refutes it.
