## Rule (2026-11-12, the owner's instruction to main — adopted): A SELECTION IS TESTED BY CHANGING WHAT IT DECLARES

**What happened.** The record has tried many times to make the mathematics pick one thing: a thread, a least departure,
a value of the modulus, a hand, one of three. Each time the pick rested on something that had been put in. On
2026-11-12 the owner asked whether the expectation itself was wrong, asked that the answer be made central so that the
mistake is not made again, and asked that it be tested at once (register row P-831). It was tested the same hour, and
the test refuted half of main's own answer ([[ARC]]).

**What is established.**
1. **The law says what takes part and does not weigh it** (GENESIS, the principle's meaning; P-755, P-774). A number
   needs a weight.
2. **A rule that has the weave's symmetries returns whole orbits or their symmetric points, never one member.** Any
   count of letters returns mirror pairs (B1867, B1869). A read-out built from the quotient alone is greatest at one
   point only where every symmetry fixes that point (B1881). Every one of the 88 sets of least departures on record is
   closed under reversal ([[ARC]]).
3. **Rates do not escape this.** With every move acting and the acts compounding, the one sector that never cancels,
   whichever moves are counted, is the parity of the number of moves. The sector that cancels slowest is a different
   one for the two moves alone and for the moves with their inverses, at 11 of the 12 levels from 2 to 13 ([[ARC]]).

**The rule.** Before any result is called a selection (a pick, a least, a first, a survivor, a preferred value or
state) in an arc, a page, a card or a message:
- **(a) Type it.** It is one of three things.
  - A COORDINATE: a choice of representative in an orbit, on which no stated observable depends. The invariance is shown.
  - A DECLARED BIT: a choice the record makes, counted as an input.
  - A RATE OR A STATE OF THE WHOLE: a quantity the joint action defines, with every declared item it uses listed.
- **(b) List what it declares:** every item the pick uses and does not derive. The set of moves, the level or levels,
  the sense of a test, the measure, the background, the thread.
- **(c) Change each one.** For each declared item at least one other admissible value is run. A pick that moves is a
  result about that declaration. It is labelled so, as a thread result is under the weave rule, and it is not
  presented as a selection.
- **(d) Name its orbit.** The symmetries of the rule are stated, and the pick's orbit under them is given. One member
  of a larger orbit is not a selection.

A result that passes (c) and (d) for every item it declares is the only thing this record calls a selection. None is on
record on the day this rule is written.

**What the rule does not say.** It does not forbid a declared bit: a declared bit is counted, and the plan's packages
for the hand and for the root may close that way. It does not say that a rate is useless: a rate is a proper read-out,
typed with the grammar it was computed under.

**Enforcement.** Main applies (a) to (d) at intake of every result, its own included, and the readers of each periodic
review read for it. A field for it in the verdict file, and a gate that refuses a selection with no change run, are
asked of the builder with the weave discipline's schema (the plan's package WP-0.11).

## B. For GENESIS.md, under "[v1.65] WHAT THE OWNER MEANS BY THE PRINCIPLE" (a dated addition; no status changes)

**[v[[V]]] WHAT FOLLOWS FOR ANY SELECTION (main's reading, made central at the owner's direction of 2026-11-12;
register row P-831; tested in [[ARC]]).** The clauses above say that nothing is ranked and that the law does not weigh.
Three consequences are now part of how the record works, and the working rules bind every session to them:
- a pick made by a rule that has the weave's symmetries is a whole orbit or a symmetric point;
- a pick that changes when a declared item is changed (the set of moves, the level, the sense of a test, the measure)
  is a result about that declaration;
- with every move acting and compounding, what never cancels under either set of moves is the parity of the number of
  moves, and nothing else to level 13.
None of this is derived from the principle. The first two are how the mathematics on record has behaved, without
exception so far; the third is a computation.
