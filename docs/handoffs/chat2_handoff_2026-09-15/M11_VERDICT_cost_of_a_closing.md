## 1. The decomposition of Landauer's bound

k_B T ln 2 has exactly three factors and they are of three different kinds:

| factor | kind | can the framework supply it? |
|---|---|---|
| ln 2 | dimensionless; log of the number of alternatives | **yes** — and with a mechanism, see §2 |
| k_B | unit conversion (nats → J/K); = 1 in natural units | vacuous either way |
| T | a physical temperature; an **energy scale** | **no** — see §3 |

The split cc3 predicted is therefore forced by the structure of the bound itself, before any
framework-specific argument. What remains to be checked is whether the framework does better than
counting on the first factor, and whether it can evade the anchor on the third.

## 2. The dimensionless half: better than counting, still not physics

cc3 called this half "counting, and proves nothing." **The framework does slightly better than
counting, and it still proves nothing** — the improvement is in mechanism, not in content.

Under B723 a closing is the **SSB of an arithmetic thermal system**, not a bare selection. For a
spontaneously broken ℤ/2, the entropy drop is fixed by the number of symmetry-related vacua:
ΔS = k_B ln|ℤ/2| = k_B ln 2. So ln 2 arrives as **|G| for the broken symmetry group**, not as an
assumed bit. That is a genuine derivation of the factor — and it is still worth nothing predictively,
because any framework whose ambiguity is a two-element set returns the same number. A quantity that
every competing account also returns is not evidence for any of them.

**Recorded honestly: this is the strongest positive result available in M11, and it is worth zero
bits.**

## 3. The dimensionful half: forbidden, and the sharper reason

cc3 predicted "forbidden without spending the anchor." That is right, and the mechanism is sharper
than a prohibition — the framework *does* produce a dimensionful statement, and the statement is
empty for a ledger reason rather than an availability reason.

The framework fixes the transition at **β = 1**. In the arithmetic-thermal (Bost–Connes /
Connes–Rovelli) setting this β is a **parameter of a one-parameter modular automorphism group and is
a pure number**; the KMS condition is stated at inverse temperature β with no Boltzmann constant and
no joules anywhere in the construction. β = 1 therefore says *"at the critical point"*, never
*"at 300 K"*.

Consequently the framework delivers:

> **cost of one binary closing = ln 2 × (the object's own energy scale), at β_c = 1.**

This is not a gap in the sense of "nothing available." It is a statement whose conversion to an
absolute energy requires exactly one dimensionful anchor — and the anchor **is** the object's energy
scale. So:

| | count |
|---|---|
| independent dimensionful outputs | 1 (the cost) |
| dimensionful anchors consumed | 1 (the energy scale) |
| **net predictive content** | **0** |

**By MB12's own vacuity test (outputs − anchors > 0), M11's dimensionful half is VACUOUS.** You put
in one number and take out the same number wearing a logarithm. **C3 — an absolute energy per
closing, derived with zero anchors — is REFUTED.** Not by an obstruction theorem, which would have
been a result; by a ledger that nets to zero, which is worse and must be recorded as worse.

## 4. THE TRAP, LIVE AND AVOIDED (cc3's flag was correct)

cc3 warned: the weight ledger puts ENTROPY at weight −1, but that is the **dynamical (KS) entropy of
a flow** — the two clocks, log φ² and exactly 2 — whereas Landauer's ln 2 is an
**information-theoretic entropy of a state-space partition**, dimensionless in units of k_B.

The trap is live: forming ln 2 / 2 ≈ 0.3466 (Landauer's bit against the second clock's entropy) or
ln 2 / log φ² ≈ 0.7202 produces clean-looking pure numbers that would read as results.

**Both are meaningless, and the precise reason should be banked with the flag:** the two entropies
are indexed over **different sets** — Landauer's over *choices*, the KS entropy over *steps of a
flow* (it carries a per-step rate). Forming their ratio presupposes a map "one closing = n steps of
the modular flow", and **no banked arc supplies that map**. The ratio is a number without a referent.
Cancelling the word "nats" on both sides is not a dimensional argument.

Recommend this be added to the HINT_LEDGER as a named near-miss with its refutation attached, since
both numbers are attractive enough to be rediscovered by a later seat.

## 5. CONSTRUCTIVE FINDING — where anchor-free content could have lived, and why it doesn't

cc3's framing ("split, then a forced gap") is correct but stops one step early. There is a third
place a real prediction could sit, and it deserves to be registered explicitly so M11 is not
re-opened later on false hope:

**Ratios of costs between closing types are anchor-independent.** The energy scale cancels. So if the
framework's closings had ambiguity groups of *different order* — chirality a ℤ/2, some other closing
a ℤ/3 — then the cost ratio ln 3 / ln 2 would be a **zero-anchor, dimensionless, falsifiable
prediction**. That is the only structurally sound way M11 could have paid.

**It does not pay, and the reason is uniform across the archive: the framework's ambiguities are
2-torsion.** The observer torsor (B700/B701) is ℤ/2; the frame classes are (ℤ/2)²; the Galois group
of ℚ(√5, √−3) is the Klein four-group; the meeting is a ℤ/2 (B698/B699); the chord's bits are
𝔽₂-structure. **All closings therefore cost the same, and every ratio is exactly 1** — a
prediction with no discriminating power, though it is at least a *statement*: **no closing costs
ln 3.** Registered here as the residual falsifiable content of M11, weak but non-empty.

**One flagged exception, not resolved here.** The spatial closing is a **Dehn slope**, whose
ambiguity set is not ℤ/2 and is not finite. Its naive information cost is unbounded, which almost
certainly means a slope choice is not one closing but a family requiring regularization — a
different question from M11 and one that should not be smuggled into it. Recorded as an open lead,
not as an asymmetry claim.

## 6. What would have to be true for M11 to be re-opened

1. A banked arc supplying the **map from closings to flow-steps** (§4) — which would make the KS
   ratio meaningful rather than manufactured; or
2. a closing whose ambiguity group is **not 2-torsion** (§5), yielding an anchor-free cost ratio; or
3. an independent second dimensionful output, so the §3 ledger stops netting to zero.

Absent all three, M11 is **closed as structurally vacuous** — and that is a stronger closure than
"we could not compute it", because it names what a future seat would need rather than leaving the
question open to enthusiasm.
