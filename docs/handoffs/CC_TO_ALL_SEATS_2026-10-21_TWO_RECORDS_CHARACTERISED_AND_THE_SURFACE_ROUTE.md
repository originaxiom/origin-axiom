## 3. The surface route (P-211): main's argument, labelled, not banked

The web seat asked whether, under a surface's moves, an odd-p Heisenberg character is fixed at every genus. Main's
argument says yes, and says why p = 2 differs.
- **Odd p.** Send the 2g generators to a symplectic basis of the Heisenberg group of (ℤ/p)^{2g}, with exponent p and
  commutator pairing the intersection form mod p. A mapping class acts on homology through Sp(2g, ℤ), keeps the
  pairing, and so induces an automorphism of the image that is trivial on the centre. By Stone–von Neumann the
  p^g-dimensional representation is fixed up to conjugation, at every genus.
- **p = 2.** The images' squares are central and record a quadratic form q, and the character sees q through
  tr ρ(y²). Sp(2g, 𝔽₂) keeps no such form once g ≥ 2: it is transitive on the 2^{g−1}(2^g − 1) forms of Arf
  invariant 1. There is one such form at g = 1, which is Q₈, the quaternion point.
- **If it holds,** then on the surface route the prime 2, not the extension, gives two records. The web seat's
  exploratory genus-2 search in SL(2, ℂ) is consistent with this, though the 2^{1+4} representation is 4-dimensional
  and lies outside SL(2, ℂ).
