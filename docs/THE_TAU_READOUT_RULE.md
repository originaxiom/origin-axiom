# The τ read-out rule: which construction is "τ at a point of the circle" (P-714; data-free; a rule written first)

*Main, 2026-11-07, S306m, at the owner's "continue". This is a ruling on P-714's construction question, derived from
rulings the record already holds. It is written before any τ is set beside the six, and no value of the six is read

## The question

The record holds four readings of the fibre's modulus at the excursion's tube (B1846, B1847, P-723):
- the Teichmüller apex of the monodromy's axis, 0 + 3.65i at L⁶RLR (the √(40/3) value, exact, at Re 0)
  [corrected 2026-11-10, S306n: this read "½ + 3.65i at L⁶RLR (B1845; the √(40/3) value)"; Re ½ is L⁵RLR's apex,
  B1852's correction];
- the deepest pleated layer, height 2.87;
- the deepest tetrahedron's shape, height 2.58;
- the conformal modulus of the harmonic level surface through the systole, 0 + 6.149i at L⁶RLR (B1847, B1852, the web
  seat's row 1614).

Which one is the τ that a read-out at a point of the fibration circle uses?

## The rule

**τ at a point of the circle is the conformal modulus of the fibre at that point of the metric's own clock.**

It follows from three rulings already on the record:
1. **The clock is harmonic time.** The hyperbolic metric fixes it with no choice (B1846). The moduli-space clocks
   (Teichmüller arclength and the others) are CHOSEN (B1815).
2. **The fibre at time s is the level set Σ_s.** A time function's fibre is its level set.
3. **τ(s) is the conformal class of Σ_s in the metric it inherits.** No structure beyond the metric is added.

## What the rule excludes, and why

- **The Teichmüller apex.**
  - It is a point of the monodromy's axis in moduli space, parametrized by the moduli space's own geometry: a reading on
    a clock the record counts as chosen (B1815).
  - It is also a point of the model, not of a fibre of M.
  - It stays the record's (N2) reading, a declared alternative.
- **The pleated layers.**
  - They are surfaces of the canonical decomposition. They are discrete, and no time function has them as its level
    sets, so "the layer at time s" is undefined.
  - They remain a geometric description of the excursion.
- **The tetrahedron shape.** It is the cross-ratio of one ideal tetrahedron, not the conformal class of a fibre. Reading
  it as τ was an identification (P-723).

## What the rule does not decide (still CHOSEN)

- **The point or the weight.**
  - The metric supplies a canonical point: the systole, through which passes σ's fixed level, the tube (B1846, Review 76).
  - It also supplies a weight, the volume weight ρ, which peaks at the same level.
  - Reading at the point gives τ* = τ(Σ₀). Averaging gives a weighted τ. Which one the read-out uses is the choice P-726
    left open.
- **The history.**
  - The one-departure history's depth is selected by least action only under the level-5 charge premise (B1853: the run
    of 6, by length and by volume alike).
  - The premise, and minimisation as the use of the action, stay chosen.
  - The k → ∞ limit removes the number of golden periods, so k is not chosen.

**The count.** The construction is no longer a menu item: of P-714's four, one survives these rulings. Three choices
remain: point or weight, the charge premise at 5, and minimisation. The last two act jointly, through the depth.
[2026-11-10: the count is four, and the surviving reading is conditional on the framing; see the dated note below.]
