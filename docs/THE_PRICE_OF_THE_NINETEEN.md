# THE PRICE OF THE NINETEEN — what the field has bought of the Standard Model's free parameters, at what price

*Opened 2026-10-17 by the referee lane (B1668, sealed ad3dd5d44). The page for readers; the numbers are the sealed
instrument's (`frontier/B1668_the_price_of_the_nineteen/verification/the_price_of_the_nineteen.json`), recomputed
in-sandbox from PDG 2024 and Huang–Zhou 2021 at every landing. A row is a relation among the nineteen that the
literature has claimed; its price is what it assumes beyond the Standard Model; its status is against today's data
under the sealed rubric (HOLDS |pull| ≤ 2; APPROXIMATE within 10%; FAILS; FIT; STRUCTURE). "Buys" means: HOLDS or
APPROXIMATE without a fitted continuous parameter, one degree of freedom per independent equation.*

**The measured state (B1668):** six of the nineteen degrees of freedom are bought — one gauge relation, m_H, m_s from
Georgi–Jarlskog, m_τ from Koide, θ_C, θ̄ — never for free except Koide, never more than two by one framework, and
thirteen by nothing. This is the comparison class for any claim about the nineteen, the program's included: a
structure that bought one dof at a GUT's price would be average for the field; three would be the best anyone has.

## How to read it

- A HOLDS at a price is a relation, not an explanation: unification holds given a group and a content nobody has
  seen; Koide holds given nothing and is understood by nobody.
- The Georgi–Jarlskog rows are graded at M_Z; the relation lives at M_GUT, and the Yukawa part of the running moves
  the double ratios by −12% to +14% between the two scales (the factual review's E2, from Antusch–Hinze–Saad 2025). In
  the MSSM at M_GUT the first-generation relation would be APPROXIMATE and buy m_d (T1 7, SUSY GUT 3). Antusch and
  Spinrath (2009) found the Clebsch 3 disfavoured against 9/2 or 6 for the same reason. A successor arc grades at M_GUT.
- The nineteen are renormalised couplings; every relation here is tested at a scale (M_Z, 2 GeV, pole, or M_X by
  one-loop running). Koide is the one relation that holds at a scale (the pole) and fails at another (M_Z): a fact
  about pole masses that no symmetry at a high scale explains.
- The weave (B1669): its parameter-free sector buys nothing (m₂/m₁ ≥ ½ and θ_C = 0 for every τ), and its strong CP
  angle is π·Re τ. On this ledger the program's structure would enter with zero rows bought; that is the honest
  comparison the referee's report 03 asked for.
