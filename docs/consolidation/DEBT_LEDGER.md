### Two limitations, declared rather than assumed

1. **The `absent` side proves nothing about content.** A consolidation may carry an arc's result
   under a different arc's name. Absence of a citation is absence of a *pointer*, not of a fact.
2. **The `present` side is untested, and can overstate in the other direction.** A citation may
   be a passing mention that does not carry the law. Testing it needs body reads, so it stays a
   stated limitation.

**Consequence for the rows below: a row is a CANDIDATE, not a verdict**, until its body is read
(campaign step 4). Four have been.
