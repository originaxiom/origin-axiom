## The answer in one paragraph

There is no path to the full Standard Model with its 19 numbers in sight, and the record's own theorems say why: the object fixes
priors, not points. There is a path to the Standard Model's qualitative content: the gauge group, three chiral generations and a
Higgs. It needs three bridges the record does not have, and two of them are blocked by theorems in the frames the record uses. The
ingredients are present as structure and as menus. The derivations that would turn them into the physical Standard Model are what
is missing.

## The three missing bridges

1. **A physical vacuum that carries a chiral generation** (I-26, unearned).
   - Closed spaces are vector-like (B1351).
   - In the Pantev–Wijnholt frame, which comes from M-theory, every one-cusped member is a theorem-zero (B1374).
   - The backgrounds where main's index fires are non-semisimple and non-unitary. They have no harmonic metric (the Corlette–Donaldson
     fence, B1378), so they are not supersymmetric vacua.
   - A free cusp carries its count with a continuum (B1392).
   - A completed cusp point's chirality is an input (B1500, B1504), and no known local G₂ model supplies a chiral one (B1501–B1505).
2. **Three families in one vacuum.** The record's three is an orbit of vacua. One configuration would need structure group E₆ ≀ ℤ/3,
   or a parent carrying three different SL(2)_β holonomies, and the record has neither (B1506 §5).
3. **The values.** The record's torsor pattern (THE_FORCED_AND_THE_FREE; B1225) says the object fixes the prior, not the point. Even a
   perfect qualitative model would have its couplings set by data the object does not contain.
