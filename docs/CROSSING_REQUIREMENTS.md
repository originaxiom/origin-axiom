## 5. THE INSTRUMENT PRINCIPLE — what is priced, and what is free

Building or refining an instrument is not itself a crossing and spends nothing. The welded coupling
instrument (B593/B856/B1011), the Listening Protocol, and the listener-map problem statement
(`docs/LISTENER_MAP_SPEC.md`) all carry `instrument: true` in the corpus's own vocabulary
(`docs/PRACTICES.md`'s arc-verdict rules) — **methodology, never a physics claim**
(`docs/LISTENING_PROTOCOL.md`'s own header: *"A METHODOLOGY instrument… NOT a physics claim"*).
What is priced is only the sealed act of pointing a finished instrument at measured data.

That price has two independent bills, and **a crossing prereg pays both, in full, with N/A written
as its own row where an item does not apply** — the binding rule from
`docs/INPUT_COMPLETENESS_LEDGER.md`'s 2026-08-13 reconciliation: this document's R1–R12, and the
Input-Completeness Ledger's own twelve items (scheme, scale, uncertainties, multi-modal fits,
convention constants, fit-vs-direct, look-elsewhere, the matched null, the MB13 grep, the firewall
question, source freshness, sealing). The reconciliation found eight of the ledger's twelve items
already covered by an R-number and **four genuine gaps that no R-item mandates** (scheme, multi-
modal fits, fit-vs-direct, the matched null's hit-branch) — gaps this document does not fill and
does not pretend to; they ride the Input-Completeness Ledger alone. Together the two lists are the
programme's **measurement price-list**: nothing about the instrument is charged; everything about a
data contact is, and every charge so far levied against the coupling channel's rows has come back
MISS (§4).

## 6. PROTOCOL — the certification envelope

Any crossing prereg sealed, or verdict banked, while a certifying suite is running additionally
satisfies **the certification envelope** (`WORKING_RULES.md` §CE; B1101, Review 47's methodology
delta): the working tree is read-only by convention — landings stage in the scratchpad and land by
explicit filename at bank time, never a glob, never a new arc dir mid-suite; pre-commit gate checks
run on the STAGED state; the prereg hash and every verdict digest enter by command substitution,
never retyped, with the `seal-digests` gate recomputing all of them at gate time (latest-row-per-
path; corrections-by-append supersede); on a tree collision mid-suite, fold forward into one commit,
one suite — never a stale certificate re-run. **This governs HOW a crossing lands, not whether it
should seal; R1–R12 remain the only criteria for that.**
