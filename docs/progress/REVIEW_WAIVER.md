# Review waiver (GOVERNANCE §15, amendment 2026-10-03; gate `review-fires`, B1461)

Past twice its period — 40 merges on main's first-parent line since the last review's anchor — the decadal review is
not a report but a block: the push fails. The owner, and only the owner, may waive it here, one line per waiver:

    - waived <YYYY-MM-DD> by the owner through <N> merges: <reason>

A waiver covers while the merge count since the last anchor is below N. Lines are appended, never edited; the review
that follows a waiver quotes it.
