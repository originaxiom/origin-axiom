## 1. What the tower is

The object is the figure-eight knot complement, fibred over the circle with fibre the punctured torus F and monodromy
φ: a ↦ a²b, b ↦ ab (abelianised Φ = [[2,1],[1,1]], Alexander polynomial Δ = t² − 3t + 1). Its **closings** are the n-fold
cyclic branched covers Y_n of S³ along the knot — the Fibonacci manifolds, closed hyperbolic for n ≥ 4 — and B1303 showed
that π₁(Y_n) = ⟨a, b | φⁿ(a) = a, φⁿ(b) = b⟩, the fixed quotient of the monodromy. Their first homology is the Alexander
module at the n-th roots of unity:

| n | H₁(Y_n) | order |
|---|---|---|
| odd | ℤ[φ]/(L_n) ≅ (ℤ/L_n)² | L_n² (Lucas) |
| even | ℤ[φ]/(√5 F_n) ≅ ℤ/F_n ⊕ ℤ/5F_n | 5F_n² (Fibonacci) |

The deck transformation acts on H₁ as multiplication by φ² (the golden ratio squared); the **half-deck** Ψ = [[1,1],[1,0]]
acts as multiplication by φ. Everything below is about which characters ψ: H₁(Y_n) → ℂ* carry a class, h¹(Y_n; ψ) = 1,
because the Wilson lines of the E₈ theory on Y_n (B1278) are valued in these characters and the matter they keep is
counted by them.

## 2. The criterion (B1303)

For a non-trivial character ψ, with x_k = ψ(a)^{F_{2k+1}} ψ(b)^{F_{2k}} (F the Fibonacci numbers):

> h¹(Y_n; ψ) = 1 ⟺ A(x_{n−1}) ⋯ A(x₁) A(x₀) = I, A(x) = [[1 + x, x²], [1, x]]; otherwise h¹ = 0.

No character has h¹ ≥ 2. The product P is unipotent on every non-trivial character (rank(P − I) ≤ 1, det P = 1; B1304),
so h¹ is the vanishing of a rank-one nilpotent. Validated against the Reidemeister–Schreier Fox calculus at every level
2 … 13 and on Y₂₀'s 41-part, on the alphabets and lines of Y₉ and Y₁₂, and by homomorphism counts of the presentations.
Y₂₁'s 599 million characters take 45 minutes; Y₂₄'s 10¹⁰ are not needed (§4).

## 3. The law (B1301, corrected and completed in B1303, B1304)

**Odd part.** A character of odd order N has h¹ = 1 iff (i) it is an eigencharacter of the half-deck, ψ∘Ψ = ψ^u with u a
root of x² − x − 1 modulo N; (ii) N is prime to 5 (the ramified prime of ℤ[φ], where the eigenvalue of the deck is −1:
the lens space Y₂'s characters, at 0 on every even level); (iii) Ψⁿ acts on ψ by a global sign, uⁿ ≡ ±1 mod N — not +1 at
one prime and −1 at another; (iv) that sign is + when n is even, i.e. since Ψⁿ = t^{n/2} the character is pulled back from
Y_{n/2}. So **new odd support arises only at odd levels**, at the primes where the roots of Δ have exact order n, and the
odd support of an even level is Y_{n/2}'s. Exact at every level 2 ≤ n ≤ 21 (Y₁₁, Y₁₃ predicted before computed; Y₁₅'s
mixed characters decided by (iii); Y₂₀'s 41-part by (iv), confirmed independently).

**2-adic part.** The family characters (order 2, the three sign characters of the descended 3_ρ, present at every level
divisible by 3, from the flat Y₃) multiply the odd support into the support at odd n. New 2-adic support exists at
**three levels only: 3 (order 2), 6 (24 of order 8, carrying one generation), 12 (96 of order 16, letters)** — Y₁₈ and Y₂₄
pull back and add nothing. (6 and 12 are the indices at which the Fibonacci numbers have no primitive prime divisor;
registered, not claimed.)

## 6. The doublet–triplet problem on the tower

On Y₉ every tree-level flat direction leaves generation g's light Higgs pair together with generation g's light
colour-triplet pair (B1283), and no Wilson line removes a D (B1300). On Y₁₂ the line thins D to one generation, and on
**768 lines** (six patterns: D in generation g₀, D̄ in g₁, N only in g₂; 64 deck orbits; the lines whose image is the whole
2-torsion (ℤ/16)²) the flat direction ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ with the flavon pair {g₀g₁} leaves **exactly one light triplet pair —
generation g₂'s own d^c — and its three light doublet pairs: one vector-like generation with two Higgs doublets and no
exotic triplet** (B1302). Its gauge group at tree level is SM × U(1)² (SM × U(1)_Z′ with the ν^c VEV), and its light
generation has vanishing tree-level Yukawas (the family tensor's zero diagonal, B1271). And with ⟨N_{g₂}⟩ alone (no
flavon VEV) the same lines carry **three generations of Q, u^c, d^c, L, e^c, ν^c, one Higgs pair and no exotic — the
MSSM's field content with right-handed neutrinos, vector-like** — with rank-2 Yukawas through the light Higgs (generation
g₂ massless, g₀ and g₁ degenerate; the three-generation reading, B1302's addendum). Y₂₄ repeats Y₁₂; no odd closing thins
the triplet. **This is the closest the tower comes to the Standard Model's field content, and it is Y₁₂'s.**

## 7. What the tower does not do

- **Chirality.** Every closing is closed, so every spectrum is vector-like and the net count is zero (B1260; the net
  count is −χ(∂⁺M), B1290, and a closed manifold has no boundary). The chirality map (B1280) stands unchanged.
- **Three chiral generations.** Three light generations exist (⟨N_{g₂}⟩ alone on the one-triplet lines), but each with
  its mirror, and with one massless generation and two degenerate at tree level; the hierarchy is not the tower's.
- **Numbers.** 0 of 19. Every scale is a modulus; the light generation is massless at tree level.
