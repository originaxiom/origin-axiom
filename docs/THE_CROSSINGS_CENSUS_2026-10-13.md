## 2. What the failures specify

1. **Every crossing identified by fiat.** Each attempt read a number of the object (a trace, a period, a phase, a
   probability) as a physical number. The record priced that step as the master identification I-13, UNEARNED
   (B1241). The seals killed each identification cleanly. The failures are failures of the dictionary, not of the
   structure: the chain from the principle to the SM skeleton has zero retroactive designer freedom (B1028, COMPRESSION).
2. **The admissible lanes are almost spent** (B1020, the KIND_TABLE).
   - The coupling channel to the mixing sector is the only fully open lane. Its tones, probability and phases rows are
     consumed (B1066, B1027 + B1063); only the "mirror" row remains, and B1349 advised not spending it.
   - The pair channel to mass ratios is CLOSED until a frame-invariant residue is exhibited. The frame is the choice
     among the Hermitian structures (B936's torsor); in field terms it is the kinetic (Kähler) normalisation of the
     generations.
3. **What survives** (B926): a crossing must be boundary-relational — exact dimensionless arithmetic against measured
   *shape* data, with no scale output, no object-owned running and no fermions put in by hand, under forced rules only.
4. **Physics crosses through a field, not by identification.** The geometry's zero modes give couplings by overlap, a
   potential fixes the field's state, and only then is a number compared. No crossing on the record has done this.
