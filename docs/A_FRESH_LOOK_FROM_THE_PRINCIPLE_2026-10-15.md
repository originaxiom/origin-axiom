## 1. From nothing, in five steps

1. **The principle** (GENESIS PF1–PF3, POSTULATED). Existence is what remains when cancelling to nothing cannot
   complete. The remainder is an inexhaustible description, and the programme reads it as structure. PF1's
   mathematical reading is the fibre's commutator trace κ = tr[a, b] ≠ 2: the puncture is where the cancellation
   fails.
2. **The grammar** (GENESIS GM1–GM4). The grammar has two records (ℤ²), two unit shears L and R, and aperiodicity,
   which makes every closed word hyperbolic. Words are carried as mapping classes of the once-punctured torus. The
   moves generate SL(2, ℤ) acting on the records.
3. **The weave** (the owner's rule). It is every thread, each a closed word, and the joint action of every move on the
   shared fibre. What that action forces is derived:
   - the three even spin structures, permuted transitively with none distinguished (SL(2, 𝔽₂) ≅ S₃): the flavour
     three;
   - the common point, 2T, at level 3;
   - the null triplet T = (c_i θ₂², c_j θ₄², c_k θ₃²), null by Jacobi at every τ, with its modular symmetry.
4. **The field stage** adds the rest, priced at 4 + 9 = 13 inputs:
   - τ;
   - the zero modes given Λ;
   - a Higgs;
   - a flat line with the 6Y dictionary;
   - the end condition and the cusp's unit;
   - M₁,₂, the read-out and the gauge group.

   It buys 0 of 19.
5. **The boundary is located.** The principle reaches discrete data: the three, signs, the triplet, the possibility
   of chirality. It stops before values, and the record says where and why (§2c).

## 3. What we keep missing: the arena

Every value on the record is read in one of two places:
- **at a point,** τ = ω or i: the field stage, B1617, B1656–B1658 and W94;
- **along one thread:** chat1's thread frame, whose couplings are cycle integrals along one closed geodesic.

THE WEAVE asks for all threads at once. The record has kept that as a group action on the fibre, which is not a place
where threads can meet. There is such a place, and it is the principle's own.

### 3.1 The space where every thread exists at once (weave)

- **The space.** Place the two records in the plane as a lattice of area one. The space of all such placements is
  SL(2, ℝ)/SL(2, ℤ), the unit tangent bundle of the modular surface. It is classical that this space is the
  complement of the trefoil knot in the 3-sphere (Milnor 1971, §10).
- **The threads are its closed orbits.** Its geodesic flow has exactly the threads as closed orbits. A thread's word
  in L and R is its orbit's cutting sequence (Series 1985).
- **They are braided on one template.** Ghys (2007) proved that these orbits, the "modular knots", are the Lorenz
  knots. The Lorenz template has two bands, and its bands are the two moves.
- So the weave is literal: every thread is a knot in one 3-manifold, braided with every other thread on a two-band
  template whose bands are L and R.

### 3.2 The threads interact: they link (weave; computed here)

- **Every two threads link.** On all 69 threads to length 8, every one of the 2 346 pairs links positively, with
  linking numbers from 1 to 15 (`the_arena_check.json`, A7, by the Lorenz braid of Birman and Williams).
- **The numbers do not change when the letters are exchanged,** the template's own symmetry.
- **This is a weave quantity in the rule's sense.** It exists only for pairs: it is defined by the threads' joint
  placement, and no thread has it alone.
- **It gives mathematics to the owner's question.** The question was whether threads, or whole weaves, interact
  (relay §132). In the arena they do, by linking.

### 3.3 Each thread links the cusp, and the record already computed it (weave)

- **Linking with the trefoil.** The trefoil itself is the set of degenerate lattices, the cusp. A thread's linking
  number with it is Rademacher's function Ψ (Ghys 2007).
- **The formula, checked.** On the record's words Ψ = #R − #L. It holds on all 745 threads to length 12 (A1, from
  Dedekind sums).
- **The record had this number without its meaning.** The recognition campaign's R1 (2026-07-03) found Rademacher's
  Φ(RᵐLᵐ) = 3 on the metallic family and banked it as "a new small exact datum". Since Φ = Ψ + 3 there, the datum
  says that balanced threads do not link the cusp.
- **The one-dimensional multipliers are linking numbers.** Rademacher's Φ also governs the multiplier of Dedekind's
  η, and Atiyah (1987) gave seven definitions of this one invariant. On threads, Ψ modulo 12 is the abelianisation of
  SL(2, ℤ), which sends R to 1 and L to −1. So the multipliers of η's powers around a thread are its linking number
  with the cusp, read modulo 12 or 24.

## 4. The weave's natural values: traces by discriminant

**The rule.** For each discriminant D:
- take every thread of discriminant D, or every CM point when D < 0;
- sum a modular-invariant quantity over them: along each closed geodesic for a thread, and at the point with weight
  1/∣stabiliser∣ for a CM point.

It meets THE WEAVE's three conditions. No thread is hand-picked. The quantity is defined by the joint action. The
claim, the generating function over D, is about all threads at once.

**Known mathematics shows that these sums are the natural objects.**
- **CM points.** Zagier (2002): the traces of j over the CM points of each D < 0 are the coefficients of a form of
  weight 3/2.
- **Threads.** Duke, Imamoḡlu and Tóth (2011): the traces of j's cycle integrals over the closed geodesics of each
  D > 0 are the coefficients of a mock modular form of weight ½, whose shadow is Zagier's form.
- **Cusp forms.** Shintani (1975) and Kohnen–Zagier (1981): the traces are coefficients of forms of half-integral
  weight, and their squares give central L-values.
- **The limit.** Duke (1988): as D grows the traces equidistribute, so in the limit no point is charged. That is the
  record's own finding (B1629; relay §41.4).

**The record's three places are the first terms of this sequence:**
- D = −3: ω, where the field stage sits;
- D = −4: i, P-173's other symmetric point;
- D = 5: the root alone, where chat1's thread frame was read first.

D = 12 is the one closed geodesic of LLR and LRR, through ω. So the record has computed three terms of a sequence it
never assembled, one term at a time. Half-integral weights also appear on the record's side: W91's twisted coupling
vector has weight −½. Whether the record's objects are traces in this sense is the question of N2 below.
