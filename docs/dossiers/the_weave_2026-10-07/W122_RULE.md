## Why

- **W120's scope gap.** Every thread is the space of an exact 4d vacuum universe, the Milne cone (W120, A1 and A2).

    J. Differential Geom. 89 (2011) 1). But those theorems are stated for closed spatial sections, and the threads
    are cusped.
  - So W120's statement that the tick's universe is least was a scope-limited READING.
- **The closed universes.** Dehn filling the thread's one cusp gives closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds, inside the
  theorems' scope.
  - Their volumes are below the thread's and rise to it as the slope grows (Thurston).
  - **A filling is a choice:** a slope, closing the thread's cusp. The principle does not select one, and this arc
    takes every slope in a stated box.
