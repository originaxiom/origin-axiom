# W147 — what assigns the Higgs pair? ## Why

- **The question on record.** W143 found one Higgs line led by the non-null eigenline in each three-generation
  twisted space, F₋₆ in h(12, 1) and F₋₂ in h(20, 1). The web seat's TWO_HIGGS took H_u = F₆ from h(12, 1) and
  H_d = F₂₂ from h(20, 1), a choice made by the data. W146 and the web seat's reading test leave two independent
  doublets as the natural reading (relay §248). Nothing on record says which space each doublet takes, or whether the
  weave can tell them apart.
- **The owner approved this in order** ("lets go for all systematically", 2026-10-24).

- **The pairs.** All ten unordered pairs {A, B} of the four spaces, the same space twice included.
- **The conditions on a pair {h(r, n), h(r′, n′)}:**
  - **(a)** both are three-generation spaces at one common condition;
  - **(b)** T and S allow a holomorphic pair term, r + r′ ≡ 0 mod 24 (χ_η^{r + r′} trivial);
  - **(c)** they allow a Hermitian mixing term, r ≡ r′;

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **P1 holds.** Of the pairs the admissible spaces offer, only both doublets in h(12, 1) meet a common
  three-generation condition and allow a pair term. That holds whether the term is holomorphic or Hermitian.
  - TWO_HIGGS's pair has a common condition, but T and S forbid every pair term between its two doublets.
  - Whether a pair term is required is not decided here, so this is a conditional selection, not an assignment.
- **P2 holds.** Under GENESIS PF1-W the Higgs pair is never W132's absorber. If the moves are gauged, their SU(2)
  remainder goes to τ's counterterm or to a sign twist on one doublet of matter.
- **P3 holds.** The weave assigns no order to the pair: nothing at weave level tells H_u from H_d. A thread's line of
  order N ∤ 6 can, so any assignment is a thread's choice, as the hypercharge split is (W140).
- **Together: what assigns the Higgs pair is not the weave.** At most the weave selects the space, h(12, 1) for both,
  if a pair term is required. The order, and the line within the space, stay choices.
- **Any cell fails:** the failure is the finding, and it is diagnosed before anything else is said.
- **This is data-free.** It is not a test against data, and not a trial.
