## The two carriers (fixed now)

The record supplies two natural ways to put its 3d objects into four dimensions. This arc computes both and chooses
neither. Which is physical, the records' torus as space or as an internal fibre, is a dictionary choice (GENESIS FK11).

### Carrier A: the cone, the thread as space

- **The construction.** For a thread M with its hyperbolic metric g, take the four-dimensional metric
  −dt² + a(t)² g on M × (0, ∞).
- **By hand (to be checked).**
  - For a 3d metric with Ric_g = 2κ g, the warped metric has R_tt = −3ä/a and R_ij = (aä + 2ȧ² + 2κ) g_ij.
  - So the cone a = t is Ricci-flat exactly when κ = −1, the record's own normalization (Λ = −1 in 3d).
  - With κ = −1, a = sinh t gives Ric = 3g₄, a de Sitter universe in its open slicing, and a = sin t gives
    Ric = −3g₄, anti-de Sitter in its open slicing.
- **What it would mean.**
  - Every thread is then, with no choice and no free number, the spatial section of an exact 4d vacuum universe: the
    flat Milne universe, an open (k = −1) Friedmann vacuum, on which gravitons propagate.
  - The one free quantity is the scale (t), the record's one scale input.
- **The 4d dynamics' energy** (a READING, cited, not computed). For closed hyperbolic spatial sections, the Milne
  universe is a stable attractor of the 4d vacuum Einstein flow (Andersson and Moncrief, J. Differential Geom. 89
  (2011) 1–47). There the reduced Hamiltonian is proportional to the hyperbolic volume (Fischer and Moncrief).
  - The threads are cusped (finite volume, one cusp), and these theorems are stated for closed manifolds. For a
    cusped thread the cone is still an exact vacuum solution, but its stability is not covered.
  - The volume is computed below as a census; its reading as an energy carries this scope.

### Carrier B: the fibre, the records' torus inside four dimensions

- **The construction.** The records' torus is the fibre of a four-dimensional space over a two-dimensional base, with
  its shape τ a field on the base. The Euclidean metric on (z = u + iv; x, y), with x and y periodic, is
  e^φ |dz|² + (1/Im τ) |dx + τ dy|².
- **By hand (to be checked; Greene, Shapere, Vafa and Yau, Nucl. Phys. B 337 (1990) 1).** This metric is
  Ricci-flat when τ(z) is holomorphic and e^φ = Im τ · |h(z)|² with h holomorphic and nowhere zero.
- **The Lorentzian form** (a READING, cited). In 4d vacuum gravity with two commuting spacelike Killing fields (the
  Gowdy class, Ann. Phys. 83 (1974) 203), the torus's shape satisfies the wave-map equation into the hyperbolic plane.
  Its two components are the two polarizations of the gravitational waves.
  - **So in that sector the 4d gravitons are waves of τ on the τ-line, the record's own modulus.** The moves L and R,
    acting on the fibre, are that sector's large diffeomorphisms.
- **What the moves force (by hand, to be checked).** The 4d metric must be invariant under the moves acting on τ and on
  (x, y).
  - Im τ transforms as Im τ/|cτ + d|², and |η(τ)|⁴ as |cτ + d|²|η(τ)|⁴.
  - So h = η(τ)² up to a holomorphic invariant, and the base factor is Im τ · |η(τ)|⁴.
- **The pinched record.** Near a point where the fibre pinches (τ → i∞, τ ≈ (1/2πi) log z, monodromy R), |η(τ)|⁴
  behaves as |z|^{1/6}.
  - The regular metric divides by |z|^{1/6}, the 1/6 power of Δ's simple zero, so it uses Δ/q, which is Δ times a
    simple pole at the cusp. A READING: that is W47's unit (the trivial bundle λ¹² extended by Δ⁻¹), here inside a 4d
    metric.
  - Each pinch is a cosmic string with deficit angle 2π/12 at large distance.
  - By Gauss–Bonnet, 24 strings close the base into a sphere (K3), and 12 leave a flat cylindrical end (the rational
    elliptic surface).
- **The moves count the units.** The moves generate SL(2, ℤ), whose abelianization is ℤ/12.
  - R maps to 1 and L to −1, and every unit's monodromy is a conjugate of R or of L.
  - So on a closed base the units' signed count is a multiple of 12.
- **Compactness forces units.** A compact Riemann surface has no non-constant holomorphic map to the open modular
  curve. So every closed torus-fibred solution with non-constant τ has pinched records, 12χ(O_S) of them: 12 for the
  rational elliptic surface (χ = 1) and 24 for K3 (χ = 2).
