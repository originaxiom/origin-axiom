## The objects

- **2I.** The binary icosahedral group: the 120 unit quaternions of 2T together with the even permutations of
  (0, ±1, ±φ, ±φ⁻¹)/2. It is built by closure in floating point, identified to 10⁻⁹, with an integer multiplication
  table.
- **Its automorphisms.** Aut(2I) is found by brute force from a pair (s, t) with s² = t³ = (st)⁵ = −1. Every pair of
  images that satisfies the same relations and generates 2I is one automorphism. Inn(2I) is the conjugations.
- **Q₈, the positive control,** is built the same way.
- **Epi(F_n, G).** The n-tuples that generate G, decided by the maximal subgroups: a tuple generates G exactly when no
  maximal subgroup contains all of it. The maximal subgroups come from the subgroups generated by pairs, since every
  finite subgroup of SU(2) is generated by two elements.
- **The moves.**
  - **Full**, as main's design has it: every elementary Nielsen move a_i ↦ a_i a_j^{±1} and a_i ↦ a_j^{±1} a_i
    (i ≠ j), every inversion a_i ↦ a_i⁻¹, and the transpositions of the letters.
  - **Transvections only**, without inversions or permutations.
  - **At n = 2, also the record's ⟨L, R⟩:** L is a ↦ a, b ↦ ab; R is a ↦ ab, b ↦ b.
  - A move acts on a tuple by composition: (ρ∘φ)(a_i) is φ(a_i) evaluated on the tuple.
- **The two criteria.**
  - **K, the class.** The class of ρ in Epi(F_n, G)/Aut(G). "K fixed" means ρ∘φ lies in ρ's class for every move φ.
  - **X, the character.** The trace function of ρ through the inclusion G ⊂ SU(2). For n = 3 it is read on the seven
    traces of a₁, a₂, a₃, a₁a₂, a₁a₃, a₂a₃ and a₁a₂a₃; for n = 2, on those of a, b and ab. "X fixed" means every move
    keeps these traces.
- **A common point** is a class (K) or a character (X) fixed by every move of the set named.

## The cells, with predictions and priors

- **A0, the controls.** Prior 97%.
  - 2I has 120 elements and centre {±1}.
  - Its maximal subgroups are 21: five of order 24, six of order 20 and ten of order 12.
  - |Aut(2I)| = 120 and |Inn(2I)| = 60. Aut(2I) acts freely on Epi(F_n, 2I) (checked).
  - Q₈: |Aut(Q₈)| = 24, |Epi(F₂, Q₈)| = 24 in one class, and |Epi(F₃, Q₈)| = 336 in 14 classes (main's count).
- **H, two routes to the counts.** Prior 95%. |Epi(F_n, G)| from the enumeration equals Hall's formula
  Σ_H μ(H, G)|H|ⁿ on the subgroup lattice, for G = 2I and Q₈ and n = 2, 3.
- **P, the positive control at n = 2.** Prior 99%. Q₈'s single class is fixed by every full move and by L and R, and
  so is its character, (0, 0, 0).
- **N2, 2I at n = 2.** Prior 90%. No class and no character of Epi(F₂, 2I) is fixed by every move, under the full
  moves or under ⟨L, R⟩.
- **C1, 2I at n = 3, the conjecture's open case.**
  - **C1-K.** No class of Epi(F₃, 2I)/Aut(2I) is fixed by every full move. Prior 97%.
  - **C1-X.** No character is fixed by every full move. Prior 98%.
  - **C1-T.** Neither is fixed by every transvection alone. Prior 95%.
- **C1-Q, Q₈ at n = 3** (the solvable case, as a check on Dunwoody). No class fixed by every full move. Prior 95%.
- **O, the orbits.**
  - **O-full.** The full moves act transitively on Epi(F₃, 2I)/Aut(2I): one orbit, the Gilman-type statement.
    Prior 70%.
  - **O-T.** The transvections alone are transitive too. Prior 60%.
  - **Reported.** The number of orbits and their sizes, at n = 2 and n = 3, for both move sets and both groups.

## What the outcomes mean

- **If C1 holds,** the binary icosahedral group gives no common point at n = 3. With Dunwoody's theorem for the
  solvable groups, P-205's conjecture holds at n = 3 for every finite non-abelian subgroup of SU(2), on the K and X
  readings both. Two records would then be forced by the existence of a common point (given SU(2) and a finite image,
  still priced), and the triplet would follow from the record's T2.
- **If C1-K holds and C1-X fails, or the reverse,** the conjecture depends on the reading. That is exactly the point
  of §162's class-or-character question, and it is reported as such.
- **If C1 fails,** the instrument names the class, the character and the moves that fix it. Two records is then a
  choice at n = 3, and the multiplicity is named.
- **O** says whether the moves mix every generating triple of 2I. If they are transitive, no fixed point can exist once
  there are two classes or more. That is the mechanism, not a separate claim.
