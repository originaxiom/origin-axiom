   W72's Y (normalised residual 0.0024-0.068, median 0.024, over 560 admitted threads), so the hybrid cannot detect a
   dependence on Y. With each thread's K fixed, a random complex symmetric Y gives median m2/m3 at 0.9-6% of the
   thread's own (the root x0.0086, about 0.003 instead of 0.369); a random real symmetric Y about x0.010 at the root;
   perturbing W72's entries by factors in [1/2, 2] moves m2/m3 by x0.26-3.75. The hybrid drifts to x0.77-1.64 at
   lengths <= 8 and x0.70-2.39 at lengths 9-12. What survives: K carries most of the trend with height (a generic Y
   still falls 4.8 of the 5.4 decades from LR to LLLLR); Y's shape sets the level, including the root's 0.37.
