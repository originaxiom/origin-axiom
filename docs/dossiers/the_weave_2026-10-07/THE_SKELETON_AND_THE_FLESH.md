## The vision (a READING, from relay §169 and the conversation of 2026-10-21)

- **The pattern on record.** The principle forces integers and structure, and every real number has missed:
  - integers and structure: two records, three, alike, odd chirality, Q₈, and the exponent patterns of W68;
  - real numbers: W53's χ² 1887, W57's 32σ, W68's double ratio 15, W69's best χ² 100.
- **The weave symmetrises.** Its joint action gives scalars and symmetric points (W107, W59–W62). The asymmetry lives
  in departures from those points.
- **"Forced to choose, not forced which"** is the shape of spontaneous breaking. The nineteen may be data of where the
  world sits near a symmetric point, not of the law.
- **So the goal splits in two.**
  - **The skeleton:** the integer pattern of the hierarchies, which the principle can force.
  - **The flesh:** one departure δ and order-one coefficients, either contingent or set by one canonical datum.

## The goal

- **The skeleton.** Show, or refute, that the forced structure fits the data's hierarchy pattern better than chance.
- **The flesh.** Find, or rule out, a parameter-free mechanism that sets δ.
- **What would count.** A prediction that is confirmed and was not fitted, so that the count of free numbers drops. A
  fit alone never counts; a coincidence never counts.

   - What lifts the zero, with its power forced? The candidates are the reading after W69's (c), (d) and (e): end data
     at the puncture, a composite field, or an exponential sector.
4. **After each stage:** a relay to main, the record's surfaces, and a push. The next stage's rule is written only
   after the last stage's read-out.
