## Why

- **The owner's question (2026-10-22),** the second of three checks. W127 asks whether the principle constructs the
  field. This arc asks the question the way the Standard Model's own history answered it: given chiral matter that is
  massive, what must exist for the theory to be consistent?
- **The historical argument (cited, not recomputed).**
  - Tree-level unitarity of W_L W_L → W_L W_L and of f f̄ → W_L W_L fails at high energy unless a scalar couples to
    each massive fermion with strength m_f/v (Llewellyn Smith 1973; Cornwall, Levin and Tiktopoulos 1974; Lee, Quigg
    and Thacker 1977).
  - Appelquist and Chanowitz (1987) bound the scale of fermion-mass generation without such a scalar.
  - The argument forces that something exists with the Higgs's quantum numbers, elementary or composite. It does not
    force its form.
- **What the record supplies.**
  - F-MC derives the gauge group and the hypercharge direction (main's B862, B864, B991).
  - B1674, which re-banks this seat's W101, gives the moves' mixed anomalies when the moves act on the fermions by
    T or T̄. They are free with SU(3) iff s_u = s_d = −s_Q, never free with SU(2), and free with gravity iff
    s_e = s_ν = −s_L, given ν^c.
  - W46 and W47 give the four-dimensional index n·f + t_r on the weave's surface.

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **C0 to C4 hold.** Then:
  - **The consistency route forces the field's gauge "which", not its existence.**
    - Given massive chiral matter, the field (or condensate) that gives the masses is a colour-singlet SU(2) doublet
      with |Y| = ½, and one suffices.
    - Its existence is conditional on the empirical bit (C3), not on the principle.
  - **With the moves as a symmetry and constant couplings, the field is a move singlet.** It is consistent exactly at
    B1674's anomaly-free assignments.
    - So the moves' anomaly freedom and a flavour-blind Higgs are one condition.
    - The record's triplet-valued forms (∂R, η⁴R) cannot be that field's flavour content. They can enter only as
      τ-dependent couplings (B1674's coupling layer), with the moves broken by τ.
    - This is recorded as a READING for W129: "the Higgs" in W123's sense (a weight-3/2 section of the matter's
      representation) and the Standard Model's doublet are different objects. The question may have treated them as
      one.
  - **Index matching, if adopted, forces the unit at the natural conditions,** and with it W123's Higgs and three. The
    record does not state it, and it is named here, not adopted. For the field twisted by all six characters it can
    never hold.
- **C1 fails.** The anomaly solution or the bilinears differ from the textbook. It is recorded, and the instrument is
  checked post hoc before anything is read.
- **C2 fails.** Then the anomaly-free set and the flavour-blind Yukawa set differ, or a triplet field couples. It is
  recorded as found, with the assignments that differ.
- **C4 fails.** The matching set differs from the prediction. It is recorded with the set.
