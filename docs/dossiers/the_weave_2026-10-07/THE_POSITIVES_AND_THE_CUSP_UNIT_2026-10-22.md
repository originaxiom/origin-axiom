## Part 2. Why the cusp's unit is not yet forced

### What the record has established (each cited)

1. **The unit is the one missing datum for a chiral three.** On the weave's own surface the four-dimensional index is
   n times the fibre's ±3, with n the cusp's units of end data, and every natural condition gives n = 0 (COMPUTED, W45,
   W46).
2. **Nothing inside the weave supplies it** (COMPUTED, W47).
   - No cusp exponent is an integer, because the moves' twist is an odd power of η's multiplier. So the natural cusp
     conditions coincide, and none of them gives n ≠ 0.
   - The 24 flat line bundles move the index by at most one, never by three.
   - The unit is a non-flat source: the trivial bundle λ¹² ≅ O on the open surface, extended by Δ⁻¹, with a simple pole
     at the cusp.
3. **Nothing the principle forces visits the cusp** (main's B1629; GENESIS v1.38, scoped in v1.44). The tick is the
   weave's lowest closed geodesic, and under the weave's uniform measure the time spent deep in the cusp has no atom.
   So, in that scope, the unit is not dynamical. If it is forced, it is forced as a boundary datum.
4. **The outside sources tried give one, not three** (COMPUTED, W48). A dressing by a chiral c = 24 sector gives one
   mode per eight lattice-free bosons. The heterotic string gives one; only 24 lattice-free bosons give three.
5. **A field-free end gives up something each time** (COMPUTED, W71). A flat end that gaps every channel exists, but it
   costs the dictionary, the R-symmetry or U(1)_em.
6. **Its status.** Main's GENESIS v1.39 (B1631) records the owner's decision of 2026-10-09, made directly to main: the
   unit is a tagged working postulate, and results that use it carry "given the unit". This branch had recorded only
   the earlier relayed ruling, "keep GENESIS FK10 open" (the state page now carries a dated note).

### A READING: what the unit is (by hand; no run; for main to check)

- **The cusp is the record that pinches.** As Im τ → ∞ one cycle of the records' torus shrinks to nothing. Adding that
  limit closes the τ-line: the closed curve has one boundary point δ, the nodal record (the Deligne–Mumford point).
- **The unit is that boundary point, counted once.**
  - On the closed curve the discriminant Δ is a section of λ¹² with a simple zero at δ (Mumford's relation in genus
    one: λ¹² ≅ O(δ)).
  - W47's description of the unit (the trivial bundle extended by Δ⁻¹, simple pole at the cusp) is then O(δ).
  - n units are O(nδ), and the canonical end (n = 0) is O.
- **Why the principle gives n = 0, in one line.** Everything the principle forces is flat: the moves' local systems on
  the open τ-line. A flat bundle on the open curve has one canonical extension across the cusp, with exponents in
  [0, 1) (Deligne's). W47's K2 shows that every natural condition on record is that one, and it gives n = 0. Any other
  n is a different extension: an integer that the flat data do not contain.
- **So the gap is not hidden in a computation.** The principle says what records and moves are. It does not say what a
  pinched record carries. That is GENESIS FK10's "end law", and it is a question the principle has not yet been made
  to answer. This is why no forcing has been found: every search so far looked inside the flat bulk, where the
  integer cannot live.
