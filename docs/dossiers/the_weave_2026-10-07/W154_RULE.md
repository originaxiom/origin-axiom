## The objects (B1686's and B1704's units, read from main's commit 22b7d5739)

- **The basis functions:** E(τ, s) = Σ′ y^s/|m + nτ|^{2s} (main's `E_cs`, Chowla–Selberg) and S_c(τ, p), the
  characteristic sum (main's `S_c`).
- **A content** is a set of real weights, bosons positive and fermions negative:
  - w₀ on the trivial characteristic (0, 0);
  - w_p on each parity characteristic (½, 0), (0, ½), (½, ½), the same on all three;
  - w_ρ in the matter's representation ρ_Q, with PH2's shape.

  The unit is B1686's and B1704's: a complex scalar 2 and a Weyl fermion −2 on a characteristic; a real vector in six
  dimensions 4 per real field; the graviton 9; a complex scalar in ρ_Q 2.
- **The reduction:** F = w₀ E₃ − (15/16) w_p E₃ − (31/1024) w_ρ (E₃ − E₂), so
  - A = w₀ − (15/16) w_p − (31/1024) w_ρ;
  - B = (31/1024) w_ρ;
  - V = −(A E₃ + B E₂).

  The cusp coefficient is 2A in ζ(6) units (B1704's holding condition: a content holds τ off the cusp only if it is
  negative).
- **The domain:** SL(2, ℤ)'s fundamental domain, |Re τ| ≤ ½, |τ| ≥ 1, searched to Im τ = 14.
- **The target window:** B1704's hierarchy band, 2.1 ≤ Im τ ≤ 3.2, fixed now. Any other window can be read off the
  curve.
- **The rows:**
  - the record's contents at the forced line: the matter (45 Weyl fermions in ρ_Q, w_ρ = −90); plus the Higgs's three
    copies (w_p = 2); plus the 12 gauge fields (w₀ = 48); plus the graviton (w₀ = 57);
  - E₈'s 248 as six-dimensional vectors, decomposed under the fibre's SU(2) as 55·1 + 52·2 + 27·3 + 2·4 (W81): w₀ = 220
    (55 real fields × 4), w_p = 108 (27 × 4 per parity), and w_ρ = 224 (the 112 real fields of the 2 and 4 parts,
    × 4, halved to B1704's ρ_Q unit). The alternative unit, w_ρ = 448, is run beside it.
