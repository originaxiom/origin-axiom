## 2. The test that replaces the word

A choice is a **convention** when the joint action's symmetry carries every option to every other. The symmetry can be
a move, the mirror (the weave is closed under it: W26, N1) or a relabelling the moves permute. Then every invariant
quantity is the same whichever option is named, and naming one carries no information. The rule adopted today says
the same of minima: "Naming one point of an orbit of minima is a convention, as the hand is" (`docs/THE_WEAVE.md`).

Two cautions:
- **A convention is one choice for the whole.** Once two such choices are made, the relation between them is physics,
  unless the symmetry carries the pairs jointly. A relative hand and a second scale against the first are examples.
- **A choice that fails the test is information.** It is then forced by the joint action, or it is a free number,
  counted against the 19. "The observer's" is neither, so the word hides which of the two it is.

## 4. What it says

- **No closing needs an agent.** Each one is forced (the arrow; that the index is chiral), or a convention (the hand's
  sheet, the unit, the McKay naming, a point in an orbit), or information that is still open (the ratios of scales;
  the filling, which is the end condition, GENESIS FK10; the trit until its orbit is checked). Reading the chiral index
  as gauge chirality is GENESIS FK11's step, which is the dictionary, not an observer.
- **In the m004 frame, what lay outside the object was the observer.** Under the weave, what lies outside a thread is
  the other threads and their joint action. Main's B1327 (OPEN), which reads the closings as relations that need a
  partner, points the same way. On the weave the partner is the joint action.
- **So, as a source of anything in the derivation, the observer has no role left.** The word is also a hazard. The
  ledger's first draft filed the ratios of scales as "the observer's", and so hid free numbers (the ledger's section 6).
- **The one item of the old list that is information and is not supplied is the space closing, the end condition**
  (GENESIS FK10, the link Λ). It is the open question, under its own name.
- **What the word keeps, outside the derivation:**
  - GENESIS FK12's question of experience, which Gate 5-Q keeps apart from the mathematics (W34);
  - the observer-selection control of W57 ("Among worlds that can hold observers"), a labelled statistical check,
    not a source.
- **W34 found on the weave that the observer layer is not the missing ingredient.** This page goes one step further. It
  re-sorts the layer's closings and finds none left for an observer to supply.
- **The open numbers were never closings.** τ, the loop's content (GENESIS FK11), the prime 5 (W153) and the couplings'
  constants are counted against the 19 under their own names. They were never the observer's.

## 6. Pre-mortem: how this reading could be wrong

- **A convention needs the symmetry to be the joint action's.** The mirror is a symmetry of the set of threads (W26,
  N1). Whether the swap P is a legal move is GENESIS GM5c, OPEN. The hand's row rests on the mirror, not on P.
- **The arrow's row rests on main's B1083 and B1662,** under GENESIS GM5b, adopted at v1.48. If GM5b were withdrawn,
  the row would reopen.
- **The Galois row is checked only for complex conjugation.**
- **A convention is not nothing.** An overall hand and an overall unit are still named once, as every theory names
  them. What this page removes is the idea that naming them carries information.
- **The filling's row may move.** If the joint action's own functional selects a filling (W122's least volume read
  under the rule adopted today), the space closing becomes forced. Nothing on record has done that.
