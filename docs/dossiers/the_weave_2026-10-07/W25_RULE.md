## The question, made exact

- **Gauge chirality.** A gauge group G′ (the commutant of a bundle's holonomy in E₈, or a subgroup of it) has chiral
  matter only if some multiplicity space U_σ = Hom_H(σ, 248) is a complex representation of G′, not equivalent to its
  conjugate. Here H is the holonomy.
- **The lemma** (standard, proved in the note).
  - The 248 is real.
  - If an irreducible σ of H is real, U_σ is a real representation of the centraliser.
  - If σ is quaternionic, U_σ is quaternionic.
  - Only a complex σ, with σ̄ ≇ σ, gives a U_σ that need not be self-conjugate.
  - So if every irreducible of H that occurs in the 248 is real or quaternionic, every gauge reading is
    self-conjugate. Chirality then needs a choice: a Lagrangian half, or a complex character added by hand.
- **What the weave forces, by object.**
  - On the fibre, the even-dimensional object where the index lives (W21, W22): the holonomy of the common point's
    local systems, Q₈.
  - On the six local solutions at the puncture: the group of the moves' lifts, the fibre's holonomy and the parity
    grading.
  - On the cohomology: the moves' group of order 96 (W10).
  - On an odd-trace thread: the extension of the common point by the monodromy, 2T (B1601, W3).

## What each outcome means

- **As predicted.** The dictionary gap is a theorem about the weave.
  - The weave forces three (the parities), alike, with an odd net chirality on the fibre (three when the parity
    grading is kept) and a complex flavor triplet T. It forces no gauge chirality.
  - Gauge chirality needs one of three inputs:
    - a selected parity (E₆), which is a selection;
    - the order-3 move in the holonomy, which lives on threads, where there is no index;
    - a structure outside the common point's local systems.
  - The stop hook's demand (three chiral generations with gauge content, derived) cannot be met by the weave's local
    systems. It needs a new forced structure, or the owner's acceptance of one named postulate.
- **If F4's odd-trace centraliser has no complex irreducible in the 248.** Then even the threads give no
  chirality-capable group, and the statement strengthens.
- **If F2 is not quaternionic.** Then the six local solutions carry a complex structure that the moves force. That
  would be read at once as the candidate for the dictionary.
