- **The couplings** are functions of the modulus τ, equivariant under that whole group.

  i and ω.
- Weave-type.

## The facts by hand

**1. Words** (in Aut(F₂), composed right to left; σ is the sign a ↦ a⁻¹, b ↦ b⁻¹, and P the swap):
- the braid relation L R⁻¹ L = R⁻¹ L R⁻¹; with Δ = L R⁻¹ L, Δ⁴ = conj(u), u = a b⁻¹ a⁻¹ b (W45);
- σ L σ L⁻¹ = conj(a⁻¹) and σ R σ R⁻¹ = conj(b);
- P L P R⁻¹ = conj(b) and P R P L⁻¹ = conj(a⁻¹).

**2. The kernel.**
- ⟨L, R⟩ is the image of the braid group B₃ (σ₁ = L, σ₂ = R⁻¹). The kernel of B₃ → SL(2, ℤ) is ⟨Δ⁴⟩ (classical).
- Every element of that kernel has exponent sum ±12m. So up to length 12 the only reduced words in L^{±1}, R^{±1}
  whose H₁ matrix is I are words of length 12 for Δ^{±4}.
- So ⟨L, R⟩ meets the inner automorphisms only in ⟨conj(u)⟩. conj(a) and conj(b) are not in it, since a is not a
  power of u.

**3. The residuals on T** (W21's construction, W49's basis t_p, the period rule of CONVENTIONS §3). A move fixes τ
when its Möbius image does; ±I fix every τ, and Δ² maps to −I.
- **A generic τ** is fixed only by ±I. Their preimage in ⟨L, R⟩ is generated by Δ², which acts on T as a scalar of
  order 4. So the residual is four scalars, and its commutant has all nine dimensions.
- **At i** the residual is generated by Δ (τ ↦ −1/τ). On T, tr Δ = e^{iπ/4} and Δ² = i, so Δ has eigenvalue e^{iπ/4}
  twice and −e^{iπ/4} once. It has order 8, T splits as a plane plus a line, and the commutant has dimension 5.
- **At ω** it is generated by w = L U L⁻¹, the move W40 found fixing ω under the period rule (τ ↦ −1/(τ + 1)). On T, w
  is U's conjugate: order 12, three distinct eigenvalues (W40's turns ¼, 7/12, 11/12, or ½ more), and w³ a scalar. So
  the residual is cyclic of order 12 and its commutant has dimension 3.
- **With the inner automorphisms and −I added** (W40's frame):
  - at a generic τ the residual is {±1, ±i} × the parity signs, of order 16, with commutant 3 (the parity lines);
  - at ω it is W40's group, of order 48, with commutant 1.

**4. The couplings in τ alone.**
- Given Λ the masses' tensor is T ⊗ T (W39). A coupling of two zero modes and a flavour-blind Higgs is a
  vector-valued modular form Y(τ) for ρ_T^∨ ⊗ ρ_T^∨, with ρ_T = (Δ, L⁻¹) as W45 and W46 define it.
- ρ_T is unitary in the basis t_p (W49's M3), so ρ_T^∨ is its complex conjugate. Its S-matrix squares to −1, so the
  allowed weights are the odd integers.
- **In the parity basis the representation splits into three pieces:** D (diagonal), O₊ (symmetric off-diagonal) and
  O₋ (antisymmetric). The weave's matrices are monomial, so each piece is invariant.
- **The inner automorphisms** act on D trivially and on O± by the non-trivial parity characters. So where they act at
  every τ, only D survives, which is main's theorem.
- **The dimensions by W45's formula.** tr ρ(S) is −i, −i and +i on D, O₊ and O₋. The exponent sums are 7/4, 3/4 and
  5/4, and tr ρ(ST) = 0 on each. For k ≥ 3 these numbers are dimensions, since no dual cusp form has negative weight;
  at k = 1 they are lower bounds.

| k | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 | 11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| O₊ | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| O₋ | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 |

- **So the lowest symmetric couplings (k = 1 and 3) lie entirely in O₊.** They are off the diagonal in the parity
  basis: exactly the part the inner automorphisms forbid.

**5. The mixing.**
- At a generic τ₀, two such couplings at two weights have singular bases that are not the parity basis. Their mixing
  is not a permutation.
- With D alone (W40's and B1620's frame), every mixing is a permutation.

**6. The theta constants.**
- ρ_T's traces on S, T and ST are e^{iπ/4}, those of the exponents {1/8, 3/8, 7/8}, and 0. The same holds for
  (θ₂², θ₃², θ₄²)·η⁻³:
  - under τ ↦ τ + 1, θ₂² gains i, θ₃² and θ₄² swap, and η⁻³ gains e^{−iπ/4};
  - under τ ↦ −1/τ, θ₂² and θ₄² swap with weight-one factors, and η⁻³ adds its phase. That gives S = e^{iπ/4} times
    the swap at weight −½, so tr S = e^{iπ/4} and S² = i, as for Δ;
  - tr(ST) = 0 in both.
- So ρ_T should be the representation of η²¹·(θ₂², θ₃², θ₄²), a holomorphic form of weight 23/2. That is the weight
  where W45 found the first three. The intertwiner should be monomial, pairing each parity with one even theta
  constant.

## A correction before the run (2026-10-09, found in review of the script; no code had run)

- **N2's wording is wrong as written.** It says every reduced word of length ≤ 12 with H₁ matrix I is empty or a word
  for conj(u)^{±1}. That overlooks the braid group's relators. A reduced free word in L^{±1}, R^{±1} can represent the
  identity of B₃, and then its H₁ matrix is I. The braid relation itself, L R⁻¹ L R L⁻¹ R, is one, of length 6.
- **The claim fact 2 derives is about the automorphisms.**
  - Every such word acts on F₂ as the identity or as conj(u)^{±1}.
  - The words for conj(u)^{±1} have length 12 and use only L and R⁻¹, or only L⁻¹ and R.
  - conj(a), conj(b) and their inverses never occur.
- **The script tests that corrected claim.** The original wording stays above and is recorded as wrong. No other cell
  changes.
