## Weave or thread?

- **A WEAVE result, in a supplied frame.** It takes every primitive hyperbolic class (thread) of each trace t with
  3 ≤ t ≤ 40, under a stated rule, none hand-picked. The trace is the length the moves preserve, and the claim is about
  the layers of all threads at once.
- **The frame is W95's:** the surface frame, cell C1, neutral, given Λ and the cusp's unit (GENESIS FK10). The claim is
  bounded by the range.

## The quantity

- **The matrices** K(τ) and Y(τ) are W95's (W72's units, no hypercharge line), evaluated at the reduced point by their
  series. Their invariance is W95's V2, to 10⁻⁴⁰, so every thread's integrand returns with phase 1.
- **The layer of trace t:** every narrow class of primitive forms of every discriminant D = (t² − 4)/u² whose
  fundamental automorph has trace t. Each class is one oriented closed geodesic. Both orientations are counted when
  they are distinct classes.
- **The layer's trace:** T_t(M) is the sum over the layer's classes of the cycle integral ∫ M ds over one period.
- **The masses** are the singular values m₁ ≤ m₂ ≤ m₃ of T_t(K)^(−½) T_t(Y) (T_t(K)^(−½))ᵀ, as in W95. The same is done
  for each class alone.

## The instrument (W95's, with one repair)
