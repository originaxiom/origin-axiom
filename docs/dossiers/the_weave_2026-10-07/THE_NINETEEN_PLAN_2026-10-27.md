## 3. Stage 0: the charter (committed before any stage runs)

A number counts as derived only if all of the following hold.
- **(i) Blind producer.** It comes out of a function of frame quantities, and the target cannot be read at the point it
  runs.
- **(ii) A counterfactual.** Moving the target leaves it unchanged. This is run and reported.
- **(iii) A sensitivity table** over every literal in its chain, published with it.
- **(iv) Every menu frozen and priced.** This covers recipes, weights, exponents and cutoffs. The menu is frozen before
  the run and priced by a class-choosing null over the whole menu (agy's B1760 method, rebuilt on own code).
- **(v) A weave state.** It sits at a state selected under THE_WEAVE's clause, not at a hand-picked thread, τ or line.
  Otherwise it is labelled a thread result and not counted.
- **(vi) It passes THE_BAR** against data, or PRECISION_BAR for a value claim. The error bars come from the data's
  source only.
- **(vii) Its test recomputes in process.** No test asserts a saved count.
- **(viii) Its looks are counted** in the look ledger.

Anything else is a free number, counted against the 19.
