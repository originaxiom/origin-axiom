## Why

  no singlet. Main's scans (B1827 § 2, S290, S291) found:

  - an even singlet does nothing;
  - the frame's own twisted Higgs is even (B1744);
  - the odd singlet is the adjugate reading, and on order-5 lines the leptons already read the down sector that way
    (P-583).
  P-566, Q1 asks what in the object makes the singlet odd: "one definition written first, then the corner order,
  ∣V_ud∣ and the six at the frame's points, data-free".
- **The candidate (RI-1).** The web seat proved (its relay of 2026-11-03 on the channel norms, § 5) that every channel
  kernel depends on the twists only through the summed twist S = (q_L + q_R)w, and that K_s(u; −S) = −K_s(−u; S). So
  the Laurent constant a₀ of K_s(u) = 1/u + a₀ + O(u) is odd: a₀(up) = −a₀(down) = −a₀(lepton). B1656 uses only
  πε²∣a₀∣², so the sign is dropped there.
- **What is missing, and what this arc does.** Nothing on record says the Higgs's singlet reads the kernel's constant.
  This arc does not derive that. It writes the identification down as one definition, with its sign, phase and size
  fixed before any number is computed, and asks whether the frame then gives the CKM's coarse shape at the points the
  principle marks.
- **A name to keep apart.** Main's P-566 writes "a0" for the Higgs's singlet coefficient (a0 = c·√(h·h), c a free
  phase). Here a₀ is always the kernel's Laurent constant. RI-1 is the claim that the first is the second.

## The definition (fixed now)

- **The frame.** W178's instrument at τ = i, unchanged except for the Higgs: B1656's frame as W162's point computes it,
  W177's norms in working precision, no residue cut, the θ-zero rule, the Higgs direction DR, L_max, the weighted M and
  W93's coupling. Two precisions as in W178 (p₁ = 40 + ⌈8π Im τ/ln 10⌉ and p₂ = p₁ + 60).
- **The kernel's constant.** In this seat's kernels (W177), K_s(z) = e^{2πiAz}θ_s(z + ū)θ₁′(0)/(θ_s(ū)θ₁(z)), with ū
  reduced to the centred cell and A = Im ū/Im τ. Its Laurent constant is
  a₀^{(s)}(ū) = 2πiA + ∂_z log θ_s(z + ū) at z = 0.
  The singlet reads the identity channel, s = 1 (θ₁), the channel of the identity unit. For sector X,
  ū_X = (q_L + q_R)w: −3w for up and +3w for down and leptons. So a₀(X) := a₀^{(1)}(ū_X), and
  a₀(up) = −a₀(down) = −a₀(lepton).
- **The Higgs in sector X.** S_X = σ_X·1 + DR_i·i + DR_j·j + DR_k·k. Every sector reads S_X as it stands, with no
  conjugation. This is the reading of P-566's scans and of P-583's relation (equal readings, B1739). W178 reads up with
  S̃; that reading is kept only for the tie, O0.
- **Two sizes, both fixed now:**
  - **Bare (B):** σ_X = a₀(X), in the frame's own units (u with periods 1 and τ; DR as the frame builds it). No free
    number.
  - **Natural (N):** σ_X = ∣DR·DR∣^{1/2} · a₀(X)/∣a₀(X)∣. P-566's natural magnitude (∣σ∣² = ∣h·h∣), with the sign and
    phase from a₀. The magnitude is main's convention, not derived.
- **Controls, same points:**
  - **none:** σ_X = 0 (the frame with equal readings);
  - **even:** σ_X = ∣DR·DR∣^{1/2} · a₀(down)/∣a₀(down)∣ in all three sectors (the same sign in up and down);
  - **twelve phases:** σ_up = e^{2πik/12}∣DR·DR∣^{1/2}, σ_down = σ_lepton = −σ_up, for k = 0, …, 11 (an odd singlet at
    main's twelve phases, with no a₀).
- **A caveat stated now.** a₀ has modular weight 1 and DR weight 3/2, so σ_X/DR is not invariant under the moves of τ.
  The points are read in the record's convention (τ in the standard fundamental domain), as in W178–W182.

## The points and the quantities

- **The points.** τ = i on all 266 census lines (W179's Part B), the fixed point of S that W179 and W182 read. A line
  where θ₁(3w) = 0 has no identity kernel. That is the 5 lines with 3w in the lattice: (0, 0), (0, 1/3), (1/3, 0),
  (1/3, 1/3) and (1/3, 2/3). They are excluded and counted. That leaves 261.
- **Where the singlet vanishes.** a₀ is odd, and K is unchanged when ū moves by the lattice. So where ū is a
  half-period (6w in the lattice, 3w not), a₀ = 0 exactly in every sector, and the definition gives no singlet.
  - That is 15 lines: (0, 1/2), (1/2, 0), (1/2, 1/2), (0, 1/6), (1/6, 0), (1/6, 1/6), (1/6, 1/3), (1/6, 1/2),
    (1/6, 2/3), (1/6, 5/6), (1/3, 1/6), (1/3, 1/2), (1/3, 5/6), (1/2, 1/6) and (1/2, 1/3).
  - There σ is set to 0 under B, N and even (decided from the line's fractions, not from a computed a₀). Those lines
    are computed and counted, and left out of O3–O8, where they would only repeat none.
  - O3–O8 run on the other 246 lines. O0–O2 run on all 261.
  - This bullet was added before the rule's push, when writing the script showed that N divides by a₀ there.
- **Towards the cusp (read-outs only):** the same at Re τ 1/8 and 3/8, Im τ 6 and 12.
- **The CKM.** V = U_up† U_down, with U each sector's left singular vectors, lightest first (the record's ckm function).
  - The corner order is ∣V_ub∣ < ∣V_td∣ (V[0, 2] against V[2, 0]).
  - **Status.** A point is decided when the two precisions give ln∣V_ub∣ and ln∣V_td∣ within 10⁻⁶. The two must also
    differ by more than 10⁻⁶ at both precisions, and ∣V_ud∣ must agree within 10⁻⁶.
  - **Zeros.** A corner element within 30 digits of both precisions' floors is zero (W178's zero rule). If exactly
    one of ∣V_ub∣ and ∣V_td∣ is zero, the order is decided (zero is the smaller). If both are, the point is decided as
    tied, which is not nature's order.
  - **Exclusions.** A point is degenerate, excluded and counted, when two singular values of the up or down Yukawa
    agree within 10⁻¹⁰ relative. The singular vectors are not defined there.
- **The joint coarse fact (P-566's):** nature's corner order and ∣V_ud∣ > 0.9 together. A variant's rate is its
  fraction among the decided lines. Each comparison between variants uses the lines decided under both.
- **The six** (W178's ln σ₂/σ₁ and ln σ₃/σ₁ per sector, resolved as W178) under B and N, and W179's double ratios
  ln r₂ = f₃ − f₅, ln r₁ = f₄ − f₆, ln D = ln r₁ − ln r₂.
- **The parity, two routes.**
  - Route 1 is the closed form above.
  - Route 2 is (K(h) + K(−h))/2 with K evaluated by W177's `K_direct` at h = 10⁻²⁰, with 60 more digits.
  - Both routes apply to all four channels and all three sectors, at every point where θ_s(ū) ≠ 0.

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **O0 or O1 fails:** the instrument or the parity is not as stated. The arc's values are withheld and the failure
  recorded.
- **O4 holds and O5 holds:** the kernel's constant supplies an odd singlet that lifts the corners, as any odd singlet
  does. The parity is the object's own, which is new on record. Its phase is generic, and its size is a convention.
  RI-1 then stands as an IDENTIFICATION CANDIDATE that does what P-566 asks of the sign and nothing more. It is not
  earned: the rule that the singlet reads the kernel's constant is still a choice, and N's size is main's convention.
- **O5 fails:** a₀'s phase does better (or worse) than every fixed phase. That is a sealed target for the next arc
  (with main), not a result.
- **O6 holds:** the bare identification, with no free number, lifts the corners. That is the strongest outcome on the
  sign. It goes to main as a candidate for the IDENTIFICATION_LEDGER (an UNEARNED row), with nothing banked.
- **O6 fails (B does not lift):** read with the read-out ∣a₀∣/∣DR∣ (too small to matter, or too large and then
  dominating). RI-1 at bare size is then closed in this frame. N remains a convention.
- **O7 or O8 fails:** a line's shape or D lands, read as in W182's C3 and C4: a sealed target, nothing banked.
- **O2 or O3 fails:** the controls do not behave as main's scans did at nature-like points. That is recorded, and O4–O6
  are read against the controls as computed.
