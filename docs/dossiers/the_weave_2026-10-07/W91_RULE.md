## The object (fixed here)

- **The line and the pair.** The line is ψ = (ψ₁, ψ₂) ∈ (1/N)ℤ², and the pair of charges is (k_u, k_d) = (q_x + q_y,
  q_x − q_y).
- **The channel sections,** in B1653's argument form and order of units (1, i, j, k) ↔ thetas (1, 2, 4, 3), with
  c = (−i, −1, −1, i) and w = ψ₁τ − ψ₂:
  V_s(τ) = c_s e^{πiψ₁²(k_u² + k_d²)τ} θ_s(k_u w|τ) θ_s(k_d w|τ) / θ₁′(0|τ), with θ_s(z|τ) = θ_s(πz | e^{iπτ}).
  - The exponential factor is common to the four channels. It makes each channel a q-series whose exponents are the
    characteristic ones (below), and it keeps B1653's null identity.
  - Products of pure characteristic thetas θ[α_s + kψ](0|τ) differ from these by constant phases that depend on the
    channel. Those break the null quaternion, so they are not the delivered object. Checked before this rule: null
    to 10⁻⁴¹ in the up sector on (1/9, 2/9) at three heights.
  - θ₁′(0|τ) = 2πη(τ)³.
- **The exponents.** Each section is written as Σ_E a_E q^E with q = e^{2πiτ}. The exponents E are exact rationals:
  (n + a_u)²/2 + (m + a_d)²/2 − 1/8 over the integers n and m, together with η⁻³'s integer steps. Here a_u and a_d are
  the two characteristics' first entries.
- **The delivery.** For each (N, ψ, k_u, k_d) asked, the coefficients with exponents up to 30, at 50 digits, as exact
  rational exponents with decimal coefficients. A small script regenerates them for any characteristic.
