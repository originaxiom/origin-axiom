## The data and the predicate (fixed here)

- **Masses:** `docs/dossiers/the_weave_2026-10-07/received/HZ2021_running_masses_MZ.json` (value and σ). Ratios are
  taken within a sector: m₂/m₃ and m₁/m₃.
- **CKM:** `docs/dossiers/the_weave_2026-10-07/received/B1612_data.json`, `ckm_abs` (value and σ).
- **J from the moduli,** by the unitarity identity
  J² = |V₁₁|²|V₂₂|²|V₁₂|²|V₂₁|² − ¼(1 − |V₁₁|² − |V₁₂|² − |V₂₁|² − |V₂₂|² + |V₁₁|²|V₂₂|² + |V₁₂|²|V₂₁|²)².
  Its σ comes from 10⁵ Gaussian draws of the four moduli with seed 88. Draws giving J² < 0 count as J = 0.
- **The predicate is W66's.** For an equality X = Y between positive quantities,
  z = (ln X − ln Y)/√(r_X² + r_Y² + a²), with relative errors r and a = 0.10. A relation of k equalities fails if
  Σz² exceeds the 95% point for k degrees of freedom: 3.84, 5.99 or 7.81 for k = 1, 2, 3.
- **A relation that sets a quantity to zero** fails if the datum is more than 5σ from zero.

## What the outcomes mean

- **A relation REFUTED** excludes its frame or class as a description of nature. It is recorded as an exclusion on the
  map's inverse side, not as a failure of the principle.
- **A relation that holds** would be the first relation among the nineteen with no free size. It would go to main as a
  candidate for its register, with its premises named.
- **A failed prediction** (for instance Q6 passing) is recorded as failed, and any further look is labelled post hoc.
