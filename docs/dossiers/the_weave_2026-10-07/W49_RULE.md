## The reading (by hand)

1. **The three are a multiplicity.**
   - 𝕎 ≅ ρ_Q ⊗ M (W44's reading; the intertwiners u_p are the quaternion units, checked exactly there).
   - So V = H¹(F₂; 𝕎) ≅ H¹(F₂; ρ_Q) ⊗ M, and the holomorphic triplet should be T = ℓ ⊗ M, with ℓ the
     Hodge–Riemann-positive line of the two-dimensional H¹(F₂; ρ_Q).
   - The three generations are then one zero mode ℓ, three times.
2. **Geometry acts on ℓ, flavour on M.**
   - Every datum the weave's geometry supplies acts on the ρ_Q factor: the modulus τ, a point z of the records' torus,
     the natural end conditions, the cusp's units, and the flat twists (W45 to W48).
   - The weave's group acts as c(g) ⊗ S(g): a scalar on ℓ and a signed permutation on M (W42's normal form).
   - The inner automorphisms act as a sign on ℓ times the parity signs on M.
   - So every coupling built from flavour-blind data is a scalar times a fixed tensor on M. A Hermitian pairing of the
     zero modes is a multiple of the identity on M: three degenerate, orthogonal generations.
3. **The only data that touch M destroy the three.**
   - The puncture's middle conditions V2 and V4 are not of the form X ⊗ M. V2 is the Clebsch–Gordan copy
     {Σ_p u_p v ⊗ e_p}, whose matrices have rank 2 (W28 E2).
   - They change the fibre index from ±3 to ∓1 (W28), and the four-dimensional count with them (W46).
4. **So it is three or split.** A datum that keeps three generations cannot tell them apart. A datum that could tell
   them apart does not keep three.
5. **The values must come from a tensor on M that breaks G:** a flavon, or a Higgs flavour structure. The principle
   supplies none beyond G's own subgroup projectors, the residuals of B1620 and the clock and tick of B1621, and those
   leave the values free (W42 to W44).
6. **This is the root of "forced to choose, not forced which"** for the 13 flavour numbers. Schur's lemma is its
   algebra, and the multiplicity is its geometry.

**The reading, written before the run.**
- **If M1 to M4 hold,** the weave's three generations are one zero mode in a three-dimensional multiplicity. No
  geometric datum on the weave tells them apart, and the only data that touch the multiplicity leave the count ∓1, not
  ±3.
- **The free numbers' freedom is then structural, not a gap in effort.** Their values need a flavour tensor that breaks
  G, which the principle does not force.
- **If M1 fails** (the three lines do not coincide under the intertwiners), the multiplicity reading is withdrawn. Some
  geometric datum might then tell the generations apart, which would be the opening the owner asked for.
