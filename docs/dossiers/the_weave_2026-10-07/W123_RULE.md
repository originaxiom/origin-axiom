## The object (by hand, to be checked)

- **The representation.** ρ(T) = e^{iπ/4}L⁻¹ and ρ(S) = e^{iπ/4}P, with L the quarter-turn about e_i and P the swap of
  e_i and e_j (main's convention, W64 and B1691).
  - R = (−θ₂², −θ₄², iθ₃²)/(πθ₂θ₃θ₄) is a form of weight −½ for it (B1645). Checked by hand from the θ laws:
    R(τ + 1) = e^{iπ/4}L⁻¹R and R(−1/τ) = τ^{−1/2}e^{iπ/4}PR.
  - ρ(S)² = i·I, so the allowed weights are −½ mod 2, among them 3/2.
- **The eigenlines of ρ(T).** Exponents ⅛ on e_i (the parity line L fixes), ⅜ on e_j + ie_k and ⅞ on e_j − ie_k.
  The ⅞ line is B1700's v₊.
- **End data.** m = (m_⅛, m_⅜, m_⅞) ∈ ℤ³. A section has order at least λ_j − m_j on line j: m_j units of pole allowed
  there, or −m_j extra zeros when m_j < 0. m = 0 is the canonical end (W45); m = (1, 1, 1) is W46's uniform unit
  (B1700).
- **The counts.**
  - d(m) is the dimension of such holomorphic forms of weight 3/2, the Higgs's weight (B1691) and the matter's (W45).
  - h¹(m) is the dimension of the dual forms of weight ½ for the dual representation with order at least 1 − λ_j + m_j
    on the dual line j (Serre duality, as W45's second route).
- **The index law.** W45's formula with the exponents shifted by m gives χ(m) = d(m) − h¹(m) = χ_{3/2} + Σ m_j = Σ m_j,
  since χ_{3/2} = 0 (W46 H2).
- **The four-dimensional count.** On the weave's surface with the canonical puncture condition, the count is −χ(m). This
  is W46's formula −χ_{3/2+12n}, carried from uniform n to per-line m.
  - So a single unit on one line gives ∓1. Three needs Σ m_j = 3, for example the uniform unit.
- **The poles.** A single unit on the line with exponent λ allows a leading order λ − 1: −⅞ on the ⅛ line, −⅝ on the ⅜
  line and −⅛ on v₊.

## What the outcomes mean (fixed now)

- **Y1 to Y5 hold.**
  - A Higgs at weight 3/2 exists only with a unit at the cusp, so the Higgs, the unit and the chiral count are one
    datum.
  - The least end data that make room for a Higgs is one unit on one line, and the least pole is on v₊, whose Higgs is
    ∂R: the hierarchy's Higgs is the least Higgs.
  - That end datum gives a four-dimensional count of ∓1, one chiral generation, not three. Three needs a unit on every
    line, which the generation-blind reading of W46 supplies and minimality does not.
  - So route 1 does not force three. Under reading 1 or 2 with least remainder, a forced Higgs forces one unit, on v₊,
    and one generation. The chiral three needs in addition that the cusp's source be generation-blind. That is the
    sharpened form of GENESIS FK10's question for main.
- **Y3 fails** (some single-line unit gives d = 0 or 2). Then the least Higgs end data is another; it is recorded, and
  the reading is rebuilt post hoc.
- **Y4 fails.** Then ∂R's end data is not one unit on v₊, and B1700's "∂R uses the unit on v₊ alone" goes to main as a
  discrepancy.
- **Y2 fails.** Then the per-line index law is wrong, and the count −Σ m_j does not hold; recorded as failed.
- **Any cell failing** is recorded as failed.
