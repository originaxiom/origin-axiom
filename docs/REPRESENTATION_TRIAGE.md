*Purpose (L143, from B976): **the repo lost nothing; the summaries forgot.** Eleven banked
cascade arcs (B860–B873) were cited zero times on any synthesis surface — including **B864,
which derives hypercharge**, while a ledger row written five days later called hypercharge

exist. Neither notices a row that was **never written**. This registry does.*

**How it works.** `scripts/checks/representation_sweep.py` (gate `representation-sweep`) lists
every **substantial** banked arc — non-instrument, PROVED or NEGATIVE, `claim_one_line` ≥ 500
characters — that is cited on **no** synthesis surface. Each must appear below with a
disposition. **An untriaged unrepresented arc is the defect.**

**Why claim length, not file size.** Calibrated on the block that was actually lost: a
6 KB **file-size** floor would have caught **1 of 11** (B864's FINDINGS is 3.7 KB — short and
dense). A **claim-length** floor of 500 catches **11 of 11**. A seat writes a long claim line
when there is a lot to say.
