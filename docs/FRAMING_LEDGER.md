### Main's reading — the owner's question answered

**Are we asking the proper existential question?** In the arcs, often; on the board, no. The headline measures Tier 4, a
tier the record retired, and so it cannot register what the programme actually learns. Stated in the order it can be
answered, the question the record supports is:
1. **Existence.** Can a frame built from the object carry the Standard Model at all, with parameters allowed? This is cheap
   and unsealed, and comes before any sealed look. FR-FIB's answer: not at one address, because of how its Higgs is built
   (B1825, B1739, B1826). The adjugate reading balances the corners; it does not yet place them.
2. **The count.** What is the total input vector (P-418): continuous inputs, discrete choices in bits, and unearned
   identifications? Progress is its fall. The parameter-free theory is the same aspiration, now measurable on the way: the
   vector at zero, or at one anchor.
3. **Relations and exclusions.** What the frame forces and forbids (FL-15).
4. **Specificity.** Whether this object does it better than comparable objects and standard rivals (FL-25, FL-26).

**Would we recognise the answer?** Not reliably, as the apparatus is built, and each reason is computed from the record's
own numbers:
- **A correct theory at its own scale fails K.** R1: running alone costs 8.5 bits.
- **A correct frame a σ away passes K one time in ten.**
- **The flat allowance discards 29.7 bits**, about twice the gate.
- **The look horizon** is m ≈ 108.
- **The board cannot move at all** while the dictionary is unearnable (FL-20).

On the other side, the statistic alone cannot tell a derived frame from an engineered one; only the seal procedure does
(FL-23). None of this weakens a negative on record: B1825's NO SUPPORT is a χ² of 291 against 23.21, and its compromise
misses ∣V_ub∣ by a factor of 3.7 and u/t by 3.2. Running to 2 × 10¹⁶ GeV moves each of the ten by at most 28% (∣J∣) and 13% elsewhere. The
audit concerns positives: whether we would see one.

**The missing insight, tonight's.**
- The leptons already read the down sector through the adjugate on every order-5 line, Y_e(S) = −λY_d(adj S)ᵀ. So the
  reading that balances the CKM's corners is the frame's own down–lepton relation, not an import (P-583).
- The 45 does not give Georgi–Jarlskog's pattern: the first generation goes the wrong way. The candidate's second half is
  retracted.
- The scale is load-bearing (R1).
- The record has already answered its restated goal ("all 19 are free"), and the board does not say so.

**What would reshape the foundations — the triggers, as numbers.** These are A6's proposals; main concurs with the ones
marked main's.
- **The count (main's, Q1).** If P-418's vector does not fall over the next review cycle, the programme is a reformulation,
  not a derivation, and says so.
- **Specificity.** If the structural package's rate among comparable objects stays above about 10%, the genesis selection
  carries no physics evidence and A = LR stands as mathematics.
- **The scale.** If a frame's χ² moves by more than its allowance between M_Z and its own scale, the scale is load-bearing
  and must be derived or priced. R1 says it is: 11.8.
