## Gate A — Mutanguha (cite-and-apply). PASSES, and is STRONGER than B497 assumed.

**The theorem:** the mapping torus of a graph immersion has word-hyperbolic fundamental group **iff**
the endomorphism produces no Baumslag–Solitar subgroups — and it **"applies to all injective
endomorphisms of F₂"**, which is exactly P5's setting. The stated form: *if φ: Fₙ → Fₙ is
irreducible and non-surjective and the ascending HNN extension contains no BS(1,m) for any m ≥ 1,
that extension is word-hyperbolic.*

- **B497's Q3 becomes exact, not merely suggestive.** `G_dec = ⟨A,B,t | tAt⁻¹=A², tBt⁻¹=B²⟩` contains
  `BS(1,2) = ⟨A,t⟩` by Britton's lemma ⟹ **not word-hyperbolic**. That is now *the theorem's own
  criterion*, applied in the direction it was proved.
- **⚠ B497's Q2 is a HYPOTHESIS GAP.** Mutanguha's hypothesis is **no BS(1,m) subgroup** (plus
  irreducibility). B497's evidence is a **bounded search for periodic conjugacy classes** — 117
  cyclic classes, length ≤ 6, k ≤ 6, powers ≤ 4 — which is evidence for **atoroidality**, a related
  but *different* condition. **The paper must not write "by Mutanguha, φ_TM is hyperbolic" as if the
  hypothesis were checked.** It must say: bounded evidence for atoroidality; the theorem's actual
  hypothesis (no BS(1,m), irreducibility) is **not verified here**.

**Also surfaced:** *Hyperbolicity of Multiple Ascending HNN Extensions of Free Groups*
(arXiv 2604.19154, 2026) — directly adjacent and post-dating the outline. **Must be read before the
draft.**

## Gate B — the four-strata classification. NO PRIOR ART FOUND.

Searches over endomorphism classification, Hopfian/co-Hopfian structure, determinant and
abelianization invariants return **adjacent but distinct** objects: Carvalho–Delgado on
*free-abelian by free* groups; the classification of End(F_n × F_m) (a **product**, not F₂);
and the AFST 2020 paper below.

**Checked directly and cleared:** *"Endomorphisms and bijections of the character variety
χ(F₂, SL₂(ℂ))"* (AFST 2020) — despite the title matching P5's object, it proves that **restriction
to a proper subgroup never induces a bijection** between character varieties. **It classifies maps
between varieties, not endomorphisms of F₂, and uses neither κ = tr[a,b] nor a Hopf × det
stratification.** Adjacent, not prior art.

**One known theorem the paper MUST cite on the Hopf axis:** *ascending HNN extensions of finitely
generated free groups are Hopfian* (Geoghegan–Mihalik–Sapir–Wise). It bears directly on the frame's
Hopf coordinate.

## Gate C — κ-laws vs Cantat–Dujardin. HEAVILY POPULATED, and the delta is now precise.

Cantat–Dujardin and Cantat–Dupont–Martin-Baillon study **dynamics on Markov surfaces / character
varieties**, in a setting the literature states as *"the once-punctured torus with fundamental group
generated by two elements, where the commutator represents a simple loop around the puncture"* —
**that is κ = tr[a,b]'s setting exactly.**

> **They study AUTOMORPHISMS — the mapping-class-group action by polynomial automorphisms of the
> cubic surface. P5's object is the ENDOMORPHISM MONOID, including the non-invertible verbs.**

**This is the paper's real novelty claim and it must be written that way.** "The κ-laws are new" is
not defensible against this literature. **"The κ-laws for NON-INVERTIBLE endomorphisms — the
singular verbs, which the automorphism literature does not cover"** is.

## Gate D — the drift ledger. THE METHOD IS CLASSICAL; only the object can be the delta.

`E[log|2cos t|]` by the Fourier series `log|2cos t| = Σ(−1)^{k+1}cos(2kt)/k` is **textbook** (it is
the Mahler-measure computation). And Lyapunov exponents of random matrix products are a deep,
developed field — Furstenberg; Viswanath on random Fibonacci sequences; CLT results for exactly
these products.

> **The delta cannot be the computation.** B498's proof is a correct classical argument. **The claim
> must be the per-verb LEDGER over End(F₂)'s strata** — `E[log mult_D] = −2` and `E[log mult_M] = 0`
> *as invariants distinguishing the verbs* — not the technique that evaluates them.

**Highest residual risk of the four.** A specialist may know this ledger; the searches did not
settle it.

## Gate E — Stern–Brocot / Farey. **NOT IN THE OUTLINE'S LIST, AND IT IS THE SHARPEST GATE.**

Added on the review seat's prompt: *"R and L as monoid generators is Northshield's own territory —
a monoid paper about R, L words is much closer to his work than P4 was."* **The outline named
Axel–Peyrière/Bellissard, polynomial-semigroup/Fricke and Sapir/Mutanguha, and missed this entirely.**
Four gates had already run without it.

**The finding, and it is classical:**

> **The monoid SL₂(ℕ) is generated FREELY by the matrices L and R.**

This is standard in the Stern–Brocot/Farey literature (Reutenauer, *On the Stern–Brocot expansion of
real numbers*; Northshield on Stern's diatomic sequence; the L/R-word ↔ continued-fraction encoding).
**A paper claiming the free monoid structure on R, L as new would be pre-empted outright.**

**Why P5 survives it — and the reason is the paper's own thesis:**

| | object | level |
|---|---|---|
| Stern–Brocot / Farey literature | the monoid **SL₂(ℕ)** of positive integer matrices | **TORAL** (abelianization) |
| **P5** | the endomorphism monoid **End(F₂)** | **End(F₂)**, above the abelianization |

`End(F₂)` surjects onto the matrix level by abelianization, and **everything the Stern–Brocot
literature owns lives downstairs**: the free generation by L, R, the continued-fraction encoding, the
Farey/Stern combinatorics. **P5's Hopf coordinate — injective vs non-injective — is invisible there**,
because abelianization forgets it. U2 is *called* the toral floor for exactly this reason.

> **This is the paper's own methods paragraph, arriving as a lit-gate result: "every identity WITH its
> level (End(F₂)/trace-map/toral)." The gate that could have killed the paper is disarmed by the
> discipline the paper is about.**

**Binding consequence for Phase 2:** the toral floor must be **cited, not claimed**. Any sentence
about R, L words generating freely belongs to Stern–Brocot with a citation, and the four-strata
frame must be stated as a classification **of End(F₂)**, never of the matrix monoid.

**A second reason this gate matters:** the P4 correspondence went to Northshield, who replied that
he had not seen its non-principal phenomenon — **while noting he has been out of the field a long
time.** If P5 goes to the same reader, it lands much closer to his own work, and an uncited
Stern–Brocot claim would be conspicuous to precisely that audience.
