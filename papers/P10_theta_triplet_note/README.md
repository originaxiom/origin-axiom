# The theta triplet of Γ(4): a nilpotent field, a determinant identity, and a period phase along closed geodesics

A short mathematical note (draft 1, 5 pp.), drawn from the program report `papers/P9_what_a_grammar_fixes/` at the
owner's choice of both venues, on an outside referee's recommendation.

- `theta_triplet_note.pdf`: the built note.
- `tex/`: the sources and a subset of the report's checked bibliography.
- `checks/`: one script per computation (the nilpotent field, the determinant identity, the period phase on eight closed
  geodesics, the surface average of f, the cusp law); `verify.sh` runs them all.
- `build.sh`: builds the PDF.

Two sets of numbers are quoted from the record (B1666) and not recomputed here: the phase on the 30 674 closed geodesics
of trace at most 600, and the trace-class means.
