\title{What a single arithmetic 3-manifold forces,\\ what it withholds,\\ and what an observer costs}
\date{Draft --- \today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We ask what gauge structure can be extracted from one hyperbolic 3-manifold --- the figure-eight
knot complement $m004$ --- without importing a value. \textbf{We begin with what is generic.} The
$E_6$ recurrence that motivates such programmes is forced by a single ADE classification and is
\emph{not} evidence of specificity; on a census of one-cusped manifolds, roughly one in three admits
the surjection onto the binary tetrahedral group that the construction starts from, and a sibling
sharing the same trace field is not the knot. What survives is narrower and is a theorem of record:
$m004$ is the unique arithmetic knot complement, so its invariant trace field $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$ is
distinguished where the group-theoretic scaffolding is not.

Working from that surviving datum, we show the object forces a breaking chain that terminates at the
Standard Model gauge algebra, \emph{derives} the global form
$[\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)]/\Z_6$ --- an ambiguity the Standard Model
itself does not fix --- and admits a unique gaugeable abelian direction. We then show it provably
\emph{withholds} values: no period of the object is a Standard Model ratio, across an exhaustive
comparison, and the hypercharge normalisation is not derivable in principle from the anomaly
equations as algebraic forms.

\textbf{We report the misses.} $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$ is a known grand-unified relation reproduced,
not predicted, and its run to $M_Z$ misses at $16\sigma$. Three chiral generations are not derived.
No unique four-dimensional field theory follows, and unrestricted uniqueness is refuted.

Finally we price the residue. What the object cannot supply, it costs exactly: a freedom ledger with
one dimensionful unit that is external by design, two continuous dimensionless anchors, a
projective line of Higgs data cut to within one condition of a point set, and two discrete labels ---
one of which is a relational invariant of a pair, belonging to neither relatum, and requiring no act
of selection.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Ask what structure can be extracted from a single mathematical object without importing a physical
measurement, and two failure modes present themselves immediately. The first is to find structure and
overstate what it implies. The second is to find that the structure is generic and quietly not
mention it. This paper is organised to make both difficult.

We therefore invert the usual order. \S\ref{sec:generic} states what is \emph{generic} about the
construction, with the base rates, before any positive claim is made; \S\ref{sec:unique} states the
two facts that survive it. Only then do \S\S\ref{sec:forced}--\ref{sec:observer} say what is forced,
what is provably withheld, and what the residue costs. The freedom ledger of
\S\ref{sec:ledger} lists every input the construction requires; a reader who accepts nothing else
should still be able to audit that table, and it is the honest summary of the paper.

The object is the figure-eight knot complement. Its interest here is not that it is a knot
complement, nor that a chain of classifications leads from it to an exceptional Lie algebra --- both
of those turn out to be common. Its interest is that it is the \emph{unique arithmetic} knot
complement, and that the arithmetic, rather than the classification-theoretic scaffolding, is what
carries every claim we are prepared to make.

The result is a boundary rather than a theory. On one side: a gauge algebra with a derived global
form, a chain that terminates for a stated reason, and a unique gaugeable abelian direction. On the
other: values that are provably not present, a normalisation that is not derivable in principle, and
a residue that we can price but not eliminate. We end on the second side, in
\S\ref{sec:wall}, because that is where the work actually stops.

\section{What is generic}\label{sec:generic}

We state the base rates first, because a reader's prior on this subject is set by decades of
numerology and because we can measure most of them.

\paragraph{The recurrence is a graph identity.} Programmes of this kind typically observe that $E_6$
recurs across several apparently independent structures --- a McKay correspondence, a Lie
classification, a modular-invariant list, a du~Val singularity classification --- and treat the
coincidence as evidence. It is not. Those four are \emph{one} ADE classification presented four ways,
canonically linked; conditioned on the appearance of a single ADE label, the probability that it
``recurs'' across the others is $1$. There is no coincidence to explain.

\paragraph{And the catalogue is short.} $E_6$ is moreover the only exceptional label an imaginary
quadratic field can reach, so there is not even a birthday problem to argue about: the space of
outcomes has one exceptional element in it.

\paragraph{The entry point is common.} The construction begins with a surjection
$\pi_1(m004) \twoheadrightarrow 2T \cong \mathrm{SL}(2,3)$, of which there are exactly two. That
count is not distinctive. Over the first $400$ one-cusped census manifolds by volume, restricted to
two-generator presentations, \textbf{$37.2\%$ admit such a surjection and $32.8\%$ admit exactly two}
--- $m004$'s own count --- with the ties reproducing exactly across two independent implementations.
Roughly one hyperbolic $3$-manifold in three carries the fact that carries the chain.

\paragraph{The sibling is not the knot.} $m003$ shares the invariant trace field $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$
with $m004$ without being a knot complement, so the field alone does not single out the object
either. And at the level of grammars rather than manifolds, access to the relevant McKay group is
likewise generic across the metallic family.

\begin{scopenote}[What this does and does not do]
It does \emph{not} break the construction. The chain derives $m004$ rather than selecting it from a
census, so the population at its first steps is of size one and a base rate over manifolds cannot
falsify it. \textbf{What genericity removes is the endpoint's power to confirm the beginning.}
Arriving at $E_6$ is not evidence that the earlier steps were right, if a third of possible entry
points arrive at $E_6$ as well. \emph{The chain can be sound and the Standard Model still fail to
corroborate it.} Every positive claim in this paper is stated under that restriction.
\end{scopenote}

\begin{scopenote}[On our own history]
We did not begin with this discipline. The programme applied base-rate reasoning to numerical
coincidences long before it applied the same test to its flagship structural claim; when the test
was finally run, the structure came back generic. We record that lag because it is the honest
provenance of \S\ref{sec:generic}, and because the rule it produced --- \emph{before treating a
structural recurrence as object-specific, ask whether the faces are canonically linked, how short the
catalogue is, and whether a comparable object shares it} --- is the one we now apply throughout.
\end{scopenote}

\section{What is unique}\label{sec:unique}

Two facts survive \S\ref{sec:generic}. Everything this paper claims is built on them and on nothing
else.

\paragraph{Arithmeticity.} Among knot complements, $m004$ is the \emph{unique} arithmetic one. This
is a theorem of the literature, not of ours, and it is the reason the invariant trace field
$\Q(\sqrt{-3})$ is distinguished where the group-theoretic scaffolding above it is not. Where our
construction uses the object rather than its ADE shadow, it uses this.

\paragraph{A uniqueness at the level of grammars.} The second is ours, and it is a proof over an
infinite family rather than a census.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:golden}
Among the metallic grammars $R^m L^m$, exactly one has a modular shadow of McKay type, namely the
golden grammar $m = 1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Two independent facts meet once. First, $|\mathrm{SL}(2,\Z/N)|$ is the order of a binary polyhedral
group for exactly $N \in \{3,4,5\}$, giving $24 = |2T|$, $48 = |2O|$ and $120 = |2I|$ --- that is,
$E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$, the whole exceptional series and nothing else. Completeness here is a proof and
not a bounded search: from
\[
  |\mathrm{SL}(2,\Z/N)| \;=\; N^3\!\!\prod_{p \mid N}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{1}{p^2}\right)
  \;\ge\; N^3 \prod_{p}\left(1 - \tfrac{1}{p^2}\right) \;=\; \frac{N^3}{\zeta(2)} \;=\; \frac{6}{\pi^2}N^3 ,
\]
and $\tfrac{6}{\pi^2}N^3 > 120$ for every $N \ge 6$, no modulus beyond $5$ can qualify. Second, the
conductor of the metallic grammar $R^mL^m$ is $m^2+4$. Setting $m^2 + 4$ equal to each admissible
modulus: $m^2+4 = 3$ has no integer solution; $m^2+4=4$ gives the degenerate $m=0$, which is not a
metallic word; and $m^2+4 = 5$ gives $m = 1$. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\noindent That the three qualifying moduli are exactly the three exceptional McKay groups is a
coincidence we note and do not lean on. What the theorem supplies is narrow and real: a uniqueness
that survives \S\ref{sec:generic}, obtained over an infinite family, at the opposite end of the
construction from the census whose base rate defeated the earlier claim.

\paragraph{An outside meeting point.} Independently of this programme, a mixed Tate motive has been
constructed for finite-volume hyperbolic $3$-manifolds whose Beilinson regulator is the complex
volume, defined over the invariant trace field --- which for $m004$ is exactly $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$ --- with
the figure-eight complement as a verified case. We record it as corroboration that the arithmetic
datum, rather than the ADE scaffolding, is the load-bearing one.

\section*{Non-claims}
This paper does \emph{not} claim:
\begin{nonclaim} to derive the Standard Model. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} to predict any measured value. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} that the object is the universe, or a model of it. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} that three generations are forced. The object's honest content is one generation;
the family count is exhibited elsewhere and is not forced here. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} that $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$ is a prediction. It is a known grand-unified relation,
reproduced; the run to $M_Z$ misses at $16\sigma$. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} that any dynamics, rate, or action is supplied. \end{nonclaim}
\begin{nonclaim} that the observer's bit is consciousness, or that it is not. \end{nonclaim}

\section{What is forced}\label{sec:forced}

This section comes last in the writing for a reason. It is the easiest part of the argument to
overstate, and everything in it is now bounded by \S\ref{sec:generic}: what follows is forced
\emph{given the object}, and arriving here is not evidence that the route to the object was right.

\paragraph{The boundary and its half.} The construction's boundary algebra is $E_6$, and the build is
proved isomorphic to the exceptional Jordan-type model over $\mathbb{C}$. What the object supplies is
the \emph{adjoint} half of a grand-unified breaking and not the $27$ half. This is a statement about
the path and not about the landing site, and it comes with a mechanism: the adjoint does not occur in
$27 \otimes 27$, so \textbf{no adjoint expectation value can give any $27$ fermion a mass}. The
adjoint half does gauge structure and provably cannot do matter mass. The $27$ half is an input in
every theory anyone has, which is why we treat its absence here as a boundary rather than a defect.

\paragraph{The global form is derived.} The Standard Model's own data leaves the global form of the
gauge group ambiguous among $\{1, \Z_2, \Z_3, \Z_6\}$; the quotient is not determined by the algebra
and the representation content alone. Here it is forced:
\[
  \Gamma \;=\; \Z_6, \qquad
  \bigl[\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)\bigr]/\Z_6 ,
\]
with the kernel computed exactly, and uniformly across six independent realisations of the relevant
Weyl data. \textbf{This is the clearest positive result in the paper}: a structural ambiguity that the
Standard Model does not fix, fixed --- and it is falsifiable in principle through line-operator
spectra.

\paragraph{Hypercharge is the unique gaugeable direction.} In the abelian sector of the chain, the
alternative candidate directions are anomalous over chiral matter; hypercharge is the only one that
survives. \emph{Direction only}: by \S\ref{sec:withheld}, the normalisation is not derivable in
principle, and we claim nothing about it.

\paragraph{The cascade terminates, and the termination is the theorem.} The descent halts at the
Standard Model algebra not by stipulation but because that algebra is \emph{terminal} among the
registerable ones: every proper further descent destroys registerability, while the Standard Model
itself remains chiral. The positive control matters here --- the criterion is one that other
candidates fail --- and it is what converts ``the chain stops here'' from an observation into a
statement.

\begin{scopenote}
Absent from this list, deliberately: the \emph{arrival} at the Standard Model algebra. That the
descent lands on $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)^3$ is a classical fact
about $E_6$'s Levi subalgebras, not a discovery of ours, and it appears in
\S\ref{sec:recognition} instead. What survives as forced is the \emph{termination} and the
\emph{global form}, not the arrival.
\end{scopenote}

\section{What is withheld, and why each is a theorem}\label{sec:withheld}

The negative half of this paper is not a list of things we did not manage to compute. Each entry
below is a statement about what \emph{cannot} be extracted, with the computation or argument that
establishes it.

\paragraph{No period of the object is a Standard Model ratio.} The comparison is exhaustive rather
than illustrative: the object's periods were tested against the sealed target list under a fixed
bounded-height criterion, and none yields a relation. The natural invariants of the object --- its
volume, its Mahler-type quantities, its spectral data --- are likewise disjoint from the targets.

\paragraph{And the regulator route closes too.} The strongest form of the question asks whether any
\emph{regulator} enters an algebraic relation with a target. Over a $216$-cell grid against $18$
sealed targets, the number of relations passing the regulator gate is \textbf{zero}. When the basis
is extended to include the object's own complex volume --- a direction verified independent of the
existing basis, not a redundancy --- the count remains zero. The disjointness verdict therefore holds
on a basis containing the object's own canonical regulator, which is the sharpest form we can state.

\paragraph{The hypercharge normalisation is not derivable in principle.} This one is a theorem about
the equations rather than about the object. The anomaly-cancellation conditions, treated as algebraic
forms, are homogeneous in the abelian charge assignment; consequently they fix a \emph{direction} and
never a scale. The statement holds for any $\mathrm{U}(1)$ charge assignment in any theory, so it is
not a limitation peculiar to this construction, and no amount of additional structure of the kind we
have will remove it. Where this paper says hypercharge is forced, it means the direction, and says so.

\paragraph{Rank reduction is a theorem, not an absence.} The units the cascade cannot shed are the
standard extra abelian directions, and the reduction lives entirely in the steps the construction's
second measurement skips. Skipping them is skipping the rank reduction; it is not an oversight that
a longer search would repair.

\paragraph{Scale is free by construction.} Mostow rigidity fixes the shape of a finite-volume
hyperbolic $3$-manifold and not its size. Nothing downstream can recover what the geometry does not
carry.

\begin{scopenote}
These are stated at the strength of their evidence and no further. Each is a statement about
bounded-height algebraic combination at a declared precision, or about the algebraic form of a fixed
system of equations. None is a claim that no relation exists at any height, and we have not proved
that no construction of any kind could supply what this one does not.
\end{scopenote}

\section{The observer, priced}\label{sec:observer}

What the object cannot supply, it prices. This section states what that price is and what is known
about its structure; it is the part of the paper with the least precedent and therefore the part we
fence most carefully.

\paragraph{The obstructions are one class.} The construction meets several apparently independent
failures to close on itself --- an orientation cannot be chosen canonically, a sign in a chord sector
cannot be fixed, and two further probes of the same kind. These are \emph{one} $\Z/2$-torsor class
under a single global involution $c$, whose restrictions are simultaneously a Galois generator on the
trace field, a complex conjugation on a cyclotomic sector, and the geometric mirror. The proof is an
equivariance: a map of torsors under one group, equivariant for that group, is an isomorphism. One
basepoint choice trivialises all of them at once, and no invariant choice exists.

\paragraph{The bit is relational.} For a heterogeneous pair --- the object together with a partner ---
the mirror is realisable over $\mathrm{GL}_2(\Z)$ only with determinant $-1$. The resulting class is
mirror-odd, dimensionless, and belongs to the \emph{pair}: it is an invariant of neither relatum
separately, and it is invisible to the traces.

\paragraph{And it requires no act of selection.} That class is invariant under simultaneous
$\mathrm{GL}_2(\Z)$-conjugation of the pair. The bit therefore arrives as invariant relational data,
not as the output of a choice function --- which matters, because an invariant selector provably
cannot pick a point of its own orbit.

\paragraph{It is governed by the founding invariant.} The criterion deciding whether a pair carries
the bit is not an ad hoc construction: it is the same Fricke invariant $\kappa$ whose vanishing
condition founds the object's existence in the first place. The existence obstruction and the
observer-bit criterion are one invariant.

\paragraph{It cannot be absorbed.} Every operation internal to the object --- letter exchange, word
reversal, inversion, the lift signs, the Fricke twists --- preserves $\kappa$ \emph{identically}, as a
polynomial identity rather than at a point. But $\kappa$ takes different values at the object's own
point and at its mirror. No composition of internal operations can therefore carry one to the other:
the mirror is precisely the operation the object cannot perform on itself. \textbf{This is why the
bit is exactly one, and external.}

\paragraph{It is spent once.} The trace ring of the construction is generated at every depth by the
same quadratic integers; it does not grow. The single Galois choice is made when the abstract trace
field is embedded in $\mathbb{C}$ --- at coordinatisation, logically prior to anything dynamical ---
and is never demanded again. The observer's discrete cost does not accumulate with the depth or the
age of what is described.

\begin{scopenote}[Two supports we withdrew]
An earlier form of this section leaned on two further claims, and we removed both. \emph{First}: that
the anti-conjugating element acts as the Galois generator on both spectral fields, which we offered as
evidence that the class restricts to $c$. It does act so --- but \emph{necessarily}, for every
anti-conjugating element, including in a case the construction treats as inert; a property with no
failing instance discriminates nothing and supports nothing. \emph{Second}: that the partner is
canonical. It is not. Admissibility in the sense we required is satisfied by a large fraction of
candidate fields, and even at a fixed field the outcome varies with the relative embedding. Which
pair is used remains supplied.
\end{scopenote}

\begin{scopenote}[The limit of the section]
Nothing here asserts that the bit is, or is not, associated with experience. The construction's
language contains no proposition about experience, so the question is not answered negatively --- it
is not expressible, and we say so rather than converting the silence into a position.
\end{scopenote}

\section{Recognition}\label{sec:recognition}

Everything in this section is known, and appears here so that no reader has to wonder whether we
believed otherwise.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{The landing algebra.} That the descent from $E_6$ reaches
$\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{u}(1)^3$ is Borel--de~Siebenthal / Dynkin: it
is exactly the $A_2{+}A_1$ Levi subalgebra, whose dimension, semisimple part and centre reproduce the
three numbers the construction reports. \emph{Reproduced, not discovered.}
\item \textbf{The location of the rank reduction.} That the rank reduction lives in the steps above
the Standard Model, and that the unsheddable units are the standard extra abelian directions of
$E_6$, is standard structure theory.
\item \textbf{$\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$.} A known grand-unified relation at the unification scale,
\emph{reproduced}. Its run down misses badly, and we report the miss in the abstract rather than
here.
\item \textbf{The chain $E_6 \supset \mathrm{SO}(10) \supset \mathrm{SU}(5)$}, anomaly cancellation
over the $16$, and the decomposition $27 = 16 + 10 + 1$. All standard.
\item \textbf{Mostow rigidity's scale-freedom.} Standard, and the reason \S\ref{sec:withheld} can
state the scale row as a theorem rather than a gap.
\end{itemize}

\noindent What is \emph{not} on this list, and to our knowledge is not standard: the derivation of the
global form; the termination theorem; the exhaustive disjointness of the object's periods from the
target set; the normalisation no-go stated as a property of the equations; and the pricing of the
observer in \S\ref{sec:observer}.

\section{Falsifiers}\label{sec:falsifiers}

Each of the following would refute a specific claim of this paper, and for each we state the
computation that settles it.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Exhibit a quantity computable from the construction that differs between the two
anchored models.} This kills the one-bit result of \S\ref{sec:observer}: the claim is that the two
anchorings are isomorphic as models, so any internal quantity separating them refutes it.
\item \textbf{Exhibit two of the odd-parity signs as independently settable.} The paper predicts a
\emph{correlation} among them, not a value for any one; independent settability kills the
correlation.
\item \textbf{Exhibit a period of the object entering an algebraic relation with a target within the
stated height and precision.} This kills \S\ref{sec:withheld}'s central negative.
\item \textbf{Exhibit a proper descent below the Standard Model algebra that remains registerable.}
This kills the termination theorem.
\item \textbf{Exhibit a second gaugeable abelian direction in the chain over chiral matter.} This
kills the hypercharge-direction claim.
\item \textbf{Supply the missing condition on the projective Higgs line.} This one does \emph{not}
kill anything --- it \emph{upgrades} a row of the freedom ledger, and we name it here so that the
asymmetry is visible. Should it be supplied, the row becomes a finite point set and not a unique
prediction; see the scope note in \S\ref{sec:ledger}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The wall}\label{sec:wall}

We end where the work stops.

\paragraph{What a specialist would have to supply.} The remaining open rows of
\S\ref{sec:ledger} are not vague. The $\sigma$ row needs one object: a graded character of a boundary
chiral algebra at a stated central charge, meeting a short list of conditions --- a named module, the
correct prefactor, non-negative integer coefficients, and an asymptotic growth recovering the right
number of degrees of freedom, where the construction currently supplies one and needs six. The
candidate set is presently empty, and we have documented the emptiness rather than left it implied.

\paragraph{What has no gate at all.} The $\lambda$ row is weaker than that, and we say so. It is the
weight completing a tracial structure to one with a modular period, and we cannot presently state what
would count as deriving it. One sub-route is closed by a textbook fact; an attempted exhaustion
argument failed and has been withdrawn. \textbf{A row with no acceptance criterion is the least
defensible thing in this paper}, and naming it as such is the only honest treatment available.

\paragraph{What is permanent.} The dimensionful unit will not close, and it is a category error to
list it as missing --- nobody derives the metre, and no dimensionless quantity flows from it. The
finite labels will not reduce further either: an invariant selector cannot pick a point of its own
orbit, so a finite menu is the terminal state and not a deficiency.

\paragraph{What we are not claiming.} This is not a parameter-free Standard Model, and it is not a
theory of everything. Three chiral generations are not derived. No unique four-dimensional field
theory follows, and unrestricted uniqueness is refuted. There is no dynamics, no rate, and no action
beyond the structural statements above. What there is, is a boundary --- drawn in both directions,
with the negatives proved rather than reported, and with the residue priced.
