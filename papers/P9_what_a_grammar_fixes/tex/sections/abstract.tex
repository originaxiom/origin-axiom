We ask what a minimal principle of existence fixes once it is given a mathematical form, and we price everything it
does not.

The principle is non-cancellation: what exists is what remains when cancelling to nothing cannot complete. We give it a
chosen form, a grammar of two integer records acted on by two unit moves and their pull-backs. Every primitive hyperbolic closed path of the
moves is a thread, a closed geodesic on the modular surface. We study all threads at once, as one joint action, the
weave.

On the weave we show three things, the first for every word and the others in stated frames, and we state a fourth
conditionally.
\begin{itemize}
\item The records' three parities carry a triplet of $A_4$, for every word. A stated lift of the moves to it is the
  multiplier of $\Gamma(4)$'s weight-one theta triplet twisted by $\chi_\eta^{21}$, in a stated hand. That some power of
  $\chi_\eta$ appears is automatic once the lift agrees with the theta multiplier projectively; the agreement and the
  exponent are what we compute.
\item In the fibre frame, given the triplet postulate, every single-point coupling on that triplet has the triplet's
  isotropic vector as a two-sided null vector, by Jacobi's identity. This is a property of the coupling ansatz in one
  frame, not a prediction of a massless fermion.
\item In a twisted frame the coupling matrix's determinant and adjugate factor exactly through the determinants of
  its two arguments and their pairing, an identity over $M_2(\C)\times M_2(\C)$.
\end{itemize}
Conditionally, given the triplet postulate, one unit at the cusp, an extreme boundary domain, a reading of the weave's
surface that is verified only in part, and an index formula taken from the record, the four-dimensional index is
$\pm3$: the dimension of the postulated triplet read through a chosen domain.

The triplet's determinant character is $\chi_\eta^9$. If the moves act on the fermions as a symmetry, they are free of
the mixed anomaly with QCD, at the level of the character, exactly when they act on left- and right-handed quarks
alike. On the ruled branch a counterterm in $\arg\eta(\tau)$ then leaves a strong angle that is constant at weight one
and, at weights two and three, vanishes only where CP is conserved.

In the frames we studied, the same structure fixes none of the Standard Model's thirteen flavour numbers, and we locate where each must be
supplied. A census of one Higgs at the symmetric point, a scoreboard on seen data, fits the five non-electron mass ratios
on none of the admissible lines; it tests a model of the program's own, so it bounds the program and not the grammar. The miss is the down--lepton ratio $(m_\mu/m_\tau)/(m_s/m_b)$.

The motion of the grammar, read through B1663 (sealed) and B1666 (a scoreboard, sealed after B1664 and B1665 failed their
gates), gives three results.
\begin{itemize}
\item The pull-back's strength sets the side and the speed of the motion, but not the defect it averages, to the test's
  resolution.
\item Averaged by trace up to trace $600$, the threads approach the modular surface's constant mode, as equidistribution requires (a check of the
  instrument).
\item The matter amplitude's coherence decays, and the normalised coherence of the cusp family climbs toward one (a read-out).
\end{itemize}

No value of the Standard Model is derived. Every input the results use is listed in one table, with what it buys and what would discharge it.
