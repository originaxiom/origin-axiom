## §9. What the construction does not yield

We ask the reader to weigh this section equally with §§6–8. A construction that reaches
structure and **cannot** reach magnitude is a different object from one that has not *yet*
reached magnitude, and only the first is what we claim.

### 9.3 Scale-blindness is a theorem, not a shortfall

The misses above are empirical. The following is structural, and it explains them.

> **Theorem 9.3.** For this object the boundary action reduces to
> ```
>        S  =  −CS·k  −  Vol·σ,
> ```
> and its amphichirality forces `CS ≡ 0`. Hence
> ```
>        ∂S/∂k  =  −CS  ≡  0      identically.
> ```
> ## **The object is blind to the level `k`, and `k`-blindness IS amphichirality** — the same
> fact stated two ways.
>
> *(N7; B811, B1012.)*

The vanishing deletes **the only term in the action that could carry a scale.** So the
construction's silence about magnitudes is not a gap awaiting a better calculation; it is a
consequence of the symmetry that makes the object what it is. **A reader who wants magnitudes
from this object is asking it to be chiral, and it is not.**

### 9.5 The declared inputs of the cascade

§1.3 reported that of the 20 steps below the entrance, 18 are standard algebra. Those 18 carry
**declared input riders**, and they are named here rather than left implicit:

- **chirality** (three separate steps),
- **the VEV direction**,
- ## **the generation count.**

The last needs four clauses, and they travel together or not at all — a reader taking any one
of them alone will misread the record.

1. **A three is object-forced.** `h¹(M; 27) = 3` is a theorem.
2. ## **That three is NOT a generation triple.** No `ℤ/3` acts on the object — `Isom(4₁) = D₄` —
   and the classes are incommensurable blocks. The relevant triality is a symmetry of the
   *representation*, not of the object.
3. ## **Therefore "three generations" is a DECLARED INPUT wherever a derivation uses it.** Not
   smuggled — booked, and priced. **But not an output either.**
4. The branch structure that carries the ratios remains banked independently of (2) and (3).

**A reader who takes "3 generations" as this programme's prediction has misread it; a reader
who takes clause 3 as denying clause 1 has misread the theorem.**

### 9.6 What this section does and does not concede

**It concedes** that the construction makes no successful contact with measured values, that
the comparison surface it licenses is exhausted, and that it is provably unable to produce a
scale.

**It does not concede** anything about §§3–8. Those are theorems about a manifold and an
algebra, and their status is unaffected by the failure of a physical identification — **which
is precisely why Gate 5 forbids such an identification in the body.** The negatives here are
reported as negatives of *pre-registered comparisons*, and the structural results are reported
as mathematics. **Keeping those two ledgers separate is the discipline this paper is built
on**, and §10 gives the conditions under which the structural half would itself have to be
withdrawn.
