## §10. What would falsify the construction

A construction that cannot fail is not a construction. The four statements below are the ones
whose truth would end this programme, and **each is computable** — none requires a
measurement, and none is a matter of taste. **One of the four has already fired.**

### 10.1 The four

> **(F1) Chirality is impossible from any closing.** If it is *proved* that no closing of this
> object yields chiral structure, the construction cannot reach its stated endpoint.
>
> **Status: open in the falsifying direction.** Chirality is currently an **external input**
> (§9.5), not a proven impossibility. This is the falsifier nearest to firing.

> **(F2) Every dimensionless invariant the object produces is base-rate reachable.** If the
> quantities the construction emits are the kind of numbers a random comparable object also
> emits, then agreement with anything is not evidence.
>
> ## **Status: FIRED — 2026-08-09.**

> **(F3) The value layer is frame-relative in every frame.** It is already known to be
> frame-relative for the Hermitian pair. **A proof for *all* frames closes the value question
> permanently.**

> **(F4) The rank obstruction has no escape.** Proved already for the centralizer class. The
> one escape is an external object whose canonicity is now known to be **structurally**
> unreachable by orbit invariants. **If the arithmetic route also closes, the last gap in the
> structural layer is permanent.**

**Two of these four are one result from firing.** We state that here rather than in a
discussion, because it is the honest position and because it was written down *before* the
comparisons of §9 were run, not after.

### 10.2 The one that fired, and exactly how far it fired

F2 is reported in full, because a fired falsifier that is quietly rescoped is worse than no
falsifier at all.

Of **23 enumerated dimensionless invariants**, **22** were graded unreachable or base-rate
dead. The 23rd survived longer and then fell **three independent ways**: it is a
parametrization tautology for every phase; its apparent sharpness was **refuted** — the
quantity sits `0.89σ` from the proposed value, not the `7σ` originally claimed — and it is
firewalled as a post-hoc, convention-framed fit the construction does not derive; and a
systematic search over the object's entire real algebraic tower and canonical analytic values
returned **zero gated hits**, with that candidate *found and correctly rejected*.

> ## **Scope, and it is the whole difference. The falsifier fires FOR THE ENUMERATED SPACE, not
> in principle.** The atlas is 23 *enumerated* invariants, not a proof that no invariant
> exists; the tower search covered the algebraic tower and canonical analytic values, not every
> possible construction. A later scope note recorded that the enumerated space never contained
> one further family, which has since been weighed and **neither admitted nor excluded**.

**What follows for this paper is a constraint we accept.** The construction has **no live
candidate** for a derived dimensionless value; every candidate it enumerated is dead, and the
enumeration was its own best attempt. Any future attempt requires a candidate from **outside**
both that atlas and that tower.

**And this is the standard the entrance theorem of §5.4 is held to.** The uniqueness proved
there **yields integers, not measured ratios** — it is structure-without-values by
construction. **It is therefore not a candidate under F2, and must not be presented as one.**

### 10.3 What would not falsify it

For symmetry with §10.1, and to prevent a defence the paper does not want:

- **A failed comparison with a measured quantity does not falsify §§3–8.** Five such failures
  are reported in §9; none touches the theorems, because none of the theorems asserts a
  measured value.
- **Nor does the exhaustion of §9.2 falsify the mathematics.** It closes a route, and we say so.
- **Conversely — and this is the direction that matters — a *successful* comparison would not
  confirm §§3–8 either.** By §1.3 and §10.2, arrival at this structure is generic and the
  value layer is base-rate dead; an agreement would need to survive the same base-rate test
  everything else failed.

**The construction is falsifiable as mathematics and, at present, not testable as physics.**
That asymmetry is the paper's honest position, and §9 and this section are where it is stated
rather than managed.
