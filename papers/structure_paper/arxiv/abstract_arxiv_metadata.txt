Let R^mL^m, m >= 1, be the metallic family of Sturmian morphisms. We characterize it
intrinsically: the hyperbolic unimodular classes whose dominant eigenvalue has purely
periodic continued fraction of period one are exactly those of determinant -1, with traces
exactly the integers m >= 1. The characterization is by trace and determinant, not
conjugacy class, and period one is itself a declared input, which we price.

Inside the family we exhibit four exceptionality criteria, three selecting the golden
member m=1 and a fourth narrowing to {1,2}. The three collapse to one arithmetic
condition, each reducing to m^2=1; the surviving independence is mechanistic.

The golden member is the figure-eight knot complement. From there nothing is added: we measure
(pass to centralizers) and track (follow where the centralizer jumps).
The target algebra comes from the manifold's own arithmetic: reducing the trace-field
representation at the ramified prime gives pi_1(4_1) ->> SL(2,F_3) = 2T, and McKay gives
e_6, carrying a canonical four-dimensional charge algebra C. A separate simplex filter
selects d=3, though that criterion is imported rather than derived.

We prove C is toral, not merely abelian, so every centralizer in the cascade is a Levi
subalgebra, and the terminal type A_2 + A_1 is forced by the classification of Levi
subsystems of e_6. The charge field is computed exactly: K = Q[x]/(x^3-12x-5), totally
real, discriminant
6237 = 3^4.7.11, class number one. One stratum has Levi type A_4; that SU(5) contains
su(3)+su(2)+u(1) with kernel Z/6 is recorded, not claimed.

We report the ceiling with equal weight -- a theorem about the method, not a limit of
effort: every centralizer of a subset of C contains a Cartan subalgebra, so every rung has
rank 6, and any lower-rank target is unreachable by any sequence of measurements. We make
no physical identification, and we do not treat real forms.
