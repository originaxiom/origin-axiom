Let R^mL^m, m >= 1, be the metallic family of Sturmian morphisms. We characterize it
intrinsically: the hyperbolic unimodular classes whose dominant eigenvalue has purely
periodic continued fraction of period one are exactly those of determinant -1, with
traces exactly the integers m >= 1. The characterization is by trace and determinant,
not by conjugacy class -- at m=6 a second class occurs -- and period one is itself a
declared input.

We exhibit four exceptionality criteria on the family, three selecting the golden member
m=1 and a fourth narrowing to {1,2}. The three collapse to one arithmetic condition:
they test chi_m(1), chi_m(-1) and tr(phi_m) of one characteristic polynomial, each
reducing to m^2=1.

The golden member is the figure-eight knot complement. From there nothing is added: we
measure (pass to centralizers) and track (follow where the centralizer jumps).
Nor is the target algebra chosen from a menu: the regular d-simplex has rotation group
A_{d+1}, which lies in SO(3) only for d in {2,3,4}, and of those only d=3 has a McKay
image both exceptional and with a complex fundamental -- and d=3 is what the
figure-eight is glued from. This yields E_6 with a canonical charge algebra C of
dimension four.

We prove C is toral, not merely abelian, so every centralizer in the cascade is a Levi
subalgebra. Hence the terminal type A_2 + A_1 is forced by the classification of Levi
subsystems of E_6; the attainable dimensions form an explicit 14-element set; and the
second measurement is exhaustive. The charge field is K = Q[x]/(x^3-12x-5), monogenic,
totally real, of discriminant 6237 = 3^4 * 7 * 11 and class number one.

We report the ceiling with equal weight: every rung has rank 6, so any lower-rank target
is unreachable by any sequence of measurements. We make no physical identification and
treat no real forms.
