# THE PAPER CAMPAIGN — masterplan

*Opened 2026-08-15 after the owner's assessment that the paper was being produced
reactively — "stamping train tickets" — and that the preparation deserved better than an
"unprepared, uninformed paper." That assessment is correct and this document exists to
name the failure precisely enough to design against it.*

---

## 0. What actually went wrong, stated without softening

Three drafts were produced. Each responded to the last critique instead of to the
corpus. The diagnostic evidence:

| # | failure | the evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ## **Wrote before inventorying** | **B251 — the manifold-level uniqueness `H₁(Mₘ) = ℤ⊕(ℤ/m)²`, the strongest single result now in the paper — was found on the SECOND sweep, after two full drafts existed.** So was B92, which is what answers the "you chose the family" objection. The paper's content was my context window, not the programme. |
| **2** | **Patched instead of designed** | v1 → v2 → v3, each triggered by an external report. **No version was ever designed from a settled claim set.** The §-structure was chosen once, in one pass, by one seat. |
| **3** | ## **Let "forced" stand until challenged** | The period-one restriction and the principal `sl₂` placement were both unpriced until an outside reader caught them. There has never been a systematic hunt for unpriced choices — and the cost claim is *the paper's thesis*. |
| **4** | **Verified my own work** | Every check was run by the seat that wrote the claim. The §§9–10 certificates have **never** been recomputed by anything that did not author them. |
| **5** | **Considered no alternatives** | One architecture, never compared against another. |

**The campaign inverts all five.** Inventory before writing; design from the inventory;
hunt unpriced choices as a first-class phase; verify by agents that did not write; and
generate competing architectures before committing to one.

## 1. The standard the paper must meet

From the owner's framing and both referee passes, the target is:

> **We classify the cost of a chain from minimal symbolic dynamics to a hyperbolic
> arithmetic manifold, and show that, conditional on a small explicit set of choices,
> all subsequent structure through an `A₂+A₁` centralizer is forced.**

For that sentence to be **literally true**, every occurrence of *forced* must be backed
by a theorem or an exhaustive classification. **That is the campaign's acceptance test**,
and it is not met today: `§7.6` assumes exceptionality, `C4` is an argument from economy,
and several §§9–10 rows are certificates.

---

## 1b. Preproduction is the film

**The owner's framing, adopted as the campaign's organizing principle: preproduction is
the most important part.** You do not fix a bad script in the edit — and v1 → v2 → v3 was
precisely an attempt to fix the paper in the edit. Every defect an outside reader found
was a **preproduction** defect surfacing late:

| defect found in the edit | where it was actually made |
|---|---|
| the period-one restriction unpriced | no choice ledger existed |
| the principal `sl₂` unpriced | same |
| `B251` missing from two drafts | no inventory existed |
| "McKay group" vacuous | no claim pool with warrants |
| "exactly two arithmetic bundles" false | no independent recomputation |
| one architecture, never compared | no design phase |

**None of these was a writing problem. All six were preproduction problems**, and each
cost a full rewrite because it was caught after the shoot.

### The consequence for this campaign

> ## **Waves 1–3 are not preparation for the work. They ARE the work.**
> **Wave 4 is a shoot: it executes a locked plan and invents nothing.**

Concretely, three rules follow, and they are the campaign's spine:

1. ## **Nothing is written until preproduction LOCKS.** No prose, no LaTeX, no section
   drafts. The current 19-page draft is frozen as `v3` and is **evidence, not a base** —
   it may be quoted from, but the new paper is built from the locked plan, not edited
   from v3.
2. ## **A locked plan is one where every claim has a warrant and every choice has a
   price.** If a claim's status is unresolved at lock time, it does not enter the paper.
   There is no "we'll tighten it in revision," because that is the habit being broken.
3. ## **Wave 4 has no authority to add.** A drafter who finds a gap does not fill it;
   it goes back to Wave 1. The shoot does not rewrite the script.

**Budget follows the principle.** The waves are weighted toward preproduction —
roughly **14 agents before a word is written, 6 to write, 11 to check** — and if the
campaign must be cut, it is cut from the back, never the front. A locked plan with a
hand-written paper beats a fanned-out draft from an unlocked one, which is exactly what
we have now.

## 2. The waves

Each wave ends in a reviewable artifact and **must be read before the next launches.**
No wave writes prose except Wave 4.

### Wave 1 — INVENTORY (fan-out, read-only)
*Question: what does the programme actually contain that belongs in this paper?*

Not "find strong results." A **typed, complete census**, one agent per territory:

| agent | territory |
|---|---|
| 1 | genesis: words, continued fractions, the metallic family, the bundles |
| 2 | the object: `m004`'s arithmetic, congruence, spectral, the `V₄` |
| 3 | the entrance: McKay, shadows, the two ends, conductor/modulus |
| 4 | the algebra: `𝔢₆`, the torus, the cascade, real forms, the `27` |
| 5 | the negatives: crossings, exhaustion, scale, gravity, falsifiers |
| 6 | **the anti-inventory**: retracted, withdrawn, quarantined, contradicted — *what must NOT enter* |

Each returns rows of `(arc, exact claim, PROVED/COMPUTED/CENSUS/ASSERTED, lock path,
lock passes?, cited-in-paper?)`. **Every lock run.** The union is the claim pool; nothing
enters the paper that is not in it.

### Wave 2 — ARCHITECTURE (judged design)
*Question: given the pool, what is the paper?*

**Three independent architects**, each given the Wave-1 pool and the acceptance test,
each producing a §-plan with every claim assigned and every "forced" traced to its
warrant. Then **three judges** scoring on: does the cost claim survive; is the novel
content foregrounded; can a referee reconstruct it. Synthesis takes the winner and
grafts the runners-up's best moves.

**This is the wave that never happened.** It is why the paper has one architecture.

### Wave 3 — THE PROOF AND PRICE AUDIT (adversarial, per-claim)
Two independent passes over the Wave-2 plan:

- **The proof auditor** — for every claim: proof, classification, certificate, or
  assertion? Anything not a proof or classification that carries the word *forced* is a
  defect and is either proved, demoted, or cut.
- ## **The unpriced-choice hunter** — the phase whose absence cost us twice. Walk the
  chain and at every step ask *what else could have been done here, and is the answer
  recorded?* Output: a complete choice ledger. **The census of declared choices is
  whatever this returns**, not what the paper currently says.

### Wave 4 — WRITE
Section drafters working **only** from the Wave-2 plan and the Wave-3 ledger, each
producing one section with its proofs. No drafter invents a claim; anything missing goes
back to Wave 1.

### Wave 5 — INDEPENDENT RECOMPUTATION
Agents that did **not** write the claims recompute every number from the statement alone
— never from our scripts. Divergence is a finding, not a nuisance. **This is what has
never been done.**

### Wave 6 — HOSTILE READ
Per-section referees, plus one "what's missing" critic, plus one reader who has only the
PDF and must reconstruct the argument. Their findings feed Wave 3, not Wave 4 — a
finding is a price or a proof gap, never a wording fix.

---

## 3. Rules binding every wave

1. **No agent may cite what it has not opened.** Arc IDs come with quotes.
2. **Cross-search before asserting absence** — the owner's standing instruction, and it
   has already overturned two of my conclusions today.
3. **A lock that is not run is not a lock.**
4. **Retracted material is fenced by the Wave-1 anti-inventory**, not by memory.
5. **Every "forced" carries its warrant inline** or is demoted in the same edit.
6. **Disagreement between agents is escalated, not averaged** — the two sweeps today
   disagreed about B258 and the resolution was a terminology distinction neither had.

---

## 4. What this campaign will cost, honestly

Roughly **6 + 6 + 2 + 6 + 5 + 6 ≈ 30 agents** across six waves, plus synthesis. That is
well beyond a single fan-out and it is the owner's call. **It is also the only design
that fixes the five failures in §0** — a smaller campaign reproduces them.

**Waves 1–3 alone (14 agents) are the campaign's essential half**, because they produce
the claim pool, the architecture and the choice ledger the current draft never had.
**Per §1b, if the campaign must be cut, it is cut from the back.** Preproduction plus a
hand-written paper is strictly better than a fanned-out draft from an unlocked plan —
and the latter is what exists today.
