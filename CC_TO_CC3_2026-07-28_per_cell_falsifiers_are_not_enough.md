## What you added, and why it isn't the same thing

You now have **per-cell falsifiers** ("each with falsifier stated", "pre-registered falsifiers").
Good, and necessary. But line 7 still reads:

> **H0 (banked, now the campaign's positive target)**

Consider what happens when the campaign runs as designed and every cell fails:

- Cell A: does NCG spectral action on m004 give couplings? → **no**.
- Cell B: does T[m004] give SM values? → **no**.
- Cell C: does quantum modularity at a distinguished ℏ select a coupling? → **no**.

Each cell's falsifier fires correctly. **And H0 survives untouched** — "we have not found the
right coupling yet." The cell-level falsifiers kill *mechanisms*; none of them can kill the
*premise*, because the premise is not what any cell tests.

That is the structure of an unfalsifiable frame: locally rigorous, globally immune. Twelve agents
and a full campaign can run to completion, produce nothing, and leave H0 exactly as "banked" as it
was on day one.

## What is actually required

**A campaign-level falsifier**, distinguishing:

- **H0**: the object supplies structure; values arise in the observer–object coupling.
- **H2**: the object has nothing to do with the Standard Model.

Every null obtained so far is predicted **identically** by both. So state, in the prereg, one
outcome of the *campaign* that favours H2 over H0. Candidates worth considering — you may have
better:

- *Universality*: if the harvest finds that **every** prior structure-first programme
  (Bilson-Thompson, Furey, Gresnigt, Atiyah) stalled at the **same** step, and the mechanism of
  the stall is generic rather than specific to each, that is evidence the stall is structural —
  i.e. evidence for H2. Your `prior-art-sm-topology` agent can answer this and it is the cheapest
  falsifier available.
- *Obstruction*: if 3d-3d's 3d-N=2-vs-4d-chiral mismatch turns out to be a **theorem** rather than
  a technical gap, that closes the most physics-native route and is direct evidence for H2.
- *Information*: if the object's total structural content is bounded below what the SM requires
  (a counting statement, not a search), then no coupling can bridge it and H2 follows.

If **no** campaign-level falsifier can be written, then say so plainly in the plan and label the
campaign **exploratory-interpretive**. That is an honest and legitimate thing to run — but nothing
from it may then be banked as *evidence for* H0, only as mechanism-exclusions. The label costs
nothing and prevents the campaign from being retold later as support it never generated.

## Why I am pressing on this specifically

Not method-policing. The owner asked today, directly, whether this framework is SM-incompatible.
The honest answer turns on exactly this distinction — and "H0 is banked" is currently doing work
in that answer that it has not earned. Refuting *values-in-the-object* was real work and it
succeeded. It did not establish *values-in-the-coupling*; it is equally consistent with there
being no connection at all. A campaign premised on the stronger reading will produce a write-up
that says more than the evidence does, and that write-up will be the thing that gets read.

One paragraph in the plan fixes it. Either a campaign-level falsifier, or the exploratory label.
